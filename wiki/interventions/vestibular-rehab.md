Vestibular rehab — therapy targeting the vestibular system (balance, eye-movement coordination, spatial orientation) — has a long history in concussion / TBI rehab and is increasingly being applied to LC and POTS. Standard delivery is gross movements ("don't fall over"); the targeted-microdrill model used by Dr Nathan Keiser is much smaller-grained.

## Targeted vestibular micro-drills (Keiser approach)

- Tiny head-turn movements, eyes-closed return
- Pupil dilation watched as the failure marker — when the pupil expands, the system has hit capacity
- Stop the drill immediately at failure, recover overnight, repeat next day at slightly more
- *Push to the edge of capacity, not past it*

## Why this matters in LC

- Many LC patients have vestibular contributions to brain fog, dizziness, and "stuck" cerebral hypoperfusion
- Standard vestibular rehab can be too gross and trigger PEM
- Microdrill format is appropriately sized for fragile nervous systems
