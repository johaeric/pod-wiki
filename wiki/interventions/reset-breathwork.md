# Reset Breathwork

A long-form breathwork modality used by [[meg-anderson]] (#135) for LC patients who cannot tolerate any traditional exercise. The protocol takes the practitioner gently into a sympathetic state and then drops them into a deeper parasympathetic state — *simulating* the sympathetic→parasympathetic cycle that exercise would otherwise produce, but at much lower physiological cost.

The clinical use case: in patients whose autonomic dysfunction prevents them from coming back down after exercise (heart rate up, no recovery — the marathon-all-day pattern), reset breathwork can open a path to recovery training that traditional exercise cannot.

## Caveat

Distinct from short paced/cadence breathing (the integrative-breathing-therapy / Buteyko / Wim Hof / boxbreathing families). Reset breathwork is a longer guided session designed for nervous-system retraining, not a daily quick-tool.
