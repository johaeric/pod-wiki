Cordyceps (commonly *Cordyceps sinensis* or *C. militaris*) is a fungal supplement used in traditional Chinese medicine for fatigue, energy, and respiratory/immune support. Available widely as a daily capsule or tincture.

## Why used in LC

- Recommended by integrative practitioners for patients with persistent LC (>1 year) to "fill the void of fundamental qi" — i.e. rebuild basic energetic capacity
- Generally considered low-risk and over-the-counter
