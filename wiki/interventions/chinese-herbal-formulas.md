# Chinese Herbal Formulas

Multi-herb balanced combinations from the East Asian medical formulary, distinct from single-herb supplementation. A formula is selected against the patient's specific pattern (the "internal landscape") and adjusted as the pattern shifts — root/foundational treatment combined with branch/symptom treatment.

⚠️ Drug-interaction caveat: integrative practice (e.g. integrative inpatient hospitals in Taiwan) prescribes these alongside Western meds with full understanding of interactions; lay use without practitioner supervision is risky.
