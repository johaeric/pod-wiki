# Meditation

Mindfulness, focused-attention, body-scan, and guided variants. On the podcast it appears most often as part of a multi-component **rest-and-down-regulation** practice rather than as a stand-alone treatment. Suzy Bolt's recovery and Amy Anker's recovery both name meditation as part of the daily routine — neither isolates it as the single key.

## Sub-flavours referenced

- **Insight Timer** — most-named meditation app on the podcast (AJ in #13, multiple others)
- **Body scan** / yoga nidra–adjacent (see [[yoga-nidra]])
- **Mindfulness as meta-habit** — Amy Anker (#20) names mindfulness of *thoughts about symptoms* as the lever for breaking the looping pattern
