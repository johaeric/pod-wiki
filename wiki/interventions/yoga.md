# Yoga

Hatha-style postures and pranayama, typically delivered gently and at low intensity for LC patients (often supine / restorative work, not flow). On the podcast it almost always shows up in **combination with breathwork** rather than as standalone exercise — the line between "yoga for LC" and "breathwork" is fuzzy.

Distinguish this from **vinyasa flow / hot yoga** which are physically demanding and risk PEM in this population.
