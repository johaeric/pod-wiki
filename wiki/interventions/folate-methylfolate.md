# Folate / Methylfolate / Folic acid

Vitamin B9. Methylation cofactor, partner to [[vitamin-b12]] in homocysteine clearance and one-carbon metabolism, essential for DNA synthesis and red-cell production. **Folate** is the natural food form (leafy greens, legumes); **folic acid** is the synthetic form used in fortification and most cheap supplements; **methylfolate** (L-5-MTHF) is the active form preferred for MTHFR-variant patients who can't efficiently convert folic acid.

## Honesty about the evidence here

This page is thin. The LCP/Raelan corpus contains **one** explicit folate mention — [[lily-spechler]] (LCP #129), a long-COVID dietitian, using folate as an example of how restrictive low-carb diets deplete B-vitamins. That's `mentioned_only`, not a treatment recommendation for folate as an intervention. No corpus guest credits folate or methylfolate as having helped their recovery; no clinician in the indexed episodes calls it out as a key LC supplement to test or supplement.

The relevant context for Eric: folate is almost always **co-mentioned with [[vitamin-b12]]** (methylation pair) in nutrition discussions outside the corpus. If you're acting on the B12 page, folate status is the standard co-test.

## What the corpus says

### Dietary B-vitamin depletion via low-carb diets
**[[lily-spechler]] (LCP #129)** — frames folate alongside magnesium and "all the B vitamins" as nutrients that drop dramatically on overly-restrictive (e.g. "chicken and ghee") diets, and rebound when whole grains are reintroduced. Her broader argument: carbs are not "just sugar" — they carry the B-vitamin micronutrient load that LC patients often need.

> "Look at your folate levels, look at your magnesium levels, look at all the B vitamins, they're so low. Now what happens when we simply add two slices of whole wheat bread and you see all of those levels up so dramatically." — Lily Spechler, LCP #129

This is **food-first** framing, not a folate-supplement recommendation. Spechler doesn't address methylfolate-vs-folic-acid form choice in the segment.

## Forms (context, not from corpus)

- **Folate** — natural form in leafy greens, beans, lentils, asparagus, avocado
- **Folic acid** — synthetic; cheapest supplement form; the form in food fortification (cereals, flour in many countries). Some MTHFR-variant patients metabolise poorly.
- **L-5-MTHF (methylfolate)** — active form; preferred for MTHFR variants and methylation-sensitive patients. More expensive.

## Cautions

- **Mask B12 deficiency** — high-dose folate can correct the megaloblastic-anaemia presentation of B12 deficiency while letting the neurological damage progress. **Always check B12 status before supplementing folate at therapeutic doses.** Standard nutrition-medicine caveat; not explicitly covered in the LC corpus but load-bearing for the [[vitamin-b12]] pairing.
- **Methylfolate "overmethylation"** — some patients (especially MTHFR-variant on methylation protocols) report anxiety, irritability, or insomnia at higher methylfolate doses. Start low.
- **Folic acid + MTHFR** — controversial topic outside the corpus; standard nutrition-medicine guidance is "if you have an MTHFR variant, use methylfolate." Not addressed in the corpus.
