# COVID Vaccine (post-acquisition / mid-LC)

The vaccine taken **after** an LC patient has already been infected and is symptomatic with long covid. Distinct from prophylactic vaccination of the never-infected. In 2021 a wave of anecdotal patient reports surfaced of LC symptoms improving (or worsening) post-vaccination; the formal evidence picture has remained mixed across cohorts.

> Wiki position: log anecdotal patient outcomes only. Mechanism speculation is **not** the wiki's job — the literature is contested and Eric should treat individual-patient claims here as the data point they are. Vaccine-injured patients in this corpus (notably [[caroline-pover]], LCP #84) anchor the *opposite* direction of this evidence axis — that page captures the harm side; this page captures the help side. Both can be true across different patients.
