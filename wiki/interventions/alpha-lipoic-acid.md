# Alpha-Lipoic Acid (ALA / R-ALA)

A fat- and water-soluble antioxidant cofactor for mitochondrial enzymes; well-established outside the corpus for diabetic peripheral neuropathy. **The corpus signal is thin** — only one explicit recommendation, and it's nerve-pain-specific.

> **Disambiguation note for transcript searches**: LCP #33 (Pretorius / Kell) discusses "lipoic acid" as a gram-positive bacterial cell-wall component that mimics LPS in triggering amyloid microclots. That is **lipoteichoic acid (LTA)**, mis-captioned as "lipoic acid" by YouTube auto-captioning — not the antioxidant supplement alpha-lipoic acid. Excluded from this page.

## Why it shows up

[[jacob-teitelbaum]] (#167) names alpha-lipoic acid and acetyl-L-carnitine as the **natural-medicine pair for severe nerve pain** in long-COVID and ME/CFS — small-fibre neuropathy, painful neuropathic burning, neuropathic LC pain. Pair-recommendation, not standalone.

## Forms

- **ALA (racemic, S+R)** — generic supplement, cheaper
- **R-ALA** — the biologically active enantiomer, more expensive, claimed-better-absorbed (not raised in corpus)

## Use cases (corpus)

### Nerve pain / small-fibre neuropathy
[[jacob-teitelbaum]] (#167):

> "If you're having severe nerve pain… the natural remedies are lipoic acid and acetyl-L-carnitine."

Paired with the medication sequence he covers in chapter 3 of his pain book (over a dozen meds, ordered, plus dosing guidance). The natural-remedy pair is the *starting* layer; medications layered on if insufficient.

## Dosing

- **Not specified by Teitelbaum in this episode**. Standard non-LC neuropathy dosing outside the corpus is 600–1800 mg/day (often given in divided doses, on empty stomach for absorption).

## Cautions (general, not corpus-specific)

- **Hypoglycemia** — ALA can lower blood glucose; relevant if on insulin/sulfonylureas.
- **Thiamine depletion** — chronic high-dose ALA can deplete biotin and thiamine; some clinicians dose with B-complex.
- **Heavy-metal chelation** — ALA mobilises heavy metals; in mercury-amalgam contexts, dose carefully (raises Andy Cutler / Hal Huggins debates outside this corpus).
- **MCAS** — like any supplement, excipient reactions possible; not raised by Teitelbaum.
