# Rivaroxaban

Direct oral anticoagulant (Factor Xa inhibitor). Included as the **anticoagulant arm of the [[stimulate-icp]] platform trial** as of #72 — the first formally trialled anticoagulant for long covid in the wiki.

Distinct from the Khan-Pretorius **triple-therapy** protocol (clopidogrel + apixaban + aspirin) seen earlier in the wiki — those are off-label, empirical, and high-risk; rivaroxaban here is single-agent, monitored, and being formally trialled. Banerjee explicitly notes (#72) that triple-therapy trials cannot precede single-agent trials due to bleeding risk.
