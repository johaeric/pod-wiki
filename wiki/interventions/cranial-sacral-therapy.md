# Cranial sacral therapy

A gentle hands-on therapy involving light touch around the head, neck, and sacrum, intended to release restrictions in the cranial-sacral system (the membranes and cerebrospinal fluid surrounding brain and spinal cord). Mechanism is contested in mainstream medicine; reported subjective benefits include relaxation, autonomic-tone shifts, sleep improvement, and pain reduction.

In a chronic-illness / autonomic-dysregulation context, the most defensible framing is that any extended period of **gentle, parasympathetic-supporting touch** can downregulate sympathetic tone — the specific cranial-sacral mechanism may or may not matter. This puts it in the same category as gentle massage, vagus-nerve stimulation, and meditative bodywork.

## What's been said in the podcast

- **[[saskia-mulder]] (#48).** Reports `helped_partial` — specifically credits it with regulating her menstrual cycle (which had become every 2 weeks). Recommended to her by [[fola-oyinlade]], who says cranial sacral was the first thing that started healing her own ME.

## Caveats

- **Practitioner-quality variance is huge.** Choose a registered cranial-osteopath or BCST-trained practitioner.
- **Cost.** Sessions £50-100 typical; multiple sessions usually needed.
- **Travel + lying still.** PEM-significant for severe LC patients.
- **Mechanism uncertain.** Hold this as a parasympathetic-bodywork intervention rather than reading the cranial-sacral framing literally.
