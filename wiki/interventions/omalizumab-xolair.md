# Omalizumab (Xolair)

Anti-IgE monoclonal antibody. Approved for chronic spontaneous urticaria, allergic asthma, nasal polyposis. In LC: relevant for the **MCAS / urticaria / angioedema** subset.

> Cross-ref: see `ercipedia/wiki/interventions/omalizumab.md` for trial evidence in CIU.
