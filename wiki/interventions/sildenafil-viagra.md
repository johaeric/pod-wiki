# Sildenafil (Viagra) — low-dose for LC

PDE5 inhibitor. Causes endogenous NO production, vasodilation, and stem-cell mobilisation that supports blood-vessel repair.

**Already approved at low doses for babies in ICU with damaged lung vasculature** — exactly the kind of injury seen in severe covid lungs.

## Why it might matter for LC

Per William Li (#73): a candidate microvascular-repair drug at low doses. Distinct from the high doses used for erectile dysfunction. Research direction is opening up around lung-microvascular repair in LC.
