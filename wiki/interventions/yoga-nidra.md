# Yoga Nidra

A guided supine deep-rest practice: lie still, follow the teacher's voice through body-scan / sensation-shift / visualisation. Unlike active meditation it is *intended* to induce a hypnagogic state (sometimes sleep). Andrew Huberman (Stanford) frames it as a form of **non-sleep deep rest (NSDR)**, citing claims that 1 hr of yoga nidra approaches the nervous-system recovery of ~4 hr of sleep.

For LC, the case is **vagal-tone / down-regulation** — explicit interruption of the sympathetic-dominant pattern.

## Standard delivery

- 20–60 min audio (apps: Insight Timer, plus countless free YouTube)
- Lie down. Eye mask + weighted blanket common.
- Pre-bed for sleep; mid-day for an energy reset.
