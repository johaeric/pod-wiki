Water with high dissolved molecular hydrogen content. Galland (#191) lists it among adjunctive therapies that may help mitochondrial function in LC, particularly to address oxidative stress without the risks of hyperbaric oxygen or ozone.

Evidence base for hydrogen water in LC is small; Galland positions it alongside red light therapy at specific frequencies as part of a careful mitochondrial-support stack.
