# EFT Meridian Tapping

A self-administered technique combining **tapping on acupressure / meridian points** with **verbal acknowledgement of the negative emotion or symptom**. Developed by Roger Callahan (1960s) and simplified into the modern EFT format by Gary Craig. "EFT" = Emotional Freedom Technique.

The protocol: rate the negative emotion / symptom 0-10, tap on each meridian point (side of hand, inner eyebrow, outer eye, under eye, under nose, under mouth, collar bone, under arm, top of head) while saying the negative content out loud, then re-rate. Repeat until 0-1.

## Why it might work in LC

- **Vagal / parasympathetic stimulation.** Tapping at the listed points (especially the chest / collar-bone / under-arm spots near the vagus nerve) likely engages the same nervous-system pathways that breathwork, [[hrv-biofeedback]], and singing engage.
- **Acknowledgement-first model.** Distinct from brain-retraining; doesn't try to convince the mind "you're fine" when it isn't. Acknowledges what's felt → amygdala registers safety → the felt-sense shifts naturally.
- **Self-administered, scalable, accessible.** No practitioner required after learning; can be done lying down; can be done by *imagining* the tapping if physical movement causes PEM.

## Cautions

- Severe PTSD: work with a trained EFT practitioner. Tapping can surface trauma material that becomes overwhelming alone.
- Don't drive while tapping until experienced (then it can be done one-handed, but most novices won't).
- Possible side effects: yawning, burping, drowsiness, occasionally a feeling of being more stirred up before settling (release effect, not a sign to stop).

## Recommended dose

Lorrie recommends 2× daily, daily, ideally for the duration of the work; less frequent (every other day) for highly sensitive nervous systems.
