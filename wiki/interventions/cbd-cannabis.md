# CBD / Medical Cannabis

Cannabis (THC + CBD) and CBD-only preparations act on the endocannabinoid system, which has receptors in both the immune and nervous systems. Mechanism: simultaneous calming of immune activation **and** nervous-system sensitization — making it conceptually well-matched to nociplastic pain, MCAS, and autonomic dysregulation.

**UK status**: not on the NHS for LC. Available privately or via medical cannabis clinics. Some patients self-source; legal exposure varies.
