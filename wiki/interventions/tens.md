# TENS (Transcutaneous Electrical Nerve Stimulation)

A small, battery-powered device delivering low-voltage electrical pulses through skin electrodes. Mechanism (debated): gate-control modulation of pain at the spinal cord, plus likely autonomic-system effects (parasympathetic engagement). Cheap, non-pharmacological, low-harm.

Distinct from **vagus-nerve TENS** ([[vagus-nerve-tens]]), which targets the auricular branch of the vagus specifically for autonomic modulation.
