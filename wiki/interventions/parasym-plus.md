# Parasym Plus

Acetylcholine-supporting supplement designed by **Dr Diana Driscoll** (US optometrist, founder of POTS Care, see ep #173) and sold via **vagusnervesupport.com**. Marketed as a non-prescription way to support the parasympathetic / vagal arm of the autonomic nervous system, with five US patents covering its formulation.

## Mechanism (per Driscoll)

Driscoll's working theory: a subset of POTS / dysautonomia / LC patients have an **acetylcholine deficiency** at parasympathetic nerve endings — the receptors themselves work (proven via nicotine-patch testing in her studies), but production / availability of the neurotransmitter is impaired by genetic variants and malabsorption. Parasym Plus supplies cofactors and precursors that:

- Cross the blood-brain barrier
- Bypass common genetic bottlenecks in acetylcholine synthesis
- Compensate for malabsorption from impaired gut motility
- Trigger vagal-nerve activity within ~45 min (target: bowel movement in patients with gastroparesis-style severe constipation)

## Reported effects

- Resolution of gastroparesis / pseudo-obstruction
- Heart-rate normalisation (alongside other measures)
- Pupil normalisation (visible in mirror within ~2 days, used as patient-side objective marker)
- Improved sleep
- Reduced brain fog (acetylcholine in cognitive circuits)

## Caveats

- This is a single-source / single-clinician intervention. The peer-reviewed evidence base is limited; what exists is mostly Driscoll's own work + patient case reports.
- Sold direct-to-consumer; not a prescription product.
- Driscoll's framing is that it must be paired with treating high intracranial pressure and oxidation (NAC Max) — she explicitly rejects mono-supplementation.
