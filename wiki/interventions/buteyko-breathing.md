# Buteyko Breathing

A nasal-breathing, low-volume breathing method developed by Soviet-era physician **Konstantin Buteyko**. Core principles:
- **Nose breathing only** (close the mouth)
- **Reduce breath volume** — counter the modern tendency to over-breathe
- **Build CO₂ tolerance** — measured via the **BOLT score** (breath-hold-after-exhale)
- **Slow + light + low** — minute volume reduction across many sessions

Modern lineage: **Patrick McKeown** (oxygenadvantage.com) is the most-cited current teacher in the LC space; his work is referenced across multiple podcast episodes.

## How it sits next to "breathwork" in general

Treat this page as the **named-protocol** version of the broader [[breathwork]] page. Buteyko is one specific school — others include yoga pranayama, physio-style diaphragmatic retraining, paced exhalation, etc. Buteyko's specific contribution is the **CO₂-tolerance + reduced-volume** axis.

## Cautions raised on the podcast

- **High-counting exercises (e.g. 7-in-5-out) are too hard for most LC patients to start.** Ep #29 Lorna explicitly warns against starting there.
- Nose-breathing during exercise can produce air-hunger panic at first — usually CO₂ tolerance has dropped, not actual oxygen shortage. Build slowly.
- Build seated first; only add to walking later; only add to higher-load activity when comfortable.
