# Triptans (Imitrex family) — for Acute Migraine

5HT-1 agonists (sumatriptan, rizatriptan, etc.) for **acute migraine abortion**. Corpus picture is mixed-to-negative for LC headache *unless* the headache is genuinely migrainous AND the dose is taken early enough.

## Teitelbaum's protocol (LCP #167)

The pacing detail is critical and the most actionable piece in the corpus:

- Take the triptan **before the pain wraps around the eyes/ears**.
- Once you're at the eye/ear-tenderness threshold, the stomach has shut off — oral medication won't absorb.
- A shot of **strong coffee with the triptan** can sometimes re-open absorption.
- "Better to hit it earlier instead of toughing it out and waiting."

Pair acute triptans with **prophylactic [[vitamin-b2-riboflavin]] 400 mg/day × 6 weeks + [[magnesium]] + food-sensitivity management** to reduce frequency.

## Where they fail in LC (corpus pattern)

The corpus has two cautionary stories of triptans (or migraine drugs broadly) being unhelpful — both involve the same underlying issue: **the headache wasn't actually migraine.**

- **[[chiara-berardelli]] (LCP #3)**: misdiagnosed by a neurologist as "chronic menopausal migraine" because she was "a woman of a certain age." Offered migraine tablets; declined and didn't take them — she knew the headache wasn't migrainous, it was post-viral. Headaches improved markedly within weeks of stopping all exercise (PEM-aetiology). Triptans would not have addressed the mechanism.
- **Devon Carter (RA #984)**: ER drugged her up with "a bunch of migraine medication" on her first crash; "definitely mellowed me out — that's what it's for." But no follow-up, no diagnosis; this was LC presenting as a sudden-onset crisis, not a migraine.

The framing implication: if you're using triptans for an LC headache and they're not working, *that may be diagnostic*. Most LC headache is [[nociplastic-pain|nociplastic]] (Ravindran #53) — central sensitisation — and triptans don't target that mechanism. Move to **prophylactic [[vitamin-b2-riboflavin]] + [[magnesium]]**, nervous-system work ([[brain-training]] / [[somatic-tracking]]), and rule out other causes (medication side effects, thyroid, structural).

## Cautions

- **Cardiac contraindications** — coronary disease, uncontrolled hypertension; the 5HT-1 agonism is vasoconstrictive.
- **Serotonin syndrome risk** when combined with SSRIs/SNRIs (debated clinically; flag with prescriber).
- **Rebound headache (medication-overuse headache)** with frequent use — a real concern in the LC subset who try to "push through" with daily triptans.
- **Don't keep escalating doses** in LC if early-dose protocol isn't helping after a few months — the headache may not be migrainous.
