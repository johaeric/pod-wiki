Journaling — variously as expressive writing, symptom-tracking, recovery diary, or "body of proof" log — is a recurring tool across LC recovery frameworks. Different sub-types serve different functions:

- **Expressive writing** — fast, unedited, used to access feelings and bypass analytical brain (Nora Rodden, ep #211)
- **Activity + symptom journal** — repeat at intake then monthly; becomes anchor against selective amnesia of progress (Dan Neuffer, ep #198)
- **Setback diary / "body of proof"** — record progress when going well so you can return to it during dips
- **Boundary / emotional processing** — write about difficult interactions to discharge emotional load

## Why it works

- Externalises rumination
- Creates a record that bypasses the brain's tendency to forget gains during setbacks
- Fast handwriting can override the analytical brain's filter (Nora Rodden's specific technique)
