MOTS-c is a mitochondrial-encoded peptide investigated for mitochondrial-dysfunction-related conditions including type 2 diabetes, exercise capacity, and aging. Used off-label in some integrative-medicine practices for chronic-fatigue and post-viral syndromes. Typically administered as a weekly subcutaneous injection.

## Why it matters in LC

- Mitochondrial dysfunction is a core LC mechanism (see [[mitochondrial-dysfunction]])
- Targets the underlying energy-production deficit at the mitochondrial level
- Distinct from oral mitochondrial supplements (CoQ10, NR, ubiquinol, etc.) — bypasses gut absorption and the cell-uptake bottleneck

## Cautions

- Off-label, prescription-required, not FDA-approved for LC
- Limited human trial data
- Dose, duration, and source/quality of compounded peptide all matter
- Not the same as the [[peptides-thymosin]] family (thymosin α-1, β-4) which target the immune system
