# Famciclovir + Celebrex (EBV reactivation protocol)

A **6-month combination protocol** designed to suppress reactivated **Epstein-Barr virus (EBV)** in post-viral CFS / LC populations. Cited by [[jacob-teitelbaum]] as standard in his clinic.

## Components and dose

- **Famciclovir** 500–750 mg three times daily (TDS)
- **Celebrex** (celecoxib, COX-2 inhibitor NSAID) 200 mg twice daily with food
- Duration: 6 months
- Improvement typically visible by month 4

## Mechanism story

- Reactivated EBV is reported in CFS and now post-COVID populations
- Famciclovir is a guanosine-analogue antiviral with EBV activity
- Celebrex is added empirically; mechanism story is anti-inflammatory + possible direct viral effect

## Caveats

- Off-label use of two drugs together.
- Famciclovir long-term not without risk; needs medical supervision.
- COX-2 inhibitors carry cardiovascular risk warnings (especially with prior CV disease).
- Not part of any RCT for LC.
- Cross-ref: see also [[viral-persistence]] for the broader debate about whether antivirals help LC.
