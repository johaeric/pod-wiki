# Healing Codes / Trilogy (Alex Lloyd)

A self-administered energy-medicine method developed by **Alex Lloyd**. The "Healing Codes" component uses specific hand positions held over named "healing centres" on the body. **Trilogy** is Lloyd's combined protocol that adds [[eft-tapping|EFT meridian tapping]] and chakra-style energy-centre work to the Healing Codes — three modalities in roughly three minutes per cycle.

The practitioner-credited mechanism is **rapid sympathetic-to-parasympathetic shift** via vagal/autonomic engagement. Lloyd's organisation has self-published HRV / heart-rate-improvement studies; these are *not* peer-reviewed independent trials and are uncross-checked on this wiki.

> Wiki position: **logged because a recovered patient names it as part of her recovery practice**, not because the underlying mechanism claims are validated. Sits in the same evidence territory as [[eft-tapping]] — plausibly vagal-engaging, plausibly mostly placebo / down-regulation effect, hard to distinguish from any other parasympathetic practice.
