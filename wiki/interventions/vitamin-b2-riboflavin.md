# Vitamin B2 (Riboflavin)

**For LC migraine specifically** — high-dose riboflavin (400 mg/day) is the corpus's anchor specific-protocol for migraine-frequency reduction in long covid. Cheap, OTC, decades of non-LC migraine evidence outside this corpus.

> "Vitamin B2 riboflavin 400 milligrams a day for 6 weeks in repeated studies decreases migraine frequency by 67 to 70%. Magnesium will also decrease [it]." — [[jacob-teitelbaum]], LCP #167

## Protocol (Teitelbaum, LCP #167)

- **400 mg/day for at least 6 weeks** before judging effect
- Pair with [[magnesium]]
- For migraine-trigger management, also address **food sensitivities**
- For acute migraine, **[[triptans-imitrex]] taken early** (before pain reaches the eye/ear-tenderness threshold — after that, the stomach has shut off and oral absorption fails)

## Why this dose

Riboflavin's migraine evidence base (outside LC) is mitochondrial — riboflavin is a precursor to FAD/FMN, cofactors for mitochondrial electron-transport-chain complexes. The "migraine brain" has documented mitochondrial-energy-deficit signatures; high-dose B2 supplementation is one of the longest-standing non-pharmacological migraine prophylactics in neurology.

This dovetails with Teitelbaum's broader **hypothalamic-circuit-breaker / mitochondrial-energy-shutdown** model of LC — the same mechanism that produces fatigue + PEM may also produce the migraine-prone state. B2 is a low-cost lever on the underlying biology, not just symptom relief.

## Cost / access

- OTC essentially everywhere.
- Brand-agnostic at this dose; "riboflavin 400 mg" is a recognised migraine-prophylaxis SKU.
- iherb has multiple options; Teitelbaum points to iherb as his preferred international supplier in this episode.

## Cautions

- Bright yellow urine — harmless, expected, but startling on first dose.
- Genuinely contained: water-soluble, no significant toxicity at this dose.
- Allow the full 6 weeks before judging — onset is gradual, not acute relief.
