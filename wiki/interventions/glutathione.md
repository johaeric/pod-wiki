# Glutathione (oral / liposomal / IV)

The body's master intracellular antioxidant — a tripeptide (glutamate, cysteine, glycine) that neutralises reactive oxygen species and supports phase-II liver detoxification. Comes up across the corpus wherever oxidative stress, mitochondrial dysfunction, mast-cell load, or mold/heavy-metal detox is discussed. The signal is **clinician-dominated `recommended`**, with one notable patient `harmed` row.

## Forms (corpus mentions)

- **Oral glutathione (generic supplement)** — [[jacob-teitelbaum]] (#55), [[sarah-myhill]] (#118)
- **IV glutathione** — RA #546 (Jamie) — given alongside IV vitamin C; Jamie reports it "made me worse." Single data point but salient.
- **Recycled-glutathione product (NAC Max)** — [[diana-driscoll]] (#173) — Driscoll's branded product designed to both increase synthesis *and recycle* existing glutathione. See `wiki/interventions/nac-max.md` for the dedicated page; that page is the `key_to_recovery` row for Driscoll's own arc, not duplicated here.
- **Implicit dietary / endogenous synthesis** — [[robert-groysman]] (#187), [[taylor-krick]] (#106), [[kendal-stewart]] (#185) all reference glutathione mechanistically without prescribing a specific form.

## Why this comes up so often

Five distinct framings:

1. **Mitochondrial oxidative-stress scavenger** — [[robert-groysman]] (#187): "The top one that everybody knows about is glutathione. It's a scavenger. It tends to reduce these oxidants chemically." Stores deplete under PEM-level oxidative load.
2. **Low glutathione as a LC risk marker** — [[taylor-krick]] (#106): "low glutathione is associated with long COVID." Mold toxins deplete glutathione — relevant to the mold/mast-cell/LC overlap.
3. **Detox of dead infections and heavy metals** — [[sarah-myhill]] (#118), [[jacob-teitelbaum]] (#55): supports phase-II detox; remove dying-pathogen byproducts; chelate heavy metals.
4. **Recycling failure in chronic inflammation** — [[diana-driscoll]] (#173): inflammation → oxidation → glutathione recycling collapse. Designed NAC Max to address that specifically; carried the bottle around in her own recovery.
5. **Methylation / detox pathway lever** — [[kendal-stewart]] (#185): part of the "methane glutathione system" he assesses for individualised LC protocols.

## Dosing (across corpus)

- **Form and dose largely unspecified** — most clinicians mention glutathione as a target/pathway rather than prescribing a discrete dose.
- **IV glutathione + IV vitamin C** — RA #546 — Jamie's holistic doctor's protocol; Jamie reports negative outcome.
- **NAC Max** — see [[nac-max]] for Driscoll's recycle-focused product.

## Use cases

### Mitochondrial / oxidative-stress defense (PEM-adjacent)
[[robert-groysman]] (#187) — frames glutathione as the body's primary mitochondrial oxidative-stress scavenger. PEM-level exertion depletes stores and is "not harmless" because mitochondrial damage and inflammatory cascade follow.

### Mast-cell / mold-overlap LC
[[taylor-krick]] (#106) — "many different pieces of mechanisms in the literature associated with low glutathione and long COVID, and how mold toxins deplete glutathione." Treats the mold/MCAS/LC triad as one biochemical context.

### Detox (dead-pathogen, heavy-metal, environmental)
[[sarah-myhill]] (#118) — pairs glutathione with minerals and clays (diatomaceous earth, chelating agents) to remove heavy metals (aluminium, dental amalgam, lead, vaccine adjuvants). [[jacob-teitelbaum]] (#55) — explicitly warns that detox supplements like glutathione *can make you feel worse* as they pull pathogens/toxins out: "anything that you take that leaves you feeling worse, your body either wants a lower dose or it wants less."

### Inflammatory-POTS recycling collapse (Driscoll model)
[[diana-driscoll]] (#173) — see [[nac-max]]. Driscoll's recovery arc puts recycling — not synthesis — at the centre. NAC Max is her productised answer.

### Post-COVID neuroinflammation
[[jacob-teitelbaum]] (#55) — paired with high-absorption curcumin and boswellia for post-ARDS / post-pneumonia lung healing, leveraging glutathione's free-radical-quenching role.

## Cautions (from the corpus)

- **Detox die-off** — [[jacob-teitelbaum]] (#55) — "things that detox you like glutathione… anything you take that leaves you feeling worse, your body wants a lower dose." Real risk in LC where pacing margin is thin.
- **IV glutathione can backfire** — RA #546 — Jamie's clear "didn't do anything, in fact I think they made me worse" row. Mechanism unspecified — could be detox die-off, could be mast-cell flare, could be excipient.
- **Mold context matters** — [[taylor-krick]] (#106) — if low glutathione is from active mold exposure, supplementing without removing the source is symptomatic management.
- **Form variability** — bioavailability differs hugely (oral vs liposomal vs IV vs precursor-based via NAC). Corpus doesn't compare directly.

## Cross-ref: NAC and NAC Max

[[diana-driscoll]] (#173) created **[[nac-max]]** specifically because, in her inflammatory-POTS model, glutathione *recycling* fails in chronic inflammation — synthesis-side support (i.e., generic NAC) isn't enough. NAC Max claims to both increase glutathione synthesis *and* recycle existing stores. See the [[nac-max]] page for her `key_to_recovery` row — it isn't duplicated here because it's a branded product with its own evidence layer.
