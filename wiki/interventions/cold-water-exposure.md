# Cold-Water Exposure

Brief deliberate exposure to cold water — wild swimming, cold showers, cold face splash, ice baths. Proposed mechanisms relevant to LC:
- **Forces breath control** under acute stress → trains nervous-system flexibility
- Vagal afferent stimulation via the diving reflex (cold water on the face)
- Acute sympathetic spike → parasympathetic rebound after the stressor ends → "trains" the nervous system to move between states more flexibly
- Anti-inflammatory cytokine response in some studies

Cross-ref: see [[wild-swimming]] for the broader picture (mind-body, social, outdoor-exposure layers).

⚠️ **Safety caveats** (per Lorna ep #29):
- Don't start in winter — start in summer
- Don't go alone in open water — find a guide / group
- **Safest entry point: cold face splash or cold shower** (no swimming required)
