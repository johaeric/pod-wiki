# Oxaloacetate

A Krebs-cycle intermediate. Sold as a supplement (most commonly under brand names like benaGene). Used in elite-athlete and chronic-fatigue contexts to support **mitochondrial efficiency** and **clear reactive oxygen species** generated as energy-production waste.

## Why for LC

Putrino (#181) describes it as part of an interventional cluster — alongside CoQ10, NAD+, low-dose rapamycin, metformin, HBOT — that has been used for decades in elite-athlete settings to:

- Make mitochondria more **resilient to over-exertion**
- Make mitochondria more **efficient**
- **Eat up reactive oxygen species** (oxidative stress)

If LC has features of accelerated cellular aging and mitochondrial dysfunction (a hypothesis Putrino takes seriously), these athlete-derived tools become candidate interventions for subsets.

## Caveats

- Off-label for LC; no LC-specific RCT.
- Single anecdotal recommendation row.
- Generally considered well-tolerated, but fatigue-illness patients can react atypically to anything new — start low.
