# HRG80 Red Ginseng

A specific high-quality preparation of Korean red ginseng — Teitelbaum claims most cheap-ginseng products are "stale" and not bioactive, while HRG80 retains the wild-ginseng active compounds.

Standard form (per Teitelbaum): **chewable tablets**, 100mg chewable as effective as 400mg capsules.
