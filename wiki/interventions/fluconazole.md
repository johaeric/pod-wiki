# Fluconazole (candida overgrowth protocol)

**6-week course of oral fluconazole 200 mg/day** to treat presumed candida overgrowth in post-viral CFS / LC. Used in integrative-medicine practice; not standard NHS care.

## Indication (per [[jacob-teitelbaum]] #55)

Presumptive candida overgrowth diagnosis based on symptoms (no reliable test):
- Nasal congestion, sinusitis
- Gas, bloating, diarrhea
- Post-nasal drip
- Sugar cravings

Combined with:
- Probiotic
- Sugar restriction (starve the candida)

## Caveats

- Candida-overgrowth-as-systemic-illness is **contested** in mainstream medicine; integrative-medicine consensus differs from mainstream gastroenterology.
- Fluconazole has hepatic toxicity in chronic use; LFT monitoring needed.
- Drug interactions are extensive (CYP3A4 inhibition).
- Requires holistic / integrative physician — most NHS GPs will not prescribe.
