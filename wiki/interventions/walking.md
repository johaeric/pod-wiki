# Walking

Daily low-intensity walking — sometimes literally five minutes around the block — appears across the podcast as the lowest-bar form of movement most LC patients can attempt. **Distinct from "exercise"** in the framing: it is movement for vagal/circulatory/lymphatic/cognitive benefit, not training stimulus.

A theme through the podcast — Wibbs Coulson (#17) explicitly contrasts movement-vs-exercise; Cass MacDonald (#19) tracks fitness-for-work via sit-up time at a sewing machine, but acknowledges walking as the baseline; **Amy Anker (#20) credits daily walking as one of four pillars in her full recovery**.

⚠️ **For PEM-prone patients**: walking is *not* universally safe. Push past your envelope and you'll PEM. The walking-as-recovery framing assumes a patient whose physiology can tolerate low-intensity movement without crashing. The Kelly Mitchell / Wibbs Coulson view is that breathing must be addressed first if hyperventilation-driven dysautonomia is in play.
