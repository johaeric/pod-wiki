# Diaphragmatic Breathing

Slow, conscious nasal in / nasal out breathing using the diaphragm rather than the chest. Hand on belly + hand on chest as a feedback loop — belly should expand on the in-breath, contract on the out.

**Why a cardiologist endorses it heavily**: the lung is the only major autonomic-controlled organ that is **also under voluntary control**. So conscious breath is the leverage point for autonomic reset.

## Boon Lim's "breath stress reset" (#40)

- 2 minutes, every hour where possible
- Slow nasal in / nasal out
- Belly out on inhale; "imagine your belly button touching your spine" on exhale
- Pair with **gratitude / compassion / forgiveness** as the emotional anchor — not optional, materially affects the autonomic outcome
- Take your radial pulse during the practice — feel the HR drop

> "It's better than any drug, and it's in everyone's reach right now." — Boon Lim (#40)

## Practical caveats

- In a severe crash, breath-work can be too cognitively expensive (Khan #37) — fall back to passive vagus-nerve TENS or vagus-nerve exercises in those moments and return to breath when capacity returns.
- Mouth-breathing, breath-holding, sighing are common dysfunctional breathing patterns to **self-monitor** — Lim emphasises this in his clinic and self-practice.
