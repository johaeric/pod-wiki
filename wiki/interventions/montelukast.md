# Montelukast

Leukotriene D4 receptor antagonist. Approved for asthma + allergic rhinitis. In LC: relevant as an MCAS-cytokine modulator (leukotrienes are mast-cell mediators).

> ⚠️ **~30% of patients develop depression on montelukast** (per Peers). Monitor and discontinue if mood drops.
