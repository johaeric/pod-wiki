# Low-dose Aripiprazole (Abilify)

Aripiprazole is a dopamine partial agonist atypical antipsychotic. **Standard psychiatric dose is 15–30 mg.** The intervention here is **a tiny fraction of that — 0.25 mg** (one-sixtieth of the antipsychotic dose) — for severe near-bedbound CFS / LC.

Origin: Stanford University research (Bonilla et al.), summarised in coverage by Cort Johnson at Health Rising. Mechanism (proposed): dopamine modulation of microglial inflammation; partial agonism at low dose may reduce neuroinflammation without the antipsychotic side-effect burden.

Practical: **liquid formulation needed** — tablets (typically 5–30mg) cannot be reliably dosed at 0.25mg.

## Caveats

- One Stanford lab's signal, not a settled finding.
- Off-label.
- Standard antipsychotic Abilify dose is grossly inappropriate — must be 0.25mg.
- Possible side effects even at low dose; specialist supervision indicated.
