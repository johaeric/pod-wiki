Old (1972) drug originally for influenza prophylaxis and Parkinson's disease, derived from star anise. Acts as an NMDA glutamate-receptor blocker; "slows the brain down" by reducing glutamate-driven over-stimulation.

## Stewart's clinical use

Microglial activation releases glutamate (a stimulant), keeping the inflamed brain in a racing, resource-burning state where it can't rest or repair. Amantadine reduces this glutamate signal so the brain can recover even after the upstream immune trigger is addressed.

> "Even if you cleared the immune challenge, if the brain's in this racing method... good luck getting rid of the cognitive problems. The causation may be gone but the brain still needs to heal."
> — Dr Kendal Stewart, ep #185
