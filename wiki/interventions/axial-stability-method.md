# Axial Stability Method (ASM)

A **low-force chiropractic protocol** developed by [[lydia-knutson]] over 23+ years of practice in Cambridge MA. Combines established low-force chiropractic techniques with **Eastern Energy Medicine** (acupuncture meridians + chakras). Taught to chiropractors via a six-day course. Knutson adapted it into an LC-specific protocol in summer 2020.

## What it is

- **No spinal manipulation** (not a "crack-and-pop" chiropractic). Gentle sequence of touch on cranium, upper cervical spine, thoracic spine, and chest.
- **Three phases**:
  - **Phase 1 (~8 visits)** — work on parasympathetic division: upper-cervical / vagal-exit-point sensory-motor inputs. Knutson's published series saw ~70% reduction in severe-symptom severity at this point alone.
  - **Phase 2** — work on sympathetic division: thoracic-spine "concordances" (specific levels mapped to organ systems, named via lineage from acupuncture + 19th-century osteopathy + various 20th-century chiropractic systems). T1+T2 = heart; T3 = lungs/respiration; T7 = spleen/immune; etc. Knutson calls this "tonifying" the sympathetic chain — giving it degrees of freedom rather than disabling it.
  - **Phase 3** — biomechanical / muscular-skeletal rehab. Old neuromuscular injuries flagged in long-history-taking are addressed here.
- ~3-hour intake (history back to childhood, all illnesses, injuries, life events). Benchmark goals tracked across visits.
- Average treatment course in Knutson's published series: **~18 visits** for ~90% reduction in severe-symptom severity.

## Mechanism (Knutson's framing)

- LC is fundamentally **autonomic dysregulation**. ASM rebalances sympathetic + parasympathetic by addressing both spinal-anatomy substrates.
- **Hierarchy of systems**: every body wants to restore order in the same sequence — parasympathetic first, sympathetic second, then biomechanical. This is universal across patients even though presentations vary.
- Once the ANS rebalances, the immune system + hormonal system + sensory-motor system follow. "We're not whack-a-moling individual symptoms — we're working upstream."

## Published outcomes (Knutson's two open-label series)

- **n=13 LC patients, 40 symptoms tracked, 171 symptom-instances reported.**
- **67 symptoms rated severe (5–10/10) at baseline.**
- **Average 18 visits → 90% reduction in severe-symptom severity.**
- **Fatigue scoring**: dropped from 80th percentile of US-population norm to 32nd percentile (less tired than the average American at end of treatment).
- Heart-rate-variability data collected as objective ANS-dysfunction marker (specifics not detailed in episode).
- Funded by grateful patients. Open-label / no control arm. Published by Knutson + Stephanie Sullivan (Director of Chiropractic Research at Life University, near Atlanta GA). Next step: controlled trial.

## Caveats

- **Practitioner availability is limited** — Knutson runs the only multi-doctor ASM clinic and teaches the method via her six-day chiropractor course. Geographic access is a real barrier.
- **Cost** — multi-visit private chiropractic. Estimate $$$$.
- **Open-label outcomes**: the n=13 study has no sham control. Effect sizes are large enough to motivate a controlled trial, but this is not the same evidence grade as Hadanny's HBOT RCT ([[153-dr-amir-hadanny-hbot]]).
