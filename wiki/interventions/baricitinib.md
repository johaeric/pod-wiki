# Baricitinib

JAK1/JAK2 inhibitor used for rheumatoid arthritis and other autoimmune conditions. **Already FDA/EMA-approved for acute covid** (lifesaving in severe disease — see [[wes-ely]]'s COV-BARRIER trial).

William Li (#73) flagged it as a candidate to investigate for the autoimmune leg of LC. Wes Ely (#131) is now PI of the [[rvlc-trial]] — a 550-patient placebo-controlled, double-blind RCT of baricitinib 4 mg PO daily × 6 months in established LC, NIH-funded.

Caveat: powerful, expensive, requires clinician supervision. Not OTC. EBV reactivation is the main monitoring concern at long durations.
