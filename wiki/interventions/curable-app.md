# Curable App

A smartphone app (~$15) developed with a team of neuroscientists, marketed primarily to **chronic pain** patients. Educates users on the neuroscience of pain (Moseley-style explain-pain framing) and provides **in-the-moment exercises** for reframing fear / catastrophising during symptom flares.

Although marketed for chronic pain, also commonly used by chronic-fatigue, fibromyalgia, and LC populations as the **fear-and-symptom-amplification reframing layer** that complements brain-retraining programs like [[ans-rewire]] / [[gupta-program]].

## Mechanism story

Connects to [[nociplastic-pain]] / central sensitization theory — the intensity of symptoms is amplified by fear about those symptoms. Reducing the fear loop reduces the symptom amplitude.
