# Metformin (acute-phase for LC prevention)

Cheap, decades-old, well-tolerated diabetes drug. **In a clinical trial (Bramante et al.) given during acute covid, it reduced subsequent long-covid incidence by more than 30%.**

This is a **prevention** intervention — not a treatment for established LC. Useful framing: if Eric (or anyone with prior LC) catches covid again, metformin during the acute phase is a candidate to discuss with the clinician.

## Why it might work

Hypothesised mechanisms span all three of Li's legs (#73):
- AMPK pathway → reduced viral replication
- Anti-inflammatory effect
- Reduces vascular injury
