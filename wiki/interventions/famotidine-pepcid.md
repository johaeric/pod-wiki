# Famotidine (Pepcid) and Cimetidine (Tagamet)

**H2 receptor antagonists** — the older generation of acid-reducers, distinct from the newer PPIs (omeprazole, lansoprazole, esomeprazole). Mechanism in LC context goes well beyond stomach acid:

- **MCAS coverage** — H2 antihistamine action complements H1 (Zyrtec/cetirizine etc.), forming the standard MCAS pair
- **Immune support** — particularly against EBV reactivation
- Lower adverse-event burden than PPIs in long-term use (Teitelbaum cites studies showing 44% increased dementia risk and 25% increased mortality with chronic PPI use — *to be cross-checked against ercipedia*)

Standard dose for LC/MCAS: **40 mg twice daily.**
