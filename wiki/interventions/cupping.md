# Cupping

Suction-based East Asian medical technique. Cups are placed on the skin and create negative pressure, drawing biological debris from underlying tissue and bloodstream toward the surface — the characteristic purple-red marks. Used in LC for the same reason as [[gua-sha]]: clearing chronic-inflammation debris and supporting circulation.
