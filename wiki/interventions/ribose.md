# D-Ribose

A simple sugar (5-carbon pentose) that is a structural component of ATP, NADH, and FADH2 — the body's energy currencies. Theory: in mitochondrially-impaired states, supplementing ribose accelerates re-synthesis of these molecules and restores cellular energy.

Standard dose (per [[jacob-teitelbaum]]): **5g, 2–3×/day**, powdered form (it tastes like sugar; tablets impractical because of dose volume).
