# Rapamycin (low-dose)

Originally an immunosuppressant (transplant medicine). At **low doses** it is used off-label in longevity science as an **mTOR inhibitor**, with reported effects on:
- Cellular efficiency at energy generation
- Immune-system priming against pathogens
- Slowing markers of cellular aging

## Why on this podcast

Putrino (#181) introduces the framing that **LC may be partly an illness of accelerated cellular aging**. If cells age faster, then interventions originally developed for the aging population may help LC subsets. Anecdotal community signal: "rapamycin has been helpful for my friend down the road" (Putrino's words, not a recommendation).

Sits alongside **metformin**, **NAD+**, **CoQ10**, **oxaloacetate**, **HBOT** in this same conceptual bucket — interventions originally tested in elite-athlete or longevity contexts that may help LC patients with mitochondrial / cellular-aging features.

## Caveats

- Strongly off-label for LC. No published RCT in LC.
- Immunosuppressive at higher doses; even low-dose use should be supervised.
- Single anecdotal recommendation row at this point — wiki position is *watch this*, not *try this*.
