# Midodrine

α1-adrenergic agonist; oral vasoconstrictor used for orthostatic hypotension and as a second-line POTS agent. Increases peripheral vascular resistance → raises BP → reduces orthostatic symptoms.

> Cross-ref: see `ercipedia/wiki/interventions/midodrine.md` for trial-evidence picture.

## Practical considerations (per Boon Lim #40)

- Used when blood pressure runs persistently low (<100/70) and standard non-drug strategies are insufficient.
- Often gateway to better engagement with non-drug protocol (more energy → more upright time → less deconditioning).
- Standard side effects: scalp tingling, urinary retention, supine hypertension; avoid taking near bedtime.
