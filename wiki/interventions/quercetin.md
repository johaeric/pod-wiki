# Quercetin

A natural flavonoid antioxidant found in fruits, vegetables, and flowers. **OTC supplement** widely used as a mast-cell stabiliser in MCAS protocols. Often **combined with bromelain** in commercial formulations to improve absorption.

## Practical considerations (per Saperstein #68)

- Typical dose: **250–500 mg**, sometimes higher.
- **Caveat**: can interact with thyroid medications and others — check with healthcare provider before adding to existing prescription stack.
- Generally well-tolerated.
- "Mast-cell stabiliser" mechanism overlaps with cromolyn / ketotifen but supplement-grade and OTC, so usable as an entry-level MCAS intervention.
