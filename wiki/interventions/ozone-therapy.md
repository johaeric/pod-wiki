# Ozone Therapy

Medical ozone (O₃) administered via various routes (autohemotherapy, rectal insufflation, ozonated water/oil) — used in integrative medicine for proposed antiviral, anti-inflammatory and oxidative-stress modulation effects. Outside Germany / Russia / Cuba / Mexico, mostly outside the medical mainstream.

## Anchor

[[096-dr-masha-makeeva-hbot]] flags ozone therapy as a useful intervention for two LC sub-pictures:
- **Viral persistence** — patients still testing positive or with detectable viral particles.
- **Lung fibrosis / scar tissue** from severe acute COVID — patients whose oxygen saturation hasn't returned to baseline + ongoing breathlessness on exertion.

## Caveats

- **Limited evidence base in LC**; most ozone-LC literature is integrative-medicine practitioner reports.
- **Route + dose vary widely** — protocols not standardised.
- **Not legal/standardised in many jurisdictions** — practitioners may be operating outside conventional regulation.
- **Clinician-only recommended** — not a self-experiment intervention.
