# AXA1125

A drug candidate from **Axcella Therapeutics**. Class: endogenous metabolic modulator (EMM) — a **6-amino-acid** oral composition. Multi-target mechanism: fatty-acid oxidation, cellular respiration / ATP, inflammation, immune modulation. Already in Phase 2 for non-alcoholic steatohepatitis (NASH); the same biology rationale carries into LC.

## Phase 2a readout (announced #52)

The placebo-controlled Oxford trial (n≈42, 28 days) reported in mid-2022:

- **~70% of the AXA1125 arm had clinically significant improvement in physical fatigue**, vs essentially no one in placebo.
- **3 of 21** in the active arm reported being back to their pre-COVID baseline.
- Most others moved from moderate-to-severe → mild physical fatigue.
- Both physical and mental fatigue improved.
- Six-minute walk distance improved alongside fatigue.
- Mitochondrial function tests at baseline were markedly abnormal — corroborating biological mechanism.

This is the **strongest placebo-controlled efficacy signal for any fatigue-targeted LC therapy** indexed in this wiki to date.

> Cross-ref: see [[axa1125-oxford]] for the trial details. See `ercipedia/wiki/interventions/axa1125.md` for trial evidence handling.

## Caveats

- N≈42, 28 days — early-phase, short, small.
- No PEM-specific outcome used. Future trials need PEM-relevant tools (sustained activity, return-to-work).
- Not yet regulator-approved; next phase in design with FDA + MHRA.
