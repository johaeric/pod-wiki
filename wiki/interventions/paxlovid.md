# Paxlovid (nirmatrelvir + ritonavir)

Pfizer's oral antiviral combination for SARS-CoV-2:
- **Nirmatrelvir** — Mpro protease inhibitor; blocks viral replication.
- **Ritonavir** — pharmacokinetic potentiator (also causes the classic metallic taste).

Initially under FDA EUA; full FDA approval as of 2023. Standard acute-COVID dose: 5 days, three pills twice daily. Reduces hospitalisation/death significantly in unvaccinated; benefit in vaccinated is debated.

## In Long Covid

The wiki anchor is [[097-harlan-krumholz-listen-paxlovid]]:
- **Hypothesis**: persistent virus drives LC for some sub-cohorts ([[viral-persistence]]); longer Paxlovid course should clear residual virus and improve symptoms.
- **Trial**: [[paxlovid-lc-trial]] — 15-day extended-dose RCT (vs ritonavir-only placebo to preserve blinding) at Yale; N=100 with deep immune phenotyping.
- **Why 15 days**: 5-day standard rebound is common; 10-day pilot data; 15-day balances safety and clearance.

## Caveats

- **Acute-COVID benefit is established; LC benefit is hypothesis-stage** — pre-RCT.
- **Drug interactions** — ritonavir interacts with many drugs (statins, hormonal contraception, calcium-channel blockers). Specialist review needed.
- **Metallic taste** is the major nuisance side-effect; tolerable at 5 days, less clear at 15.
- **Not a routine LC prescription** — currently trial-only or off-label clinician-by-clinician.
