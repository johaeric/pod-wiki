Nicotinamide riboside (NR) is a form of vitamin B3 that is the most direct oral precursor to NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) — a co-enzyme central to mitochondrial energy production. NMN, niacin, nicotinamide, and oral NAD+ all have less effective routes into cells. Marketed primarily as **Niagen / TrueNiagen** (Niagen Biosciences).

## Why it matters in LC

- Severe acute COVID is associated with NAD+ depletion (PARP enzyme activation defending the genome consumes NAD+)
- LC NAD+ depletion is *assumed* by extension but not directly confirmed
- NAD+ deficiency degrades immune function, mitochondrial energy, neuroinflammation control, and cognitive performance
- Tanzi's analogy: cells are toys with batteries; mitochondria are the batteries; NR is a recharge

## Trial evidence

See [[niagen-lc-trial]]. Headline: 2,000 mg/day for 10–20 weeks raised NAD+ ~3-fold. Pooled analysis showed significant improvements in **fatigue, sleep quality, depression**, and **Trail Making B (executive function, +8.4 seconds in 10 weeks)**. Direct placebo-vs-NR group comparison was under-powered.

## Dosing

- Trial dose: **2,000 mg/day**
- Bottle-recommended: 1,000 mg/day (or per product instructions)
- The trial dose was approved by the IRB at Mass General based on prior safety data; not common over-the-counter practice
- Should be done under medical supervision at trial dose

## Cautions

- Other supplements taken alongside (especially NMN, niacin, "pre-workout" formulas containing related compounds) should be discontinued during a structured trial
- People who self-experiment without a baseline have no way to evaluate efficacy
