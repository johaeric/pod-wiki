# Probiotics

Live microbial supplements (Lactobacillus, Bifidobacterium, Saccharomyces boulardii, etc.) intended to support gut microbiome diversity. Clinical evidence base outside LC is mixed — strong for *C. difficile*-associated diarrhoea and for some IBS subtypes, weaker elsewhere.

In Long Covid the proposed rationale is gut microbiome dysbiosis post-COVID (cf [[gut-health]], [[gi-dysfunction]]), and the gut-immune-axis influence on systemic inflammation.

## What's been said in the podcast

- **[[gez-medinger]] (#46).** "The probiotics do help." Brief mention; doesn't expand on strain or dose. Ranks them among the few supplements he found made an observable difference (alongside NAD/niacin).
- **[[viola-sampson]] (#99).** Anchor microbiome episode — the wiki's most detailed probiotic guidance. Argues **prebiotics > probiotics for long-term shifts** (probiotics are transient — persist days to weeks; cf [[prebiotics-gos]]). Names specific strain-products with evidence base:

## Caveats

- **MCAS / histamine-sensitive patients**: some probiotic strains are histamine-producing (Lactobacillus casei, L. bulgaricus) and may worsen symptoms. Histamine-friendly strains include B. infantis, B. longum, L. rhamnosus, L. plantarum. ([[joachim-gerlach]] #41 specifically warns about probiotic-triggered MCAS reactions.)
- **No standardisation** — products vary wildly in CFU count, strain mix, viability.
- **Strain-specificity matters more than species** — Sampson #99 emphasises that L. rhamnosus *Rosell-52* and L. rhamnosus *GG* have completely different evidence bases despite same species name.
- **Probiotics are transient** — they pass through the gut over days to weeks; **don't reseed extinct species**. You have to keep taking them. For longlasting microbiome shifts, prebiotics ([[prebiotics-gos]]) are more effective.
- **Don't broad-spectrum-antimicrobial first** — see [[099-viola-sampson-microbiome]] for the wiki's anchor "first do no harm to your microbiome" warning.
- **Stop if symptoms worsen** — gut dysbiosis is real but not all probiotic interventions help every dysbiosis profile.
