# Acupuncture

Insertion of fine needles at body points used in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Common adjunct in chronic pain, headache, autonomic dysregulation. Limited LC-specific peer-reviewed evidence.

> Cross-ref: see `ercipedia/wiki/interventions/acupuncture.md` if/when an evidence page exists.
