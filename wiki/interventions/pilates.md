# Pilates

Mat- or equipment-based movement system focused on muscle strength + endurance, joint range of motion, balance + coordination, and breath-work — **explicitly de-emphasising cardiovascular load**. That last point is the case for using Pilates instead of cardio-based exercise in POTS / LC / hypermobility populations: it builds the *other* components of fitness while sparing the cardiovascular system that's under autonomic dysregulation.

Lorna Nicholson's POTS / LC / hypermobility-specific online class is the example on the podcast.

## Why this is POTS-friendly (per ep #29)

- Most "go exercise" advice from POTS clinics gets misread as "go run" → patient crashes
- Components of fitness:
  - Muscle strength + endurance ✅ trainable in Pilates
  - Joint range + flexibility ✅ trainable in Pilates
  - Balance + coordination ✅ trainable in Pilates
  - Cardiovascular ❌ deferred until later
- Online format avoids the energy cost of travel + the social pressure of in-person class
