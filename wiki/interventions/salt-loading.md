# Salt Loading

Generous oral sodium intake — sea salt, Himalayan, or table — to expand plasma volume in POTS / autonomic dysfunction. Standard non-drug first-line alongside [[fludrocortisone]] and increased fluid intake.

## Boon Lim's framing (#40)

- **Caveat first**: monitor BP with an upper-arm cuff (e.g. Omron M2, ~£35). Target sub-135/85.
- If BP runs <100/70 or low-normal: **succumb to salt cravings** — generous salt at every meal.
- Prefer complex / unrefined salts (sea, Himalayan ground) — placebo or real micronutrient benefit, but doesn't hurt.
- Pair with electrolyte sachets (SiS, Precision 1500, etc.) — front-loaded fluid (2 L by lunchtime, 3rd L by 5 pm).

## Subset note

#129 is the wiki's first explicit warning that POTS sub-phenotype determines whether salt-loading helps or harms. Boon Lim (#40) and Stiles (#66) treat the pooling phenotype as the dominant LC-POTS picture, which it likely is — but the vasoconstriction phenotype exists and its salt-load response is opposite. Match the intervention to the subtype, not the diagnosis label.
