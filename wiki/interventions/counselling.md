# Counselling / psychotherapy

Talk therapy — typically private, weekly, online — focused on **adjustment, acceptance, self-compassion**, and the chronic-illness identity work patients have to do. Not framed as a *treatment* for the disease, but as essential coping infrastructure.

Distinguish from explicit **brain-retraining programs** (Gupta, DNRS) which claim *physical* benefit through nervous-system retraining, and from **CBT-as-cure** approaches that fall under the older PACE-trial paradigm. This page is for general supportive psychotherapy.

## Common reported benefits

- Reframing the need-for-diagnosis ("it's real because *you* feel it")
- Tolerance for uncertain timelines
- Self-compassion vs. self-criticism during crashes
- Working through grief for the previous active life
- Holding the relational impact (partners, family, work)
