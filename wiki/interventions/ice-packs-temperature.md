# Ice Packs for Temperature Dysregulation

A pragmatic low-cost adjunct for the **heating-up / hot-flash / temperature-dysregulation** episodes common in LC. Place ice pack on neck, face, or wrists during the surge. Often paired with sensory withdrawal / "bat cave" approach.

Mechanism story (informal): cold to thermally-sensitive areas (carotid, vagus afferents) → autonomic shift toward parasympathetic + acute reduction of subjective distress. Same family of interventions as cold face splash and cold-water exposure, but adapted for the in-bed crashed-state patient.
