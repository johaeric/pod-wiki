# Prebiotics (GOS / galactooligosaccharides)

**Prebiotics** are non-digestible fibres or compounds that **feed beneficial gut bacteria** — distinct from [[probiotics]] (live bacteria). The wiki's anchor distinction (from [[099-viola-sampson-microbiome]]):

| | Probiotic | Prebiotic |
|---|---|---|
| **What** | Live bacteria | Compounds that feed beneficial bacteria |
| **Persistence** | Days to weeks | Long-term ecosystem shift |
| **Use** | Targeted strain, transient effect | Background ecosystem support |

Sampson's clinical claim: **prebiotics drive longlasting microbiome changes; probiotics do good in passing through**.

## Galactooligosaccharides (GOS) specifically

- Feeds **bifidobacterium** — the genus most consistently low in chronic-fatigue / LC patients.
- Bifidobacterium interfaces with **gut-brain axis** + autonomic nervous system.
- General-tolerance: positive across most patients; some constipation/bloating-presenting patients may not tolerate.

### Brand options

- **Bimuno** — UK-available; one option.
- **Vivomixx Healthcare** — Sampson calls it "the better version" (formulation-specific).

## Caveats

- **Some patients with constipation / bloating may not tolerate** — start low.
- **Not a magic bullet** — it feeds bacteria *if they're present*. If your bifidobacterium is severely depleted, GOS alone won't rebuild a wiped-out ecosystem.
- Cross-reference [[099-viola-sampson-microbiome]] for the wiki's larger "don't broad-spectrum-antimicrobial yourself" caveat — prebiotics are positive-feeding, not antimicrobial.
