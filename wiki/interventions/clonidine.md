# Clonidine

Centrally-acting α2-adrenergic agonist; reduces central sympathetic outflow. Originally a hypertension drug, used off-label in POTS and dysautonomia for the **"hyper-adrenergic" / "wired"** subtype where excessive sympathetic activity drives the symptom load.

## Per [[sanjay-gupta-cardiologist]] (#57)

- Used as an **adrenaline antagonist** — particularly for the wired-and-tired phenotype
- Adjunct rather than first-line; deployed when the patient profile is dominated by sympathetic overdrive (heart racing, sleep fragmentation from adrenaline surges, hot/trembly)
- Care needed with BP / sedation
