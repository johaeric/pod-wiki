# Antidepressants

Pharmaceutical class — SSRIs, SNRIs, tricyclics, etc. — used in long covid for affect regulation, sleep, neuropathic-pain modulation, and (more rarely) targeting putative neuroinflammatory mechanisms.

This page is a starting placeholder; specific molecules will be split out as separate pages once meaningful patient-outcome data accumulates per drug.

## What's actually been said in the podcast so far

- **Alison Twycross (#42).** Started in October 2020 after realising she was "spending a lot of the day trying not to cry." Helped night-time sleep (which then helped her permit a daytime nap without anxiety). She does **not** credit them for any LC recovery — purely affect / sleep regulation. Drug name not specified on-air.

## Mechanisms-of-interest in long covid

- **Anti-depressant effect** on the depression component that often coexists with chronic illness (whether cause or consequence is contested).
- **Sleep regulation** via sedating effects (notably trazodone, mirtazapine, amitriptyline).
- **Neuropathic pain** — TCAs and SNRIs commonly used.
- **Anti-inflammatory hypotheses** (SSRIs and IL-6 modulation) — some animal work, weak human evidence.

Cross-ref `ercipedia/` for any rigorous trial evidence.

## Caveats

- **Pharmacological withdrawal.** Stopping abruptly is harmful for SSRIs/SNRIs especially.
- **Weight, libido, GI side-effects** — well documented.
- Long-haulers with **MCAS overlap** may have unusual responses; some antidepressants are histamine-active.
