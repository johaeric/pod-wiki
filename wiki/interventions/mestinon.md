# Mestinon (Pyridostigmine)

Reversible acetylcholinesterase inhibitor. Approved for myasthenia gravis. In POTS: increases cholinergic tone, improves exercise capacity and orthostatic symptoms. David Systrom (Brigham, Harvard) showed exercise-capacity improvement in invasive-CPET-proven LC.

**Eric is on mestinon** — see project `CLAUDE.md`. Pages are unusually load-bearing.

> Cross-ref: see `ercipedia/wiki/interventions/pyridostigmine.md` for trial-evidence picture.
