# Gentle restorative movement / stretching from bed

Sub-PEM-threshold movement, started from bed: 5-minute sit-ups, very light stretching, tiny scaled increments. Distinct from [[graded-exercise]] (which has caused harm in this corpus and is not symptom-titrated) and from [[yoga]] (which presupposes an able body). The pattern here is **the smallest possible dose of motion that doesn't crash the patient**, used to break the immobilization–fatigue feedback loop.

This page exists because Raelan Agle's stretching videos surface repeatedly in her own channel as a "starter intervention" — patients credit them as the first thing that worked when nothing else was tolerable. Watch for this pattern in subsequent Raelan ingests; it may consolidate into a named program (Raelan's specific video set) rather than a generic intervention.

> Cross-ref: contrast with [[graded-exercise]] (harmful when applied indiscriminately to PEM); see [[pacing]] for the threshold-respecting framework this fits inside.
