# Sodium Cromoglicate

Mast-cell stabiliser. Oral form (Nalcrom in UK / Gastrocrom in US) is **specifically a gut-MCAS drug** because it is poorly absorbed — concentrates locally on gut mast cells. Used for systemic mastocytosis with GI involvement.

## Tina Peers's clinical signal (#38)

- **Epigastric tenderness on palpation** (upper-mid abdomen, where transverse colon crosses) is her flag for gut-localised mast-cell aggregation. Sodium cromoglicate is the targeted drug for that pattern.
- One of the more expensive items in her protocol.
