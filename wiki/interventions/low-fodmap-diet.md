# Low-FODMAP diet

A three-phase elimination diet for IBS / functional GI symptoms — **fermentable oligo-, di-, mono-saccharides and polyols** are removed, then systematically reintroduced to identify personal triggers, then maintained at the patient-tolerated subset.

Originally developed at Monash University. Strong evidence base in IBS literature, decent evidence in functional dyspepsia, weaker in other DGBI subtypes.

## Phases

1. **Elimination** (2–6 weeks) — strict avoidance of high-FODMAP foods.
2. **Reintroduction** (6–8 weeks) — challenge each FODMAP group sequentially to identify personal sensitivities.
3. **Personalisation** — long-term diet incorporating the FODMAPs the patient tolerates.

## Caveats

- **Not a forever diet.** Long-term strict elimination depletes microbiome diversity and may worsen long-term outcomes.
- **Requires a dietitian for the reintroduction phase**, otherwise patients stay in elimination indefinitely.
- **Rule out organic disease first** (coeliac, IBD, *H. pylori*, SIBO).
