# MARCoNS protocol

**MARCoNS** = Multiple Antibiotic-Resistant Coagulase-Negative Staphylococci — a chronic biofilm sinus colonisation common in patients with mould exposure, Lyme disease, and other immune-compromised states. The biofilm makes MARCoNS notoriously hard to clear: standard antibiotics, nasal sprays, nebulised treatments, and steam inhalation often fail.

## What it is

- Sean Moran ([[sean-moran]]) used a **30-day protocol from a US practitioner** who had MARCoNS + Lyme themselves and developed the protocol from their own recovery. The specific name of the practitioner / protocol was not stated on-air.
- Many similar protocols exist in the functional-medicine / chronic-Lyme literature (BEG sprays + biofilm-busting agents + sequential-rotation approaches) — this evidence-log row covers Sean's specific 30-day course only.
- Diagnosis via swab culture; functional / integrative practitioners more likely to test for MARCoNS than mainstream ENT.

## Why it shows up in LC

- MARCoNS sinus colonisation is upstream of significant **brain fog + cognitive dysfunction + fatigue** because:
  - Sinus inflammation has direct cognitive impact.
  - Toxins released by the biofilm circulate.
  - Chronic immune activation drains energy.
- Sean's clearance produced the most dramatic single before/after effect in his decade-long recovery: *"My brain just started to switch on again for the first time in 9–10 years."*
- Pairs with mould exposure — MARCoNS is much more common in mould-exposed and Lyme patients. Cross-ref [[heavy-metal-detoxification]] + [[mould-as-trigger]] (placeholder).

## Caveats

- **Diagnosis first.** Don't run this protocol without a positive swab; many LC patients with brain fog do not have MARCoNS.
- **Practitioner-supervised.** The protocols use prescription-only nasal sprays + biofilm-busters; not a self-administered intervention.
- **Mainstream ENT is unlikely to recognise the diagnosis.** Functional / integrative / chronic-Lyme-aware practitioners are the typical access route.
- **Cost + access** for the testing and the protocol.
- **Self-reinfection risk** if mould exposure isn't remediated first — Sean's collapse was triggered by mould, the MARCoNS was downstream. Treating MARCoNS without addressing mould is failure-prone.
- Cross-ref [[ercipedia]] for any peer-reviewed evidence on chronic biofilm sinus infections.
