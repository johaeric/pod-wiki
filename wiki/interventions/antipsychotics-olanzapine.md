Atypical antipsychotic prescribed in psychiatric/mental-health emergencies. Jamie (#186) was prescribed it during a hospitalised mental crisis; she credits it with stabilising her sleep and helping her overall during that acute period.

## ⚠️ Important caveats

- **Not a treatment for Long COVID.** Used as an emergency psychiatric medication.
- **Hard to taper.** Jamie was still in the tapering process at time of recording.
- Patient explicitly **does not recommend** anyone treat olanzapine as an LC intervention.
