# Modafinil / Provigil

Wakefulness-promoting agent. Standard licensed indication: **narcolepsy**. Off-label use on the podcast: **stabilising slow-wave (deep) sleep** in the LC subpopulation whose fatigue is driven by disrupted SWS, identified by David Joffe and used by Putrino's clinic.

## Why for LC

Putrino (#181) describes a phenotype:
- Patient reports fatigue as their most disabling symptom.
- They can fall asleep "just fine."
- They have **vivid dreams and lots of dreams**.
- They will sleep as long as you let them — into the afternoon.
- They wake **unrefreshed regardless of duration**.

This pattern is consistent with **REM dominance + slow-wave-sleep skipping**. SWS is when restorative work happens; without it, no amount of total sleep is restorative. Modafinil / similar agents stabilise SWS and the same patient comes back to baseline.

## Caveats

- Off-label for LC. Use under a clinician familiar with chronic-illness contexts.
- Standard cautions: cardiac history, history of psychosis, certain drug interactions, pregnancy.
- Putrino's framing emphasises that fatigue has **multiple drivers** — modafinil is for the SWS-disruption subset, not a generic fatigue prescription.
