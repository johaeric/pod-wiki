# Bowen Therapy

A gentle hands-on soft-tissue therapy developed in 1950s Australia by Tom Bowen. The practitioner makes small **rolling moves over muscles, tendons, and fascia**, then pauses (often leaving the room) to let the nervous system respond. Sessions are typically 30-60 minutes; most clients are clothed.

In a chronic-illness / autonomic-dysregulation context, the most defensible framing is the same one applied to **[[cranial-sacral-therapy]]**: any extended period of *gentle, parasympathetic-supporting touch* can downregulate sympathetic tone. The specific Bowen mechanism may or may not matter — the wiki position is "parasympathetic bodywork" rather than literal claims about fascial release.

## Caveats

- **Practitioner-quality variance is huge.** Look for accredited training (Bowtech / Bowen Therapy Academy of Australia or equivalent national body).
- **Cost.** Sessions £40-80 typical; multiple sessions usually needed.
- **PEM exposure.** Travel + lying-still time can be costly for severe patients — start with a short session.
- **Mechanism uncertain.** Hold as parasympathetic bodywork; the Bowen-specific claims are not well-validated outside practitioner literature.
