# Desmopressin (DDAVP)

Synthetic analogue of vasopressin / antidiuretic hormone (ADH). Originally developed for childhood enuresis (bedwetting) — promotes water retention. **Off-label use in POTS** as a "pill in the pocket" for events that require sustained standing (long shifts, presentations, social events).

## Per [[sanjay-gupta-cardiologist]] (#57)

- Used as an **occasional / event-targeted drug**, not chronic
- Patient takes it when they need to perform — gives them a few hours of better volume retention
- Example use case: nurses on 12-hour shifts who would otherwise crash mid-shift

## Caveats

- Hyponatremia risk if combined with high water intake — doctor supervision needed
- Not a daily-stack drug; the use case is targeted
