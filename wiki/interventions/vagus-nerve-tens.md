# Vagus Nerve TENS Stimulation

Transcutaneous electrical stimulation of the auricular branch of the vagus nerve (typically a TENS electrode clip on the tragus or cymba conchae of the ear). Aim: increase parasympathetic tone, reduce sympathetic overdrive.

## Khan's framing (#37)

- 30-min sessions, **3×/day** (morning, pre-lunch, evening)
- "Did help me avert some crashes and come out of them sooner."
- **Useful when breath-work is too cognitively expensive** in a severe crash. Passive — no thinking required.
- Some patients find it hard to tolerate; tolerance varies.

## Compatible with

- [[vagus-nerve-exercises]] (Sukie Baker manual maneuvers — e.g. eye-press, neck stretches)
- [[diaphragmatic-breathing]] when capacity returns

**Synthesis**: VNS is high-leverage for some (Conrad's full key_to_recovery), helpful as foundation for others (Clark, Brennan), and demonstrably harmful for some (Keiser's caseload). Mechanism check + correct parameters + slow dose-up are the gating conditions. Both the "anchor" pro-row (#203) and the "anchor" anti-row (#207) belong on the same page.
