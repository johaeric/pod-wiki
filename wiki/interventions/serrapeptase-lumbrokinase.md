# Serrapeptase + Lumbrokinase (natural blood thinners)

Two enzyme supplements with proteolytic / fibrinolytic activity, used in the LC microclot community as a **self-managed alternative or follow-on** to prescription triple therapy.

- **Serrapeptase** — proteolytic enzyme originally isolated from the silkworm. Anti-inflammatory and fibrinolytic effects in some studies.
- **Lumbrokinase** — extracted from earthworms (*Lumbricus rubellus*). Stronger fibrinolytic activity than nattokinase. Used in cardiovascular medicine in some Asian markets.

(Often paired with **nattokinase** — fermented soybean origin — by other patients. Mulder #48 doesn't mention nattokinase specifically.)

The microclot framework hypothesises these enzymes can degrade the amyloid-stabilised microclots over time, similar to triple therapy but slower and milder. Evidence is thin but mechanism-plausible.

## What's been said in the podcast

- **[[saskia-mulder]] (#48).** Used both pre-trip-to-South-Africa as natural blood-thinner stack. After 9 weeks of prescription triple therapy was cut short by bleeding-induced anaemia, **resumed the serrapeptase + lumbrokinase as maintenance**. Reports specifically that **serrapeptase is the only thing that touches her clothing-touch-pain symptom** (clothes feel painful on the limbs).

## Caveats

- **Bleeding risk.** Even natural enzymes have anticoagulant effect. **Do not combine with prescription anticoagulants without medical oversight.** Stop before surgery / dental work.
- **Quality varies.** Enzyme units (SPU, FU) and stability (enteric coating) differ wildly between brands.
- **MCAS interaction.** Some patients react; introduce one at a time.
- **No LC RCT evidence.** Mechanism-plausible only.
