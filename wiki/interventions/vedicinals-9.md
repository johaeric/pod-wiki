# Vedicinals-9

A 9-molecule plant-derived nutraceutical formulation developed in 2020 by Joachim Gerlach's network. Targets multiple SARS-CoV-2 / Long-Covid pathways simultaneously: viral replication (3CL protease), ACE2 cleavage, mast-cell activation, IL-6 / TNF-α inflammation, thrombosis, hypoxia, glutathione depletion.

**Named ingredients in podcast** (5 of 9; the other 4 are not stated on-air): quercetin, EGCG, piperine, curcumin, baicalein.

Manufactured by a large Indian GMP contract manufacturer; certified in India as a "food for special medical purpose"; sold online in ~27 countries.

## Evidence

The product has a Phase 2 RCT in **acute COVID-19** (not Long COVID): n=124 in India, 62 treatment + 62 control, all on the standard Indian early-treatment protocol (hydroxychloroquine, antibiotics, paracetamol, dexamethasone, supplemental O₂). Reported outcomes:

- Mean daily PCR Ct value rose 1.76 cycles/day (faster viral clearance vs control)
- Reduced IL-6 / TNF-α
- Reduced thrombotic markers
- 75% reduction in lung-x-ray abnormalities at day 12
- 45-day follow-up: no Long-Covid symptoms or imaging abnormalities in treatment group
- LD50 ≥ 5,000 mg/kg in animal toxicity testing (Class 5)

There is **no published Long-Covid RCT** as of the podcast date.

## Cross-ref ercipedia

No `ercipedia/` page exists for Vedicinals-9. Quercetin, EGCG, and curcumin individually have weak-to-moderate Long-Covid evidence as separate supplements; no peer-reviewed evidence for the 9-molecule combination as such.

## Caveats

- **Founder bias.** All efficacy claims for Long Covid in podcast come from Gerlach himself, who is the founder. No independent patient outcome data on the show.
- **No Long-Covid RCT yet** — only acute-COVID trial.
- **Product is online-only** with significant import duties (UK customs flagged on podcast).
- **Anecdotal "people leave their inhaler at home" reports** — unverifiable, attribution-free.
