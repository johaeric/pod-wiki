# Reiki / Energy Work

Japanese-origin energy-healing practice. The wiki position is agnostic about the proposed mechanism (channelling universal energy through the practitioner's hands); the relevant signal here is that the **practice itself**, when self-administered to oneself, functions as a structured calming / down-regulating ritual that engaged the parasympathetic nervous system.

## Why it might matter for LC

- **Self-administered.** No practitioner required after certification; can be done lying in bed.
- **Highly calming** — the structured laying-of-hands routine appears to function as a guided meditation with an embodied dimension.
- **Engagement effect.** Alisha (#178) explicitly notes Reiki was the *first* thing post-illness that brought her **joy and curiosity**, breaking her obsession with her own health and engaging her mind in something else — a mechanism that lines up with Dan Buglio's "engage the mind in something other than your symptoms" advice.

## Wiki position

Single recovery row, but a strong one. Treated as an instance of a broader principle (a structured calming practice the patient finds engaging) rather than as a discrete recommendation — patients with no spiritual / energetic affinity should look at meditation, somatic tracking or Reiki-equivalent practices that fit their worldview.
