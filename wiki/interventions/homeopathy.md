Homeopathy is a 250-year-old modality founded by Samuel Hahnemann (early 19th century Germany). Practitioners give highly diluted preparations of natural substances chosen to match a patient's unique "symptom picture." Mainstream pharmacology cannot explain how diluted-past-Avogadro's-number remedies could have direct biological action; the *deep listening* component of long case-taking sessions plausibly accounts for much of the reported benefit.

**Evidence grade**: anecdote / expert opinion. Mainstream RCT evidence for homeopathy as a pharmacological agent is contested at best. Use with that grade explicitly attached.

## Where the value may genuinely sit

- 2-hour case-taking sessions provide depth of listening unavailable in 10-minute conventional consultations
- Being heard at depth is itself nervous-system regulating
- Some practitioners are particularly skilled at sitting with the whole-person history, which patient testimony repeatedly cites as therapeutic in itself

## Cautions

- Avoid Facebook-protocol-style "homeopathy" that prescribes the same remedies for everyone — this is not classical homeopathy
- Verify that any practitioner is board-certified through a recognised body (e.g. NAHE in North America)
- Should *not* replace conventional medicine for serious or treatable conditions
