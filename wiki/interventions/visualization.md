Visualization is the practice of mentally rehearsing oneself doing recovery activities, being well, or completing goals. Used in sports psychology and increasingly in chronic-illness recovery (especially as part of brain-retraining programs).

## Why it may help in LC

- Activates the same neural pathways as the actual activity, in a low-energy way
- Counters the freeze / depletion mode by giving the brain a "we're moving toward this" signal
- Often paired with safety affirmations and somatic-tracking
