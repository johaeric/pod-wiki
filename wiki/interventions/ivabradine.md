# Ivabradine

Selective sinus-node ($I_f$ channel) inhibitor — slows the heart rate without blocking sympathetic activation. Useful in POTS and in patients who can't tolerate beta-blockers (e.g. asthma) or whose hypotension makes beta-blockers risky.

**Eric is on ivabradine** — see project `CLAUDE.md`. Pages on ivabradine are unusually load-bearing.

> Cross-ref: see `ercipedia/wiki/interventions/ivabradine.md` for trial evidence (Cardona et al. POTS RCT, etc.).
