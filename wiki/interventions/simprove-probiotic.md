# SymProve

UK-made water-based **multi-strain live bacterial probiotic** (formerly stylised as Symprove). Distinct from yoghurt-based or capsule probiotics in that the bacteria are suspended in a barley-water medium with no live food source, intended to keep the strains dormant until they reach the gut.

The intervention reached the wiki via a **Sheffield Hallam University clinical trial** in long-covid patients ([[simprove-trial-sheffield]]).

## Why it might matter for LC

- LC literature increasingly points at gut-microbiome dysbiosis (Wei-Shun Chang, Putrino, Iwasaki) as a driver of fatigue, brain fog and immune dysregulation.
- Multi-strain probiotics have a longer evidence history in IBS than in LC; the SymProve trial is one of the first to specifically test this product class in an LC cohort.
