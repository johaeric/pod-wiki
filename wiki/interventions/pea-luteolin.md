# PEA (palmitoylethanolamide) ± Luteolin

**PEA (palmitoylethanolamide)** — an endogenous fatty-acid amide produced by the body in response to cellular stress; supplemented as a flavoured powder or capsule. Acts on PPAR-α, peripheral cannabinoid receptors, and mast cells; non-narcotic, OTC. Long history in Italian / European medicine for chronic pain.

**Luteolin** — a flavonoid found in celery, parsley, broccoli, peppers, chamomile. Mast-cell stabiliser; crosses the blood-brain barrier.

Often combined as **PEA + Luteolin** for central-sensitisation / sensory-recovery / mast-cell sensitivities.

## Teitelbaum's protocol (#167)

### For brain pain / central sensitisation / mast-cell sensitivities

- **PEA (micronised / high-absorption form): 1,200 mg/day for 1 month, then 2,400 mg/day for 2 months** (1,200 mg twice daily).
- ⚠️ Use micronised / high-absorption — non-micronised PEA is poorly absorbed.
- US brand: "Healthy Inflammation Response."
- Cites a 350+ study literature on chronic pain (disc/compression/nerve/depression-related/fibromyalgia) — most recent: ~70% pain reduction at 3 months for disc/compression pain.
- Mechanism story: PEA *refills* the molecule that the hypothalamus uses to mount the "superhero" stress response — when exhausted, mast-cell sensitivities, central sensitisation, MCAS-pattern symptoms emerge.

### For smell + taste loss

- **PEA 1,200 mg + Luteolin 70 mg daily for 3 months.**
- Recovery of smell + taste reported.

## Caveats

- N=350+ studies cited but Teitelbaum doesn't reference specific journals on the show — independent verification recommended.
- Teitelbaum cites a European researcher (unnamed on the show) using PEA at this dose for thousands of pain patients — the practitioner's anecdotal evidence base is wider than the literature.
- 3 months to fully kick in.
