# Brain Tap

Audiovisual-entrainment device — earphones + a visor providing combined sound + light pulses, accompanied by classical music + guided meditation. Sessions are 10-20 minutes. Marketed for relaxation, sleep, focus, mindfulness; sits adjacent to [[safe-and-sound-protocol]] and [[hrv-biofeedback]] in the broader autonomic-regulation device space.

## Anchor

[[096-dr-masha-makeeva-hbot]] (Mediterranean-coast naturopath + LC ~80% recovered) names Brain Tap as **significantly improving sleep quality** in her own recovery. Did not use during the acute LC phase but adopted later — flags it as a sleep-quality intervention rather than an acute-symptom intervention.

## Caveats

- Specific brand-product (US-based). Cost has not been benchmarked against alternatives (Calm app + low-stim environment, etc.).
- Sleep-quality outcome claim is the only outcome flagged in the wiki so far.
- Light-flashing devices: standard caution for photosensitive-epilepsy patients.
