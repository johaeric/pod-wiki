# NAC Max

Glutathione-supporting supplement designed by **Dr Diana Driscoll** (see [[parasym-plus]]) and sold via **vagusnervesupport.com**. Aimed at the **oxidation arm** of her POTS / inflammatory-LC model, distinct from Parasym Plus which aims at the parasympathetic / acetylcholine arm.

## Mechanism (per Driscoll)

The Driscoll model of inflammatory POTS holds:

> Inflammation → oxidation (always) → leaky vascular damage → blood-brain barrier compromise → brain-chemistry changes (anxiety, OCD-flavour hyperarousal, "buzzy fear", panic-like episodes, fear of heights, short fuse).

NAC Max claims to:
- **Increase glutathione** synthesis
- **Recycle** existing glutathione (which is what fails in chronic inflammation)
- Address oxidative damage faster than vitamin C alone (which Driscoll says didn't touch her case during a hospital crisis triggered by an inflammatory antibiotic reaction)

## Reported effects

- Reduction of "buzzy" anxiety, hyperaginuric fear, OCD-flavour control needs
- Better tolerance of stress
- Repair of vascular leakage signs (subjective)
- Cleaner emotional regulation in the LC + POTS subgroup

## Caveats

- Single-source / single-clinician intervention. Peer-reviewed evidence is thin.
- Sold direct-to-consumer.
- Driscoll explicitly pairs NAC Max with treating intracranial pressure + Parasym Plus — she rejects single-supplement protocols.
- The general N-acetylcysteine / glutathione literature is broader than this branded product; patients may benefit from generic NAC at lower cost (not a claim Driscoll endorses).
