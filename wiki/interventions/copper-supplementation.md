# Copper supplementation / high-copper diet

Copper is an essential trace element involved in connective-tissue formation, mitochondrial function, iron metabolism, and antioxidant defence (e.g. superoxide dismutase). Deficiency presents with anaemia, neutropenia, fatigue, neurological signs (sensory ataxia, paraesthesias), and connective-tissue weakness.

**Long-covid / vaccine-injury context**: there is no peer-reviewed evidence base in this wiki for copper supplementation in LC. It appears in #84 as a single guest's recently-started personal protocol after testing positive for copper deficiency.

## Caveats

- **Toxicity is real**: copper accumulates; chronic excess causes liver damage, GI upset, neurological symptoms.
- Copper-zinc balance matters — high copper can drop zinc; high zinc can drop copper.
- "Pure copper powder" oral supplementation is not a mainstream protocol; doses and bioavailability are variable.
- Test before supplementing; monitor.
