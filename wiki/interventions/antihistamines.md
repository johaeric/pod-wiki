# Antihistamines (H1 + H2)

Combined H1 + H2 receptor antagonism is one of the most accessible MCAS-management strategies. Both classes cheap, OTC (UK; H2 by prescription) or low-prescription cost.

**Eric is on a daily zyrtec/pepcid combination** (cetirizine + famotidine) — see project `CLAUDE.md`.

> Cross-ref: see `ercipedia/wiki/interventions/antihistamines.md` for trial-evidence picture and Dani et al's UK MCAS-LC paper.

## Tina Peers's protocol (#38)

Introduce one drug at a time, two-week trial each:

**H1 antihistamines** (start here):
1. **Loratadine** 10 mg → titrate to 3×/day if helping
2. If unhelpful after 2 weeks → **Cetirizine** 10 mg
3. If still unhelpful → **Fexofenadine** 120 mg OTC (180 mg by prescription)

**H2 antihistamines** (add once H1 is settled):
1. **Famotidine** 20 mg twice daily → 40 mg twice daily if helping
2. Or **Ranitidine** (less potent)

Add **mast-cell stabilisers** (ketotifen, sodium cromoglicate) if H1+H2 insufficient.
