# Emotional Awareness and Expression Therapy (EAET)

Structured therapy developed by **[[howard-schubiner]]** + **Mark Lumley** for chronic-pain / neuroplastic-symptom presentations. Designed specifically to surface, allow, and express stored repressed emotions — **especially anger** — that the unconscious survival brain has stuffed down and that operatively drive ongoing danger-interpretation.

Distinct from:
- **Expressive writing** (Pennebaker) — written, often solo, focused emotional disclosure. Adjacent and often paired.
- **CBT** — works on conscious thought patterns, doesn't aim at repressed emotion.
- **General talk therapy** — no specific protocol for emotion-up-and-out.

## Mechanism story

- Repressed emotions (anger, grief, fear) get pushed below conscious awareness as a child to keep us safe.
- The unconscious brain *carries* them, treating their potential re-emergence as danger → keeps the nervous system primed in fight-flight-freeze → drives chronic symptoms.
- EAET protocol surfaces, allows, and expresses these emotions in session → unconscious brain gets the signal that emotional state is safe → danger signalling dials down → symptoms reduce.

## Wiki position

Logged as `recommended` from a clinician (Kennedy #146) actively using it in LC and chronic pain. Pre-RCT in LC specifically; supportive evidence base in fibromyalgia and chronic pain.
