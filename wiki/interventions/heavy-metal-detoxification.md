# Heavy metal detoxification (Christopher Shade / Quicksilver Scientific lineage)

A **slow, gentle, drainage-organ-prepared protocol** for removing heavy metals (predominantly mercury and lead) from chronic-illness patients. Distinct from chelation therapy (which is older + more aggressive and has its own adverse-event profile). The version Sean Moran ([[sean-moran]]) used was developed by **Dr Christopher Shade** at **Quicksilver Scientific** and delivered through a naturopath trained in his approach.

## Core concepts

- **Drainage funnel must open first.** Liver, kidneys, bowels, lymph, and skin all need to be working before mobilising stored toxins. Mobilising metals into a body that can't excrete them re-distributes them into other tissues and makes the patient much sicker. Sean's case study: he tried to DIY this and got significantly worse — became the trigger to engage a knowledgeable practitioner.
- **Skin-route + binders + bowel/lymph support before serious mobilisation.** Order matters.
- **Slow over months** — months to ~2 years for high-burden cases. Sean took ~18–20 months to see exponential progress.
- **Functional lab testing first.** Mercury + lead via blood/urine/hair/microtoxin panels; the panel result is the gating diagnostic to even consider the protocol.

## Why it shows up in LC

- LC patients often have **pre-existing toxin burden** (mould, mercury from amalgam fillings, lead from historical exposure). LC is not the sole driver — Sean's framing is that "the rain barrel is full"; the virus is what overflows it.

## Caveats

- **Practitioner expertise is critical.** Sean's experience: DIY without proper drainage prep was actively harmful. Anyone considering this should engage a naturopath / functional-medicine practitioner trained in the Shade / Quicksilver approach (or equivalent slow-drainage-first methodology), not self-administer.
- **Cost.** Multi-month consultation + supplements + lab testing. Significant out-of-pocket for those without private health insurance.
- **Diagnostic question first.** A heavy-metal panel showing high mercury or lead is the gate; without that, this is not the right protocol.
- **Not all LC is toxin-driven.** Sean's case is one shape of LC recovery, not the universal one — patients with no toxin-burden signature should not assume detox is their lever.
