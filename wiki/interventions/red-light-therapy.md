# Red Light / Photobiomodulation

Therapeutic exposure to red and near-infrared light (typically 600-1000 nm) — proposed to stimulate mitochondrial cytochrome-c oxidase, increase ATP production, reduce inflammation, accelerate tissue repair. Used clinically in dentistry (oral mucositis), dermatology, and increasingly in chronic pain / mitochondrial-disease integrative practice.

## Anchor

[[096-dr-masha-makeeva-hbot]] reports it **helped her own joint pain** during LC — she used it adjunct to HBOT and dietary changes.

## Caveats

- Substantial product-quality variation in the consumer device market — therapeutic dose-response depends on irradiance, wavelength, time per session, and proximity.
- Limited LC-specific evidence base; mechanism-plausible (mitochondrial cytochrome-c oxidase) but not LC-RCT validated.
- **Not for use over recent skin cancer / undiagnosed lesions / pregnancy abdomen.**
