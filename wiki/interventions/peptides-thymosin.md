Bioidentical peptides naturally produced by the thymus gland to support T-cell recovery. **Thymosin alpha-1** discovered 1992; thymosin beta-4 also part of the family. Originally developed for athletic doping (Russian Federation of Sciences pre-2008, leaked 2018). Used now in functional-medicine peptide therapy.

## Stewart's clinical use

When CD4/CD8/CD56 T-cells become exhausted from chronic spike-protein presence, thymosin alpha-1 is used to recover thymic function and restore appropriate T-cell activity.

## Caveats

Peptide therapy in the US is largely outside FDA-approved use — typically obtained via compounding pharmacies. Less well-evidenced than the framing in the episode suggests; cross-ref ercipedia for any RCT data.
