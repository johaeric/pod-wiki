# Arc Microtech Device

Wearable microcurrent device. Originally developed for racehorse injury recovery → adopted by jockeys → consumer product. Detects the user's natural frequency (sweeps ~4.5 million options) → delivers matched microcurrent for 3 hours/day. 6-week initial protocol then maintenance use.

Mechanism claim: boosts mitochondrial ATP. Limited published evidence at recording — clinician anecdote only.

## Tina Peers's signal (#38)

- ~95% of her MCAS / LC patients report some improvement after the protocol
- Gains: pain, headache, sleep, brain fog, fatigue
- She uses it herself
