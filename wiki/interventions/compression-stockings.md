# Compression (Waist-High)

External squeezing of the lower limbs and abdomen reduces venous pooling on standing, augmenting the second pump. Standard adjunct in POTS / autonomic dysfunction.

## Boon Lim's framing (#40)

- **Waist-high** specifically — not below-knee. Abdominal vasculature is a large reservoir.
- **Daytime only** — remove at night.
- Combine with **internal compression**: glute / calf / quad squeezes; toe-lifts; "active fidgeting" at the desk.
- A foot-stool under the desk to keep legs moving.

Wild swimming is endorsed by Lim partly because the water itself provides external compression — pair the cold + horizontal + compression triple.
