# Vitamin D

Fat-soluble steroid hormone. The most-mentioned single supplement in the corpus alongside magnesium — every long-covid clinician interviewed checks vitamin D levels, most recommend supplementation when low, and several frame it as foundational before more aggressive therapy. The corpus also contains substantive cautions about megadosing.

The signal is broad-base **clinician `recommended`** rather than patient `key_to_recovery`: no guest in the corpus credits vitamin D as a top-3 factor in their recovery arc. It's part of the floor, not the lever.

## Why this comes up so often

Two distinct framings dominate, plus a long tail of "obviously check this":

1. **Stewart (LCP #185)** — vitamin D as a steroid lever that turns off inflammation via the JAK-STAT off-switch system, reducing microglial activation
2. **Galland (LCP #191)** — part of his **ACE2 restoration** triad (with curcumin and resveratrol), used from acute COVID onward to prevent LC progression
3. **Foundational/MCAS framing** — [[tania-dempsey]] (#88) treats vit D status as a precondition before MCAS therapy can land; mast cells have vit-D receptors. [[sarah-myhill]] (#118) pairs vit D 10,000 IU with [[magnesium]] for absorption in her ME/CFS base stack.
4. **General "low in LC patients"** — [[robert-groysman]] (#92), [[taylor-krick]] (#106), [[claire-taylor]] (#116) all routinely find vit D low in their LC populations.

## Forms / dosing (across corpus)

- **10,000 IU/day** — [[sarah-myhill]] (#118), paired with magnesium for absorption. Eric note: high end of the corpus range, requires monitoring.
- **1,000 IU/day** — [[carrie-bailey]] (#163) ceiling for "self-experimenting" patients; she explicitly warns against megadoses (above ~10,000 IU) because liver "dumps" excess and damage can result.
- **National-guideline winter dose** — [[dr-sarah]] (#144) — "the sort of national guidelines anyway that we should all be doing"; ~400–1000 IU.
- **Form matters in MCAS** — [[tania-dempsey]] (#88) emphasises that the *excipient* (binder/filler) is what MCAS patients react to, not the vitamin itself; switch formulations rather than abandon the supplement.

## Testing / target levels

Multiple clinicians test 25-OH-D before supplementing:

- [[tania-dempsey]] (#88) — "it would be good to have a vitamin D level" before supplementing
- [[robert-groysman]] (#92) — test, then supplement if low; do it with a physician
- [[joachim-gerlach]] (#41) — "even taking vitamin D3 and zinc without checking your serum levels is also not a good idea"
- [[carrie-bailey]] (#163) — sceptical of single 25-OH-D snapshot ("blood is a snapshot in time"); she notes the body activates D in liver+kidney and a single blood marker can miss the picture

## Use cases

### MCAS / mast-cell foundational therapy
**[[tania-dempsey]] (#88)** — mast cells have vit-D receptors; foundational nutritional health is a precondition for MCAS treatment landing. Common patient response "I tried vit D and reacted" is almost always to the excipient, not the vitamin — try a different formulation.

### ACE2 restoration (Galland triad)
**[[leo-galland]] (#191)** — vitamin D + curcumin + resveratrol, started at acute-COVID onset. Galland reports near-zero LC progression in patients treated this way. See [[ace2-restoration]].

### Microglial / neuroinflammation lever
**[[kendal-stewart]] (#185)** — vitamin D as a steroid lever that turns off microglial activation via the JAK-STAT off-switch system.

### Mitochondrial base stack
**[[sarah-myhill]] (#118)** — 10,000 IU/day paired with [[magnesium]] for absorption + CoQ10 100 mg in her ME/CFS base stack. Cross-ref the [[magnesium]] page for this pairing.

### General "low in LC patients"
[[robert-groysman]] (#92), [[taylor-krick]] (#106), [[claire-taylor]] (#116), [[keith-littlewood]] (#45) all routinely find low vit D in their LC populations and supplement when low.

## Cautions (from the corpus)

- **Don't megadose** — [[carrie-bailey]] (#163) — above ~10,000 IU/day chronically can trigger liver "dumping" of stored D; she finds the field overweights D vs. C, lysine, zinc.
- **Check serum first** — [[joachim-gerlach]] (#41), [[tania-dempsey]] (#88), [[robert-groysman]] (#92) — supplement to a target, don't blanket-dose.
- **Excipient reactions in MCAS** — [[tania-dempsey]] (#88) — switch formulation, don't drop the vitamin.
- **Toxicity is real but unlikely at modest doses** — [[robert-groysman]] (#92) flags vit D as one of the few common supplements where toxicity is plausible at high chronic dose.
- **Sunshine > pill where possible** — [[keith-littlewood]] (#45) — UVB exposure delivers vit D *plus* full-spectrum benefits (red, orange, yellow light) that supplementation can't replicate.

> Cross-ref: see `ercipedia/wiki/interventions/` for any peer-reviewed vit-D RCT evidence — large non-LC literature on deficiency and immune function exists outside this corpus.
