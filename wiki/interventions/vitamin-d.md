Fat-soluble steroid hormone. Vitamin D is one of the more frequently mentioned base supplements in this batch — for two distinct reasons:

1. **Galland (#191)** — part of his **ACE2 restoration** triad (with curcumin and resveratrol), used from acute COVID onward to prevent LC progression
2. **Stewart (#185)** — vitamin D is a steroid that can turn off inflammation via the JAK-STAT off-switch system, reducing microglial activation
