# HELP-Apheresis

**HELP** = *Heparin-induced Extracorporeal LDL Precipitation*. Originally an FDA-approved treatment for severe familial hypercholesterolaemia. Since ~2021 used off-label for long covid by **Dr Beate Jaeger** (Mülheim, Germany) and a small number of US/Cyprus centres on the rationale that it physically removes circulating microclots and inflammatory mediators from blood.

Mechanism (LC application):
- Patient's blood is run through an extracorporeal circuit, mixed with heparin at low pH which precipitates LDL + fibrinogen-microclot complexes; the precipitate is filtered out; cleaned blood returns to the patient.
- Each session lasts hours and removes a fraction of the circulating microclot load.
- Typically combined with **triple therapy anticoagulation** afterwards to prevent regrowth.

> Cross-ref: see `ercipedia/wiki/interventions/apheresis.md` for the trial evidence picture (no LC RCT yet at recording).

## Practical considerations from the podcast

- **Travel costs** are real — Jaeger's Mülheim clinic is the most-cited centre; Khan (#37) flew from UK; others to Cyprus / US.
- **Multiple cycles** typically needed (Khan: 7–8 first phase + more after reinfections).
- **Anticoagulation co-prescription** is standard.
- **Bleeding risk** non-trivial; requires experienced physician and proven microclots.
- **Reinfection undoes gains** — Khan's pattern.
