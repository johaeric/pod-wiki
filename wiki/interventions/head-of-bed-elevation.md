# Head-of-Bed Elevation

Raising the head end of the bed by **10–15°** chronically (bricks under the bed-head, wedge mattress, or stacked pillows). Counter-intuitive; rationale: at night with normal lying-flat blood-pressure, the kidneys waste sodium because plasma BP signals "we're full." Slight elevation lowers nocturnal BP enough to **upregulate endogenous mineralocorticoid** → kidney reabsorbs more sodium → plasma volume expands over weeks.

Effectively endogenous fludrocortisone via positional cue.

## Boon Lim's framing (#40)

- 10–15° is enough; not full sit-up.
- Effect builds over **weeks to months** — not an instant fix.
- The "couples" caveat: a partner sharing the bed has to agree.
