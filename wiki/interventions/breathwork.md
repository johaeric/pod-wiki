# Breathwork

Practices that retrain breathing patterns: nasal/diaphragmatic breathing, slow-paced exhalation, breath-holds, box-breathing, and yoga-derived pranayama. The thesis on this podcast is usually that **long covid involves dysfunctional breathing** (chronic mouth-breathing, upper-chest pattern, hyperventilation tendencies) and that retraining the pattern improves CO₂ tolerance, vagal tone, anxiety regulation, and — sometimes — energy/dyspnea.

Closely intertwined on the podcast with **yoga**, **somatics**, and **vagal-tone work**.

> Cross-ref: see `ercipedia/wiki/interventions/breathwork.md` if and when an evidence page exists.

## Mechanism (consolidated from #12, #17)

- LC patients commonly hyperventilate (often unconsciously, often through the mouth, often into the upper chest).
- Over-exhalation → low end-tidal CO₂ → mild chronic alkalosis → kidneys excrete bicarbonate to compensate.
- Low CO₂ → **vasoconstriction** (cold extremities, reduced cerebral perfusion → brain fog).
- **Bohr-effect inversion**: haemoglobin holds onto O₂; pulse-ox reads 99% but tissues are O₂-starved.
- Depleted bicarbonate impairs lactate clearance → contributes to **PEM-like heavy legs**.
- Electrolyte disturbance (Mg/Ca/K) → tremor, twitching, perioral pins-and-needles.
- Cited paper (Kelly Mitchell, ep #12): **74% of LC patients had low end-tidal CO₂.**

## Sub-flavours referenced

- **[[buteyko]]** — nasal-only, low-volume breathing (Patrick McKeown lineage)
- **Yoga pranayama** — paced extended-exhalation
- **Diaphragmatic / belly breathing** — physio-style retraining
- **Long Covid Breathing** — [[jackie-baxter]] + [[vicky-jones]]'s six-week online course (see [[long-covid-breathing]])
- **Nose-breathing** — first-line in both Kelly's and Wibbs's protocols
- **CO₂ tolerance training** — measured via [[bolt-score]] and CO₂-tolerance test (Wibbs's class, ep #17)

## Practical first-line (consolidated)

1. **Close your mouth.** Nose-breathe at rest. Build with practice if it feels suffocating — it usually means CO₂ tolerance has dropped, not that you're under-oxygenated.
2. **Slow down.** Aim 8–12 breaths/min. Not all at once; build over weeks.
3. **Breathe low.** Hand on chest, hand on tummy, mirror — only the tummy hand should move at rest.
4. **Measure with [[bolt-score]].** Track tolerance objectively over weeks.
5. **6–8 weeks minimum** for a retrained pattern. "A month per year of bad breathing" rule of thumb.
