Spanish-made probiotic based on a specific Lactobacillus plantarum strain. Available in the UK via a London pharmacy (per Galland #191).

## Evidence

A controlled trial (cited but not detailed by Galland) showed faster recovery from acute COVID-19 with AB21 vs placebo. L. plantarum naturally grows on plants and is found in sauerkraut and kimchi (different strains).

Galland uses it in his protocol for restoring the gut microbiome — particularly in the context of post-COVID dysbiosis where *Faecalibacterium prausnitzii* (a keystone species) is depleted, secondarily collapsing bifidobacteria.
