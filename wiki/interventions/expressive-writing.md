# Expressive writing (Pennebaker)

Structured **emotional-disclosure writing** developed by James Pennebaker (1980s). **Distinct from journaling** — typically time-bounded sessions (e.g. 15–20 min, 4 days in a row) focused on the deepest thoughts and feelings about a stressful or traumatic event. Substantial research base in immune function, pain, and mental health outcomes.

In the neuroplastic-symptoms / Schubiner lineage, expressive writing is paired with **[[eaet]]** as the patient-self-administered version of emotional disclosure between sessions or where therapy isn't accessible.

## Wiki position

Logged as `recommended` from a clinician (Kennedy #146). Cheap, accessible (paper + pen), low-risk. Eric-relevant: a contained experiment that doesn't change medication or pacing.
