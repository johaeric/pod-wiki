# Intermittent Fasting

Time-restricted eating — typically a 14:10 / 16:8 / 18:6 daily window. Often combined on the podcast with **gut-microbiome work** (probiotics, kefir) and **anti-inflammatory or low-histamine diets**.

## Wiki position

Two patient-recovery rows. Both are men using it alongside other interventions, neither claiming it as the single inflection point. Both report a noticeable contribution.

## Caveat for LC

LC patients in the under-eating / malnourished pattern (see #181 Putrino: many patients arrive *malnourished* because they've cut everything that triggers them) should be careful with restrictive eating windows. Intermittent fasting is **not** for the patient who is already failing to hit calorie / protein targets in their current eating window.
