# Dietary Nitrates (Beets, Spinach — Nitric Oxide Donors)

Naturally-occurring nitrates from **beets and spinach** convert to nitric oxide (NO) in the body, dilating blood vessels and mobilising stem cells from bone marrow that may help regenerate damaged microvasculature.

## How it works (per William Li #73)

1. Beets/spinach grown in soil naturally absorb nitrates (distinct from preserved-meat nitrites used in factories).
2. **Chew slowly** — the natural microbiome on the tongue converts nitrate → nitrite.
3. Swallowed nitrite is absorbed and converted to **nitric oxide (NO)** in the body.
4. NO dilates blood vessels, lowers blood pressure (mildly), and triggers bone-marrow stem cells that help regenerate organs / blood vessels.

> "Eat-to-beat-disease principle: you can repair from the inside out."

## Why it might matter for LC

- **Microvascular leg of LC** (Li's three-legs model): damaged capillaries struggle to repair themselves. NO is a repair-promoting signal.
- Studied (in non-LC contexts) for blood pressure, post-exercise recovery, endothelial function.

## Caveats

- Not yet trialled in long covid specifically.
- Generic "nitrates are bad" advice (from preserved meats) does not apply to whole-food sources.
- Eric note: probably worth incorporating regardless — low cost, low risk, broad cardiovascular benefit picture.
