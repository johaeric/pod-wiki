# Triple Therapy (Antiplatelet + Antiplatelet + Anticoagulant)

The drug-side of the Pretorius / Kell / Laubscher microclot framework. Designed to **stop new microclots forming** while the body slowly degrades existing ones (the body still does this — just slowly because of the amyloid form's resistance to proteolysis).

## Composition (typical)

- **Aspirin** (low dose, COX-1 antiplatelet)
- **Clopidogrel** (P2Y12 antiplatelet)
- **+** one of: **dabigatran** (direct thrombin inhibitor) or **apixaban** (factor Xa inhibitor)

All three are well-established medicines, individually approved for cardiovascular indications (AF, stroke prevention, etc.). The combination as an LC protocol is **off-label**.

> Cross-ref: see `ercipedia/wiki/interventions/anticoagulation.md` for the trial evidence picture (UK trial not running at recording; Pretorius/Kell argue the SA replication should be enough).

## Practical considerations

- **Bleeding risk is real** — requires proven microclots + experienced physician.
- **Often paired with HELP-apheresis** to clear existing load + suppress new formation.
- **Reinfection undoes gains** (Khan): new clots appear after each reinfection.
- Pretorius/Kell explicitly do not prescribe — they speak via clinical collaborators (Dr Jaco Laubscher in SA; Dr Beate Jaeger in Germany).
