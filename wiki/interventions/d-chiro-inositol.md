A form of inositol (sometimes called vitamin B8, though the body can synthesise it). Stewart uses D-chiro-inositol specifically to support insulin's ability to deliver glucose to mitochondria — relevant in LC because severe autophagy weakness allows debris to build up inside cells, impeding glucose delivery and contributing to insulin resistance.
