Somatic therapy is a body-centred therapeutic approach that works with bodily sensations as a *separate language* from cognition. Practitioners teach clients to notice, track, and respond to the body's signals (tension, contraction, ease) rather than primarily working with thoughts and beliefs. Adjacent to but distinct from somatic experiencing (Peter Levine), polyvagal-informed work, and embodiment coaching.

## Why it matters in LC

- LC patients are typically stuck in sympathetic / freeze nervous-system states that are not accessible through cognitive intervention alone
- Recovering the ability to *sense* the body is a precondition for self-regulation
- Especially relevant for patients who pre-illness lived predominantly "in their heads" (achiever, perfectionist, masking patterns)

## Distinguishing somatic therapy from somatic *tracking*

- [[somatic-tracking]] (Alan Gordon) is a specific technique within the broader nociplastic-pain framework
- Somatic *therapy* is the broader practice of body-based work, often by an embodiment coach, somatic experiencing practitioner, or sematic-trained therapist
- Both can coexist with cognitive work like EFT, brain retraining, etc.
