# Anti-Inflammatory Diet

Whole-food, low-processed diet emphasising:
- Fresh fruit + vegetables (rainbow plate / phytochemicals as antioxidants)
- Healthy oils — olive, coconut, oily fish
- Nuts and seeds — chia, pumpkin, walnuts
- Sufficient protein, sufficient healthy fats
- Reduced/eliminated ultra-processed foods (>5 ingredients = processed)
- Reduced added sugar, inflammatory fats, additives/preservatives

Theoretical case: inflammation is the common thread under the named LC mechanisms (MCAS, neuroinflammation, microvascular damage, mitochondrial dysfunction) — feeding the body anti-inflammatory inputs across many meals lowers the chronic inflammation budget.
