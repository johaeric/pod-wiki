# Zinc

Essential trace mineral. Mentioned across the corpus mostly as one of several base supplements practitioners check or recommend; only one episode contains a vivid first-person "this changed everything" account, and that account itself attributes the effect to belief/placebo rather than pharmacology.

The overall signal is **mild clinician-recommended**, with a strong caution thread: most who recommend it explicitly pair it with copper, warn against extended use, or ask for serum testing first.

## Forms and dosing (across corpus)

- **Zinc sulfate** — [[carrie-bailey]] (#163) — her preferred form because the sulfur compound aids detoxification and clears "toxic copper." Suggests ~7 mg twice daily (lower than picolinate form because more bioavailable).
- **Zinc picolinate** — [[carrie-bailey]] (#163) — backup form, 15–30 mg capsules. Most common OTC form.
- **Generic zinc** (form unspecified) — [[moira-newiss]] (#21) lists it in her mitochondrial-nutrient stack alongside CoQ10, selenium, magnesium.

## Use cases

### Immune support / chronic viral infection
**[[carrie-bailey]] (#162, #163)** — pairs zinc with vitamin C + lysine as her "immune-system support trio" for chronic-infection / post-viral patients. Argues most clinicians overweight vit D and under-weight C/zinc/lysine. White-blood-count below ~5 is a marker she uses to escalate this stack.

### Mitochondrial / energy
**[[moira-newiss]] (#21)** — zinc as part of "mitochondrial nutrient support" (CoQ10 + selenium + zinc + magnesium). Food first, supplements only after gut absorption is optimised.

### Belief / placebo illustration
**[[jamie]] (LCP #186, RA #546)** — the most striking zinc story in the corpus, and Jamie herself frames it as a placebo case. Bedbound, scared to take anything because of MCAS-style reactions. Protocol creator insisted "zinc is the reason you can't walk, take the zinc." She took it, was told she could now walk, and walked to the toilet for the first time in weeks. Her own read: blind faith in the practitioner did the work, not the mineral. **A useful entry against assuming pharmacological mechanism whenever a recovery follows a supplement.**

## Cautions (from the corpus)

- **Long-term zinc depletes copper** — [[carrie-bailey]] (#163) makes the case that zinc sulfate specifically helps clear "toxic copper" excess, but the standard cautionary read still applies: chronic zinc supplementation can drive copper deficiency. Cross-ref [[copper-supplementation]].
- **Don't take for extended periods** — [[rachel-whitfield]] (#8), recovering patient — "actually you shouldn't be taking zinc for instance for extended periods."
- **Check serum first** — [[joachim-gerlach]] (#41) — "even taking vitamin D3 and zinc without checking your serum levels is also not a good idea."
- **Hypersensitivity in MCAS** — [[joachim-gerlach]] (#41) reaction-prone patients should be cautious; [[jamie]] (#186) was terrified of zinc reactions before being persuaded to try.

> Cross-ref: see `ercipedia/` for any peer-reviewed zinc-and-acute-COVID evidence (there is a substantial acute-phase literature; long-covid-specific data is sparse).
