# Jeremy Rossman

UK virologist (Hon. Senior Lecturer, University of Kent) and President of [[research-aid-networks]]. Co-author with [[vicky-van-der-togt]] on the 2024 paper proposing **acid-base disregulation** as the central inter-mediator across LC symptoms.

The framing he uses on this episode: cause vs effect. Lots of LC interventions that target effects (inflammation, clotting) are useful but not curative; understanding the *cause* — what drives the persistent disregulation in the first place — is where curative leverage might come from. Their paper picks a candidate node (acidosis) and proposes how to test it.
