# Glenn Chan

Ontario Canada computer programmer. Vaccine-injured June 2022 (second mRNA dose). Mostly recovered (~95%). Runs **sickandabandoned.org** — patient-led survey research using Python to analyse treatment outcomes across LC, ME/CFS, and vaccine-injury cohorts. Useful corpus contribution: data-driven rankings of which treatments tend to help vs. harm, with explicit small-N caveats.
