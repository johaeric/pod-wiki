# AJ

Friend of [[jackie-baxter]] from a Scottish hill-walking community. Sustained a brain injury in March 2018 (sledging accident in the "Beast from the East" snowstorm), diagnosed with **post-concussion syndrome**. Three-and-a-half years post-injury at recording. Founded findingmysparkle.co.uk for awareness/community.

**Not a long-covid patient** — included on the podcast for parallel chronic-illness wisdom that maps directly onto LC. Hypersensitivity to sensory stimuli (hyperacusis, "pinging limbs"), fatigue, cognitive limitation, partner Bay JP, screen-load, identity loss.

## What she brings

- **The Happy List** — daily-joy reminders on the fridge; small things, e.g. hot chocolate in a favourite mug, lavender candle.
- **Cognitive load = real load** framing, before any LC researcher had said it on the podcast.
- **Rest before you need it.**
- Honest partner-guilt conversation; relationship-survival framing.
- Specific tools: **TENS machine**, **acupressure mat**, **flotation tank**, **yoga nidra + weighted blanket + eye mask** for sleep, **Insight Timer** meditation app (Bethany Auriel-Hagan).
