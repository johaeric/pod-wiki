# Dr Marco Leitzke

ICU consultant in Leipzig whose pre-pandemic research field was **nicotinic acetylcholine receptors (nAChR) in sepsis**. Watching critically ill covid patients in 2020, he noticed strong overlap between severe covid and septicaemia and started building the receptor-blockade hypothesis for both acute and long covid.

His core claim (Changeux/Pasteur lineage): the spike protein's similarity to snake-venom toxins gives it high affinity for nAChR but no agonist function — it parks on the receptor, breaking autonomic coordination across the body without producing an infection-like signal. Nicotine, with much higher affinity, can displace it.

He works pro-bono in this space, takes no money from patch manufacturers or pharma, and is trying to get a randomised trial off the ground in Germany while regulatory barriers slow him down.
