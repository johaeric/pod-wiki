# Dr Elizabeth So

Doctor of Acupuncture & Chinese Medicine, NYC. Specialises in complex chronic idiopathic conditions that Western medicine struggles with. Did clinical rotations including a NYU-Hospital stroke-rehabilitation residency and an integrative-hospital internship in Taiwan. Recovered from her own LC.

Her LC framing combines:
- *Foundational/root* — rebalancing the patient's specific "internal landscape" (every patient's ecosystem is unique)
- *Branch* — addressing the symptom that's surfacing today

Treats acupuncture, herbal formulas, gua sha, cupping, moxibustion and EAM nutrition as different levers in one toolkit. Values educational/collaborative practice — explicitly walks patients through what each session is doing and why.
