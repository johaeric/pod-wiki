# Barbara Melville

Linguist by background (works on language and forensic linguistics), first-wave long-hauler (got ill mid-March 2020). Founded [[long-covid-scotland]] because the devolved Scottish health system needed its own voice — Westminster letters can't just be ported north.

Her own clinical picture: orthostatic intolerance + breathlessness + autonomic dysfunction; provisionally diagnosed with **POTS**; **research MRI revealed heart damage** invisible on standard tests. Notably she does **not** report classical PEM, which she says lets her campaign harder than peers who do.

## On the policy fight

- Built a relationship with the Scottish government's clinical priorities team (monthly meetings since Dec 2020).
- Organised a "Long Covid Summit" with patients + parliamentarians + doctors + sister-condition advocates.
- Argues for **specialist multidisciplinary clinics** as the central missing piece, with the heart-failure GP model as a cheaper template.
- Has met with a health economist to make the cost-of-doing-nothing argument.
