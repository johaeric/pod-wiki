# Patrick Ussher

ME/CFS patient (6 years at recording, originally pretty non-functional, more functional now). Self-taught researcher who read the ~300-paper primary-polydipsia literature from 1930s onward and recognised what he was reading: **ME/CFS-like patients being mis-diagnosed as psychogenic since at least 1959**.

His book *The Myth of Primary Polydipsia* (~50k words) makes the argument formally: the hypovolemic thirst centre wasn't even known when the original psychogenic frame was built; ME/CFS patients have measurably low blood volume + suppressed RAAS; the resulting paradoxical thirst-without-salt-craving is the textbook misdiagnosis pattern.

His self-treatment — 1.5 L/day **oral rehydration solution** — has been the single most useful intervention in 6 years for him. Shares the protocol broadly.
