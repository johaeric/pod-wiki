UK occupational therapist and mother. Her son developed paediatric ME/CFS at age 13 (October, post-viral, year 8). After ~8 years of illness — through pacing-led local NHS care, ANS Rewire (Dan Neuffer), and finally Mickel therapy — he is now 23 and fully recovered.

## Background

- Older child died of spinal muscular atrophy at 5 months old before the recovered son was born
- Family stress; high-strung mother by her own description; volatile relationship through son's first 8 years
- These contextual factors plausibly contributed to son's nervous-system sensitivity

## Pivot moment

- Mid-illness, after Claire had been correcting her son during a household repair, his speech slurred and his energy collapsed within minutes. She realised: 10 minutes of negativity → cascade. *His thoughts were driving the condition.* That changed everything for her advocacy.

## Recovery framework she advocated for her son

1. Bristol/Bath ME/CFS pediatric clinic (Dr Esther Crawley) — referral within 3 weeks of GP request
2. Pacing / activity management with paediatric ME specialist — 0–40% over 18 months
3. ANS Rewire (Dan Neuffer) at age 16¾–17 — 40% → 80%
4. Mickel therapy (David Mickel) at age 21 — 3 sessions → full recovery
5. Joy-led activity at home — Rubik's cube, juggling, RSPB volunteering
