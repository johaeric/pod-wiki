# Ewa Beldzik

Polish-trained cognitive neuroscientist; postdoctoral fellow in **Laura Lewis lab at MIT** working on sleep neuroscience. Co-leads the LC fatigue fMRI study with [[daniel-gomez]]. Background: brain mechanisms behind cognitive control processes; uses fMRI + EEG.

## Wiki contribution

- Brings the **cognitive-control task design** lens to the LC fatigue study — selecting tasks (color-word matching / Stroop) that produce measurable behavioural degradation over 12 minutes.
- Articulates the wiki's clearest **anatomy-vs-function** distinction for LC: clinical MRI shows normal brains; functional MRI is where the LC signal lives.
