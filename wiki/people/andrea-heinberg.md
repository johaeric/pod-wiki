German Olympic biathlon competitor (cross-country skiing + shooting). After her competitive career, became a personal trainer. Met **Katie Brennan** when Katie joined her outdoor exercise class while recovering from LC. Co-founded **Thrive90** to bring elite-athlete brain-body coordination drills to chronic-illness recovery.

## Approach

- Sprinkles cognitive tasks into exercise classes — adds a brain-body skill layer to fitness work
- Drills are tiny, energetically cheap, can be done at home
- Self-assessments demonstrate immediate measurable changes (mobility, symptoms)
- Pacing was already deeply embedded in her elite-athlete training culture (no late nights in season; nutrition; recovery > volume)
