# Joshua Roman

Concert cellist whose LC trajectory upended an active touring practice. The first post-Covid concert he played (Saint-Saëns Cello Concerto, a piece he's known for decades) became the moment he learned how cognitively expensive *practising* — not performing — was. After three months with the cello in its case, he played at a friend's solstice party, cried, realised he needed it, and began the work that turned into the **immunity** project.

Strong public voice for the role of vulnerability and creative self-expression in chronic illness. Cites Ivy Ross & Susan Magsamen's *Your Brain on Art*. His Princeton talk-with-performance (commissioned for *Healing With Music*) was the prototype.
