# Dr Diana Driscoll

US optometrist who got post-viral dysautonomia / POTS, was disabled for 12+ years, and had two affected children. Was repeatedly told she was "perfectly normal, just more aware of her body" by major-research-institution clinical trials. Reverse-engineered her own treatment, founded **POTS Care** clinic and **Genetic Disease Investigators**, and runs a small **POTS Rebels** Patreon community.

## What's distinctive

- **Patient first, then practitioner.** Driscoll lived through 12 years of disability, multiple gastroparesis-class crises, an antibiotic-induced inflammatory event requiring blood transfusions, and watching her own son tell her at age 9 that "I can't keep going like this." This biographical context is load-bearing in her clinical framing.
- **Refused the standard "dysautonomia is autoimmune receptor blockade" line.** Her receptor-testing studies (using nicotine as a probe) returned working receptors — sending her in the opposite direction toward acetylcholine support.
- **Builds her own tools.** Parasym Plus and NAC Max are her commercial products; she frames them as patient-reach mechanisms because the clinical reach is too narrow.

## What's she like clinically

- Sees patients globally for consultations.
- Frames recovery as fragile for ~2 years post-recovery, then stable.
- Insists patients leave the clinic understanding *what type of inflammation they have* + *what genetic susceptibility they carry*, so future triggers don't restart the cycle.
