# Dr Élaina Gauthier-Mamaril

Edinburgh-based interdisciplinary research fellow. Background: history of philosophy (Canada + UK); now at the Centre for Biomedicine, Self and Society. Long-time ME/CFS patient (since age 10).

Identifies as **queercrip** — episodic / cyclical / invisible illness; deliberate political alignment with disability-justice movements. Her main research output is a **6-episode podcast mini-series** *Massively Disabled* using podcasting itself as a form of scholarship.

Performed *Long Covid, Zombies & Crip Prophecies* at the Edinburgh Fringe (Cabaret of Dangerous Ideas, August 2023).
