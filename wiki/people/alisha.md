# Alisha

US mountain-living organic farm worker (~10 years pre-illness). Got COVID 2020, then again 2021. Couch-bound for 1.5 years through ~15 symptoms — extreme fatigue, panic attacks, GI dysfunction (bloating / constipation / burning), dizziness, fear of sun and heat, plus the new "extreme anxiety" that didn't exist before her first COVID.

## What's distinctive

- **TMS / mind-body lineage practitioner.** Now a mind-body coach 4 days a week. Recovered using Sarno + Dan Buglio + Rebecca Tolin + Rob Ensor — one of the cleanest TMS-only recovery arcs on the wiki, no significant medical-management leg.
- **Reiki as a calming-practice case study.** 2 hrs/day at peak. The wiki position is agnostic about the energetic claims; the relevant signal is that Reiki *as a practice* was a structured calming + engaging-the-mind ritual that consistently moved her into safety. The certification process itself broke her obsessive-symptom-focus loop — first thing post-illness that brought her joy.
- **One-session inflection late.** Saw Rob Ensor (TMS coach, [[rob-ensor]] — see Dan Buglio's channel) for one session about her remaining "detox sensations." He reframed them as nervous-system, not detox. They never returned. Went on a date the next day; that was when she said she was healed.
- **Empath learning.** Read books about being an empath; learned that hard boundaries were a load-bearing part of safety. Boundaries continue to be central in her wellness now.
