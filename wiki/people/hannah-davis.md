# Hannah Davis

Pre-pandemic ML researcher (generative models for art and music; AI bias-detection pedagogy). Got Covid first wave (March 2020); severe cognitive + clotting + ME/CFS + dysautonomia symptoms persist at recording (2023). Co-founder + co-lead of [[patient-led-research-collaborative]]; lead author of PLRC's 2023 LC review paper that received >1M downloads.

## Wiki contribution

- Drives PLRC's **biomedical research direction** more than the policy or community sides.
- The wiki's strongest single articulation that **LC patients with bad cognitive symptoms can still produce world-class scientific work** — provided structural support is there (intentional redundancy, time-off-when-needed, skip-meetings policy at PLRC).
- Cites pre-Covid ME/CFS literature as the most under-utilised LC research foundation — directly references **ganglionitis on mecfs autopsy** as something LC researchers should have built on.
