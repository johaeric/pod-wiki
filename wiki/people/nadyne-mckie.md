# Nadyne McKie

UK psychotherapist + yoga therapist + breathing coach with NHS acute-psychiatric and private practice. Specialism is management of chronic illness — predominantly fatigue-based. Brings personal LC experience plus her son's paediatric LC recovery plus several years of clinical practice with LC + ME/CFS patients and their carers.
