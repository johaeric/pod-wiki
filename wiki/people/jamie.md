Patient recovery story. Severely bedbound at her worst, unable to crawl to the toilet. Cared for by her mother, then by her partner. Was prescribed but never started LDN. Hospitalised after a mental breakdown; treated with olanzapine (still tapering at time of recording). Recovered through brain retraining (Primal Trust → Lightning Process) and group coaching (Healing Dudes). Now back at full-time work + parenting + Pilates/yoga/personal training.
