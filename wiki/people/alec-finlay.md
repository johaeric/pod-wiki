# Alec Finlay

Edinburgh-based artist and poet. **Has had ME/CFS for ~30 years** (since age 21). Developed long covid in March 2020 (pre-lockdown wave). Works on landscape, ecology, and themes of health/illness/well-being. Walks ~150 m max; depends on supported delivery and friends; collaborates rather than producing alone.

Designed **Scotland's national Covid Memorial** in Pollok Country Park, Glasgow — installed Spring 2022 with support from collaborators **Lucy Richards** and **Ken Cockburn**. Centrepiece: tree-supports inscribed *I remember*; QR codes to audio of memories read by Robert Carlyle; book + permanent archive at National Library of Scotland.

## Why this guest matters

- The wiki's strongest **ME ↔ LC parallel** voice: lives both, has the long view on medical neglect of post-viral illness
- Frames disability as creative-and-political rather than purely clinical
- His own creative method (collaborative, "useless" in the productive-economy sense) is an embodied-pacing practice
