# Prof Nisreen Alwan MBE

UK-based Professor of Public Health at the University of Southampton, public-health doctor, researcher, and one of the **earliest and most cited academic voices on Long Covid**. Got Covid in March 2020 (no test); went public about her own LC in summer 2020. Co-authored 2020 pieces with [[alyssa-perego]] arguing for retaining the patient-coined term *"Long Covid"*.

## Wiki contribution

- **2020 LC survey (n=2,500+)** — characterised the fluctuating-episodic pattern, identified stress as a symptom trigger, and produced the strongest population-level signal in this wiki for **early-rest as a protective factor** ([[radical-rest]]).
- **Stigma scale** — co-developed with [[maria-pantelic]]; captures three dimensions (external discrimination, internalised stigma, anticipated stigma).
- Leads the **inequalities arm of [[stimulate-icp]]**.
- Articulates the **lived-experience-as-strength-not-bias** position for LC researcher culture.
