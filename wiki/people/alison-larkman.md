# Alison Larkman

Bristol-based artist; severe ME ~11 years (initial trigger glandular fever in her 20s; ~20 mild years; severe onset ~2012). Previously ran her own t-shirt company internationally. Now a creative working from severe physical limitation, with Arts Council England funding.

## Episode

- [[104-alison-larkman-i-would-be-here]] — *I Would Be Here If I Could* art project: a touring mirror box that plays absent people's audio messages at the places they would go if they could.

## Why she matters on the wiki

Bridge between the **severe-ME experience** and the **LC community**. Project is open to LC participants. Articulates invisibility-as-political-condition more cleanly than the wiki has had it from a guest still living it.
