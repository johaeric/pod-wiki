# Jan Rothney

UK author of *Breaking Free from Long Covid and Chronic Fatigue* — a book-length recovery resource in the Mind-Body / nervous-system lineage. Not yet a podcast guest; named substantively by [[lizzie-recovery]] (#165) as a load-bearing recovery tool.

## Why she matters on the wiki

- The wiki has [[the-way-out-book]] (Alan Gordon — chronic-pain framing) and the [[curable-app]] as named-text mind-body tools. Rothney's book is the **chronic-fatigue / long-covid-specific equivalent** — same lineage, different application.
- **Amygdala-pep-talk technique** is operationally specific and named in this exact form by Rothney via Lizzie. Wiki-new in this wording.
- **Only-positive-experiences progression principle** is distinct from graded-exercise-prescription — and aligns operationally with Rachael's [[baseline-formula]] in [[168-rachael-rebound-method]].
