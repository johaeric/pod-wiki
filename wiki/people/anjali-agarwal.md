# Dr Anjali Agarwal

UK-trained, India-based consultant physiotherapist specialising in EDS/hypermobility, women's health, and (since 2020) long covid via telehealth. Advocacy co-director of [[long-covid-physio]].

Frames LC as a connective-tissue disease producing the EDS-POTS-MCAS trifecta. Sees the trifecta in the majority of her LC caseload — including patients with no pre-LC hypermobility history. Treats with a structural + lifestyle + trigger-control + circadian model rather than drugs (which she defers to clinicians).

## Notable views

- Connective-tissue damage as a unifying mechanism for LC's multi-system picture.
- Mast cell control upstream of POTS / hypermobility flares.
- Circadian rhythm + digital detox as concrete MCAS interventions.
- Don't push exercise; "find your bar" and stay below it.
- Don't fear the dislocation — fear itself drives the next sympathetic spike.
