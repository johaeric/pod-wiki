# Susanna Johnson

Belfast Northern Ireland physiotherapist. ME/CFS triggered at age 20 by **3 weeks of LDN** (low-dose naltrexone, prescribed for hormone imbalance). 8-year arc. Substantial recovery in last 2 years via Alex Howard program + IFS therapy + EFT-with-exercise + Pilates + Zone-2 running. Now back to part-time work and training for a triathlon.
