Doctor of Chinese Medicine (master's + doctorate). Specialist in herbal medicine and acupuncture. Works with **Pierre Brunschwig** (MD) at Helios Integrated Medical in Boulder, Colorado. They have collaborated for 12+ years and have developed a paired East-West LC treatment model.

## Approach

- Chinese-medicine subtyping by *constitution* — most LC patients fit Shao Yin pattern (deficient kidney + heart channels)
- Acupuncture as oxytocin-restoring intervention (patients walk out "needle drunk")
- Custom herbal formulas based on individual symptom picture (not protocol-based)
- Strong on the diet/digestion/blood-building foundation before more advanced interventions
