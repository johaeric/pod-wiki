# Dr Alison Twycross

UK registered nurse of ~40 years' experience. Former Deputy Dean and Professor of Children's Nursing in London; latterly Senior Lecturer at the Open University. Editor-in-chief of *Evidence-Based Nursing* journal since August 2010. **Founder of Long Covid Nurses & Midwives UK** (LCNMUK).

Caught COVID at her workplace **March 2020** while in the clinically vulnerable group on immunosuppressants. Persistent cognitive dysfunction + fatigue ever since. Took early teachers' pension at 56 (summer 2021) for financial security.

Has used her professional networks (Royal College of Nursing, four-nation chief nursing officers, RCN editorial roles, parliamentary briefings, *Financial Times* feature) to amplify LCNMUK's lobbying impact.

## Positions

- **Long Covid as occupational injury.** Health and Safety at Work legislation arguments deserve case law; many employers were not following it during the unprotected pre-PPE phase of the pandemic.
- **Counting matters.** No data by profession on LC prevalence in NHS staff — without it, no targeted intervention is possible.
- **Two-archetype employer model.** Balance-books-staff-as-bother vs people-as-asset. Mostly the former.
- **Reasonable adjustments before IHR.** Ill-health retirement is misused as soft dismissal when employers skip the phased-return + adjustment phase.

## Personal practice

- Daily 1.5–3 hour nap after lunch (took 18 months to figure out).
- Antidepressants (Oct 2020) — helped sleep, helped affect, did not recover.
- Refuses meetings outside her two productive windows (mid-morning + after 4 PM).
