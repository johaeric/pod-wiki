# Dr Mark Faghy

Associate Professor in Respiratory Physiology, University of Derby. Active LC research lead. Strong PPI orientation; explicit critique of "rebadging" interventions from other clinical areas without addressing LC's novel multi-system challenges.

By #120 the Derby team has finished the 16-week cohort analysis (~100 patients, weekly contact), launched two-day CPET mechanistic studies in 100+ patients (Rebecca Rowe, Callum Thomas), banked 500+ biomarker samples, and pivoted into trial work — partnering with David Strain (Exeter) on the UK's first LC antiviral trial.
