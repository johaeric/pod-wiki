# Cass MacDonald

46, non-binary (assigned female at birth), Edinburgh. Office-based nurse at NHS Lothian doing surveillance, audit, and research work. **Pre-LC chronic conditions**: Ehlers-Danlos hypermobility type, chronic pain syndrome, asthma, autism, major depressive disorder.

Caught covid April 2020 (atypical presentation — no cough, brief mild fever, severe headache, exhaustion, smell loss). Severe long covid since. Phased return to work May 2020 → crashed → second sick-leave by August 2020. Part-time return February 2021 → second crash August 2021. Signed off again at recording.

## Why this is the operational episode

Cass has years of pre-LC chronic-illness toolkit. Their episode is the most procedurally useful in the wiki for someone organising their life around LC: aids, OT, return-to-work negotiations, UK benefits (PIP, Access to Work, Universal Credit), union escalation, communication with friends/family, mindset shift.
