# Jules Rogers

UK mother of two; recovered from LC after a ~3+ year arc. Mind-Body therapist + life coach now in independent practice. Joins a small but growing cohort of LC-recovered guests who trained as practitioners during their own recovery and finished the recovery through the training itself.

## What's distinctive

- **HRT signal** in perimenopausal LC. Wiki's first explicit positive HRT row from a recovered patient.
- **Mind-Body therapy as 80% of recovery, training as the last 20%.** Mirrors [[lorrie-rivers]]'s arc.
- The **conditioned fear-of-PEM** pattern (every previous PEM episode reinforces fear of doing the thing again) named explicitly, with a practical mantra-based override.
