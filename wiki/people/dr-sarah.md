# Dr Sarah

UK GP / dermatologist with a young child during illness; husband working abroad. Got mild COVID Autumn 2020 → unwell after vaccinations January 2021 → hard crash after second viral hit Autumn 2021 → recovered ~2.5 years later. Self-published a recovery resource Jackie linked in show notes.

## Episode

- [[144-dr-sarah-recovery]] — recovery story; **freeze-response** sub-pattern; six-pillar mental scaffold; HRT contribution.

## Why she matters on the wiki

- **16th full LC recovery** logged on the wiki.
- Wiki's clearest articulation of **freeze (vs fight/flight)** as the dominant autonomic state in LC. Eric is in the sympathetic-overdrive half but the freeze sub-pattern is real and explicitly named here.
- **Second HRT recovery row** (after Jules Rogers #138) — perimenopausal-LC sub-pattern firming up.
- The "six pillars" mental model is a clean audit framework for any LC recovery stack.
- Lightning Process logged at ~30% recovered as a self-limiting-belief lever — adds nuance to the LP evidence (works *as a lever*, not as a primary driver).
