# Viola Sampson

UK-based registered microbiome analyst + certified health coach. Lifelong gut-symptom history; ill with ME/CFS from age 19 → 10+ years before diagnosis → recovered via **cranial-sacral therapy + psychotherapy + herbalism**. Trained as a CST practitioner; specialised in chronic-fatigue conditions for 15 years before pivoting to microbiome practice in 2018.

Founded **The Microbiome Group** — group practice with multiple practitioners trained in her approach, daily case discussion. Uses **whole-microbiome testing** (e.g., Biomesight) rather than narrow species panels.
