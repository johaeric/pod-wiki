# Uroš Čimžar

Slovenian entrepreneur and business mentor in his late 40s. Got LC after a second COVID infection in December 2023. Bedbound, ~300 steps/day, brain fog so bad he couldn't read for the first weeks. Discovered Jackie via Raelan Agle's channel — "the first positive thing on this journey." Recovered to "back at the gym, full life" within ~10 months.

## What's distinctive

- **Pattern-matcher.** Calls pattern-matching his pre-illness superpower; used it on himself during recovery to diagnose his own crashes before they happened. Highly reflective; jotted down insights so he wouldn't lose them in brain fog.
- **Five-phase recovery framing** he articulates very cleanly — useful for others trying to locate themselves on the arc.
- **Multi-source recovery stack.** Layered Jackie's LC breathing course, Becca Kennedy's class, his existing therapist, intermittent fasting, expressive writing, Kristin Neff's self-compassion, somatic tracking, EFT-tapping. No single source, no single magic bullet.
- **Emotional layer was the unblock.** Years of suppressed emotion ("the cool guy who never gets angry") cleared in days once he started writing and self-compassion-meditating.
