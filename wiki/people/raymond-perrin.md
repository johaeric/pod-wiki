# Dr Raymond Perrin

Manchester-based osteopath + neuroscientist. Born and trained in London (British School of Osteopathy 1980–84), set up his clinic in Manchester. PhD at University of Salford (1994–2005) on lymphatic drainage of the brain in ME. Now honorary clinical research fellow at University of Manchester. Author of *The Perrin Technique* and *Through the Looking Glass: Diagnosing and Treating Post-COVID-19 Syndrome*.

## Why he matters on the wiki

- **Anchor lymphatic-drainage / Perrin Technique voice on the wiki.** The treatment + the mechanism story are both his.
- **35-year arc from hypothesis to mainstream validation** — useful concrete example of how slow the "but the textbooks say drainage doesn't exist" inertia can be. The 2024 Oregon paper was the first real-time human visualisation, ~one month before this recording.
- **Free self-massage protocols** on his website are accessible to any LC patient regardless of geography or budget — a wiki-distinctive *democratised access* to a clinician-recommended modality.
- **Jigsaw-puzzle framing of recovery** (rest/relaxation/meditation/pacing as corner pieces; specific interventions as middle pieces) is one of the cleanest articulations on the wiki of multi-component LC recovery.
