US-based; Cornell biology background then Harvard Business School (MS + MBA). Worked in NYC. Now CEO and co-founder of **Nirvana**, a neuroplastic-recovery app distilling the methodology that helped her recover from three sequential health conditions:

1. Chronic back pain after being hit by a car at Cornell — failed steroid injections + nerve ablation; resolved after PSRT clinical trial
2. GI symptoms in NYC — resolved using outcome-independence framework applied to FODMAP triggers
3. Severe insomnia at HBS — resolved via outcome-independence (mantras + analytical-data review)

## Recovery methodology

- Mind-body / TMS-style framework
- Somatic tracking, breath work, expressive writing (fast handwriting to bypass analytical brain)
- Outcome independence: "even zero hours of sleep, your body will be okay"
- Used analytical brain to her advantage — verifying the inconsistency of supposed triggers
