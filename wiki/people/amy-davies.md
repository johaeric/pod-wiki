UK-based embodiment coach and somatic practitioner. Recovered from chronic pain and fatigue she developed in 2018. Now coaches LC, ME/CFS, and chronic pain clients.

## Approach

- Internalised pressure framework — naming the achiever / perfectionist / helper / masking patterns that act as second-layer stress
- Compassion-based (not shame-based) recognition of patterns
- Microscopic action when in freeze response (toe wiggle, three breaths, blanket-noticing)
- Self-trust over comparison
