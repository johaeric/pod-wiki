# Jackie Baxter

Host of the **Long Covid Podcast**. Scottish; herself a long-hauler from the first wave. Recovered/recovering, advocates, runs support groups. Background in opera/voice (Scottish Opera connections — see ep #25).

Co-founded **Long Covid Breathing** with [[vicky-jones]] — a six-week, twice-weekly online breathwork course tailored for long covid; advertised mid-episode in early shows.

## On the show

- Tone: warm, advocate-leaning, comfortable with both lived-experience and clinician guests.
- Format: usually 30–60 min interviews; occasional host-solo and compilation episodes.
- Disclaims that none of the content is medical advice.

## Episodes

- Host of every episode in the series. Solo or co-host on selected episodes (compilations, reflections).
- Solo reflection episodes anchoring her own recovery arc: [[064-2022-reflections]] (2-year mark), [[075-jackie-3-year-reflections]] (3-year mark), [[094-jackies-recovery-story]] (full recovery, interviewed by partner Mally).

## Recovery arc (per #64 + #75)

- **March 2020**: hit by covid post-hill-walk; pre-illness was a "tinder box" of stress + push-through.
- **Year 1 (2021)**: worse, still pushing through; "looked like a corpse."
- **Year 2 (2022)**: stopped working (~6 months prior to anniversary), started podcast, learned breathwork + yoga nidra + salt + cold water + counselling. "Acceptance that pushing through wasn't working."
- **Year 3 (2023)**: most symptoms largely gone; nervous system regulated; chest pain rare.
- **Late 2023 (#94)**: declares **full recovery**. Career break (start-2023) and Fern programme were the final-phase levers; the realisation came mid-interview with [[lorrie-rivers]] for the holistic-healing summit when she caught herself doing recovery-perfectionism. **Seventh full recovery on the wiki.** See [[recovery-stories/jackie-baxter-recovery]].

## What she credits

Three converging credits across multiple episodes (her own #2, #64, #75; mentioned across many others):
- **Breathwork** (heart palpitations, breathing)
- **Yoga nidra** (deep rest)
- **Cold-water exposure** (nervous-system regulation; Loch Ness)
- Plus: **stopping work**, salt + hydration, counselling, reduced doing.
