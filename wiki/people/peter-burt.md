# Peter Burt

Got Covid early in the pandemic, hit hard physically (had to monitor oxygen at home), dragged through 2+ years of work without giving the virus the respect it deserved. Hit "biggest crash" in summer 2023 after a brief spring upswing.

Brought to the podcast by listening to Jackie's voice in a hospital car park and recognising his own internal monologue. Episode #125 is built around men's mental health in chronic illness — the bind between "must keep being the action dad / breadwinner / hardest worker in the room" and the body refusing to allow it.

His specific recovery levers (so far): breathwork (came from this podcast), talk therapy (Amy), and slowly relearning the skill of asking for and accepting help — what his therapist called *"denying people the opportunity to do for you what you love doing for them."*
