Board-certified classical homeopath in NYC. Background in arts management/fundraising in NYC for ~15 years. Came to homeopathy through his own health journey — multiple migrating pain symptoms and severe insomnia for which conventional medicine offered no answers. Trained in homeopathy in Philadelphia after his recovery; now in private practice.

## Approach

- 2-hour case-taking sessions covering physical, mental, emotional history and life context
- One remedy at a time, watch over time, adjust potency / dosage
- Collaboration with conventional medicine (sees patients also under MD care)

## Notable position

- Encourages avoidance of "Facebook protocol" homeopathy — same remedies prescribed for everyone — which he flags as not actually homeopathy
- Wants conventional + homeopathic worlds to integrate; cites UConn Health's Nov 2025 academic homeopathy conference as evidence of growing engagement

## Caveat

- Mainstream RCT evidence for homeopathy as a pharmacological agent is contested
- The deep-listening case-taking component plausibly does much of the therapeutic work
