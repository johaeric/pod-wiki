# Dr Michael Bagnell

US-based functional neurologist with 33 years' clinical experience. Started in chiropractic / physical medicine, drifted into clinical neuroscience as the field developed, now practises what he calls **brain-based health care** — asking which brain region's dysfunction may explain a presentation rather than treating organ-system silos.

## Wiki contribution

- Single most coherent statement of the **brain-stem-as-substrate** hypothesis on this podcast — see [[brain-stem-dysfunction]].
- Concrete, **at-home, low-invasive rehab toolkit**: gargling, singing, eye-movement therapy, cross-crawl, cold-water facial immersion, primitive-reflex check (Moro), vagal-nerve stimulation devices (gammaCore + clip-on aural).
- Names the **slow-medicine movement** — slowing clinical pace to recover empathy and listen to histories before chasing tests.
