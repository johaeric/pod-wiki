# Ryan Prior

US journalist, author, documentary producer, advocate. Has had ME/CFS for over a decade and brought decade+ of post-viral-illness reporting experience to the LC moment.

Books / films:
- **The Long Haul** (Hachette, 2022) — book on the LC movement.
- **Forgotten Plague** — documentary on ME/CFS.

Writes the *Patient Revolution* column at Psychology Today, working at the intersection of patient power, citizen science, drug repurposing, and disability rights. Previously reporter at CNN (2016 election, science, health).

## Notable views

- Patient lived experience is *practical wisdom* (Aristotle's phronesis), not anecdote.
- Drug repurposing is the highest-leverage strategy for under-funded chronic illnesses.
- The naming of "long covid" worked because it foregrounded the cause, breaking the ME/CFS naming-war pattern.
- Healthcare must be patient-centred or it's not healthcare.
