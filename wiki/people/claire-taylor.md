# Dr Claire Taylor

UK GP since the early 2010s, with cross-specialty backgrounds in rheumatology (Shetland) + cardiology (where she supervised tilt-table tests during pregnancy and learned POTS clinically). Worked for an ME charity during 2020-21 and saw early LC patients there. **Now runs a UK-wide private GP clinic specialising in LC + ME/CFS + MCAS + POTS** — virtual or in-person, takes housebound patients across the UK.

## Wiki contribution

- The wiki's **first practising GP-as-LC-specialist voice**. Models what every GP could in principle deliver if trained.
- Recommends the **same Eric-stack architecture** as Gupta #57 and Stiles #66 — three-way independent confirmation.
- Emphasises **individualised care + sufficient consultation time** — the standard 10-min GP appointment is not compatible with chronic LC management.
- Advocates for **bringing cytokine + chemokine panels into routine medicine** for LC diagnosis and treatment guidance.
- Names the **vagus nerve thickening + diaphragm flattening** imaging study (~1 in 5 LC patients affected) as mechanism-confirming for the breathing-rehab approach.
