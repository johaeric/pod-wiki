# Prof Vassilios "Vass" Vassiliou

Consultant cardiologist + Professor of Cardiology at the University of East Anglia. Day-to-day cardiac patients (heart failure, valve, ischemic disease) plus 50% research time. During the 2020 lockdowns the lab was closed and the research focus shifted to COVID-related cardiology. Has continued some COVID and long-covid research alongside his core cardiac imaging programme.

## Episode

- [[108-vass-vicky-cardiologists-lc]] — meta-analysis of long covid risk factors, with Dr Vicky Tsampasian.

## Why he matters on the wiki

- **Highest-evidence single source for long covid risk factors** on the podcast: 41 studies, ~860k patients meta-analysed.
- **Quantifies vaccination-as-prevention** at ~50% risk reduction — the wiki's clearest single-number summary.
- **Public-health policy critique**: argues UK booster eligibility is too narrow (asthmatics under 65 specifically named).
- **Cardiac-imaging perspective on LC**: echocardiogram, Holter monitor, cardiac MRI — what each test answers, what "normal" means prognostically, why "normal" is good news rather than dismissal.
