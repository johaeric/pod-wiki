# Chimére

Middle-school English teacher in Baltimore (5 years teaching at recording). Black woman, first-wave US long-hauler — got sick **March 22, 2020** with what she eventually identified as Covid + post-viral long covid. At her worst was bedbound for months, lost vision for 5 months (cataract surgery on left eye left a residual eye-droop), wrote a will, considered suicide. Substantial recovery in progress at recording (RA #76, December 2020).

## Recovery arc

See [[chimere]] under recovery-stories for the full timeline. Inflection points: started sitting up 5 minutes/day from her bed (Oct 2020) following Raelan Agle's stretching videos, then read Joe Dispenza's *Breaking the Habit of Being Yourself* and reframed her recovery as a mindset-shift problem.

## Activism / advocacy

When dismissed by ERs (often without a positive Covid test through the early-pandemic months), she:
- Wrote **public Google reviews** of doctors who turned her away
- Wrote letters to **Baltimore city councilmen**, several of whom intervened with the local hospital and unlocked her access to specialist care (eye surgery, post-Covid clinic)

She has since pulled back from advocacy specifically to protect her own recovery — a recurring pattern across the corpus's recovered guests.

## Public project

**"Respect the Reroute"** — Instagram-based motivational project. Slogan: *give your life permission to change.* Distinct from any structured program — it's her own framing.
