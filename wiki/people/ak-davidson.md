# A K (Anna) Davidson

UK writer/editor and mother of three. Had **CFS in 2000** (post-glandular-fever, ~1 year, recovered spontaneously); then **covid three times + LC three times** between 2020 and 2023, recovering each time. Author of *Pearls for Life and other Chronic Conditions* (June 2024) — a poetry collection from her LC years.

The strongest signal in her story is the role of **brain training as the final piece** — earlier rounds recovered without it; the third recovery added it and produced a felt sense of resilience to reinfection that the previous rounds didn't. Closely paired with [[lc-recovery]] threads on neuroplasticity-based finishing-tools (cf. [[032-esther-recovery-story]], [[127-rachael-recovery-story]]).
