# Lucy Gahan

Clinical psychologist (UK NHS) who got covid in April 2020. Off work 19 months. Returned to NHS work in a long-covid service. Author of **Reclaiming Life and the Things That Matter** — written from her bed during the worst of her illness.

Heavily references **Kaethe Weingarten** on witnessing, illness narratives, self-loss, and chronic sorrow.

## Notable views

- Trauma in LC is layered and repeated, not single-event.
- Witnessing matters; un-witnessing harms.
- Language matters — "lingering" understates LC; "chronic sorrow" beats "depression."
- Even quiet activity is energy expenditure for an LC patient.
