Clinical / rehabilitation psychologist at University of Alabama at Birmingham. Originally researched physical rehabilitation post-stroke (Constraint-Induced Movement Therapy with Ed Taub). Pivoted to cognitive rehab during COVID era. Leads the **CI Cognitive Therapy** trial — small initial study showed 80% return-to-work outcomes vs 0% in usual care; confirmatory study underway.
