# David Joffe

Australian respiratory and sleep specialist; lab-based sleep physician. PhD on circadian rhythm and cognitive impairment in obstructive sleep apnea; later interests in reflux, lung disease, neuromuscular disease, non-invasive ventilation, dopaminergic pathways, PTSD (he treats "thousands of veterans"). Started reading about LC in April 2020 after his first post-COVID patient and "hadn't really stopped." Now writes about LC sleep and collaborates with David Putrino, Doug Hill, and others on a forthcoming LC-sleep paper.

## Episode

- [[143-david-joffe-sleep]] — comprehensive LC sleep frame: SWS vs REM, dopaminergic injury, melatonin protocol, vagal disregulation, sleep-hygiene basics, gut–sleep axis, sleep-study recommendations.

## Why he matters on the wiki

- **First comprehensive clinical sleep frame** for LC on the podcast. Sleep had been mentioned by other guests but not anchored.
- Extends the wiki's **dopaminergic / catecholaminergic** strand (cf. Marco Leitzke #130 on nAChR; Bruce Patterson on neuroinflammation; Asad Khan on autonomic). Joffe brings the *sleep* face of dopamine dysregulation.
- His **REM-behaviour-disorder warning** is the strongest neurological-aging signal logged on the wiki for LC — worth tracking longitudinally given the Parkinson's progression rate in non-LC RBD cohorts.
- Vagal-nerve-of-everything framing complements the breathing/CO₂ story (Courtney + Golan #132) and the autonomic-thread broadly.
