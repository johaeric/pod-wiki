# Rosie Pidgeon

UK teenager (16 at LC onset, 17 at recording), got LC at the start of the school year ~2021. Symptoms: dizziness, fainting on getting up, **constant headache for over a year**, exhaustion, muscle pains. Diagnosed clinically (no positive PCR — test access timing).

Recovery trajectory: substantial improvement at recording (#61), attributed to a bundle of [[low-dose-naltrexone|LDN]] + acupuncture + pacing + time. Achieved a B grade at A-level art despite barely attending school.

Has a December 2022 art exhibition for the [[long-covid-kids]] charity titled **"I am still here"** — including a canvas series with one paint dot per UK child with LC.
