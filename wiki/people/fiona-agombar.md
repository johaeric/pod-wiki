# Fiona Agombar

UK yoga therapist with a 15-year ME history of her own (recovered). Specialises in fatigue conditions: ME/CFS, burnout, LC. Runs a teacher-training course for LC yoga therapists. Author of two books, with a third co-authored LC-specific book in progress with Nadine Mai.

Distinct contribution to the wiki: the **trauma-informed yoga therapy** framing — why breath-focus can re-traumatise, and how to scaffold around it.

## Notable views

- Generic group yoga is wrong for LC; 1:1 trauma-informed therapy is right.
- Buteyko-family breath-holds excluded for LC until near-recovery.
- Society's signals (productivity-as-worth, sugar, news cycle) actively undermine nervous-system recovery.
- Forgiveness toward dismissers: it's a culture defending its own assumptions, not personal cruelty.
