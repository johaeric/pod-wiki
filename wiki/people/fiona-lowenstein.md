# Fiona Lowenstein

One of the **architects of the global Long Covid patient movement**. Founded **Body Politic** in 2019 as a queer-feminist wellness collective in NYC; pivoted it in March 2020 into the world's first major LC support group after their NYT op-ed went out and 2,000 people signed up overnight to a Google form linked from the article.

Hospitalised in NYC March 14, 2020 with severe shortness of breath. Now LA-based, mild-end-of-spectrum LC patient. Edited [[long-covid-survival-guide|The Long Covid Survival Guide]] (The Experiment, 2022).

## Wiki contribution

- Founded [[body-politic]] (40,000+ cumulative signups, 14,000+ active users; Slack-based since April 2020).
- Body Politic incubated [[patient-led-research-collaborative]] (PLRC) and [[long-covid-sos-uk]] — both of which split off into independent organisations.
- Edited the **first major patient-authored book on LC** — non-prescriptive, multi-author, "support group in book form."
- Names **internalised ableism** as a concept LC patients (including themself) have to actively unlearn — wiki-load-bearing for [[advocacy]].
- Names the **race and class gating of acute care** in early-pandemic NYC as a real and fatal phenomenon.

## Notable framing

- *"Workflows designed by chronically ill people"* — the book itself was produced by a chronically-ill team using async docs, soft deadlines, and explicit communication norms. Argues this is a model for how all knowledge work could be restructured.
