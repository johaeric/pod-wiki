# Sophie Reynolds

UK ex-prison-officer (graduate scheme), now self-employed Pilates teacher. Got ME/CFS in March 2018 — **pre-Covid, pre-pandemic** — recovered ~2-3 years later. Wiki's first dedicated *pre-Covid ME/CFS* recovery story.

## Pre-illness pattern

- Prison-officer graduate scheme + full-time Masters in criminology + psychology
- Eating disorder (over-exercise variety)
- Recent breakup; chronic perfectionism + isolation
- Running on cortisol/adrenaline daily

## Recovery stack (named in #111)

- [[optimal-health-clinic]] — STOP technique
- [[pilates]] — single biggest factor; now her teaching career
- [[breathwork]] — integrated with Pilates exercises
- [[antidepressants]] — early-stage
- Understanding of the autonomic nervous system as the unifying mechanism

## Why she matters on the wiki

- **Pre-Covid ME/CFS recovery** template — useful as cross-condition signal (cf. [[me-cfs-parallels]])
- **First explicit no_effect / harmed signal for cold water exposure** on the wiki (her experience: drained, energy-rush during, knackered after) — a counterweight to Jackie's strong key_to_recovery
- **Pilates → key_to_recovery** — first such classification on the wiki for Pilates
