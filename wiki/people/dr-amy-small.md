# Dr Amy Small

GP, occupationally acquired covid (April 2020), long-hauler with **POTS**. Lost her partnership at 6 months. Now clinical advisor at **Chest Heart & Stroke Scotland**, BMA elected representative, sits on Scottish and UK GP committees.

A central voice on this podcast for the **clinician-patient** perspective: brings GP-level vocabulary to the patient's experience of being failed by the system she works in. Her POTS diagnosis came from her own internet research and a pivotal beta-blocker trial — not from a referral pathway.

## Recovery picture

Substantial. POTS controlled with beta blockers; pacing structures the day. Working but not back to general practice.
