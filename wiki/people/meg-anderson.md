# Dr Meg Anderson

US Doctor of Physical Therapy (DPT). Originally a hospital-based PT; thrown into the 2020 East Coast covid surge as the designated co-positive PT (volunteered to cover for older colleagues). Worked head-to-toe in PPE with severely ill patients and watched survivors not get better — that experience pushed her to pivot to entirely virtual LC rehab in 2021.

Pre-pandemic she had already started teaching herself about disautonomia / POTS — niche knowledge that turned out to be the right foundation. She is now part of the small clinical group ([[lily-spechler]] is another) explicitly addressing LC's autonomic + nutrition + breath axis as an integrated rehab approach.

Co-runs the **Energy Expansion Project** with Lily Spechler — combines self-paced autonomic-rehab modules + nutrition modules + group calls. Strong stance: **pacing must not become inactivity**.

## What's distinctive

- The RV CAMPING checklist is one of the most operational pre-exercise readiness frameworks on the wiki.
- She will **refuse to start exercise prescription** until upstream conditions (breathing, MCAS, metabolic intake) are stable. This is unusual — most providers start exercise prescription without the upstream stability check.
- Her HR-ceiling rule (resting + 15–20 bpm) reframes "training" as **HR-staying-low practice**, the opposite of conventional fitness training.
