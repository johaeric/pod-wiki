# Dr Boon Lim

Cardiologist + electrophysiologist at Imperial College London, with a strong autonomic / syncope subspecialty. The Imperial Syncope Diagnostic Unit at Hammersmith is one of London's largest tilt-table services and has tested 60–80 LC patients (paper in submission at recording). Author of stop-fainting.com (autonomic-dysfunction patient resource with downloadable PDFs) and *Keeping Your Heart Healthy*.
