# Caroline Pover

UK author, entrepreneur, philanthropist. Vaccine-injured (AstraZeneca, 2021) within hours of injection — first ambulance ride, A&E admission, diagnosed as adverse reaction at point of care. Two years post-onset at recording, still ill, working at ~45% function via her own experimental protocol.

Co-founded **UK CV Family** support group. Author of a book about her experience (referenced in episode but not titled in transcript).

## Notable views

- The vaccine-injured cohort is the most socially isolated subset of post-pandemic chronic illness.
- Honest acknowledgement of vaccine harms is the *responsible* pro-vaccine position.
- Three personal protocols she credits: therapeutic phlebotomy, high-water-content fruit/veg diet, high-copper diet (in trial).
- Acceptance over hope; "today" not "forever."
- Recovery as a project, symptom-by-symptom.
