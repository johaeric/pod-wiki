# Dr Adrian Baker

UK GP based in the north of Scotland (Inverness area) for 20+ years. Caught COVID early March 2020 and made a full but slow (~18 month) recovery. Lifelong cold-water swimmer / surfer; trained for an outdoor triathlon ~8 years before recording, which got him into year-round open-water swimming.

He is one of the few **recovered-LC clinicians** in the wiki so far who is also actively prescribing the lifestyle interventions (cold-water swimming) that helped him. His framing is gently first-principles, deeply patient-centred, and he is a useful counterweight to clinicians who've never experienced LC themselves.

## Positions

- **Recovery is real but slow.** ~18 months for him; costochondritis last to go.
- **Cold-water swimming is anti-inflammatory + microcirculation + endorphin + diving-reflex + social.** All five matter.
- **Gradual exposure** — 5–7 consecutive daily immersions to lose cold-shock; knee-deep counts.
- **Slow rewarming** — never hot shower immediately. Real injury risk.
- **Pacing + rest + self-kindness** is core LC management.
- **Community matters** — chronic illness recovery is harder in isolation; the social/tribe element of group swimming is a real intervention.
