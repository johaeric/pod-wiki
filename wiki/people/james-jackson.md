# Dr James Jackson

US neuropsychologist; ~20 years at Vanderbilt Medical Center, Nashville. Pre-pandemic studied cognitive outcomes of ICU survivors; pivoted into LC cognition when non-hospitalised LC patients turned out to have similar (sometimes more disabling) trajectories.

Author of *Clearing the Fog: From Surviving to Thriving with Long Covid — A Practical Guide* (US + UK editions).

Runs **5 weekly LC support groups (~100 patients/week)** at Vanderbilt — one of the wiki's most concrete clinical-side support-group models.

Open about his own 2018 OCD diagnosis; uses self-disclosure as a clinical tool to give patients permission to discuss mental-health struggles.
