# Sam Munslow

UK-based leadership and mental-health coach. Got covid 5 April 2020 (very early UK wave). Became severely ill — months bedbound — but recovered fully. Now coaches others through long covid alongside her broader leadership-coaching practice. Book *The Covid Coach* turns the lived diary into transferable coaching content.
