# Dr Becca Kennedy

Family medicine physician (US); 20+ years in practice. Founder of **[[resilience-healthcare]]**. Former **lead of the Kaiser Permanente long-COVID clinic** — assessed hundreds of LC patients. Trained in Howard Schubiner's neuroplastic-symptoms model after years of seeing chronic-symptom patients the standard medical model couldn't help. Developed her own chronic pain + fatigue *while doing this work* and worked through it using the same approach.

## Why she matters on the wiki

- Wiki's clearest **clinician-side articulation** of the neuroplastic-symptoms / mind-body / EAET model. Complements [[esther-rotterdam]] (#32 patient-side TMS), [[curable-app]] (Alan Gordon PRT route), [[sam-munslow]] (#34 coaching framing).

- Her framing of **Kaiser-LC-clinic experience** lends the model real-world LC-clinic credibility — distinct from "this works in chronic pain, maybe LC."
- **Howard Schubiner / Mark Lumley** lineage now anchored. Worth a separate page.
- The **damage vs changes** distinction is the cleanest reframe on the wiki for patients struggling with "feeling broken."
