Scottish NHS physiotherapist who developed POTS-pattern Long COVID in autumn 2021 (age 40). Recovered by autumn 2024 — her recovery overlapped with thyroid-cancer surgery that may have removed some symptoms (migraine, tinnitus) attributed to LC.

Now runs **Sage Health and Wellness**, a private bite-sized education and coaching offer covering pacing, baseline-finding, nutrition, sleep, breathing, and CBT-I. Left NHS the day before recording ep #182.

## Approach
- Low-histamine diet as her own first turning point
- Heavy use of breathwork and meditation, especially yoga nidra
- Treats movement, breathing, and nutrition as integrated rather than siloed
