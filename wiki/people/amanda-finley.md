# Amanda Finley

Archaeologist and former opera singer in Kansas City, Missouri. First-wave (6 March 2020); reinfected October 2020; suspected Delta third round summer 2021. Lost her home and is camping in the woods at recording. Founder/admin of the **Covid-19 Long-Haulers Discussion Group** (~14,000 members).

## Pre-covid

Anthropology/archaeology background; sang with the St Louis Symphony / Union Avenue Opera; worked extensively with the Kansas City homeless. Lifelong sleep disorder; alopecia (post-pregnancy, never resolved); asthma.

## Now

Cannot return to archaeology fieldwork (cannot walk uphill without near-syncope). Channels it into [[covid-19-long-haulers-discussion-group]] moderation and US-policy advocacy.
