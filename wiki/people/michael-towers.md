# Michael Towers

Australian comic-book creator. ~20 years of post-viral fatigue on/off; severe-bedridden phase of ~1 year. Created **Chronic Fatigue Superhero** during the early severe-bedridden phase and built it over five years (8-month gap mid-project where he couldn't lift his hand). Wife colours.
