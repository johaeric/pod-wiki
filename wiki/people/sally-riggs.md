# Dr Sally Riggs

UK-trained psychologist (CBT for psychosis specialism); private practice in Manhattan since 2015 (group practice with five other psychologists). Caught COVID 13 March 2020; never tested positive (PCR + antibody all negative). **Substantial recovery** by Feb 2022 (per #23); **"more or less recovered"** by #101 — working full-time, polyvagal-frame practitioner, has launched LC-specific 3-module e-course (sensitivities / strategies / support).

## Recovery framing

Multi-component, no silver bullet. Crediting (in roughly chronological order of helpfulness):
1. **Low-dose naltrexone** — from a chronic-fatigue / infectious-disease specialist; first inflection
2. **Zero-sugar keto** — game-changer for GI symptoms
3. **Safe and Sound Protocol** (Stephen Porges-derived listening intervention) — biggest cognitive/energetic uplift; began offering it to clients herself
4. **Polyvagal-theory framework** — let her stop blaming herself for not being able to "do acceptance"

Also: bloodwork showed EBV reactivation — antiviral trial unclear if useful.

What didn't help her: low-histamine diet (no_effect); stasis breathing (twice — abandoned, but blames spoon-budget conflict more than the intervention).
