# Dr Sarah Myhill

UK GP since 1981 with a long-standing heterodox approach to ME/CFS treatment. Books include *Diagnosis and Treatment of Chronic Fatigue Syndrome and Myalgic Encephalitis* (with Craig Robinson), *The Underactive Thyroid: Do It Yourself Because Your Doctor Won't*, and the Paleo-Keto Cookbook. Has been in regulatory dispute with the UK GMC historically. **The wiki should treat her position as a coherent heterodox-clinician framework — strong internal logic, weak external mainstream validation, frequently cited in patient recovery communities (especially [[114-sarah-lotus-path-yogi-recovery|Sarah / Lotus Path Yogi]]).**

## Framework summary

The car analogy:

1. Right fuel in the tank → paleo ketogenic diet + fix the upper fermenting gut
2. Oxygen → slow breathing (Buteyko) + Bohr-effect awareness
3. Mitochondrial engine → supplements + remove toxic load
4. Thyroid accelerator pedal → empirical replacement when blood-tests-normal-but-symptoms-clear
5. Adrenal gearbox → adrenal support
6. Inflammation control → addressing chronic infections (LDN, methylene blue, DMSO, photodynamic therapy)

## Wiki contribution

- Strongest single articulation of the **upper-fermenting-gut → MCAS** mechanism on the wiki. Counterpart to [[099-viola-sampson-microbiome]] (more cautious) and [[038-dr-tina-peers-mcas]] (mast-cell-first).
- Provides a coherent framework for **why mitochondrial-cofactor supplements should be sequenced after gut work** — bacteria in the upper gut compete for the same nutrients.
- Recommends specific cheap accessible diagnostic tools — **continuous glucose monitor + Acetrack ketone breath meter** — as patient-side feedback loops, regardless of dietary framework.
- The **Groundhog Protocol** (basic / acute / chronic levels) as a portable framework for "what to do all the time" + "what to do during infection" + "what to do for chronic disease."

## Caveats (wiki-flagged)

- **DIY thyroid replacement** is logged but the wiki does not endorse self-medication.
- **Methylene blue + DMSO + photodynamic therapy** as antimicrobial trio is logged but the wiki does not endorse self-administration without clinical oversight.
- **Lugol's iodine** has interactions with thyroid medication — flag for any patient on thyroid drugs.
