# Chrissi Kelly

UK-based founder of **AbScent**, the charity for people with smell loss and parosmia. Lost her own sense of smell in 2012; built a Facebook group in 2015 that grew into a charity registered May 2019; is now the leading patient-advocacy voice in the UK olfactory-dysfunction space, with ~85,000 members across her platforms post-pandemic (up from ~1,500 pre-pandemic).

Has appeared on multiple academic platforms; collaborates with rhinologists (Simon Gane is an AbScent trustee); produces smell-training resources, a Sniff app, instructional YouTube channel, and a covid-kids subgroup.
