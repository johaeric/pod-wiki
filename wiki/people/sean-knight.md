# Dr Sean Knight

Academic clinician — half clinical (respiratory medicine), half research (Lydia Becker Institute). Grew up in Africa; trained in the UK. Around late 2019 had taken time out of clinical work to set up his lab with Tracy Hussell — was sort of "in disbelief mode" through the early pandemic before pivoting fully to Covid research.

The CIRO study evolved into an LC study because some patients didn't recover, and the divergence in trajectory was the interesting signal — driven by something more nuanced than acute severity. B-cell affinity-maturation differences between recovery and LC groups are one of the threads he and Hussell are pursuing.
