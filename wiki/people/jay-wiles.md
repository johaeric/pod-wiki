# Dr Jay T Wiles

Clinical health and performance psychologist; board-certified in biofeedback and HRV biofeedback. Co-founder and CSO of [[hrv-biofeedback|Hanu Health]] (continuous HRV wearable + coaching). Did not develop long covid himself but treats many LC patients via the clinic.

His distinctive contribution to the wiki: HRV as a **directional guide** for autonomic adaptation, not a pathology score. Pairs hard with the wiki's existing dysautonomia + breathwork stack.

## Notable views

- HRV is only useful as a personal trend, never as a normative metric.
- The data motivates; the breathwork heals.
- Stuck-sympathetic state in LC is downstream-readable in HRV; can guide pacing decisions.
- PMS HRV-suppression has a clean physiological account — energy mobilisation for ovulation.
- Stress mitigation deserves equal billing with the other three lifestyle pillars.
