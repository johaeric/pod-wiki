# Dr Amy Proal

Microbiologist. **President + Chief Scientific Officer of [[polybio]]** (PolyBio Research Foundation, founded ~2018). **Director of the Long Covid Research Consortium**, a collaborative network of teams at Harvard Medical School, Stanford, Karolinska, UCSF (Henrich/Peluso), Penn, Mt Sinai, Johns Hopkins (Diane Griffin), and others. Active research collaborator with Mike Van Elzakker (Harvard, mitochondrial-hijacking paper) and Mt Sinai's David Putrino (clinical-translation collaboration).

PolyBio existed before COVID, focused on **infection-associated chronic illness** — ME/CFS, post-Lyme, post-Ebola. Knew from day one that COVID would produce a chronic-illness cohort because every well-studied pathogen has one.

## Why she matters on the wiki

- **The wiki's strongest single voice on viral persistence as a mechanistic hypothesis for LC.** Combines the research framing with the operational reality of doing tissue biopsy + PET imaging at scale across multiple academic sites.
- **The methodological argument that blood-test studies are looking in the wrong place** for persistence is hers — and it reframes how to read sceptic papers (e.g. [[robert-groysman]]'s blood-based scepticism in #92) that rely on circulating-virus measures.
- **The 676-day gut biopsy finding** is the wiki's longest single documented persistence interval.
- **The "spike protein in blood up to 16 months → must still be replicating" argument** is the wiki's clearest pro-replication evidence pointer.
- **The combination-antiviral design principle** (per HIV experience) is the most operationally important framing for future LC RCT design.

## Her position vs other framings on the wiki

- **vs [[dan-neuffer]] (#102)**: Dan would dissolve LC into PVFS / dysautonomia; Proal explicitly defends keeping the LC label for trial-design purposes (need SARS-CoV-2-onset cohort for antiviral trials). **The wiki holds both positions.**
- **vs [[robert-groysman]] (#92)**: Groysman is microclots-sceptical based on blood findings; Proal would say the relevant evidence is in *tissue*, not blood. (Microclot mechanism still compatible — spike protein from tissue reservoir seeds clots in blood.)
- **vs polyvagal/brain-retraining frames ([[sally-riggs]] #101, [[dan-neuffer]] #102)**: Proal's frame is upstream, theirs are downstream. Compatible — the vagal/autonomic dysregulation they target may be a downstream consequence of viral inflammation around the vagus nerve. **Frames operate at different levels.**
