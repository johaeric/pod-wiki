# Vikki Jones

US-based personal trainer, dietician, wellness coach, and former international athlete (modern pentathlon, triathlon). Long-haul covid since July 2020 (27 months at recording). Trained as a breathwork instructor under [[patrick-mckeown]] (Oxygen Advantage / Buteyko Method); adapted the protocols for the LC population because standard breathwork was making LC patients worse, including herself.

Co-founder of [[long-covid-breathing]] with [[jackie-baxter]].

## Position

Breathwork is a core lever for LC because it directly addresses the trauma that LC inflicts. The right breathwork protocol for LC is **scaled to the individual's Control Pause** — not the protocols that work for healthy or athletic populations.
