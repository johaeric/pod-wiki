# Moira Newiss

Nutritional therapist trained at the Institute for Optimum Nutrition, London (3-year course); BANT registered; Public Health Collaborative ambassador. Practices on Scotland's west coast near Oban. Specialism: helping professionals beat burnout, chronic fatigue, and long covid via nutrition + lifestyle. Implies own past experience ("I've been there") but does not name a recovery story.

## Framework she uses

Functional-medicine lens. Five LC mechanisms she watches for, all sharing **inflammation**:
1. Mast Cell Activation (MCAS)
2. Neuroinflammation (microglial priming)
3. Dysautonomia
4. Microvascular damage / microclots
5. Metabolic dysfunction
6. Mitochondrial dysfunction → cell danger response (Naviaux frame)

Plus gut microbiome / gut-brain axis / leaky gut, viral persistence, autoimmunity.

## Diet stance

- Anti-inflammatory whole-food, rainbow plate, healthy oils (olive, coconut, oily fish), nuts/seeds
- Low-carb / keto for blood sugar + mitochondrial flexibility (with medical oversight if medicated)
- Low-histamine for suspected MCAS — restrictive, supervised
- Mitochondrial co-factors: CoQ10, selenium, zinc, magnesium — food first
