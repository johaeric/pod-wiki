Assistant Professor of Neurology at Rutgers Robert Wood Johnson Medical School and at the Center for Healthy Aging Research. Previously at Harvard Medical School / Mass General Hospital, where she designed and led the [[niagen-lc-trial]].

## Notable work

- Principal investigator on the Niagen-for-LC pilot trial (open-access publication)
- Trial design adapted to retain power despite small sample (cross-over with placebo run-in; pooled-baseline post-hoc analysis when attrition reduced direct group n)

## Quote

> "We saw a 3-fold rise in NAD+ in 5 weeks, sustained through 20 weeks. Pooled analysis showed significant improvements in fatigue, sleep, and depression. We need larger trials, but it's a meaningful start."
