# Dan Neuffer

Australian recovered-ME/CFS patient (physicist by training); founder of [[ans-rewire]]. Ill ~6.5–7 years ("only accepted the ME/CFS label late"); descent into severe illness reached the point where "living with it as best as I could no longer seemed an option." Recovery took ~18 months once he had a working hypothesis. Wrote a book to share the hypothesis; eventually contacted by a top fibromyalgia researcher whose audience said "you sound like you're saying what Dan Neuffer says" — that contact catalysed the program, which launched 2016.

## Why he matters on the wiki

- The **dysautonomia-as-root-cause hypothesis** is one of the major synthesis frames the podcast holds. Dan articulates it most explicitly. Compatible with — but distinct from — the polyvagal frame ([[sally-riggs]]), the viral-persistence frame ([[amy-proal]]), and the microclots frame ([[douglas-kell]]).
- The **multilateral / somato-neurological approach** is the wiki's clearest articulation of why the brain-training/biomedical war is a false dichotomy.
- The **terminology counter-position** (PVFS over "long covid") is logged as Dan's framing without the wiki adopting it. Useful counterweight when reading other guests; pairs with [[ryan-prior]]'s opposing argument (#83).
- The **recovery-as-resilience** framing in #147/#148 is the wiki's strongest articulation of the post-recovery picture: not Nirvana, not symptom-free-in-cotton-wool, but a body that handles normal stressors without crashing.
- The **60–80% danger zone** observation is a wiki-relevant signal: many patients plateau here for years because pain-motivation has dropped below the threshold but they're not yet at full recovery. Pleasure-motivation (what would 100% feel like?) has to take over as the engine.
