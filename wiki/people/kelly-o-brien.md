# Kelly O'Brien

Mentioned by [[darren-brown]] in #51 as the **creator of the Episodic Disability Framework** and the **Episodic Disability Questionnaire** — both originating in her HIV rehabilitation research, both being actively adapted for long covid through a CIHR-funded multinational study. Has not been a guest herself; this page exists so that intervention-/theme-pages can link cleanly to her name.

She delivered a session on episodic disability at the Long Covid Physio International Forum (September 2022).
