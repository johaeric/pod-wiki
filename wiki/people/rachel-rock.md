# Rachel Rock

US-based freelance journalist (Boston). Approached LC the way she approaches stories — deep dives into every corner. Her recovery is one of the wiki's most explicit *frameworks*: ANS as the stool, four supporting legs.

## What's distinctive

- **Researcher-patient.** Watched Jaz Mediger early; read every Putrino paper out of Mount Sinai; brought specific protocols to local Boston physiotherapists. Used herself as a journalist source.
- **Pre-illness data baseline.** Apple Watch HRV from before COVID gave her objective evidence her body knew "off" before her conscious mind did. Useful pattern for other quantified-self LC patients.
- **Psychopharmacologist for ADHD meds.** Found a chronic-illness-aware specialist who let her return to ADHD medication late in recovery once her nervous system could handle work-related stress without destabilising — she names this as the final piece before going back to work.
- **Reading.** Cites James Nestor's *Breath* (epilogue), Ed Young's Atlantic article on chronic fatigue ("depression is sitting on the side of your life and not caring about getting back; chronic fatigue is sitting there desperate to get back and unable to").
