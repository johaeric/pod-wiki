Professor of Neurology at Harvard Medical School / Mass General Hospital. Directs the Genetics and Aging Research Unit (11 labs, primarily Alzheimer's pre-clinical research). Founding director of the McCance Center for Brain Health at MGH, which funds trials of natural-product / supplement interventions for neurological conditions.

## Conflict of interest

- Long-time consultant to **Niagen Biosciences** (manufacturer of NR/Niagen)
- Holds equity in the company
- For the Niagen LC trial, he was *not* involved in trial execution; involved in early conception/design and later interpretation, following Mass General COI rules

## Notable work

- Co-investigator on the Niagen-LC trial (#196) and Niagen-Alzheimer's trial
- Frame: cells are toys with batteries; mitochondria are the batteries; NR recharges them
- Strong proponent of natural-product-as-clinical-trial pipeline at McCance
