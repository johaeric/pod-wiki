# Amitava Banerjee

Cardiologist + Professor of Clinical Data Science at UCL. Trained in epidemiology and data science. Chief Investigator on STIMULATE-ICP, alongside Co-CI Melissa Heightman (UCLH LC clinic founder).

Trustee of [[long-covid-sos-uk]] — describes the conflict of interest as "a good kind."

## Notable views

- Strong PPI/E advocate: panel of 11, monthly meetings, integrated into all outputs.
- Holistic / "zoom out" cardiology approach — informed integrated-care-pathway framing of STIMULATE-ICP.
- Cautions against rushing to combination therapies without single-agent evidence (trial design / bleeding-risk argument).
- Believes patients/PPI co-investigators should lead, not just be consulted.
