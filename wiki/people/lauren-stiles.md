# Lauren Stiles

US lawyer-turned-researcher-turned-advocate. Co-founded **Dysautonomia International** ~2012 after a 2010 snowboarding-concussion-and-mild-infection episode triggered full-blown autonomic failure that took her two years and Cleveland Clinic to diagnose as Sjögren's-driven POTS. After **IVIG** treatment got her function back, she pivoted into the advocacy and research career she still has. She has a Research Assistant Professor of Neurology appointment at Stony Brook University despite no MD/PhD (background: marine biology + JD).

She got Covid in February 2020 and went on to develop a year-long worsening of her pre-existing POTS — i.e. she is "LC plus" pattern, not a fresh-LC patient.

## Wiki contribution

- Most comprehensive single source on the **autonomic-treatment toolkit** (#66) — salt + fluids dose targets, compression placement (abdominal not just calf), exercise framing, dietary recommendations, and the medication menu (beta blockers / [[ivabradine]] / [[midodrine]] / [[mestinon]] / antihistamines).
- **Dysautonomia International** (the org) funds **multiple LC research streams**: a forthcoming long-COVID-POTS IVIG immunotherapy trial, [[low-dose-naltrexone|LDN]] trial, ECP (enhanced external counterpulsation), and autoantibody discovery work.
- Publicly states the **"build a bigger tent"** framing — the post-infectious patient communities (LC, ME/CFS, MCAS, EDS, dysautonomia) need joint advocacy infrastructure.

## Eric-relevance

Stiles is the **second authoritative voice** in the wiki recommending Eric's exact stack architecture: ivabradine + mestinon + fludrocortisone + antihistamines + midodrine. (First is [[057-sanjay-gupta-pots-specialist|Gupta #57]].) Her position adds independent corroboration. The standout her-vs-Eric delta is **midodrine** (which Eric is not on) and the **8–10 g salt + 3 L fluids** target (which Eric should self-audit).

## Return appearance (2024-02, ep #115)

Stiles returns for a deep-dive on **LC dysautonomia research pipeline**. The wiki's most detailed map of current LC autonomic-research trials. Key updates from the 2 years between #66 and #115:

- **NIH funded an LC POTS IVIG trial + ivabradine arm** — the trial Stiles advocated for in #66 became real
- **VYVGART (efgartigimod, argenx)** moving from positive phase-2 to phase-3 in LC POTS
- **Walter Reed/DoD ivabradine for POTS+IST** trial added
- **Vagus nerve stimulation in POTS** — three Dysautonomia-Intl-funded trials with positive emerging results; Stiles uses VNS herself before bed for sleep + parasympathetic shift
- **Ganglia tissue collection in NIH RECOVER autopsy** — Stiles's advocacy succeeded; pathologists were trained mid-study to extract sympathetic ganglia
- **Lost access to her IVIG** when her doctor left clinical medicine for pharma; symptoms slightly worse but not crashed; **using VNS instead now**
- **Proteomics paper just published** — LC patients (with and without POTS criteria) show elevated inflammation + coagulation markers; supports **broader-than-POTS LC dysautonomia framing** that Peter Novak's Harvard IVIG trial also takes
