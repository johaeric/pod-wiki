UK; pre-illness extremely physically active 34-year-old (mountain biking, road cycling, snowboarding, surfing). 2013 onset triggered by a seizure-like neurological event (not infection-triggered). Symptoms dominated by sensory hypersensitivity (light, sound, vibration, chemicals) plus severe fatigue and brain fog.

## Diagnosis path

- ~2.5 years of self-treatment (yoga, tai chi, reiki, acupuncture, breathwork, mindfulness body scans, restoration-project mindset) before seeking diagnosis
- GP at year 2.5 confirmed ME/CFS
- Worst phase came post-diagnosis: ~1 year mostly in bed, ~2 years sofa/bed mixture

## Recovery framework

- **Mindfulness body scans** — entry point, foundational
- **Breathwork** — testing the edge of his window of tolerance
- **GET** (caveat: contentious; pre-2021 NICE update) — useful for him given his atypical presentation
- **Year in Portugal** — sunlight + geographic reset broke a plateau
- **Activity reframe** — question fatigue: am I actually depleted, or afraid to do this?

## Status

- Recovered ~2.5 years before this 2026 interview
- Wrote *Leaving ME Behind* about his journey (book recommended in episode)
