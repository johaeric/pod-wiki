# Iain

Musician and teacher, friend of [[jackie-baxter]] from music school. His partner **Cal** (non-binary, they/them) has lived with chronic fatigue and mental-health conditions for years. Iain caught covid Christmas 2020 but did not develop long covid. He's on the podcast not as a long-hauler but as an experienced **partner of someone with a chronic illness** — and his framing is among the cleanest carer-perspective material on the show.

## What he brings

- **Team framing.** Both partners are working — just on different things. Avoids the carer/patient slide.
- **Backup-plan reflex.** Plans collapse routinely; refuse the doom spiral by pre-loading alternatives.
- **Pre-arranged signals** for socially-difficult moments (hand-pinky-squeeze = get me out of here).
- **Concentric-circles support.** Patient at centre, partner ringed around, both have outer rings of friends.
- **Allow the rant.** Patients and partners both need to be allowed to say "this is awful" without immediate optimism.
- Direct anger at the **DWP / UK benefits system** as adversarial — "evil organisation designed to deprive people of what they need to survive." Practical advice: take someone with you to assessments.
