# Dr Lydia Knutson

US chiropractor in her 24th year of practice. Runs Lydian Chiropractic in Cambridge MA — a multi-doctor clinic a mile from Harvard, with what she describes as "an extremely educated patient base who keeps challenging us." Created the **[[axial-stability-method]]** technique. Teaches a six-day course for chiropractors on the LC adaptation.

## Why she matters on the wiki

- **First chiropractic-anchored LC protocol on the wiki.** Adjacent to but distinct from the Perrin Technique ([[raymond-perrin]]) — both target ANS rebalancing via low-force spinal work; ASM is more sequence-driven (parasympathetic → sympathetic → biomechanical) and uses Eastern Energy Medicine modalities; Perrin frames the same outcomes through glymphatic-drainage mechanism.
- **Hard outcome data from a small open-label series** with n=13 and 90% severe-symptom reduction in 18 visits. Self-funded by grateful patients; she explicitly calls for **outcomes research on low-harm modalities** that are not pharmaceutical.
- **Living-systems-return-to-blueprint** framing — a different vocabulary for the same operative move as the neuroplastic-symptoms model ([[becca-kennedy]], [[eleanor-stein]]).
