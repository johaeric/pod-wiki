US physician with surgical (ENT, skull-base neurosurgery) background who has practiced functional medicine for 20+ years. Cash-pay practice — does not take insurance, citing the time required for patient education. Hosts the *Coffee with Dr Stewart* podcast.

## Treatment philosophy

- Build the foundation (turn off inflammation) before tackling other layers
- Prefers older plant-derived medicines (1970s pharmacopoeia)
- Genetic testing as a "fridge inventory" — only put back what's missing
- Common tools: LDN at 3mg, vitamin D, thymosin α-1, amantadine, ivermectin, hydroxychloroquine, D-chiro-inositol, glutathione
