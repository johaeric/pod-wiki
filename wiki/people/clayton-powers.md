# Clayton Powers

US physical therapist (Utah) specialising in mecfs / POTS / LC. Originally trained in orthopaedic / vestibular / concussion PT; pre-covid pivot when an ME/CFS patient told him "no, that's not going to work for me" in response to graded exercise.

## Trajectory

Reached out to [[todd-davenport]] / [[workwell-foundation]] pre-covid to learn how to treat ME/CFS. Set up a Utah-PT journal club, joined Davenport's pots-exercise-research review. Connected with [[bateman-horne-center]] in Salt Lake City; now an active educator + outreach voice for both organisations. Publishes patient-education videos via Bateman Horne Center YouTube channel.

## Position on the wiki

Powers is the **clinic-side US complement** to Davenport's research/education work. Where Davenport anchors the PEM mechanism + 2-day-CPET methodology ([[141-todd-davenport-pem]]) and Brown anchors the World Physiotherapy advocacy line ([[047-darren-brown-long-covid-physio]] / [[051-darren-brown-disability]]), Powers anchors **PEM-aware in-clinic operational practice + PEM-recovery tactics + telehealth advocacy**.

His **recharge-faster strategies** for PEM crashes — cold packs, pneumatic compression, IV saline, supplemental O2 — extend the wiki's pacing-and-rest practice from prevention to active crash recovery.
