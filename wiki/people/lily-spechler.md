# Lily Spechler

US registered dietitian (centralised credentialing → can practise medical nutrition therapy in hospital settings) specialising in LC. Recovered her own LC and shifted her practice from general nutrition to LC after the experience.

Co-launched the **Energy Expansion Project** with Dr Meg Anderson, PT — a self-paced + group-coaching program combining autonomic rehab (modified Levine/CHOP protocol) with nutrition modules. Strong stance that pacing must not become inactivity once a patient is stable.

Her clinical-critique direction is what makes #129 distinctive on this wiki: she pushes back on widely-recommended LC protocols (low-histamine, keto, carnivore, blanket sodium) on the grounds of malnutrition risk and mechanism mismatch — without dismissing that they have a place when used appropriately.
