# Harlan Krumholz

US cardiologist; Director of CORE (Center for Outcomes Research and Evaluation) at Yale. Long career in clinical-research method-design + healthcare-policy work. Founder of [[hugo-health]] — startup giving patients agency over their own data.

In LC: co-leads the Yale **LISTEN** study and the **Pax LC** trial with [[akiko-iwasaki]] (Yale immunologist).
