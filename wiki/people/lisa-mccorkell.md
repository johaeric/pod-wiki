# Lisa McCorkell

US public-policy professional turned LC patient advocate. Co-founder + co-lead of [[patient-led-research-collaborative]]. Got Covid March 2020; co-founded PLRC April 2020 inside [[body-politic]] Slack. Testified at US Congress on LC. Lives in Oakland, California.

## Wiki contribution

- One of the four leads of PLRC (with [[gina-assaf]], [[hannah-davis]], Hannah Wei).
- Frames the **LC patient research project as a generalisable model for other disease areas** — patient engagement scorecards co-developed with the Council of Medical Specialty Societies are *cross-disease tools*, not LC-specific.
- Articulates the **policy-world parallel** to the patient-led-research argument: "people closest to the pain should be closest to the power" (Ayanna Pressley).
