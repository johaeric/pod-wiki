# Gina Short

US recovery-story guest. **Onset July 2021** (vaccinated; mild acute course; lingering shortness of breath that escalated to severe neuro-autonomic cascade by month 3-4 with internal tremors, bedbound, HR-spike on minimal exertion). **Declared recovery December 17, 2022** — ~17 months total.

Recovery arc: free Suzy Bolt yoga → Bolt's Fern programme → Stasis Breathing → pacing + radical inputs-strip + joy + declaration.

## Notable contributions to the wiki

- **Declaration as inflection point** — write the recovery, sign it, tell people. First wiki articulation of this technique.
- **Pre-planning crashes** — noticing one is rehearsing the future-crash is a route out of the cycle.
- **Designated-witness sentence** — son told to give a hug + say "you're getting better" daily.
