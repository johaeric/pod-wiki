# Bruce Patterson

US virologist, founder of IncellDx, developer of the **CDx / CCL5 / RANTES** biomarker panel that has become a reference Long-Covid testing protocol in the early years of the pandemic. Patterson advocates the **maraviroc + atorvastatin** combination targeting CCR5-driven monocyte persistence as a treatment-by-biomarker approach.

Patterson has not been a primary guest on the podcast yet. He is referenced repeatedly as **"a very good beginning"** for biomarker-based diagnostics by Joachim Gerlach (#41). Other episodes likely to mention him: any patient or clinician who discusses CCL5 panels or maraviroc/statin protocols.
