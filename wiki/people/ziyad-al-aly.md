# Dr Ziyad Al-Aly

Nephrologist + clinical epidemiologist at the VA St Louis Health Care System / Washington University. Big-data LC researcher; **2021 Nature paper** ("High-dimensional characterization of post-acute sequelae of COVID-19") drawn from millions of US veteran records was among the first formal evidence pieces on LC. Authored further studies on:
- Breakthrough infections + LC (n≈33,000)
- Multi-organ sequelae across organ systems
- Comparison vs influenza outcomes

## How he frames LC

- Multifaceted disease — not one thing
- Different sequelae cluster by demographic (cognitive in elderly; skin/hair in younger; new-onset diabetes even in 9–10 yr-olds with no risk factors)
- Viral persistence credible based on autopsy preprints (NIH n=44 cohort showing virus in brain/lung/kidney/liver months post-infection)
- Patient advocacy as an under-credited driver of the research field
