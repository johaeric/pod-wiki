# Becky Sharpe

London UK acting coach. 7 years of escalating fatigue + back pain + IBS + joint pains pre-Covid. Covid → ~3 years severe housebound/bedbound LC. Full recovery driven primarily by reading Dr John Sarno's *The Mindbody Prescription* (TMS / [[tms-mind-body-syndrome]]) — went 30% → 80% recovered in 3 days. Final 20% via emotional-feeling work. Now coaches LC patients alongside acting coaching.
