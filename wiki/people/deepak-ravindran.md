# Dr Deepak Ravindran

UK consultant in pain medicine, with 20 years' specialism in chronic pain. Lead for the **Berkshire Long Covid Integrated Service** since 2020 (~1,800 LC patients seen in 18 months). Brings deep prior expertise from fibromyalgia, ME/CFS, EDS, and chronic pain populations into his LC service — explicitly leverages those parallels.

Author of *The Pain-Free Mindset* (Vermillion, 2021) — book on chronic pain self-management.

## Position

LC pain is **dominantly nociplastic** — the third pain category formalized by IASP in 2017–18: central sensitization with low-grade neuroinflammation. It does not respond well to drugs or injections; it responds to systematic immune + nervous-system calming (sleep, nutrition, mind–body therapies, gentle movement, TENS, cold water, hydrotherapy).

He is unusually willing to pragmatically fund and recommend **non-evidence-RCT interventions** when they're low-cost, low-harm, and patient-centred (e.g. hydrotherapy + movement classes via charity funding).
