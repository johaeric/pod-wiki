# Hadas Golan

Speech-language pathologist at Boston University Medical Center's ENT department, specialising in upper-airway / voice disorders / vocal-cord dysfunction. Her own lifelong asthma was healed by Buteyko early in her career — she became a Buteyko trainer, then trained with Rosalba Courtney in IBT during the pandemic, and has run the LC breathing arm of her clinic since 2020.

Co-author with Courtney + Wolfberg on a 2024 case-series paper in LC (5 consecutive patients, before/after a 6-week IBT-derived protocol). Now sees ~80 LC patients ongoing.

## What works in her clinic

- **Structured assessment** (capnometry + Nijmegen / SEBQ / Dyspnea Index questionnaires + manual assessment of respiratory motion).
- **Group classes** (1 hr × 6 weeks Zoom) + individual personalisation sessions.
- **Order matters**: nasal function → mind-body / posture / awareness → diaphragm → hyperinflation/hyperventilation → cadence breathing → speech & physical pacing.
- **Slowed-down energy** in delivery itself — over-stimulating a fatigued LC patient with high practitioner energy backfires.

She emphasises that for many patients, completing the 6-week course twice (or moving to her advanced course) is when benefit consolidates.
