Software engineer in Seattle. Got COVID for the second time in February 2023 (mild symptoms with strong leg-specific pain on each fever spike) and developed Long COVID. Misdiagnosed initially as strep throat — took antibiotics for 2–3 days before COVID was confirmed, missing the Paxlovid window.

Recovery layered: functional-medicine doctor + IV nutritional therapy → physical therapy 3x/week (the first real progress) → quitting her corporate job → SSRI (the step-change).

Married in August 2024 dancing at her own wedding, honeymooned in Scandinavia, hiked 7-mile trails and walked 15k steps/day in Japan in May 2026 with no PEM crash. Started identifying as "no longer sick" ~6 weeks before recording.
