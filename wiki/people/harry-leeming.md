# Harry Leeming

UK-based founder/CEO of Visible Health, an LC/ME/chronic-illness measurement and management app. Background: Formula 1 engineer, then SF early-stage tech, then long-covid patient from September 2020.

Distinct contributions to the wiki:
- **Digital biomarker** as a strategic frame — measure to legitimise, then to treat.
- **No-screen wearable** as a deliberate design decision against hypervigilance.
- **Lived-experience-by-default** company building.
