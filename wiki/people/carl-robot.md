# Carl Robot

UK secondary-school English teacher (Leeds). Former Head of PE in Bristol; near-20-year career in PE before switching subject. Active life pre-illness — football, running, cycling, swimming. Got LC after a mild COVID infection summer 2020 that emerged through autumn into 2021 with breathing difficulties, dramatic weight gain, swollen abdomen, and an eventual A&E crisis on the last day of school year 2021.

## What's distinctive

- **Long diagnostic limbo.** Cardiac and lung tests negative; lung-function testing wasn't running due to COVID precautions; took almost 18 months between A&E and his Leeds LC clinic referral.
- **Probiotic-trial inflection.** Sheffield Hallam SymProve trial was the wiki's strongest single-patient gut-microbiome recovery row. Cognitive baseline put him in bottom 7% nationally; 3 months on the active arm reversed it. After the trial, he switched to homemade kefir to maintain.
- **Athletic-marker recovery.** Couch-to-marathon programme used as a deliberate "I'm not going back" punctuation. Reflective about graded exercise: it worked because he had laid every other piece of his recovery first.
- **Outstanding employer.** Repeatedly cited his head teacher who told him "do whatever it takes" without a name for his diagnosis — a counterweight to the bad-employer pattern that often appears on this podcast.
