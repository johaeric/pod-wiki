# Raelan Agle

Canadian recovered ME/CFS patient (10 years ill; ~4 years fully recovered at recording, ~2019 onward). Mother had CFS from when Raelan was 5; lifelong; mother died of it. Trigger: severe flu within a year of mother's death; never recovered until self-built recovery program ~2019. Lived 8 years in Southeast Asia during illness arc; lived in five countries post-recovery.

## Appearances and host role

- **As guest on LCP**: [[107-raelan-agle-recovery-stories]] — recovery story + meta-observations from 100+ recovery interviews + her advice to patients early in their journey.
- **As host of her own channel** (`raelan-agle` source): every Raelan ingest in this corpus is one of her interviews. Her own stretching videos and book are credited as recovery inflections by some of her guests (first instance: Chimére, [[RA-0076-chimere]]).

## Why she matters on the wiki

- **Single biggest meta-source for recovery patterns** in the ME/CFS and LC space, via her YouTube channel (booked 6 months out for new interviews at recording). The wiki should treat her three-themes synthesis as the highest-evidence summary of recovery commonalities currently available.
- **9th full recovery on the wiki** — but unlike most, indexed primarily as a *meta-observer* rather than a unique recovery template.
- **Scaffolding-first principle for graded movement** — operationally critical re-frame of "graded exercise therapy doesn't work."
- **Hope-as-input** — the wiki's clearest articulation of why staying in the recovery-story stream is a recovery input rather than wishful thinking.

## Her three universal themes (anchor synthesis)

1. Nervous system / fear-response (everyone, named or unnamed)
2. Brain training / neural pathways (most)
3. Physical mechanism work (some, not all)

Compatible with: polyvagal ([[sally-riggs]] #101), dysautonomia / ANS Rewire ([[dan-neuffer]] #102). In tension with: viral-persistence-as-cause ([[amy-proal]] #103), mould-as-major-mechanism ([[taylor-krick]] #106) — both of which would say "tests came back normal because the wrong tests were run."

The wiki holds both positions and lets the patient decide.
