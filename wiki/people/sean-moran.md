# Sean Moran

Australian (Melbourne) recovered ME/CFS patient + coach. Got sick in late 2011 / early 2012 in his early 20s while working a demanding legal job, was bedbound for months, never returned to baseline; the next decade was a slow climb to ~50% function and a relapse during covid-era WFH mould exposure that became the catalyst for proper recovery.
