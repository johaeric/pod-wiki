# Amy Anker

Stained-glass artist, ~23 years in business, Rocky Mountains, Montana. Pre-LC: very active — hiked, fished, gym 3×/week (husband is a personal trainer). Caught covid early November 2020. **Fully recovered** by late 2021 / early 2022.

⚠️ **Not Dr Amy Small** (ep #5). Different person, different country, different story. The dr-amy-small.md "see also" cross-ref to ep #20 was a previous-indexer error and should be corrected.

## Recovery summary

Linear-ish over ~14 months. ~6 months in, felt much better. Around month 11, valley-filled-with-wildfire-smoke caused a major relapse. Last few months: identified a "looping pattern" in her nervous system (anticipatory pain → produced pain), interrupted it, finished recovery.

## What she credits

- **Neuroplasticity framing** — was reading Norman Doidge's *The Brain That Changes Itself* at infection
- Daily four-pillar routine: positive mindset · real rest · nourishment · walking
- Mindful interruption of the symptom loop (mind-body, NS regulation)
- Avoiding doom-scrolling LC support groups

## Built post-recovery

[[positively-covid]] — free site with meditations she wrote during illness, recovery stories, daily-routine framing. Founded specifically because **she could not find recovery stories** — recovered patients had moved on, leaving support groups dominated by still-suffering members. She made a point of staying public.
