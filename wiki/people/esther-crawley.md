UK pediatrician specialising in paediatric ME/CFS and chronic fatigue. Bristol Medical School. Has led one of the most respected paediatric ME/CFS clinics in the UK (the Bath/Bristol service, recognised as a national gold-standard pathway).

## Notable contribution to this wiki

- Claire (#208) cites that early referral to her clinic was the foundation of her son's eventual recovery
- Provided the pacing / activity-management diary system that took the son from 0–40% baseline over 18 months — necessary foundation before ANS Rewire could lift him further

## Caveat

- Her past involvement in pediatric ME/CFS research has had its share of public controversy. Position on the wiki: clinical work has helped patients per direct testimony.
