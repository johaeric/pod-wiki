# Howard Schubiner

US clinician, mind-body-medicine specialist; lineage descends from John Sarno (TMS). Co-creator with Mark Lumley of **Emotional Awareness and Expression Therapy (EAET)** — a structured therapy for emotional disclosure of repressed anger and other stored emotions in patients with chronic-pain / neuroplastic-symptom presentations. Not yet a podcast guest; named substantively by [[becca-kennedy]] (#146) and earlier by [[esther-rotterdam]] (#32) as part of the Sarno → Schubiner → Sachs lineage.

- [[166-rebecca-tolin-not-broken]] — Tolin's 13-year ME/CFS recovery via the Sarno/Schubiner/Gordon lineage; she trained in this model and now coaches in it.

## Why he matters on the wiki

- Schubiner is the clinician most often referenced *across* multiple guests on the podcast as the source of the modern neuroplastic-symptoms framework. EAET is the most-named structured therapy in this lineage.
- His work is the bridge between **TMS / John Sarno** (1980s, often dismissed) and the modern neuroplastic / curable-app PRT family. Naming him explicitly anchors that lineage.
