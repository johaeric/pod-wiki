# Daniel Gomez

Engineer-turned-MR-physics postdoc at Massachusetts General Hospital + MIT. Co-leads the LC fatigue fMRI imaging study with [[ewa-beldzik]]. Background: high-resolution functional MRI methods + acquisition development for clinical populations. Studies blood-oxygenation, blood-flow, and neural-vs-vasculature signal separation in fMRI.

## Wiki contribution

- The wiki's **first detailed mechanism-targeted neuroimaging study** for LC fatigue specifically (rather than cognitive symptoms broadly).
- Articulates the **breath-hold-as-vascular-control** technique — letting fMRI separate neural-activation from physiological-perfusion signal.
- Provides the wiki's clearest formulation of the **tests-should-exclude-not-identify** principle (research-design version).
