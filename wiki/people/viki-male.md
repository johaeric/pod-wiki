# Dr Viki Male

UK reproductive immunologist at Imperial. Studies the immune-cell populations in the uterine lining that support pregnancy implantation — pulled into pandemic-era research first by vaccine-period misinformation (showing ~1% have late periods, ~4% heavier — both back-to-normal next cycle), then into LC-period research by [[gez-medinger]] reaching out.

Co-leads two parallel strands of LC-menstruation research with Imperial colleague Danny Altmann:
- **Visible-app collaboration** (large-N, low-burden symptom pattern study).
- **[[wilco-imperial]] substudy** (N=30 deep immune phenotyping across one cycle).
