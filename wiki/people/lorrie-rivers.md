# Lorrie Rivers

A 20-year ME/CFS recovery coach, herself recovered from severe ME/CFS in her 20s (~8 years severe + 2 years recovery), then **fully recovered from Long Covid in ~8 months** in 2020, and then **fully recovered from lifelong "weather-dependent fibromyalgia"** ~2021–2022 by treating what she now believes is the root cause: **hidden parasitic and fungal infections** in the gut, nervous system, and other organs.

## Wiki contribution

- Articulates a **strong, controversial root-cause theory** of all chronic illness as gut dysbiosis driven by hidden parasites/fungi.
- Reports a clinical cohort (her clients) where **strict elimination diet → anti-parasitic protocol → recovery** has produced full recoveries (her self-report — no peer-reviewed publication).
- Names **Dr Simon Yu, Dr Alex Kingart, and Kelly Rivera** as the clinician sources of her protocol.
- The softer components of her protocol (low-carb eating, gut-axis attention, slow detox titration, herxheimer monitoring) overlap with mainstream ME/CFS / functional-medicine guidance.

> ⚠️ **Wiki position**: this is a **minority claim** without peer-reviewed support for the strong "parasites cause ME/CFS" framing. The patient-passes-worms-after-treatment phenomenon is anecdotal and confounded — de-worming agents can drive normal gut flora to mass-egest, dietary change alone can produce visible egesta. Logged because Lorrie is an experienced coach with a recovered cohort (anecdotal); **not endorsed**.
