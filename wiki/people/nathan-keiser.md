Doctor of Chiropractic Neurology with 17 years of practice. Came from concussion / TBI rehab background and pivoted into LC, POTS, ME/CFS as the demographic shifted. Sees people on 2-week intensive visits.

## Approach

- **Mechanism over syndrome** — find what specifically is failing, target with right intervention dose, push to edge of capacity, recover, repeat
- Uses **Doppler ultrasound** of middle cerebral arteries to measure cerebral perfusion changes (especially with tilt)
- Targeted neuroplastic micro-drills (vestibular, ocular-motor, etc.) with self-assessment markers
- "Push the bottleneck, not the whole body"

## Notable cautions

- Tools (VNS, breathwork, cold exposure) are tests that give feedback. If a tool isn't working, it's not your tool — find the actual bottleneck.
- Many people have been harmed by VNS done at the wrong dose or for the wrong mechanism
- Hypocapnia from over-breathing is widespread and missed
