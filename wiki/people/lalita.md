# Lalita

Australian neuroplasticity coach based in Tasmania. **Recovered patient** — twice. Pre-COVID: lupus, fibromyalgia, chronic fatigue, chronic pain. ~14 years well before her second LC bout in August 2024. Recovered the second time in **12–14 months** using essentially the same nervous-system framework she'd used the first time, refined through her Schubiner training.

Trained directly with **[[howard-schubiner]]** and his team in the EAET / neuroplastic-symptoms methodology — currently practising as a coach.

- **Practitioner + recovered patient overlap**: rare to get both perspectives clearly articulated in one episode.
