# Roberto Escobar

Registered nurse from Queens NY. Worked first COVID unit at his hospital March 2020 (severe occupational trauma). Got Covid himself Dec 2020/Jan 2021 — hospitalized 2 weeks, almost ventilated. Followed by 1.5 years of severe LC + PTSD. Full recovery in 2.5–3 months once he discovered polyvagal-theory framing for breathing/yoga + a self-compassion mindset shift. EMDR resolved his PTSD layer separately.
