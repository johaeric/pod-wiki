Internal medicine specialist with 40+ years working with post-infectious syndromes and complex chronic illness. Author of *Power Healing* (1996) and the freely-available **Long COVID Prevention and Treatment** document (drgalland.com — ~90 pages, AI-augmented, tiered for cognitive load).

## Framework

Patient-as-individual approach: triggers, mediators, antecedents (TMA model). The **Web of Long COVID** is his organising visual: 10 interconnected strands, with ACE2 and mitochondrial damage at centre.
