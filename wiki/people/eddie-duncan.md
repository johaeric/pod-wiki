# Professor Eddie Duncan

Professor of Applied Health Research at the University of Stirling, Chief Scientist Office NMAHP Research Unit. Occupational therapist by training, ~20 years in applied health research. Pre-COVID work focused on Scottish Ambulance Service and ambulance-services research; pivoted to LC rehabilitation when CSO funding opened.

Co-PI on **[[loco-rise]]** with Prof Kay Cooper (RGU, physio).

## Framing

- Strong PPI ethos — three lived-experience panellists meet his team monthly: Fiona Smyth, Allison Dewar, [[jane-ormerod]]
- Broad team across statistics (Paul Swinton, RGU), respiratory clinical academia (James Chalmers, Dundee), and OT/physio expertise across boards
- Cross-conditions perspective — explicitly says LC research findings should also benefit ME/CFS and other chronic illness pathways
