# Gez Medinger

UK-based filmmaker turned LC patient-advocate / science-communicator. Caught COVID March 2020 in London (untested), never recovered. Marathon-runner pre-illness; previously had post-glandular-fever illness for ~1 year in 2000 — that history gave him a faster "this is post-viral" frame than most.

Runs a Long Covid YouTube channel (~80 films at recording date) interviewing clinicians, researchers, and other patients. Co-author of **The Long Covid Handbook** (Penguin, late 2022) with Prof [[danny-altmann]] of Imperial College London — pitched as the consolidated public resource for LC patients, drawing together what's currently scattered across the internet.

## Recovery status

Not recovered. MCAS-dominant in 2020, less so now. Dysautonomia without classic POTS. Cognitive function vulnerable to live-conversation processing (Zoom, phone, in-person dialogue) more than to passive media (TV, driving). Has to rest before/after recordings.

## What he uses

- Antihistamines + low-histamine diet (per [[tina-peers]] #38 — credited as breakthrough)
- Dysautonomia management (pacing, salt, fluids, breathwork)
- NAD⁺/niacin (B3) maintenance — via Dr Bindi Venel (~20% energy boost)
- Probiotics
- Vitamin C, D (no specific outcome claimed)

## What didn't work / what harmed

- Tilt test crashed him for 6 weeks
- Most supplement experiments — no clear effect
