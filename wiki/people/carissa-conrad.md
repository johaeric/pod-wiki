Doctor of Physical Therapy and therapeutic-pain specialist. Got LC after a May 2022 COVID infection; recovered using vagus-nerve stimulation (VNS) via TENS as her primary tool, alongside low-dose LDN and a low-dose SSRI. Now coaches LC/CFS recovery internationally with VNS as the primary modality. Sells preconfigured TENS kits with her recovery protocol via carissadpt.com.

## Method

- Auricular tVNS via tunable TENS units
- Specific parameters (mode, pulse rate, pulse width) — *not* the out-of-box presets
- Newer ultrasound-VNS option for those who tolerate it / can afford it
- Pairs VNS with breath work (5.5-second paced breathing has research support) and pacing

## Background

- Pre-illness: small children at home; severely ill enough that LC put recovery into "mom of two unable to function" territory
- 8 months from couch to 5 hours/week working
- Now teaches international clients
