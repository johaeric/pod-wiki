# Pamela Rose

Diagnosed with ME/CFS in 2010, recovered over ~7 years, transitioned out of banking to coach others with fatigue conditions. May have been the first to use the "fatigue coach" title.

Her coaching mix: pacing / energy management; emotional coping; mindset; nervous-system calming; everyday problem-solving. Cross-condition rather than LC-specific. Episode #121 is anchored on the *communication* dimension of chronic illness — the conversational situations that quietly drain energy and how to reduce that load.

Quietly states the same thing a lot of recovery-coach guests do: doesn't think of these as positives that came out of the illness, but the wisdom transfers — *"difficult conversations don't stop happening just because the illness stopped."*
