# Dr Amir Hadanny

Israeli physician, dual-qualified: neurosurgery training (initial residency) + hyperbaric-medicine specialist since 2008. Bioinformatics PhD focused on AI/ML modelling of treatment outcomes. Currently practising across Aviv Clinics in Tel Aviv, Florida (US), and Dubai.

## Why he matters on the wiki

- **Pairs with [[masha-makeeva]] ([[096-dr-masha-makeeva-hbot]]) to give the wiki two complementary anchor voices on HBOT for LC**: clinician-and-patient experience (Makeeva) + RCT science + sham-controlled methodology + 18-month sustained gains (Hadanny).
- The **Aviv RCT** is the single strongest single-paper evidence base for HBOT in LC discussed on this podcast — three publications + larger retrospective LC cohort paper in preparation.
- **Imaging-first patient selection** is wiki-distinctive: SPECT scan to confirm the orbital-frontal / temporal-pole / hippocampal metabolic deficit signature before treating. Defines a **subgroup hypothesis for HBOT response** — patients without the signature are not predicted to benefit.
