# Kelly Mitchell

Specialist respiratory physiotherapist. NHS-trained 2007, hospital trust through 2020 (CF, bronchiectasis, COPD). Left in 2020 to specialise privately in **breathing-pattern disorder**, including a substantial long-covid caseload. One of the most physiologically-grounded breathing voices on the podcast.

## What she brings

- A clear **hyperventilation → low-CO₂ → systemic** model of LC symptoms
- Cited paper finding **74% of LC patients had low end-tidal CO₂**
- First-line advice: **nose-breathe, slow to 8–12/min, breathe diaphragmatically**
- Cohort overlap insight: women, asthmatics, voice-professionals, athletes — same demographics over-represented in both breathing-pattern disorder and long covid
- Explicit caution against HR-pacing in the dysautonomic + over-breathing subgroup
