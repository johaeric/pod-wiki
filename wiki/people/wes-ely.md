# Dr Wes Ely

Senior critical-care researcher at Vanderbilt; ~30-year career in acute brain dysfunction during critical illness. Author of *Every Deep-Drawn Breath* (Steinbeck reference); royalties fund social workers in his program who help LC and PICS survivors navigate jobs, housing, cognitive rehab.

The thread that runs through his work: **listen to the patient** (Osler aphorism, Vanderbilt-style). He insists on patient-investigators (Hannah Davis, Jaime Seltzer, Lisa McCorkell) on RVLC and explicitly credits patient feedback for shifting his earlier PICS misframing.

## Eric-relevance

- RVLC is the most patient-relevant LC trial in the NIH pipeline. North-American-only initially; if positive, off-label baricitinib use will follow quickly.
- Ely is a credible, heavyweight independent voice on LC. Track his future communication.
