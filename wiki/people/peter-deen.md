# Peter Deen

Dutch professor (now ex-Radboud) of water-homeostasis biology, originally a researcher on **nephrogenic diabetes insipidus** (the opposite problem — losing too much water due to kidney-receptor failure). Read Patrick Ussher's Health Rising blog, recognised the topic as his lifelong field, and reached out to collaborate.

Now founder of [[stress-for-health]] — a med-tech startup built around a clinical-diagnosis tool + a 24/7 inflammatory-marker wearable for ME/CFS / LC. Sensor design in active development; specifics under IP. Ambition is to give patients real-time pacing data of the kind glucose CGMs give to diabetics.
