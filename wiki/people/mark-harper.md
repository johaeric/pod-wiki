# Dr Mark Harper

UK-and-Norway-based consultant anaesthetist who pivoted his research from preventing perioperative hypothermia to deliberately exposing people to cold for therapeutic adaptation. Author of *Chill*. Runs **ChillUK** confidence-and-fun introduction programmes (8 swims / 8 weeks, 60% continuation rate).

## Notable views

- The same stress-response physiology that complicates surgery responds to cold-water adaptation — modulating it should reduce surgical complications and chronic-inflammation symptoms alike.
- LC's stuck sympathetic overdrive is precisely the pattern cold-adaptation attenuates.
- Cold-water mental-health benefit is partly anti-inflammatory, partly Jill-Bolte-Taylor-style left-brain-suspension.
