# Dr Gavin Francis

GP in Edinburgh + locum GP in Orkney. Author of multiple medical/travel books; on this podcast for ***Recovery* (2022)** and ***Intensive Care* (2021)** (a pandemic GP diary). The *Recovery* book is deliberately concise — designed to be readable inside the cognitive budget of LC patients.

## Framing he brings

- **Convalescence** as a lost concept worth recovering
- The doctor-as-gardener metaphor (vs the doctor-as-mechanic culture of post-1950s medicine)
- **Distinction:** acceptance-of-this-moment ≠ acceptance-of-being-this-way-forever
- Recovery as a dynamic balance, not a return to a prior static state
- Importance of belief / expectation / story-shape as inputs to the recovery process
- Snakes-and-ladders as a metaphor for the non-linear journey
