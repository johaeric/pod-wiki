# Chiara Berardelli

Singer-songwriter and former GP. Got ill the first week of UK lockdown (March 2020). Her dominant symptom is a debilitating recurring headache — the song *Tyrannosaurus* (named for the headache) plays out the episode. Lives alone — flags the special isolation of long covid through a pandemic for single people.

Brings a doctor's eye to her own care without being captured by it: skeptical of unhelpful diagnoses (the neurologist's "menopausal migraine"), clear that bloods being normal does not mean nothing is wrong, and explicit that medicine is poor at supporting things it can't fix.
