# Julie Black

Scottish science-advisory-role professional. Caught COVID early July 2022 from her partner; recovered ~3–4 months after starting Curable app. **10th full recovery on the wiki**, and the wiki's clearest single articulation of pain-reprocessing-therapy / mind-body recovery for LC.

Endurance horse-rider; back competing post-recovery (completed first 50km event two weeks before recording).
