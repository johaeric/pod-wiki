# Prof Louise Cummings

Professor of clinical linguistics at Hong Kong Polytechnic University; UK-trained speech-and-language therapist. Was in the UK during the first wave (March 2020) doing field research on neurodegenerative disorders; pivoted to LC cognitive-linguistic research after stranding in the country for 5 months and observing the brain-fog pattern in LC patients via Facebook groups.

## What she brings

- N=142 study with six matched groups (LC w/ brain fog, covid no symptoms, healthy, ESL ×2, ME/CFS).
- **Three robust LC deficits**: verbal recall, verbal fluency, discourse informativeness.
- Coined / popularised **"cognitive pacing"** on the podcast — concept that TV-and-scrolling are stimulating not restful for an LC brain.
- 2022 edited volume forthcoming: *Covid-19 in Speech-Language Pathology*.
- Six-month longitudinal follow-up planned with matched (not identical) materials.

## #122 follow-up & ongoing work

- 6-month follow-up of the original cohort (41 LC + 26 healthy controls, replacing 15 controls who had since had Covid). Most language-task improvement was spontaneous; verbal recall remained significantly impaired. Naming scores looked normal but with marked cognitive-struggle and effort-to-target.
- **Employment outcomes** added as a primary objective. Strongly negative picture; details on the episode page.
- Currently writing up an **international 4,432-adult survey** across 62 countries / 21 languages — looking for **brain-fog subtypes** and the statistical link from subtype to employment outcome.
