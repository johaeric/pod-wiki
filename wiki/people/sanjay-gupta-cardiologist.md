# Dr Sanjay Gupta (cardiologist, York)

UK cardiologist based in York. Originally a general cardiologist; spent ~5-6 years building a POTS / dysautonomia subspecialty practice on top of mainstream cardiology. Active YouTube channel (York Cardiology) — used as patient education infrastructure when traditional referral pathways fail.

**Note on disambiguation**: not the Indian-American CNN figure of the same name. This is a UK NHS+private cardiologist.

## Position summary

POTS, CFS, fibromyalgia, FND, LC are part of the same dysautonomia umbrella. Treat them as such. The four-pillar treatment is **lifestyle + physiotherapy + medications + advocacy** — done simultaneously, not sequentially. IV saline is the most transformative single intervention he has found.
