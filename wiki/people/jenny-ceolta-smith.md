# Dr Jenny Ceolta-Smith

Occupational therapist by training; specialism in vocational rehabilitation. Member of the Vocational Rehabilitation Association. Researcher on DWP employment-support programmes (managed a Condition Management Programme; later worked alongside DWP Work Coaches as a job-centre OT). **Has long covid since March 2020**; **lost her own job October 2021** (technically agreed-to-leave; eight-page formal complaint to her university went unanswered).

Lead voice on the **Long Covid Support Employment Group / @longcovidwork** advocacy, focused on staying-in-work, dismissal/resignation choice, and the UK benefits system (New Style ESA, PIP, Universal Credit, Permitted Work).
