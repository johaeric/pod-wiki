# Sarah (Lotus Path Yogi)

UK yoga teacher (Sheffield) and host of the **Long Covid Hope Podcast**. Caught Covid first wave (March 2020) on maternity leave. Full LC for ~2 years; declared recovery spring 2022. 11th full recovery on the wiki.

## Pre-illness context

- New mum to a baby; on UK 1-year maternity leave
- Yoga teacher; very active; Peak District hiker
- Worked in anatomy publishing alongside teaching

## Wiki contribution

- **D-ribose felt within 1-2 weeks** — the strongest single anecdote for [[ribose]] on the wiki to date.
- **Patient-side validation of [[118-dr-sarah-myhill]] protocol** — keto + B12 injections + mitochondrial supplements + fermented foods microbiome work.
- **Patient-side validation of [[107-raelan-agle-recovery-stories]] scaffolding-first graded resistance** — 1 minute body-weight resistance per day, increase weekly if no PEM. Built up to 15 min/day; then walks 1 → 3 → 5 → 7 miles with no PEM.
- **Articulates the wrong-yoga-vs-right-yoga problem** — LC patients trying generic vinyasa often crash; her style is seated/lying, no inversions, no fast flow.
- **Now teaches 400-500 students** via online classes (Mon Sheffield + Thu online + Fri Yoga for ME charity); "**zero patients reported PEM after one of her sessions.**"
