# Dr Liza DiLeo Thomas

US emergency physician, certified patient experience professional, and long-hauler. Teaches empathy and communication courses to clinicians; revising her own practice on the basis of being on the patient side of normal test results.

## Notable views

- Empathy is teachable — and trains clinicians to "believe" the patient, not just acknowledge.
- The "must be / sounds like" linguistic technique vs the reflex "I'm so sorry."
- Negative test results need different language; "everything's normal" is iatrogenic in chronic illness.
- Education for medical *students and residents* is the highest-leverage intervention; mid-career retraining is harder.
