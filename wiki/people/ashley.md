Got severely ill in early lockdown phase (March 2020). Bedbound for extended periods. Had positive lab tests for Lyme disease, mold, and multiple latent viruses → went down those treatment paths first. Years of alternative-medicine protocols brought her from "very severe" to "less severe but still bedbound" without finishing the job.

## Recovery framework

1. **Brain retraining** — initial 6 weeks gave a small but real shift
2. **Visualizations**
3. **Somatic therapy with a coach** — the inflection. Coach taught her to *listen to the body's language* rather than the mind's.
4. **EFT tapping** — for limiting beliefs
5. **Pacing within window of tolerance** — body as guide

## Status

- Recently married; planned the wedding and danced 5 hours; on her feet 12 hours that day
- Working out of the house
- Continues to recognise that recovery has more layers as time passes
