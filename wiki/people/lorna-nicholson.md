# Lorna Nicholson

Nurse background — A&E for many years, then ANP in primary care. **Co-founder of POTS UK (2010)** with Susan Whitfield; now stepped back from trustee role to information-production / website. Multi-modal practitioner now teaching:

- **Pilates** for POTS / LC / hypermobility — emphasis on muscle strength, joint range, balance/coordination, taking cardio out for POTS-friendly conditioning
- **Buteyko-trained** breathing instructor; also done Brad Cliff LC-specific training
- **SIRPA practitioner** — Stress-Induced Recovery Practitioner; mind-body / nervous-system framework that takes pre-COVID stress patterns and trauma history seriously

## How she frames LC

Same model across POTS, LC, ME: dysregulated autonomic + immune + mitochondrial + hormonal systems, all driven by chronic sympathetic overdrive whose origins are usually well upstream of the COVID infection (childhood patterns, personality types, in-utero or trans-generational stress).

Specific clinical tools she names:
- At-home stand test (HR + BP supine vs upright at 3/5/10 min) as a free, day-to-day data point
- Slow belly breathing + nose breathing (start when seated; only add to walking later)
- Cold water exposure / wild swimming (with safety caveats: not in winter; not alone; start with cold face splash / cold shower)
- Mindfulness, gratitude practice, sticky-note prompts
