# Keith Littlewood

UK practitioner with an unusual career arc: fitness industry → personal trainer → gym owner → rehab / pain science → MSc endocrinology (~2017) → PhD candidate at Reading studying environmental pollutants and thyroid physiology. Practices privately; significant client volume. Reads heavily across endocrine literature and is comfortable with **bioenergetic / Ray-Peat-adjacent** frameworks (anti-PUFA, pro-saturated-fat, pro-progesterone, pro-glucose-in-stress).

## Where his frame sits

Littlewood's framework is **heterodox** relative to mainstream LC commentary. It is not the same as mainstream functional medicine (Newiss-style), and it sits in **direct tension with the keto / low-carb framing** that several other podcast guests endorse. The wiki tracks both as live hypotheses; the truth value is downstream of the wiki — and clinical decisions remain with Eric's specialists.

He is **not a medical doctor**. He is a researcher with an endocrinology MSc and a private endocrine-nutrition practice.

## Positions

- **Long Covid is heterogeneous individual deficit, not a unified disease.** Variability in LC trajectories reflects pre-existing endocrine + nutritional + environmental load.
- **Thyroid under-diagnosed.** TSH is unreliable in stress; full panel + body temperature + pulse is more revealing.
- **Progesterone is central.** Antiviral, restores aerobic metabolism, deficient in PCOS / PMS / fibroids / endometriosis women and in older men.
- **Estrogen excess suppresses thyroid.** Environmental endocrine disruptors + endogenous estrogen accumulation.
- **Anti-PUFA, pro-saturated-fat.** Cardiolipin damage from oxidised PUFAs implicates seed/vegetable oils in LC.
- **Pro-glucose in acute stress.** Critical of low-carb/keto for the chronically ill.
- **Polypharmacy critique.** Sees clients on 15+ supplement stacks; first move is to weedle them down.

## Practical tools he uses

- Body temperature + resting heart rate (waking + post-meal)
- Bag breathing (CO₂ retention)
- Methylene blue
- Progesterone (where deficiency suspected)
- Vitamin A from organ meats; vitamin C (orange juice in his clinical anecdote)
- Six "markers of health": energy, digestion, sleep, libido, absence of pain, mood
