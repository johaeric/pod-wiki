# Rosalba Courtney

Australian osteopath who has worked on dysfunctional breathing for ~30 years, originally trained in Buteyko (chairperson of the Buteyko Australia association in the 1990s) before developing the broader **Integrative Breathing Therapy** model in her PhD work and clinical practice.

The three-dimension model is the key contribution: biomechanics + biochemistry + perception, treated independently and with structured measurement at each stage. Hadas Golan is one of her IBT graduates and the lead clinician on the 2024 LC case series.
