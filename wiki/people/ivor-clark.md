Edinburgh, Scotland; former Great-Britain American-football player and health coach. Got COVID + UTI in June 2023; long COVID for ~15 months until September 2024. Recovered fully. Wrote a book about his journey; 50% of profits go to **Compass charity** in Edinburgh, which provides HBOT outside the NHS.

## Recovery sequence

1. HBOT at Compass charity (28 sessions in 3 months) — catalyst
2. Pacing with heart-rate cap at 55% max HR
3. Breathwork
4. Visible app (later in illness)
5. Non-sleep deep rest with binaural beats during work breaks
6. Vagus-nerve work
7. Peptides — MOTS-c (mitochondrial), then immune-support peptides — the inflection
8. Methylene blue, urolithin A, rhodiola rosea — tried alongside
9. Cleaned diet (no alcohol, caffeine, overstimulating music)

## Background

- Pre-illness: athletic background (BG American football, mountain biking, etc.)
- Married, two stepdaughters
- Manager at workplace was extremely supportive on return; HR less so
- Took 6 months full-pay sick leave; financial pressure forced gradual return
