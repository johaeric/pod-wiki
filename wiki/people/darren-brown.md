# Darren Brown

UK clinical-academic physiotherapist working at NHS in London. Pre-pandemic specialism: HIV disability and rehabilitation, plus cancer care. Has long covid himself. **Founding chair of Long Covid Physio** (international physiotherapist association with LC, formed November 2020).

Brings a strong activism backbone to LC advocacy — explicitly references the HIV activist tradition (Peter Staley's "connecting to create change") and applies HIV-rehab disability-inclusion principles to LC events. The Long Covid Physio International Forum (Sept 2022) — 80 speakers, 17 countries, scholarship-funded for PWLC — is largely his project.

## Positions

- **Pacing IS rehabilitation.** Rehabilitation is not synonymous with progressive exercise.
- **Stability and maintenance** before improvement. Mitigate deterioration first.
- **Lived experience belongs at the table.** Long Covid Physio is patient-led; the Forum has 50%+ speakers living with LC, paid stipends.
- **Cross-org collaboration over silos.** Active partnerships with LC Support, LC Kids, LC SOS, LCNMUK, World Physiotherapy.
- **Disability-inclusive event design** is a campaigning issue, not just a courtesy.

## Episodes

- **#47** — Long Covid Physio overview, World Physiotherapy briefing paper, the video series, the September 2022 Forum.
- **#51** — disability frameworks (biomedical → social → ICF → **episodic disability**), Episodic Disability Questionnaire as a benefits-assessment tool, rehab redefined as education/equipment/pacing rather than exercise.
