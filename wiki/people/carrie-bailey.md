# Carrie Bailey

US functional nutritionist trained at the Institute for Integrative Nutrition (health coaching) → functional-medicine training → functional-nutrition program. Niche-shifted to LC after a YouTube video on LC symptoms went viral. Decade-plus practice running food-based cleanses; now LC-focused.

## Position on the wiki

Bailey sits in the **functional-nutrition / pathogen-and-toxin** lineage — adjacent to [[070-lorrie-rivers]] (parasites/dysbiosis as root cause) but more clinically grounded and less reductive. Her stack is OTC-supplement-based, distinct from Teitelbaum's branded SHINE products.

Her **vitamin-D scepticism**, **liver-fat-burden** thesis, and **food-combining** framing are minority positions on the wiki. Logged as such; not endorsed.
