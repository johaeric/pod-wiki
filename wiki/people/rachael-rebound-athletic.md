# Rachael (Rebound Athletic)

LC patient → recovered → reconditioning coach. Recovery sequence as she describes it:

1. *Stop working* (after 12+ months of trying to push through). First and biggest catalyst.
2. *Suzy Bolt's program* — community + first time meeting other LC patients.
3. *Curable app* — ~12 months of practice, broke the conditioned bed-rest pattern in 2–3 weeks of consistent application.
4. *Move from London flat to countryside.*
5. *Cold-water exposure* — second attempt, ~12 months in. (First attempt at 6 months was awful — strong "timing matters" data point.)

Now coaches LC clients through reconditioning. Designed her 12-week program over two months while still ill ("would have taken two days normally"). Pairs autonomic-resilience principles with strength training designed to avoid setback and injury.
