# Dr Calum Carson

Senior research associate at Lancaster University. Politics undergraduate → sociology MA (disability + Social Security stigmatisation) → PhD on the voluntary real living wage at Manchester business school → UN Geneva (decent-work agenda) → Manchester Metropolitan (Universal Credit impact on low-paid workers) → Lancaster (current). **Degenerative disc disease** (back issues) since PhD years; pandemic-onset home-working credited with keeping him in academic research.
