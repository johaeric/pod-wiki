# Michelle Irving

Australian coach for women with chronic illness; not a Long Covid patient herself but lives with two chronic conditions (autoimmune liver disease where her blood cells attack her liver cells, and a debilitating vertigo condition). Years in bed across both. Has built a coaching practice (programs, podcast *The Pajama Interviews*, free downloadable Emotional Empowerment Map + workbook) around the inner work she developed for herself. Increasingly works with Long Covid clients in the last few years.

- [[100-michelle-irving-emotional-empowerment]] — the framework, four stages, vertical/horizontal life model.

## What she brings to the wiki

The wiki is rich in *interventions* (LDN, mestinon, Gupta, breathwork, HBOT…) and *recovery stories* (Suzy Bolt, Jackie, Charlotte…). What Irving adds is a **shared vocabulary for the emotional substrate** those interventions and recoveries are sitting on:

- **Stage names** that other recovery stories can be tagged against (Suzy was at-sea then in the forest; Jackie's #94 is village-re-entry; Charlotte #98 is beach-to-forest with a long therapy spine).
- **The "vertical" language** that finally names why village-re-entry feels disorienting (Jackie and Charlotte both describe this without a label for it).
- **Permission to pity-party** — a counterweight to the "always-finding-the-positive" tone that creeps into recovery-story podcasts and can shame patients still in the at-sea phase.

## Specific recommendations (from this episode)

- *The Lady's Handbook for Her Mysterious Illness* — Sarah Marie Ramey (also flagged elsewhere in the wiki). Cited as someone who has moved through the *emotional* stages while remaining physically severe.
- The Map + workbook are free; programs are paid.
