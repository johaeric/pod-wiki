# Joachim Gerlach

German businessman; retired ~10 years ago after selling his company; relocated to Spain. Long-time private funder of organic-agriculture, ethnobotany, and biodiversity-restoration projects. Pivoted his scientific network to COVID-19 / Long-COVID intervention research in January 2020. **Founder of Vedicinals** (9-molecule nutraceutical formulation manufactured in India by a large GMP contract manufacturer) and **co-founder of the Long Covid Coalition**.

Gerlach is **not a clinician, researcher, or recovered patient** — he is an industry founder with an obvious commercial interest in Vedicinals-9. Treat his claims about Vedicinals-9 efficacy as `recommended (founder, biased)`.

## Positions

- **Multi-target, body-recognised molecules.** Argues nutraceuticals work *with* body homeostasis (assimilating to sites of need; doing context-dependent things like apoptosis-vs-repair) rather than blunt receptor-blockade.
- **Polypharmacy warning.** Long-haulers stacking ten supplements is itself a harm vector. Target categories (anti-inflammatory / anti-allergic / anti-pathogenic), don't duplicate.
- **Coalition over competition.** Argues no single company/expert can solve LC; the Coalition exists to map all pathways and tick which tool covers which — explicit invitation to apheresis, mechanical, and oxygen-therapy companies.
- **Politics-vs-science.** "Whenever politics meet science, science will lose." Strongly anti-political-framing of LC research.
