# Dr Jacob Teitelbaum

US-based MD, recovered post-viral CFS himself (was knocked out of medical school by it; spent time homeless). Author of *From Fatigued to Fantastic* (multiple editions). Practising integrative-medicine clinician with strong commercial-supplement engagement (Recovery Factors, Smart Energy System, HRG80 red ginseng).

## Position

LC is post-viral CFS. Treat with the **SHINE protocol** (Sleep, Hormones, Infections, Nutrition, Exercise) plus LDN, ANS retraining, and selective use of low-dose pharmaceuticals (Abilify, hydrocortisone, sex hormones).

Free resources: Vitality101.com (online quiz), fatigueDoc@gmail.com (4000-word LC article).

## Wiki framing

Teitelbaum embodies the **"treat everything at once"** end of the spectrum — explicit counterpoint to [[joachim-gerlach]], [[keith-littlewood]], [[gez-medinger]] who warn against polypharmacy. Both positions are now in the wiki under [[polypharmacy-warning]].

His commercial engagement with named supplements is a confounder for evaluating any specific recommendation; the underlying SHINE structure is more durable than any specific brand call-out.
