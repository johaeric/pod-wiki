# Gareth Williams

Composer / songwriter; primarily makes operas + music theatre; especially interested in expanding participation in the genre. Originally led the **2013 Breath Cycle pilot** at Scottish Opera with cystic-fibrosis patients (n≈18, 12 weeks, ~14 % lung-capacity uplift in the pilot cohort). Now leads the LC adaptation of **[[breath-cycle]]** with David Douglas, songwriter Martin O'Connor, and producer Lisa under head of education Jane Davidson.
