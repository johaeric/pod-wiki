# Dr David Clarke

US gastroenterologist + internal-medicine consultant (40+ years). President of **ATNS** (Association for the Treatment of Neuroplastic Symptoms). Career was redirected by his very first encounter with a patient who recovered from a serious physical illness in 3 months of weekly counselling — until then he had assumed nothing in mainstream medicine could explain it.

## What's distinctive

- **Estimates the size of the problem.** 20% of adults; 40% of GP visits. Most western-trained doctors don't know what to look for.
- **Treats this as biological, not psychological.** Brain circuits physically change; treatment shows them physically change back. Cites pain-perception research, including the BMJ construction-worker case where a nail passed cleanly between toes but produced agonising pain.
- **Big-T vs little-t trauma agnostic.** What matters is the magnitude exercise — imagine your child going through it — not whether the trauma "qualifies" as conventionally significant.
- **Resources he plugs**: symptomatic.me (questionnaire + 7-min documentary), his own *Story Behind the Symptoms* podcast, his teaching for clinicians.
