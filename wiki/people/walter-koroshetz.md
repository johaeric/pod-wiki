# Walter Koroshetz

Director of NINDS (National Institute of Neurological Disorders and Stroke) at NIH. Pre-pandemic worked on ME/CFS research with Avindra Nath. Lead administrator of the $1.15B Congressional **RECOVER** appropriation.

## Notable views

- LC is multifactorial; different mechanisms may operate in different patients.
- RECOVER is "the backstop should there not be a quick answer" — designed for the long haul (decades), not just the next 1-2 years.
- Two-track treatment strategy: disease-modifying *and* symptomatic master protocols in parallel.
- Cautions against single-cause framings.
