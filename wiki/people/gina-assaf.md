# Gina Assaf

US tech-research professional with background in participatory + human-centred design. Co-founder + co-lead of [[patient-led-research-collaborative]]. Lives in Washington DC. Self-reports ~70-90% recovered at recording (March 2020 onset).

## Wiki contribution

- Brings the **community-design lens** to PLRC's research priorities — how do you design a study so episodic patients can actually participate?
- Articulates the **rare-disease lineage** of patient-led research: long covid is not the originator of this approach; rare-disease communities have been doing it for decades. LC's contribution is *scale + visibility*.
