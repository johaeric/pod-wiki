# Jo Thomas

UK fatigue coach, NLP-trained, with a nursing background (still does 2 days a week in a specialist department). Got POTS / dysautonomia in 2017 after a tick bite; recovered in three layers (medical management → mindset & pacing → personal-driver work after a pandemic-era ICU burnout that resurged her symptoms).

## What's distinctive

- **Recovery perfection** — the central concept of her ep #172. She and Jackie both name the post-improvement trap of fearing every step forward and converting recovery practices into rigid rituals. Jo's coaching reframe: *your responsibility is the attempt; the body's response is not your responsibility.*
- **Believable mindset shifts.** When working with clients she gauges resistance non-verbally; if "I'll fully recover" lands as resistance, she dials it back to "I can improve" or another believable antidote. The nervous system reads premature mindset shifts as danger.
- **Symptoms as signal, not identity.** Now (1–2+ years symptom-free), occasional mild symptoms function as her early-warning system — something is "out of sync." Not feared.
