# Prof Chris Ponting

Lead investigator of [[decodeme|DecodeME]] — the world's largest GWAS of ME/CFS. Came to ME/CFS research from a fundamental-biology background after his friend Simon McGrath was devastated by the disease over decades. Argues for evidence-based, lived-experience-co-designed biomedical research as the route out of the prior CBT/GET paradigm.

## Wiki contribution

- PI of [[decodeme]] (#67).
- Articulates the **"we know almost nothing"** epistemic stance that underpins DecodeME's design — a launch pad for follow-on biomedical research.
- Endorses [[douglas-kell|Doug Kell]]'s microclot work as the most-replicated mechanistic finding to date and argues it should become part of the diagnostic toolkit.
