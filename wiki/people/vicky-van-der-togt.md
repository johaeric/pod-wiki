# Vicky van der Togt

LC patient (March 2020 onset, age 28) and communications-background advocate who founded the international network arm of [[research-aid-networks]] when she found no functioning international collaboration platform for LC advocacy.

The role she carved out for herself is **facilitator-translator**: she connects researchers with community groups, hosts the meetings where information flows in both directions, and explicitly tests scientific writing for lay accessibility before public release.

Co-authored the 2024 paper with Jeremy Rossman proposing **acid-base disregulation** as a central mediator across LC symptoms.
