# Dr David Saperstein

US neurologist who began as a neuromuscular specialist (small-fibre neuropathy) and over the last ~6 years has expanded into **autonomic dysfunction, MCAS, EDS-hypermobility, and post-COVID syndrome**. Notable for being one of the first US clinicians to publicly articulate the **integrated MCAS-dysautonomia-EDS picture** — patients walking into his clinic with all three conditions, not three separate ones.

## Wiki contribution

- Articulates the **trifecta / pentad / septad** clustering: hypermobility (EDS spectrum) + dysautonomia + MCAS (+ GI + immune + auto-immune + anxiety, depending on count).
- Argues that **post-Covid POTS may have a distinct presentation** from classic POTS — racing-HR-dominant rather than orthostatic-intolerance-dominant. He's not yet sure whether it's a true subtype or an acute-phase artefact (LC patients are seen earlier than classic POTS patients usually are).
- Detailed **MCAS antihistamine cocktail**: H1 twice daily at higher than OTC dose, H2 twice daily, plus mast-cell stabilisers (cromolyn, ketotifen, quercetin).
- Articulates the **mast-cell ↔ sympathetic feedback loop** as a treatment-implication: must treat both layers.
