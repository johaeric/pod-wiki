# Wibbs Coulson

Cardiac and A&E nurse (~13 years) turned yoga + breathwork teacher (~7 years). Hosts the *Move Breathe Live* podcast. Runs an online long-covid-specific breath/movement class — Jackie attended through autumn 2021 and tracks her own BOLT-score progress (6 sec → 24 sec) in [[017-wibbs-coulson-breathing]].

## What he brings

- A nurse's physiological framing applied to a yoga/breath practice
- Breathwork built on **CO₂ tolerance** (bolt score, CO₂ tolerance test, Patrick McKeown lineage)
- Yin yoga + yoga nidra for nervous-system down-regulation
- The Mount-Sinai pattern (LC patients shallow-mouth-breathe upper chest; goal is to shift down + slow)
- Clear patient-autonomy ethos: drop the pose, take child's pose, leave the class
