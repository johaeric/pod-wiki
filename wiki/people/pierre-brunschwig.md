MD, integrative internal-medicine physician. Founded **Helios Integrated Medical** in Boulder, Colorado, ~1992 (33+ years in practice). Has worked with **Margaret Hampton** (Doctor of Chinese Medicine) for 12+ years to develop a paired East-West LC treatment model.

## Approach

- Subtypes patients by mechanism (T-cell exhaustion, latent viruses, persistent SARS-CoV-2 antigen, mitochondrial, etc.)
- Goal: restore the patient to feeling like themselves, not eliminate every symptom
- Frame: LC is a traumatic event in the patient's life that calls for a holistic rather than broken-machine paradigm

## On treating latent virus reactivation

- Antivirals to push the balance back toward immune competence
- Then rebuild that competence so they don't have to stay on antivirals forever
- Tests for *H. pylori*, prebiotic + LC-specific probiotic protocols, stomach acid support
