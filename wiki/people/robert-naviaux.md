# Dr Robert Naviaux

UC San Diego clinician-scientist, originator of the **Cell Danger Response (CDR)** model — a unifying framework for mitochondrial behaviour during chronic illness. CDR describes how cells, under sustained stress, downregulate energy production and switch to a defensive metabolic state, producing the population-level symptoms of fatigue, brain fog, and post-exertional malaise. CDR has been adopted as a working model for ME/CFS and Long Covid pathophysiology.

Naviaux has not appeared as a podcast guest. He is referenced in [[moira-newiss]]'s second appearance (#44) as the originator of the framework she uses with her clients.
