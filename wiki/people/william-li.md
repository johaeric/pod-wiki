# William Li

US internal-medicine physician + vascular biologist. President / medical director of the **Angiogenesis Foundation**. Pre-pandemic, helped develop 40+ FDA-approved cancer therapies. **Led the early-pandemic NEJM paper showing direct coronavirus invasion of endothelial cells**, opening the modern microvascular-damage research line.

Author of *Eat to Beat Disease* and *Eat to Beat Your Diet* — connects food/lifestyle interventions to the same mechanisms.

## Notable views

- LC is best framed as the convergence of three persistent post-acute injuries: microvascular, autoimmune, chronic-inflammatory.
- Patients can be *empirically* helped now using existing treatments — pending formal trials.
- Diet (especially nitric-oxide-generating foods, anti-inflammatory diet) is a self-deployable lever.
- Optimistic: drug development for LC is "barely begun" but the toolkit exists.
