American by background, lived in UK ~20 years working as an economist in NHS population health management. Got COVID March 2020 (early UK wave). Reinfected after moving back to US — brain fog became severe enough to require board-presentation scripts to compensate. Took time off work, dove into research, formed nervous-system-centric hypothesis. Met **Andrea Heinberg** (Olympic biathlon competitor, personal trainer) at an outdoor exercise class. The two co-founded **Thrive90**.

## Recovery framework

- Vagus-nerve work + eye exercises + breathwork + pacing as the foundation
- Cognitive-motor coordination drills (added through Andrea's class) accelerated recovery
- Anti-inflammatory diet + reducing toxic-load at home (cleaning products)
- Sense of humour and ability to plan returned as recovery markers
