# Todd Davenport

Doctor of Physical Therapy and exercise scientist at the University of the Pacific (Stockton, CA). Scientific advisor to the [[workwell-foundation]] (the group that developed the 2-day CPET methodology under Stacy Stevens, Mark VanNess, and Christopher Snell). 25 years in PT practice; 15 years studying ME/CFS, latterly long-COVID-with-PEM as a major subset.

## Episode

- [[141-todd-davenport-pem]] — anchor PEM deep-dive. Plug-in hybrid analogy, 2-day CPET methodology, energy envelope, crash care plan.

## Why he matters on the wiki

- Davenport's group provides the **closest thing the wiki has to a published, reproducible biomarker for PEM** (2-day CPET) — even though it's not yet widely deployable.
- His framings are the cleanest articulation on the podcast of why **graded exercise harms PEM patients** while pacing helps. Useful counterweight to any guest who treats movement as monotherapy.
- Sources the wiki's energy-envelope language back to Leonard Jason; the plug-in hybrid analogy back to Chris Snell. Good lineage.
- Aligns with [[meg-anderson]]'s framing in #135/#136 — both source resting-HR/sub-threshold ceilings to Workwell. Anderson is the practitioner-applied version; Davenport is the research-physiology version.
