# Dr Jacqueline Maybin

Senior Clinical Research Fellow + Consultant Gynaecologist, MRC Centre for Reproductive Health, University of Edinburgh. Splits time clinic / theatre / lab; main research is on heavy menstrual bleeding. Pivoted to LC reproductive-health research when patient/social-media reports of vaccine-and-period changes emerged.

Funded by the Royal Society of Edinburgh. Runs three parallel studies (with Oxford, Bristol, Newson Health collaborators) — see [[ergo-menstrual-study]] and [[026-jackie-maybin-menstrual-health]].
