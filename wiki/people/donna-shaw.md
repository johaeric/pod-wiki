# Donna Shaw

UK first-wave long-hauler (Covid April 2020). Severe acute, near-bedbound 1+ year, eventual full recovery via a multi-component stack (Pamela Rose pacing + Alex Howard / Optimum Health Clinic mindset + Sopfit graduated walking + Hill with Liz affirmations + heal-the-gut nutrition). Now a qualified life coach specialising in anxiety, working with new LC patients via the buddy system that helped her.
