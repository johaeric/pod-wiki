# Dr Tania Dempsey

US internist + integrative/functional-medicine physician at AIM Center for Personalized Medicine (NY). Pivoted to complex-chronic-disease specialty after frustration with the symptom-pill model. Now centres practice on MCAS-as-unifying-diagnosis. Has published on post-vaccination MCAS-pattern conditions and on MCAS/ME-CFS overlap.

Distinct contributions to the wiki:
- The **vulnerability + trigger** model (with the iceberg metaphor).
- The **comorbid-clusters slide** with infection in the centre.
- Open admission about the **randomized-trial heterogeneity problem** for these conditions.
- Practical guidance on **what patients can self-trial** when no clinician is available.
