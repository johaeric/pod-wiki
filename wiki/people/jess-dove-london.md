# Jess Dove London

Recovered ME/CFS patient (severe, age 20s, ~12 years before recording) and mother of a child with rare cerebal palsy. Founder of [[turnto-app]] — a daily-health-breakthrough mobile app aiming to make research, trial findings, and practitioner / peer experience easier to find and follow.

The founding insight came from her son's medical experience: a paediatric consultant told her not to bother reading research; she ignored it and rapidly found life-changing interventions in the literature. The patient/parent has to do the searching; an app can do most of that work for them.

## Wiki position

Useful counterpoint to the LC stories on the wiki: an early ME/CFS recovery in the pre-internet era, where there was effectively *no* information to find — recovery was forced rest. The arc of "patient now needs an app to *not drown* in too much information" is the inverse problem of her own era.
