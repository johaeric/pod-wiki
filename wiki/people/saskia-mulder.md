# Saskia Mulder

Northern Irish psychiatric nurse who caught COVID at work in March 2020 (deputy manager on the over-65 acute psych admissions ward — first ward in her hospital to take COVID patients, with inadequate PPE). 27 months in at recording. Not recovered. Multiple intervention attempts including a self-funded trip to Stellenbosch (South Africa) for the Pretorius-collaborator triple-therapy protocol.

Pre-COVID was a busy full-time nurse running a private alternative-therapy practice on the side (reiki, kinesiology, aromatherapy). Family ethos of "get up and get on with it" — in retrospect she suspects she was in burnout for years before the infection.

## Recovery status

Not recovered. Improved in specific domains (sleep, circulation, sensory, limb pain) following 9 weeks of triple therapy, but treatment was cut short by anaemia from bleeding. Currently on lumbrokinase + serrapeptase as natural blood-thinner maintenance.

## What she's tried (summary)

- Multiple antibiotics for chest / sinus infections
- Steroids (private respiratory consultant)
- NICHS rehab program (Northern Ireland Chest Heart & Stroke) — pivoted from exercise-led to meditation/breath
- Irish private physio rehab (~9mo) — vital-signs-led, gentle floor pilates + meditation
- Daily meditation + breath work (her core lever)
- Chinese herbs (London-based practitioner)
- Cranial sacral therapy (regulated menstrual cycle)
- HBOT
- Microbiome work via Fola Oyinlade (monthly stool tests)
- SSRI (mild) — best decision; stopped active suicidality
- Triple therapy in Stellenbosch via Pretorius's lab (Aug–Oct 2022)
- Lumbrokinase + serrapeptase (post-trip)
