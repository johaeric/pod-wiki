# Leela O'Brien

US-based aerospace engineer (PhD) with prior contract work on a NASA space mission. Avid rock climber, ice climber, runner, skier pre-Covid. Got Covid (date not specified in episode); long-hauler with cognitive + motor-function symptoms. **Recovered substantially once, then re-flared; still managing symptoms at recording (early 2024).** Not a full-recovery story.

## Wiki contribution

- Articulates **belief-without-pressure** as a recovery mindset — a balance between hope and acceptance the wiki has logged in pieces but never as cleanly. (Cf. [[107-raelan-agle-recovery-stories]] hope-as-input, [[100-michelle-irving-emotional-empowerment]] emotional-empowerment-map.)
- Names the **ego-fuel as relapse mechanism** — the pattern where initial-recovery + ego-return → re-flare. The wiki's clearest articulation of why "what fuels you" matters more than "what you do."
- **Hot-chocolate-on-the-couch** as the wiki's most concrete *can-rather-than-cant* anchor.
- The **Stephen Hawking framing** — physical capacity sets the frame but not the quality of life inside it.
