Former US professor of media and communication at a small regional university in Pennsylvania. Mother of two young children. Got COVID after 2022 mask mandates lifted in her area; never returned to work. ~3-year recovery. Identifies as having had mild ME/CFS pre-COVID that the virus tipped into severe.

## Recovery framework

1. Acupuncture — not curative but the practitioner's listening was nervous-system medicine
2. Connection with old friends/acquaintances who already had ME/CFS
3. CFS Health Program (Toby Morrison) — ~1 year on free YouTube content, then paid membership; she now mentors there
4. Pacing / activity management — the largest single factor
5. Meditation — already a yoga instructor pre-illness
6. Pet snuggles as nervous-system regulation
7. Communicating with husband about *his* experience — turning illness into "us vs the illness"

## Practice now

- Career and wellness coach
- Specialises in: LC/CFS recovery, moms who are also burnt out, higher-education clients
- Mentor at CFS Health Program
