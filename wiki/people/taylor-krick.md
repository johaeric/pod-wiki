# Dr Taylor Krick

US chiropractor (DC) practising functional medicine at the Washington Wellness Center, Salt Lake City. Trained as a chiropractor, expanded into nutrition / supplements / lab-based wellness, then moved to exclusively functional-medicine practice ("lab-based wellness with diet + supplements + lifestyle strategies"). Pre-LC specialism: mould toxicity, autoimmunity, ME/CFS, complex chronic illness. Host of *The Autoimmune Doc Podcast*.

## Episode

- [[106-taylor-krick-autoimmune-doc]] — functional-medicine framing for LC, mould as standout mechanism, OAT as anchor lab.

## Why he matters on the wiki

- **First strong mould-as-LC-mechanism voice on the wiki.** Reports mould burden as the most common standout lab finding across hundreds of LC patients tested. Articulates the "mould logs + covid match" framing.
- **Anchors the Organic Acid Test (OAT)** as his most-useful single LC test. Mosaic Labs, 76 markers covering gut/mitochondrial/neurotransmitter/methylation.
- **Specific gut→neurotransmitter mechanism** — clostridia metabolites blocking dopamine→noradrenaline conversion, producing the "wired and tired" pattern. Distinctive contribution.
- **"Sniper not machine gun"** supplementation philosophy — high-dose, targeted at lab-confirmed mechanism — counterweight to the multi-supplement, sub-therapeutic patient self-experimentation pattern.
- Compatible with — and complementary to — the polyvagal frame ([[sally-riggs]] #101) and viral-persistence frame ([[amy-proal]] #103). Operates at the lab-mechanism layer below them.

## Caveats for the wiki

- US functional-medicine context. UK / European patients may not have ready access to OAT, mould testing, or his preferred supplements at therapeutic doses.
- Chiropractor + functional-medicine background; not MD. The wiki should hold this alongside MD-level voices ([[asad-khan]], [[robert-groysman]], [[tania-dempsey]]) without elevating or discounting.
- Mould-as-major-mechanism is a strong claim with no podcast counter-voice yet — worth flagging when more mainstream clinicians appear.
