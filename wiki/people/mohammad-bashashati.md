# Dr Mohammad Bashashati

US-based gastroenterologist, internist, and senior GI fellow at Texas Tech University Health Sciences Center, El Paso. Pre-pandemic research focus: functional GI / motility disorders, the role of the immune system in pathophysiology, the endocannabinoid system as a regulator of GI motility, gastroparesis. Pivoted to COVID-19 GI research in 2020.

Published case series on **post-COVID Disorders of Gut-Brain Interaction (DGBI)** in *Neurogastroenterology & Motility*. One of the few clinicians actively classifying and tracking the post-COVID GI-symptom cluster.

## Positions

- **Post-COVID DGBI is a real, classifiable entity.** Defined as chronic GI symptoms ≥ 3 months meeting DGBI criteria, onset ≥ 6 months post-infection, with prior COVID-19.
- **Walkerton precedent.** Post-infectious IBS post the 2000 Ontario water-contamination outbreak showed eventual favourable remission — a useful prognostic anchor.
- **Symptom-led, layered treatment.** Patients often have multiple overlapping DGBIs; treat each cluster (IBS-D, dyspepsia, rumination, bloating) with its own toolkit.
- **Clinical-medical-community awareness gap.** GP and even some gastro practitioners don't yet recognise post-COVID DGBI; patients report being dismissed.
