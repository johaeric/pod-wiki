# Dr Eleanor Stein

Canadian medical doctor and psychiatrist; 25+ years specialising in ME/FM/MCS and latterly LC. Got ME herself in 1989 as a second-year psychiatry resident — pushed through 8 years before finding a family physician in Calgary who herself had ME and could teach self-management. Moved to Calgary to learn from her. Retired from clinical practice; now focuses on education, webinars, and her *Self-Management Manual* (self-published 2012, evolved since).

- [[149-eleanor-stein]] — anchor self-management episode. Self-management as biology; "imminently dangerous / familiar" filter; sunlight + eat-real-food + cave-person-sun-cycle + mindset top tips; wearables nuance; quantum-biology pointer to Eric Gordon.
