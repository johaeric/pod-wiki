# Jenny Adams

UK-based chronic-illness coach. Recovered from ME/CFS / fibromyalgia after 12-ish years of illness following glandular fever at 15. 33 now, ~5 years stably recovered. Trained in **compassionate inquiry** (Gabor Maté). Founder of [[heal-good]] (membership programme). Has [[adhd]], diagnosed in the last year — she now sees the **ADHD-burnout → CFS** pipeline in many of her female clients.

## Why she matters on the wiki

- **17th full recovery** logged.
- Wiki's first explicit articulation of **Internal Family Systems / Dick Schwartz / No Bad Parts** as a post-recovery tool. Different vocabulary from brain-retraining or somatic work; same operative goal — regulating an over-protective nervous system. Worth a separate page.
- Wiki's first **ADHD-burnout → ME/CFS** sub-pattern flag. Pairs with [[hormonal-dysfunction]] and [[trauma-and-illness]] in showing how upstream conditions stack.
- Wiki's first articulated **n=1 vegan-as-recovery-anchor** outcome. Counter-balances the keto / Sarah Myhill / Lorrie Rivers carnivore-ish framing already on the wiki — diet is individual.
- The [[gabor-mate]] / **compassionate inquiry** lineage is now anchored — useful pointer for any patient seeking trauma-aware therapy.
