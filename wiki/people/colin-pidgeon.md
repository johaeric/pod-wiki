# Colin Pidgeon

Father of [[rosie-pidgeon|Rosie Pidgeon]] (paediatric LC patient). Articulates the carer-perspective on living with paediatric LC. Author of the poem *"Stormy"* read in the [[065-long-covid-creativity-2022|#65 creativity episode]] — written in response to a paediatric patient being told *"it's all in your head, it's anxiety"* by a clinician.
