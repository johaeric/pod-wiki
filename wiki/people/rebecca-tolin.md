# Rebecca Tolin

US former TV broadcast journalist (nature/science). Recovered ~6 years ago from 13-year ME/CFS plus multiple comorbid chronic symptoms, after 50+ doctors / practitioners in the biomedical model failed. Now coaches LC + ME/CFS clients in the mind-body / Sarno / Schubiner / Gordon lineage.

## Recovery inflection

Met **Kathy** (recovered ME/CFS layperson) via an online poetry class. A 3-hour conversation in which Kathy explained the John Sarno / Howard Schubiner framework — symptoms generated by brain-and-nervous-system in heightened-stress state, not pathology — was the inflection. Tolin reports getting up and running around the block immediately afterward, after 13 years without running.

The breakthrough was **cognitive shift + somatic experience together**. Not a "book cure" — full recovery took further years of somatic + emotional work, but the door opened that day.
