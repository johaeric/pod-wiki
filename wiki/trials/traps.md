# TraPS — Tracking Persistent Symptoms in Scotland

CSO-funded three-phase observational study of long covid in Scotland led by [[nick-sculthorpe]] at UWS:

1. **Scoping review** — produced a ~50-category symptom list (compressed from ~198 raw).
2. **Cross-sectional online survey** of LC patients across Scotland.
3. **9-month longitudinal app-based tracking** of newly covid+ recruits — captures who develops persistent symptoms and the *dynamics* of those symptoms (high-frequency, not just baseline-vs-endpoint).
