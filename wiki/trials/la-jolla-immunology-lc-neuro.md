# La Jolla Institute for Immunology — LC Neurological Symptoms Study

**Population**: LC patients with neurological symptoms lasting >3 months, seen by a neurologist, with a **confirmed positive COVID test** (this excludes the early-2020 cohort).

**Design**: Primary research / biomarker-discovery. Blood biomaterial collection — must be local to San Diego County (volume + speed-to-test constraints).

**Aim**: Identify biomarkers / circumstance correlates that distinguish neurological-LC subtype.

**Recruitment partner**: [[consuli]] (membership-based research-recruitment platform).
