# ERGO + parallel menstrual-and-COVID research

Three parallel studies investigating long covid's effect on women's reproductive health, with **Dr Jacqueline Maybin** (Edinburgh) as the Edinburgh-side co-PI:

## 1. ERGO study (Edinburgh — open)

- Tissue + blood study, n≈10–20 (recruiting ~5–10 more at recording)
- Three timepoints across the menstrual cycle: proliferative phase (high oestrogen) / secretory phase (post-ovulation) / period itself
- Bloods at each visit (immune cells, hormone levels)
- **Optional endometrial biopsy** (start similar to a smear test; fine straw through the cervix; uncomfortable but tolerable without anaesthetic)
- Compared against pre-pandemic endometrial-tissue bank for matched analysis
- Inclusion: regular cycles, **not** on hormonal contraception, long covid present
- Recruiting at Royal Infirmary of Edinburgh / East Lothian Community Hospital
- Looking at endometrial hormone receptors, immune cell populations, inflammation markers

## 2. App-based daily-tracker study (UK-wide — open)

- Lead: **Dr Gemma Sharp** (Bristol, epidemiologist)
- Daily questionnaire over 2–3 cycles tracking bleeding pattern + LC symptom severity
- Inclusion: regular cycles, with or without hormonal contraception (broader than ERGO)
- Targeting thousands of participants
- LC Scotland support group reviewed PIS / consent forms before submission

## 3. Pandemic-and-menstruation survey (Oxford / Mellier — closed)

- Lead: **Dr Alexandra Alvergne** (anthropologist)
- Closed at recording; data analysis ongoing
- **First analysis published**: ~80 % of respondents had no menstrual change post-vaccination; ~20 % did (irregular bleeding, longer/heavier flow, missed periods, more frequent). No long-term effect signal. Hormonal contraception associated with less disturbance.
- Follow-on analyses on COVID-itself + long-covid-specifically queued.
