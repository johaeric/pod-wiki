# EECP (enhanced external counterpulsation) for LC POTS — Sweden

Sweden-based clinical trial of **enhanced external counterpulsation (EECP)** for long-covid POTS, funded by **[[dysautonomia-international|Dysautonomia International]]**. EECP is a non-pharmaceutical intervention — large pneumatic cuffs around legs squeeze in synchrony with the cardiac cycle, counterpulsating to push blood flow to the upper body.

## Why this matters on the wiki

- **Non-pharmaceutical option** for patients who don't want medications or have intolerances/financial barriers.
- Swedish-based, which makes it geographically nearer to Eric than the NIH or Walter Reed trials.
- Pre-Covid EECP literature exists (mostly for refractory angina); applying it to LC POTS is novel.
