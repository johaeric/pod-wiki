# Virax T-Cell Exhaustion Diagnostic & 2-Year LC Cohort

Partnership between Virax Biolabs (Nigel McCracken, COO) and Manchester's Lydia Becker Institute (Sean Knight, Tracy Hussell). Builds on the [[ciro-manchester]] longitudinal Covid cohort.

## Goals

1. **Diagnostic IVD development** — Virus-specific peptide mix stimulating PBMCs, reading out a T-cell phenotype (cytokines + cell-of-origin via CD4/CD8/B/dendritic separation). Distinct from existing serum-cytokine ELISAs which can't tell which cells are producing what. Targets CE-mark / FDA approval so GPs can act on the result.
2. **2-year longitudinal LC cohort** — track immune-system change against symptom change, prospectively. Looks at:
   - **T-cell exhaustion markers** (PD-1 etc.) — prediction: persistent up-regulation in LC after virus-clearance, mirroring tumour micro-environments
   - **B-cell affinity maturation** — preliminary CIRO data suggested LC patients refine antibody specificity differently from people who recover. Will follow this to maturity.
   - Symptom trajectory vs immune signature

## Mechanistic hypothesis (per #128)

Prior viral history (EBV, CMV, herpes) primes T-cell exhaustion. Covid is the tipping straw for some — not the sole cause. Exhausted T-cells keep virus at bay rather than clearing it → chronic low-grade inflammation → oxidative stress → mitochondrial dysfunction → fatigue + brain fog (mirrors ME/CFS).

If the diagnostic reads positive: **antioxidant / anti-inflammatory interventions** are the immediate justifiable pre-drug step. PD-1 blockade itself (oncology drugs like pembrolizumab) is too aggressive for non-cancer indication.

## Subset framing

Both investigators emphasise **LC is probably several conditions sharing symptoms**. This diagnostic should *identify* the T-cell-exhausted subgroup, not relabel everyone. Other LC patients may be deconditioning, lockdown sequelae, or other mechanisms.

## Cross-applicability

Same T-cell-exhaustion endpoint can be probed with virus-specific peptide mixes for Lyme, CMV, EBV. Common immunological signature could service multiple post-viral diagnoses.
