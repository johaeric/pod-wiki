# argenx VYVGART (efgartigimod) for LC POTS

Commercial pharma trial by **argenx** of **efgartigimod (brand name VYVGART)** — an **FcRn inhibitor** that reduces pathogenic IgG levels — for long-covid POTS. Lauren Stiles (#115) reports that the **phase-2 pilot study went well** and they are **expanding to phase 3**. The drug is **already FDA-approved for myasthenia gravis**, so the safety profile is established.

argenx are also studying VYVGART for:
- Sjögren's syndrome
- CIDP (chronic inflammatory demyelinating polyneuropathy)
- Other neurological-immune-mediated conditions

## Mechanism

FcRn inhibitors block the FcRn receptor that normally recycles IgG, accelerating IgG clearance from circulation. The mechanism rationale for LC POTS: if the pots is autoantibody-mediated (anti-adrenergic / anti-muscarinic / anti-ganglionic), reducing pathogenic IgG should reduce symptoms.

## Why this matters on the wiki

- **Strongest single commercial-pharma LC trial signal** the wiki has logged. Already-approved drug + positive phase-2 + active phase-3 = the closest to a **near-term FDA-approval pathway** for an LC dysautonomia drug.
- **Tests the autoimmune hypothesis at scale** — if positive, validates the autoantibody-LC framework; if negative, narrows the autoimmune subtype.
