Phase-1 small RCT at University of Alabama at Birmingham testing **CI Cognitive Therapy** for Long COVID brain fog. Three-component intervention:

1. **Brain HQ "double decision"** — software cognitive processing speed training
2. **In-clinic everyday-task training** — calendar setup, online shopping, email composition, organised by the *shaping* technique
3. **Transfer package** — goal-setting, self-monitoring, problem-solving (techniques originally from CI Movement Therapy for stroke)

Dose: 30 hrs over 2–4 weeks, outpatient.

## Initial published results

n = 7 (CI Cognitive Therapy) vs 7 (usual care)

- **80% return-to-work** in the CI Cognitive Therapy arm vs **0% in usual care** (among those for whom return-to-work was relevant)
- Large improvements in: everyday-activity performance, fatigue, brain fog symptoms, depressive symptoms

## Confirmatory study

Currently recruiting. Phone 205-934-9768. Newer arm pairs the intervention with **vagus nerve stimulation** (hypothesis: VNS reduces neuroinflammation including in brain).
