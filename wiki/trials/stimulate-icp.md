# STIMULATE-ICP

> **S**ymptoms **T**rajectory **I**nequalities and **M**anagement: **U**nderstanding **L**ong covid to **A**ddress and **T**ransform **E**xisting **I**ntegrated **C**are **P**athways.

UK NIHR-funded long-covid platform study; CI Amitava Banerjee + Melissa Heightman. **The most comprehensive LC trial in the UK** as of recording. Funder: NIHR.

## Three arms

### 1. Symptoms / trajectory

- UCLH long-covid clinic EHR (running April 2020)
- NHS England EHR across 90 LC clinics
- Six deep-cohort sites: **Hull, Leicester, Derby, Liverpool, Exeter, North Central London**
- Patient + clinician interviews

### 2. Platform trial (n = 4,500)

- **Cover-scan** (Perspectum multi-organ MRI)
- Drug platform: **colchicine** (anti-inflammatory), **rivaroxaban** (anticoagulant), **loratadine + famotidine** (H1+H2 antihistamines)
- Digital rehab via **"Living with Covid Recovery"** app
- Primary outcome: **fatigue at 3 months on the Fatigue Assessment Scale**; broad secondary measures
- ~400 enrolled at recording (Banerjee #72); target completion early 2024
- Recently added drug-only sub-cohort to accelerate at struggling sites

### 3. Inequalities + cross-condition comparison

- Inequalities arm (Nzar Akhalon)
- LC vs cardiac/respiratory/mental-health/ME-CFS healthcare utilisation (Christina van der Feltz-Cornelius)

## Industry partners

- **Perspectum** — cover-scan multi-organ MRI (pre-pandemic: liver imaging; pivoted)
- **Living With** — Living with Covid Recovery app (digital rehab)

## Status

Active / recruiting at recording (Spring 2023). Pre-print of 2020 cohort showing 1-year lasting organ impairment in press at *Journal of the Royal Society of Medicine*.

## Eric-relevance

- Confirms loratadine + famotidine (H1+H2) as a trialled LC hypothesis — third independent endorsement after Khan #37 and Saperstein #68
- Cover-scan is a multi-organ biomarker frame; corroborates Li's (#73) "blood tests look normal but something is wrong" message
