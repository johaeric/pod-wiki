# Perrin Technique self-massage feasibility study (NHS)

A **2023–2024 NHS-overseen feasibility study** of the Perrin Technique self-massage routines in 100 LC patients. Results discussed in [[161-dr-raymond-perrin-recovery-pt2]]. Sponsored / overseen by the **Northern Health Alliance** trust grouping; not NHS-funded, but NHS-managed.

## Design

- **n = 100** LC patients.
- **Self-massage routines only** — *not* practitioner-delivered Perrin Technique. The trial tested the daily home practice that Perrin makes freely available on his website.
- **Feasibility-primary, not efficacy-primary** — the question was: can patients adhere to the protocol? Will they complete it?

## Outcomes

- **79% completion rate** — high adherence. Suggests patients felt enough benefit to stick with it.
- *Most* patients reported some improvement on self-massage alone.
- *Some* patients did "particularly well" — Perrin's framing is that for some patients self-massage alone may be sufficient, while practitioner-delivered work remains the load-bearing intervention for others.
- No formal efficacy outcome (effect size, significance vs control) discussed on-air — feasibility was the primary endpoint.

## Implications

- **The free self-massage protocol (perrintechnique.com) is feasible for patients to follow** — operationally critical given that practitioner Perrin Technique is private + geography-limited + cost-significant.
- **Suggests the self-massage may produce real benefit for at least some patients** — but feasibility studies are not efficacy studies; the effect-size question remains open.
- A larger controlled study would be needed to establish whether the self-massage routine produces benefit greater than placebo / time-and-rest.

## Caveats

- **Feasibility, not efficacy** — must not be over-interpreted as evidence that the protocol works.
- **No control arm.** The 79% completion rate doesn't tell us about effectiveness.
- **Self-selection** — patients who participated were probably motivated; their adherence is not a typical-LC-population estimate.
