# Wilco — Imperial College London

Imperial's autoimmunity-focused LC characterisation study, led by Prof Danny Altmann. Tests the **autoimmunity-as-LC-mechanism** hypothesis with deep immune phenotyping across LC cohorts.

## Menstruation substudy ([[viki-male]] led)

A nested substudy added to address the cycle ↔ symptom patterns that were emerging from patient communities and from [[gez-medinger]]'s patient interviews:

- **N target**: 30 LC women living near Hammersmith Hospital.
- **Procedure**: blood samples at multiple points across one menstrual cycle.
- **Aim**: characterise immune-system shifts cycle-by-cycle and tie them to symptom patterns.
- **Status at #93**: just started recruiting (Aug 2023).

## Companion: Visible-app strand

Run by Male alongside Wilco; uses Visible-app symptom-tracking data (with user opt-in) to look for cycle-coupling patterns at scale (large-N).

## Eligibility (substudy)

- LC + having periods.
- Age 18–45.
- Live near enough to Hammersmith Hospital (West London) for 5 study visits in one month.
