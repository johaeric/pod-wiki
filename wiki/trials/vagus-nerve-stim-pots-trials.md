# Vagus nerve stimulation in POTS (3 trials)

Three pre-Covid clinical trials of **vagus nerve stimulation (VNS)** in POTS, funded by **[[dysautonomia-international|Dysautonomia International]]**. Results emerging at recording (Feb 2024). Per Lauren Stiles (#115), the **early results look pretty good** — VNS reduces orthostatic tachycardia *and* improves overall symptomatology + functional quality of life (chores, food shopping, school runs).

## Mechanism (Kevin Tracy lineage)

Pre-Covid research by **Kevin Tracy** (Feinstein Institutes) showed vagal stimulation reduces inflammatory markers via the vagal anti-inflammatory pathway (acetylcholine → α7nAChR → suppressed cytokine release). Downstream studies have used VNS to put **drug-resistant Crohn's disease** and **drug-resistant rheumatoid arthritis** into remission.

If much of LC inflammation-driven, VNS is a **safer mechanism-targeted lever** than systemic immunosuppression.

## Eric-relevance

- VNS via clip-on device (e.g., gammaCore, FDA-approved for migraine; or transcutaneous auricular VNS) is **already accessible to Eric**.
- Stiles personally uses it before bed for sleep + parasympathetic effect; reports faster sleep onset.
- Eric's wiki has [[vagus-nerve-tens]] as 2 mentions / helped-some — these results would, when published, materially increase that evidence weight.
