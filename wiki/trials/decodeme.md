# DecodeME

The world's largest **genome-wide association study (GWAS) of ME/CFS**. Funded ~£3.2M (UK government + Action for ME). Aims to recruit 25,000 UK ME/CFS patients aged 16+ and compare their DNA against general-population controls to identify genetic predispositions — i.e. *which biological pathways* (mitochondrial, immune, neurological, etc.) the disease emerges from.

Chief investigator: **Prof Chris Ponting**, University of Edinburgh. Patient-co-investigator: **Andy Devereux-Cooke** (40+ years ME/CFS, Forum Science for ME co-founder). The study is built with patient representation woven into every working group.

URL: `decodeme.org.uk`.

## Why it matters for Long Covid

DecodeME added a deliberate post-pandemic arm: **5,000 of the 25,000 DNA samples are reserved for people with ME/CFS *post-COVID***. This lets the study compare:

- pre-COVID-trigger ME/CFS (n=20,000) vs
- post-COVID-trigger ME/CFS (n=5,000)

If the genetic patterns overlap, the "Long Covid is ME/CFS triggered by SARS-CoV-2" hypothesis gets direct support. If they diverge, that's a research-priority finding too.

**Plug**: anyone with an ME/CFS diagnosis post-Covid is currently under-represented in the study and is specifically being recruited.

## Method (per ep #67)

1. **Online questionnaire** (or paper version for severe patients) — open to UK ≥16 with a clinical ME/CFS diagnosis. Captures symptomology, fluctuation pattern, triggers, severity. ~150,000 cumulative person-years of lived experience captured to date.
2. **DNA sample selection** — Canadian Consensus or IOM criteria. Selection criteria deliberately not published in detail (peer-review requirement to avoid biasing recruitment).
3. **Saliva donation** — postal home kit; not all questionnaire respondents are asked (selection-only, not a judgement on diagnosis).
4. **GWAS analysis** — compare allele frequencies between cases and controls.

## Expected outputs

- Per Ponting (#67): "We hope to find five of these things — we don't know, of course we don't. We may find none, we may find ten."
- Each genetic hit indicates which biological pathway is implicated → drug repurposing candidates → follow-on RCTs.
- Precedent: the **acute-COVID GWAS** (severe ICU patients) identified an existing drug whose application reduced mortality.

## Wider value

- Questionnaire data feeds into the UK Department of Health and Social Care's forthcoming **ME/CFS strategy** — direct policy influence.
- Acts as a **launch pad** for the next decade of ME/CFS biomedical research.

## Eric-relevance

- Eric is not UK-resident in the participation sense, but the post-COVID arm of this study is one of the most important biomedical research programs the wiki tracks.
- If DecodeME identifies LC genetic signatures overlapping ME/CFS, the case for treating LC as a post-viral ME/CFS variant gets stronger — and the existing ME/CFS clinical playbook becomes more directly relevant.
