# Derby 16-Week Observational Cohort (Long Covid)

Bi-weekly multi-domain observational study of LC patients over 16 weeks (8 touch points per patient). Captures physiological / biological / psychological / quality-of-life / functional changes.

**Distinctive features**:
- **Lived-experience diary** alongside the quantitative measures
- **Bi-weekly cadence** captures fluctuations between baseline and the typical 6-month follow-up
- **Multi-country** — UK + USA + India

**Recruitment source**: patients newly admitted to acute care or referred to LC clinics.

**Status**: Recruiting closing summer 2022 (per Faghy in #36). Extended (~100 patients total) given how powerful the data was; analysis finished December 2023 on a train back from Liverpool. Communications/papers rolling out 2024 (per #120).

## Headline finding (per #120)

LC's day-to-day episodic nature is now visible in the graphs. Patients can swing 180° within 48 hours. **Standard 6/12-month cohort designs miss this**: their conclusions are hostage to whichever single day each participant was assessed. The Derby cadence (weekly contact, daily reporting, intermittent home visits) is — by Faghy's knowledge — the only study to date that has captured the granular fluctuation pattern.

Implication for service design: services must be **fluid enough to accommodate** today-being-good-or-awful. Static one-shot assessments at 6 and 12 months can't drive the right intervention.
