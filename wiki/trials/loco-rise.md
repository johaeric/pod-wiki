# LOCO-RISE

Scottish observational / mixed-methods service evaluation of **community rehabilitation for long covid** across four NHS Scotland health boards. Co-led by **Prof Eddie Duncan** (Stirling, OT) and **Prof Kay Cooper** (Robert Gordon, physio). Funded by the Chief Scientist Office. Strong PPI panel: Fiona Smyth, Allison Dewar, **[[jane-ormerod]]**.

> Cross-ref: see `ercipedia/wiki/trials/loco-rise.md` if/when an evidence page exists.

## Design (from #22)

- 4 NHS Scotland boards: **Grampion**, **Tayside** (CARES clinic — Scotland's only dedicated LC clinic at the time), **Lothian**, **Ayrshire & Arran**
- Selected for geographical/urban-rural diversity *and* for differing models of LC rehab delivery
- **Mixed-methods**: outcome measurement (~100 patients per board, 400 total) at baseline, end of rehab, 3-month follow-up; **plus** qualitative interviews with patients **and** clinicians
- Standardised LC outcome measures: fatigue, respiratory, daily functioning, mental health, cognition
- Inbuilt cross-board learning sessions to disseminate findings while the study runs
- "**What works for whom in what circumstances and why**" frame — not a definitive treatment trial; recognises no LC rehab gold standard exists

## Backdrop / context Eddie shared in #22

- ~880,000 people in Scotland believed to have LC at recording
- Patient flow into community rehab is below prevalence — referral-pathway / GP-awareness gap, not a needs gap
- £10M Scottish gov funding announced (highest per-capita of UK nations; favours bolting LC capability onto existing services rather than creating new clinics like England's £33M model)
