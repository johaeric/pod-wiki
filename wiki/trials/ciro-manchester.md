# CIRO Study (Greater Manchester)

Longitudinal Covid cohort set up early in the pandemic at the Lydia Becker Institute in Manchester. Recruited acute-Covid patients on admission and followed them through recovery; cohort split naturally as some made full recovery and others did not — pivoted into an LC study.

**Investigators:** Sean Knight (academic respiratory clinician), Tracy Hussell (immunologist), with broader Greater Manchester collaboration.

## Findings (per #128, paper imminent)

- **Severity-of-acute ≠ recovery trajectory.** Some ICU-level acute patients made full recoveries by 3 months; others with milder acute illness had protracted post-Covid trajectories.
- **B-cell affinity-maturation differences** — LC patients refine antibody specificity differently from those who recover. Adaptive-immune-system genetic randomisation gave participants different starting populations of B-cells able to detect SARS-CoV-2 (some pre-primed); follow-up shows maturation pattern diverges by recovery group.
- Provides the mechanistic launch-pad for the [[virax-tcell-exhaustion-lc]] follow-on.
