# SymProve Long Covid Trial — Sheffield Hallam University

A **three-month placebo-controlled trial** of the multi-strain water-based probiotic **SymProve** in long-covid patients, run out of **Sheffield Hallam University** (UK). Ran in 2023 (Carl was in the active arm and reports the trial concluded by January 2024 with him "markedly improved").

## Trial design (per ep #176)

- 3-month duration
- Active arm vs. placebo (juice)
- Active arm received the product; placebo arm given the active product for 4 months free as a thank-you afterward (patient-friendly design)
- Baseline + endpoint testing included:
  - Standard blood and physical work-up
  - **Cognitive testing via a Cambridge iPad-based app** (multiple validated scales)

## What we know about outcomes

- **No published primary-endpoint result on this wiki yet.** Status will be promoted from "completed" to "published" once a journal article surfaces.
- **One patient testimony**, well-characterised: Carl Robot (#176). On enrolment, his cognitive scores placed him in the **bottom 7% nationally** on three of the cognitive subscales. He fell asleep during the testing. Three months on the active arm he was back on the bike, ~3-stone weight gain reversing alongside intermittent fasting, dysautonomia lifting.

## Wiki status

- Carl's outcome was the only patient outcome reported in this episode; it can't carry the trial conclusion alone, but it is the strongest single-patient probiotic recovery row on the wiki.
- Watch for the follow-up publication.
