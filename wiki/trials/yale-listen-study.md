# Yale LISTEN Study

⚠️ **Naming clash**: this is **not** the UK NIHR LISTEN trial in [[058-fiona-jones-listen-trial]] (Bridges-self-management RCT). Yale LISTEN is a separate, observational, US-led study.

## Overview

Longitudinal observational study from Yale (Krumholz + Iwasaki). Open globally.

- **N**: ~2,200 enrolled at recording (mid-2023, ongoing).
- **Modalities**: surveys for everyone; biospecimens (blood + saliva) for a US subset.
- **Cohorts**: long covid + vaccine-injury reports.

## Innovations in design

- **Town halls** — investigators meet participants directly to share findings and discuss.
- **All home-based** — no travel.
- **Always returns results** — contractual commitment to share back.
- **Hugo Health data agency** — participants permission their data into the study and can revoke.
- **Frames participants as partners, not subjects** — Krumholz explicitly disowns earlier-career hierarchical research culture.

## Goal

Build the **Atlas of Long Covid** — a symptom-phenotype map overlaid with deep immune-phenotype map (Iwasaki's lab). Goal: identify LC sub-cohorts and therapeutic targets.

## Status

- Active enrolment.
- No defined end date — accumulating cohort.
- Feeds the [[paxlovid-lc-trial]] participant pool indirectly.
