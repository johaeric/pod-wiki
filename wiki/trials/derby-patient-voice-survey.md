# Derby Patient Voice Survey (Long Covid)

70-question survey designed **by the patient PPI group**, not the researchers. Captures the journey from acute infection through recovery and into the LC phase.

**Closed January 2022** with **185 complete responses** / **500+ pages** of narrative data. Analysis underway at recording (#36).

**Themes already emerging**:
- Inconsistency / absence of support
- Lack of being heard
- Day-to-day living impact
- Work impact

**Disseminating non-academically** — visual / exhibition formats — because "academic journals are great but nobody reads them."
