Pilot clinical trial of **Psychophysiologic Symptom Relief Therapy (PSRT)** for chronic back pain. MGH-affiliated investigator team. The study is one of the few RCT-grade investigations of mind-body / neuroplastic-pain methodology in the John Sarno / TMS tradition.

## Design (as described in #211)

- Group format with virtual sessions; small groups
- Protocol over ~3 months
- Components: pain neuroscience education, mind-body framework training, somatic tracking, breath work, expressive-writing prompts
- Comparison arms: placebo and a mindfulness-only arm (separate from PSRT)

## Results

- **66% of PSRT participants pain-free at end of trial** vs ~10% in placebo arm
- PSRT was substantially better than mindfulness-only — important because it shows the *neuroplastic-pain methodology* is doing something distinct from general mindfulness practice

## Why this matters for LC

- Demonstrates that nociplastic / mind-body / neuroplastic recovery has real RCT support
- Aligns with the recovery framework Nora Rodden then applied successfully to GI symptoms and severe insomnia after the trial
- Methodology informs the Nirvana app

## Notes for Eric

This is the strongest RCT evidence on this wiki for the *mind-body recovery framework* itself (separate from any specific app or coach). Worth holding when LC mind-body approaches need defending against "isn't this just CBT?" or "isn't this just the placebo effect?" — the placebo and mindfulness arms in this trial controlled for those.
