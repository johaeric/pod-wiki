# ZOE Covid Study

App-based covid (and now LC) prevalence and symptom-tracking study run out of King's College London with the company ZOE. Anyone in the UK can register and report daily symptoms / vaccination / test results. The dataset has produced some of the earliest LC prevalence and symptom-cluster figures in the UK.
