# AXA1125 Phase 2A — Oxford

Single-site Phase 2a randomised trial run by Axcella Therapeutics with the University of Oxford as the clinical site. **Now reported with positive readout** (#52, mid-2022).

## Design

**Population**: ~42 LC patients meeting a fatigue threshold (patient-reported outcome scale, same scoring used in chronic fatigue syndrome trials).

**Intervention**: Oral AXA1125 (6 amino acid composition) vs. taste/weight/appearance-matched placebo. **Placebo-controlled**.

**Duration**: 28 days dosing.

**Co-primary readouts**:
- 31P magnetic resonance spectroscopy (MRS) — phosphocreatine recovery rate after exercise (Oxford-specialised)
- Patient-reported fatigue (physical + mental)
- Six-minute walk test

## Results (announced #52)

- **~70% of AXA1125 arm** had clinically significant improvement in physical fatigue; **basically zero in placebo arm**.
- **3 of 21** AXA1125 patients reported being back to their pre-COVID baseline.
- Mental fatigue improved in parallel.
- Six-minute walk distance improved alongside fatigue (function followed feeling).
- **Baseline mitochondrial-function tests were "way, way off"** — corroborating biological mechanism.

## Caveats

- Small, short. N≈42, 28 days.
- No PEM-specific endpoint. Six-minute walk does not capture next-day crash.
- Not generalisable yet — Phase 2b/3 needed with PEM-relevant tools.
