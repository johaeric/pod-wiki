# NIH IVIG for LC POTS

US NIH-funded trial of **intravenous immunoglobulin (IVIG)** in long-COVID POTS patients. Pretty strict enrollment criteria — patients must have **certain autoimmune markers** (not detailed in the episode), plus pretty intense pre-trial testing. Tests the **autoimmune-pots subtype** of LC dysautonomia.

The trial also has an **[[ivabradine]] arm** — testing the same drug Eric is currently on as the autonomic-suppression comparator. Two parallel mechanism tests in one trial.

## Why this matters on the wiki

- IVIG is one of the highest-evidence **off-label autoimmune-LC treatments** in the dysautonomia/MCAS specialist community pre-Covid (cf. [[lauren-stiles]]'s own recovery via IVIG; [[sanjay-gupta-cardiologist]]'s clinical experience).
- The NIH trial is the **first formal RCT** of IVIG for LC POTS; if positive, this opens IVIG as an FDA-supported route for the autoimmune subtype.
- **Strictness of eligibility = useful filter**: the autoimmune markers will define what subset of LC patients respond.
