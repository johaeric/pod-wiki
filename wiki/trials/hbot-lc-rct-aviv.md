# Aviv HBOT for Long Covid RCT

A **sham-controlled, double-blind randomised trial of hyperbaric oxygen therapy** for long covid. Designed and run by **[[amir-hadanny]]**'s team at the Aviv Clinics + Sagol Center (Tel Aviv). Three publications + an 18-month follow-up paper. Now the **strongest single LC RCT evidence base** for HBOT discussed on this podcast.

## Design

- **N ≈ 90** LC patients.
- **2 arms (50/50)**: real HBOT vs sham.
- **Sham was rigorous**: pressure raised slightly (so participants needed to equalise ears, heard chamber noise) + air through mask (not 100% O₂). Patients couldn't reliably distinguish the arm — **post-trial guesses were 50/50** across both groups.
- **Treatment dose**: 40 sessions × 5 days/week × 2 hours/day = 8 weeks total.
- **Within-session protocol**: oxygen fluctuations (cycles of 100% O₂ via mask + air breaks) — Hadanny names this as the mechanistic key (mitochondrial interval-training analogy).

## Outcomes

### Subjective (questionnaires)

- Significant improvements in:
  - Symptom severity
  - Quality of life
  - Energy / fatigue
  - Sleep quality
  - Pain

### Objective imaging

- **MRI / fMRI** showed:
  - Improved brain perfusion in specific areas (new blood vessels grew).
  - Improved DTI micro-structure (neurons repaired + new neurons formed).
  - Improved network connectivity in fMRI.
- **SPECT** scans confirmed metabolic-deficit improvements in the orbital-frontal lobes + temporal poles + hippocampi (the "covid horns" pattern).

### Cognitive battery

- Significant improvements in attention, executive function, processing speed, and memory.

### Correlation

- Subjective improvements correlated with objective imaging changes — *not* a placebo signal.

### 18-month follow-up

- **Gains sustained.** Direct evidence against the "feels good then fades" critique of HBOT.

## Three publications + ongoing work

1. Subjective + symptoms paper (8-week endpoint).
2. Imaging paper (8-week endpoint).
3. 18-month follow-up paper.
4. **Forthcoming**: retrospective cohort paper on hundreds of routine-clinic LC patients treated with the 60-session real-world dose.

## Implications

- Established LC (>6 months illness duration) needs **60 sessions, not 40** in clinical practice — 40 was the trial protocol; clinical experience says longer-duration patients require more.
- **Patient selection by SPECT** — patients without the orbital-frontal / temporal-pole / hippocampal metabolic deficit signature are not predicted to benefit; Hadanny declines those patients.
- **Mild-HBOT (1.3 ATA, soft chambers, no on/off cycling)** likely weaker than the Aviv protocol — the oxygen-fluctuation cycling is the proposed mechanistic key.

## Cross-ref ercipedia

- Pairs with the earlier Israeli LC HBOT RCT (Zilberman-Itskovich 2022, *Scientific Reports*) — same broad finding, 40 sessions, smaller effect sizes.
- HBOT for cognitive impairment (multiple non-LC indications) has ongoing peer-reviewed-evidence build; cross-ref ercipedia for current standard-of-evidence.
