# LISTEN Trial

NIHR-funded UK randomised controlled trial of a **co-designed self-management support intervention** for long covid. Co-PIs: [[fiona-jones]] (St George's London) and Prof Monica Busse (Cardiff).

## Design

- **N = 550**
- **Intervention**: 6 remote 1:1 sessions with a trained LISTEN practitioner + co-designed book
- **Comparator**: usual care (varies markedly by region)
- **Outcome window**: 3 months
- **No positive COVID test required** (unusual; covers the early-2020 cohort)
- **Self-referral permitted**

## Sites (at #58)

- All Wales (central self-referral)
- England: Wigan, Coventry, South Warwickshire, parts of London, Yorkshire, Essex
- Opening: Oxford, Sheffield

Each England site recruits ~30; not all areas covered. Patients in non-site areas can't enrol.

## Codesign

- 30+ patients with LC + 10 clinicians (some with LC) shaped the intervention and book
- 8-hour practitioner training, co-delivered with patients
- ~50 practitioners trained at recording

## Status

- Recruiting (Aug 2021 start)
- Final results expected **Autumn 2023**
- Project plan: by Oct 2023, results + ready-made training package + resources for NHS England / Wales scale-up

## Caveats

- It is a self-management trial, not a drug trial — the active mechanism is patient-practitioner relationship + book + narrative-based detective stance
- Different intervention philosophy from typical RCT framework; the "intervention" is co-designed and narrative
