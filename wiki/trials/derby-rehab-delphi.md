# Derby Long Covid Rehabilitation Delphi

Multi-stakeholder Delphi-consensus methodology to define what coordinated LC support / rehabilitation should look like.

**Stakeholders**: GPs, respiratory + non-respiratory physios, clinicians, healthcare workers, patients (with a "tick if also a long-covid sufferer" sub-group). 275 responses in 2 weeks at first round.

**Status**: Phase 2 imminent at recording (mid-2022).

**Output**: a *blueprint* for an interconnected support pathway — Faghy's "buffet" model where patients are screened into a menu of supports they can re-access as needs change. Built on **system-science** modelling of the inter-relations between primary care / specialists / public-sector partners.
