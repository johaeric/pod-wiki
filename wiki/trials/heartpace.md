# HEARTPACE

NIHR-funded **RCT of heart-rate-based pacing** delivered via Fitbit + custom UWS app. Led by [[nick-sculthorpe]].

- Individualised HR threshold; app sends a message if minutes-above-threshold cross a daily limit.
- For [[pots]] patients (where HR is unreliable) the app tracks **steps** instead.
- Intervention: 6 months. Control: standard pacing advice; crosses over after.
- Primary aim: reduce PEM frequency/severity/duration.
- Stated future trajectory: validate in LC → extend to ME/CFS → consider paediatric.
