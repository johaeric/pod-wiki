# RVLC (Reverse Long Covid) — baricitinib RCT

Multi-centre placebo-controlled, double-blind RCT of **baricitinib (4 mg PO daily × 6 months)** vs placebo in established LC, with 6 months off-drug follow-up.

- **Sponsor**: NIH (5-year grant). Drug-only support from Eli Lilly; no financial control by Lilly over design, analysis, or reporting.
- **PI**: [[wes-ely]] (Vanderbilt).
- **Co-investigators**: Carolyn Bramante (Minnesota — also led the metformin RCT), Priscilla Chu, Michael Peluso, Steve Deeks, Matt Durstenfeld (UCSF), Vince Marconi, Nadine Rouphael, Christina Gavegnano (Emory; baricitinib in HIV), Akiko Iwasaki (Yale), Marian Levy (Penn). **Patient-investigators**: Hannah Davis, Jaime Seltzer, Lisa McCorkell.

## Design

- **N = 550**, randomised 1:1.
- Sites: California, Nashville TN, Atlanta GA, Minnesota.
- **Inclusion**: covid-confirmed (50 slots reserved for first-wave patients without test proof) + ≥6 months of LC symptoms + objective cognitive battery findings.
- **Exclusions**: profound liver disease, active TB, active major infection.
- 6 months treatment, 6 months follow-up off drug; testing at 0 / mid / 6m / 12m.
- Tracks EBV reactivation specifically (known JAK-inhibitor concern at long exposures).
- Patients reimbursed for time/inconvenience.
- Classic NIH 5-year timeline (6m startup + 3y enrolment + 1y follow-up + 6m closeout); compressible if enrolment moves faster.

## Hypothesis

Baricitinib (JAK1/2 inhibitor) is FDA-approved for RA, lupus, alopecia areata, and acute covid. The hypothesis is that **persistent immune activation** in LC — interferon, TNF, interleukin signalling, microglial / astrocytic reactive states — can be dampened by a JAK inhibitor and that "turning the light switch off" relieves symptoms.

Parallel and complementary to the antiviral-suppression hypothesis ([[paxlovid-lc-trial]], [[gilead-antiviral-lc-uk]]) — Ely explicitly notes both may eventually need to be combined.

## Either answer matters

Ely's stance: a null result is not failure. If dampening these specific immune pathways doesn't help LC, that's important mechanism-narrowing data for the field.

## Eric-relevance

- Strongest immunomodulation arm in the trial pipeline. Watch for results ~2027–2028.
- If positive, off-label baricitinib use will follow quickly given existing FDA approvals.
- Eric-specific: a JAK inhibitor would not be a first-line option (mestinon + ivabradine + florinef + antihistamine stack is well-tolerated) but is worth tracking as a back-pocket lever.
