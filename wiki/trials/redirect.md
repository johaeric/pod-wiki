# ReDIRECT

**Re**mote **D**iet **I**ntervention to **R**educe long-Co**v**id symp**T**oms. UK-wide remote-delivered weight-management trial for people with long covid (≥3 months symptoms, BMI > 27). Repurposes the **DiRECT** trial protocol (which put type-2 diabetes into remission via total diet replacement → 10–15 kg loss → 1–2-year follow-up).

> Cross-ref: see `ercipedia/wiki/trials/redirect.md` if/when an evidence page exists.

## Design (full detail in #16)

- **N = 200** with LC ≥3 months and BMI > 27.
- **1:1 randomisation** to immediate intervention vs 6-month wait-list control (control then crosses over).
- **Intervention**: 12 weeks total diet replacement (TDR) — soups + shakes ~800 kcal/day → average 10+ kg loss → structured food reintroduction with 1:1 dietitian. Counterweight delivers via app.
- **Outcomes**: standard (weight, QoL, productivity) **plus a personalised symptom outcome** ("mosaic outcome" — each participant nominates their most-troublesome LC symptom from fatigue / dyspnea / pain / anxiety-depression / other, tracked individually). Combet flags this as a relatively novel design fit for LC heterogeneity.
- **Process evaluation** (Blane) — qualitative interviews on participant experience.
- **Health economic** evaluation (McIntosh).
- **Recruitment**: social media + LC networks + GP practices in Glasgow. UK-wide; remote only.

## Hypothesis

> "There's commonality between LC symptoms and overweight/obesity-associated symptoms. Anti-inflammatory effects of weight loss might help some LC patients." — Combet, ep #16

Caveats explicit in the episode:
- Plausibly subgroup-specific; trial designed to detect null/positive cleanly.
- LC patients have varied weight trajectories (some lose with fatigue/GI; some gain with deconditioning).
- A positive result would seed mechanism follow-on studies (this trial doesn't probe inflammatory markers directly).
