# Becky Sharpe's Recovery (full, ~3 years severe LC + 7 years pre-existing fatigue → recovered via TMS)

London UK acting coach. **7 years of escalating fatigue, back pain, IBS, and joint pains pre-Covid** — managed but never resolved. Covid was the final straw → ~3 years severe housebound/bedbound LC. Full recovery via TMS reading + safety-with-naturopath + 4×-daily meditation + emotional-feeling work.

## Pre-illness pattern

- 7 years of cyclical fatigue + multi-symptom dysregulation (back, IBS, joints)
- Already interested in holistic healing + alternative practitioners
- **Self-identified as "a calm person without many feelings"** — retrospectively recognised as severe emotional repression
- High-achiever / type-A stubbornness

## Long-covid course

- ~3 years severe — housebound or bedbound. Couldn't walk to the kitchen.
- 1.5–2 years on a harsh naturopath protocol (30–40 capsules/day + harsh restricted diet) under the *"herxheimer reaction means it's working"* framing → **got worse and worse**.

## What did NOT work — strong warning

**Harsh naturopath protocol** with herxheimer-as-progress framing → `harmed`. *"People kept telling me 'you feel worse but it's because you're getting better' — and now I look back and realize that really wasn't true for me."* When she actually did start getting better, she could feel it; the herxheimer framing was a stuck-loop trap.

## The pivot

Three things converged:

1. **Found a new (80-year-old, London) naturopath whose first contribution was *safety*** — *"If I have to drive down to London and pick you up, I will not let you die."* That moment was the start of recovery.
2. **Asyra body-scan machine** with the new naturopath gave her concrete reassuring data ("no cancer, no parasites, just avoid olives for a week") — calming via data.
3. **PEMF mat 4×/day with meditation** — she now retrospectively re-attributes the benefit to the *meditation* part, not the mat. Strong epistemic re-frame.

## Then the load-bearing intervention

**Read Dr John Sarno's *The Mindbody Prescription* (TMS framework)** → went **30% → 80% recovered in 3 days**. Striking timeline. Pairs with [[032-esther-recovery-story]]'s TMS recovery on LCP.

## Final 20% — emotional-feeling work

The last 20% (80% → 100%) came from learning to feel and express emotions — including crying in yoga class without apologising for it. Pairs with the corpus's recurring "emotional-repression as predisposing factor" pattern (Roberto, Matt, Natalie Gold).

## Wiki position

Logged as full recovery. **`key_to_recovery` for TMS / Mind-Body Syndrome — second on the corpus** (Esther LCP #32 was first). Strong harm-row signal on harsh naturopath protocols + herxheimer-as-progress framing.
