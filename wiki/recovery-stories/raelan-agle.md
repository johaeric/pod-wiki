# Raelan Agle — recovery story (ME/CFS, 10 years → full recovery)

Canadian; mother had CFS from when Raelan was 5 (lifelong; died of it). 10 years ill; ~4 years fully recovered at recording.

## Trigger

"Really bad flu" within a year of mother's death. Pre-trigger pattern: over-exerciser, high-stress, high-anxiety, lots of travel. Looking back: virus was the final straw on an already-loaded system.

## Arc

- **First 2 years**: severe; mostly housebound, bed/couch.
- **Years 2–8**: mild but persistent; pushing-and-crashing; symptomatic every day.
- **Years 8–10**: built her own recovery program; gradual emergence.
- **From ~year 10 (~2019)**: fully recovered; running, full-intensity workouts, no PEM.

## What she tried in Phase 1 (partial improvement, no clear attribution)

100 pills/day from integrative doctor. Antivirals. Heavy antibiotics for suspected chronic Lyme. HRT. Sauna, dry skin brushing. All-organic diet. Mould testing of home. Meditation. **Estimates < 50% improvement**; couldn't isolate which intervention helped what.

## What worked in Phase 2 — her own self-built program

- **Plant-based diet** (subsequently moved off without re-symptoms — diet was supportive, not load-bearing)
- **Gut health work** — intermittent fasting, juice fasting, fermented foods for natural probiotics
- **Sleep at all costs** — top priority; whatever it took
- **Daily sunlight + outdoors**
- **Therapy + journaling + meditation** for stress
- **New healthy relationship** (under-credited at the time but retrospectively major) — partner moved in, loneliness + stress dropped
- **Graded movement starting at 1 min/day** — wall push-ups or squats in her living room, +1 min/week. Within a year: full-hour gym workouts at intensity, no PEM.

## Operational signature: scaffolding-first

The graded-movement piece had failed every prior attempt. The Phase 2 difference was that **everything else was dialled in first** (sleep, stress, gut, environment). Re-frames "graded exercise therapy doesn't work" as: it doesn't work *without scaffolding*. With it, gradual re-exposure works — and the active ingredient is the nervous-system signal that movement is safe, not the cardio-fitness training.
