## Arc

| Phase | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-illness | <2021 | CFO of an Italian company, lives on a small farm next to grandparents. Long-standing psoriatic arthritis (16 years, well-managed). Wildfire trauma background. Subconsciously dysregulated nervous system. |
| Acute COVID | July 2021 | Mild infection. 7 days flu-like. |
| First weird sign | Day 7 | Heart racing without panic — body alone, mind calm. |
| Decline | months 1–6 | Tachy, chest pain, palpitations, severe insomnia, severe orthostatic intolerance. 5 minutes upright → 90 minutes recovery. Sound sensitivities, severe cognitive impairment. |
| Stuck plateau | 18 months | Still working bedbound (CFO for old job using muscle memory). 15–25% function. Repetitive intrusive thoughts ("you should buy salami"). |
| Self-prescribed aspirin | autumn 2022 | Low-dose aspirin for burning-lung sensation — modest boost (placebo or real). |
| First inspiration | Dec 2022 | Found podcasts including this one — Joanna Rayl's recovery story (ANS Rewire) clicked. |
| ANS Rewire | Feb 2023+ | 6 weeks of doubt then committed to the program. Slow recovery over 2 years. |
| Job exit | June 2023 | Left the CFO role to give recovery the space it needed. |
| Character reframing | 2023–2024 | Internal-family-system style: stressful characters (controller/perfectionist/helper/worrier/achiever) named and dialogued with. Created counter-characters (rebel returns library books late). |
| Recovery declaration | ~2024 | Returning to Lindy Hop swing dance late nights, sometimes without earplugs. Friends say she dances *better* than before. Psoriatic arthritis, chronic migraine, chronic back/neck pain all resolved or near-whisper. |

## Turning points

1. **Hearing Joanna Rayl's recovery story** on this podcast
2. **ANS Rewire** as the foundation, then freestyling on top of it
3. **Identifying small-t trauma messages** despite "good childhood" — e.g., over-responsibility for others' nervous-system regulation
4. **Body-sensation tracking via meditation** to find where wounded-child reactions lived

## What she'd tell her past self

> "You can let go of more than you think you can."

## Disclaimer

Anecdotal evidence — N=1. ANS Rewire is a paid program and Dan Neuffer's framework is proprietary. Take her psoriatic-arthritis improvement as patient testimony, not as a claim that nervous-system retraining cures autoimmune disease.
