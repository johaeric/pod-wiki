# Lorrie Rivers — Recovery from ME/CFS, Long Covid, and lifelong fibromyalgia

A three-illness recovery story spanning ~25 years.

## Arc

**Phase 1: Severe ME/CFS (~early 1990s–2000s, age 20s)**. ~8 years severe (parts requiring her father to feed her by hand because she couldn't lift a fork) + ~2 years partial recovery. Diagnosed two years in. Doctors had no answers; passed her between psychiatrists. Self-managed via what she now calls pacing (then unnamed). Pioneered her own coaching practice toward the end of recovery as patients sought her out.

**Phase 2: Long Covid (May 2020 → ~Jan 2021)**. ~8-month course. Recognised LC as ME/CFS-recurrence within 24 hours of onset; freaked out for ~3 days; then deployed her own protocol and was fully recovered in ~8 months.

**Phase 3: Lifelong "weather-dependent fibromyalgia" (childhood through 2021)**. Always assumed permanent. Triggered her into investigating root causes more aggressively post-LC. Discovered the **hidden-infection / dysbiosis theory** via Dr Simon Yu, Dr Alex Kingart, and Kelly Rivera. Self-treated with anti-parasitic protocol (after her cautious first attempt with home ozone therapy produced harsh herxheimer responses). Fully recovered within ~6 months.

## What she credits

- **Strict low-carb / carnivore eating plan** as the foundation
- **Anti-parasitic + anti-fungal "gently aggressive" treatment** as the active ingredient
- **Detox-pathway preparation + binders** to manage herxheimer reactions
- **Heavy-metal chelation** in later phase
