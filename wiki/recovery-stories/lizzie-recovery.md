# Lizzie's Recovery

UK patient. Triathlete + 110%-into-everything pattern pre-illness. Caught covid at her sister's wedding in July 2022 (the day after, she completed a triathlon while symptomatic). ~2-year recovery arc; recovered ~late 2024 / early 2025; now doing therapy as the post-recovery integration step.

## Trajectory

- **July 2022** — covid + triathlon while symptomatic.
- **First month** — flu-symptoms + crushing fatigue + brain fog (especially conversational drain).
- **Returned to work too soon** at 1 month off → ~6 months of boom-and-bust.
- **First Christmas (Dec 2022)** — extended family witnessed her crashes for first time; mother's "be positive" → Lizzie's "I can't walk" exchange marks low point.
- **~Month 6** — brain fog lifted enough to research → discovered Mind-Body / nervous-system framing.
- **Jan Rothney's book** + **Curable app** = inflection. Mindset shift to "this is biology, this is recoverable."
- **One month later** — out of bed, walking, then running.
- **Gave herself a further month before returning to work** — explicit consolidation period.

## What helped

1. **Stopping pushing** (after a year of trying not to)
2. **Jan Rothney's book** — nervous-system explanation + slow-and-safe activity-progression techniques + only-positive-experiences principle
3. **Curable app** — specifically the language-shift technique ("I feel less energised than I would like" not "I feel awful")
4. **Amygdala pep talk** — calm, authoritative, repeated mantra: *"I am safe. Thank you body for trying to protect me with this fatigue, but I just don't need it. I am safe."* Reports stopping fatigue mid-meal at her mother's birthday dinner using this.
5. **Discipline + routine** with explicit rest periods + small celebrations for every new capacity
6. **Therapy** post-recovery — to process the trauma + grief

## What didn't help / actively harmed

- **Step-counting (~1,000-step ceiling)** — caused emergence of palpitations + worsening crashes. Lizzie's retrospective interpretation: the *threshold-anxiety itself* put her in fight-or-flight, which manifested as palpitations she didn't have before counting started.
- **Facebook LC support groups** with multi-year-illness anecdotes — left them; the terror-of-permanence was contaminating her mindset.

## Now

- Recovered. Therapy ongoing for the trauma-of-illness.
- Different relationship with effort: more balanced, less driven.
- Doesn't think she'll ever run at the pre-LC pace; explicit acceptance.
