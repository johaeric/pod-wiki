# Julie Black — full LC recovery (Scotland; mind-body route)

Caught COVID from partner ~1 July 2022. By ~3–4 weeks, knew it wasn't the standard recovery curve. Symptoms peaked at potty-in-the-bedroom severity (couldn't safely walk to bathroom from upright presyncope). Recovered to "I don't have long covid anymore" within ~3–4 months of starting Curable app. Now competing in 50km endurance horse-riding events.

## Pre-illness lead-up (the perfect-storm pattern)

- Cognitively demanding job, persistently intense
- Organising endurance ride event (admin + planning + own training)
- Helping plan a friend's wedding
- Acute trauma ~1 month pre-COVID: horse stuck in bog, near euthanasia rescue
- Looked after severely-acute partner for a week before catching it from him
- Probably perimenopausal (untreated)
- Massive garden, list of jobs that "never ends"

## Symptom profile (~20 listed)

- Insomnia (zero sleep for weeks at peak)
- Adrenaline waves
- Breathlessness sitting up in bed
- POTS pattern (presyncope on standing; tachycardia)
- Histamine intolerance ("fizzy" feeling in body + head)
- Brain fog → "out of space and time"
- Sensory overstimulation (no TV/music/reading)
- Daily panic attacks
- Suicidal ideation early in arc

## What didn't work

- Premium supplements (£££, no isolatable benefit)
- Doom-loop Facebook groups (caused active harm via "you will never recover" messaging)

## What worked

1. **Low-histamine diet** (Facebook tip, early) — dramatic relief of fizzy/brain-fog cluster
2. **Validating doctor** (not GP) ran ruling-out tests, validated experience, explained dysautonomia
3. **Suzy Bolt's program** — breathing classes from bed, **yoga nidra** (the rest she'd never had), gradual yoga
4. **Alan Gordon's *The Way Out* audiobook** — chronic-pain framing, applied to LC by replacing "pain" with "symptoms"
5. **Curable app** — went all-in; within 2 weeks chalk-and-cheese; within 2–3 months not-sick

## Operational signature

The mind-body route done deliberately, scaffolded by:
- Negative tests as *permission* to commit to the mind-body frame (not "everything is fine," but "no detectable damage means this approach is now coherent")
- Yoga-nidra-as-real-rest (vs lying in bed catastrophising)
- 24/7 thought-monitoring during peak Curable phase ("is that the helpful thought right now?")
- Working through bog-rescue trauma the app surfaced
