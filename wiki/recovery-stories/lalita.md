# Lalita — Recovery Story

Lalita's arc is unusual on the wiki because she had already done a full nervous-system-led recovery once, ~14 years before LC, from a chronic-illness cluster (lupus, fibromyalgia, chronic fatigue, chronic pain). When LC hit her in 2024, she recognised the autonomic-pattern symptoms early and applied the same framework — refined by her formal training with **[[howard-schubiner]]** in between.

## Timeline

- **Pre-pandemic (first bout, unknowing)** — six weeks of severe illness post-conference; recovered acutely but left residual: BP went from chronically low to **high** (started low-dose BP medication), numb lips, daily fatigue, afternoon naps. Did not connect these to COVID until later.
- **End of August 2024 (second bout)** — mild acute illness; symptom cascade followed:
  - Within weeks: tachycardia → vertigo → couldn't lie flat (slept sitting up) → leaden limbs → chest tightness
  - Three ER visits for chest pain; all cardiac workup clear; told "anxiety" each time
  - Symptom load grew to ~30 different complaints: POTS-pattern, GI, gut, hormonal, irritable bladder, costochondritis, brain fog, headaches, migrating stabbing pains, air hunger / claustrophobia, tinnitus, anemia
  - Reactivated EBV + herpes (picked up via bio-resonance)
  - Formal **dysautonomia** umbrella diagnosis
- **Months 1-12** — daily nervous-system protocol (see below). Sometimes 30-40 tool-uses per day at peak.
- **Months ~3-9** — POTS resolved early (surprised her); GI, sleep, brain fog gone progressively
- **Months 12-14** — full recovery; **tinnitus the last to clear**, helped substantially by antihistamines

## What she credits

**Primary (her own ranking):**
1. **Breathwork** — *"propelled my healing more than anything else."* 4-in / 6-out, dozens of times per day, combined with self-talk in the moment
2. **Nervous-system framework / brain retraining** — Schubiner's 5 Fs as the danger-response inventory; belief in the framework as central
3. **Self-compassion and self-talk** — done inside every breath cycle, not separately

**Stacked supporting tools:**
- EFT tapping (combined with breath for high-symptom moments)
- Somatic tracking + body scan
- Yoga nidra nightly
- Schubiner's EAET work — used specifically to dig under repeating patterns. Identified her chest-pain-when-alone panic as a transgenerational echo of her father's death (heart attack at 48, alone). One session of EAET-style work; pattern never recurred.

**Body-side support (naturopath-guided):**
- Antihistamines (specifically credited for tinnitus)
- Nattokinase, quercetin, dandelion, stinging nettle, black seed oil, curcumin, multivitamins, iron, pre/probiotics, electrolytes
- Bowen therapy (aches/pains)
- Cranial sacral therapy
- Bio-resonance therapy (diagnostic — found EBV/herpes/anemia; non-mainstream)
- Low-carb / paleo-pattern small meals (gut)

## What was striking

- **Decided up-front: "12 months max."** She acknowledges this likely had a placebo / expectancy effect. Paired with a no-pressure framing: had she gone past 12 months, she'd have reassured herself ("look how much has gone, just a couple left"), not used the deadline as a stick.
- **Combination is the unit, not the components.** Breath + self-talk + tapping done together; not stacked as separate disciplines.
- **Belief × relentless application.** Same tools many people try; what stood out was 30-40 in-the-moment uses per day and total framework conviction.
- **The trauma-pattern dig.** When a symptom pattern (panic on heart symptoms only when alone) repeated, she didn't just keep applying tools — she got curious, did EAET-format work on the underlying emotion (her father's death), and the pattern resolved. Worth noting as a model for "stuck" patterns.

## Caveat

N=1 recovery. Lalita's prior recovery-experience and formal training give her a head-start most patients won't have. She herself ends with *"it can be different for everybody."*
