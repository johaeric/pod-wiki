# Amy Anker — recovery story

Stained-glass artist in Montana, ~14 months from infection to full recovery. Strong **neurological** LC presentation that resolved through what she frames as nervous-system-loop interruption + a daily four-pillar routine.

> ⚠️ Distinct from Dr [[dr-amy-small]] (ep #5). Different person, different country, different story.

## Trajectory

- **November 2020** — caught covid. Acute strikingly neurological from day one (numb palms, scalp burning, hyperacusis, tremors, insomnia, tachycardia).
- **Months 1–2** — severe physical and mental anguish; "psychosis-adjacent hell-surges" began, lasting 2–3 hr at a time.
- **Was reading Norman Doidge, *The Brain That Changes Itself*** when infected. Carried a neuroplasticity belief from day one — "I will recover."
- **Months 3–6** — established daily routine: positive mindset, real rest, healthy food, walking even 5 min/day. Could not initially meditate (too agitated despite years as a meditation teacher).
- **Month ~6** — substantial improvement. ~95% for an extended stretch.
- **Month ~11** — wildfire smoke fills the valley she lives in. Major relapse. Bedbound, fatigued, < 20 min walk capacity.
- **Smoke clears, last few months** — final piece clicks: she identifies a **looping pattern** in her nervous system. The acute injury healed but the NS keeps firing on minimal triggers (pulling a weed in the garden → bedbound). Likens it to phantom-limb pain: "limb is gone, the pain is real." Once she sees the loop, she can interrupt it. Anticipating pain → producing pain.
- **Recovered.** Sets up [[positively-covid]] as a free resource.

## What she credits

1. **Belief in possible recovery** — "the most important thing"
2. **Slowing down** — "I refuse to operate at the pace society imposes"
3. **Joy** — "elbow out the despair"
4. **Daily routine** — positive mindset · real rest · nourish · walk
5. **Loop-interruption** — mindful catching of "I'm going to crash" anticipations and replacing with "I'm okay, we'll get through this"

## What she explicitly stopped

- **LC support groups** in their unfiltered form. Triggering, hope-eroding. Stayed only post-recovery to *contribute* recovery stories back.
- Doom-scrolling LC news.

## Notable quotes

> "If you don't think recovery is possible, it won't happen."

> "I had to plant a lot of different seeds over the year for them to finally come to fruition."

> "My nervous system was in a looping pattern. Once I saw that, I could break it."

## Mapping to Eric

- **Profile match**: Amy's LC was strongly neurological / dysautonomic. Eric's POTS+PEM is autonomically-driven — partial overlap.
- **What's universal-low-cost**: walking · real rest · positivity practice · gratitude · interrupt-the-anticipated-symptom thoughts.
- **What's profile-specific**: the "looping pattern" framing presupposes the acute pathology has healed and the persistent symptoms are NS-driven. For LC patients with structural / organ damage, the framing doesn't substitute; it complements.
- **Independent verification**: Cross-check ercipedia for neuroplasticity / NS-retraining evidence-base.
