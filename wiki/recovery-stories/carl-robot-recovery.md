# Carl Robot — Recovery Arc

## Pre-illness

UK teacher (Leeds), age uncertain (mid-40s+). Had been Head of PE in a Bristol school, 20-year PE-teacher career. Sport was central — football, running, cycling, swimming. Considered himself fit and healthy.

## Trigger

Mild COVID summer 2020 (no test available; only signs were a mild cough + smell-related changes that didn't match the publicised "high temp + bad cough" pattern). Probably never identified as COVID at the time.

## Slow descent

- **Autumn 2020** — breathing got bad. Carl assumed he was getting unfit and tried to exercise more. *That made it worse.*
- Sofa-bound after work weeks. Persistent breathlessness.
- **Weight gain** — slow at first, then dramatic. Stomach distended.
- Standard endoscopy / colonoscopy / blood / heart / lung work-up was delayed by NHS COVID backlog — appointments took months / years to come through.
- **Last day of school year 2021** — A&E presentation: chest pain, breathing crisis, struggling for breath. Standing-up posture-related BP/HR swing noticed by a doctor (now-retrospectively dysautonomia).
- ECGs / heart tests / lung tests *all negative*. Lung function tests not running for ~9 months due to COVID precautions; result eventually normal.
- Carl proposed COVID / LC link himself. Specialist agreed. Referred to Leeds LC clinic (~Feb 2023).

## Recovery work

- **LC clinic** — useful but largely confirmed what he was already trying.
- **Cold-water immersion** (Feb 2023, Devon family holiday at Westwood Ho! sea pool, frost on ground). Felt "peace" — first thing that visibly helped. Switched to **daily cold showers** in Leeds (Andrew Huberman + Søberg "end on cold").
- **Yoga nidra** (Andrew Huberman) — afternoon sessions when crash-fatigue hit by 4 pm.
- **Wim Hof-adjacent breathwork**, then gentler conscious breathing.
- **Electrolytes** every morning — helped some.
- **A pile of supplements** (~7) — stopped them all, no difference.
- **Sheffield Hallam SymProve probiotic clinical trial** (3 months active arm) — *the inflection point*. Cognitive baseline put him in bottom 7% nationally (he fell asleep doing the test). Three months later he was back on the bike.
- **Intermittent fasting** alongside SymProve — weight came down.
- **Kefir** (homemade) — to maintain microbiome after the trial ended.

## Recovery moment

By **January 2024** he could ride. Started a **couch-to-marathon programme** as a deliberate punctuation — "I'm not going back from here." Ran his **first marathon** ~6 months later. Did another marathon-distance run in autumn. Planning the **45-mile Green Man Challenge** around Bristol (his hometown) for May 2026 as the closing chapter.

## What he tells himself / others

- Generic graded exercise advice doesn't work. *His* graded escalation worked because every other piece had been laid first.
- Listen to your body; back off when it tells you.
- Look for clinical trials — don't wait for the standard system.
- Chronic illness teaches gratitude for boring things ("I was excited to do chores again").
