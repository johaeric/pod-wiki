# Karen Knutson's Recovery (in-progress, halfway, single parent)

Watertown MN single mom of a 7-year-old. June 2022 stress → Sept 2022 viral exposure → ME/CFS-pattern long covid → black mold exposure compounding. ~2 years in at recording. **Halfway recovery; still in progress.**

## Long-covid course

- **June 2022**: brain fog from stress.
- **Sept 2022**: caught a virus from daughter; pushed through; symptoms cascaded.
- **Months later**: ER, neurologist, infectious-disease, long-covid clinic; eventually diagnosed long covid + post-viral syndrome + suspected MCAS + ME/CFS.
- Black mold found in her apartment — moved out; symptoms continued.
- Quit job; lost business; lost independence (couldn't drive for 2 months).

## What didn't work

- **SGB** (stellate ganglion block) — flew to Texas; no effect.
- **Many supplements** — significant spend; most ineffective.
- **Detoxes** — crashed her for 4 weeks each.
- **Sarno's *Mindbody Prescription*** — couldn't get through; didn't resonate.

## What helped (multi-component, halfway)

1. **Sleep medication** — ended 8 months of insomnia.
2. **Anxiety medication** — needed pharmacological scaffolding for nervous-system access.
3. **Cold showers** — depression lift.
4. **Affirmations + handwritten reminders** — brain-retraining variant via repetition.
5. **Yoga Nidra** (free on YouTube).
6. **Ayurvedic practitioner** — heart-rate diagnosis + plant-based diet + herbs; recent improvement.
7. **Recovery stories** as hope-input.

## Wiki position

In-progress recovery (`partial` status). Useful **counter-evidence rows** on standard interventions and an in-progress single-parent voice for corpus completeness.
