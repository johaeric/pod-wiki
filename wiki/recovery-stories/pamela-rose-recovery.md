# Pamela Rose — recovery story (ME/CFS, pre-Covid)

Diagnosed with ME/CFS in 2010 (had been unwell longer than that). 7-year recovery arc, predominantly self-directed because in 2010 there was even less guidance available than there is today. Returned to full-time work at her bank, got a promotion during the journey, then chose to leave banking once she realised she didn't want to spend her newly regained energy on it.

## Arc

- **Onset**: standard post-viral pattern (no dramatic acute trigger named on the episode); first several months largely housebound, unable to work.
- **Stage 1 — denial/resistance**: didn't want to believe she was unwell; it was the worst of strategies.
- **Stage 2 — acceptance + figuring out the blend**: pacing, emotional coping, mindset techniques, nervous-system calming.
- **Stage 3 — return**: full-time work returned, then she chose to leave it.

## What helped (her own report, generalised — she's deliberately wary of one-size-fits-all)

She names *blends* rather than single interventions:
- Pacing / energy management
- Working through emotional adjustment to chronic illness
- Mindset techniques and nervous-system calming
- Practical problem-solving for everyday demands
- Trusted "inner-circle" of 2–3 people who really got it; everyone else stayed surface

## Where she landed

Now full-time fatigue coach (>1,000 clients across ME/CFS, fibromyalgia, post-viral fatigue, LC). Her coaching design is short engagements that hand the toolkit over — patients should be able to function without her, not be retained in long-term coaching.
