# Johanna Rayl — Recovery Story

A full or near-full recovery from a 2.5-year LC course through brain-retraining + radical rest + structured leave from work.

## Timeline

| Phase | Date | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-illness | 2018-2020 | High chronic stress; high-volume training (cyclist/runner; triathlon plans) |
| Acute | April 2020 | Moderate COVID; in bed 18hr/day for ~1 week. No positive test (early-2020). |
| Failed phase 1 | mid 2020 | Cardiology, allergy, immunology workups — all clear. Acupuncture / herbal — minor benefit. |
| Failed phase 2 | fall 2020 | Started PhD program; abandoned rest progress; physical therapy made things worse. |
| Murky middle | 2020-2021 | Roller-coaster pattern: weeks of partial improvement → weeks of total relapse. Diagnosis still informally "long covid" — clinicians not comfortable using the term. |
| Discovery | year 1.5 | Searched "full CFS recovery" → CFS Unraveled (Dan Neuffer) → ANS Rewire program. Light-bulb on autonomic dysregulation as the root mechanism. |
| Turning point | January 2022 | Took **paid medical leave** from PhD. Insurance retained. ~2 months later, "things really began to change." |
| Recovery | 2022 | Full daily ANS Rewire commitment + Curable app + radical rest + movement-not-exercise. By mid-2022, walking to Lake Michigan multiple times/day, swimming again. |

## What she credits

| Lever | Role |
|---|---|
| ANS Rewire program | **Primary** — the autonomic-retraining structure |
| Medical leave (paid) | Critical privilege; "best decision besides the program" |
| Curable app (~$15) | Chronic-pain neuroscience reframing for in-the-moment fear-reduction |
| Radical rest + "give it back" | Hold rest gains on good days, don't bank-and-spend |
| Movement, not exercise | Reframed away from achievement; only do what feels good |
| Mind-body integration | Surrender phase; recognising emotional and physical as one system |

## Patterns that align with other recoveries

- Multi-component mind-body approach (vs. single-drug)
- Privileged-leave window (cluster: Whitfield #8, Anker #20, Suzy Bolt #7)
- Brain-retraining family (cluster: ANS Rewire / DNRS / Gupta)
- Pre-illness "pushing hard" personality + chronic stress
- Search-driven discovery (formal medicine didn't refer them)
