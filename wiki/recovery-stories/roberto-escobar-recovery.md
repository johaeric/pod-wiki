# Roberto Escobar's Recovery (1.5 years severe LC + PTSD → full recovery)

Queens NY registered nurse. Front-line COVID-unit work in March 2020 → ~9 months of pre-illness occupational trauma → his own severe Covid Dec 2020 (2-week hospitalisation, near-ventilation) → 1.5 years of severe LC + PTSD → full recovery in 2.5–3 months once he found the right framework.

## Pre-illness pattern

- **Occupational trauma** (front-line nurse, first-COVID-unit, March 2020 NYC) — load-bearing predisposing factor by his own assessment.
- Type-A personality.

## Long-covid course (Dec 2020 → mid-2022)

- Severe acute → hospitalized 2 weeks, almost ventilated, refused vent.
- Discharged extremely weak; wheelchair; ~3 months severe weakness despite Type-A push.
- Persistent shaking, GI dysfunction (purée diet), severe PTSD with nightmares, sleeping 2–3 hr/night.
- All specialist tests clean.
- 6 months disability; tried return to work after 1 year; couldn't; had to quit.
- Year 1: tried supplements, talk therapy, brain therapy, behavioural therapy, more — nothing broke through.

## The pivot (~year 1.5)

Two threads converged:

1. **Self-compassion / self-love mindset shift** — chose acceptance-of-where-he-was over comparison-to-who-he-was. Books that helped: *The Self-Love Experiment* by Shannon Kaiser; *13 Things Mentally Strong People Don't Do* by Amy Morin.
2. **Polyvagal-theory framing** — discovered via a YouTube psychologist's video on type-A + past trauma → LC. This made coherent sense of his symptoms for the first time.

Once he had both pieces, his existing breathing + yoga + laughing yoga practice gained traction it hadn't had before. **Day-on-day improvement** for the first time in 1.5 years.

## Recovery components

1. **Polyvagal-informed daily practice** — breathing, yoga, laughing yoga (specifically: with the polyvagal frame). Load-bearing.
2. **Self-compassion mindset** — precondition for the nervous-system work to take hold.
3. **EMDR therapy** — resolved his PTSD layer (sleep restored). Did **not** help his physical symptoms — separate axis.

## What he tried and didn't work

- **Gupta program** — *"too long, too much."* Logged as `no_effect`. Not a knock on Gupta — a "frame fit" claim that polyvagal-theory framing matched his clinical-nurse mind better.
- Year 1 supplements, talk therapy, brain therapy.
- Western-medicine specialist tests — clean but no answers.
