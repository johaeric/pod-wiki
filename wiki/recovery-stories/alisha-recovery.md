# Alisha — Recovery Arc

## Pre-illness

US mountain region; 10 years of organic-farm manual labour (from college onwards). 40 hrs/week active outdoor work; ran, biked, hiked, did yoga in addition to farm work. Active introverted-extrovert pattern with retreat needs.

## Trigger

- 2019 — moved across the country, new relationship, life-stress baseline rose.
- 2020 — first COVID infection, ~1 month acute illness. Then a **summer of new-onset extreme anxiety + heart palpitations + shortness of breath** working a farm job she'd previously loved. Multiple panic attacks, fear of sun and heat. Mainstream doc visit: nothing wrong.
- 2021 — second COVID infection. **Body shut down.** ~15 symptoms (extreme fatigue, GI dysfunction — bloating / constipation / burning / tingling / dizziness, anxiety). Couch-bound for **1.5 years**.

## Diagnostic phase

- Functional-medicine workup → gave her diagnoses (Lyme, Epstein-Barr, mold, heavy metals, parasites, possibly long covid). Sent home with **20 supplements on day one.**
- Switched to a second functional-medicine doctor → **a year-long detox protocol** for everything.
- "Months of moments thinking 'this is it'." Nothing changed.

## The shift

Heard **Dan Buglio** on Raelan Agle's channel. Read **Sarno's *The Mind Body Prescription*.** Started noticing her own non-rational pattern: *walking up the stairs* crashed her, but *walking the same number of steps in the yard* didn't. The cognitive turn that **she wasn't broken** was the recovery turn.

## Recovery work

Took ~1 year from the cognitive turn to feeling fully healed. Daily layered practices:

- **Rebecca Tolin's somatic-tracking meditation** — daily. Central practice. Calming voice telling her she was safe in this moment.
- **Self-Reiki** — became Reiki-certified online. **2 hrs/day at peak.** First thing post-illness that brought her *joy* and engaged her mind in something other than her health.
- **Calming guided meditations** — many.
- **Reading** — engaged her mind elsewhere.
- **Smiling on purpose** when alone (Dan Buglio's "24 ways to create safety"). Felt fake first time. Did it anyway.
- **Saying no / boundaries** — learning she's an empath. Hard limits became part of safety. Continues to be central in her wellness.
- **Slow reintroduction** with joy framing — "first time walking into my garden in 2 years" treated as celebration, not failure marker. Letting go of perfectionism (no required hour-long runs anymore).
- **One session with Rob Ensor** (TMS coach) cleared the lingering "detox sensations" (burning after activity, GI issues). She went on her first date the next day. *That* was when she said she was healed.

## What life looks like now

- Mind-body coach (4 days/week). Farms ~1 day/week. Hikes, walks, runs again.
- Rests more than she used to — and is at peace with it.
- Boundaries continue. Asks "do I want to do this thing?" before saying yes. Continues morning meditation, somatic-tracking, breathwork.

## What she tells herself / others

- Every safety moment counts. Even 10 minutes. They stack.
- Ask whether the practitioner / decision feels **loving and supportive** — that's the screening question.
- Wherever you are, you are completely lovable right now. **Productivity ≠ worth.**
