# Rebecca Tolin's Recovery

US former TV broadcast journalist. Recovered ~6 years ago from a 13-year ME/CFS arc — the **longest pre-recovery arc** documented on this wiki. Now a Mind-Body coach for LC + ME/CFS patients.

## Onset

- Pre-illness: athletic broadcast journalist; world-traveller; covered nature + science.
- **Sexual assault** in her 30s → shutdown / blackout response, didn't tell anyone, no therapy for years.
- Cluster of viruses in the period after → severe post-viral crash → mecfs.
- Severe fatigue, insomnia, migraines, interstitial cystitis, brain fog, multi-symptom picture; eventually housebound.

## What didn't work — 13-year detour

- **50+ doctors / practitioners** in the biomedical model.
- Targeted EBV with antiviral IV medications → "really just crashed my system more."
- Heavy-metals + mould + chronic Lyme treatments.
- Three years on chicken/fish + green vegetables only (candida + leaky gut protocol) — "more bloated than ever."
- Lost career → federal disability → out of money → very lonely.

## Inflection

- 3-hour conversation with a recovered ME/CFS layperson named **Kathy** (met via online poetry class). Kathy explained the John Sarno / Howard Schubiner framework — symptoms generated by brain + nervous system in heightened-stress state, *not* a pathological process.
- Tolin: *"I got up and ran around the block after that conversation, which I had not run in 13 years."*
- Not a lasting "book cure" — but the cognitive shift + somatic experience together opened the door.

## What worked — recovery practices

1. **Somatic tracking** — central practice. Watch sensations *in the body* with mindfulness — naming the actual quality (burning / tingling / shooting / dullness / numbness / warmth / coolness; widespread vs localised).
2. **Inner-emotional work** — months → years of feeling repressed emotions (anger, grief, shame, fear) physically in the body before they would dissipate or surface as memory.
3. **Personality-trait awareness** — perfectionism, people-pleasing, self-criticism, holding-in-emotions, goodist patterns surfaced and worked with.
4. **Self-compassion** — inner-dialogue shift from criticism + blame-for-not-recovering to *"I'm here with you. I know you're scared. I know you're suffering. I love you anyway."*
5. **Eckhart Tolle present-moment practice** — picked up early in illness because she couldn't plan future given how she felt; integrated this as ongoing meditation orientation.
6. **Gentle yoga + nature time** — these were the pre-mind-body coping tools that gave her enough relief to keep going during the biomedical-rabbit-hole years.

## Now

- ~6 years post-recovery. Mind-Body coach for LC + ME/CFS. Says she finally feels grateful for what the experience cultivated in her — though emphatically not grateful for the suffering itself.

## Pattern flags

- **Capital-T trauma → cluster-of-viruses → post-viral crash → 13-year ME/CFS** — this is one of the wiki's clearer sequencings of trauma-as-precursor.
- **Sarno / Schubiner / Gordon mind-body lineage** — load-bearing. Same lineage as Esther #32, Julie Black #109, Becca Kennedy #146 (clinician), Adams #145.
- **Longest documented detour through the biomedical model** before finding the inflection — a useful boundary case for the wiki's "tests can be true but not the answer" framing (Tolin's labs showed real findings; treating them missed the autonomic root cause).
