# Jenny Adams's recovery arc

A **~12-year arc** with 8 years to diagnosis and 3–4 years wheelchair-bound at the worst phase. The 2017–2019 window was the recovery proper.

## Timeline

- **Age 15 (~2006)** — glandular fever; never fully recovered.
- **8 years undiagnosed** — adult life of "everyone says they're tired, I must just be an adult." Pushed through; multiple jobs; abusive relationships; landlord chaos.
- **2015** — car accident; nervous system already maxed. Crashed hard. Wheelchair, mobility scooter, housebound.
- **2017** — turning point. Lying in bed at her mum's house, fed up with pain, decided to *get curious* — listened to the screaming knee instead of fighting it. Pain quietened.
- **End 2017** — saw a private doctor (infectious-diseases interest) who took a 1-hour history. Connected the trauma stack to the illness for the first time. Started multi-component work.
- **2018** — Pilates in a chronic-illness-friendly studio + talk therapy weekly + vegan dietary reset + safe-movement + boundary work.
- **May 2018** — last use of mobility scooter (at a Beyoncé concert).
- **Aug 2019** — Ibiza solo, partying, on Tinder. Year-of-freedom catching up on missed decade.
- **2019 onward** — substantively recovered. Then stably so. Now coaches others.

## What she identifies as load-bearing

1. **Got curious about pain instead of fighting it** — "tantruming toddler" framing. Listening shifted the relationship.
2. **A doctor who listened for an hour** — named the trauma-stack-as-substrate. First time anyone connected the dots.
3. **Pilates in chronic-illness-friendly studio** (Bristol). "If you just need to lie down for the hour, that's fine." Sense of belonging + safe movement.
4. **Vegan diet (6 years stable)** — "like someone put my batteries back in." Inflammation quieted; then movement became possible.
5. **Talk therapy weekly** — saw the pattern of attracting controlling partners; broke codependency.
6. **Boundary work** — out of codependent relationships, ditched unsupportive friends, learned to ask for what she needs.

## Tools she uses now (post-recovery)

- **Internal Family Systems** (Dick Schwartz, *No Bad Parts*). "What does this part need? Can the protectors move aside?" Drives off in a huff → IFS exercise in the car → home, regulated.
- **Compassionate Inquiry** training (Gabor Maté) — now treats PTSD/cPTSD clients.
- **Cycle-aware living** — names her luteal phase, asks for hugs, watches TV, doesn't beat herself up. "I work *with* it now."
- **Mantras / reminders** — "if you can get into it, you can get out of it" (her mum's driving advice).

## Distinctive framings

- **"Familiarity becomes safety, even when familiar is sickness or trauma."** Patients sometimes unconsciously seek what they've always known.
- **"The way you got here is not the way you'll get out."** Pre-illness life patterns are not the recovery template.
- **"You survived worse with no tools — you can survive this with the tools you have."** Reminder she gives to PTSD / cPTSD clients.
- **"Recovery costs money."** Acknowledged as a structural problem; she built [[heal-good]] to be affordable in response.

## Wiki position

17th full recovery. Distinct contributions:
- **IFS** as a post-recovery dysregulation tool.
- **ADHD-burnout → CFS** sub-pattern flag — increasingly seen in female clients.
- **Vegan diet n=1** — counterweights keto/carnivore framings already on the wiki.
- **Gabor Maté / compassionate inquiry** lineage anchored.
