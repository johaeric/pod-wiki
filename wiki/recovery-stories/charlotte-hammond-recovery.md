# Charlotte Hammond — Recovery Story (Onset October 2021 → Recovered ~Late 2022)

Eighth full recovery on the wiki. UK; ex-school-teacher; rapid arc with multi-component recovery + an unspecified medication that "sped it up."

## Pre-LC profile

- Head of English + MFL at a UK secondary school.
- "12-hour-day" school culture; pressure to never take a break.
- Type-A; "not great at giving herself a break."
- Birmingham; lived alone; cats.

## Onset (Oct 2021)

- Caught COVID at school.
- Acute moderate; never hospitalisation-grade.
- Tried 3 days back at work — got significantly worse.
- Decided couldn't keep the job.

## The crash + life reorganisation

- ~4 months in: **moved back to Buckinghamshire to live with parents.** Rented out her house; kept afloat financially; "best decision."
- Improvement started after the move + family support — social-isolation-relief was the inflection.

## What helped

### Step 1 — A private doctor who'd had LC himself

Recognised LC as real; treated whole-person not symptom-by-symptom. Prescribed supplements + **antihistamines**. Gave permission to celebrate small wins. Validated her experience. **Helped her find Baseline faster than NHS could.**

### Step 2 — Diet (sugar-free + gluten-free)

Reports clear benefit. (Different shape from #96 Makeeva's lectin-free; same anti-inflammatory direction.)

### Step 3 — Cold water + cold showers

Joined the wiki's cold-water-helps cohort. **Cold showers as entry-point** — accessible without water access.

### Step 4 — Antihistamines

Prescribed by private doctor — MCAS-adjacent or histamine-sensitive sub-cohort.

### Step 5 — Therapy via a UK charity (£25/session, ~20 sessions)

**The partner-can't-substitute-for-therapist principle articulated explicitly:**

> "Talking to a therapist, you can properly vent. Having someone with no vested interest is so important. My parents — much as they love me — were upset for me every time I got worse. That puts pressure on you."

### Step 6 — Family support

Parents believed her, could see what worked + didn't. Living with people who back you up matters.

### Step 7 — Unspecified medication ("sped it up")

She references a medication she can't disclose details of that converted a slow trajectory to a faster one. Not actionable since unidentified, but logged for completeness.

## Post-recovery residuals

- **Sensory sensitivity** — can't tolerate large groups for as long as she used to; sounds/lights bother her more.
- **Lower stamina** — building strength back is its own phase.
- **Hypervigilance** — every twinge can trigger "is LC back?" — trauma residue.

> "I would class myself as not having LC anymore but I'm not as fit as I was. My body's taken a battering."

## On the mental gift of LC

> "I'm so much more mentally resilient now. I'm a totally different person. I know myself better."

Comfort with emotions in herself + others. More natural connection.

## Advice to past-self

- Quit your job (but wouldn't have listened).
- You're not as alone as you feel.
- Everything is temporary.
- Get therapy.
