## Arc

| Phase | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-illness | <2021 | NHS physiotherapist, age 40, "always on", CrossFit, mountain climbing, two boys aged ~10 and 12 |
| Acute COVID | autumn 2021 | Got infected during her own work in NHS Long COVID rehab service development |
| Crash | autumn 2021 → spring 2022 | ~16 symptoms — POTS-pattern, couldn't raise her voice, couldn't drive, couldn't care for her children. Sledgehammer. |
| Anger | year 1 | "Doing everything right" but still crashing |
| First win | year 1 | Noticed digesting food drained her — tried **low-histamine diet** → within 24h body's "revving" calmed, restorative sleep returned. Hope. |
| Building | year 2 | Added breathwork, meditation, yoga nidra (lying down), regular activity-rest cycles |
| Recovery near-complete | spring 2024 | Back at work 30hrs, parenting, hiking, paddle-boarding |
| Curveball | 2024 | Thyroid lump → half-thyroidectomy → was cancer (contained). Migraines and tinnitus disappeared with the thyroid. Possibly never were LC symptoms. |
| Full recovery | autumn 2024 | New lease of life. Started Sage Health and Wellness. |

## Turning points

1. **Recognising digesting food as an energy drain** — the fish-and-chips meal that left her unable to walk for two days. Made nutrition the first lever.
2. **Low-histamine diet** — first concrete intervention with felt benefit
3. **Reframing rest** — from cultural shoulds to physiological need

## What she'd tell her past self

> "Take your foot off your neck. You're enough. However you show up, however greasy your hair, you're valuable as you are."

## Disclaimer

Anecdotal evidence — N=1 patient testimony. Karen is now offering paid coaching, so weigh framing accordingly. The thyroid-cancer overlap means *some* of her ascribed-to-LC symptoms may not have been LC.
