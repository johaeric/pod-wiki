# Rachael — full LC recovery (UK; mind-body + environment route)

Ex-competitive-tennis player, corporate professional, personal trainer, married, with a toddler born during her illness. Recovery arc spans roughly 2 years.

## Pre-illness lead-up

Both Rachael and her wife had recently completed IVF; wife was 7 weeks pregnant when Rachael got Covid in July 2021. Rachael was running online PT sessions for colleagues every morning, then doing 10–12 hour workdays, and training for a marathon — the lockdown-era classic high-output pattern. Reflects retrospectively that "Joe Wicks was the smart one when he said he needed a rest. I didn't."

## Two phases of illness

### Phase 1 — boom-bust (~6 months)

Pushed through; relatively mild illness with crashes interspersed. Late 2021 had a panic attack hiding in a work toilet; boss sent her home for the rest of the year. Reinfected during that rest period.

### Phase 2 — bedbound (~12+ months)

Reinfection collapsed her into bedbound/housebound. Met an ME specialist who told her LC was ME and non-recoverable. **2 months pre-daughter's birth, suicidal ideation.** Daughter born February 2022 with Rachael still very ill.

## What turned it around (her sequence)

1. **Stopped working.** Sat down with her wife, looked at the finances, agreed worst-case was acceptable. *Biggest catalyst.*
2. **Suzy Bolt's program.** First gentle-movement community session: broke down at the chat afterwards. Liz (a fellow participant) reached out and told her about Gupta. Couldn't afford face-to-face → went with Curable.
3. **Curable app (~$50/year).** Bite-size neuroscience modules. Listened to a 14-year-ME recovery story → formed her own hypothesis: she had been *conditioned* to need bed. Refused to lie down regardless of how bad she felt; 2–3 weeks later didn't routinely need to. Doubled down for ~12 more months.
4. **Move from London flat to countryside.** Body audibly exhaled.
5. **Cold-water exposure (second attempt).** First attempt at 6 months in: awful, made fatigue worse. ~12 months later, after the rest of the stack had stabilised her: game-changing.

## Recovery line

No single line in the sand. Glastonbury 2023 on a friend's shoulders during Elton John's *Rocket Man* — first time she ever told herself she was proud. By spring 2024: residual immune-system catch-up still ongoing (toddler-sourced bugs hit harder than her wife's), but otherwise fully engaged in life including back-country skiing, uphill skiing, music festivals, extended bike trips.

## What she'd tell past Rachael

1. Stop working *immediately*.
2. Drop the ego about identity and timelines.
3. Try not to respond with so much anxiety to the condition itself (acknowledges this is hard).
4. **Movement with joy.** Same physical exertion in a different emotional frame doesn't trigger the same fear / PEM association. Examples that worked for her: swings with her daughter, drum-and-bass dancing with toddler on her back, walking the village with toddler in a backpack.

## Now

Founder, Rebound Athletic — 12-week online reconditioning program for de-trained / post-LC bodies. ~50% of clients are LC.
