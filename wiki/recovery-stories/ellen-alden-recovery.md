# Ellen Alden's Recovery (substantial; multi-modal stack; LCP-157 cross-corpus)

Manchester MA author. Got Covid Christmas 2021. Long covid → severe (couldn't read or write — devastating for an author). ~3-year multi-modal recovery via MBSR + TMS / Sarno + Pain Reprocessing Therapy training + breathwork + cold plunging + visualisation. Now MBSR-certified, certified breath coach, runs LC / CFS workshops + retreats + 1:1 coaching.
