# Lily Spechler — full LC recovery (US; Levine + nutrition route)

US registered dietitian who recovered from her own LC and shifted her practice to LC nutrition.

## Phase 1 — disorientation

Standard LC presentation; eventually pots diagnosis. Connected through chance with someone in a Mayo Clinic study running a modified Levine protocol. Says she would now **never** recommend doing the protocol the way she did — went in blind. Worked for her largely because she was already a dietitian and could match the metabolic load.

## What turned it around

1. **Discovered she was underweight without intending it** — laboured breathing was burning enormous extra calories. Started reading the COPD-nutrition literature and added ~1,000 kcal/day to a baseline 2,000-kcal diet.
2. **High-calorie / high-protein / high-fibre nutrition** stabilised the muscle-wasting and immune-cell starvation pattern.
3. **Modified Levine protocol** — her autonomic-rehab arm. She acknowledges this is treatment-strategy-tied-to-her-physiology and not a generic recommendation.
4. **Combined the two.** "I absolutely accredit recovery to muscle health and fitness + autonomic resiliency."

## Where she is now

Functional in a way that means: back-country skiing, uphill skiing, downhill skiing, extended bike trips, music festivals, travel. Co-launched the **Energy Expansion Project** group program with Dr Meg Anderson, PT — combines self-paced autonomic-rehab modules + nutrition modules + two weekly group calls.

## What she'd tell past Lily

- Match metabolic needs *adjusted for disease state* — not just body size.
- Track on Chronometer (free) — see micronutrient gaps, not just calories.
- "Pacing ≠ inactivity." Once stable, gradual movement is essential. Deconditioning is itself a risk.
- Don't override your internal voice with an external one — if an intervention feels wrong, pause and reassess. You aren't in a position to experiment to the point of crisis.
