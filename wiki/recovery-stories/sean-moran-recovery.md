# Sean Moran's recovery arc

A **~10–12 year arc** (April 2012 → ~2022). One of the longest pre-Covid ME/CFS recovery arcs on the wiki. Recovery shape: **toxin-burden + chronic-infection remediation** rather than nervous-system / brain-retraining / mind-body. This adds an important shape variant to the wiki's recovery-pattern landscape.

## Timeline

- **Late 2011 / early 2012** — vulnerable-to-everything phase. Working full-time as a barristers' clerk in Melbourne; frequently sick. Saving for a one-way ticket to Europe.
- **April 2012** — diagnosed CFS. Trip cancelled. Threw most of his savings at IV vitamin C, ozone therapy, glutathione injections — none clearly helpful.
- **2012–2017 (Years 1–5)** — kitchen-sink phase. Tried acupuncture, cupping, EFT tapping, homeopathy, brain retraining, chi gong, restorative yoga, chiropractic, hypnotherapy, GET, CBT, bioresonance, matrix reimprinting. Some maybe contributed marginally; no single one was load-bearing in retrospect.
- **~2017–2018** — clawed back to ~50% function. Working part-time. Still significantly limited; no sport, no full-time work, residual cognitive issues.
- **2020 (covid lockdown)** — Working remotely under an air-conditioning split-system unit riddled with mould. **Major relapse.** Couldn't keep up walking with his 30-years-older father.
- **2020–2022 (the "detective" phase)** — engaged a building biologist; integrative GP; functional-medicine practitioner. Functional lab tests:
  - Mercury + lead high on microtoxin panel.
  - **MARCoNS** sinus colonisation confirmed.
  - **Dental infection** identified.
- **2020–2022** — **Christopher Shade / Quicksilver Scientific lineage detoxification protocol** with practitioner supervision. ~18–20 months. Sean's framing: "the art of detoxification." First DIY-attempted it and got much sicker — that became the trigger to engage a knowledgeable practitioner. Slow, gentle, drainage-funnel-first.
- **~2021–2022** — **30-day MARCoNS protocol** from a US recovered-practitioner. Single biggest cognitive step-change of the entire recovery: *"My brain just started to switch on for the first time in 9–10 years."*
- **~2021–2022** — **dental extraction** of the infected tooth. Step-change in physical + cognitive energy literally overnight.
- **~2022 onward** — substantively recovered. Now coaching LC + CFS patients; running, gym, full-time work.

## What he identifies as load-bearing

1. **Mould remediation** + recognition that the AC unit was a chronic ongoing exposure source. Building biologist + integrative GP made the call.
2. **Functional lab testing** as the diagnostic gate — mercury, lead, microtoxins, MARCoNS swab, dental imaging.
3. **Heavy-metal detox via Christopher Shade protocol** (slow, drainage-funnel-first, multi-month). Practitioner supervision was essential — DIY was actively harmful.
4. **MARCoNS clearance** — 30-day protocol from a US recovered-practitioner. Brain came back online.
5. **Dental extraction** — step-change in energy.

## What he tried and doesn't credit

The **kitchen-sink list** (per #154 transcript): IV vitamin C, ozone therapy, acupuncture, cupping, EFT tapping, homeopathy, brain retraining, chi gong, restorative yoga, chiropractic, hypnotherapy, graded exercise therapy, CBT, bioresonance therapy, matrix reimprinting, glutathione injections.

Sean's framing: each *might* have helped a bit but none stand out in retrospect. He describes this as the **"scattergun" trap** — desperation drives trying everything, which makes it impossible to attribute outcome. His advice to past self: don't expedite; find one practitioner who looks at *you*, run targeted tests, fix what's actually driving your fatigue.

These items are **not** entered as `no_effect` rows on their respective pages — that classification implies a clean trial. They're entered as `mentioned_only` (named in passing without an attributable outcome).

## Distinctive framings

- **"You go from full-time employee to full-time patient."**
- **"50% wasn't satisfying"** — he didn't pause to appreciate progress because the goal was always 100%; this prevented him from learning what had helped.
- **"Don't look backwards, you're not going that way."** A quote he found post-recovery and put on social media; useful for the up-and-down nature of recovery progress.
- **Mould → detox → MARCoNS → dental** as a working sequence: address chronic exposure first; then prepare drainage; then mobilise toxins slowly; then clear hidden infections; then deal with oral cavities. *Order matters.*
