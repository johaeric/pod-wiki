# Aaron West's Recovery

US (South Carolina) IT manager + film enthusiast + business-builder. Recovered from 2.5 years of long covid via an **Omicron reinfection on January 5, 2024** — confirmed by his own GP (20 years his physician) as a "light-switch recovery."

## Pre-illness

- Highly athletic — ride-up-every-mountain-in-America project; once cycled 237 miles across South Carolina in one day.
- IT career; manager-level remote-work patterns during early pandemic.

## Long-covid course (Fall 2021 → Jan 5 2024)

- **Acute infection** Fall 2021 (Delta).
- **Post-acute cough** ~1 year, ~500-1,000 coughs/day at peak; muting Zoom meetings became second-nature.
- **6 months in** — diagnosed by epidemiologist father, then GP. Pharmacological stack: Zyrtec, Pepcid (sometimes Claritin), beta blockers (the strongest helper for PEM), benzodiazepines (saved him at the worst — tapered post-recovery).
- **Symptom load**:
  - Severe insomnia (14 h sleep scattered over 24 h periods, palpitations on dream wake-up)
  - "Electromagnetic pulse coursing through veins" — adrenaline surges
  - Spike anxiety
  - Severe brain fog (forgot his cofounder's name mid-conversation; she developed memory-trick prompts to recover his train of thought)
  - **GI dysfunction** — vomiting daily for periods, lower-GI issues
  - **Smell + taste alteration** — everything tasted *amazing*; vegetables that he disliked pre-illness craved; led to poor eating habits → 9 months on keto post-recovery to rebalance
- **Severity**: bedbound much of the time; couldn't sit at a desktop computer for >1 h; built a startup business + website on iPad/iPhone from bed for ~year.
- **Pacing**: HR ceiling 100 bpm walking, spoon theory framing, 2-3 day rest after meaningful activity.

## The light-switch event (Jan 5 2024)

- Wife caught Omicron at a meeting; same household → Aaron caught it Jan 5.
- **Took Paxlovid.**
- Day after positive test (Saturday): worked productively for hours, more than at any point in 2.5 years.
- Sunday: ran 2 blocks (masked, still acutely infected) to identify a neighbourhood criminal; *did not look at his HR.* Realised mid-run: the long covid was gone.
- Fog cleared *during the acute infection* before its symptoms had even resolved.
- Sleep dropped from 10-14 h → 2 h initially; took ~6 months to normalise to ~6 h.
- His GP told him post-recovery he had thought Aaron was too far gone to recover; saw the difference at first sight.

## Now

- Recovered. Wrote a book on the A24 film label; ran a Kickstarter; binge-watching 6 movies in a research day.
- Reports cognitive ability *better than pre-illness* on creative tasks (the GP speculated Omicron-recovery sequence may have stimulated brain activity in a way that pulled forward creative capacity — speculative).
- Left the 50,000-person Facebook LC support group ~6 months post-recovery: "after a while, people were asking me for proof I'd recovered. It was time to graduate."

## Aaron's caveats

- "Don't go out to get covid." Several friends in his LC support group hoped for the same outcome — most did not get it.
- He recognises this is rare and tells the story carefully. Doctor said he had seen this "a couple of other times" in his 20-year practice.

## Pattern flags

- **20th full-recovery on the wiki**, *first* clear "reinfection-as-healing" anchor.
- **Reinfection theme is bidirectional**: most reinfections are negative (cf. [[077-suzy-bolt-reinfection]] — the fear-of-reinfection anchor; [[037-dr-asad-khan]] — clinical observation of reinfection-as-relapse-trigger). Aaron is the wiki's first patient-side recovery-via-reinfection.
- **Confounded data point**: Paxlovid + Omicron + still-active pharmacological stack (antihistamines + beta-blockers + benzodiazepines) + 2.5 years of accumulated systemic recovery → all coincided. No single-factor mechanistic claim is logged.

## Eric-relevant takeaway

Aaron's pre-recovery stack was structurally similar to Eric's — **antihistamines (incl famotidine) + beta-blocker for HR + autonomic-dampening agents** — and got him through 2.5 years of long covid. The recovery itself is rare and not engineerable. The validating finding for Eric: this stack is a reasonable bridge while the deeper recovery factors play out.
