# Sam Munslow's Recovery — Coaching / Mental-Skill Rebuild

**Onset**: 5 April 2020 (very early UK wave). **Recovered**: ~2021-2022. **Duration**: ~18-24 months. The wiki's **fifth** full recovery and the first where the "what worked" wasn't an intervention but a sustained psychological-skill rebuild.

## The shape of the arc

- **April 2020** — acute breathlessness; husband doing nightly breath checks for months. Untested (early UK wave).
- **Months bedbound** — couldn't read, joint pain, insomnia, skin so hyperaesthetic the shower felt like hailstones.
- **Mid-2020 → 2021** — slow recovery. Diary later assembled from social-media posts.
- **End-of-book relapse** — kept in deliberately so the book doesn't present recovery as a clean line.
- **By recording (April 2022)** — fully well. Persistent chemical-sensitivity legacy (no hair dye / makeup) is consistent with MCAS-overlap (likely flagged by her own family or a Dr Peers-like protocol if pursued).

## What worked, in her framing

1. **Mental self-harming awareness** — recognise the inner-critic loop. Write down what you say to yourself; re-read; it loses force.
2. **Language-as-treatment** — "rest days" instead of "bad days." "Resentment of the situation, not of the person."
3. **Live in the now** — predicting "I'll never run again" guarantees a loss; today's question is "what can I do *today*."
4. **Communicate openly** — give the people supporting you permission to hate the situation. Communication is the through-line that kept the family functional.
5. **See it from their perspective** — only months later did she recognise her husband's nightly breath-check as traumatic *for him*; that recognition reshaped how she received care.

## Caveats

- No claimed magic intervention here; the pace was slow and non-linear.
- Permanent chemical-sensitivity legacy suggests an underlying MCAS-style change that wasn't addressed pharmacologically; "recovery" is shape-of-life, not pre-illness baseline.
