# Sarah (Lotus Path Yogi) — full LC recovery

UK yoga teacher (Sheffield); caught Covid first wave (March 2020); full LC for ~2 years; declared recovery spring 2022. **11th full recovery on the wiki.**

See [[114-sarah-lotus-path-yogi-recovery]] for full episode summary.

## Recovery arc

- **March 2020**: acute Covid hit hard (lung-focused; pneumonia + ground-glass on x-ray; SpO2 88; hospital A&E but discharged); ~3 weeks of feeling normal before LC
- **March 2020 → early 2022**: bedbound at worst; POTS diagnosed Sept 2020 via tilt; beta blockers helped first
- **2021**: discovered the Body Politic Slack early — knowledge-as-power inflection
- **2021 onwards**: built recovery stack from multiple sources (Myhill protocol + Raelan Agle channel + her own yoga background + fermented-food microbiome work)
- **Spring 2022**: 5+ months without PEM; doing 5-7 mile walks; declared recovery in Body Politic's "recovered" channel

## Recovery stack (her named order)

1. **Body Politic Slack** — validation + knowledge-as-power
2. **Beta blockers** ([[beta-blockers]]) post-tilt-test — first concrete win
3. **Pacing + activity tracking**
4. **Yoga nidra + meditations + breath-work practice 10-12× per day** — single biggest factor for nervous-system + pacing stability
5. **[[118-dr-sarah-myhill|Dr Sarah Myhill protocol]]**: paleo ketogenic (9 months); B12 injections (for neuropathy + fatigue); D-ribose powder ([[ribose]]) — felt within 1-2 weeks; mitochondrial supplements
6. **Gluten-free diet** — Sarah's interpretation: keto helped largely because it removed gluten (post-Covid intolerance)
7. **[[107-raelan-agle-recovery-stories|Raelan Agle's YouTube]]** — recovery stories for hope; scaffolding-first graded resistance protocol (1 minute body-weight resistance per day, increase weekly)
8. **Fermented foods** ([[probiotics]]) — Raelan recommendation; food-based, not capsule
9. **Gradual movement reintroduction** — 1 → 15 min/day resistance; walks 1 → 7 miles
10. **Daily yoga (the right kind)** — supportive, no inversions, no fast vinyasa

## What didn't work

- **Capsule probiotics for a year** — no isolatable effect; Sarah believes they may have been dead. Switched to food-based fermented foods (kombucha, kefir, etc) and saw real change after a brief Herx-reaction.
- **NHS chronic fatigue clinic activity-management protocol** — she recognised this as GET-coded and discharged herself before doing it.

## Why this story matters on the wiki

- **Multi-stack recovery**: shows how patients integrate multiple frameworks (Myhill biochemical + Raelan scaffolding-first + her own yoga + microbiome) rather than one protocol fixing everything.
- **D-ribose anecdote** is the wiki's strongest single signal for [[ribose]].
- **Patient-side validation of [[118-dr-sarah-myhill]] protocol** + [[107-raelan-agle-recovery-stories]] scaffolding-first.
