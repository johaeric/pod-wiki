# Kristine's Recovery (post-viral ME/CFS, fast)

Latvian living in Denmark. Severe insomnia + sleep paralysis after a Covid-vaccine + virus stack triggered post-viral ME/CFS. Failed to find traction with supplements, sleep pills, alternative therapies, Headspace meditation, or her psychologist's body-scan / relaxation work. Full recovery via Jason's brain-retraining program (the same coach Kristyna RA #147 found via the same Raelan-channel discovery chain).

## Inflection timeline

- **Week 1 with Jason**: slept 8–9 hours through the night for the first time in months.
- **Weeks 4–5**: skiing 8 am to 4 pm for a week in Norway.
- **~3 months**: full recovery.

## Recovery components

1. **Jason's brain-retraining program** — load-bearing. Visualisations 10–20× per day, identity / self-confidence training, "self-fulfilling expectations" framing.

That's the only component she names. Her recovery is single-vector, not multi-component.

## What she tried that didn't work

- Multiple supplements
- Sleep pills (single, double, triple doses)
- Massage and alternative therapies
- Headspace meditation app (religious daily practice, did not help her)
- Psychologist sessions with body scans and relaxation (kind clinician, but didn't move the needle for her)
