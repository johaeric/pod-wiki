# Donna Shaw's Recovery (full, multi-component, ~2 years)

Surrey UK first-wave long-hauler. Severe acute Covid April 2020 (two ER visits, scans clean) → severe LC → October 2020 crash (post-birthday-cake-and-restaurant) → near-bedbound for months → multi-component recovery stack over ~2 years → full recovery. Now a qualified life coach helping LC patients with anxiety.

## Pre-illness pattern

Healthy, no underlying conditions, never been ill — the *"I'm always fine"* identity. About to be promoted at her school/college job.

## Long-covid course

- **Acute (April 2020)**: severe dyspnea, throat-closing sensation, two ER visits, both came back "no pneumonia, fine, go home."
- **Months 0–6**: terrified, gasping awake at night, severe anxiety + depression + leg pain. Slow improvement to *"not dying anymore"* by ~12 weeks.
- **October 2020 — the cake crash**: pushed through her daughter's 21st birthday cake + drive to Brighton + restaurant. From that day onwards bedbound; couldn't lift a glass; couldn't decide; couldn't think.
- **Diagnosis**: GP suggested CFS, 4–5 month wait. Harley-Street CFS doctor: *"5% recover; buy this book; good luck."*

## The pivot — finding the right people

Her daughter said *"there can't be just you that Covid affected like this"* — Donna found Raelan's channel on Facebook + Google. Through Raelan's interviews she found: Pamela Rose (her main coach), Hill with Liz (Liz's affirmation/vision-board work), Alex Howard's Optimum Health Clinic, and "Sophie" (Sopfit on Instagram).

## Recovery components (multi-component, no single key)

1. **Pamela Rose coaching** — found her baseline (which was nothing) and built from there. *"This is not forever, it's for now."*
2. **Optimum Health Clinic / Alex Howard** — *"healing state vs. stress state"* meta-frame. Mindset + ANS work.
3. **Sopfit / Sophie's graduated walking** — countered earlier "don't walk while legs hurt" advice with *"you cannot not move."* Started at 30 seconds in the snow on her front lawn; built to 20 min over 6 months.
4. **Hill with Liz affirmations + vision board** — paddle-boarding + 25th wedding anniversary holiday + a porn-star martini. ~6 weeks to feel like a different person.
5. **Heal-the-gut nutrition program** (12 weeks with a nutritionist).
6. **Magnesium-salt baths** for leg pain.
7. **Camper-van micro-outings** as graduated re-exposure to the outside.
8. **Buddy system** for new LC patients — emotional support work that contributed to her own recovery.

## What contradicted earlier advice

A clinician early on told her *"don't walk while legs hurt — it's a warning."* She didn't walk for 3 months. Sopfit's *"you cannot not move"* — with proper micro-dosing — was what unlocked her movement axis. **Pattern worth holding**: the don't-move-while-symptomatic advice can be wrong for some patients; sub-30-second graduated movement is a different intervention than walking-through-pain.

## What helped: meta-framing

Donna's strongest takeaway: *"Your body is built to heal — but you have to be in a healing state to do that."* Pairs with [[polyvagal-theory]] and Roberto Escobar's framing (RA #221).
