## Arc

| Phase | What |
|---|---|
| Pre-illness | COVID-cautious, masking |
| Acute COVID | Felt "poisoned" days 3–8, partial recovery, then crash |
| LC onset | Tachycardia → POTS → PEM → light/sound sensitivity (earplugs + noise-cancelling headphones constantly) |
| Detox protocol harm | Engaged with a 50-supplement protocol that reframed every adverse reaction as "die-off". Got progressively worse. |
| Severe bedbound | Mother became carer for two months at her mother's house. Then 8-hr car journey home triggered deeper crash. Couldn't crawl to toilet. |
| Mental crisis | Couldn't be the mother she wanted to be. Became suicidal, asked people for knives. Hospitalised. |
| Hospital phase (3 weeks) | Clinic 19 care: POTS meds (modest), prescribed but never took LDN, started **olanzapine** (an antipsychotic) — stabilised sleep, helped overall. **NOT recommended** by Jamie as a treatment — she's still tapering. |
| First belief | Heard from uncle (CFS recovery) and a hospital physio (CFS recovery) that recovery was possible |
| First action | Took zinc on protocol creator's insistence + got up to walk to toilet — Jamie attributes this to belief/placebo, not zinc itself |
| Reframe | Wrote on her hand "this is just a hypersensitive nervous system"; took off the headphones permanently |
| Brain retraining (Primal Trust) | Light-visualisation while gently expanding capacity. Daily walks slightly further. Self-talk reassurance. |
| Group coaching (Healing Dudes) | Win-celebration culture, no symptom-talk |
| Lightning Process (90% mark) | 3-day intensive. Theory of stuck fight-or-flight clicked. Used as final-stretch tool. |
| Recovery declaration | When she went back to work (full-time, 4 days/week) |

## Turning points

1. **Hearing two real-life recovery stories** while hospitalised
2. **Reframing the illness as "hypersensitive nervous system"**
3. **Primal Trust** brain retraining + group coaching
4. **Lightning Process** for the final 10%

## What she'd tell her past self

> "You're going to be okay. People are recovering and you'll be one of them."

## Disclaimer

Anecdotal evidence — N=1 patient testimony. Olanzapine is **not** a treatment for LC; it was an emergency psychiatric medication. The Lightning Process is controversial — Jamie's read is that it can be useful late but possibly harmful early.
