# Adam Langdon's Recovery (substantial-in-progress, ~90-95%)

Oakville Ontario; Type-A pre-illness (gym 5×/week, 50hr work, small kids). Got mild Covid Christmas 2022 (Omicron) + returned to gym after 1-2 days rest → massive crash week 2 with severe **neurological long covid**: depersonalization, derealization, anxiety, panic 6-7/day, depression, loss of emotion, tinnitus, "burning brain," head pressure.

## Recovery timeline

~6-7 months in: started piecing together protocols from Reddit's long-covid recovery subreddit. Multi-component recovery built over the next year. ~90-95% recovered at 18 months.

## Recovery components

1. **Tom Bunker's fasting protocol** (intermittent → 24hr → 72hr → 3-day) — load-bearing. *"Felt amazing during; crashed after eating — refeed matters."*
2. **Fluvoxamine** off-label for brain inflammation. He participated in the Toronto CMHA PET-scan study that **confirmed brain inflammation in LC** patients (published June 2023).
3. **Sauna + cold water immersion** combined.
4. **Meditation + breathwork** (apps: Insight Timer, Breathwrk — has LC-specific sessions).
5. **Grounding** (barefoot, grounding mat).
6. **Daylio symptom-tracking app** — pattern recognition over weeks/months.

## Notable framing

- **Foundational vs. trial interventions**: meditation, breathwork, grounding, sauna, cold water = run in parallel always. Supplements / medications / fasting = trial one at a time.
- **Patient-led research as the actual LC frontier** — Tom Bunker's protocol going from forum → clinical trials is his cleanest example.
