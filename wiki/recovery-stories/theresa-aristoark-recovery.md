## Arc

| Phase | When | What |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-illness | <2023 | Software engineer, Seattle. Yoga, half marathons, social. |
| First COVID | early 2021 | Mild — recovered fully in ~2 weeks |
| Second COVID | Feb 2023 | Misdiagnosed as strep first; antibiotics for 2–3 days before COVID PCR confirmed; missed Paxlovid window |
| Acute → Long COVID | spring 2023 | Severe localised quad-muscle pain on every fever, persistent leg pain, lost ability to walk dog / shop / cook / carry laundry |
| Hidden phase | year 1 | Kept working (could still sit at a desk). Hid illness from everyone except partner Jake. |
| Functional medicine + IVs | mid-2023 | Mitochondrial supplements, IV nutritional therapy, modest benefit. Validation. |
| LDN trial | 2023 | Couldn't tolerate it. |
| 2024 reset | Jan 2024 | Started physical therapy 3x/week + massage 1x/week. Confidence built. |
| Job exit | Apr 2024 | Quit corporate role to focus on recovery |
| Vestibular setback | summer 2024 | New onset symptoms — vertigo, screen intolerance, panic attack 8 weeks before her wedding |
| SSRI inflection | Jun 2024 | Husband-to-be confronted her: physical work has plateaued, mental health is the blocker. SSRI prescribed → step-change improvement |
| Wedding | Aug 2024 | Danced at her wedding |
| Honeymoon | autumn 2024 | 3 weeks travelling Scandinavia |
| Confidence transition | Jan 2025 → 2026 | PT → personal training, lifting heavy. Hiked 7 miles, walked Japan 15k steps/day for 10/14 days, no PEM. |
| Identity shift | ~Mar 2026 | Started saying "I might not be sick anymore" |

## Turning points

1. **Physical therapy at integrated clinic** — 3x/week consistency, believing-clinician + push
2. **Quitting the job** — removed the energy sink that was masking her depletion
3. **SSRI** — released the obsessive health anxiety; physical strength followed within weeks
4. **Talking openly with friends/family/colleagues** — vulnerability → connection → no longer alone

## What she'd tell her past self

> "Where we are now, we're better — not just recovered, better than ever before. Keep going."

## Big takeaways (Theresa's framing)

- Mental health was the unaddressed blocker — physical work plateaued without addressing it
- "I was never going to get better alone"
- Recovery is non-linear, expect the swirl

## Disclaimer

Anecdotal evidence — N=1. SSRI worked for her; this is not a recommendation for everyone. LDN intolerance is also useful negative data.
