# Rachel Whitfield — recovery arc

A focused narrative of [[rachel-whitfield]]'s journey from infection to recovery, drawn from ep #8.

## Pre-illness

Bristol-based leadership trainer, single mum, runner training for marathon, regular cyclist. Heavy travel-and-perform pattern; acknowledges (and friends confirm) she was over-extended for years. Survived a near-fatal scuba accident 10–15 years prior — adopted a "yes drive" attitude that compounded the bucket-filling.

## Acute (December 2020)

Brought home from her ex by her daughter. Symptoms unusually atypical: brain fog and a striking **dread-anxiety** sensation rather than respiratory. Pretended to function for 1.5 weeks, returned to running, then crashed.

## The plateau (Jan–April 2021)

Boom-bust → "just bust". Cancelled all work for 6 weeks. Symptoms migrated: pins/needles, burning back, throat, ribs; grey-skinned, swollen purple toes. Worst single symptom was **insomnia**, broken eventually by mirtazapine + giving up the fight.

Built her own framework from books and podcasts: *The Intelligent Body*, *Breath* (Nestor), *Cure for Chronic Pain* (Sachs), Optimal Health Clinic (Howard), Greenfield's change framework. Accepted the autonomic-nervous-system-stuck-in-fight-flight model.

## Turning point (May 2021)

[[lightning-process]] training course (Zoom, 3 days). Booked a deliberately busy work fortnight after to "force" the recovery. Key reframe absorbed: "leaking bucket of fear, not lacking energy." On day one decided she was well; bike ride to HR 150; 36 hours later the dread came as predicted; she walked through it without crashing.

## Confirmation

Around 31 May 2021: woke up and "just thought, I'm better." Has had echoes (a cold; a stressful school-gate trigger) but no crashes; identifies and de-escalates them.

## Carry-overs

- Self-compassion, not self-criticism, at the early sign of fatigue/dread.
- Outsourcing as an active practice, not a reluctant fallback.
- Yoga + breathwork as routine, not rescue.
- Conscious resistance to re-acquiring her old "yes drive".
