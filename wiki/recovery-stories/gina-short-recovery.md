# Gina Short — Recovery Story (Onset July 2021 → Recovered December 2022)

Sixth full recovery story logged on the wiki.

## Pre-LC profile

- US (Washington State).
- Mum of two boys (then 8 + 16).
- Full-time corporate career.
- Active outdoor enthusiast — hiker, runner, cyclist, multi-mile backpack hikes.
- Self-described "always multi-tasking, every moment full."

## Onset

- Outdoor party for her mum's 80th birthday, July 2021.
- Vaccinated; ~half of 24 guests caught covid.
- Initial acute illness moderate; recovered alongside husband + younger son.
- Lingering shortness of breath — couldn't read aloud to her son comfortably.

## The crash

- Months 3-4 from onset: severe neuro-autonomic cascade.
- Adrenaline surges, HR ~160 putting shoes on.
- Internal tremors, headaches, dizziness.
- Bedbound; multiple naps per day.
- Couldn't drive; couldn't make own meals; showering once every few days.
- Started losing belief that recovery was possible.
- Long-covid-clinic wait was 4 months.

## What turned it around

### Phase 1 — first crack (~month 4-5)

- Found **Suzy Bolt's free 10-minute supine yoga** video.
- Crucial framing in Bolt's video: *"this is temporary."*
- Started doing it before bed; then multiple times a day.

### Phase 2 — joining Fern (Bolt's small-group programme)

- 12-person cohort.
- Mind-body framing; language re-frame; "play again" prescription.
- Started doing **Stasis Breathing programme** alongside.

### Phase 3 — pacing + the pre-planning realisation

- Month 6: noticed she was unconsciously *pre-planning crashes* — anticipating two-day crashes after events.
- Counter-intuitive realisation: needed to *play more and task less* to recover.
- Stripped outside inputs: removed wearable (HR-watching anxiety), stopped news, pulled away from negative-skewed online communities.

### Phase 4 — joy strolls + present-moment

- Drove to trailhead, walked 100m to a bench, sat, drove home.
- Built up gradually from there.
- "Joy mix" music; danced a little while walking.
- "Feeling photos" instead of camera photos at viewpoints.

### Phase 5 — designated-witness sentence

- Told her 16-year-old: every day when you come home, hug me and say *"Mom you're getting better."*
- He did. Eventually he believed it before she did.

### Phase 6 — declaration (Dec 17, 2022)

- Suzy Bolt asked her in a recovery interview *"are you recovered?"* — Gina paused.
- A few days later: wrote a journal entry, *"I am recovered from long covid"*, signed and dated.
- Told family and friends. Telling them shifted her own felt sense.

## Setbacks she rode through

- Mid-recovery: her mother (Parkinson's since age 30) passed away.
- Initial reaction: this will set me back.
- Actually: the support around it + the inner-strength frame she'd built from her mum's example carried her through.

## What she names as the key levers

1. **Suzy Bolt's gentle yoga** as the first crack.
2. **Stasis Breathing** for the breath layer.
3. **Mind-body re-framing** — through Fern programme.
4. **Designated-witness sentence + community** that skewed forward-looking.
5. **Declaration**.
6. **"You don't have to be well to love and to give"** — reframing self-worth away from productivity (mum-as-model).

## What she names as the *wrong* turn she nearly made

- Trying to push through, ignore symptoms, "just lay in bed all day until it lifts."
- Both wrong; a balance between play and rest is the actual answer.
- "Acceptance" misread as "this is forever" was a trap; correct read is "this is now, not forever".

## Quotes

> "When I could see my future self — a *new* version of myself, not the old one — that's when real healing began."

> "I planned my crashes. Once I noticed I was doing it, I had to stop."

> "You don't have to be well to love and to give."
