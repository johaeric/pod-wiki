# Rachel Rock — Recovery Arc

## Pre-illness

Boston-based freelance journalist. Cyclist; trained for the **Pan-Mass Challenge** (PMC, 192-mile two-day cancer-fundraiser ride) for 12+ years. Self-described introvert / extroverted-introvert who already knew her own pattern of needing to retreat after social exposure. ADHD medicated for 20 years.

## Trigger

Mild COVID **May 2022**. Recovered from acute illness "as expected." Got back on the bike — 10 miles — flattened for two days. Repeated; same outcome. Even dog walks. Cardiologist friend: "I don't want to call it long covid, but take it easy — pushing can do damage."

## Diagnostic phase

- BP cuff at her mother-in-law's house revealed significant orthostatic drop on standing.
- Recognised dysautonomia from Jaz Mediger's YouTube content.
- Primary care doc believed her from the first visit; full battery of tests; all major organs fine; no answer.
- Found Putrino's published Mount Sinai LC protocol; couldn't access the clinic from Boston, used the protocol to find a Boston-area physiotherapist who understood it (rehab compared to TBI / stroke recovery).

## Recovery work — the four legs of the stool

The seat of the stool: **the autonomic nervous system**. The four legs:

1. **An ANS practitioner.** [[dr-lydia-knutson]] in Cambridge (chiropractic + energetic medicine + kinesiology). LC protocol, then beyond — Dr Knutson cleared reactivated EBV, herpes zoster, and Lyme over the recovery arc.
2. **Functional-medicine workup** — discovered gluten + dairy allergies, supplemented for measured deficiencies. Several **"fake recoveries"** — declared LC over, only to crash months later — until the viral-reactivation leg was addressed.
3. **Diet** — keto for a phase (helped brain fog), now off keto.
4. **Trauma processing** — Rachel's mother died when Rachel was 15. From the outside she had triumphed; the 15-year-old had never processed it. **EMDR with a trained therapist** was load-bearing. Striking observation: the bodily sensation while in EMDR was *identical* to the chronic-fatigue feeling.

## What also mattered

- **Safe and Sound Protocol** with Jackie — gave her the language and felt-sense for up- vs down-regulation. Had to pause EMDR while doing SSP. The teaching component, not the sound itself, was the lever.
- **Apple Watch HRV** from before LC — pre-illness data baseline let her see her body's signal before her conscious mind did.
- **Acupuncture** weekly to bi-weekly — measurable BP improvement before/after.
- **Reading**: Nestor's *Breath* (epilogue), Ed Young's Atlantic chronic-fatigue piece, Susan Sontag's *Illness as Metaphor*.
- **Eventually returned to ADHD medication** under a chronic-illness-aware **psychopharmacologist** — last piece before going back to work.

## Recovery moment

- **Summer 2024** — rode 86 miles of the PMC (one day, not the full two). Strategy on hills: closed eyes, breathed in 4 / out 6, let everyone else fly past. "Did I just recover from long covid?" Answered by getting **COVID again 10 days later** and clearing it in 2-3 days, no Paxlovid. That was the answer.

## What she tells herself / others

- "Modern medicine cuts and stitches well; chronic systemic dysregulation is what eastern modalities have addressed for thousands of years."
- Daily question: "What is making my nervous system feel unsafe right now?" — and act on the answer.
- The most painful part was **loss of self.**
- Job is to be the **agent of your own healing** — adapt to your environment (Darwinian survival of the *most adaptable*).
