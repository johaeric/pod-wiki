# Kristyna's Recovery (post-viral CFS, ~1 year)

Czech-born, Vienna-based. ~1 year from a probably-Covid post-viral onset (Summer 2020) to full functional recovery (Summer 2021). Bedbound at peak. Recovery driven by an unnamed brain-retraining coach found via Raelan Agle's channel + quitting her stressful job. Kambo (frog-poison ceremony) used at the late stage for emotional release at ~95% recovery.

## Pre-illness pattern (predisposing)

- 1–2 years of cyclical infection: lyme, tonsillitis, multiple antibiotic courses
- **Panic attacks at night** — explicit suppressed wake-up call
- Outwardly fine; internally chronically stressed in a high-pressure job she didn't want

## Long-covid course

- **Summer 2020 onset**: viral-feeling illness, no positive test (timing). *"My body felt like it was faking the illness"* — atypical from her own infection history.
- **Months 1–6**: progressive deterioration → bedbound. Whole-body pain; severe sore throat; 30 min effort to speak a sentence.
- **Many specialist visits**: all "perfectly healthy" reports.
- **Month 5**: an Austrian neurologist diagnosed post-viral fatigue / CFS in 30 minutes; recommended pacing; recommended LDN; **never responded to her follow-up calls** when she asked to start it. **LDN never taken.** That experience was the moment she stopped seeking medical care.

## The pivot

Following Raelan Agle's YouTube recovery interviews (re-watched some 5–10×), Kristyna chose what resonated for her. She framed her own pattern as **stress → mind-body lock**, and the brain-retraining axis felt right.

She found **a brain-retraining coach named "Jason" through Raelan's channel** (specific identity not given on transcript). Did his program for ~2–3 months as her primary work.

## Recovery components (in her own ranking)

1. **Brain-retraining program** — load-bearing. *"The hardest thing I've ever done."* Discipline + commitment + confronting her own perfectionism / monkey-mind. Mental-shift gains in days; physical gains in weeks (gentle skiing at week 2–3); functional recovery by month ~3.
2. **Quitting her high-stress job** at ~month 6 — frees her to focus 100% on recovery.
3. **[[kambo]] ×2 at ~95% recovery** — emotional / trauma release at the late stage; not the physical recovery driver. Plans yearly cycles.
4. **Structured daily self-care** during severe phase: hot/cold showers, dry brushing, celery juice, supplements. Mostly placebo / scaffolding by her own assessment. Supplements explicitly *no effect*.

## What didn't help

- **Supplements** — significant spend over months; *"I haven't seen the difference with or without"*
- **Acupuncture** — tried; didn't believe in it; got nothing. *"It is also really important to do what you believe it helps."* (One of the cleaner first-person articulations of belief / expectancy effect on this corpus.)
- **Doctors / Western medicine** — useless; never harmful; just unhelpful. LDN never started.

## Late-stage curiosity: body recovers before mind catches up

For weeks after her body was functionally recovered (50-mile hikes, 3-hour bike rides), Kristyna had to keep checking with her husband whether tiredness after exertion was *normal-tired* or *CFS-tired*. *"My mind had to learn how to be healthy again."* This is a recurring late-stage pattern across the corpus's recoveries — worth noting for Eric as a phenomenon to expect, not a relapse signal.
