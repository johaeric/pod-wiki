# Chimére's Recovery (in progress)

Baltimore middle-school teacher, Black woman, first-wave US long-hauler. Got sick **March 22, 2020**. By December 2020 (recording) she was on a clear recovery trajectory after a deep mindset-and-movement pivot — but not full recovery: residual occipital + trigeminal neuralgia, spinal misalignment, post-cataract eye-droop.

## Pre-illness

Successful eighth-grade English teacher; her class had top standardised-test scores in Baltimore the year she got sick. Outwardly thriving — relationship, social life, "going to the clubs and bookstores." In retrospect frames this as **stress-loaded perfectionism** ("trying to be all things to all people, going non-stop, never sat my behind down"). No prior chronic illness.

## Long-covid course (March 2020 → August 2020 — the worst phase)

- **Acute**: cough, dyspnoea, fatigue described as "10 coats on my body."
- **Months 0–5 — bedbound**: couldn't lift head off pillow; couldn't eat for ~2 months (godmother had to coax her); couldn't move limbs reliably.
- **Vision loss** for 5 months; eventual **cataract surgery** on left eye left a residual eye-droop.
- **Memory loss**: forgot her own name on bad days; couldn't remember family members; couldn't remember favourite students.
- **Neurological residuals**: developed **occipital neuralgia + trigeminal neuralgia** (attributed to brain inflammation by her doctors); **spinal misalignment**.
- **Mental health**: rock bottom — wrote a will; suicidal ideation (*"what's the point of me living anymore"*).
- **Gaslighting**: turned away repeatedly from ERs because she didn't test positive. Attributes part of this to racial bias. Eventually unblocked through a non-medical lever — see "Activism" below.

## The pivot (August → October 2020)

1. **August** — found Raelan Agle's YouTube channel. Watched all the videos; read Raelan's book.
2. **October** — still bedbound; mind-vs-body conflict ("body was telling me 'you're staying here for the rest of your life'"). Decided: *"if i can't do anything but sit up in the bed for five minutes, i'm going to do that."* Set a 5-minute phone timer. That was the inflection.
3. **From sitting up → gentle stretches** following Raelan's videos. Movement-from-zero, scaled in small increments.
4. **Joe Dispenza's *Breaking the Habit of Being Yourself*** — read it in roughly a day; started a daily mindset-and-meditation practice. Began studying autonomic-nervous-system content.

## Activism / unblock

When ERs kept turning her away (no positive Covid test, lots of symptoms):
- **Wrote public Google reviews** of dismissive doctors as warnings to other potential long-haulers.
- **Wrote letters to Baltimore city councilmen** — multiple of whom intervened with the local hospital. This unlocked her access to the eye surgery and the post-Covid clinic that finally took her seriously.

This is a striking pattern worth keeping on file: **non-medical political-channel advocacy as an unblock for medical gaslighting**, distinct from the more common *patient-organisation advocacy* pattern in the LCP corpus.

## At recording (December 2020)

- **Sitting up 90% of the day** (vs. 0% pre-pivot).
- Conversational and engaged for ~57-min interview.
- Daily stretching practice; daily mindset/meditation practice.
- Has stepped back from support-group activity to protect her own recovery state.
- Still on prescription medication for residual symptoms (medication unnamed — clear that she does not credit it as the recovery driver).

## What she names as load-bearing

- **Gentle-stretching-from-bed** (via Raelan's videos) — *"that honestly started the process of changing my life because i had to move."*
- **Mindset shift** (via Dispenza) — *"nothing changes until your mind changes."*

## What she names as not-the-driver

- **Prescription medication** (unnamed) — was on it for 6 months pre-pivot without improvement.

## Source-bias note

Recovery-in-progress, not retrospective. Listed as `key_to_recovery` because she explicitly credits these two interventions as the inflection — but with the caveat that she still has neurological residuals.
