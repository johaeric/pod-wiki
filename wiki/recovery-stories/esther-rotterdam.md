# Esther's Recovery — TMS / Mind-Body Syndrome

**Onset**: March 2020 (Netherlands, first wave). **Recovered**: 2 June 2021. **Duration**: ~15 months.

## The shape of the arc

- **March → December 2020** — relapse-remit pattern; tested negative because no testing was available; hospital dismissed her despite 5 m exertional dyspnoea.
- **Dec 2020 → spring 2021** — persistent, severe. OT had her grading activity from 150 → 6 points/day; physio gave up. Sensory hypersensitivity (light, sound, touch). Wheelchair through the forest behind her house.
- **2 June 2021** — a single Nicole Sachs podcast → ~80% symptom drop within hours.
- **June → autumn 2021** — remaining 20% faded over months. Brain "tested" with new symptom forms (neck/shoulder seizure); recognising them as protective signals dissolved them.

## What worked, in her own framing

1. **Recognising the fear-of-symptoms loop** as the engine, not the symptoms themselves.
2. **Journal speak** (Sachs's freewriting) — daily, private, throwaway.
3. **Schubiner's *Unlearn Your Pain*** — neural-pathway / neuroplastic framing.
4. **Kristin Neff self-compassion meditations** — paired with the above; without the self-compassion piece the practice didn't land for her (high self-critic).
5. **Acting anyway** — small, reframeable acts the brain could log as safe.

## The retrospective insight

A 30-year history of migraines, recurring fatigue, knee/back pain — same nervous-system pattern. COVID was the trigger that brought it to a head, not the underlying cause.

## Caveats

- N=1. Recovery via the TMS route presupposes structural rule-out by a clinician.
- This is currently the wiki's **only** TMS-route recovery; pattern strength will only be established with more.
