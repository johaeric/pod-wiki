# Erik Hajj's Recovery (substantial-in-progress, ~11 months in)

27-year-old American wedding videographer + educator from St. Louis MO. Got Covid in November 2020 (filming a wedding pre-vaccine), developed long covid. Substantial-recovery-in-progress at recording (October 2021, 11 months in). Two named inflection points: **Moderna vaccine (March/April 2021)** and **[[ans-rewire|ANS Rewire]]** (Dan Neuffer's brain-retraining program).

## Pre-illness pattern

Healthy, no medical history, no medications. *"My wife used to say I was perfect."* Stacked stressors in 2020 — pandemic, St. Louis civil unrest, mid-career change to a new job, beloved dog died, exhausting wedding-filming schedule (12–14h on his feet).

## Long-covid course

- **Acute (Nov 2020)**: 2 weeks mild Covid. Wife also infected, recovered fully on schedule.
- **Post-acute (Dec 2020 → Mar 2021)**: shortness of breath at rest for 2 months straight; chest pain; chest neuropathy; brain fog; fatigue. Bedbound; lived on meal-replacement shakes. Pure misery, no improvement.
- **March/April 2021**: got Moderna vaccine. *"I didn't start recovering until I got my vaccine."* Beginning of upward curve.
- **Mid-2021**: bought and worked Dan Neuffer's [[ans-rewire|ANS Rewire]] program. Compounding gains over months.
- **October 2021**: doing 8-hour wedding shoot without next-day repercussions. Substantial-recovery-in-progress; still has occasional chest-pain and breath flare-ups.

## Recovery components

1. **Wife as primary caregiver** — load-bearing, non-medical.
2. **Moderna vaccine** — first inflection point. Anecdotal vaccine-helped-LC pattern.
3. **ANS Rewire** — structural brain-retraining work.
4. **Daily mindfulness / meditation / breathwork** — habituated daily practice.
5. **Miguel Bautista's pain-receptor visualization** for chest pain — practiced ~1 month, saw results.
6. **Pacing / energy envelope / no boom-bust**.
7. **Diet cleanup** — fast food eliminated; generic nutritious-meals approach.
8. **Recovery stories** as hope-input (Raelan + Miguel Bautista's channels).

## Frameworks he names

- *"Get out of your own way"* — the high-leverage question.
- **Stages, not linear** — discrete symptom-disappearance jumps every few weeks/months.
- **Pendulum** — wide symptom swings early, narrowing as the autonomic nervous system settles (Miguel Bautista's metaphor).
- **Submit to recovery** — surrender the always-on / always-progressing personality pattern.

## Source-bias note

Substantial-not-full recovery; he is on the curve, not past it. The vaccine-helped-LC claim is a single anecdotal report (March 2021 was around the period of multiple such anecdotal reports in long-hauler communities; the formal evidence picture is mixed). Not double-counted on ANS Rewire — he's a clean `key_to_recovery` for that program (per CLAUDE.md cross-podcast same-speaker-same-outcome rule, this is one row).
