# Uroš Čimžar — Recovery Arc

## Pre-illness

Mid-40s. Slovenian. Ex-corporate, mid-sabbatical, building a small mentoring/consultancy practice. Pre-existing thyroid issue treated. Already in psychotherapy. Wife and two young children. Considered himself in a good phase of life — relaxed, healthier than the previous 20 years.

## Trigger

Second COVID infection late December 2023. Acute illness mild. By 5 January 2024 (~10 days post) felt OK enough to try the gym; energy left him mid-session. Tried again a week later — worse. Each subsequent attempt at activity (walking, mobility class, cancelling client meetings, taking his son to a bookstore) triggered a deeper crash. Bedbound within weeks. ~300 steps/day.

## Diagnostic phase

- Self-identified PEM via the Slovenian National Public Health website's LC symptoms list — "such an unusual symptom it was easy to identify."
- Down the rabbit hole on Reddit's CFS subreddit — "the most depressing week of my life."
- GP eventually believed him; referred him to LC specialist.
- LC specialist at Slovenian clinic confirmed; placed him on long-term state disability. Useful framing he received: "people do get better."
- GP suggested antidepressants prophylactically ("you might be depressed soon"). He declined.

## Recovery work (in chronological-ish order)

1. **Found Jackie via Raelan Agle's channel** — the first hopeful video in days of fatalistic forums. Inflection point of his entire recovery — first proof that recovery existed.
2. **Yoga nidra** (daily) — first thing that brought mental rest. Couldn't read but could listen. The moment he realised mental rest was even *possible* preceded any physical recovery.
3. **Cold showers** — tried too soon, didn't work, stopped.
4. **Long Covid Breathing course** (LCB cohort, ~6 people, twice a week with Jackie + Vicky) — 2-3 weeks in, during a left-nostril practice while watching TV, he felt rest-and-digest physically — saliva, tummy rumbling — *for the first time in 15 years*. Inflection of physical recovery.
5. **Becca Kennedy's class** — 8 modules covering nervous-system regulation. Movement was Module 7.
6. **Self-compassion meditation** (Kristin Neff, 20 min guided) — third session, crying for the first time in years. Catalyst for emotional layer.
7. **Expressive writing** (Pennebaker / Schubiner-adjacent) — 3 sessions dissolved memories that had bothered him for 20 years.
8. **Core-belief work** with his therapist — identified "only if you give everything are you a good boy"; learned to ask *why* before saying yes to activity.
9. **Intermittent fasting** + cold-turkey coffee quit (no headache — body was too depleted).
10. Tried somatic tracking, EFT-tapping — kept the ones that resonated.

## Recovery moment

Eventless. By month 10 he was back at the gym. The "head tension" that was his last lingering symptom faded so quietly he noticed its absence rather than its departure. By February 2024 the symptoms returned briefly when he fell back into duty-driven work; gone in a week once he reframed his motivation.

## What he tells himself / others

- Recovery is **five phases**: calm NS → remove stressors → gradual exposure → leave the sick-person mindset → integrate everything into normal life.
- Don't rush cold water before nervous-system stability is in place.
- The emotional layer is often where the unblock is.
- Self-compassion + expressive writing are absurdly cheap and absurdly load-bearing.
