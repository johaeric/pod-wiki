# Matt Butler's Recovery (full, multi-component, ~1 year)

31 yo Londoner. Full recovery from a probably-LC / post-viral-CFS arc in roughly a year via an explicitly multi-component stack — no single program owns the credit. Identifies more with the ME/CFS frame than the LC-clinic frame.

## Pre-illness pattern

140-mile cycle the week before infection. High-pressure work. *"Riding waves of stress"* by his own description. Multiple commitments (music + work + hobbies). Recurring identity-and-overcommitment pattern across many corpus recoveries.

## Long-covid course

- **Acute Covid**: mild, kept working through isolation.
- **Crash event**: ~3–4 weeks post-acute, after a stressful emotional day + intense workout, sudden collapse at his work desk. *"I just felt very unsafe."*
- **Months 1–3**: severe boom-bust, bedbound much of the time, HR 140–150 standing (POTS-like, undiagnosed), insomnia, depression. Moved in with parents.
- **NHS long covid clinic**: clean scans, no treatments offered.

## Recovery components (in his own ranking)

1. **Pamela Rose pacing course** — first inflection. Plan-based pacing.
2. **Yoga + meditation + breathing — 2 hrs/day, 2 months straight** — daily relaxation/down-regulation block.
3. **CBT with a psychologist** — graded behavioural experiments, fear-of-activity work, catastrophising challenge. Used as recovery-stage tool, not coping tool.
4. **Lightning Process intro audio (~4 hrs)** — *"I am safe"* self-talk. First time he walked 15 minutes was after using this.
5. **DNRS full program** — visualisations, talking-to-brain, exposure rounds.
6. **Lightweight training** — graduated re-entry to movement.
7. **Identity shift** — explicit recognition that the always-on personality pattern needed to change.

## Frameworks he names

- **Belief as precondition**: progress only began when he believed recovery was possible.
- **Forums can hurt**: dialled back time in unwell-skewed forums; replaced with recovery-story content.
- **Change requires change**: incremental life and identity changes are part of the work.
- **In-the-moment language techniques**: "I am safe" mantra during walks blunted his crash-anticipation enough to stay out longer.

## Wiki position

Logged as full recovery. Multi-component stack — no single program credited as primary. Per CLAUDE.md outcome rules, evidence-log rows for each program/intervention go as `helped_partial` rather than `key_to_recovery`, since he names a stack and is explicit about no single component being load-bearing.
