# A K Davidson — full LC recovery (UK; three rounds; brain-training as the closing piece)

UK writer / editor / mother of three; recovered three times from LC after three reinfections. Final round added brain-training and produced both recovery and a *felt* resilience to future reinfection.

## The arc

- **Round 1 (April 2020)** — mild covid; intermittent breathlessness through 2020; lockdown context masked features.
- **Round 2 (late 2021–2022)** — full LC for ~1 year. Recovered through diet adjustment, osteopathy → breathing revelation → breathwork practice, POTS diagnosis (Boon Lim's materials), peer-support groups, sea swimming where accessible.
- **Round 3 (Dec 2023–2024)** — recovered again, this time adding **brain training** as the closing piece.

Year-2000 post-glandular-fever CFS recovery in the background — happened spontaneously without active intervention; that memory underpinned belief in recovery during LC, but the *path* she had to take in LC was completely different.

## What turned things around (cumulative across rounds)

1. **York food-intolerance test + elimination diet** — flagged foods cut; mood lifted significantly; gut-health / microbiome story self-credited.
2. **Osteopathy** — first practitioner who visibly recognised something was wrong. Found her breathing was upper-chest only; manual work re-opened upper-chest expansion. Inflection point.
3. **Breathwork** — taking it seriously after the osteopath revelation made a major difference.
4. **POTS diagnosis** — late but validating; [[boon-lim]]'s materials load-bearing.
5. **[[yoga-for-life-project]] peer-support group** — connection + creativity-witnessing community.
6. **Brain training** (final round) — closing piece; both the recovery and the resilience.
7. **Sea swimming** — where geographically possible.
8. **Switching off the news** — Radio 4 → Radio 3 / Petroc Trelawny. Removing the daily onslaught of bad news was directly therapeutic.

## What she didn't try / mention

No HRT, no LDN, no antihistamines, no Suzy Bolt program, no specific brain-retraining product (Gupta / DNRS / ANS Rewire) — she describes "brain training" generically.

## What she'd tell past Anna

- You can recover. *And recover more than once.*
- Don't try to take on the world while still ill — the energy isn't there. Wait until strong, then advocate.
- Pearls for Life (poetry book) is the artefact she made of the lessons. Available online (will link in show notes).
- "Pulling the ladder up" after recovery is a temptation. Resist it.
