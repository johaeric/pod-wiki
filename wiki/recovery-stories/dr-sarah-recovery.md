# Dr Sarah's recovery arc

A **2.5-year arc** to substantially-full recovery, marked by climbing Snowdon in March 2024 in driving rain and 50 mph gusts.

## Timeline

- **Autumn 2020** — mild COVID; no fatigue afterward.
- **Jan 2021** — NHS first-wave vaccinations; started feeling unwell.
- **Summer 2021** — moderate symptoms, still working, struggling. "Pushed through."
- **Autumn 2021** — second viral hit. **Hard crash**: severe fatigue (housebound), severe brain fog (couldn't construct sentences), sensory hypersensitivity (cold to bones, light, sound), GI bloating, freeze response (HR 40, BP very low). Couldn't put 3-year-old son to bed in evenings even with day-time childcare.
- **2022** — Lightning Process at ~30% recovered (helped surface self-limiting beliefs).
- **Through 2022–2023** — incremental rebuild via the **six pillars**.
- **Late 2023 → early 2024** — at ~95–100%, transitioning toward identity reintegration.
- **March 2024** — Snowdon climb in driving rain → "I could trust my body again."

## The six pillars (what she actually did)

1. **Nervous-system regulation** — Sensate app, Insight Timer, [[suzy-bolt-recovery]] (3 Mind Body Soul), [[yoga-for-life-project]], YouTube vagus-nerve / ear-rubbing exercises, breathwork, sitting in nature.
2. **Nutrition** — anti-inflammatory, no ultra-processed; **Yuka app** for ingredient scoring; cooked all veg (no raw), included carbs (low-carb worsened dizziness).
3. **Sleep** — blue-blocker glasses, **red bedside bulb**, no screens 1–2h pre-bed, sleep meditations.
4. **Hormones** — slow-titrated **HRT** for perimenopause overlap (year-long titration). Couldn't isolate the contribution but logs as meaningful.
5. **Movement** — paced, intuitive walks in nature; ditched the smartwatch when it triggered fear-of-numbers; positive-frame mindset.
6. **Healing mindset** — gratitude practice (lists, spoken), EFT-style tapping app with positive affirmations, listening to recovery stories, post-it affirmations on the mirror, "where focus goes, energy flows."

## What didn't work for her body

- **Cold water at 40% recovered** — significant harm (tired all day, unwell). Tried multiple times. Only marginally tolerable at 90%. Not for her body.
- **Heart-rate watch / wearable** — pushed her into fear-of-numbers territory. Removed.
- **Vast supplement stacks** — money/energy sink without measurable benefit (vitamin D and Omega-3 retained, the rest dropped).

## Notable framings

- **Freeze response** as the LC autonomic state (HR 40, BP low, dizzy on standing). Mammalian play-dead. "All on a subconscious level — you can't talk yourself out of it."
- **Trust your body again** as the operative recovery threshold (vs symptom-free as the threshold).
- **Six-pillar audit** as a way to find the missing layer when stuck.
- **Doing recovery imperfectly is fine** — perfection isn't possible; the journey itself is the work.
