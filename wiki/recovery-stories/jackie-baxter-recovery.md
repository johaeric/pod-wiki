# Jackie Baxter — Recovery Story (Onset March 2020 → Recovered 2023)

The wiki's seventh full recovery story — and the host's own. Three-year arc; characterised by sequenced breath / rest / cold-water "Big Three", later supplemented by mental-health work, a career break, and the Fern programme.

## Pre-LC profile

- Hill-walker / cyclist / runner.
- UK (Inverness area).
- Education-sector job; long commute.
- Self-described people-pleaser who "didn't know how to sit still".

## Onset (March 2020)

- Acute COVID confirmed by paramedics; borderline-hospitalisation severity.
- ~5–6 weeks effectively bedbound.
- Apparent recovery at month 3; thought it was over.
- **Crash at month 3.5** — long-covid proper began and lasted three years.

## Symptom profile

- **Severe fatigue**, beyond anything previously experienced.
- **Severe breathing-pattern disorder** — would have struggled to be much worse.
- **Tachycardia + palpitations**: post-shower HR 30+ min to settle.
- **COVID toes** early.
- **PEM from basically anything.**
- **Intermittent sleep + mild GI**.
- All retrospectively grouped under [[dysautonomia]].

## What didn't help

- **Pushing through** — every variant.
- **Working through it** for 12 months.
- **A long list of supplements** — no benefit, depleted bank account.
- **Nattokinase** — no benefit; gave her chaotic periods.
- **Diet eliminations** — no perceptible effect.
- **Frequency machine** — no effect.

## What helped — the "Big Three"

### 1. Breathwork

Realised her breathing pattern was completely dysfunctional. Two phases:
- Re-learning to breathe → directly fixed breathlessness, indirectly fixed palpitations.
- Using breath as an autonomic-regulation tool — slowing HR, dampening palpitations actively.

She is now completing **breathing-instructor training** to teach this. Co-created **Long Covid Breathing** courses with [[vikki-jones]].

### 2. Yoga Nidra + gentle yoga

Yoga Nidra was the only modality that genuinely switched her brain off. Pre-LC Jackie had no rest-skill at all. Yoga Nidra and gentle yoga were the inflection where she understood that **rest isn't earned — everyone needs it**.

### 3. Cold water

Sequencing matters: had to wait until after breath-pattern was repaired to be safe in cold water. Once safe:
- First dip very brief; "felt like I'd been plugged into a socket" afterwards.
- Pattern: each session a bit better; effects lasted longer over time.
- **Never PEM-triggered** by cold water or swimming — supports [[mark-harper]]'s water-buoyancy-as-PEM-protection hypothesis (#87).
- Joined a local swimming group → social benefit.

## Subsequent layers

- **Counselling** — started after [[suzy-bolt]] said *"you can't put all that on Mally"* (Oct 2021). Still ongoing at recording. Big help with anxiety, trauma, perfectionism.
- **Letting go of chores.** Mally took everything over.
- **Sick leave** at ~18 months in. Should have done it earlier.
- **Career break** at start of 2023. Removed work-stress that she hadn't realised was so destructive. Major inflection.
- **Fern programme** with [[suzy-bolt]] (~March 2023). At ~90–95% by then. Useful for: structured consolidation of knowledge + naming her perfectionism as a recovery-blocker.

## Other helpers (lower impact)

- **Safe and Sound Protocol** — found useful.
- **Vedicinals-9** — small gut benefit.
- **Journaling** — occasional use for stress.

## The mental-health iceberg

As physical symptoms cleared, anxiety, trauma, perfectionism surfaced. Pre-LC she had been so physical-symptom-focused she hadn't been aware of these layers. Treating them was central to the final phase of recovery.

## How she found out she was recovered

During an interview with [[lorrie-rivers]] for the holistic-healing summit. Lorrie asked how she was doing. Mid-answer Jackie caught herself doing the perfectionism thing — "wouldn't say I'm recovered" — and realised: no-one is 100%; she's better than pre-LC in some ways (trauma processed; perfectionism understood; better boundaries). Walked through to Mally afterwards: *"I'm recovered."* Mally: *"I know."*

## "Jaxter 2.0"

- More respect for own health.
- Boundaries (newly learned word for it).
- Listens to her body.
- Same brain (always-on, firework idea generation), but **remembers to turn it off sometimes**.
- Identity-shift: turning lived experience into a way to help others (breathing-instructor training).

## Advice to her past self

1. **Keep an open mind** — the things that helped were the things she'd have dismissed.
2. **Recovery is not linear.** Speed bumps, not roadblocks.
3. **Ask for and accept help.**
4. **Be kind to yourself.**
5. **Belief in recovery** — without it, the dark places get very dark.

## Brain-body asynchrony principle

- Early LC: brain ahead of body → push, crash.
- Coming out of LC: body ahead of brain → body fine, brain still says "careful, last time we crashed". Re-learning to trust the body takes time.
