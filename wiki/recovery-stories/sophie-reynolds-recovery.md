# Sophie Reynolds — recovery from ME/CFS

Pre-Covid ME/CFS recovery story. **Wiki's first dedicated pre-Covid ME/CFS recovery — useful as cross-condition signal, but Sophie was never a Covid long-hauler.**

See [[111-sophie-reynolds-recovery]] for full episode summary.

## Recovery arc

- **March 2018**: started feeling flu-like; couldn't shake it; bedbound within a month
- **4-5 months in**: ME/CFS diagnosis from GP; "this could last 6 months, a year, two years"
- **Forced back to work** within 2 months on adjusted duty (mental-health unit; sit-and-observe role) — partly necessary, partly recovery-bottlenecking
- **Found Optimum Health Clinic** — first proper framework; STOP technique becomes load-bearing
- **Yoga first, then Pilates** — discovered low-impact YouTube videos; Pilates breath-with-movement structure became core practice
- **Lightbulb moment**: not 20 different things wrong, one thing not functioning — autonomic nervous system as unifying mechanism
- **Now**: self-employed Pilates teacher; recovered

## What worked (Sophie's named items, in order of weight)

1. **Pilates** ([[pilates]]) — single biggest factor; she now teaches it; framework key to her recovery
2. **STOP technique** (from Optimum Health Clinic) — hands on chest, deep breath, "stop", notice the calm
3. **Breathwork integrated with movement** — Pilates' lateral-rib breathing, exhale on exertion
4. **Antidepressants** (early stage) — partial relief from anxiety + decision-paralysis
5. **Understanding the nervous system** — turning point: "now I have a target"
6. **Self-compassion + reframing identity** — letting symptoms be a signal not a failure

## What didn't work

- **Cold water / cold showers** — drained her; energy rush during but knackered after. **First explicit no_effect / harmed signal for cold-water exposure on the wiki.**

## Eric-relevance
