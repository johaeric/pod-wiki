# Convalescence

Latin *con-valescere*: "to grow strong with". A pre-1950s medical concept, with infrastructure (convalescent hospitals, sanatoria, the Florence-Nightingale ward design) that *enforced* the conditions of recovery — rest, environment, nutrition, balance of activity. Largely abandoned in modern medicine in favour of the prescription-as-magic-bullet model.

Coined and articulated on this podcast by **Dr Gavin Francis** (#30) in his book *Recovery* — explicitly applied to long covid as a population that's been failed by the modern medical model's collapse of the convalescence framework.

## Why this matters for LC

- LC is fundamentally a recovery condition, not a fix-it-with-pills condition
- The infrastructure that used to support recovery (time off, environment, slow re-entry to work, nutritional support) has been culturally + economically eroded
- Modern culture's "push through" ethos is the opposite of what convalescence prescribes
- Restoring small versions of convalescence — environmental design, rest, slow re-entry, listening to the body — is something patients can do for themselves even without the infrastructure

## Practical Nightingale-axis levers (per #30)

- Light and space in your environment
- Cleanliness, view of something green and growing, time outside
- Sleep and rest as non-negotiable, not as weakness
- Nutritional support (cf [[anti-inflammatory-diet]], [[gut-health]])
- A clinician you trust, even if "I don't know" is the answer
- Carers and those around you — their needs are part of yours

> "Doctors are far more like gardeners than mechanics. Nature does most of the work." — Francis, ep #30

> "Acceptance of *this moment* — not acceptance of being this way *forever*. The body is dynamic." — Francis, ep #30
