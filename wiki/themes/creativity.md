# Creativity (as recovery substrate)

A recurring practice across recovered + recovering LC patients. Creativity surfaces in the wiki as a **reliably-portable, energy-economical, purpose-restoration tool** — not a treatment in the medical-intervention sense, but a load-bearing substrate of recovery for patients in highly varied conditions.

## Recurring patterns

- **Low-cost / low-stakes start.** Sarah's watercolours during lockdown ([[158-creativity-2024]]) — cheap supplies, "if I want to throw it in the bin, I can" was the unlock. Jade learning crochet on a friend's recommendation. Misty Brackett: "It doesn't have to be expensive."
- **Process over output.** Misty Brackett: process painter, no pre-image, "the downloads come." Anna Bell: free-flow writing as therapeutic centring. Reduces the perfectionism-trap risk.
- **Energy-economical micro-practice.** Crochet, watercolours, journaling, and choir all scale down to severe-cohort capacity. Sarah housebound ~90% with moderate-to-severe ME runs an online watercolour shop and Zoom paint-with-me sessions.
- **Connection-as-recovery.** Long Covid Choir (founded March 2021) — weekly Zoom, no auditions; first formed "in the darkness to others living with LC." Hibernation Sessions concert series (Olga, [[158-creativity-2024]]) — accessible to LC + ME + isolated communities.
- **Identity-restoration.** Sally ([[158-creativity-2024]]): going from "fragile, vulnerable patient" to "long-covid art advocate" was empowering and fun. Olga: organising concerts when she can't play "makes me feel like myself again."
- **Advocacy through art.** Larkman's *I Would Be Here If I Could*; Sally's planned May-2025 LC-artist exhibition; Alec Finlay's Scotland Covid Memorial.

## Why it shows up so consistently

- **Pacing-friendly.** Most creative practice can be modulated to fit the energy envelope.
- **Cognitive engagement without the high-cognitive-load of work.** Different brain regions; often experienced as restful.
- **Process-based satisfaction** that doesn't require recovery to be complete to feel valuable. Rebuilds purpose + agency without requiring full functional restoration.
- **Community-bonding** without the high-social-load of in-person socialising — Zoom choir, online Hibernation concerts, Facebook art groups.

## Caveats

- **Not a substitute for medical care.** Creativity is a substrate, not a treatment.
- **Perfectionism-aware framings matter.** Patients prone to over-doing (most of the wiki's recovery cohort) need permission to let things be unfinished, ugly, mid. The "5-minutes-at-a-time principle" ([[079-michael-towers-superhero]]) is operationally useful.
- **PEM-aware.** Creative engagement still expends cognitive + sometimes emotional energy. Not zero-cost.
