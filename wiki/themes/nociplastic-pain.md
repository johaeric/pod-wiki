# Nociplastic Pain / Central Sensitization

A pain category formalized by the International Association for the Study of Pain (IASP) in **2017–18** as a third type alongside nociceptive and neuropathic pain. The defining features:

- **No tissue damage detectable** (scans/labs clean)
- **No nerve damage detectable**
- **The nervous system itself is sensitized** — pain signals are amplified by central (spinal cord + brain) processes
- **Low-grade neuroinflammation** at points where nerves meet tissue (joint capsules, tendon insertions) and within the CNS
- Nervous + immune systems feed each other — sensitization is bidirectional

## Why this is load-bearing for LC

- **The dominant pain pattern in LC is nociplastic** ([[deepak-ravindran]] #53; see [[pain]]).
- The same mechanism is the consensus model for **fibromyalgia, ME/CFS, chronic migraine, IBS, and some chronic low back pain** — all conditions LC strongly resembles.
- It explains the consistent finding that **>90% of LC patients have clean MRIs + normal inflammatory blood markers** despite very real, severe pain.
- It connects pain pathology to **autonomic dysfunction** and **breathing pattern dysfunction** — all are downstream of the same nervous-system sensitization.
- It implies LC is **potentially reversible** (no structural damage) but requires re-tuning the nervous + immune systems, not analgesia.

## Treatment implications

Drugs (NSAIDs, opioids, gabapentin, amitriptyline) and injections (steroids, nerve blocks) **rarely work well** for nociplastic pain because they are designed for nociceptive or neuropathic mechanisms. What does work:

- **Sleep, nutrition** (whole foods, anti-inflammatory)
- **Mind–body therapies** (yoga, tai chi, Pilates, mindfulness, yoga nidra) — measurably dampen the inflammatory response
- **Gentle, paced physical activity**
- **TENS, cold water, hydrotherapy**
- **CBD/cannabis** (acts on the endocannabinoid system; calms immune + nervous system in parallel; patient reports positive but NHS-unavailable)
