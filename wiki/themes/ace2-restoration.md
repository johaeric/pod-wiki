**ACE2** (angiotensin-converting enzyme 2) is the cell-surface enzyme SARS-CoV-2 uses to enter most cells. It is also a critical part of the body's response to injury and inflammation — present in blood vessels, immune cells, kidneys, heart, and brain.

## Why "restoration" not "blockade"

In early 2020 there was a notion that since the virus uses ACE2 to enter cells, we should *block* ACE2. This proved wrong:

> ACE2 is the **victim, not the cause**. The virus damages it during entry, and ACE2 is needed for the corrective response that brings inflammation back under control.
> — Dr Leo Galland, ep #191

Damaged ACE2 → unchecked inflammation → much of acute COVID's vascular pathology. Long COVID's central web strand begins here.

## Galland's restoration triad

- **Vitamin D**
- **Curcumin** (turmeric)
- **Resveratrol**

Plus a fruit/vegetable-rich diet and judicious exercise. Galland reports near-zero progression to LC in patients he treated with this protocol from acute COVID onward.

## Why this matters mechanistically

ACE2 sits central in Galland's Web of Long COVID. Damage to ACE2 propagates outward to:
- Blood vessel / capillary damage
- Immune dysregulation
- Gut microbiome changes (ACE2 in gut regulates immune responses there)
- Microclot formation
- Eventually contributing to mitochondrial dysfunction via reduced perfusion
