# Gut health

The gut as a **second brain** and a primary battleground in LC:
- Houses ~70 % of the immune system
- Produces ~70 % of body serotonin
- Communicates bidirectionally with the brain via the vagus nerve (the gut-brain axis)
- Hosts the microbiome — trillions of bacteria, viruses, yeast that we co-exist with

The thesis recurring across podcast guests: when the gut is dysregulated (microbiome imbalance, intestinal permeability / "leaky gut", inflammation), it produces multi-system symptoms that look like LC's cardinal features: fatigue, brain fog, mood disturbance, food sensitivities, MCAS-style reactivity.

## Mechanisms named on the podcast

- **Microbiome dysbiosis** — covid + antibiotic exposure + stress + dietary disruption
- **Intestinal permeability** ("leaky gut") — bacterial fragments crossing the gut wall → systemic inflammation
- **Mast cells** in the gut → MCAS-style hypersensitivity
- **Vagal afferent signalling** — gut state directly influences brain state via the vagus nerve

## Practical playbook

- "Heal and seal" the gut **before** elimination diets
- Whole-food, fibre-diverse diet — feed the microbiome
- Anti-inflammatory diet (#21 Moira)
- Address blood-sugar instability (low-carb / keto for some — #21, #23)
- Fermented foods *if* not on a low-histamine protocol
- Possible MCAS angle if symptoms map (cf [[mcas]])
- Optional: low-histamine **only if indicated** + supervised (cf [[low-histamine-diet]])

## Clinical layer — DGBI taxonomy (added from #43)

[[mohammad-bashashati]] (#43) introduces the **post-COVID Disorders of Gut-Brain Interaction (DGBI)** framing — the modern clinical classification. Up to ~1/3 of acute COVID has GI symptoms (45% diarrhea, 35% nausea±vomiting, 30% abdominal pain in his cohort); ~9% develop persistent DGBI at 6 months in mixed populations; and 40% of those cases had **no acute-phase GI symptoms** at all.

Mechanism: ACE2-driven direct gut-epithelial infection → low-grade inflammation + microbiota shift + ENS rewiring + vagal cross-talk to CNS. Cross-ref [[viral-persistence]] for the gut-as-reservoir hypothesis.

Prognosis precedent: the **Walkerton 2000 outbreak** (E. coli + Campylobacter water contamination, 36% post-infectious IBS at 2 years) showed eventual favourable remission for most. See [[post-infectious-ibs]].

## Microbiome layer (added from #99)

[[099-viola-sampson-microbiome]] is the wiki's deepest microbiome anchor:

- **Pro-inflammatory vs anti-inflammatory microbiome states** — gram-negative bacteria releasing LPS drive systemic inflammation; beneficial bacteria producing **short-chain fatty acids (esp. butyrate)** are systemic anti-inflammatory.
- **Microbiome ↔ vagus ↔ inflammation loop** — gut state directly influences brain state via vagus afferents; vagal tone in turn dampens gut inflammation.
- **Prebiotics > probiotics for long-term shifts** — probiotics are transient (days to weeks); prebiotics ([[prebiotics-gos]]) feed beneficial bacteria over time.
- **Specific strain recommendations** for LC overlap: see [[probiotics]].
- **Don't broad-spectrum-antimicrobial yourself** — once a beneficial species is wiped out, probiotics don't reseed it. Berberine, oregano essential oil, and similar antimicrobials common in functional medicine can do real damage.
- **Predisposing factors that worsen LC microbiome impact**: pre-existing IBS-C / slow transit; PPI or H2-blocker use (suppressed stomach acid → SIBO); antibiotics within 6 months of infection; ADHD; MCAS.

This is the wiki's clearest counter-voice to [[070-lorrie-rivers]]'s aggressive-anti-parasitic-protocol position. Holds both as recorded hypothesis and (Sampson) endorsed clinical practice.
