# Reinfection (and its Damage)

The wiki's first concentrated episode on reinfection in established LC: **#37 Asad Khan** describes a likely 4 reinfections in 18 months, all PCR/LFT-negative, several confirmed by:
- New microclots on imaging (none on previous baseline weeks earlier)
- Anti-N antibody trajectory (undetectable in Dec 2021 → above-detectable + Spike antibodies in thousands at recording, May 2022)

## Pattern

- **Each reinfection knocks his baseline down a step** — not a temporary crash that resets, a step down.
- **PCR / LFT unreliable**, especially in Omicron / sub-variants. Antibody trajectories + clinical pattern + microclot reappearance are the better signal.
- **Triple therapy + apheresis gains do not persist through reinfection** — gains have to be rebuilt.

## Implications

- "Living with covid" ≠ letting it rip. Air-quality + masking + airborne PPE in shared spaces remain protective for established long-haulers.
- The "Omicron is mild" narrative (Khan's strong critique) ignores absolute volume + reinfection effects on existing LC patients.
- Reinfection is a meaningful covariate for any LC outcome study — should be tracked, often isn't.

## Bolt's 2023 reframe (#77) — fear-of-reinfection as a clinical issue

#77 introduces a complementary frame to #37: **fear-of-reinfection is itself a pattern that locks autonomic dysregulation in place.**

- Most people Bolt has known with LC who got reinfected report **milder** illness — bodies recognise the virus.
- The community over-shares horror stories and under-shares mild-reinfection stories — read both.
- **"5% stretch"**: don't ask LC patients to leap back into pre-illness life; don't ask them to never leave the house. Stretch incrementally.
- **"Immune system as superpower"**: the brain is the supercomputer for the immune system. Pre-loading the belief "I won't cope if I get it again" is itself an instruction. Replace with a more useful priming belief.
- Bolt is herself 3-times reinfected by recording; rounds 2 and 3 each markedly milder than 2020 — and her own narrative attributes this to the resolution of the major life-stressors (esp. relationship) that were active in 2020.

## Prevention add-in (#73)

Li (#73) adds one further consideration: **metformin during the acute phase reduced LC incidence by >30% in the Bramante RCT**. If reinfection happens, this is a candidate to discuss with a clinician *during the acute window* — meaningfully changes the catastrophe-frame for someone with a prior LC episode.

## The bidirectional pattern (#171 — Aaron West light-switch recovery)

#171 introduces the *third* and rarest reinfection pattern documented on the wiki: **reinfection can apparently trigger recovery.** Aaron West had 2.5 years of LC; took standard-course Paxlovid during an Omicron reinfection on Jan 5 2024; reported "light-switch" reversal *during the acute infection*, before its symptoms had even resolved. His GP confirmed having seen this in "a couple of others" over a 20-year practice.

Logged as **rare** and **confounded** — variant + Paxlovid + accumulated-stack + immune-reset are co-confounds; no single-factor mechanistic claim is being made. **Do not engineer reinfection.** But the wiki should now track all three patterns:

- **Reinfection-as-relapse** (Khan #37, Peers, most clinical observation) — common.
- **Reinfection-as-fear-trap** (Bolt #77) — psychological pattern; brain priming amplifies actual outcomes.
- **Reinfection-as-recovery** (West #171) — rare; mechanism unknown.
