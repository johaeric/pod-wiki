# Vaccine-Triggered Long Covid

A subset of long covid presents as triggered by covid vaccination rather than infection. The two clinical voices in the wiki most willing to name and treat this: **Asad Khan (#37)** and **Tina Peers (#38)**. Both frame themselves explicitly as **pro-vaccination** — and argue that the religiosity around vaccines suppresses honest discussion of reactions, which itself fuels hesitancy.

## Mechanism (Peers, #38)

- mRNA enters cells beyond the deltoid — concentrates in ovaries, testes, liver, heart, brain, spleen, lymph nodes.
- Cells produce spike protein internally → flagged as virus-infected → autoimmune attack on own tissues.
- In MCAS-prone individuals (Peers's ~17.5% population estimate), this produces an LC-style reaction.

## Mechanism (Khan, #37)

- Vaccinated while still in active LC (early Feb 2021) → instant PEM/POTS crash.
- Immune system already up-regulated by the recent infection; the vaccine added stimulation it couldn't tolerate.
- "We don't vaccinate sick kids — we wait until they get better. The same principle should apply."

## Treatment

- Same as virus-triggered LC. Microclot, MCAS, autonomic dysfunction protocols all apply.

## Editorial framing

- All three speakers (Khan #37, Peers #38, Pover #84) emphasise: not anti-vax. All got vaccinated themselves. All argue **honest acknowledgement of reactions** is the responsible framing.
- The wiki tracks this as one outcome bucket, not a separate diagnosis.

## What Pover (#84) adds — the patient perspective on social isolation

The clinician voices (Khan, Peers) anchor the mechanism. **Pover anchors the social experience**, distinct from regular LC:

- Onset within hours of injection (vs gradual progression from infection).
- **Disbelief PLUS social ostracism** — people physically cross the street, take steps backward when she names the diagnosis.
- Often advised by other patients to *just say it's long covid* because the support is more available there.
- "I wish I'd had long covid rather than vaccine injury."
- Symptom catalogue surveyed by **UK CV Family** maps onto LC almost completely — neurological, autonomic, immune, hormonal, GI, sleep, psychological — supporting the "same physiology, different trigger" framing of #37/#38.

## Treatments Pover personally credits (n=1 each)

- **[[therapeutic-phlebotomy]]** — key_to_recovery; took her from zero to 30%.
- **High-water-content fruit and vegetable diet** (private clinical protocol) — 30% → 45%.
- **High-copper diet** ([[copper-supplementation]]) — just started; copper deficiency confirmed.

These are logged as her personal experiment, not as wiki recommendations. None is mainstream.
