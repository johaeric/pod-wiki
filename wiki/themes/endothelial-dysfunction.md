# Endothelial Dysfunction

Inflammation of the inner lining of blood vessels — *endothelitis* — sits in the same feedback loop as microclots and hyperactivated platelets in the Pretorius / Kell mechanism story.

> "The whole blood vasculature is inflamed. If all of these inflammatory layers of your vasculature are present, plus microclots in circulation plus hyperactivated platelets — capillary entrapment + oxygen deprivation + widespread hypoxia." — Pretorius (#33)

## Why it matters across the wiki

- **Pre-existing endothelial inflammation** in diabetes / hypertension / dyslipidaemia is named as a risk amplifier for the LC trigger.
- **ACE-2 receptor expression** on endothelium = direct entry point for SARS-CoV-2.
- **Statins as anti-inflammatory** (Kell, #33): the cholesterol story is largely a myth in normal range; statins help via endothelial calming.
- **Boon Lim's framing** (#40): vasoconstriction-vasodilation balance is what the autonomic system fights to control; chronic endothelitis adds rigidity to the system.

## Treatment implications

- Anti-inflammatory background (statins, polyphenols, antioxidants).
- Anticoagulation as part of triple therapy breaks the feedback loop.
- Lifestyle: BP, cholesterol, weight, diet — Pretorius's "make your starting endothelium as healthy as possible" advice.

## William Li's microvascular-loss extension (#73)

Beyond endothelitis as inflammation, **#73 adds a structural-loss layer**: in severe acute covid the coronavirus directly invades endothelial cells (NEJM autopsy paper, Li et al.); in long covid, **small lung blood vessels are physically still missing months later** on deconstructed CT scans — a layer of damage that standard imaging misses.

- **VEGF as a *health* biomarker** (not disease marker): a repair signal released by injured tissue trying to grow new capillaries. Hidden in plain sight on standard scans.
- **Capillary loss** in lungs (breathlessness), brain (executive-function "ripped lining"), olfactory neurons (smell loss), even testicles.
- **Repair candidates**: nitric-oxide donors ([[dietary-nitrates]] beets/spinach; low-dose [[sildenafil-viagra]]), vasculogenic stem-cell mobilisation.

So the wiki now distinguishes: **inflammation of the lining (endothelitis)** vs **structural loss of microvasculature** vs **microclots in flow** — three layers, partially separate, all converging on hypoperfusion.
