# Patient and Public Involvement (PPI)

**PPI** = doing research **with** people who have lived experience, not for them or to them. Not the same as participating in research (taking the drug, filling the questionnaire).

## The LC patient community has been ahead of the curve

- **The term "long covid" was coined by patients** (Elisa Perego, Twitter, May 2020).
- Patient advocacy groups led on diagnosis recognition, research priorities, and rejection of GET-style approaches.
- Lim's #40 explicitly ties LC's progress to "very strong patient advocacy groups challenging the medical community."

## Ibbotson + Laidlaw's framing (#39)

- PPI is **values-driven** (power-sharing, equality, trust, inclusivity), not a method.
- **Build relationships, don't extract.** Helicopter-in-helicopter-out involvement is harmful.
- **Resource it properly** — pay people; cover travel; schedule with breaks for chronic-illness reality.
- **Get researchers when they're young** — embed PPI in undergraduate, masters, PhD curricula.
- **The Goldilocks problem**: experienced patient contributors get rejected as "too research-literate"; inexperienced ones as "not knowing enough." Both moves preserve power.

## Faghy's living application (#36)

- 16-week observational cohort with **lived-experience diary alongside quantitative measures**.
- 70-question patient-voice survey **designed by the patient PPI group, not the researchers**.
- Delphi consensus including "tick if you're a long-covid sufferer" stakeholder layer.
- Outputs disseminated visually for patients, not in journals only.

## Quote

> "Researchers come into our world. Don't parachute in and piss off." — Laidlaw

## Further wiki anchors

- **[[062-nisreen-alwan|#62]]** — *"lived experience is a strength, not a bias"* in research culture; PhD students hide their LC experience for fear of dismissal — needs to flip.
- **[[067-decodeme|#67 DecodeME]]** — patient-rep is **structural** in every working group. Not decorative. *"As scientists we know nothing — what we do have is lived experience."* (Ponting)
- **[[069-fiona-lowenstein|#69]]** — patient-led research as institutional output: **[[patient-led-research-collaborative|PLRC]]** ran the first major LC survey in May 2020 from inside the [[body-politic]] Slack. Now an independent funded organisation working with WHO, CDC, etc.
- **[[083-ryan-prior-journalist-author|#83 Ryan Prior]]** — generalises the pattern. Names the **"expert patient"** category (people whose authority comes from lived experience *plus* a professional skill set: data, policy, journalism, medicine). Argues this is how science should work — citing **David Fajgenbaum's drug-repurposing** model (Castleman disease, *Chasing My Cure*) and **Amy Marcus's *We the Scientists*** (NPC) as parallel cases. Frames "Santa Claus syndrome" — patients waiting for some external smart person to deliver the cure — as the failure mode to avoid.
