# Caregiver support

Long covid is rarely a solo experience. Partners, parents, friends-and-family who become caregivers carry significant load — often unacknowledged — and their state directly affects the patient's substrate for recovery via **nervous-system-to-nervous-system coupling**.

## Anchor episodes

- **[[011-iain-partner-with-chronic-illness]]** — partner-as-carer perspective; team framing; "oxygen-mask-first" principle.
- **[[156-nadyne-mckie-caregivers-children]]** — anchor caregiver-of-paediatric-LC episode; first wiki articulation of caregiver-depletion-as-recovery-rate-limiter.
- **[[061-rosie-colin-pidgeon]]** — father as carer to paediatric LC patient.

## Operative patterns

- **"You can't pour from an empty cup."** Recurring. Caregiver self-care is operationally essential, not a nice-to-have.
- **NS-to-NS coupling**: depleted, dysregulated carers transmit dysregulation to the patient — particularly visible in paediatric LC where the mother–child / father–child autonomic substrate is tightly linked.
- **Advocacy load**: schools, GPs, PIP / disability-benefits navigation, employer accommodations — the carer often takes on the cognitive-energy work the patient can't do, on top of the household + emotional load. Underweighted in standard LC discussion.
- **Watching someone you love stay ill is its own trauma**. Nadyne ([[156-nadyne-mckie-caregivers-children]]) explicitly relapsed during her son's LC arc — the stress + uncertainty + advocacy load triggered her own residual symptoms.
- **Asking-for-help / accepting-help skill**: many LC patients (and partners) struggle to ask for help; recovered patients often name learning to do so as load-bearing. Cross-ref [[125-peter-burt-mental-health]] (the therapist's "denying people the opportunity to do for you what you love doing for them" line).

## What helps for carers

- **Permission to take care of themselves first.** Reframe self-care as a recovery-input for the patient, not a luxury.
- **Their own therapy / support.** Often a different practitioner than the patient's.
- **External validation** — peer groups, partner groups, advocacy charities. Cross-ref [[long-covid-kids]] for UK paediatric carer community.
- **"Just for now" framing** ([[156-nadyne-mckie-caregivers-children]]) — keeps the door open between acceptance + hope without catastrophising.
- **Practical advocacy templates** — letters to schools, employer language, disability-benefits phrasing (the wiki's [[027-jenny-ceolta-smith-leaving-work]] is the closest existing operational anchor).

## Caveats

- **Support availability is privilege-correlated.** Geographically, financially, socially. Acknowledge structural barriers when discussing this with any reader.
- **The carer is also a person with a life.** Avoid pathologising.
