# ME/CFS parallels

A recurring thread on the podcast: the long-covid wave is in many ways a **rerun of the ME/CFS scandal** — same post-viral physiology, same multi-organ symptom picture (PEM, autonomic dysfunction, dysregulated immune response), same medical-system pattern of disbelief and gaslighting, but at scale (millions instead of hundreds of thousands).

Several guests carry both diagnoses or have ME-experienced perspective on LC.

## What guests draw from the parallel

- **The medical system was unprepared for LC because it ignored ME for 30+ years** (ep #24 Alec Finlay; ep #28 Al-Aly). If we'd invested in post-viral research a decade ago, we'd have treatments now.
- **Diagnostic criteria converge**: PEM, autonomic dysfunction (POTS, orthostatic intolerance), dysautonomia, sleep dysfunction, cognitive impairment.
- **The same mind-body vs organic-disease dichotomies** that plagued ME advocacy resurface in LC ("it's anxiety", "graded exercise will fix it"). Same dynamic of medical research moving slowly while patients self-organize.
- **Same population overlap**: many LC patients meet ME/CFS criteria after 6 months; the ME/CFS community has welcomed LC patients and shared advocacy infrastructure.

> "If we'd spent 30 years researching ME, we may have medicine for long covid now." — Alec Finlay, ep #24

> "We had 100 years to study post-viral illness and ignored it. Don't waste this crisis." — Al-Aly, ep #28

## What guidance the parallel offers

- **Pacing > graded exercise** (ME community's hard-won lesson, now widely adopted in LC)
- **PEM avoidance is non-negotiable** — push through ≠ recovery
- **Patient advocacy infrastructure** (#meaction, ME Association, Long Covid Support, etc.) has carried medical change in conditions where the formal system was slow
- **Don't trust "this is just anxiety" framing** — it was wrong for ME, it's wrong for LC
