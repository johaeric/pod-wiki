# Trauma & illness

A theme threading through several mind-body episodes: **chronic illness often has upstream roots** in unprocessed trauma — small-t and large-T — that has shaped the nervous system into a state of chronic sympathetic overdrive, predating the COVID infection that became the trigger.

## What "trauma" is allowed to include

The point repeated across guests is that trauma is **not** limited to large discrete events. It includes:
- Critical / cold parents
- Childhood medical procedures done with terror, not soothing
- Cumulative micro-stresses over many years
- High-pressure work patterns sustained for decades
- Maternal stress in utero (prenatal stress shapes infant nervous-system reactivity)
- Trans-generational stress patterns (epigenetic + behavioural transmission)
- Personality types (perfectionist, over-responsible, helper, controller) that *maintain* sympathetic overdrive

## Why this matters for LC

- It explains why some people get LC and others don't from the same exposure (the "tipping point" framing)
- It explains why purely physical interventions (β-blockers, drugs, supplements) often don't fully resolve
- It points at upstream work: somatic processing, SSP / SIRPA / polyvagal-informed therapy, trauma-aware movement
- ⚠️ It does **not** mean LC is "just trauma" or "all in your head" — the physical mechanisms (viral persistence, MCAS, microclots, mitochondrial dysfunction) are real *and* the upstream NS state shapes how those mechanisms manifest

## Cautions

- **Don't push trauma work past tolerance.** Self-help meditation / yoga / vagal-tone YouTube tricks can destabilise people with significant trauma history. Practitioner-supported pacing matters.
- **No moralising.** This is not "you should have processed your trauma" — it's "if you can find a window for this work, it might shift things upstream."
