# Paediatric long covid

Long covid in children and adolescents — qualitatively different from adult LC because the energy-expenditure profile (learning, socialising, school environment) is dominated by cognitive load not physical, and because external advocacy (parents, schools, paediatric medicine) carries far more of the load than for adult patients.

## Operative patterns

- **Energy expenditure for kids is dominated by cognitive + social + sensory load, not physical.** Learning, socialising with friends, screen time, school environment — these are the energy drains. Parents who think about energy-management in physical terms only will miss the picture.
- **School advocacy is non-trivial.** PE letters, phased return, dropping non-essential activities (sports day, long-distance run), psychoeducation for teachers — recurring operational moves across paediatric-LC episodes.
- **Believing-the-child** as a long-term skill-teaching exercise. Per [[156-nadyne-mckie-caregivers-children]], not believing a child's symptoms teaches them to ignore body signals — sets up the perfectionism / push-through pattern that recurs across adult recovery stories on the wiki.
- **NS-to-NS coupling** between caregiver and child. Depleted carer → dysregulated substrate → harder for child to recover. Caregiver self-care is *operationally essential*, not a nice-to-have.
- **"Just for now" language** ([[156-nadyne-mckie-caregivers-children]]) — keeps the door open between acceptance + hope; same operative move as Julie Black's "submit now ≠ accept forever" ([[109-julie-black-recovery]]) but adapted for child + parent dialogue.
- **Recovery is possible but ongoing-vigilance-required.** Nadyne's son recovered; she still watches for Whispers under stress.

## Caveats

- **Paediatric clinical advocacy is geographically very uneven.** UK pockets of good consultant care; many regions still see paediatric LC dismissed.
- **Long-term consequences of suppressing body signals in childhood** — the through-line to adult ME/CFS / LC patient histories is striking. This is more than a paediatric concern; it's a developmental-substrate concern.
- **[[long-covid-kids]]** charity is the central UK advocacy organisation for paediatric LC; cross-ref for current campaigns + resources.
