# Neuroplasticity

The shared substrate concept used by several recovery-route programs in the wiki. The brain re-shapes its pathways with practice; chronic-illness sensitisation patterns (often pre-existing in the person) can be re-shaped via deliberate practice over weeks-to-months.

## Episodes that develop this theme

- **#20 Amy Anker** — first wiki recovery built explicitly on neuroplasticity / nervous-system-loop interruption. Founded [[positively-covid]].
- **#32 Esther** — recovery via TMS / Schubiner's *Unlearn Your Pain* (explicit neuroplastic-pathway language).
- **#38 Tina Peers** — recommends Gupta program neuroplastic retraining as adjunct in LC ("30% improvement" in her patient cohort).
- **#178 Alisha** — TMS-lineage recovery via Sarno + Buglio + Tolin + Ensor. Daily somatic tracking + Reiki + meditation + boundary work over a year.
- **#179 Dr David Clarke** — anchor framing for the wiki: **"neuroplastic symptoms"** = real, biological, treatable symptoms generated by *physically-changed brain circuits that are capable of changing back*. ATNS / symptomatic.me. 7,000+ patients. ACEs framework.
- **#184 Mahncke + Uswatte** — first **RCT-evidenced** plasticity-based cognitive-rehab intervention on this wiki. CI Cognitive Therapy (Brain HQ + everyday-task training + transfer package) showed 80% return-to-work in pilot vs 0% usual care. The "diffuse damage → recruit healthy tissue via plasticity" mechanism story.
- **#186 Jamie** — recovery built on Primal Trust + Lightning Process (the latter at 90%). The "this is just a hypersensitive nervous system" reframe, written on her hand.
- **#189 Natalie Gold** — ANS Rewire (Dan Neuffer) over 2 years. Adds the *small-t trauma message* angle — neuroplastic dysregulation can come from "good childhood with subtle wounded messages," not only big-T trauma.

## Important caveats

- **Structural rule-out first**: neuroplasticity-route programs presuppose tissue / organ damage has been excluded by a clinician.
- **Not a substitute for autonomic / MCAS / microclot drugs** when those are indicated.
- **N=1 recoveries** dominate the testimony so far. Pattern strength grows with replication.
