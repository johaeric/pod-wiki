# Gratitude and Forgiveness

Boon Lim's #40 framing is unusually direct for a cardiologist's episode: of all emotional states, **forgiveness is the most powerful for autonomic reset**, more so than gratitude or compassion.

## Why he ranks it that way

- Gratitude opens space; forgiveness *closes* space — the chronic stress loop powered by unresolved blame, regret, anger.
- Autonomic sympathetic surge feeds on that unresolved emotional state every time the person stands up and the baroreceptor reflex fires.
- Forgiving the person who got it wrong, the doctor who told you "everything is normal", and *yourself* — collapses the amplifier.

## What he asks of patients

- **Forgive your doctor.** They had 10–15 minutes and operate within their silo. Not from harm — from insecurity and ignorance.
- **Forgive yourself.** Especially the regret of "I should have known earlier" or "I should have rested harder" or "I should have pushed less."
- **Bring the locus of control back to within yourself.** Not the white-coat tower.
- **Reframe the historical illness narrative** — Lim's own personal anecdote: caught covid two months pre-recording, can now reframe the experience as a teaching moment.
