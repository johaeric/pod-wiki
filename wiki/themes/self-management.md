# Self-management

The discipline of giving your body what it needs every day, without expecting cure. Pre-LC, framed as a fob-off — "we don't have a cure, go manage." Re-framed by clinicians-with-lived-experience (notably [[eleanor-stein]] #149) as **the biology of recovery**: respect the body's needs, modulate the nervous system through behaviour, build evidence that the body is safe.

## The two parallel levels (Stein #149)

1. **Observe + believe + give the body what it needs.** Sleep schedule, food, rest, social connection. The hard part is *believing* — Stein observed her body for 8 years without trusting it.
2. **Work on the mind to allow the body to receive what it needs.** Accept reality; resist push-through; give up the productivity script.

## Why this is biology, not psychology (Stein #149)

- Neuroplasticity is ~100-year-old science (Donald Hebb, 1930s). What you think about repeatedly hard-wires in the brain via measurable fMRI changes.
- Brain → body → brain is reciprocal. Symptoms are physiologically real *and* mind-state shifts measurably modulate them.
- "Damage is the wrong word; *changes* are reversible." Same framing as [[becca-kennedy]] #146, arrived at independently — strong convergence.

## The two-question filter for a symptom flare (Stein #149)

- **Imminently dangerous?** If yes → ER. If new symptom → check it out.
- **Familiar?** If yes (waxes and wanes, you've had it 100 times, sometimes you know what triggered it) → safe to **shift focus** to something mood-elevating. Repeated dissonance ("symptom is firing but I'm sitting in the sun") signals the brain that the danger interpretation isn't matching reality. Over many repetitions: dial-down.

## Common pitfalls

- **Hypervigilance about your own body** — opposite extreme of ignore-and-push-through. Both are dysregulation. The aim is *believe-and-respond*, not *monitor-and-fear*.
- **Wearables for the obsessive personality** — Stein is explicit: if data triggers fear, don't get a wearable. Cf. Dr Sarah #144 same conclusion.
- **Cognitive-load-rest** — Netflix, podcasts, scrolling are not rest. Rest = horizontal, eyes closed, ears closed. Cf. [[kelly-mitchell]] (#12), [[wibbs-coulson]] (#17), [[louise-cummings]] (#18) on cognitive pacing.
- **Symptom-as-identity** — when illness takes over every aspect of life, focusing on it 24/7 deepens the danger loop. Practice **shifting focus** to mood-elevating activities even briefly, with symptoms still present.

## Eleanor Stein's current "yelling from rooftops" top tips (#149)

1. **Sunlight, eyes open, no sunglasses, multiple times a day.** Quantum-biology-informed; mitochondrial benefit; modern glass blocks key wavelengths.
2. **Live like a cave person re sun-cycle.** Wake at dawn (40 min before sunrise); bed within 1–2 hrs of sunset. Massive personal shift for her.
3. **Eat real food** — one ingredient, no chemicals, not processed. No single dietary prescription works for everyone.
4. **Mindset that recovery is possible.** Placebo = belief science. Even imagining 100% primes neural pathways.
