# Immobilization (dorsal vagal shutdown)

The third state in the polyvagal model — the autonomic floor below sympathetic fight-or-flight. **The state where most LC fatigue lives.**

Phenomenology:
- Heaviness, can't move
- Brain fog, can't think
- Can't access acceptance, gratitude, self-compassion, or any "higher" cognitive function
- Often misread as depression
- Often misread as personal failure ("why can't I just be positive?")

Mechanism (per polyvagal theory): the dorsal vagal complex is an **older** evolutionary system than the sympathetic; it's the freeze / play-dead response. When sympathetic fight-or-flight has been on for too long without resolution, the system drops to dorsal-vagal shutdown as a survival measure.

## Implications

- Mindset / cognitive interventions are **largely inaccessible** in immobilization
- Need to work **upstream of cognition** — body, breath, somatic, vagal-tone
- Tools that work: SSP, gentle paced breath work, low-intensity body-based practices, social co-regulation (the warm presence of a calm other)
- Tools that often *don't* work in this state: counted breathing exercises, demanding meditation, "just push through"
