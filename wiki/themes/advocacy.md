# Advocacy

Patient organising, policy work, government engagement, and the political fight to get long covid recognised, researched, and treated.

## What this covers

- Founding and running long-covid patient groups (Long Covid Support UK, Long Covid Scotland, Long Covid Kids, etc.)
- Government engagement (Westminster, devolved nations, NHS England, devolved health bodies)
- Campaigns for **specialist long-covid clinics**, integrated multidisciplinary care
- Cross-condition solidarity with ME/CFS, POTS, Lyme, MCAS, fibromyalgia, lupus communities
- Workplace/disability/social-security campaigns
- Lived-experience-led research participation

## Recurring arguments

- Specialist clinics as the missing centre of care; the alternative is "DIY referrals" through fragmented respiratory/cardiology/neurology pathways.
- Repeat A&E attendances cost more than running a clinic.
- Patients are "anxious about being called anxious" — gaslighting suppresses honest mental-health reporting.
- Cross-condition learning: ME/CFS in particular has decades of relevant experience the LC community must not ignore.
