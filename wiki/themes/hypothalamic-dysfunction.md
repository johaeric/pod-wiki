# Hypothalamic Dysfunction

A mechanism frame popularised by [[jacob-teitelbaum]] (#55): the hypothalamus — an almond-sized brain region with disproportionately high energy demand for its size — is the **central circuit-breaker** of CFS / LC / fibromyalgia.

## Why it's a candidate central node

The hypothalamus controls (among other things):

- **Sleep / circadian rhythm** → non-restorative sleep, insomnia
- **HPA axis** (cortisol via pituitary) → adrenal symptoms with normal labs
- **HPT axis** (thyroid via TSH) → low-thyroid symptoms with normal TSH
- **HPG axis** (sex hormones) → cyclic worsening; menstrual-pattern symptoms
- **Autonomic outflow** → POTS, OI, HR/BP dysregulation
- **Anti-diuretic hormone (ADH)** → frequent urination, dehydration
- **Appetite, temperature regulation, blood sugar**

If hypothalamic energy demand outstrips supply (post-viral mitochondrial impairment), **all** these systems can fail at once with **labs reading "normal"** because population reference ranges are 2 standard deviations wide. The patient looks crazy; the labs look fine.

## Therapeutic implications

Teitelbaum's SHINE protocol is explicitly designed to reduce hypothalamic load (sleep + nutrition + hormone trials + low-dose pharmaceuticals + autonomic retraining via ANS Rewire / DNRS).
