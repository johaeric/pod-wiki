# Bioenergetic framework (Ray-Peat-adjacent)

A heterodox metabolic / endocrine framework that sits at tension with mainstream LC nutritional advice (and the keto-leaning advice of several other podcast guests). Articulated on the podcast by [[keith-littlewood]] (#45). Influences traceable to Ray Peat (US biologist, d. 2022) and earlier mid-20th-century metabolic researchers.

The wiki tracks this position as a **live hypothesis**, not as endorsed truth.

## Core claims

1. **Saturated fat is more protective than polyunsaturated fat (PUFA).** PUFAs in cell membranes are oxidatively unstable; their breakdown products damage cell membranes (especially cardiolipin in mitochondrial membranes). Saturated fat is metabolically cleaner.
2. **Glucose is not the enemy.** Acute stress recovery is glucose-dependent. Chronic low-carb / keto in chronically ill patients drives compensatory adrenaline + cortisol, embedding the stress state.
3. **Aerobic metabolism is the goal.** Anaerobic glycolysis (lactic acid build-up) is a sign of system distress — hypoxia, mitochondrial dysfunction, nutrient deficiency.
4. **Progesterone is central.** Antiviral, restores aerobic metabolism, deficient in many female reproductive-tissue conditions and in older men.
5. **Estrogen excess (environmental + endogenous)** drives thyroid suppression, liver dysfunction, reproductive-tissue overgrowth.
6. **Thyroid hormone is functionally underdiagnosed** because TSH-only panels miss stress-suppressed pituitary signalling.
7. **CO₂ retention matters.** Hyperventilation → low CO₂ → impaired oxygen dissociation. Bag breathing is a useful intervention.
8. **Real-food + glucose + saturated fat + adequate protein + targeted hormone restoration** — not elimination diets, not chronic supplementation stacks.

## Where it disagrees with other podcast frames

| Position | Bioenergetic (#45) | Keto / mitochondrial (#21, #44, #23) |
|---|---|---|
| Carbohydrate | Useful, especially in stress | Drive of inflammation; reduce |
| Saturated fat | Protective fuel | Acceptable but secondary |
| PUFA / seed oils | Harmful (cardiolipin damage) | Avoid (inflammatory) |
| Ketogenic state | Stress signal in chronically ill | Anti-inflammatory, mitochondrially supportive |
| Primary lever | Hormone restoration (thyroid, progesterone) | Mitochondrial + gut diet support |

The two frames **agree** on: real food, polypharmacy critique, environmental-toxin avoidance, gentle movement, sleep, the value of breath work / CO₂ retention.

## Why the wiki carries it

- Not all LC patients respond to the keto / mitochondrial-support stack. The bioenergetic frame may explain a subgroup.
- The thyroid / progesterone diagnostic claims are testable and low-cost (full thyroid panel, body temp + pulse tracking).
- The PUFA / cardiolipin claim is consistent with some COVID-era cardiolipin antibody findings (interpretation contested).

## Cautions

- **Methylene blue** (a Littlewood-recommended bioenergetic tool) has serious drug interactions — cannot combine with SSRIs/SNRIs, MAOIs, or many psychotropics. Specialist oversight required.
- **Progesterone supplementation** in men or women requires endocrinology oversight; OTC creams are inconsistent and the dose-response curve is not casual.
- **The framework can drift into RFK-Jr.-territory** if the pollutant / vaccine-injury claims are taken without medical context. Stay first-principles + RCT-ready.
