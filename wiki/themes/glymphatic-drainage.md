# Glymphatic drainage (as LC mechanism + treatment target)

The **glymphatic system** — the drainage system of the brain that disposes of large molecules (toxins, cytokines, post-infectious debris) that the blood-brain barrier cannot handle. Hypothesised pre-2012; mainstreamed via 2012 Rochester (mice) → 2015 + 2017 (animal + human meningeal lymph) → September 2024 Oregon (real-time gadolinium-dye human visualisation during brain surgery).

The glymphatic frame is now a working LC mechanism story: the **cribriform plate** above the nose is both the **viral entry route** (covid travels through the olfactory nerve, hence anosmia) and the **major drainage exit** for brain toxins. When drainage is impaired — by spinal misalignment, sympathetic dysregulation, prior toxic burden, or post-infectious cytokine storm — toxins accumulate in **hypothalamus + locus coeruleus + frontal lobes**, driving autonomic dysfunction, cognitive symptoms, and the multi-organ LC presentation.

## Anchor episodes

- **[[160-dr-raymond-perrin-lymphatic-pt1]]** — anchor mechanism episode. Perrin hypothesised the drainage system in 1989 from a single ME patient improving on cranial + spinal work. The 35-year arc from hypothesis to 2024 validation is the wiki's clearest worked example of "but the textbooks said..." inertia in LC-adjacent science.
- **[[161-dr-raymond-perrin-recovery-pt2]]** — treatment side: the Perrin Technique, self-massage, jigsaw-puzzle framing.
- **[[153-dr-amir-hadanny-hbot]]** — convergent mechanism from a different treatment angle. Hadanny names the cribriform plate as the covid entry route → orbital-frontal + temporal-pole + hippocampal metabolic deficits ("covid horns"). HBOT repairs the wounded brain tissue rather than the drainage; both treatments target the same anatomy from different angles.

## Convergent threads

- **Cribriform plate** as covid entry route (Perrin, Hadanny).
- **Hypothalamic + locus-coeruleus dysfunction** as the autonomic-driver site (Perrin, Teitelbaum #55, Bagnell #63 brain-stem framing).
- **Sympathetic dysfunction → reduced lymph pumping** (Perrin technical mechanism).
- **Mid-thoracic spinal flatness** as a recurring physical sign across LC + ME patients (Perrin); pairs with [[151-dr-lydia-knutson-chiropractic]]'s thoracic-concordances framing.
- **Mega-lymphatics / varicose lymphatics** under skin (palpable, photographable) — Perrin's clinical sign.
- **Heavy-metals magnet observation** (preliminary, Perrin 2024) — proposes that varicose-lymph spirals + heavy metal deposits create local Faraday-effect magnetic fields. Speculative; not yet replicated externally.

## Treatments targeting glymphatic flow

- **[[perrin-technique]]** — gentle hands-on cranial/thoracic/chest/neck-and-face massage + spinal mobilisation + breathing.
- **[[axial-stability-method]]** (Knutson) — adjacent low-force chiropractic; ANS-rebalancing-via-spinal-substrate framing rather than lymph-drainage-mechanism framing.
- **[[hbot]]** — repairs the brain tissue downstream of the toxin accumulation (Hadanny RCT).
- **Sleep optimisation** — glymphatic drainage occurs during deep sleep (delta-wave SWS); sleep dysfunction therefore compromises drainage. Cross-ref [[143-david-joffe-sleep]] (slow-release melatonin, dopaminergic injury, REM behaviour disorder).
- **Breathing exercises** — the thoracic-duct pump is influenced by diaphragm + rib mobility; cross-ref [[breathwork]].

## Caveats

- The **mechanism story** (glymphatic drainage failure as a primary LC driver) is **not yet established at the level of mainstream LC consensus**. The system itself is now mainstream science; whether it's the load-bearing LC mechanism is contested.
- **Treatments targeting the mechanism** still have weaker evidence than the mechanism itself. The Perrin Technique has a 2017 BMJ diagnostic-accuracy paper and a 2023–24 NHS feasibility study (self-massage only); HBOT has the Aviv RCT with 18-month sustained gains. No head-to-head; no large multi-arm trial.
- **Useful working frame, not settled science.** Worth tracking; not yet a guide-the-protocol mechanism.
