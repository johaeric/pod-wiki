# Sleep disturbance

A near-universal LC symptom; for many patients, recovery doesn't begin until sleep stabilises. Mechanism is multi-component — dopaminergic injury, circadian desynchrony, vagal disregulation, breathing dysrhythmia, MCAS overlap, anxiety/PTSD-style hypervigilance. Anchor episode is [[143-david-joffe-sleep]].

## The two phases of sleep (per Joffe #143)

- **Slow-wave sleep (SWS)** — restorative; flushes system; parasympathetic-dominant; SWS pressure peaks ~9 pm; mostly first half of night. Wake from SWS = exhausted next day.
- **REM sleep** — memory consolidation, executive function, new learning; mostly second half of night. Wake from REM = grumpy, less tired.

## What goes wrong in LC

- **Dopaminergic injury** — restless legs (40%+ increase in women), narcolepsy, PTSD, major depression. Under-weighted in mainstream LC research.
- **REM sleep without atonia / REM behaviour disorder** — patients acting out dreams, calling out, moving. Historically a 60–65 yr old men's condition with 80% Parkinson's progression at 14 years; now showing up in young LC patients. Worth a sleep study if anyone in network reports this.
- **Serotonin/melatonin axis** — low serotonin (demonstrated) → low melatonin → desynchronised circadian clock.
- **Vagal injury** — body can't drop into parasympathetic state SWS needs. Overnight = sympathetic-dominant = no restorative phase.
- **Carotid-body / CO₂ chemoreceptor disturbance** — disrupted sleep-onset breathing cycles in some patients.
- **Cortisol-bottoms-out-at-3-am** — Joffe argues the often-cited LC finding may simply be a forward-shifted circadian clock (3 am biologically when measured at 7 am clock time), not low-cortisol pathology.

## What helps (per podcast testimony)

- [[slow-release-melatonin]] — 2 mg at 7 pm (Joffe protocol).
- **Sleep hygiene** — 23–24°C room, dark, quiet, no screens, two duvets if partnered.
- **Sunlight in the morning, eyes-open, no sunglasses** — entrains SCN.
- **Light boxes / blue glasses with lights on top** for housebound patients (high-flux at close range).
- **Meditation / yoga nidra / mindfulness apps** — increases frontal alpha rhythm; bridges to sleep onset.
- [[probiotics]] — gut as neurotransmitter reservoir; dysbiosis correction relevant for sleep.
- **Tackling reflux + constipation** — vagal-axis stabilisation.
- **20-min nap ~1 pm** — natural second sleep window; don't go past 30 min or you'll sink into SWS.
- Treating PTSD-style hypervigilance pharmacologically when present (sleep anxiety = brain making you hypervigilant like in PTSD).

## What harms

- High-dose melatonin (3 mg+) — "more is silly"; misses the slow-release physiology.
- Late-night screens (light suppresses melatonin).
- Hot bedrooms (>32°C all night → 25% less SWS).
- Going past 30-min nap in afternoon (you sink into SWS, wake with sleep inertia).

## Diagnostic

- Lab polygraphic study (full electrodes + video) — needed for LC. Home sleep tests are robot-scored apnea screens; miss LC-relevant findings.
- Reduced SWS/REM = irrefutable EEG biomarker — "fabulous biomarker of misery."
