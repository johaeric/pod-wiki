# POTS — Postural Orthostatic Tachycardia Syndrome

A form of dysautonomia in which **standing produces a sustained heart-rate increase ≥30 bpm (≥40 in adolescents)** without orthostatic hypotension, alongside symptoms of cerebral hypoperfusion (dizziness, breathlessness, brain fog, exercise intolerance, fatigue, headache). Diagnosis: 10-minute stand test or tilt-table test. Strongly post-viral; LC has driven a large new POTS cohort.

> Cross-ref: see `ercipedia/wiki/symptoms/pots.md` and the dedicated treatment pages for the trial evidence.

**Eric has confirmed POTS** (tilt-positive) — see project `CLAUDE.md`. Pages on POTS are unusually load-bearing.

## What helps (per podcast testimony)

- [[beta-blockers]] — first-line in many cases (Amy Small ep #5: key_to_recovery)
- [[ivabradine]] — alternative HR control without sympathetic blockade (Eric is on this)
- [[mestinon]] (pyridostigmine) — improves exercise tolerance via cholinergic mechanism (Eric is on this)
- [[fludrocortisone]] — volume expansion (Eric is on **florinef** = fludrocortisone)
- [[salt-loading]] + fluids — first-line non-drug
- [[compression]] — abdominal/thigh compression
- [[pacing]] — non-negotiable foundation
