# Tinnitus

A noise (ringing, buzzing, whining, music) generated by the auditory system in the brain without an external source. Common in long covid; ~38% in Irish LC cohort study reported it (#71).

Symptoms can fluctuate; **spikes** typically follow change (new infection, stress, hearing change). Most people habituate over time as the brain stops focusing on the sound.

## What helps in LC (from podcast)

| Approach | Source | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| Hearing aids (when hearing loss is present) | #71 Wray | recommended |
| Background sound *below* tinnitus volume | #71 Wray | recommended |
| CBT | #71 Wray | recommended (best evidence) |
| Stress reduction (breathwork, meditation) | #71 Wray | recommended |
| Stellate ganglion block | #76 Groysman | small N anecdotal — one patient cleared after one block |

## What does **not** help

- **Supplements** — explicitly anti-recommended (#71)
- **Drugs** — no medication exists for tinnitus (#71)
- **Avoiding all sound** — quiet rooms make tinnitus more intrusive (#71)

## Red flags (see clinician immediately)

- Unilateral (one ear only)
- Pulsatile (beats with heart rate)
- Accompanied by dizziness
- Sudden hearing loss

## Mechanism in LC (hypothesised)

Multiple plausible mechanisms in long covid: viral-trigger neuroinflammation, sympathetic overdrive (autonomic dysfunction), stress/anxiety amplification, possibly direct ENT effects. Groysman (#76) frames LC tinnitus as an autonomic / brainstem signal — partial support from his SGB anecdotes.
