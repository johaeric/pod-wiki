# MCAS — Mast Cell Activation Syndrome (LC overlap)

A condition in which **mast cells** (immune cells responsible for allergic / hypersensitivity responses, present in large numbers in the gut and skin) become inappropriately activated and release histamine + other mediators (tryptase, prostaglandins, leukotrienes), producing a multi-system picture: hives, flushing, GI symptoms, brain fog, fatigue, headache, muscle pain, food/scent reactivity, blood-pressure swings.

Long covid has unmasked / triggered MCAS in a noticeable subset of patients. **Tina Peers (#38) estimates ~98% of her LC patients have a pre-existing MCAS pattern** when carefully reviewed. Diagnostic challenge: standard tryptase is normal in MCAS (only elevated in mastocytosis); chilled-sample tests are rarely available in UK labs.

> Cross-ref: see `ercipedia/wiki/symptoms/mcas.md` for the formal diagnostic criteria + trial picture.

**Eric note**: Eric is on **zyrtec/pepcid** as an empirical antihistamine trial — see project `CLAUDE.md`. Possible MCAS overlap is part of his clinical picture.

## Treatment protocol — Tina Peers's standard sequence (#38)

Foundation:
1. **Low-histamine diet** (SIGHI list) — often subjective improvement in 3 days
2. Filtered water, charcoal sticks, minimise plastics + EMF (Wi-Fi off at night)
3. Treat background co-morbidities: mold mycotoxins, EBV, Lyme, UTIs, etc.

Pharmacological add-ons (one at a time, 2-week trial):
1. **H1 antihistamines** — loratadine → cetirizine → fexofenadine
2. **H2 antihistamines** — famotidine, ranitidine
3. **Mast-cell stabilisers** — ketotifen, sodium cromoglicate (gut MCAS)
4. **LDN** — both stabiliser and cytokine-effect dampener
5. **Montelukast** 10 mg/day (depression caveat)
6. **Diazepam** small dose (no addiction in MCAS — drug binds mast cells, not brain)
7. Specialist: cromolyn, immunosuppressants

Adjuncts:
- **Omalizumab (Xolair)** for chronic urticaria + angioedema (Khan #37 — major effect)
- **Arc Microtech device** (Peers — anecdotal)
- **Gupta program** (Peers — ~30% improvement)
