# Headache & Migraine

A frequent but under-discussed LC symptom — present across ≥17 episodes in the corpus, often as new-onset migraine or dramatic worsening of pre-existing migraine after COVID. Tends to be folded into "pain" or "MCAS symptoms" in podcast discussions rather than treated as a primary topic; this page is the wiki's correction for that.

**Per [[deepak-ravindran]] (#53):** "There is certainly headache-like conditions developing after covid — there's no doubt about it. People who have pre-existing migraine — it gets much worse. And there have been new-onset migraine diagnoses as well happening after covid."

## Mechanism (corpus consensus)

Most LC headache appears to be **nociplastic** — central nervous-system sensitisation, not structural damage. Same biology family as fibromyalgia, ME/CFS, IBS, chronic migraine pre-COVID. Clinically: imaging clean, inflammatory bloods normal, but the pain is real and severe.

[[jacob-teitelbaum]] (#167) offers a slightly different framing: not all LC headache is nociplastic. He splits LC pain into ~7 named categories and treats migraine as its own bucket with specific protocols (B2, magnesium, triptans). [[michael-bagnell]] (#63) anchors migraine mechanism in the **brain stem** — affected by COVID-related autonomic dysregulation, hence the rationale for vagal stimulation as a rehabilitation lever.

These framings are complementary, not contradictory. The corpus suggests headache in LC is *plural* — sensitisation in some, brain-stem-mediated migraine in others, MCAS-driven in a subset.

## What helped (by category)

### Specific drug / supplement protocols

- **[[vitamin-b2-riboflavin]] 400 mg/day × 6 weeks** — [[jacob-teitelbaum]] (#167) cites repeated studies showing 67–70% migraine-frequency reduction. Cheapest, best-evidenced specific protocol in the corpus.
- **[[magnesium]]** — Teitelbaum's adjunct; also independently reduces migraine.
- **[[triptans-imitrex]] (imitrex-family) taken *early*** — before pain reaches the eyes/ears. Once at that threshold, stomach has shut off and oral meds fail to absorb. Strong coffee with the triptan can re-open absorption (Teitelbaum #167).
- **[[antihistamines]] (H1 + H2)** — for MCAS-pattern headache (with flushing/food/scent reactivity). Tina Peers (#38), Saperstein (#68): higher-than-OTC dose twice daily under supervision.
- **Gabapentin / amitriptyline / duloxetine** — only if neuropathic component; mostly ineffective for nociplastic pattern (Ravindran #53).

### Devices

- **[[vagus-nerve-tens]] (gammaCore-class, non-invasive)** — FDA-approved for migraine + cluster headache. [[michael-bagnell]] (#63) frames it as brain-stem rehabilitation; rentable in the US via telemedicine.
- **[[tens]]** — listed by Ravindran as a nervous-system-calming approach for nociplastic pain including headache.
- **[[ice-packs-temperature]]** — symptom relief only; Rosie (#61) found ice packs the only thing that took the edge off her constant year-long migraine when nothing pharmacological worked.

### Hands-on / practitioner

- **[[acupuncture]]** — [[elizabeth-so]] (#124) treated her own migraines with acupuncture pre-LC; strong personal-credit signal from a practitioner who later recovered from LC via EAM.
- **Functional-neurology chiropractic** — [[michael-bagnell]] (#63), [[lydia-knutson]] (#151) — both work brain-stem / cervical-input angle.

### Nervous-system / mind-body (where *recoveries* concentrate)

The strongest signal in the corpus for *resolving* (not just managing) chronic migraine in LC is on the nervous-system side. Patients with full LC recoveries via brain-retraining frequently resolved chronic migraine **alongside** the LC.

- **[[natalie-gold]] (#189)** — recovered via [[brain-training|ANS Rewire]]; resolved 16 years of chronic migraine alongside LC. **Closest profile match to Eric in the entire corpus** (severe POTS-presentation + PEM + migraine).
- **[[rebecca-tolin]] (#166)** — recovered from ME/CFS + chronic migraines + insomnia + interstitial cystitis via mind-body work; now teaches the approach.
- **[[esther-recovery]] (#32)** — striking single anecdote: a headache she'd had for months would "be just gone in five minutes" once she identified the emotion her brain was protecting her from. Mind-body / [[somatic-tracking]] / [[journal-speak]] framework.
- **Karen Wright (#182 / RA #961)** — migraine + tinnitus persisted *through* her brain-retraining recovery and then resolved after thyroid surgery + "surrender." Important honest counterpoint: not every migraine is purely nervous-system.

### Diet / trigger management

- **[[anti-inflammatory-diet]]** (Ravindran #53, Myhill #118) — foundation, not magic.
- **[[low-histamine-diet]]** (Peers #38) — pair with antihistamines if MCAS pattern.
- **Aggressive trigger removal during acute phase** — Miguel Bautista (RA #623, recovered): "even a small piece of chocolate would give me a migraine." Reduced to salads, rice, bone broth, chicken, yams until nervous-system settled.

### Other (lower-evidence)

- **[[homeopathy]]** — [[roddy-schrock]] (#210) reports one patient case of "extraordinary migraine headaches" majorly decreased in frequency and intensity under constitutional homeopathic treatment. N=1, low evidence grade, logged for completeness.

## What clearly did NOT help (multiple stories)

- **Standard migraine tablets / triptans when the headache wasn't actually migrainous** — [[chiara-berardelli]] (#3, herself a former GP): misdiagnosed as "chronic menopausal migraine," tried the standard meds, found they did nothing because the underlying mechanism was post-viral, not migrainous.
- **OTC painkillers (ibuprofen etc.)** — Rosie Pidgeon (#61) had constant migraine for >1 year as a teenager: "nothing you can do makes any difference."
- **Repeated rounds of antibiotics for presumed sinus infection** — Devon Carter (RA #984): three escalating rounds before LC was identified.
- **Aggressive migraine meds in ER on first crash** — Devon Carter (RA #984): drugged-up + sent home with no follow-up; the migraine meds "mellowed me out" but didn't address the underlying mechanism.
- **Pushing through ("if you've got a headache, you keep going")** — Karen Wright (RA #961) explicitly names this as the pattern that kept her stuck for years.

## Diagnostic considerations (rule-out)

When headache is severe (e.g. ER-level pain requiring morphine), the corpus suggests several rule-outs before treating empirically:

- **Medication side effects** — [[felice-groysman]] (#92) emphasises: "Some medications can cause headaches." Check current stack.
- **Structural / thyroid issues** — Karen Wright (#182 / RA #961) had migraine + tinnitus partly resolve after thyroid-nodule surgery.
- **Imaging** if not done — to rule out the small-but-real chance of secondary cause.
- **Distinguish headache vs scalp pain vs muscle pain around the head** — Groysman (#92) on a paediatric case.
