# Dysautonomia

Umbrella term for autonomic-nervous-system dysregulation — the system that controls heart rate, blood pressure, temperature, GI motility, sleep, breathing rate, and the fight-or-flight ↔ rest-and-digest balance. **POTS** ([[pots]]) is one specific dysautonomia presentation; the broader term covers all the related dysregulation seen in LC.

A strong recurring frame on the podcast: a paper (cited by Kelly Mitchell #12) suggests every LC symptom *can* be put down to an autonomic-nervous-system issue. Multiple recovered guests (Rachel #8, Suzy #7, Amy #20) frame their recovery in autonomic-regulation terms.

## How it presents on the podcast

- **POTS** — orthostatic tachycardia, dizziness, breathlessness on standing/stairs (Amy Small ep #5)
- **Hyperacusis** + sensory hypersensitivity (Amy Anker ep #20, AJ ep #13 brain-injury parallel)
- **Hyperventilation pattern** — sympathetic-dominant upper-chest mouth-breathing (Kelly #12, Wibbs #17)
- **Tachycardia** — Amy Anker ep #20
- **GI dysregulation** — Kelly #12, Cass #19
- **Tremors, twitching** — Cass #19, Amy Anker #20
- **Temperature dysregulation, sleep dysregulation, depression cycles** — Amy Small #5, Amy Anker #20

## Mechanisms / framings

- **Sympathetic dominance** — "stuck in fight-or-flight" — Suzy Bolt's recovery framing, Rachel Whitfield's recovery framing, Amy Anker's recovery framing.
- **Looping pattern** — Amy Anker #20: NS keeps firing on minimal triggers even after acute injury heals. Phantom-limb-pain analogy.
- **Hyperventilation-driven autonomic stress** — Kelly Mitchell #12, Wibbs Coulson #17.
- **Direct viral involvement** — referenced but not mechanistically detailed in eps 1–20.

## Wiki anchor episodes added in this batch (#61-70)

- **#63 [[063-michael-bagnell|Bagnell]]** — articulates the **brain-stem dysfunction** hypothesis as the single upstream substrate for the dysautonomia symptom set. Adds the [[brain-stem-dysfunction]] theme.
- **#66 [[066-lauren-stiles|Stiles]]** — the **dysautonomia umbrella is not a diagnosis** framing; subtypes (POTS, OI, OH, hyperhidrosis, GI dysmotility, dry eyes/mouth). Patients with "dysautonomia" should ask which kind. **67%** of LC patients have moderate-to-severe dysautonomia (Stanford collaboration).
- **#68 [[068-dr-david-saperstein|Saperstein]]** — the **trifecta** (hypermobility + dysautonomia + MCAS); LC POTS may present differently from classic POTS (HR-racing dominant, OI less so).
