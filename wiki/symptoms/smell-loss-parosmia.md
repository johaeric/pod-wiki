# Smell loss & parosmia in Long Covid

The olfactory-dysfunction cluster: anosmia (no smell), hyposmia (reduced), parosmia (distorted, usually disgusting, triggered), phantosmia (untriggered hallucination), and olfactory perseveration ([[simon-gane]]'s term — triggered-then-untriggered persistence). Cardinal early-pandemic symptom; less common in later variants.

## Anatomy refresher

Three nerves contribute to the eating experience:

- **Olfactory (CN I)** — smell, in the nose. The most LC-affected.
- **Gustatory (CN VII, IX, X)** — true taste (salty/sweet/sour/bitter/umami) on tongue.
- **Trigeminal (CN V)** — pain/temperature/menthol/chili in mouth+nose.

What we colloquially call "taste" is actually **flavour** — brain integration of all three, with a retronasal puff of air carrying odorants up to the olfactory cleft on each swallow. Acute early-COVID impaired all three; later variants spare gustation and trigeminal more.

## Mechanism

The olfactory epithelium normally regenerates from stem cells (one of the few neural systems with this property). SARS-CoV-2 both damages neurons and **impedes regeneration**. Parosmia is interpreted as the regrowing nerves making bad connections — distortion *because* of activity. Statistically, parosmia patients show slightly better long-term outcomes than pure hyposmia patients.

Olfactory bulb volume correlates strongly with depression; loss of smell signal → bulb shrinkage → progressive low mood. **Smell training preserves bulb activity from the top down** even when bottom-up signal is poor.

## Prognosis

- Recovery is real but slow — typically 6–18 months for the majority
- **Recovery at 2.5 years and beyond documented.** Don't accept "after a year you have to live with it."
- Children: typically resolve in ≤ 6 months. Watch for refusal of favourite foods as the parosmia signal.

## Treatment

- **[[smell-training]]** — the only evidence-based intervention. Sniff lemon / eucalyptus / rose / clove (the classical four), 2×/day, with active mental engagement (memory recall, anticipation). Compliance is the main failure mode — most patients try and give up.
- **[[stellate-ganglion-block]]** — Groysman (#76) reports often-immediate response (within 30 seconds of block) for parosmia/anosmia, including >2-year-post-covid patients. Not first-line; clinician-administered; success defined as "tolerating foods/drinks they couldn't before." Not yet standard of care; meaningful patient-reported signal.
- **Pharmacy-grade meal replacement (Huel etc.)** for parosmia patients dropping high-protein foods → malnutrition risk.
- **Mental-health support** as a parallel track. Cross-ref [[mental-health-and-suicidality]].
- **Practical food tips**: visual contrast, temperature contrast, trigeminal stimulation (chili, ginger, mint), true-taste balance.
- **Avoid:** ENT-only pathway. Most ENTs look in the nose, say "looks fine," and discharge. The work is upstream.

## What to watch for

- **Olfactory perseveration / "smell lock"** — Simon Gane's term. A smell starts as triggered (e.g. detergent → disgust), then persists for hours/days untriggered. Don't try to "outwait" it; reduce exposure to known triggers.
- **Parosmia + nutrition collapse** — high-protein avoidance is the silent harm. Catch with weight tracking + dietetic support.
- **Family + intimacy disruption** — partners, children, food rituals, self-soothing. Plan around it.
