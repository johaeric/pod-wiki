Karen Wright is a Scottish NHS physiotherapist who got Long COVID in autumn 2021 at age 40 — POTS-presentation with ~16 symptoms, including inability to raise her voice. Despite an intellectual background in Long COVID rehab service development, lived experience was incomparable. Brain fog blocked self-application of what she already knew.

## Recovery arc

- **Anger phase** at the injustice — kept crashing despite dropping 90% of life
- Realised everyday activities (upright posture, conversation, light, *digesting food*) were energy drains. The "fish and chips that left her unable to walk for two days" became golden information
- **Low-histamine diet** was her first real win — within 24h the body's internal "revving" calmed, breathing dropped, sleep became restorative for the first time in months. Hope returned.
- Built baseline, then added gentle movement, structure, sleep regularity, activity-rest cycles
- Used **breathwork** and **meditation** (lying down — couldn't tolerate upright); yoga nidra a major form of rest
- Pivotal mindset: "rest is not bad — the body needs it; I am the priority"
- Spring 2024: back at work 30hrs, parenting, hiking, paddle-boarding. Residual: reduced cognitive capacity, migraines, tinnitus
- Found a thyroid lump → **half-thyroidectomy** revealed cancer (contained). Migraines and tinnitus disappeared with surgery — possibly thyroid had been driving those symptoms all along, not Long COVID
- Autumn 2024: full recovery, "new lease of life"
- Now runs Sage Health and Wellness — private LC coaching practice (left NHS day before recording)

## Key quotes

> "Take your foot off your neck. You don't have to be that way. You don't have to do all the doing. It's okay to rest. You're enough — even with greasy hair, even at zero productivity."
