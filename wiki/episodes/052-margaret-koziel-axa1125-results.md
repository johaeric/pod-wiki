# #52 — Margaret Koziel — Update on AXA1125

Follow-up to [[035-axcella-axa1125]] (April 2022). Koziel is back to share the Phase 2a readout — and the results are remarkable for an early-phase trial.

## Trial design recap

- Placebo-controlled, ~42 LC patients (21 active : 21 placebo, identical-tasting placebo)
- 28-day dosing
- AXA1125 = combination of **6 amino acids** (oral, endogenous metabolic modulator class)
- Co-primary readouts: muscle mitochondrial function via MRS (Oxford), patient-reported fatigue, six-minute walk test

## Headline results

- **~70% of the AXA1125 arm had clinically significant improvement in physical fatigue**, vs **essentially no one in placebo**.
- **3 of 21** in the active arm reported they were **back to their pre-COVID baseline** in 28 days (using a "compared to when you last felt well" scale).
- Most others moved from **moderate-to-severe** physical fatigue → **mild** physical fatigue.
- Both **physical and mental fatigue scales improved**.
- **Six-minute walk distance moved with fatigue**, an important sign that subjective improvement tracks function.
- **Mitochondrial function tests came back "way, way off"** — confirming biological abnormality, not "in your head."

> "We are seeing fundamental changes in how the body processes and handles energy that speaks to both how we work and also the biology of this disease." — Koziel

## What the readout proves and doesn't prove

- This is a **Phase 2a placebo-controlled efficacy + biomarker signal** — strongest possible early-stage data.
- Sample is small (~42); 28 days is short; no PEM-specific outcome was used. Koziel acknowledges the next trial needs PEM-relevant tools (return-to-work, sustained activity, not just six-minute walk).
- Not yet a regulator-approved therapy; Phase 2b/3 still ahead in conversation with FDA + MHRA.
- ME/CFS overlap was considered but the company's focus stays on LC for the public-health-emergency reason.

## What's next

- Next trial designed in conversation with health authorities; expected to be **longer than 28 days** but **not multi-year**.
- Koziel commits to listing on clinicaltrials.gov when it opens.
- She emphasises the **patient-voice strategy** worked at MHRA/FDA level — encourages listeners to keep advocating to local governments.

## Significance for the wiki

- **First placebo-controlled positive readout for a fatigue-targeted LC therapy.** This is a different strength of evidence than anything else indexed so far in this wiki.
- The mitochondrial-dysfunction biological frame (per Naviaux #44) gets independent corroboration via the trial's mitochondrial readout.
- Strengthens the case for [[mitochondrial-dysfunction]] as a load-bearing mechanism page.
