# #006 — Prof Nick Sculthorpe (UWS)

**Guest**: [[nick-sculthorpe]] — Professor of Clinical Exercise Physiology, University of the West of Scotland. Research background: exercise and aging. Two LC research projects discussed.

## Studies

### TraPS — Tracking Persistent Symptoms in Scotland (CSO-funded, 3 phases)

- **Phase 1**: scoping review of all LC symptom literature → produced a list of ~198 symptoms compressed into ~50 categories.
- **Phase 2**: cross-sectional online survey of LC patients in Scotland (about to launch at recording).
- **Phase 3**: 9-month longitudinal app-based symptom tracking; recruits people with recent covid+ test, follows them.

Key methodological note: published cohort symptom-loads vary not by country but by **cohort effect** — studies that report a high incidence for one symptom tend to be high for all; those that are low are low across the board. Cause unknown.

### HEARTPACE — heart-rate-based pacing intervention RCT (NIHR-funded)

- Builds on a lockdown-era CSO study of physical activity in older adults (Fitbit + custom app).
- For LC, **flips the goal**: messages sent when HR exceeds an *individualised* threshold for too many minutes.
- For [[pots]] patients HR is unreliable → tracks **steps** as alternative.
- 6-month intervention vs standard pacing advice control; control group crosses over after.
- Goal: reduce frequency / severity / duration of PEM episodes. Aims at finding *predictive* signals (e.g. resting HR rising 10 bpm in the week before a crash).

Defines PEM strictly per **Canadian criteria** (≥14 hours). Notes that ~40–50% of LC patients meet that strict criterion; the rest have "PEM-like" episodes lasting 2–12 hours — important difference for translating findings to ME/CFS later.

## Notable arguments

- **Pacing advice is too abstract.** "Track your energy" is not a concrete cognitive task; layering brain fog on top of it makes pacing nearly impossible without external scaffolding.
- **Crash prediction needs frequent data.** A pre/post-only study design will miss the dynamics; only continuous app tracking can spot pre-crash signals.
- **Future trajectory**: validate in LC → extend to ME/CFS → potentially paediatric LC.
