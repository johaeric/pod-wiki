# #112 — Jackie's 2023 Reflections (host-solo)

Jackie's annual end-of-year reflection. **Bookends the recovery arc** — last year (#64) she was coming out of the worst crash of her LC; this year she **declares full recovery** and articulates the **post-recovery adjustment phase** that turned out harder than she expected. Pairs with [[094-jackies-recovery-story]] as the after-the-fact synthesis.

## The 2023 arc

- **Late 2022 / early 2023:** worst crash of latter recovery; coming out of it. Months to rebuild confidence in her body.
- **End of January 2023:** career break approved → "weight lifted I hadn't realised was there." The structural stress of sick-pay-running-out and Occupational Health was bigger than recognised.
- **Spring 2023:** recovery happened (told in detail at #94).
- **Mid-Sept 2023 onward:** started doing work again — recording the podcast, breathwork certification, ideas list rather than diving in.
- **Now (Dec 2023):** runs **breathing for long covid** ([[long-covid-breathing]]) with [[vikki-jones]]; just finished first cohort.

## Anchor: Johanna's "95% crash" warning

Jackie credits an off-air comment from [[johanna-rayl]] (#56): **the worst crash often comes at ~95% recovery, when patients believe they've made it.** Jackie wasn't 95% in 2022 — but the "I'm finally getting better" framing made the subsequent crash mentally devastating in a way pure-physical pain wouldn't be.

> "The mental pain — the loss of that hope, the return of that hopelessness, the numbness, the complete shutdown — that was worse than the physical symptoms in some way." — Jackie #112

## Anchor: post-recovery is its own journey

> "Recovery wasn't the end of the road as I'd thought it would be. Recovery was actually the start of another journey — the journey of who Jackie 2.0 is." — Jackie #112

Specific challenges Jackie names:

- **Limits are different but still exist** — she had to learn to think like an "unfit healthy person," not a chronically-ill person and not the pre-Covid athlete. Couch-to-5K-style: 30s run / 1min walk pattern.
- **Tiredness ≠ fatigue** — relearning what normal post-exertion feels like; not panicking when tired.
- **Emotional healing takes longer than physical** — she still works on it.
- **Brain "braining"** — Jackie's word for her always-on idea-generation. Recovery brought balance; she now has tools to switch it off.
- **Therapist's instruction:** "Maybe you don't need to rush into anything or solve all the world's problems at once."

## What life looks like now (Dec 2023)

- Sits on the floor a lot ("happy place; helps me feel grounded")
- Stress + anxiety still present; difference is *awareness* and *management* — not eradication
- "Should" and "perfect" being deliberately removed from her vocabulary
- Running [[long-covid-breathing]] with [[vikki-jones]] — first cohort just completed; described as "in sync" with Vicki, complementary skills
- Fitness not at pre-Covid yet but doesn't think about doing-too-much; listens to body day-to-day
- Recent weekends: snow camp at -5°C → wet-and-windy stay-at-home
- Podcast continues; "no plans to stop"

## Eric-relevance

- **Post-recovery adjustment is real and named here** — Eric should expect that "recovered" is the *start* of a separate phase, not an end-state. The wiki's only equally-clear articulation is [[098-charlotte-hammond-recovery]] (post-recovery hypervigilance + sensory residuals).
- **Career break as inflection point** — Jackie names this explicitly: the structural stress of being-on-sick-pay is itself a recovery blocker. Generalisable.
- The **Johanna 95% warning** is a load-bearing wiki principle — late-recovery crashes are normal; not a sign of regression to baseline.
- **Tiredness ≠ fatigue** is the operational handle Eric will need post-recovery; the wiki should hold this as an explicit named distinction.
