**Guest**: Jamie Waterhouse — Melbourne Australia. **Cross-corpus reaffirmation = same Jamie as LCP #186** ([[186-jamie-bedbound-to-thriving]]). Same recovery; tighter framing here.

## What this episode adds beyond LCP #186

- **Tighter timeline**: Covid May 2023 → severe bedbound → suicidal-break-and-hospitalisation → 3-program stack (Primal Trust → Healing Dudes → Lightning Process at ~90%) → full recovery → back at work 4 days/week + parenting at recording.
- **Three-program-stack narrative**: each program built on the previous one. **Primal Trust** for the foundation (light-visualisation-into-the-ground + "you're safe" self-talk). **Healing Dudes** for the *facing-fears* pillar (face-the-fear-of-driving / face-the-fear-of-supermarket / etc.) — this got her from ~50% to ~90%. **Lightning Process** for the final 10%. Striking corpus articulation that **multiple brain-retraining programs can be sequenced** rather than choosing one.
- **Faster EFT with Lolita Holmes** for emotional blockers at the end.
- **Post-recovery anxiety** framing — first-shower-back-to-enjoyment took 6 months. *"It's been almost a year since my last crash and I still have that lingering 'what-if-this-makes-me-crash' looping thought."* Anchor articulation of the corpus's recurring **post-recovery-anxiety / lingering-fear-loop** pattern.
