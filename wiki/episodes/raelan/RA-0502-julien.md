# RA #502 — Julien (Berlin): SGB + ketamine infusions for long covid

**Guest**: Julien — Berlin, Germany. Journalist. Long covid from July 2022 (second Covid infection + concurrent Brad Pitt interview + sleep deprivation + stress stack). Has **12-year history of depression and anxiety pre-LC** — frames LC as overlapping with his existing mental-health condition. Recovery in progress, ~2 years in. Not full recovery.

## Long-covid course

- **July 2022**: caught Covid; severe acute (smell/taste loss, brain fog, fatigue, can't work).
- **Months 1–6**: aggressive doctors-and-supplements route, refusing the psychological framing.
- **Eventually**: discovered Gupta program, then Raelan's channel.
- **Spring 2023**: improved enough to think he was close to recovery. Stopped antidepressants → crashed harder. Symptoms returned.
- **Then**: SGB → transient relief. Ketamine → mindblowing psychedelic experiences + transient relief.

## Notable interventions (key data points)

1. **[[stellate-ganglion-block|SGB]] — `helped_partial`** (Berlin clinic). After the injection, **smell and taste returned immediately** for several days. Confirmed for him that the symptoms had a sympathetic-overdrive component that was reversible. Effects faded — needed re-application. **Adds to corpus**: now SGB has both `recommended` (Groysman LCP #76, #91, #92), `helped_partial` (Julien RA #502 here), and `no_effect` (Karen RA #354). Variable patient response.
2. **Ketamine infusions** — first instance on the corpus. Used as Germany's later-line depression treatment (covered medically because his depression was chronic). **Psychedelic experiences with explicit symbolic content** — a black balloon held his anxiety; he gave the balloon his fears; woke up with smell + taste restored for days. **Mechanism interpretation he gives**: ketamine acts on the glutamate system; the deeper insight he names is that LC for him *is* an embodied form of his pre-existing fear-of-dying-alone trauma.
3. **Body therapy (one session, body therapist holding him)** — striking data point: as the felt sense of being held landed, symptoms surfaced + smell/taste returned for seconds. Direct evidence that **felt-safety produces autonomic state shift** in real-time. Pairs with the corpus's broader polyvagal-frame on co-regulation.

## What he names as still unsolved

- **Felt-safety / "I am loved and safe in this world"** — has had glimpses of how to access it (via SGB, ketamine, body therapy) but no daily-practice route to *generating* the state himself yet.
- **Repressed emotion** — can intellectualize / talk about feelings but says he can't *feel* them. The recovery work he names as remaining is closing this gap.

## Notable framing claims

- **Long covid as embodied trauma surfacing** — *"this LC thing is an image / metaphor for a bigger thing — when I solve the LC thing I'm solving an essential part of my life."* Probably the cleanest articulation on the corpus of LC as an *opportunity* for old psychological work to surface.
- **Drug-induced glimpses of recovery as proof of possibility** — *"if this drug can give me this state, my brain can do it by itself."*
- **He may need a complete-life-pause / retreat** to reset — daily life is too entangled with the patterns he's trying to change. Pairs with the broader corpus's **leaving-stressful-job / radical-rest** pattern.
