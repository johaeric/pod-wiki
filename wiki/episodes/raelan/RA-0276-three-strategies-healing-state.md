# RA #276 — How to keep your nervous system in a healing state (host-solo)

**Format**: Host-solo. Three strategies for keeping the nervous system in a healing/parasympathetic state.

## The three strategies

1. **Don't let a slip turn into a slide.** From Jeanette McCurdy's memoir *I'm Glad My Mother Died*. Distinguishing **guilt** (about an action — useful, motivating) vs **shame** (about who you are — paralysing). When you slip, name it as a slip; don't let shame turn it into a multi-day/multi-week slide.
2. **Dial down the importance of things** (via Ali Abdaal). Telling yourself *"I'm going to dial down the importance of this"* before a stressful event (doctor's appointment, work meeting) reframes it from existential threat to ordinary moment. Concrete cognitive lever.
3. **Make your head a better place to be.** Recurring theme from her recovery interviews. Tools: meditation (she names [[soma-meditation]] as her current practice), therapy, self-care reminders, social connection, nature, boundaries. Body-state follows head-state; head-as-control-center sends signals to every organ.

## Wiki value

Tighter than RA #235 — narrower in focus, more concrete tools. Useful pairing with [[polyvagal-theory]] and [[mind-body]] for any patient who has the framework but is looking for *what to actually do day-to-day*.
