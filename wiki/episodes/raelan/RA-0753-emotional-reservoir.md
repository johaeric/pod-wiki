# RA #753 — The key to recovery (almost) everyone is missing: emotional reservoir

**Format**: Host-solo. Articulates a 3-step recovery framework with **emotional reservoir** (Nicole Sachs's beaker metaphor) as the often-missed second step.

## The three-step framework

1. **Respond well to symptoms** (Miguel Bautista's Golden Rule, well-covered).
2. **Deal with the emotional reservoir** ← *this is what's often missed.* Repressed emotions accumulate; once the beaker fills, the brain registers the same threat-state as a physical danger. Tools: journaling (Sachs's "Journal Speak"), therapy, screaming into a pillow, honest conversations. *Goal isn't empty — goal is manageable level.*
3. **Slow expansion of activity** with felt-safety messaging.

> "An emotion felt is an emotion changed."

Pairs with [[tms-mind-body-syndrome]] and [[032-esther-recovery-story]]'s Sachs-anchored recovery. Anchor articulation that **"respond well to symptoms" alone is necessary but insufficient** — without addressing the emotional reservoir, symptoms come back.
