# RA #280 — Amy Engkjer (= Amy Anker): from severe long covid to a joyful full life again

**Guest**: Amy Engkjer — same person as **[[amy-anker|Amy Anker]]** (LCP #20). Missoula MT meditation teacher; recovered LC patient; founder of **[[positively-covid]]**. The surname-mismatch is a name change between the LCP recording (2021, "Amy Anker") and this Raelan recording (2023, "Amy Engkjer"). Same person, same recovery, same nonprofit.

**Cross-corpus reaffirmation** of her recovery story already on the LCP wiki.

## What this episode adds beyond LCP #20

- **Recovered in 1 year + 10 days** — a more precise timeline than LCP #20.
- **Initial reading of *The Brain That Changes Itself* by Norman Doidge** while still acutely ill — explicit named neuroplasticity book that gave her the conceptual scaffolding before recovery work began.
- **Norman Cousins** — second named figure in her recovery reading. *(Cousins recovered from ankylosing spondylitis via laughter + courage; classic 1979 *Anatomy of an Illness* reference.)*
- **Lightning Process — done AFTER she had already recovered** — explicitly to "learn the language" because so many recovered patients had used it. *Useful framing*: she does not credit LP as the program that recovered her. Logged as `mentioned_only` rather than as a recovery driver.
- **NLP (neuro-linguistic programming)** — done during recovery; helped her notice the language she was using.
- **Tightest articulation on the corpus of meditation as a layered tool**: (1) calming the nervous system *first* (5 min instead of 30) to get a foothold; (2) once the prefrontal cortex is online, using the *space* meditation creates to **respond** rather than **react** to symptoms; (3) using meditation to loosen the grip on the sickness identity. Three discrete operative levels.
- **Joy shocking** — her own coined practice: cold-water shower with full-volume singing of Florence + the Machine's *Shake It Out*. Combines cold-shock + elevated emotional state + bilateral movement (singing). Pairs with [[cold-water]] and [[mind-body]].
- **Phone-call-as-triple-threat-PEM-trigger**: walking + emotional content + breath-holding while talking. One of the cleanest dissections of an unexpected PEM trigger on the corpus — useful for any LC patient noticing post-call crashes.

## Notable framing claims

- **"Show yourself the way each moment"** — pacing as nuanced + individual + dynamic, not as a fixed plan. Pairs with the corpus's frame-fit framework (Kyle, Roberto, Kristyna).
- **WHO meta-review of 1700+ LC studies** — only a tiny percentage study the *recovered* population. Most study hospitalized or still-in-primary-care patients. *Implication*: recovery-stories channels like Raelan's are partly compensating for a research-design gap.
- **"Whatever we do to get dopamine first thing in the morning is what our brains will seek the rest of the day."** Concrete morning-routine principle: hydrate first, meditate second, walk in nature third — *before* phone.
- **Mayo Clinic protocol now includes "decrease focus on symptoms"** as part of LC care. Useful citation for any future Eric clinician conversation.
