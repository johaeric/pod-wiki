# RA #534 — Troy Roach: a highly sensitive person's approach to LC recovery (nicotine + biohacking)

**Guest**: Troy Roach — California native, Madrid Spain-based, university professor (teaches research-paper-reading to nurses + PTs). LC since 2020 + multiple reinfections. **~90% recovered**. Citizen-scientist focus on **nicotine patches for LC**.

## Long-covid course

- **2020**: caught Covid; never fully recovered; went down to ~30% over a year of pushing.
- **Second infection**: shifted to **neurological-dominant subtype** (LPR / inability to speak; gave 3 weeks of university lectures via PowerPoint). Hospitalised 4 days for cytokine storm.
- **~2 years in**: accepted he had LC; started biohacking research approach.
- **Diverticulitis hospitalisation + 9-day fast on antibiotics** → jumped from ~40% to ~60% (one of his biggest single inflections).
- **Nicotine patch protocol** during 5th acute infection → "remission event" — 36 hours of MDMA-like clarity / parasympathetic-dominant feeling.
- **Now ~90%** recovered.

## Key concrete protocols he uses + advocates

1. **Nicotine patches (low-dose transdermal)** — extends [[nicotine-patches]] coverage. Mechanism: nicotine binds to nicotinic-acetylcholine receptors (parasympathetic system); displaces residual viral debris; modulates cytokine storm; concentrates 80× in the gut. Pairs with [[130-marco-leitzke-nicotine-patches]] LCP coverage.
2. **80/20 pacing** — *don't* live at your full baseline; reserve 20% of daily energy as healing buffer. Pacing-as-recovery-input rather than pacing-as-survival.
3. **Diet — no sugar, no processed food** as the cheapest first-line. Citing one researcher's protocol where patients improve 50% from this alone before any other treatment.
4. **Buchinger-clinic fasting** (Switzerland) — ~200 kcal/day to maintain gut microbiome while staying in ketosis. Safer than complete water fast.
5. **Huberman morning routine** — outdoor sunlight, hydration, no internet first thing, exercise early.
6. **HRV tracking** + heart-rate-variability biofeedback.

## The Highly-Sensitive-Person hypothesis

Troy's framing claim: **HSPs are over-represented in the LC / ME-CFS cohort** (his unofficial Twitter + Facebook polls returned 90% positive on HSP / ADHD / autistic neurodiversity markers). Mechanism speculation: dopamine + serotonin receptor genetic variants make these patients more susceptible to neurological damage from viral / traumatic / inflammatory stimulation. Recommends every LC patient take the standard HSP test as part of self-understanding for brain-retraining work. **First HSP-as-predisposing-factor framing on the corpus.**

## Notable framing claims

- **Twitter DMs to researchers** as a hack — they answer in 10 minutes; emails take 6 months.
- **No miracle cures** — he's emphatic. Even nicotine, even MBSR, even brain retraining: each is one tool. Beware the *"the last thing I tried cured me"* fallacy.
- **Tess Falor's "Remission Biome"** patient-led trial — antibiotics + complex pre/post protocol, 50-patient ME/CFS cohort. Patient-led-research frontier example. Pairs with Tom Bunker (RA #300) and Glenn Chan (RA #465).
