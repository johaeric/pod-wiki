# RA #118 — Lorrie Rivers (severe ME/CFS recovery → long covid recovery)

**Guest**: [[lorrie-rivers|Lorrie Rivers]] — recovered ME/CFS patient (8 years bedbound in her 20s); ME/CFS recovery coach for 20+ years; co-author of *Living from Inspiration* (with Deepak Chopra and Wayne Dyer); lifelong fibromyalgia. Cross-corpus appearance — already on the wiki as guest of LCP #70 and LCP #137.

**Format**: Recovery-story interview + promo for her *Long Covid + ME/CFS Holistic Health Summit* (July 5–12, 2021) and her [[lorrie-rivers-relief-and-transformation|Relief and Transformation]] course. Recorded June 2021.

## What this episode adds beyond the existing wiki

This episode predates the **parasite / hidden-infection theory** that dominates her LCP appearances (#70, #137). In June 2021 her recovery framework leads with **energy medicine** (EFT → Healing Codes → Trilogy) on a foundation of pacing + diet + mindset. Same recovery, different framing — useful as a date-point: her holistic / energy-medicine layer was load-bearing *before* the parasite theory crystallised, so the parasite layer is best read as something she added later, not the thing that recovered her.

## Long-covid arc (May 2020 → ~Jan 2021, ~8 months)

- **May 2020**: caught COVID. Initial illness "not a big deal" — better in a week. Tested negative throughout (probable false negative).
- **~2 weeks post-acute**: woke up with full ME/CFS-style symptoms. *"i woke up one morning and i went 'i have me cfs again'."* Recognised it within 24 hours.
- **3 days of meltdown** ("i would rather die than have to go through this again"; partner-leaving-her flashbacks from her 20s ME/CFS).
- **Then** her own observe-the-emotion-without-getting-caught-in-the-story practice kicked in.
- **Recovery executed by following her own [[lorrie-rivers-relief-and-transformation|Relief and Transformation]] course**: pacing + diet + energy-medicine practices + mindset/emotional work.
- **Roughly 1 year later**: fully recovered.

## Key recovery components she names

1. **Pacing** — the foundation. *"that can really make or break it."* She had to learn this from scratch in the 1990s — wishes she'd had her own course back then to *"shave years off"* recovery.
2. **Energy medicine — EFT → Healing Codes → Trilogy.** Trilogy combines tapping (EFT), Alex Lloyd's Healing Codes (specific hand-position holds on healing centres), and chakra work. She describes it as *"meditation on steroids — literally takes you from sympathetic to parasympathetic in a minute or two or three."* Cites HRV / heart-rate / parasympathetic studies on Healing Codes (Alex Lloyd's claims; uncross-checked here). See [[healing-codes-trilogy]].
3. **Diet / nutrition** — strict and multi-year for original ME/CFS. Less explicit on specifics for LC recovery in this episode (refer to LCP #70 for the strict-low-carb/keto framing she developed later).
4. **Mindset / emotional observation** — *"my job is to sit back and to really honor these emotions and really feel them fully and to just observe — not get caught up in the story."* This is the load-bearing technique that pulled her out of the 3-day meltdown into recovery mode.
5. **Holistic frame** — explicitly *"body, mind, spirit"*; specialist-by-specialist Western medicine *"will mostly come back normal."*

## Notable framing claims

- **Predisposition pattern**: most of her clients are *high-energy, high-functioning, intelligent* people (not a moral claim — pattern). Same observation surfaces in Chimére (RA #76), Natalie Gold (LCP #189), and others.
- **Reinfection vulnerability**: having recovered from ME/CFS does not protect against post-viral relapse. *"It was like welcoming in an old friend you don't necessarily want."*
- **Long covid ≡ post-viral ME/CFS pattern** (with political diagnostic-coding caveats — she notes that in Sweden ME/CFS does not get medical aid but LC does).
