# RA #516 — Linley: The Switch turned off my long covid in 2 weeks

**Guest**: Linley Smith — Nelson, New Zealand. Caught Covid Feb 2023 (Omicron). 7 months of LC. **Recovered in 2 weeks** after doing **The Switch program by Mel Abbott**.

## Long-covid course

- **Feb 2023 acute**: 5–6 days bedridden, fever; tried to return to work after 10 days, couldn't.
- **Months 1–7**: bed-rest cycle (initially via Gez Medinger LCP #46's ebook framing); built up to 2-hour-out → 1-hour-rest cycles. Couldn't tolerate noise / restaurants. Couldn't go on family Australia trip.
- **Month 7**: friends recommended The Switch. Did the 4-day online program with Mel Abbott. **Improvement on day 1; back to full-time work within 2 weeks; walking 15–20K steps/day at NYC marathon weekend within 2 months.**

## Recovery component

**The Switch program (Mel Abbott)** — `key_to_recovery`. 4-day online program, 12pm–7pm sessions. Brain-retraining framework adapted for sympathetic-overdrive nervous system. Mel had 11 years of CFS herself before recovering and developing the program; reports thousands of patient recoveries. **First instance on the corpus.**
