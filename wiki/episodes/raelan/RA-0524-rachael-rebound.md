# RA #524 — Rachael (Rebound Athletic): from bedbound to running after toddler

**Guest**: Rachael — London UK ex-athlete + personal trainer + (now) LC recovery coach. **Cross-corpus reaffirmation = same Rachael as LCP #127 and LCP #168** (recovered patient who founded **[[rebound-athletic]]** and the **[[90-day-rebound-method|90-Day Rebound Method]]**).

## What this episode adds beyond LCP coverage

- **Tighter timeline**: ~3 years from Covid (June 2021) to "fully recovered" status. Pregnant wife at onset; daughter born 8 months in.
- **Bath-time-and-bedtime as her recovery marker** — anchor articulation for "find a single repeating activity that's a marker." Watching it gradually become easier over months and years. Useful framing for any patient looking for a reproducible-progress metric.
- **NHS Health-with-grade-exercise-therapy harm row** — she tried it (post-pregnancy) and *"got a taxi out of bed to the session, did walking lunges, crashed."* Confirms the existing [[graded-exercise]] harm pattern from a coach's perspective.
- **Detailed late-stage HBOT description** — *"often made me feel worse before it made me better"* but bridged her from can't-read/drive to driving herself to sessions. Pairs with Glenn Chan's RA #465 framing on HBOT.
- **Her own program now offered at scale**: launched a monthly subscription called the **Baseline Club** built around her **Baseline Formula** + **muscle-activation** modality — small controlled movements (lower energy expenditure than e.g. squats) + gentle mobility + 3-level progression, plus 1:1 messaging support. Affordable model designed for non-working LC patients.

## Notable framing claims

- **"Fear is the last symptom"** — most lingering, hardest to shake. Anchor articulation.
- **Movement is at the *tail end* of recovery, not the start** — explicit. Mitochondrial-function rationale for *deliberate* movement once a healing space has been created. Pairs with [[145-jenny-adams-recovery]] / [[136-meg-anderson-movement-part-2]] on the wiki.
- **Cold water at the wrong stage made her crash** (~7 months in); 9 months later it helped her energy, sleep, mood. Same intervention, different state, different outcome — pairs with the **frame-fit framework** documented across earlier batches.
- **"You can manifest things — I never would have believed this before but I've done it"** — meditation + visualization (Joe Dispenza specifically) named as load-bearing for the late-stage recovery.
