# RA #465 — Glenn Chan: I used Python to help me heal (vaccine injury + patient-led data analysis)

**Guest**: Glenn Chan — Ontario, Canada. Computer programmer. **Vaccine-injured** (second mRNA dose June 2022) — not infection-LC. Mostly recovered now (~95%). Built **patient-led survey research at sickandabandoned.org** using Python to analyse treatment outcomes across the LC + ME/CFS + vaccine-injury communities. Cross-condition framing — he establishes that LC, ME/CFS, and vaccine injury overlap heavily on symptom + treatment-response axes.

## Long-covid-style course (vaccine injury onset)

- **June 2022 acute (post-second-shot)**: severe insomnia, brain fog, can't walk + talk simultaneously, can't program for cognitive overload, debilitated.
- **~Month 8**: tried **ivermectin** ("the eye drug" — he avoids saying the name on YouTube for censorship reasons). Significant improvement.
- **Then**: **black seed oil** added. Compounding gains.
- **Now**: ~95% recovered; can program normally; can't quite return to break dancing fully (deconditioning vs lingering symptoms unclear).

## Survey findings (his Python-driven analysis of patient outcomes)

⚠️ **Sample size caveat**: small (~30–40 recovered respondents). Treatment-response rates 2–20%. He explicitly seeks more respondents.

**Treatments that ranked promisingly (top of his data):**
- **HBOT** (hyperbaric oxygen therapy) — *only LC treatment supported by a randomized controlled trial*. Soft-shell chamber as cheaper first-line trial. ⚠️ some patients have lasting setbacks; titrate doses carefully.
- **Fasting** (>48 hr wet fasting > intermittent / OMAD; wet > dry). Pairs with Adam Langdon's RA #300 fasting credit.
- **Certain antimicrobial supplements + prescription drugs** — names include ivermectin, statins (some antimicrobial properties), nattokinase, serrapeptase. **Underlying hypothesis**: LC / vaccine-injury is driven by a *microbe imbalance / pathogenic infection state*; antimicrobial interventions are what move the needle.

**Treatments that ranked at the bottom (consistent worsening):**
- **SSRIs** — strong negative-outcome cluster
- **Gabapentin / psych drugs**
- **Antibiotics** (counterintuitive given the antimicrobial hypothesis — Glenn doesn't fully reconcile this; possible ecology-disruption explanation)

**Critical warning he names**: even *interventions that work* (e.g., HBOT) can permanently set some patients back. **Discontinue treatments that are worsening symptoms** rather than pushing through under herxheimer / "die-off" framing. **Pairs with Becky Sharpe RA #309's herxheimer-as-progress warning** — same lesson from a data-analysis angle.

## Notable framing claims

- **LC + ME/CFS + vaccine-injury overlap heavily** — same treatment-response patterns. His survey data: the three are "either the same condition or overlap so heavily as to be very similar."
- **Be conservative on treatment** — start low, watch carefully, stop early if worsening. **Don't trust herxheimer-as-progress framing from clinicians.**
- **The literature has financial-conflict-of-interest contamination** — he names a published RCT where the author has financial ties to the manufacturer but declares no conflicts. Worth holding when reading any LC supplement study.
- **Survivors' guilt as a real post-recovery experience** — first explicit articulation on the corpus.

## Source-bias note

Raelan-source. Substantial-not-full recovery. Vaccine-injury cohort, distinct from infection-LC. **Patient-led-research framing** is a useful corpus contribution alongside Tom Bunker (RA #300) and PLRC (LCP #110). His survey data is small-N — treat as exploratory not authoritative.

> Sample-size caveat applies to all his rankings. Helpful as a *map of where to look*, not as a definitive recommendation tree.
