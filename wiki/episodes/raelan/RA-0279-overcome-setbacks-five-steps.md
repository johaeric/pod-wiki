# RA #279 — How to overcome setbacks: 5 steps (host-solo)

**Format**: Host-solo. Five-step framework for processing recovery setbacks (paid program failures, expensive interventions that don't work, plateaus, big disappointments).

## The five steps

1. **Acknowledge it.** Give yourself space and time to feel it. Suppression makes it last longer.
2. **Get perspective (pie chart frame).** Zoom out — list things going well. The setback is one slice, not the whole pie.
3. **Learn from it.** Detective-not-judge — what would you do differently next time? Reframe as part of your success origin story.
4. **Forgive yourself.** Self-compassion. Speak to yourself as you would to a friend in the same position.
5. **Let it go and move on.** Most stress lives in past or imagined future; bring yourself into the now.

## Wiki value

Practical setbacks-framework. Useful for any patient who's just had a brain-retraining program / supplement protocol / coach not work for them — pairs with the corpus's frame-fit pattern (Kyle, Roberto, Kristyna) where intervention-failure is information, not catastrophe.
