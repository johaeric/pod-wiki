# RA #147 — Kristyna's post-viral CFS full recovery story

**Guest**: Kristyna — Czech-born, lives in Vienna, Austria. Diagnosis: post-viral fatigue / CFS (probable but unconfirmed Covid trigger). Working in a high-stress job pre-illness; quit it during recovery. Native Czech speaker; English as second language. ~1 year from onset to full recovery.

## Pre-illness pattern (predisposing)

- Cycles of recurrent infection in the 1–2 years pre-onset (lyme, tonsillitis, multiple antibiotic courses)
- **Panic attacks at night** — explicit "wake-up call" she suppressed and pushed through
- Outwardly fine, internally chronically stressed and unhappy in her job
- Same high-functioning / perfectionist pattern noted across many Raelan recoveries

## Long-covid course (~Summer 2020 → ~Summer 2021)

- **Onset**: viral-feeling illness with no positive Covid test (timing too late). *"It was almost like my body was faking the illness"* — atypical from her own infection history.
- **Months 1–6**: progressive deterioration; bedbound at peak. ~30 minutes to a sentence; whole-body pain; severe sore throat.
- **Many specialist visits** in Austria — all "perfectly healthy" reports. Insurance covered some; out-of-pocket "crazy amount."
- **Diagnosis at month ~5**: an Austrian neurologist who self-claimed CFS specialism gave her *post-viral fatigue / CFS* in a 30-minute appointment. Recommended pacing (no explanation; she'd already learned it from Raelan's channel). **Recommended LDN** but never responded to her follow-up calls or emails when she got worse and asked to start. **She never started LDN.** That was the moment she stopped seeing doctors.

## What helped (recovery components)

1. **Brain retraining via an unnamed coach ("Jason") found on Raelan's channel — `key_to_recovery`.** A one-on-one brain-retraining program from a coach Kristyna identifies only as "Jason." She ran his program for ~2–3 months. Saw mental-shift gains in days (positivity, energy); physical gains in weeks (gentle skiing at week 2–3); full functional recovery by month 3. Quote: *"the hardest thing i've ever done in my life — i had to battle my own thoughts."* **Coach not identifiable from transcript** — logged as generic brain-retraining under [[mind-body]] until disambiguated.
2. **Quitting her stressful job** (~month 6) — `key_to_recovery` adjunct. Explicit: *"the stress was always there going on — they kept asking me when i'm coming back."* Allowed full focus on recovery; she frames the illness as "the push" she needed to leave a job that wasn't fulfilling her.
3. **[[kambo]] (frog-poison ceremony) ×2 at ~95% recovery** — `helped_partial`. Used at the late stage explicitly for emotional / trauma release, not physical recovery. *"Vomiting, screaming, crying"* — described as crazy intense. Says it cleared lingering "heaviness on my head" and post-illness depression. Plans to repeat yearly as detox/release. **Caution flag**: powerful, ceremony-only, not for unstable nervous systems.
4. **Recovery-story consumption** as a hope-input — watched some of Raelan's interviews 5–10× each. Aligned with [[raelan-agle]]'s own "hope as a recovery input" framing.
5. **Daily structure** during severe phase — hot/cold showers, dry brushing, celery juice, supplements. By her own assessment most of these *did not help* but the act of doing structured self-care was scaffolding.

## What did not help

- **Supplements (~weekly new boxes; significant spend)** — `no_effect`. Hidden from her husband. *"I haven't seen the difference with or without."*
- **Doctors / Western medicine route** — `mentioned_only` (her direct quote: "ridiculous"). Not framed as harm — just useless. **LDN was prescribed but never taken** — log as `mentioned_only` on the [[low-dose-naltrexone]] page.
- **Acupuncture** — tried; she didn't believe in it; didn't help. Notable framing claim: *"it is also important to do what you believe it helps"* — she names the placebo / belief-state factor explicitly.

## Notable framing claims

- **Belief-state matters** — she calls out that she didn't believe in acupuncture and got no benefit; her brain-retraining belief was strong and the result tracked. This is one of the cleaner first-person articulations of the placebo / expectancy effect on this corpus.
- **Healthcare-gaslighting in a non-English-speaking healthcare system** — explicitly different from the LCP / English-language pattern: in Austria/Czechia she found *zero* CFS information in her own languages and *zero* recovery stories. Recovery for her was gated on enough English to consume Raelan's channel.
- **You don't have to suffer for years** — she's emphatic that 1-year recovery should be possible to expect, not just multi-year. *"I would die for somebody at the very beginning who would tell me — you don't have to suffer for four years."*
- **Mind doesn't catch up to body immediately** — interesting late-stage observation: physical recovery preceded mental belief in recovery by weeks. Had to keep checking with husband: *"is it okay to be exhausted after hiking 50 miles?"*
