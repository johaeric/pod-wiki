# RA #150 — Erik Hajj's long covid (ME/CFS) recovery story

**Guest**: Erik Hajj — 27, married, video wedding photographer + educator, St. Louis MO. Got Covid filming a wedding November 2020 (pre-vaccine wave). Recorded ~October 2021, 11 months in. Substantial recovery in progress. Runs his own LC YouTube channel under his name.

## Pre-illness pattern (predisposing)

- *"My wife used to say I was perfect"* — 2020 vision, no medical history, no medications, athletic.
- Multiple stressors stacked in 2020: pandemic, St. Louis civil unrest, started a new job (career change from education), beloved dog died, multi-weekend filming schedule running 12–14h on his feet.
- Same predisposing-stress pattern named on Chimére (RA #76), Kristyna (RA #147), and many LCP recoveries.

## Long-covid course

- **Acute**: mild Covid for 2 weeks; gave it to wife (she recovered fully on schedule).
- **Post-acute**: shortness of breath at rest for **2 months straight**; chest pain (multiple ER visits — costochondritis dx, heart cleared); chest-area neuropathy; brain fog; fatigue scaling up over months 4–11.
- **Months 1–4**: bedbound. Worked from bed. Lived on meal-replacement shakes — couldn't make food.
- **November 2020 → March 2021**: pure misery, no improvement.

## The pivot — March/April 2021 onwards (vaccine + brain retraining)

Erik names two distinct inflections:

1. **Moderna vaccine (March/April 2021)** — *"I didn't start recovering until I got my vaccine."* Anecdotal vaccine-helped-LC report. Not a cure (he was still substantially symptomatic afterwards), but the start of the recovery curve. See [[covid-vaccine-post-acquisition]].
2. **[[ans-rewire|ANS Rewire]] (Dan Neuffer)** — bought and worked the program. *"Worth every penny — I would buy it over and over."* Credits the program with reframing negative thoughts and starting the brain-retraining work that compounded over the months that followed.

## Recovery components in his own ranking

1. **Wife as primary caregiver** — explicit acknowledgement that "being selfish" and letting her take over was load-bearing.
2. **Time + the right things** (vs. time alone).
3. **Vaccine** as the first inflection.
4. **ANS Rewire** as the structural brain-retraining work.
5. **Baseline-and-pacing** — *"submit to recovery, not defeat."*
6. **Mindfulness, meditation, breathwork** — daily practice.
7. **Visualization** for chest pain — Miguel Bautista's pain-receptor-shrinking technique. Practiced ~1 month, saw results.
8. **Pendulum metaphor for symptom swings** (also Miguel Bautista) — extreme swings early; narrower swings as nervous system settles. Useful framing for the patient experiencing wide ups-and-downs early in recovery.
9. **Diet cleanup** — eliminated fast food, generic nutritious-meals approach (no specific named diet).
10. **Recovery stories as hope-input** — Raelan's channel + Miguel Bautista's videos.

## What did not (specifically) help

- **Western medicine / ER visits** — no harm; just no answers. Steroids (prednisone) gave some shortness-of-breath relief but the symptom returned after the course.

## Notable framing claims

- **"Get out of your own way"** — explicit framing that recovery requires identifying what *you* are doing that hinders it. *"That sentence alone — something will pop into your head."*
- **Stages, not linear** — he experiences recovery as discrete jumps every few weeks/months: "this symptom is gone," "I can do X again."
- **Body-recovers-before-mind-trusts-it** pattern (same as Kristyna RA #147, Aaron West LCP #171, others).
- **Brain-as-pattern-engine** — your brain doesn't distinguish reality from imagination; constant symptom-rumination keeps the body in the ill state.
