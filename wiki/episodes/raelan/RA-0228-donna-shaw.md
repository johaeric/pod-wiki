# RA #228 — Donna Shaw's long covid + ME/CFS full recovery story

**Guest**: Donna Shaw — Surrey, England. School/college worker pre-illness. Got Covid **April 2020 (first wave UK)**. Severe acute — couldn't breathe, throat-closing sensation, two ER visits with "no pneumonia, you're fine, go home." 18-month sick leave; eventually had to give up the job. ~2 years to full recovery. Now a qualified life coach specialising in anxiety.

## Pre-illness pattern

Fit, healthy, no underlying conditions, never been ill (*"I've only ever worried about other people"*). About to be promoted to a college job that the lockdown and illness terminated.

## Long-covid course

- **April 2020 acute**: severe dyspnea, ER twice, scans clean. Throat-closing sensation no clinician understood (*"like being strangled"*).
- **Months 0–6**: poor sleep, gasping awake; held husband's hand at night thinking she might not wake up. Severe anxiety triggered by everything; depression. Slow improvement to "not dying anymore" by ~12 weeks.
- **October 2020 crash**: pushed through to make her daughter's 21st birthday cake + drive to Brighton + restaurant dinner. Massive crash from that day onwards. **Bedbound; couldn't lift a glass of water; couldn't think; couldn't decide.**
- **Diagnosis**: GP suggested CFS, put her on a 4–5-month waitlist. A Harley Street CFS doctor told her *"5% recover; buy this book on Amazon; good luck."*

## What helped (multi-component, no single key)

1. **[[pamela-rose]] coaching** — *"this is not forever, it's for now."* Found her baseline (which was nothing) and built from there. Pacing-with-self-compassion frame.
2. **[[optimal-health-clinic]] / Alex Howard** — mindset + ANS-state work; the "you must be in a healing state to heal" frame is what she names as the meta-lesson. *"Your body is built to heal — but you have to be in a healing state."*
3. **Sopfit / "Sophie"** (Instagram-based fitness/recovery coach for chronic illness — Raelan mentions she's been on the channel before; likely a future RA ingest) — countered prior advice to **never walk while legs hurt** with *"you cannot not move; muscle memory matters."* Pushed Donna to start at **30 seconds outside** in the snow; built up over 6 months to 20-min walks. The graduated-walking practice.
4. **Hill with Liz** (Liz Hill's recovery YouTube channel; not yet a wiki page) — affirmations, vision-board work. Donna built a vision board (paddle-boarding + 25th anniversary holiday + a porn-star martini) and credits the affirmation routine with a mindset reset within ~6 weeks.
5. **Daily affirmations** — replacing morning *"oh god, still ill"* thoughts with *"I deserve to be happy; I'm doing everything in my power."*
6. **Heal-the-gut nutrition program** (12 weeks with a nutritionist; specific elimination protocol) — incremental contribution.
7. **Camper-van micro-outings** — husband bought a camper van; she'd sit in it on the driveway with the door open as her "outside" for an hour. Counts as graduated re-exposure.
8. **Magnesium-salt baths** for leg pain.
9. **Buddy system** for new LC patients — supporting others as part of her own recovery.

## What contradicted earlier advice

The "don't walk while your legs hurt" advice from one clinician (3 months no walking) **was wrong for her**. Sopfit's *"you cannot not move"* advice — combined with sub-30-second graduated micro-doses — was what unlocked her movement axis. Both can be true for different patients; the wiki should hold both. See [[gentle-movement-restorative]].

## Notable framing claims

- **"Healing state vs. stress state"** — Alex Howard frame: the body cannot heal while the autonomic nervous system is locked sympathetic. Echoes [[polyvagal-theory]] and Roberto Escobar's polyvagal anchor (RA #221).
- **"5% recover" garbage stat** — Donna names this; both she and Raelan call it baseless. Real recovery rate is uncountable because most recoveries happen outside mainstream medicine and aren't logged anywhere. **Worth holding for any future Eric conversation about "I've been told only X% recover" prognosis claims.**
- **The cake / birthday dinner crash** — single concrete example of "I felt OK, did one normal thing, crashed for months" pattern. Useful illustration for explaining PEM to non-LC family.
