# RA #984 — From the ER floor to full long covid recovery (Coach Devon Carter)

**Guest**: Devon Carter — Victoria BC Canada. Devon's **own recovery story** episode (RA #864 was the Q&A tips co-host). 18 months off work; ER admission; full recovery; PRT-certified; now lead coach in Raelan's Brain Retraining 101 program; runs her own group coaching experiences.

## Notable framing claims

- **"Life looked fine on paper"** anchor — for many LC patients the predisposing-stress wasn't visible externally. Good job, good people, regular workouts — but underneath, *running on hot* (constant pressure to impress, keep up, be 100%). **Even the good stuff becomes pressure on a primed nervous system.** Anchor articulation of the corpus's recurring **stress-on-paper-vs-stress-on-the-system** distinction.
- **Science is what eliminates fear of symptoms** — *"the only thing that can eliminate your fear around your symptoms is the true understanding of what's causing them."* Pairs with Sarno's "knowledge therapy" framing on the corpus.
- **Asking for help can sometimes be the wrong thing** — context-dependent (deceptively counter-intuitive but Devon explains the mechanism).

## Source-bias note

Raelan-source. Coach (commercial interest — she works inside Raelan's program). Full recovery. **Anchor articulation of the looks-fine-but-running-hot pattern** — useful for any patient whose life-on-paper doesn't match the typical "obviously stressed" narrative.
