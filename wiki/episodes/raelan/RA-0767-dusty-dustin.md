# RA #767 — What saved Dusty at 73 (national-level cyclist's LC recovery)

**Guest**: Dusty Dustin — Valley Springs CA. Retired teacher, graphic artist, **national-level cyclist** at age 73. **Oldest recovered patient on the corpus**. ~2 years severe LC — housebound, couldn't walk to kitchen, sound-sensitive, too dizzy to eat, too foggy to think. Now riding 140 miles/week and planning a 12-hour championship race.

## Notable framing claims

- **Note on the toaster for 3 years**: *"I will be stronger."* Lived it.
- *"Long covid didn't break me, it made me."*
- **Age was not a barrier** — anchor articulation that recovery is possible at 70+. Useful counter to any *"you're too old"* defeatism for older LC patients.
