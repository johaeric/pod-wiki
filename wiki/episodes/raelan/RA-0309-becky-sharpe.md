# RA #309 — Becky Sharpe: a lifeline in long covid hell

**Guest**: Becky Sharpe — London UK (recording from Spain). Acting coach pre-illness. **7 years of escalating fatigue + back pain + IBS + joint pains** before Covid was the *final straw* that pushed her into ~3 years of housebound/bedbound LC. Full recovery; now coaches LC patients alongside her acting coaching.

## Long-covid course

- **Years pre-Covid**: cyclical fatigue, IBS, back pain, joint pain — manageable, dismissed as "just life."
- **Covid → severe LC, ~3 years**: housebound or bedbound. Couldn't walk to the kitchen. Couldn't cook. Lights blacked out. Total isolation during pandemic lockdowns.

## What did NOT work — important warning rows

**Harsh naturopath protocol** (1.5–2 years; 30–40 capsules/day + harsh restricted diet) — `harmed`. Got worse and worse under the *"herxheimer reaction means it's working"* framing. *"People kept telling me 'you feel worse but it's because you're getting better' — and now I look back and realize that really wasn't true for me."*

This is a **strong corpus warning** about herxheimer-as-progress framing. Eventually she stopped trusting outside voices saying "you feel worse but you're getting better" and started trusting her body's actual signal. Pairs with [[lorrie-rivers]]'s harsh-protocol cautions (different protocol, same lesson).

## What helped — recovery components

1. **TMS / Mind-Body Syndrome ([[tms-mind-body-syndrome]])** — `key_to_recovery`. Read **Dr John Sarno's *The Mindbody Prescription*** → went from ~30% to ~80% recovered **in 3 days**. Striking timeline. Then read more TMS books to consolidate.
2. **80-year-old London naturopath** — what helped here was specifically *the safety signal* he gave her: when she said *"I'm afraid I'll die before I get to see you,"* he said *"If I have to drive down to London and pick you up, I will not let you die."* That moment was the start of recovery. **Reframe: the load-bearing intervention wasn't the naturopath's protocol — it was the felt sense of safety from a trusted authority.**
3. **Asyra body-scan machine** (held in hand, reads vibrations) — **the data itself was the calming intervention**. Print-outs showing *"no cancer cells, no parasites, just avoid olives for a week"* gave her concrete reassurance her body was OK. Logged separately as a calming-via-data pattern.
4. **PEMF mat used 4×/day, 20 min, with meditation** — Becky's **explicit retrospective re-attribution**: she now believes *the mat itself wasn't the mechanism — the 4× daily rests with meditation were doing the work*. Worth holding for any LC patient considering an expensive PEMF mat (£600–£45,000): **lying flat 4× daily with meditation produces the same effect for free**.
5. **Daniel Vonbruck health coach** — coaching navigation; brain-fog-friendly.
6. **Emotional-feeling work** — last 20% from 80% → 100% was learning to *feel* emotions. Pre-illness she identified as *"a calm person who didn't have a lot of feelings"* — now retrospectively recognises that as severe emotional repression. Recovery completion required learning to cry in yoga class, etc.

## Notable framing claims

- **Herxheimer-as-progress is dangerous framing** — when externally told "you feel worse but you're getting better," she stayed worse. When she actually did get better, she could feel it. **Trust your body's signal over the practitioner's narrative.**
- **High-achiever / type-A keeps you in failing protocols longer** — her stubbornness ("if you're riding a horse and it dies, get off") was *the trait that kept her stuck* in the harsh naturopath protocol for 1.5+ years. Pairs with the corpus's recurring type-A predisposition pattern.
- **The naturopath's words mattered more than his protocol** — felt sense of safety was load-bearing; the protocol was incidental.
- **PEMF debunk** — explicit retrospective re-attribution of an expensive intervention to the cheap practice that ran alongside it. Useful epistemic example.
