# RA #317 — 5 ways to calm your nervous system (host-solo)

**Format**: Host-solo. Five concrete in-the-moment techniques for symptom flares.

## The five techniques

1. **Smiling.** Even forced smiling — kicks off endorphin/serotonin/parasympathetic chain. Per Dan Buglio's *body-is-not-broken* protocol: lie in bed staring at ceiling smiling for 5 min during a flare.
2. **Reassuring self-talk + word-swap game.** Replace fear-words ("fatigue", "pain") with neutral / silly ones ("banana", "pineapple"). From the [[curable-app]].
3. **Calm breathing.** Long exhales (slow inhale through nose, longer exhale through mouth). And the **physiological sigh** (double inhale + slow exhale) — Stanford 2017 evidence backing.
4. **Meditation.** 5–10 min during a flare. Recommended apps: Calm, Insight Timer. Names [[soma-meditation]] for deeper work.
5. **Time in nature** ("forest bathing"). If too unwell to go outside, even imagining nature scenes works (brain doesn't distinguish strongly between doing vs. imagining).

**Bonus**: time with animals — soothing for ANS.

## Wiki value

Practical in-the-moment toolkit. Useful pairing for any patient who has the framework but is mid-flare and needs *what to do this minute*. The physiological-sigh technique is one of the cleaner research-backed concrete tools on the corpus.
