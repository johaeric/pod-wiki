# RA #300 — Adam Langdon: from neurological hell to ~95% recovery

**Guest**: Adam Langdon — Oakville Ontario, Canada. Caught Covid Christmas 2022 (Omicron wave). 18 months into recovery at recording; substantial-recovery-in-progress (~90–95%). **Neurological long covid** subtype — brain-dominant symptoms. Runs the **Beating Long Covid** YouTube channel.

## Pre-illness pattern

Type-A: gym 5×/week, work 50 hrs/week, small kids, full-press.

## Long-covid course

- **Acute Dec 2022**: mild infection. Returned to gym after 1–2 days rest (in retrospect: too fast).
- **Week 2**: massive crash. **Severe neurological symptoms** — depersonalization, derealization, anxiety, panic (6–7/day), depression, loss of emotion, tinnitus, "burning brain," head pressure.
- **Months 1–6**: doctors couldn't diagnose; Reddit deep-dive; eventually identified as long covid.

## What helped (multi-component recovery stack)

1. **Fasting (Tom Bunker's protocol)** — `key_to_recovery` aspect. Started intermittent → 24hr → 72hr → 3-day. *"Felt amazing during; crashed after eating — refeed matters."* Tom Bunker is a patient-led-research figure whose fasting protocol is now in formal clinical trials.
2. **Fluvoxamine** (off-label SSRI for brain inflammation) — participated in a Toronto Canadian Mental Health Association **PET-scan study confirming brain inflammation in LC** (published June 2023). Used fluvoxamine off-label per his findings.
3. **Sauna + cold water immersion** — built up minute-by-minute. Reduces brain inflammation per his model.
4. **Meditation + breathwork** — daily. Apps used: Insight Timer, *Breathwrk* (which specifically has LC-targeted breathing sessions).
5. **Grounding** — barefoot on grass + grounding mat + crystals/rocks (he tried everything).
6. **Symptom tracking via Daylio app** — load-bearing for noticing patterns; explicit recommendation. Pairs with his Beating Long Covid channel content.

## Notable framing claims

- **Patient-led research as the actual frontier of LC treatment** — Tom Bunker's fasting protocol going from forum to clinical trials is his cleanest example. Pairs with [[patient-led-research-collaborative]] (LCP #110).
- **Brain-inflammation as a confirmed mechanism** — PET-scan study (Toronto, 2023) is one of the cleaner mechanistic anchors for the **neurological-LC subtype** specifically.
- **"Find recovery stories that resonate; not all flavours of LC are the same."** Mirrors Raelan's three-themes synthesis (RA #235).
- **Foundational interventions vs. trial interventions** — meditation, breathwork, grounding, sauna, cold water are *foundational* and should run in parallel to anything else; supplements / medications / fasting are *trial* interventions to test one at a time.
