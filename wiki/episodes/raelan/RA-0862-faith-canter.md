# RA #862 — This heals faster than any protocol (Faith Canter, return guest)

**Guest**: Faith Canter — Portugal. Return guest. Recovered from ME/CFS + autoimmunity + heart issues + thyroid conditions. Now coaches others.

## Key framing — "hidden fuels"

Faith's claim: **food is one fuel; air, light, thoughts, environment, connections, joy are also fuels.** Recovery stalls when patients optimise food but neglect the others. **"Coming back to nature — coming back to our own nature, simple traditional ways of being."**

**"If you can have fun, that's just as healing as any protocol."** Joy regulates the nervous system + teaches the body it's safe to be happy.
