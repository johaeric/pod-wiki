# RA #354 — A single mom's guide to navigating long covid (Karen Knutson)

**Guest**: Karen Knutson — Watertown MN single mom of a 7-year-old. **Recovery in progress** (~halfway at recording, not full recovery). Useful corpus contribution: in-progress single-parent voice + several `no_effect` data points.

## Long-covid course

- **June 2022**: stressful life events → brain fog (had been there before during stress).
- **September 2022**: caught a virus from daughter (suspected Covid, never confirmed). Got worse, kept pushing.
- **Eventually**: severe LC + black-mold exposure (apartment tested → had water damage + mold).
- Saw multiple specialists (ER, neurologist, infectious disease, long-covid clinic). Diagnosed long covid + post-viral syndrome + suspected MCAS + ME/CFS.
- Had to quit her job and her business; lost her independence.

## What did NOT work — corpus-relevant `no_effect` rows

1. **Stellate ganglion block** (SGB) — flew to Texas to a pain specialist who treats LC patients with SGB. **Didn't work for her.** Note: SGB has 3 `recommended` rows on the wiki from Robert Groysman (LCP #76, #91, #92); this is the **first patient `no_effect` row** for the actual procedure. Useful counter-evidence.
2. **Multiple supplements** — calls her shelf "the graveyard pantry." Significant spend. Most did nothing.
3. **Detoxes** — every detox crashed her for 4 weeks afterwards. *"Your body will detoxify itself slowly over time; the sicker you are, the less I would push it."*
4. **Sarno's *The Mindbody Prescription*** — couldn't get through it. *"Speaks very medically — didn't resonate with me at the sick state I was in."* Notable counter-data to Becky Sharpe's RA #309 30%-to-80%-in-3-days from the same book — **frame-fit again** (same book, opposite outcomes for two patients).

## What helped (multi-component, halfway recovery)

1. **Sleep medication** — ended 8 months of severe insomnia (sleeping 2–3 hr/night). Single biggest inflection.
2. **Anxiety medication** — needed pharmacological help to access calm; *"absolutely okay if you need that extra help."*
3. **Cold showers** — strongly helped with depression.
4. **Mindset + affirmations** — handwritten phrases around the home; brain-retraining-via-self-talk variant; recurring repetition. *"I just kept saying it over and over until I believed it."*
5. **Yoga Nidra** (free on YouTube) — load-bearing rest.
6. **Ayurvedic practitioner** — diagnosed via heart-rate reading; gave her plant-based-leaning diet + specific herbs. Helped in last few weeks.
7. **Recovery stories on Raelan's channel** — vowed to stop reading dark long-covid forum content; only consume recovery content.

## Notable framing claims

- **"5% recover" stat is baseless** — pairs with Donna Shaw RA #228, Jackie RA #335 on the same point.
- **Boundaries-as-recovery-skill** — being-around-people-pleaser is a predisposing pattern; learning to leave when tired is a skill she's still building.
- **Self-compassion as the universal lever** — *"would you talk to a friend like that?"* test for inner-critic.
- **In-progress recovery is valid content** — explicit framing that not-yet-fully-recovered voices matter; the "I read this book and was climbing mountains" recovery stories can feel alienating mid-journey.
