# RA #226 — Kristine's post-viral ME/CFS full recovery story

**Guest**: Kristine — Latvian living in Denmark. Post-viral ME/CFS. Trigger pattern was **Covid vaccine + viral infection on top** producing severe stress on her body. Months of severe insomnia + fever + sleep paralysis. Full recovery via Jason's brain-retraining program — striking timeline (sleeping 8 hrs after 1 week; skiing 8 am–4 pm at week 4–5).

## Pre-illness pattern

Childhood: not a "very healthy chat" — periodic illness; doctors couldn't figure out why. Through 2020s: super active, full-time work + outdoor hobbies + social life.

## Long-covid course

- **Trigger**: Covid vaccine, then a virus on top → "too much stress for the body."
- **Months 0–1**: persistent fever, heart racing, **severe insomnia → sleep paralysis** (lying down, can't move but not asleep).
- **Doctor visits**: high inflammation markers but no localising scan findings. Sent home with *"you're young, just a wrong virus, you'll recover."*
- **Weeks/months passed**: symptoms worsened, supportive boss let her work from home with a fever, but quality of life collapsed.

## What didn't help

- **Multiple supplements** (magnesium, vitamins, etc.) — `no_effect`.
- **Sleep pills** — even single, double, triple doses: didn't work. Severe insomnia persisted.
- **Massage + alternative therapies** — no effect.
- **Headspace meditation app** — done religiously morning + evening, didn't move the needle for her at this stage. Logged on [[meditation]] as a `no_effect` row (counter-balance to the helped_partial / key_to_recovery rows).
- **Psychologist sessions** (work-paid, kind clinician) — assessed her as deeply low; tried body scans + relaxation; "couldn't find the way through" for her case.

## What helped — load-bearing

**[[jason-brain-retraining-coach|Jason's brain-retraining program]]** — found via Raelan's channel after watching another guest's recovery story. Booked free talk within a day; subscribed same night.

Timeline:
- **Week 1**: slept 8–9 hours through the night for the first time in months. Inflection point.
- **Weeks 4–5**: skiing 8 am to 4 pm for a week in Norway after a bad cold. Full functional capacity returning.
- **Subsequent months**: full recovery. Now powerlifting, climbing, mountain-biking. Continues using Jason's techniques for non-health goals (climbing problems, presentations, life goals).

> *"After one week with Jason, I slept 8–9 hours through the night. After 4–5 weeks I was skiing 8 am to 4 pm."* — Kristine, RA #226

## Notable framing claims

- **Belief is required** — and she names this as both the precondition and the mechanism. Same point Matt Butler (RA #170) makes; same point Kristyna (RA #147) makes about acupuncture-not-believing.
- **Same-coach credit pattern** — Kristine and Kristyna (RA #147) found the same unnamed Jason via the same chain on Raelan's channel. Two `key_to_recovery` credits for the same coach. Consolidated on [[jason-brain-retraining-coach]].
- **Brain-retraining works for non-health goals too** — she explicitly names using the same techniques for climbing problems and presentations. Useful framing: it's not symptom-magic; it's a transferable skill.
