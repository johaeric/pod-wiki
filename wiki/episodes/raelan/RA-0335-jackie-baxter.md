# RA #335 — Strategies that freed Jackie Baxter (LCP host) from her 3-year long covid nightmare

**Guest**: **Jackie Baxter** — the host of *The Long Covid Podcast*. **Cross-corpus appearance** on Raelan's channel. Already extensively covered on the LCP wiki; her recovery story is [[recovery-stories/jackie-baxter-recovery]] (see LCP #2, #94, #169, etc.).

## What this episode adds beyond LCP coverage

This is mostly a reaffirmation of Jackie's existing wiki content — her **Big Three** recovery components (breathing → yoga nidra → cold water in that order) are reiterated. New material:

- **Working as a breathing instructor with Vicki Jones** ([[vicki-jones]] LCP #59) — they now run a long-covid-specific breathing program (referenced as [[long-covid-breathing]]). Jackie has formal breathing certification post-recovery.
- **Therapy at 18 months in** as a key turning point — she was reluctant initially because she felt it would be "unfair" on her partner; eventually realised she needed her own support outside the relationship. Strong endorsement of seeing a therapist alongside other recovery work for long-LC arcs.
- **Mental-health-as-equal-to-physical-health** in chronic illness — she names *"an untreated mental health condition is as bad as an untreated physical health condition"* (paraphrasing a previous LCP guest). Anchor articulation of why mental-health work belongs in the corpus's recovery axes.
- **Reaffirms food-intolerance testing as no-effect** — went down the rabbit hole, eliminated dairy/wheat/eggs for 3 months, got worse mentally without symptom change. Pairs with Kyle's low-histamine `no_effect` (RA #232).
- **Post-recovery identity work** — *"I had to think of myself as an unfit healthy person, which I'd never been before."* Anchor articulation of the corpus's recurring "Jackie 2.0" / new-identity-after-recovery pattern.
- **Three years sick stamps her experience** — explicit framing that the longer the illness, the harder the post-recovery transition.
