# RA #513 — Melissa Mansfield: successful long covid recovery (LDN + radical rest)

**Guest**: Melissa Mansfield — Brooklyn NY marketing professional + passionate salsa dancer (was producing a "traveling the world through dance" web series). Got Covid March 2022 in Cali Colombia (the salsa capital). ~2-year recovery. Notable for being the **first patient `key_to_recovery`-quality credit for LDN** on the corpus.

## Long-covid course

- **March 2022 acute**: mild cold symptoms; tested negative after 5 days; expected 2-week bounce-back.
- **Months 1–6 in Colombia**: never recovered; tried to keep dancing on good days, kept crashing.
- **6 months**: returned to NYC. Mount Sinai + Maimonides long-covid clinics — *"shrugged and said there's not much we can do."* Devastating.
- **6–9 months**: radical-rest pivot. Stopped dancing. Found long-covid community. Connected with patient-recommended doctors (not establishment).

## Recovery components

1. **Low-dose naltrexone (LDN)** — patient-recommended doctor prescribed. Slow titration. **Hit a specific dose where she "turned the corner"** — pairs with Groysman's LCP #91 dosing protocol. **First patient `key_to_recovery` for LDN on the corpus.**
2. **9 months of radical rest** — paused dancing entirely; re-prioritised quieter aspects of personality (online writing classes, fostering cats, crafts).
3. **Pacing** (learned from ME/CFS community).
4. **Mindset shift / acceptance / grief work** — naming and grieving her old identity rather than fighting to maintain it.
5. **Gradual reintroduction** — Bruce Springsteen concert sitting down → Puerto Rico trip → Havana 10-day dance trip → Croatia salsa festival.

## Notable framing claims

- **Two long-covid clinics at major NYC academic centres failed her.** Both said *"not much we can do."* Recovery happened via patient-recommended doctor + LDN.
- **LDN dose-titration matters** — she titrated up gradually under medical supervision; effect emerged at a specific dose. Pairs with [[robert-groysman]]'s LCP #91 anchor protocol (1 mg start, +0.5 mg/week, 4.5 mg target, 1–3 months minimum to assess).
- **Identity grief as load-bearing recovery work** — *"I had been haunting my life for months; when I danced again I felt like my ghost had come back into my body."* Anchor articulation of the corpus's identity-loss-and-reclaim pattern.
- **Recovery itself comes with fear** — explicit on the *"I'm scared to say I'm recovered in case it's a blip"* phenomenon. Pairs with Erik Hajj RA #150, Kristyna RA #147 same observation.
- **Confidence-building incremental trips** as proof points: Springsteen → Puerto Rico → Havana → Croatia salsa festival. Useful template for staged re-entry.

## Source-bias note
