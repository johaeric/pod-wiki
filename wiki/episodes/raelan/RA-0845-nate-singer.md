# RA #845 — The recovery tools that work for YOU (Nate Singer's best advice, return guest)

**Guest**: Nate Singer — recovered patient, coach. Joining from Sayulita Mexico. Return guest on Raelan's channel.

## Key framing — anti-checklist nervous-system regulation

Anchor articulation of a corpus-relevant pattern: **patients get lost in 47-thing morning regimens trying to "regulate the nervous system"** without understanding the mechanism. Nate's framing: nervous-system regulation isn't a checklist — it's a state, and the state is what matters, not the practices in isolation. **Why people get stuck even when "doing everything right"** is the central theme.

## Notable framing claims

- **Trauma + emotions aren't always the root cause** — counter to the dominant TMS / Sarno framing on parts of the corpus. Nate's view is more nuanced: useful axis for some patients, not for all.
- **Anti-perfectionism in the regulation work itself** — the very pattern that drove pre-illness over-doing tends to recur in how patients approach their recovery practice.

Pairs with the corpus's **frame-fit framework** (Kyle, Roberto, Karen) and **doing-the-techniques-without-the-state-shift** failure pattern (Kyle's pacing, Kristine's Headspace).
