# RA #491 — Neal Cotter: 5 surprising strategies (Primal Trust + epipharyngeal abrasive therapy + Nintendo Switch)

**Guest**: Neal Cotter — Los Angeles. ~4 years into LC at recording. POTS confirmed via tilt-table. **Substantial recovery in progress** but not full. Major milestones in second half of 2023: first Halloween out, first restaurant meal in years.

**Note**: at this episode Raelan announces that her recovery interview corpus is being **formally analysed in a University of London research study** — first formal research on her interview library.

## Long-covid course

- **2020 acute** (probable Covid, never confirmed). Mild.
- **July 2020** kitchen-smoke incident triggered chest pressure return; PEM-pattern emerged.
- **Years 1–2.5**: standard route — cardiologist, pulmonologist, integrative doctor, expensive supplements, mold protocol, Lyme protocol — *"none of it did anything."*
- **Eventually**: rheumatologist confirmed it was *"in the LC/CFS family — pick your poison."* POTS confirmed via tilt-table.

## Recovery components

1. **Chris Jameson health coaching** — first inflection. Found via Raelan's channel.
2. **[[primal-trust]] (Dr Cat King)** — load-bearing brain-retraining. Cohort + community + face-to-face peer interactions during the program (named as critical — distinct from solo-program brain-retraining).
3. **Cranial-sacral therapy** weekly — adjunct, frames it as "assisted meditation for nervous-system calming."
4. **[[epipharyngeal-abrasive-therapy]]** — Japanese treatment (EAT). Swab + saline rubbed against nasopharyngeal area. Vagus-nerve-rich zone. **First instance on the corpus.** No US clinicians do it — he learned from a Japanese patient's home-practice video.
5. **Gradual exposure / micro-incrementing** — walked from 1/10 mile to 1 mile, *one house at a time over weeks*. Same approach for sitting, standing, driving (currently 30 min). Yoga reps 3 → 21 over a year.
6. **Therapy** alongside coaching.
7. **Joy-as-recovery-input** — got a Nintendo Switch, played *Persona 5* for hundreds of hours. Made setbacks bearable: *"if the worst happens, I can play tomorrow and have a good time."*
8. **Continued working through illness** (remote, from bed) — preserved identity / structure / outside-world contact. *"If I'd stopped working that would have been another mountain to climb."*

## Notable framing claims

- **"Brain retraining is a shovel, not dynamite."** Doesn't blow away symptoms in one go; gradually clears the path. **Best corpus articulation** of the brain-retraining-pace expectation.
- **"Don't only avoid the upper limit — also rise to your activity level."** Don't pace below what you can do; that gives up ground unnecessarily. Pairs with Donna RA #228 / Karen RA #354 nuances.
- **Brain retraining ≠ believing a thing once.** *"I went out for a walk monitoring my thoughts, was 'so confident' it would be OK, and still had symptoms after."* The change is at deeper-than-conscious levels.
- **Five values framework** (post-recovery clarity work): intellectual challenge, wonder, justice, connection, health. Anchor articulation on this corpus of values-as-recovery-direction-finder.
- Plans to offer **free 1:1 sponsorship-style support** to other LC patients — Alcoholics-Anonymous-sponsor model.
