# RA #448 — Katrine: long covid full recovery (Belgium)

**Guest**: Katrine — Belgium-based. Workaholic pre-illness; got Covid March 2022; severe LC by month 4 (bedbound); ~year-long recovery via brain retraining. **This episode disambiguates the unnamed "Jason" coach** referenced in earlier Raelan ingests (RA #147, #226) as **Jason McAuliffe**, with the program name **"I Can Thrive."**

## Long-covid course

- **March 2022**: caught Covid; assumed she'd be fine; tried to push through.
- **~Month 4**: hit rock bottom, bedbound. Hospital visit dismissive ("you're completely healthy — what's the real reason you don't want to go back to work?").
- **Read alone, walked a few steps, dependent on others** for groceries / cooking — *"a coma where you're awake."*

## Recovery components

1. **[[suzy-bolt-recovery|Suzy Bolt yoga online community]]** — first net that prevented further decline. *"A place where other people who feel similar try to be proactive."*
2. **[[i-can-thrive|I Can Thrive program (Jason McAuliffe)]]** — load-bearing brain-retraining program. **First confirmed identification of the Jason coach** previously mentioned by Kristyna (RA #147) and Kristine (RA #226). Online courses + 1:1 coaching sessions; individualised pattern work; visualisations.
3. **[[joe-dispenza-method|Joe Dispenza audiobooks]]** during recovery — supplementary belief-frame.
4. **Relationship shift** — broke up with her partner; they didn't match anymore. Acknowledges the emotional stress of staying was a recovery blocker. Pairs with the corpus's recurring pattern of needing to clean up life-stressors as part of recovery.

## Notable framing claims

- **2023 was her best year ever** — recovery year, full of rediscovered Joys.
- **No price for recovering "earlier"** — explicit reframe of her own initial impatience.
- **Hugging yourself** — symbolic technique she learned in I Can Thrive that stands in for the broader self-compassion / nervous-system safety practice.
