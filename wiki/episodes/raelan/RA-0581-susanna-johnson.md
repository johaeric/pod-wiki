# RA #581 — Susanna Johnson: key steps that brought her back from severe ME/CFS + LC (Belfast)

**Guest**: Susanna Johnson — Belfast, Northern Ireland. Physiotherapist (UK term for physical therapist). **8 years sick** (started age 20 in University). Substantial recovery in the last 2 years; back to part-time work, training for a triathlon at recording. Not full recovery yet.

## Long-covid course

- **2016 (~age 20)**: end of first year of university; running 50 km/week. Fatigue, dizziness, palpitations.
- **A few months later**: prescribed **LDN** (low-dose naltrexone) for hormonal balance. **3 weeks on LDN → bedridden** and never the same again. **Important: this is a harm row for LDN — LDN was the trigger of her ME/CFS, not just a flare-amplifier.**
- **8 months later**: ME/CFS diagnosis. GP told her to stop running and rest *"or you'll be in bed for years."*
- **Years 1–6**: pushed through university (physiotherapy degree), worked, kept relapsing.
- **Years 7–8 (2022–2024)**: bottomed out, 2 reinfections in 5 weeks. Moved home. Then began real recovery work.

## Recovery components

1. **Surrender / acceptance** — naturopath (John Dorne, recovered ME/CFS himself) told her *"if you don't drop everything and stop pushing through this, you're never going to get better."* Took weeks of doing nothing in bed to reset.
2. **[[optimal-health-clinic|Optimum Health Clinic]] / Alex Howard program** — ~2.5 years ago. Frame: *deep rest → tired-and-wired → reintegration* phases. Gave her permission and a map for each.
3. **Internal Family Systems (IFS) therapy** — addressed her over-achieving / never-rest pattern. Anchor moment: traced it back to a felt-sense from her own birth ("if I don't keep going I won't survive"). Pairs with [[145-jenny-adams-recovery]]'s post-recovery use of IFS on the LCP corpus.
4. **EFT-tapping during exercise** — to retrain her nervous system to perceive exercise as safe (rather than as threat). She was a runner pre-illness; her body had encoded exercise as the trigger of her decline.
5. **Pilates** as her first strength-training (instructor whose father had ME/CFS — *safe environment to do 5 minutes and stop*).
6. **Zone-2 heart-rate-monitored running** — gradual return to her favourite activity. Mitochondrial-density / heart-rate-cap rationale (she's a physio so this is on solid ground).
7. **Diet**: gluten-free + no caffeine + low sugar. Some FODMAP experimentation that didn't help much; mostly her digestion improved as she healed.
8. **Naturopath herbal hormone-balancing** — addressed her thyroid + reproductive hormones gently.

## Notable framing claims

- **As a physio, she now teaches movement + recovery as a *package deal*** — you can't have one without the other. Her own past failure to understand this is why she got sick.
- **"Discipline from a place of love, not criticism"** — anchor articulation of how high-achiever patterns can be reframed in recovery.
- **Post-recovery is its own phase** — grief for lost 20s, recovery anxiety on tired days, reconditioning after 8 inactive years. She's emphatic this is normal, not relapse. Pairs with Jamie RA #546 + Melissa RA #513 + Erik RA #150 same observation.
