# RA #527 — Ellen Alden tried everything for long covid: this is what worked

**Guest**: Ellen Alden — Manchester MA. Author. **Cross-corpus note**: Ellen was the guest of **LCP #157** — one of the two LCP episodes whose YouTube transcript was unavailable (see `_meta/failures.json`). Her story now reaches the wiki via this Raelan ingest.

## Long-covid course

- **Christmas 2021 acute**: caught Covid from family. Friends recovered fast; she didn't.
- **Months 1–6**: progressively worse — migraines, fatigue, heart issues, couldn't read or write (devastating for an author). Saw multiple specialists at multiple Boston hospitals and Mount Sinai NYC.
- **Eventually**: long covid clinic. Specialist-by-specialist approach with no unifying treatment.

## Recovery components — multi-modal stack

1. **MBSR (Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction)** — *"the program that got me through."* Was already enrolled before Covid; finished course-by-course despite brain fog. **Now MBSR-certified herself**.
2. **TMS / [[tms-mind-body-syndrome|Mind-Body Syndrome]]** — read Sarno + *The Way Out* (Alan Gordon) + *The Mindbody Solution* + *The Power of Belief*. Now training in **Pain Reprocessing Therapy (PRT)** in California.
3. **Tried-and-rejected**: Gupta program, DNRS — neither resonated.
4. **[[long-covid-breathing]]** — took Vicki Jones's course (Jackie Baxter's collaborator). Now a certified breath coach via Oxygen Advantage.
5. **Cold plunging** — DIY in her backyard.
6. **Somatic tracking** (PRT-style) and **mouth taping** (via Ryan Evans, LC YouTuber).
7. **Bruce Lipton's *Biology of Belief*** as the conceptual scaffold for mind→body causation.
8. **Joe Dispenza visualisation** — anchor late-stage practice.
9. **Lucy Faulkner cognitive specialist** — restored her ability to read.
10. **Joy-as-recovery-input** — one joy-thing per day.

## What she now offers (post-recovery work)

- 6-week LC / CFS workshops (online)
- Half-day retreats (in Manchester MA)
- 1:1 coaching
- MBSR teaching at a Boston hospital

Her four-pillar framework: **mindset + nervous-system calming + somatic practices + brain reprocessing**.

## Notable framing claims

- **"Body is led by the mind"** (Bruce-Lipton flavour) — what we believe filters down into bodily state.
- **"Give up trying to go back where you were — when I gave up on the complete-recovery outcome and created little steps, that's when things shifted."** Anchor articulation of identity-shift framing.
- **Pierre the caveman** is Raelan's own visualisation of the threat-response-self that needs calming. Ellen affirms similar imagery is widely used.
