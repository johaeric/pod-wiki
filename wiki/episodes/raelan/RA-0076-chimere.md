# RA #76 — Chimére's Story

**Guest**: Chimére — middle-school English teacher, Baltimore, Maryland. First-wave US long-hauler (got sick **March 22, 2020**). Black woman who explicitly frames her advocacy through racial-disparity lens. Recorded **end of December 2020** (~9 months in).

**Format**: Recovery-in-progress story. Severe acute illness + months of bedbound suffering, gaslighting from providers, then a pivot starting **August–October 2020** that put her on a clear recovery trajectory. Substantial-and-improving at recording — not full recovery (still has occipital neuralgia, trigeminal neuralgia, spinal misalignment as residuals).

## Recovery status

Substantial and improving. Pre-pivot: bedbound months, lost vision for 5 months, couldn't lift head off pillow, couldn't eat for ~2 months, lost memory (forgot her own name on bad days), wrote a will from the hospital. Pivot: started sitting up 5 minutes/day → gentle stretches from Raelan Agle's videos → reading Joe Dispenza → mindset shift. By recording: sitting up 90% of day, conversational, doing stretches, on a recovery curve. **Eric-relevant**: vision loss + neuralgias + spinal involvement is unusual symptom mix; PEM-style fatigue (described as "10 coats on my body") is the dominant motif.

## What helped

- **Gentle restorative movement / stretching from bed (key_to_recovery)** — Raelan's stretching videos were the inflection. Started with 5-minute timer just to sit up; grew into a stretching practice that "started the process of changing my life." See [[gentle-movement-restorative]].
- **Mindset / nervous-system frame via Joe Dispenza's *Breaking the Habit of Being Yourself* (key_to_recovery)** — read the book (did *not* take the workshop). Key insight: "nothing changes until your mind changes." Catalyst for understanding stress→illness link in her own life. See [[joe-dispenza-method]].
- **Meditation + autonomic-nervous-system education (helped_partial)** — followed the book's mindset-shift framing into her own daily practice.
- **Stepping back from advocacy / putting self first (helped_partial)** — reduced support-group time and constant symptom-talk; framed as "respect the reroute." Recognised that constant symptom-rehearsal was actively unhelpful at her recovery stage. See [[mind-body]].

## What didn't drive recovery

- **Prescription medication (mentioned_only)** — unnamed; was on it for ~6 months before the August pivot. Quote: *"it wasn't the medicine that started to change my perspective on my recovery."* She doesn't disparage the meds — they aided symptom management — but is clear they were not the lever.

## Key themes

- **Racial gaslighting in acute care** — repeatedly turned away from ERs because she didn't test positive; explicitly frames this through the lens of Black women historically dismissed in US medicine. Her unblock came from writing **Google reviews of dismissive doctors** and **letters to Baltimore city councilmen**, who then intervened with the hospital. Pairs with LCP #69 [[fiona-lowenstein]] (NYC March 2020 racial gaslighting) and LCP #60 [[reema-ahmad]].
- **Stress-and-perfectionism as predisposing pattern** — describes pre-illness life as outwardly successful (top eighth-grade test scores, "living my best life") but characterised by always-on, never-sat-down perfectionism; explicit retrospective recognition that *"my life wasn't as great as I thought it was."* See [[perfectionism]].
- **Mental-health rock-bottom** — open about wanting to end her life pre-August: *"i was like what's the point of me living anymore."* Movement + mindset-shift was the pivot.
- **Mind-body framing** — credits the realisation that *body influences brain* and *mind changes precede body changes* as the operational shift. Approaches it via Joe Dispenza's framework rather than a structured brain-retraining program.
- **Community awareness of ME/CFS↔LC overlap** — credits ME/CFS organisations for bridging information into the LC community early.
