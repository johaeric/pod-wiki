# #158 — Creativity 2024: Transformative Stories of Resilience and Connection Through Art

Annual creativity compilation (4th in the series — #14 / #65 / #113 / #158). Eight contributors share writing, poetry, painting, music, and crochet. Closes with the **Long Covid Choir** performing a German carol arrangement.

## Contributors

- **Anna Bell** — author / writer · *free-flow writing* as a therapeutic centring practice. Reads "A Wild and Mindful Life" — a post about a Vancouver-Island wildlife trip.
- **Emma Major** — artist + poet + blind wheelchair user · LC since late 2020 · publishes via her LC consultant's encouragement; poetry book *Dora Vigilia* and a new pain series *In Pain*. Reads three poems on living-with-pain, hope, and grief. Website: llmcalling.com (placeholder).
- **Olga** — musician/music teacher · founded the **[[hibernation-sessions]]** — Zoom-format concerts of musician friends, free or pay-what-you-can, accessible for housebound LC + ME patients and isolated communities. Plays the song *The Lounge Bar* on fiddle as a sample.
- **Misty Brackett** — process painter · 16+ years · "I respect the downloads" as her painting ethic; emotional-processing tool. Encourages low-cost-supplies-experiment approach.
- **Sally** — retired nurse + artist · pre-LC was hiking 2–3×/week + writing + music + Spanish lessons. Recovered enough to make art again; her anger at the gaslighting of LC patients fuelled an art piece *Warriors*. Now organising an LC-artist exhibition (May 2025 at a US art centre) with stories-as-artist-statements.
- **Sarah** — moderate-to-severe ME, **housebound ~90%** for ~10 years · started watercolours in lockdown after husband bought cheap supplies; the cheapness was the unlock ("if I want to throw it in the bin, I can"). Now sells online (hand-wrapped brown-paper parcels) + Zoom paint-with-me sessions + a YouTube reference space. *"Watercolour is a fantastic medium for me as I have moderate-to-severe ME. I rest in between washes."*
- **Jade** — LC since December 2023, stopped working June 2024 · learned crochet on a friend's recommendation (new neural pathways without high cognitive cost) · runs a YouTube channel **Living with Long Covid Jade**.
- **Long Covid Choir** (founded March 2021) · weekly Wednesday 7pm UK Zoom, no auditions, accessible for housebound/bedridden patients · led by **Marielle Vaughan**. Performs Brahms's *All Is Silent* (Wiegenlied / "Cradle Song") — Nightingales draw with sweet melodies, tears in the eye, heaviness in the heart.

## Why this episode matters

- **Anchor episode for the [[creativity]] theme** as a pacing-friendly recovery substrate. Recurring across the wiki: art, crochet, watercolours, music, choir all surface as **reliably-portable energy-economical purpose-restoration tools**.
- **The hibernation sessions** are wiki-new — first recurring online concert series specifically for the homebound LC / chronic-illness community. Worth tracking.
- **Sarah (moderate-to-severe ME, ~90% housebound)** demonstrates a workable creative practice template for very-severe-cohort patients: low-cost supplies + day-bed-between-washes pacing. Pairs with [[079-michael-towers-superhero]] for severe-cohort representation.
