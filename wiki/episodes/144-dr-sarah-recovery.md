# #144 — Dr Sarah's recovery story

**Recovery story #16 on the wiki** (full recovery, ~2.5 years). UK GP / dermatologist, mid-40s, three-year-old child during illness, husband working abroad weeks at a time. Climbed Snowdon in driving rain at sub-zero in March 2024 as her marker — "I could trust my body again."

## Triggering arc

- **Autumn 2020** — initial COVID, mild, no fatigue.
- **January 2021** — COVID vaccinations (NHS first wave); started feeling unwell after.
- **Summer 2021** — pushed through moderate symptoms, still working, struggling.
- **Autumn 2021** — second viral hit; hard crash. Severe fatigue, severe brain fog (couldn't construct sentences), sensory hypersensitivity (cold to bones, light, sound), GI bloating (looked pregnant), couldn't put 3-year-old to bed.

## Distinctive feature: **freeze response**, not fight/flight

A cardiologist privately diagnosed Sarah's autonomic state as **freeze** — heart rate dropped to **40**, blood pressure very low, dizzy on standing. Mammalian "play dead" reflex stuck on. Wiki's clearest articulation of LC-as-freeze (vs the more common LC-as-sympathetic-overdrive picture). All systems disregulated subconsciously: hormones, gut digestion, heart rate, thermoregulation.

> "It's all on a subconscious level — autonomic. You can't talk yourself out of it and pick yourself up off the savannah."

## The six pillars Sarah retrospectively grouped recovery into

1. **Calming the nervous system** — relaxing music with binaural beats, [[sensate-app]], [[suzy-bolt-recovery]] yoga (3 Mind Body Soul), [[yoga-for-life-project]], vagus-nerve TENS / ear-rubbing exercises (YouTube), Insight Timer mindfulness, breathwork, sitting in nature even when housebound.
2. **Nutrition** — avoid ultra-processed foods. Used **Yuka app** (barcode scanner → ingredient + additive scoring) to evaluate packaged food without cooking from scratch. Personal observation: had to cook all veg, no raw, must include carbs (low-carb worsened her dizziness).
3. **Sleep** — blue-light blocking glasses, **red bedside bulb** ("the neighbours wonder what's going on"), no screens 1–2 h pre-bed, calming meditations on falling asleep ("whatever you listen to before sleep sets the tone for the whole 8 hours").
4. **Hormones (perimenopause overlap)** — saw an LC + menopause hormone specialist, started **HRT very slowly** (a year of titration). Hard to attribute exactly; she was improving anyway. Logs as a meaningful contributor, not primary.
5. **Movement / exercise** — paced, intuitive, ditched the smartwatch when it pushed her into fear-of-numbers territory; walks in nature with a positive frame mindset rather than fear.
6. **Healing mindset** — gratitude lists / spoken aloud, **EFT-style tapping app** with positive affirmations, listening to recovery stories to keep hope alive, post-it notes with affirmations on the mirror, "where focus goes, energy flows."

## On cold water (notable counter-data point)

- Tried **cold water at ~40% recovered** — "really made me so tired for the rest of the day, really unwell." Tried multiple times, decided it wasn't for her body.
- Tried again at ~90% — could tolerate it briefly but body still struggles to warm up.
- Logged in the cold-water exposure evidence as **harmed** (early), **mentioned-only** later. Pattern point: cold water has worked spectacularly for some recovered guests (Jackie #2/#94) and harmed others — readiness state matters.

## Lightning Process at ~30%

- Did Lightning Process around 2022, ~30% recovered. Helped her see self-limiting beliefs and the mindset layer. **Not** her primary recovery driver but a useful early lever.

## Recovery marker: Snowdon

- March 2024, climbed Snowdon at sunset in driving rain, 50 mph gusts, sub-zero, came down in dark — "I could trust my body again."
- Used the climb as the analogy for recovery itself: not linear, knocked sideways, painful in the face, unrelenting, but reaches a top.

## Practical apps / resources Sarah cited

- **Sensate** (binaural / vagal calming).
- **Insight Timer** (free meditation app).
- **Yuka** (food/personal-care additive scoring).
- **The Tapping Solution** (EFT app with positive affirmations).
- **3 Mind Body Soul** ([[suzy-bolt-recovery]]) — paid online yoga + community.
- **Yoga for Life Project** — UK peer-support yoga.

## Eric-relevant takeaways

- **Freeze response sub-pattern** is now properly articulated on the wiki — Eric is in the **POTS sympathetic-overdrive** half, but knowing the freeze pattern exists matters when reading other people's stories.
- **Six-pillar mental scaffold** is a clean way to audit one's own recovery stack — Eric can map his stack to it (1 NS regulation: breathwork; 2 nutrition: Mediterranean-ish; 3 sleep: TBD; 4 hormones: N/A; 5 exercise: pacing + light movement; 6 mindset: gratitude practice ad hoc).
- **HRT-for-perimenopausal-LC** is now a **second** independent recovery account on the wiki naming HRT as a meaningful late-phase contributor (Jules Rogers #138 was the first). Sub-pattern firming up.
