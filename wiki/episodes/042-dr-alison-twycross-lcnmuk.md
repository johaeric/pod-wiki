# #42 — Dr Alison Twycross — LCNMUK

Advocacy / employment-rights episode. Alison Twycross is a registered nurse of ~40 years, **professor of children's nursing**, **editor-in-chief of *Evidence-Based Nursing***, and the **founder of Long Covid Nurses & Midwives UK** (LCNMUK; launched 28 January 2022). She caught COVID at her workplace in March 2020 while on immunosuppressants and has been ill ever since — primary persisting symptoms are cognitive dysfunction and fatigue.

## Recovery status

**Not recovered.** Improving slowly over 25 months. Reports she is "nowhere near the woman who could commute into London or work for 10–12 hours a day." Symptom-management strategy rather than intervention-driven recovery arc.

## What helped (symptom management)

- **Antidepressants (Oct 2020).** Started after realising she was spending most of the day trying not to cry. Reports: improved sleep at night → reduced daytime worry about napping. Framed as `helped_partial` for affect/sleep regulation; she did not credit them for recovery.
- **Daily structured nap (1.5–3 hours after lunch).** Took her 18 months to figure out. Now structures meetings around it — mid-morning or after 4 PM only. The single biggest practical adaptation she names.
- **Tightly negotiated work pattern.** Two-hour blocks with rest in between; refusing meetings outside the productive windows.

## Suspected silent hypoxia

Worth flagging: nausea + headaches were "always worse in the middle of the night" for ~ a year. She kept a bucket under the bed for a year. Retrospectively suspects **silent hypoxia** that nobody knew to look for in 2020.

> "I suspect I had hypoxia and didn't know it because we didn't know then that there was just silent hypoxia." — Twycross, ep #42

## LCNMUK — what they did in 3 months

Quick win-list because the breadth is the story (all between 28 Jan and end of April 2022):

- **NHS England guidance changed.** Sentence on dismissal-after-12-months-sick of LC staff was *removed* after social-media outcry that LCNMUK helped catalyse.
- **Welsh half-pay reprieve.** NHS Wales staff were due to drop to half-pay on 1 April 2022 if off sick > 1 year with LC; reversed to full-pay-until-end-June. Plaid Cymru members in the Senedd led the political work; LCNMUK contributed the social-media pressure.
- **RCN web hub.** Royal College of Nursing launched a long-covid resource hub for members after Twycross flagged the lack of consolidated info to the English director.
- **UK Parliament briefing.** LCNMUK wrote a same-day briefing for the COVID-19 & Employment debate. Twycross drafted it across one day and crashed for the rest of the week.
- **Survey** with *Evidence-Based Nursing* on dismissals/half-pay/no-pay across NHS England.
- **Met the Chief Nursing Officers** of England, Wales, and Scotland.
- Launched social-media presence (Twitter, Instagram, Facebook), website, friends-of-LCNMUK group, two WhatsApp support groups.

Membership drawn from England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland — most members caught COVID March/April 2020. Spreads "tentacles" across LC Physio, LC SOS, Long Covid Support Employment Group.

## Twycross's structural critique

> "There is evidence beyond reasonable doubt that many employers didn't adhere to what they should have done under the health and safety at work legislation." — Twycross, ep #42

- Nurses get LC at higher rates than medics — she attributes this to **prolonged contact + viral load** (medics in/out, nurses bedside).
- Estimate: ~10,000 registered nurses in NHS England with LC. No data by profession — counting is poor.
- Two employer archetypes: "balance-the-books, staff-as-bother" vs "people-as-asset, retain-the-talent." Most are the former.
- New emerging tactic — **inappropriate ill-health retirement**. NHS HR using it as a pseudo-dismissal route. Reasonable adjustments often not put in place first; people pushed toward IHR before extended phased-return is tried. Her *next* campaign.
- Cultural inflexibility in nursing/midwifery: "expectation that if you're in a clinical role you'll be working at 200%" — phased return at 50% disrupts the culture.
