# #96 — Dr Masha Makeeva: HBOT & other recovery strategies

US-trained naturopathic + integrative-medicine doctor; based on Mediterranean coast in Spain. Combines clinician + patient perspective: had **multiple autoimmune conditions** (psoriatic arthritis, endometriosis, PCOS) diagnosed at 26 → went back to medical school as a naturopath → had **3 covid infections** in 2020-22.

Third infection (Omicron, August 2022) → significant LC. Recovered to ~80% by recording (June 2023), with one notable relapse from stress.

## Symptom profile during her LC

- Sense-of-smell loss (the trigger for realising she had Co)
- 4kg weight loss
- Significant **hair loss** (washing → big chunks)
- **Brain fog with multilingual word-finding failure** (speaks 3 languages; couldn't find words in any).
- Severe **mental fatigue**; couldn't make YouTube videos.
- **Joint pain** — possible psoriatic arthritis flare or LC-induced arthralgia. Not sure of attribution.

## Doctors-can-be-the-worst-patients moment

She **didn't recognise her own symptoms as LC** for several months despite seeing LC patients daily. Even MRI'd both knees looking for Baker's cyst. The *not wanting it to be LC* psychology applies even to integrative MDs — useful to log.

## The recovery stack — what worked, in order

### Step 1 — Diet (her always-first lever)

- **Eliminate inflammatory foods**: gluten, lectins (per Steven Gundry's *Plant Paradox*), seed/vegetable oils.
- **Lectin-free** during the initial healing phase.
- **No restaurant food for 3 months** — cook everything, control oil source.
- Anti-inflammatory diet pillars carry across her LC + autoimmune practice.

### Step 2 — Hyperbaric oxygen therapy (anchor intervention for her)

The episode's headline intervention. Her framing:

- **Forced oxygen dissolution** in tissue — bypasses microclot impairment.
- **Mitochondrial repair** mechanism: inflammation reduction + repair of damaged mitochondria + biogenesis of new ones.
- She names [[gez-medinger]] and "Dr Tom" as practitioner-patients who used HBOT and benefited.
- **First-session benefit common** but inflammation reduction needs **20–60 sessions** depending on prior baggage.
- **MS Charity in the UK** — provides HBOT for ~£15/session. Cheapest known LC-relevant access.
- **Worsening fatigue after first 3-4 sessions can be a positive sign** — mitochondrial turnover (clearing damaged → building new). Don't schedule activities post-session early on.

### Step 3 — Sleep + pacing

- **Same bedtime / wake-time** every day, including weekends.
- 7-8 hours minimum.
- **Brain Tap** device (light + sound therapy via earphones + visor; classical music + meditations) — she names it as significantly improving sleep quality.
- **Naps OK** — pacing matters more than nap-anxiety.

### Step 4 — Other therapies (lower individual weight, mentioned)

- **Ozone therapy** — for viral persistence + lung scar tissue.
- **Red light therapy** — helped her joint pain.
- **Hormonal evaluation** — common in 40s women who present "is this LC or is this perimenopause?" (cross-ref [[093-dr-viki-male-menstruation]] [[hormonal-dysfunction]]).

## The "spoons" / "jar of chi" pacing framework

Two pacing metaphors she uses operationally:
- **Spoons** (Christine Miserandino) — wake up, estimate energy 0-10, plan one task per low-energy day.
- **Jar of chi** — born with a jar of finite life-energy; pre-LC busy lifestyle was *taking* from the jar; now needs to *put back*. Sleep / quality food / laughter / good relationships add to the jar.

## Recovery-relapse warning (a useful signal)

> "I was doing amazing in May. Then I had a lot of stress. Then I started getting my symptoms back."

Her clinical observation across patients: **some get to 100% then relapse** when they revert to old habits. Open question whether LC is a chronic vulnerability requiring lifelong lifestyle care or a one-and-done illness. Pairs with [[077-suzy-bolt-reinfection]]'s framing of LC as creating a longer-lasting vulnerability requiring active maintenance.

## Diet's role in LC

She is emphatic: **what you eat affects energy, sleep, pain, inflammation directly**. Carbs spike blood sugar → fatigue cycle. Restaurant seed oils are inflammatory. The cost of cooking is real but pays back in symptom control.

## On caregiver dynamics

Notes that her 14-year-old daughter had to take on responsibility while she was bedbound — and **grew through it**. The dependency reversal, while painful, has a developmental upside for family members. Pairs with [[011-iain-partner-with-chronic-illness]] and [[081-liza-dileo-thomas-patient-experience]] on the carer-side picture.

## Eric-relevant intake

- **HBOT mechanism explanation** — "first-session benefit common; worsening fatigue 3-4 sessions in is a positive sign" framing is operationally useful if Eric ever evaluates HBOT. The mitochondrial-turnover analogy ("tidying makes mess before tidiness") is clinically-honest about what to expect.
- **MS Charity UK at £15/session** is the cheapest pathway flagged in this wiki for HBOT access. Worth knowing if ever going there.
- **Brain Tap** is a new device-entry in the wiki — sound + light therapy for sleep (cf [[hrv-biofeedback]] and [[safe-and-sound-protocol]] adjacent design space).
