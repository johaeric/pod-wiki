Roddy Schrock — board-certified classical homeopath in NYC (North American Homeopathy Examiners). Background: arts management/fundraising in NYC. Came to homeopathy after 8+ years of unexplained migrating pain and severe insomnia. Trained in Philadelphia after recovering, now in private practice.

## What homeopathy is (and isn't)

- 250-year-old modality, founded by German physician Samuel Hahnemann (early 19th century)
- *Not* herbalism, *not* general "alternative medicine" — its own thing
- Frame: an **energetic intervention** — a substance from nature with an energetic relationship to a patient's "symptom picture" is highly diluted and given to nudge the body toward healing
- Goal of homeopathy: gentle, swift, effective
- Remedies are diluted to the point that little chemical material remains — claim is the *energy* of the remedy is what acts (controversial; outside conventional pharmacology)
- Same remedy can work in agriculture, veterinary use, etc. (separate research traditions)

## Where the value may genuinely sit

- **Deep listening** — case-taking takes 2+ hours. The patient describes everything: physical, mental, emotional, life history, why they're on the planet. *Being heard at that depth is itself nervous-system medicine.*
- **Shared suffering** (literal etymology of "homeopathy") — sitting with the patient in their experience
- Long appointments allow connection that a 10-minute GP visit can't match
- Roddy: "What I've seen happen is that case-taking can kick-start the healing process. Just having the space to describe everything."

## On long COVID + autoimmune

- Approach individualises — each person has a unique symptom picture
- Works alongside conventional medicine (his patient with PCOS + Buerger's syndrome + autoimmune is also under MD care)
- Looks at history *before* the LC, not just current symptoms
- Wants conventional and complementary worlds to integrate, not compete

## Common mistakes

- Working with un-trained people calling themselves homeopaths
- Avoiding **Facebook protocol-style "homeopathy"** that prescribes the same 3 remedies for everyone — that's not homeopathy
- Expecting one remedy to cure everything overnight

## Where to read

- Homeopathic Research Institute (UK) — peer-reviewed homeopathy research
- UConn Health (US) hosted a homeopathy conference Nov 2025 — academic engagement is increasing

## Notes for Eric

**Evidence grade**: anecdote / expert opinion. Mainstream RCT evidence for homeopathy as a pharmacological agent is poor; the *deep-listening case-taking* component plausibly does much of the work. Whether one wants to engage is largely about whether the modality fits one's worldview. Worth flagging that the **listening / being heard** dimension that Roddy emphasises is precisely the same mechanism Kelly George (#192) credits her acupuncturist with — and is the dimension that conventional 10-minute medicine cannot deliver. The modality may be a delivery vehicle for that listening more than a pharmacological one.
