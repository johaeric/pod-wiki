# #037 — Dr Asad Khan — Apheresis & Triple Therapy

**Guest**: **Dr Asad Khan** — Consultant Respiratory Physician (sleep medicine + long-term ventilation subspecialty), Manchester. Caught covid at work November 2020 from inadequate PPE on a covid ward (surgical mask + apron only). Now one of the most prominent UK long-hauler doctor-advocates, working alongside [[jess-taylor]] ("Jess Taylor calls me Captain POTS").

The wiki's **first deep account of HELP-apheresis + triple-therapy treatment in Germany** — the clinical-side companion to Pretorius/Kell's mechanistic episode (#33).

## Illness arc

- **Nov 2020** — acute covid (mild). Test positive. Two weeks bedbound. Family all infected.
- **29 Dec 2020** — abrupt long-covid onset: palpitations, urticaria all over face/scalp, **angioedema** (eyelids closed shut), urinary frequency, full-body burning. Self-diagnoses **MCAS + IST + likely POTS** via a doctors-with-LC Facebook group.
- **Jan–Feb 2021** — six weeks "near-suicidal" from the burning/itching before getting **omalizumab (Xolair)** in an urticaria clinic — rash, tachycardia, urinary symptoms melt away within hours of first injection.
- **Feb 2021** — **vaccine triggers PEM/POTS crash** (he names this carefully: "I am pro-vaccination; that doesn't mean we should pretend reactions don't happen"). From this point: bed-bound, light/sound-triggered nausea + neuropathic pain.
- **Sep 2021** — **HELP-apheresis** with **Dr Beate Jaeger** in Mülheim. First session triggered immediately because he was fainting in the waiting room. Cognitive clearing + chest-pain resolution that hour ("realised I'd been having angina all this time"). 7–8 cycles.
- **Nov 2021** — switched to **triple therapy**: aspirin + clopidogrel + dabigatran. Major step-up; was able to fly to Cyprus alone for 2 weeks in Dec 2021 ("my best baseline").
- **Dec 2021 – ongoing** — likely **3 reinfections**, all PCR/LFT-negative but confirmed by sudden new microclots on blood imaging + rising anti-N antibodies (was undetectable on N in Dec 2021; thousands by recording).

> "Spike antibodies were below detectable in December. I just had them done — they're in the thousands now. And the nucleocapsid antibodies, which only come from the virus, are above detectable. So I clearly had infection. There are lots of false negatives out there." — Khan

## Treatments tried — outcome breakdown

| Treatment | Outcome | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Omalizumab (Xolair)** | helped_partial → key contribution to MCAS control | Game-changer for urticaria/angioedema |
| **Midodrine** | helped_partial | POTS BP support |
| **Mestinon (pyridostigmine)** | helped_partial | POTS / cholinergic support |
| **HELP-apheresis** | helped_partial | First-day clearing of chest pain + fog; needed multiple cycles |
| **Triple therapy (asp+clop+dabigatran)** | helped_partial | "Game changer"; sustained gains until reinfection |
| **Plasmapheresis** | helped_partial | Targeted GPCR autoantibodies (theoretical mechanism for POTS) |
| **Vagus-nerve TENS** | helped_partial | 3×/day. "Did help avert some crashes and come out of them sooner" |
| **Vagus-nerve exercises (Sukie Baker)** | helped_partial | When breath-work felt inaccessible |
| **Low-carb / time-restricted eating + microbiome work** | helped_partial | Resolved most food intolerances |
| **Paxlovid** | no_effect | Used in third infection; didn't respond. Notes others have differently. |
| **Vaccine** (during illness) | harmed | Triggered the major PEM/POTS phase |

He explicitly does **not** claim recovery. His current line: "I am pretty much back confined to one room [post-third reinfection]. But hopeful."

## Themes Khan develops

- **Doctor-as-patient invisibility** — being told "you are here as a patient" by a colleague to dismiss medical knowledge. The biggest lesson: the moment you cross from clinician to patient you lose credibility, and silos prevent anyone joining the dots.
- **Pre-emptive air-quality framing** — argues we missed the airborne route at the start; HEPA filters + airborne PPE in healthcare would eliminate hospital-acquired transmission.
- **"You can rush good research"** — references David Putrino. The recovery trial set up + reported within weeks for acute covid; the same urgency hasn't appeared for LC because it's a chronic illness affecting the "wrong" demographics.
- **Reinfection as a degrading process** — pattern of falling short of his previous baseline after each reinfection.
- **MCAS-overlap upstream** — his dietary intolerances + pre-existing connective-tissue issues fit the picture Tina Peers describes in #38 (98% of LC patients have prior undiagnosed MCAS, in her experience).

## Reading Eric's likely question

- The triple-therapy + apheresis path is real and reproducible. **It's not self-prescribable** (bleeding risk; needs proven microclots; needs experienced physician). For Eric, the relevant takeaway is that for a confirmed-microclots subset of LC, this combination produces a real signal.
- The **vagus-nerve TENS + Sukie Baker exercises** are accessible self-tools when breath-work is too cognitively expensive in a crash — pair with #40 Boon Lim's diaphragmatic-breathing teaching for the upstream version.
- The MCAS / low-carb / microbiome stack overlaps Tina Peers's protocol (#38) and Sally Riggs's keto observation (#23). Cross-check ercipedia before any new dietary change.
