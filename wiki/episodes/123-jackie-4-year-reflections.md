# Episode 123 — Jackie's 4-Year Reflections (Host-Solo, LC Awareness Day 2024)

Recorded 4 years after Jackie first got sick (Sun 15 March 2020 — ironically also Long Covid Awareness Day). Short solo episode, mostly emotional reckoning + advice to anyone earlier in the journey.

**Key claims (drawn from her own recovery):**

- Recovery is possible — "1% better is better than no percent." The 1% may unlock the next thing that helps.
- Recovery requires *the right environment*. People who must work for income can't recover the same way as those who can stop. Lack of support — meals, finances, child care, time to investigate what helps — actively prevents recovery.
- Stays away from politics/anger/blame: understandable, but bad for her own nervous system. Wants the conversation to be about *what we can do*.
- Her current life is genuinely better than her pre-Covid life — different work, more aligned, runs her own business, more capacity for stress, more boundaries (work in progress). Treats long Covid as "a hard and grumpy teacher."
- Acknowledges she still carries trauma; processes it more healthily now.

**Her four-point advice to her past self:**
1. Focus on *what you can do* and *what to ask for*.
2. Celebrate the little wins.
3. Keep an open mind — what helps may not be what you expected, and recovery may not look like what you expected.
4. Hold onto belief that recovery is possible.

> "Recovery would be mine. The hardest challenge of my life. And I think that's something to be hugely proud of."
> — Jackie Baxter, ep #123
