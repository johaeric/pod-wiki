# #007 — Suzy Bolt

**Guest**: [[suzy-bolt]] — yoga teacher and coach (25+ years), trained NLP master practitioner, life/career coach. First-wave long-hauler (March 2020); now recovered. Founder of **Suzy Bolt's recovery program** ([[suzy-bolt-recovery]]) — one of the most-cited LC recovery programs on this podcast.

## Recovery status

**Recovered.** Marks **June 14, 2020** (the date of her first recovery yoga video, in which she said "you must accept that you are ill, and ill in a way that you cannot recover from like anything else") as her turning point.

## Symptom picture (acute → long phase)

- Atypical acute (March 2020): "alien invading", icy travelling shivers, fever-without-symptoms, food and drink felt like poisoning.
- Pretended to function for 6 weeks, then "fell off a cliff" at week 7–8: bedbound, body imploding.
- Month 3: GP sent ambulance, A&E admission. All stats normal — **diagnostic clearance was itself a turning point**: she could now stop fearing organ failure and focus on recovery.
- Persistent: right-lung burning pain; palpitations; insomnia; food sensitivities. Now treats these as residual scars, not active disease.

## What helped — her own model

She built her **own recovery program** by combining things that helped her, and runs live classes daily with **Ross Cooper** (her PT, also a long-hauler). The program is the answer she gives.

| Component | Role in her recovery |
|---|---|
| **Acceptance / belief in impermanence** | Foundation. Buddhist *aniccā* framing — every state passes; the worst is impermanent, the recovery path is real. |
| **Counselling** | Started August 2020; still seeing him at recording. Strongly advocates: "talk to someone you do not know." |
| **Daily gentle yoga** | Accessible from bed; restores autonomic regulation; creates structure and self-witnessing. |
| **Breathwork** | Slows the system into rest-repair mode. Recommends [[stasis-breathing]] (Josh Duntz, Mt Sinai collaboration; free). |
| **Active deep rest** | Yoga Nidra and guided meditation 3× a day; explicitly NOT phone-in-bed or TV. |
| **Group support** | Live daily classes and Q&A; "no one recovers when they're lonely." |
| **Curated information environment** | She moderates her Facebook group to skew positive/forward-looking; explicitly *not* a symptom-trauma chamber. |

## Strong claims

- **The mind is in control of the body to a greater or lesser extent — get your mind in order first.**
- **Good days are the most dangerous days** — the urge to make up for lost time triggers PEM crashes.
- **Whoever runs LC clinics: assume Baseline Zero**; don't run a sit-stand test on a referral patient. They're already in.
- **Achievement-driven self-worth** is a recovery obstacle; therapy needed to look at it.
- **The Gupta Program / Lightning Process** work for some, not all — be respectful of plurality but never give up looking.

## Top-three advice (asked by Dr Boon Lim)

1. **Educate yourself** — watch interviews with [[boon-lim]], [[melanie-dani]] etc.; learn dysautonomia, MCAS, histamine diet, gut.
2. **Practice deep active rest** — Yoga Nidra, [[stasis-breathing]], 3× daily.
3. **Get support** — group + buddy + counsellor; layered, not single-source.
