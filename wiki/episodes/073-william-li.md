# #73 — Dr William Li

Internal medicine + vascular biologist; led the New England Journal of Medicine article showing direct endothelial invasion in covid autopsy tissue (March 2020). Director of the **Angiogenesis Foundation**. Helped develop 40+ FDA-approved cancer therapies pre-pandemic.

## The "three legs of the stool" model

Li proposes that long covid is driven by three convergent pathologies. **All three have existing treatments — partial reason for optimism.**

| Leg | What it is | Treatments worth exploring |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Microvascular damage** | Endothelial invasion → capillary loss in lungs, brain, heart, testicles | Nitric oxide donors (dietary + sildenafil), regenerative therapies |
| **2. Autoimmunity** | LC infection triggers smouldering autoimmune response in some; type 1 + type 2 diabetes ↑ in children/adults post-covid | Steroids (prednisone, dexamethasone), baricitinib (already approved acute) |
| **3. Chronic inflammation** | Smouldering low-grade fire; **43% increase in fatal heart attacks in <44s post-covid including Omicron** | Anti-inflammatory diet, NSAIDs, infusible biologics |

> "It's like a cigarette butt thrown into dry woods — the smoker is gone, but a smouldering fire continues."

## VEGF as a *health* biomarker

Vascular Endothelial Growth Factor — a "fertiliser" your damaged tissues release while trying to grow new capillaries. **Not a disease marker; a repair signal.** Hidden in plain sight on standard CT scans (which don't deconstruct microvasculature). Li's team used FDA-introduced tech to deconstruct LC patients' lung scans → small vessels still missing months after recovery.

> "It's like seeing bags of fertiliser on a damaged garden's driveway. A signal that the body is trying to repair itself."

## Microvascular damage — what they saw at the autopsy level

> "We literally saw the coronavirus parachuting into endothelial cells. Like burglars going straight to the electrical system pulling wires."

Frighteningly: in severe covid brains, the **endothelial lining of cerebral blood vessels is ripped out**, especially in executive-function regions. Mechanism for cognitive decline + brain fog.

Brain capillary damage → faulty wiring of olfactory neurons → loss of smell.

## Treatments suggested (clearly framed as researcher-recommended, not own-recovery)

### For microvascular damage — nitric oxide

- **Dietary nitrates**: spinach + beets — but **chew slowly** so tongue microbiome converts nitrate → nitrite → systemic NO. Generates NO that dilates vessels + mobilises stem cells from bone marrow.
- **Low-dose sildenafil (Viagra)**: NO-donor; already approved for babies in ICU with lung-vessel damage. Research direction is forming around LC lung repair.

### For autoimmunity

- **Steroids** (prednisone, dexamethasone) — dexamethasone already approved for acute covid
- **Baricitinib** — already approved for acute covid; rheumatoid-arthritis biologic; possibly helpful for autoimmune leg

### For chronic inflammation

- Anti-inflammatory diet (his own field)
- Standard anti-inflammatories where appropriate

### Prevention of long covid (acute treatment)

> "**Metformin during acute covid reduced long-covid risk by >30%** in a clinical trial" — Bramante study. Safe, cheap, off-patent. Vaccination + boosting also shown to reduce LC risk. Implication: should be part of any newly-diagnosed-covid playbook.

## Notable claims
