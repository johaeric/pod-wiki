# #002 — Jackie's Personal Story

**Guest**: [[jackie-baxter]] interviewing herself, with her partner **Mark** as co-narrator. The host's own story, ~16 months into illness (recorded ≈July 2021).

## Recovery status

Not recovered. Worst three weeks of the year happen during this episode's recording. Pattern: ups/downs but **no net improvement** since plateau ~3 months in.

## Symptom picture

- Atypical acute covid (no fever, no cough, no taste/smell loss) — just breathlessness, deep fatigue, GI symptoms.
- **No positive PCR** (got ill before testing was widely available) — repeatedly excluded from research studies as a result.
- Dominant symptoms: **fatigue, breathlessness/tight chest, panic attacks, numb extremities (Raynaud-like even at 25 °C), coordination problems, depression**.
- Classic **PEM** clearly described (without using the term): "if I do too much that week I'll feel awful the week after." Pushing always backfires.

## What helped, what didn't

### Early gentle interval training (PT-prescribed) — initially helped, then backfired

A physiotherapist she'd seen before her illness suggested **gentle interval training**: 30-second heart-rate elevations followed by full recovery. From weeks 5–13 this seemed to work — distances and speeds improved on tracked rides/runs.

> "Quite quickly that did start to work."
> — Jackie, ep #2

By month 3 she was hill-walking ≈10 km with 700 m of ascent. A week later she crashed. Three and a half months in, the breathlessness and tight chest came back fully. **In retrospect this looks like classic PEM-driven graded-exercise harm**, though she didn't have that vocabulary at the time. The doctor she spoke to attributed it to deconditioning and told her to keep going, which deepened the crash.

### Yoga + breathwork (with [[wibbs-coulson]]) — partial improvement at the time of recording

Joined a long-covid-specific online breathing/yoga class run by **Wibbs Coulson** (background in nursing; argues long covid involves **dysfunctional breathing**, retraining helps). Six weeks in:

> "My breathing has actually improved quite a lot... whenever I start panicking I sort of know how to deal with it now... doesn't seem to yet have made any difference to the fatigue."
> — Jackie, ep #2

Specifically helped: panic attacks, breathing pattern, anxiety around being in public spaces. Specifically didn't help: fatigue. Classification: helped_partial.

### What didn't help

- Bloods (× multiple) — all came back clear. Useful only to rule out other things.
- Doctor advice: "you're 30, fit, this should be nothing" — she was sent away repeatedly.

## Themes (cross-cutting)

- **Pacing**: she has had to learn it the hard way; the episode is a case study in *how* a fit healthy person learns PEM exists.
- **Pre-test exclusion**: a recurring podcast theme — March 2020 first-wavers locked out of much LC research.
- **Caregiver burden**: Mark covers the cooking, cleaning, shopping, driving so Jackie can keep working part-time from home.
- **Return-to-work** as a music teacher — a phased / WFH arrangement saved her job.
