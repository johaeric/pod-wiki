The 200th-episode milestone. Jackie reflects on starting the podcast in August 2021 during her own recovery and shares an essay on **intentions** as a tool for healing.

## Intentions vs goals

- **Goals** — outcome-based, rigid (e.g. walk 20 minutes/day; back to work by summer)
- **Intentions** — direction-based, fluid (e.g. "feel more connected to my body"; "ease"; "balance")
- For health journeys with high uncertainty, intentions are kinder and more sustainable than goals

## Five reasons intentions are useful (science-backed, not woo)

1. **Cognitive priming** — they activate neural pathways that make you more likely to notice opportunities aligned with the intention
2. **Reticular activating system** — intentions tell the brain's filter what to prioritise
3. **Emotional regulation** — narrowing focus reduces overwhelm
4. **Behavioural science** — small identity-based shifts ("I am someone who treats myself gently") are more sustainable than outcome-based goals
5. **Nervous system** — intentions influence posture, breath, tone of voice → shift toward safety/connection

## How to set them

- **Daily** — one word/phrase (ease, presence, gentleness)
- **Weekly** — what would support me this week?
- **Monthly** — themes (rest, connection, creativity, boundaries)
- **Yearly** — guiding word or phrase. Jackie's was "balance" in 2025

## Tips for setting intentions

- Start microscopic; let them be fluid; use sensory anchors; avoid perfectionism; check in gently with the body; celebrate the smallest shifts.

## Notes for Eric

A simple, low-effort tool worth holding. The yearly word/phrase is a 30-second exercise that costs nothing. The Jackie pulled-tendon-then-Christmas-cold framing in this episode is a good example of how recovery-skills (rest, surrender) carry over to non-LC setbacks.
