# #70 — Lorrie Rivers — Reclaiming life from chronic illness

A coach with 20+ years of working with ME/CFS patients, herself recovered from severe ME/CFS (~8 years severe + 2 years recovery, late 1990s/2000s), then **fully recovered from Long Covid in ~8 months** in 2020, then **fully recovered from lifelong "weather-dependent fibromyalgia"** by treating what she now believes is the **root cause of all her chronic illness: hidden parasitic, fungal, and microbial infections in the gut**.

## The claim

Lorrie's central thesis — strong, controversial, **logged neutrally** here:

> All chronic illness — ME/CFS, Long Covid, fibromyalgia, MCAS, even MS and cancer — has the same upstream root cause: **dysbiosis driven by hidden infections** (parasites, candida, worms, "bad microbes") that have set up shop because of childhood antibiotic overuse + inherited microbiome. The viral hit (Covid, EBV, etc.) only "tips the house over because the termites have already eaten it."

She frames this as the *only* approach that has produced *full recovery* in her clients (including patients with decades of severe ME/CFS) — not just management. She names two clinicians whose work she draws from: **Dr Simon Yu** and **Dr Alex Kingart**, plus advocate **Kelly Rivera**.

> ⚠️ **Wiki status**: This is a strong-claim minority position. There is no peer-reviewed support for "parasites cause ME/CFS." The patient-passes-worms-after-treatment claim is anecdotal and confounded (de-worming agents can drive *normal* gut flora to mass-egest, and healthy stools sometimes show egesta after dietary change). Logged because Lorrie is an experienced coach with a recovered cohort, and the underlying components of the protocol (keto/carnivore eating + binders + anti-fungals + slow-introduction) overlap with what other guests recommend in narrower forms. **No clinical recommendation made; cross-check ercipedia.**

## The protocol (what she actually does)

1. **Pre-treatment phase** (often 2–3 months):
   - Strict eating plan: **meat + healthy fats + low-carb veg**. No alcohol, no chocolate, no sugar. Carnivore variant if veg-intolerance present. Justification: fungi/parasites feed on carbohydrates (and excess protein → sugar above ~4 oz / serving for women, 6 oz for men).
   - Open detox pathways (binders for herxheimer reactions).
   - Some clients pass worms during pre-treatment without taking any drugs.
2. **Treatment phase** (~6–12 months):
   - Anti-parasitic medications (drug-based, with a doctor) — protocols her clients individualise.
   - Binders for die-off (herxheimer / Jarisch-Herxheimer reactions).
   - "Gently aggressive" — paced for symptom tolerance. She tried home **ozone therapy** in her own arc and had **harsh herxheimer reactions even at low dose** (logged as `harmed` outcome bucket — too much, too fast).
3. **Heavy-metal phase** later in protocol.
4. **Lyme + lingering virus** phase if needed.

## Recovery phases (her terminology)

She uses a phase-1 to phase-5 self-rating that maps roughly onto:
- Phase 1: bedbound / housebound
- Phase 2 - 4: increasing activity tolerance, still PEM-prone
- Phase 5: fully recovered

## What she's seen in her cohort (anecdotal, her own report)

- Clients with 30+ years severe ME/CFS recovering within ~12 months of full protocol completion.
- Sleep problems that lasted decades resolving within 2 weeks of the eating plan alone (one student's mother).
- A severe patient who hadn't walked to his fridge in 2+ years walking to the fridge unaided after the pre-treatment phase.
- A chronic light-sensitivity / fabric-sensitivity patient finally able to wear her favourite sweatpants.

## Notable framings

- *"Even 1% improvement is going to make a difference."*
- **Allow yourself to feel your feelings** — citing the toddler tantrum / butterfly-comes-by analogy. Push-down feelings stay; felt feelings move and shift.
- Recovery hope as instrumentally important — *"if you stop believing you can get better, you stop trying, and that's a real thing."*
- **Antibiotics as the original sin** — generation of children given antibiotics like candy created the dysbiotic substrate that is now flaring as chronic-illness epidemics (her opinion).

## Eric-relevance — caution

- This protocol is **not in the consensus**, and the strongest-claim parts (worms as causal, candida as systemic) are well outside ercipedia evidence territory. Eric should treat this as a hypothesis-of-record, not a recommendation.
- The **softer components** of the protocol have wiki-corroborating signals: low-carb eating ([[keto-diet]]), gut-axis attention ([[gut-health]]), slow titration in detox approaches, herxheimer-monitoring. These are not unique to her protocol.
- Strongest counter-signal: the trial-grade evidence space (LDN, AXA1125, IVIG, brain-retraining as discussed by recovered patients) is largely orthogonal to Lorrie's frame.
