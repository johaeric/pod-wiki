# #171 — Aaron West: Light-Switch Recovery via Reinfection

US (South Carolina) IT manager, ex-cyclist (rode 237 miles across the state in one day pre-illness), film enthusiast, podcaster. Caught covid Fall 2021 (Delta wave). 2.5 years of long covid. **Recovered overnight following Omicron reinfection** on January 5, 2024 — confirmed by his doctor as a **"light-switch recovery"**.

## Long-covid course (Fall 2021 → Jan 5 2024)

- Post-acute cough lasted ~1 year (~500-1,000 coughs/day at peak); mute-on-Zoom adaptations.
- Diagnosed by his epidemiologist father ~spring 2022, then by GP. Pharmacological stack:
  - **Antihistamines** (Zyrtec/cetirizine, Pepcid/famotidine, sometimes Claritin)
  - **Beta blockers** (helped most with PEM)
  - **Benzodiazepines** (saved him at the worst; tapered off post-recovery)
- Symptom load: severe insomnia (despite 14 h sleep some nights, scattered across 24 h); "electromagnetic pulse coursing through veins" — adrenaline / wired-and-tired pattern; spike anxiety; cognitive fog; **GI dysfunction** (vomiting daily for periods); **smell + taste alteration** (everything tasted *amazing*, including normally-disliked foods → poor eating habits); palpitations on waking from dreams (HR jump 30-40 bpm).
- Severity self-rating: bedbound much of the time; couldn't sit at a desktop computer for >1 hour; built a business + website on iPad/iPhone from bed for ~year.
- Pacing strategy = HR-based ceiling (under 100 bpm walking), spoon theory framing, 2-3 day rest after any meaningful activity.

## The light-switch event (Jan 5 2024)

- Wife caught Omicron at a meeting; same household → Aaron caught it Jan 5.
- Took **Paxlovid**.
- Day after positive test (Saturday): worked non-stop for hours, productive in a way he hadn't been in years.
- Next day (Sunday): an incident in his neighbourhood (a person threatening neighbours with a baseball bat) — Aaron *ran two blocks* to identify the suspect, masked, while still acutely positive. **Did not look at his HR.** Realised mid-run: *the long covid is gone.*
- Fog cleared *mid-acute infection* before the acute symptoms had even resolved.
- Sleep dropped from 10-14 h → 2 h initially as energy returned; took ~6 months to normalise to ~6 h.
- His doctor (who had known him 20 years) told him *post-recovery* he had thought Aaron was too far gone to recover; saw the difference at first sight.

## The cognitive-rebound observation

- Post-recovery cognition arguably *better* than pre-illness in creative tasks. His doctor speculated that the new variant + recovery sequence had stimulated brain activity in a way that pulled forward creative capacity.
- Now writing a book on a film label (A24); ran a Kickstarter; binge-watching 6 movies in a day for research — high cognitive load, no PEM.

## Caveats Aaron volunteers

- *"Don't go out to get covid."* Several friends in his LC support group hoped for the same outcome — most did not get it.
- He recognises his recovery is rare and tells the story carefully.

## Why this episode matters

- **20th full-recovery story on the wiki**, and the *first* clear "reinfection-as-healing" anchor. Pairs with the existing wiki theme [[reinfection]] (mostly negative — fear-of-reinfection [[077-suzy-bolt-reinfection]], reinfection-as-relapse-trigger pattern).
- The pattern *can go either way* — most reinfections are negative; a small subset have apparently triggered recovery. Aaron's doctor said he had seen this "a couple of other times" in his practice.
- Aaron's pre-recovery stack was **antihistamines + beta blockers + benzodiazepines + (in the recovery infection) Paxlovid** — this is the wiki's first patient-side recovery story where *Paxlovid use during a reinfection* coincides with full reversal. Logged as confounded — could be the variant, the antiviral, the patient's accumulated stack, the immune reset, or some combination. No mechanistic claim is being made here.
- **Patient-built support-group dynamics** worth flagging: Aaron eventually left the 50,000-person Facebook LC support group ~6 months post-recovery. His observation: "After a while, people were asking me for proof I'd recovered. It was time to graduate." This pairs with Lizzie #165's observation that LC support groups full of multi-year-illness narratives became net-negative for recovery mindset.
- **GI vomiting + altered-taste + everything-tastes-amazing** as a *primary* LC symptom-set is wiki-new and worth flagging. Most wiki records have parosmia as the dominant smell-anomaly; Aaron's was the opposite — hyperpalatability + cravings.

## Eric-relevant takeaways

- *Caveat first*: Aaron's recovery is rare; this is not a path to recommend or seek. Eric should not engineer reinfection.
- The wiki now has **a confounded data point** that an Omicron reinfection + Paxlovid + still-active antihistamine + beta-blocker stack coincided with a light-switch recovery. Not mechanistic; logged for completeness.
- Aaron's stack — *antihistamines (incl famotidine) + beta-blocker for PEM* — is structurally similar to Eric's (Eric has Zyrtec + Pepcid + ivabradine + mestinon + florinef). Some validation that this stack is a reasonable bridge while waiting for other recovery factors.
- **Spoon theory + HR-ceiling pacing** got Aaron through 2.5 years; matches the wiki's general operational pattern.
- **Leaving LC support groups post-recovery** is a recurring theme — not a recovery prescription, but a useful boundary signal when timing is right. Eric is not at that point but worth knowing about.
