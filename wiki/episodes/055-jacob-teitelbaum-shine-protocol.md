# #55 — Dr Jacob Teitelbaum — Chronic Fatigue Specialist (SHINE Protocol)

[[jacob-teitelbaum]] is a US-based MD, recovered post-viral CFS (the illness pushed him out of medical school and into homelessness). Author of *From Fatigued to Fantastic* (multiple editions). Has published 8 studies on CFS/fibromyalgia treatment, including a placebo-controlled RCT (his report: **91% improved, average 90% increase in QoL**).

His thesis: **LC is post-viral chronic fatigue syndrome.** Treat it as such. He cites NIH director Fauci as agreeing.

## SHINE protocol

The episode is a fast walkthrough of his **SHINE** framework:

| Letter | Domain | Key levers |
|---|---|---|
| **S** | **Sleep** | Melatonin SR 10mg, herbs (valerian, lemon balm, 5-HTP), trazodone 25–50mg, cyclobenzaprine 2.5–5mg, gabapentin 100–600mg, low-dose olanzapine 2.5–5mg. Combine LOW doses of multiple agents over high dose of one. Z-drugs to initiate sleep. Epsom salt bath pre-bed. Protein snack at bedtime if waking with low blood sugar. Target 7–8 hours. |
| **H** | **Hormones** | Trial thyroid hormone even with "normal" TSH (range is 2 SD wide; symptoms trump labs). Adrenal: licorice tea, vitamin C + B5, low-dose hydrocortisone for severe cases. Sex hormones (bio-identical) if cycling worsens symptoms. |
| **+** | **Hypotension / OI** | Salt loading, compression stockings (20–30 mmHg), midodrine, fludrocortisone, DDAVP. **Caution on beta-blockers in CFS-context fatigue** — "in pure POTS it's helpful, but in people with chronic fatigue it often pulls the rug out from under their energy." Tiny dose if used. |
| **I** | **Infections** | **Candida** is #1 — fluconazole 200mg/day × 6 weeks, probiotics, sugar restriction. **EBV reactivation** — famciclovir 500–750mg TDS + Celebrex 200mg BID for 6 months. **Famotidine (Pepcid) 40mg BID** — anti-EBV + immune. *Fluvoxamine 20–40mg BID for residual COVID* (controversial). **Avoid PPIs** (cites 44% increased dementia risk, 25% increased mortality with long-term PPIs). |
| **N** | **Nutrition** | Increase salt. Avoid sugar. Increase protein, frequent small meals. Increase water (anti-diuretic hormone is low in hypothalamic dysfunction). High-B/Mg multivitamin. **Ribose 5g 2–3×/day** (published study: 61% increased energy). CoQ10, acetyl-L-carnitine, curcumin, glutathione, boswellia. |
| **E** | **Exercise** | Walk only as much as you can do without next-day crash. If crashed, cut back 20%, hold there. After ~8 weeks of protocol, increase by ~50 steps/day. **No GET — no "more is better" cattle-prod approach.** Conditioning, not graded exercise. |

## Adjuncts beyond SHINE

- **Low-dose naltrexone** (LDN) 3–4.5 mg — **strongly recommended for everyone with LC/CFS not on narcotics**. Takes 2 months to see effect; may disrupt sleep initially.
- **Low-dose aripiprazole (Abilify) 0.25mg** — Stanford research; effective for severe near-bedbound CFS. Liquid formulation needed for accurate dosing.
- **ANS Rewire** ([[dan-neuffer]]) or **Dynamic Neural Retraining System** ([[dnrs]]) — explicitly recommended as the autonomic-rewire layer to put on top of biochemistry.

## Mechanism story

Energy goes low → trips a circuit breaker in the **hypothalamus** (almond-sized; behind forehead; controls sleep + hormones + autonomics). Hypothalamic dysfunction explains:
- non-restorative sleep
- low thyroid + adrenal + sex hormones (with normal labs)
- POTS / orthostatic intolerance (autonomic)
- low ADH → frequent urination + dehydration despite drinking
- muscle pain (low energy → muscles stick in shortened position)

Reset the circuit breaker via SHINE → patient recovers.

## Counterpoint / caveat for the wiki

This episode lands on the **opposite pole** from [[joachim-gerlach]] (#41), [[keith-littlewood]] (#45), and [[gez-medinger]] (#46), who all warn against polypharmacy and the 15-supplement stack. **Teitelbaum embodies the "treat everything at once" approach.**

The wiki is not adjudicating. Both positions are logged.

Other caveats:
- Several supplements named (Recovery Factors, Smart Energy System, HRG80 red ginseng) are commercial products with which Teitelbaum has direct or indirect commercial relationships. Read with that lens.
- The 91%/90% number is from his own fibromyalgia/CFS RCT — not reproduced for LC specifically.
- Famciclovir + Celebrex for EBV reactivation is not standard care; controversial.
- Low-dose Abilify is one Stanford lab's signal, not a settled finding.
- Fluvoxamine for COVID persistence rejected by FDA for acute COVID.
