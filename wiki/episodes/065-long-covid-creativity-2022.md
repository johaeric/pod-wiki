# #65 — Long Covid Creativity 2022 (compilation)

A creativity-themed compilation episode featuring music, poetry, and art from people affected by Long Covid. **Not an interventions episode.** Recorded across multiple contributors. Logged here primarily for the **community / advocacy / creativity** signal and for the new programs that appear (the Long Covid Choir and Long Covid Kids Choir).

## Contributors

- **Merrill Vaughan** introduces the **[[long-covid-choir]]** — established April 2021 by [[zoe-chaler]] and [[claire-hastie]] in response to having LC. Meets weekly online, members on mute, breathing exercises and mindfulness. Reach: UK, US, Germany, China. Performs *"When We Sing Together"* by Vicky Jacobs.
- **Julia (Connecticut, US)** — teacher, mother, runner pre-Covid, hospitalised acute. Tried supplements, acupuncture, CBD oil (helped chest pain), qigong, breathing exercises, stretching, pacing. Credits **plant-tending and drawing/painting** with keeping her mindset in check during medical leave.
- **Colin Pidgeon** reads his poem *"Stormy"* — written in response to a young patient being told *"it's all in your head, it's anxiety"* by a clinician. Logged as a verbatim of [[healthcare-gaslighting]] from the carer perspective.
- **Zaha (Vienna, 17)** — diagnosed with POTS and PEM. Wrote song *"Thank You"* about classmates and teachers who supported her through recovery.
- **Yana Cecilia "JC" (Norway)** — got Covid Feb 2022, LC diagnosis May 2022. Two poems: *"Long Covid"* (falling-leaf metaphor, climbing-walls-of-recovery) and *"I'm So Tired"* (PEM, hives, pacing, no racing). Collaborator with Rosie Pidgeon.
- **Tabby (16, UK)** — 15 months ill at recording. Song collaboration with [[061-rosie-colin-pidgeon|Rosie Pidgeon]] for [[long-covid-kids]] charity.
- **Carol Barwick** — runs Raise (self-esteem business). Two poems: *"I Choose to Choose Joy"* and *"A Chronic Christmas Plea"*.
- **Long Covid Kids Choir** — set up by Merrill and [[long-covid-kids]] CEO Sammy McFarlane on the 6 Nov 2022. Six-week trial; bringing joy, gentle breathing, warm-ups, ukulele, African drums. Performs Jill Galina's *"Winter Fantasy"*.

## What the episode adds to the wiki

- **Two new programs**: [[long-covid-choir]] (adult), [[long-covid-kids-choir]] (children).
- **Creativity-as-coping** is reinforced as a recurring practical strategy — gardening, drawing, music, poetry. Especially for paediatric LC where physical activities are gone.
- **Paediatric LC** voices added: Rosie (#61), Zaha, Tabby — three teenage patients now anchored across episodes #61 and #65.
- **Gentle vocal work** (singing on mute in choir) overlaps mechanistically with the [[063-michael-bagnell|Bagnell brain-stem rehab]] singing/phonation recommendation — vagal stimulation as a side-effect of choir participation.
