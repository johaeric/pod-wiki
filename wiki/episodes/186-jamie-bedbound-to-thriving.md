⚠️ **Content note:** This episode contains discussion of suicidal ideation.

Jamie was COVID-cautious before infection. After her acute illness she became severely bedbound — unable to crawl to the toilet, light/sound sensitive, headphones and earplugs constantly. Got entangled in a 50-supplement detox protocol that worsened her (every reaction reframed by the protocol creator as "die-off"). Her mother became carer; eventually had to drive Jamie home, where Jamie crashed harder.

## Turning point

A breakdown moment — son in hospital, Jamie unable to be his mother — led her to ask people around her for knives. Her partner returned from hospital with their son and called an ambulance for her.

In hospital under Clinic 19's care:
- Prescribed POTS meds (effects modest), prescribed **LDN** but never took it because she got better first
- Prescribed **olanzapine (an antipsychotic)** during the breakdown — it stabilised sleep and helped overall. She does *not* recommend it as a treatment (hard to taper); she calls it her turning point chemically.
- Took **zinc** at the protocol founder's insistence — got up and walked to toilet. Jamie attributes this to placebo / belief, not pharmacology.
- Heard her uncle had recovered from CFS by **not stopping movement**, and a hospital physio said she had recovered too. First exposure to recovery stories.

## What actually moved her forward

- **Reframe**: "this is just a hypersensitive nervous system" written on her hand
- **Primal Trust** brain-retraining course — visualised light flowing through her body while gently pushing capacity
- Daily incremental walking with self-talk: "you're safe, you're okay, this is just a hypersensitive nervous system"
- **Healing Dudes** group coaching — celebration of small wins, no symptom-talk
- At ~90% she did **The Lightning Process** (3-day intensive). She thinks the *theory of fight-or-flight stuck-state* helped most. Did it as a perceived final-stretch tool, not early on.
- "When did I declare myself fully healed? When I started a job again."

## Key quote

> "I just believed I could recover. It was like a switch went off inside me. If they can do it, so can I."
> — Jamie, ep #186

## Notes for Eric

A high-stakes recovery story illustrating: (1) the **harm of high-supplement protocols** that reframe adverse reactions as detox; (2) the role of olanzapine in acute mental crisis (not a treatment); (3) **brain retraining at the right time** (Primal Trust early, Lightning Process late). Note: the Lightning Process is controversial — Jamie's read is "useful late, possibly useless or harmful early."
