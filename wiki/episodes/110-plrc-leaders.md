# #110 — Lisa McCorkell, Gina Assaf, Hannah Davis: Patient-Led Research Collaborative

Three of the four co-leads of the **Patient-Led Research Collaborative** ([[patient-led-research-collaborative]]) — the patient-led research organisation incubated inside the [[body-politic]] Slack in April 2020 and now the most influential patient-research voice on LC globally. All three got Covid in March 2020; all three are still long-haulers (Gina ~70-90% better; Hannah still significantly affected).

## Origin story (anchor)

- **Body Politic** Slack channel called *Data Nerds* self-selected for people whose coping strategy was "learn as much as possible." Lisa, Gina, Hannah, plus Athena Akrami and others met there.
- **First survey** April-May 2020 — N=640; gave the world its first data on what is now called LC. Cited by Ed Yong's *Atlantic* piece.
- **Second survey** June-Dec 2020 — N=3,762; **200 symptoms** (collected from the patient community); 7-month timeline; remains one of the most-cited LC papers.
- **Body Politic disability-justice frame** — the group was rooted in HIV/AIDS and ME advocacy lineage from the start. Same playbook for fighting minimisation; pre-built defenses ("the support groups are causing this anxiety").

## What PLRC has built (2020-2023)

- **$6M raised in 2023**; **$5M put into biomedical research grants** (10 funded projects).
- **Long Covid review paper** — the first comprehensive LC review; one of ~70 of 24M papers downloaded >1M times in <12 months. Hannah Davis lead author.
- **Patient-engagement scorecards** with the Council of Medical Specialty Societies — generic across all disease groups; a tool to evaluate whether research engages patients meaningfully or tokenistically.
- **Reinfection survey** results pending at recording.
- **Hypothesis Publications**, **LMIC paper**, **phenotyping work** wrapping up.
- **Return-to-work paper** just published.
- **Infection-Associated Chronic Conditions** umbrella coalition — bringing LC + ME/CFS + chronic Lyme + dysautonomia + MCAS + EDS advocacy together under one frame.

## Anchor framings

> "Putting it together with a data lens was the next step. We had no idea who else had gotten together as sick people finding each other — were we the only group?" — Gina

> "Every pathogen can cause long-term effects. Chronic Lyme, ME/CFS, post-COVID — same patterns. If providers were taught this, they'd treat patients better. That education has never occurred at a meaningful level." — Lisa

> "The first wavers didn't have access to PCR tests. A third of people don't make antibodies — and that's higher in women, who are the majority of LC. Researchers requiring positive tests are excluding the people with the longest data." — Hannah

> "In the US: only 3% of first wavers got documentation. Through Sept 2021 only 25% of COVID cases got documented per the CDC. There's so much data on this no one is communicating." — Hannah

## Eric-relevance

- **PLRC is the canonical "expert-patient" organisation** — Eric should know it exists, what it produces, and that the wiki considers its outputs the highest-quality patient-side LC literature available.
- The **scorecards** are a portable tool — for any future LC trial Eric considers participating in, these are the metric for "is this real PPI/E or tokenism."
- **Reinfection survey** results, when published, will be a signal worth tracking — direct Eric-applicable data on "what happens to LC patients on infection 4 or 5."
- The **Infection-Associated Chronic Conditions framing** is the same one Stiles #66 advocates for. Two converging voices on "stop siloing these illnesses."
