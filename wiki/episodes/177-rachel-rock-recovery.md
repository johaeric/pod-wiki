# #177 — Rachel Rock — The Stool with Four Legs

Boston-based journalist. Got LC in May 2022 after a mild COVID infection while training for the **Pan-Mass Challenge** bike ride. Recovered to riding 86 miles of PMC in summer 2024, getting COVID again 10 days later and clearing it in 2–3 days. Calls her recovery building "a stool" with the autonomic nervous system as the seat and four supporting legs.

## The four legs

1. **An ANS practitioner.** [[dr-lydia-knutson]] (Cambridge MA) — chiropractic + energetic medicine + kinesiology — see ep #151. Multi-stage protocol; with Rachel, went beyond standard LC protocol because of her viral history.
2. **Functional-medicine workup.** Discovered allergies (gluten, later dairy), supplemented for measured deficiencies. Several "fake recoveries" — would feel better, then crash. Led to discovering the next leg.
3. **Diet.** Tried keto for a while (helped brain fog), now off it. Removing flagged foods stabilised her ANS.
4. **Trauma.** Rachel's mother died when she was 15. From the outside she had triumphed; the 15-year-old's experience had never been processed. **EMDR** (eye-movement desensitisation and reprocessing) was load-bearing — and the bodily sensation while reliving trauma was *identical* to the chronic-fatigue feeling. Realised her ANS was recording it all.

## What also mattered

- **Safe and Sound Protocol** with Jackie — gave her the **language and the felt sense** of up- vs down-regulation. Had to pause EMDR to do SSP. Game-changer for self-tracking.
- **Apple Watch + HRV tracking** from before LC (her data baseline) — could see her body registered "off" before her conscious mind did.
- **Acupuncture** weekly to bi-weekly — measurable blood-pressure improvement, helped with pain.
- **Reactivated viruses** — EBV, herpes zoster, Lyme. These were pushing her recoveries to fail; clearing took time with Dr Knutson's protocol.
- **Reading: James Nestor's *Breath* (epilogue: "A Last Gasp"). Ed Young's Atlantic piece** on chronic fatigue: "depression is sitting on the side of your life and not caring about getting back; chronic fatigue is sitting there desperate to get back and unable to." Validating.
- **Eventual return to ADHD medication** under a chronic-illness-aware **psychopharmacologist** — last piece before going back to work.

## Reframe Rachel landed on

Healing as adapting to environment (Darwin: most adaptable, not strongest). Modern medicine cuts and stitches well; chronic systemic dysregulation is what eastern modalities have addressed for thousands of years. Her job daily is "asking what is making my nervous system feel unsafe" and acting on the answer.

> "The most painful part of all this is the loss of self. The hardest job is recognising you can be the agent of your own healing — and that you already do this every day if you stop, breathe, hold your dog." — Rachel Rock, ep #177
