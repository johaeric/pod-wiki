# #165 — Lizzie's Recovery Story

UK patient. Caught covid at her sister's wedding (July 2022 — at the peak of personal triathlon training; pushed through and *completed a triathlon* the day after symptom onset). Two years from infection to feeling fully recovered; second-wave LC, did not pre-fear LC because thought "it wouldn't apply to me."

## Symptom presentation

- Dominant: **fatigue** (described as "wading through concrete") + **brain fog** (especially conversational drain — having to leave room when family visited).
- Pattern: **boom-and-bust** for many months; partner could see the cycle she couldn't. Only after a major crash did she internalise PEM as a real pattern.
- Late development: **heart palpitations / racing HR at rest, mid-night** — emerged ~6 months in *after* she started rigid step-counting (~1,000 steps/day cap). Lizzie now interprets this as fight-or-flight conditioning from the fear-of-overdoing-it loop, not a primary symptom.

## What helped — recovery arc

1. **Stopped pushing through** — went back to work after one month off, "definitely too soon in hindsight." Took ~6 months of boom-bust before a major crash forced full stop.
2. **Brain-fog lift around month 6** allowed research → discovered Mind-Body / nervous-system framing.
3. **Jan Rothney's book** *Breaking Free from Long Covid and Chronic Fatigue* — load-bearing. Explained nervous system; gave techniques for slow safe recovery; introduced **only-positive-experiences principle** when increasing activity.
4. **Curable app** — second pillar; designed for chronic pain but applied to chronic fatigue / autoimmune / LC. Particularly named *language-shift* technique ("I feel less energised than I would like" not "I feel awful").
5. **Amygdala pep talk** (Rothney's technique): calm, authoritative inner mantra during fatigue onset — *"I am safe. Thank you body for trying to protect me with this fatigue, but I just don't need it. I am safe."* Reports stopping fatigue **mid-meal** at her mother's birthday dinner using this and getting up walking away from the table.
6. **Discipline + structure**: routine with rest periods; small celebrations for each new capacity ("I had a shower standing up today!"); slow boundary-pushing only on confirmed-good days.
7. **Therapy** post-recovery — to process the trauma + grief of the illness itself. Recommends.

## What didn't help / actively harmed

- **Step-counting as pacing** — caused emergence of palpitations + worsening crashes through fight-or-flight conditioning. Switched from step-count to body-listening.
- **Facebook LC support groups** with multi-year-illness anecdotes — left them because terror-of-permanence was poisoning her mindset.

- The **amygdala pep talk** mantra is a specific tool to layer onto Eric's existing breathwork practice when fatigue/palpitation onset is felt.
- Lizzie's step-counting harm story is a useful caution: **threshold-anxiety is itself a stressor.** Eric is lower-risk here (his stack is meds + breathing not wearable-driven), but worth carrying as a frame.
