# #142 — Jackie's thoughts of the day (3-year anniversary)

Short host-solo (~12 min) released to mark the **3-year anniversary** of the podcast (142 episodes in). No guest, no formal topic.

## What Jackie reflects on

- **Perfectionism in recovery** — wanting to "do recovery perfectly" is itself an obstacle. Recovery is a process — try things, learn from what doesn't work, allow yourself to be a beginner at pacing.
- **Recovery is the start of a new journey, not the end of the old one** — keep regulating the nervous system, keep practising gratitude, keep boundaries.
- **Balance** as the operative word post-recovery. Saying yes to nourishing things (early run, morning swim, garden) is *also* "what I need to do" — not a luxury layered on top of work commitments.
- **Self-care isn't selfish; it's fundamental to good health.**
- "If I'd realized how much effort goes into this I'd never have started — but I'm very glad I did."
