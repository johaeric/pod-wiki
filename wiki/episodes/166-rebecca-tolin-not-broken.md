# #166 — Rebecca Tolin: Mind-Body Coach + 13-Year ME/CFS Recovery

US former TV broadcast journalist (covered nature/science). Recovered from **13 years of severe ME/CFS + migraines + insomnia + interstitial cystitis + multiple chronic symptoms**, now ~6 years post-recovery. Operates as a Mind-Body coach for LC + ME/CFS clients. Trained in the John Sarno → Howard Schubiner → Alan Gordon lineage.

## Trauma + onset

- Pre-illness: athletic broadcast journalist; world-traveller.
- **Sexual assault** triggered shutdown / black-out → didn't tell anyone, didn't get therapy for years.
- Multiple viruses afterward → severe post-viral crash. Confined to home; unable to remember close friends' names.

## What didn't work — 13-year detour

- 50+ doctors / practitioners in biomedical model.
- **Targeted EBV with antiviral IV medications** → "really just crashed my system more."
- Heavy-metals + mould + chronic Lyme treatments.
- Three years on chicken/fish + green vegetables only (candida + leaky gut protocol) — "more bloated than ever."
- *"None of those interventions were working. They felt like throwing supplements or medications at me."*
- Lost career, on federal disability, ran out of money.

## Inflection point

- One **3-hour conversation with a recovered ME/CFS layperson named Kathy** (met via online poetry class). Kathy explained the John Sarno / Howard Schubiner framework — symptoms generated by brain + nervous system in heightened-stress state, *not* a pathological process.
- *"I got up and ran around the block after that conversation, which I had not run in 13 years."*
- Not a lasting "book cure" — but the cognitive shift + somatic experience together opened the door.

## Recovery toolkit (post-Kathy)

- **Somatic tracking** — central practice. Watch sensations *in the body* (burning / tingling / shooting / dullness / numbness / warmth / coolness / widespread vs localized) with mindfulness; turns down the danger signal because you stop fighting symptoms.
- **Inner-emotional work** — months → years of feeling repressed emotions (anger, grief, shame, fear) physically in the body before they would dissipate or surface as memory.
- **Personality-trait work** — Sarno's TMS personality framework: people-pleasing, perfectionism, self-criticism, holding-in-emotions, goodist. These create chronic internal stress; LC patients have these widely.
- **Self-compassion** — inner-dialogue shift from criticism + blame-for-not-recovering to "I'm here with you. I know you're suffering. I love you anyway."

## Position on biomedical labs

- Underlying viral particles, mitochondrial damage, Lyme bacteria, heavy metals — these *do* exist in some patients. **They are not the root cause of symptoms.**
- When the brain + nervous system are in fight/flight/freeze, mitochondria don't work optimally, immunity falters, digestion stalls. Treat the autonomic state and downstream organs balance themselves.
- Tolin emphatically: **this is not "all in your head."** It's autonomic + subconscious processing — patients have **no conscious control** over the danger response.

## Eric-relevant takeaways

- **Somatic tracking** is a contained free practice Eric can layer on top of breathwork — sit with a sensation, name what it actually feels like (not what it means), watch with curiosity. Tolin frames it as "the practice that turns down the danger response."
- The **personality-trait work** is the level at which Eric's existing perfectionism / driver-pattern is operating — it's not the trauma per se, it's the chronic-stress-state from how he was being. The wiki has now anchored this from three angles (Adams #152 trauma-response, Tolin #166 Sarno-TMS-personality, Nadyne #155 self-compassion).
- The "I'm not broken" reframe is operationally significant for Eric: *being sick* is not the same as *being damaged*. POTS + PEM are dysfunction, not pathology. Tolin's strict line on this aligns with [[149-eleanor-stein]] ("damage is the wrong word; changes are reversible") and [[146-dr-becca-kennedy]] (same line).
- Tolin's 13-year detour through biomedical rabbit-holes is a useful boundary-condition: not every test result is the answer; treating the autonomic state may be load-bearing even when labs show real findings.
