# #036 — Dr Mark Faghy — Derby Cohort & Patient Voice

**Guest**: **Dr Mark Faghy** — Associate Professor in Respiratory Physiology, University of Derby. Recovery-from-acute-and-chronic-respiratory-illness programme. **Pre-2020** he was running a 6-month pneumonia-recovery cohort study; covid arrived just as that was wrapping up, and the methodology pivoted directly to LC.

## The three live studies

**1. 16-week observational cohort** — bi-weekly touch points (8 contacts per patient) capturing physiological / biological / psychological / quality-of-life / functional changes. Recruits patients newly admitted to acute care or referred to LC clinics. Cross-site: **UK + USA + India**. Distinctive feature: lived-experience diary alongside the quantitative measures, transcribed in rolling fashion to avoid analysis lag.

> "Most observational studies see patients at baseline and 6 months. We were saying: there's a lot in the middle you're not seeing. Bi-weekly captures the fluctuations." — Faghy

**2. Patient-voice survey** — 70 questions, 185 complete responses, 500+ pages of narrative data. Designed by the patient-representative group. Themes already emerging: **inconsistency or absence of support**, lack of being heard, daily-living limits, work impact. Plan to disseminate non-academically (visual / exhibition formats) — academic journals "are great but nobody reads them."

**3. Delphi consensus on LC rehabilitation** — multi-stakeholder (GPs, respiratory & non-respiratory physios, clinicians, healthcare workers, patients including LC sufferers). 275 responses in two weeks. Goal: a *blueprint* for what coordinated LC support should look like. Pushback received on the word "rehabilitation"; Faghy argues the term is broader than physical reconditioning and is keeping it.

## The "buffet" support model

Vision Faghy lays out for what comes after the data:

- Patients screened for their **biggest current challenges** at intake
- A **menu** of supports they can access as needed (physical, cognitive, mental-health, dietary, etc.)
- Re-screen when needs change (PEM relapse, new symptom cluster) — the "go back up to the buffet" metaphor
- **System-science underpinning** — explicit modelling of inter-relations between primary care / specialists / non-clinical / public-sector partners. Not multiple parallel pathways; one interconnected mesh.

This is consciously built against the "fragmented, patient-as-coordinator" status quo Cass Macdonald (#19) and Asad Khan (#37) describe.

## Methodological signature

- **Patient voice as data, not adornment.** Faghy notes a recent UK government paper urging this — he was already 12 months in by then.
- **Ultra-collaborative** — not "Mark from Derby will solve LC by himself." Cites the rapid-vaccine collaboration as the model to emulate.
- **Strong PPI group**, named by Faghy as "the beating heart" of the research.

## Reading Eric's likely question

- This episode is **not about a treatment**. It's about whether the research apparatus is being shaped well, and how data from it might land. Worth knowing because the Derby cohort + Delphi may produce one of the more coherent UK LC-care frameworks in 2023.
- The Derby framework's intended deliverable — a blueprint integrated into NHS service design — would be the upstream of any future referral pathway Eric or others might use in the UK.
