# #035 — Axcella Therapeutics — AXA1125

**Guests**:
- **Bill Hinshaw** — CEO, Axcella Therapeutics (Boston biotech).
- **Dr Margaret Koziel** — CMO. Background in HIV clinical trials → industry translational work.

A **drug-development episode** — the first deep-dive on a long-covid pharma candidate in the wiki. Axcella is a Flagship Pioneering company (same parent as Moderna). Their platform uses **endogenous metabolic modulators (EMMs)** — compositions of amino acids and derivatives, oral.

## What AXA1125 is

A composition of amino acids targeting multiple pathways at once:
- Fatty-acid oxidation
- Cellular respiration / ATP generation
- Inflammation
- Immune modulation

Already in Phase 2 trials for **non-alcoholic steatohepatitis (NASH)** when LC arrived — the same biology (mitochondrial dysfunction + chronic inflammation) was sticking out of the early LC literature.

## The Oxford Phase 2A trial

Single-site, **University of Oxford**. Designed jointly with Oxford because Oxford has world-leading expertise in **31P magnetic resonance spectroscopy (MRS)** to measure muscle mitochondrial function in vivo.

**Inclusion:** patients meeting a fatigue threshold on a patient-reported outcome (same scoring system used in CFS trials). **Outcomes:**
- MRS-measured mitochondrial function (recovery rate of phosphocreatine after exercise — the classic mitochondrial-function biomarker)
- Blood lactate (mitochondrial stress marker)
- Inflammatory markers
- Patient-reported fatigue

Recruiting at recording (April 2022). **Results expected mid-year 2022.**

## The mitochondrial framing

Koziel: mitochondria are the cell's PowerHouse *and* a central node in immune response — viruses commonly hijack them. In LC, MRS shows "profound disruption of the mitochondria" in patients meeting trial entry criteria. This explains why the symptoms are systemic: not a respiratory disease but an energy-metabolism disease in many tissues at once.

> "What you're seeing in many post-viral syndromes — including LC — is that these mitochondria, along with other things, are disrupted. The body is almost turned against itself." — Koziel

## What "Phase 2A" gets you, and what comes next

- **Already past** Phase 1 (healthy volunteers) thanks to NASH programme — known safety and dosing.
- 2A: does it work, biologically, in this population? Quick answer.
- Then Phase 3 = larger, multi-site, broader population, longer duration. Geography expansion likely (UK + EU + US).

> "We need to mirror the population who has the disease in the choice of sites. If LC is majority female, that's what we emulate in the studies." — Koziel
