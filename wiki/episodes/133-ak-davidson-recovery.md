# Episode 133 — A K Davidson — Long Covid Recovery

Anna Davidson is a UK writer, editor, and mother of three who had **covid three times and long covid three times** between 2020 and 2023. Recovered each time. Started writing poetry during her LC years; published *Pearls for Life and other Chronic Conditions: Short Pearings from Long Covid* (June 2024). Now fully recovered.

## The arc (three rounds)

- **Round 1 (April 2020)** — mild covid; intermittent breathlessness episodes through the rest of 2020. Lockdown context masked LC features; not formally identified.
- **Round 2 (late 2021)** — woke one morning "feeling like drowning"; full LC for ~1 year; recovered.
- **Round 3 (Dec 2023)** — Snakes-and-Ladders again. Recovered through brain-training as the closing piece.

Prior CFS experience (year 2000, post-glandular-fever) — a year ill with no active intervention; recovered spontaneously. This memory underpinned her belief in recovery during LC, but the path she had to take in LC was completely different — active, multi-modal.

## What worked (consolidated across rounds)

- **Diet elimination + adjustment** — saw a natural-healing practitioner (friend's referral); did a York food-intolerance test; cut the flagged foods. Mood lifted significantly. Strong subscriber to the gut-health / microbiome story.
- **Osteopath** ("what happened to you?" — first practitioner who *visibly* recognised something was wrong). Found her **breathing was completely upper-chest** at rest; manual work re-opened upper-chest expansion that she'd lost months back. Inflection point — she had been mistaking diaphragmatic-only breathing for full breathing (former flautist, prior pregnancy-breathing training).
- **Breathwork** (post-osteopath revelation) — taking the practice seriously made a major difference.
- **POTS diagnosis (late)** — after diagnosis, [[boon-lim]]'s materials were a load-bearing reference.
- **[[yoga-for-life-project]] peer-support group** — connection + creativity-witnessing community; load-bearing emotional contribution.
- **Brain training** (final round, after the third reinfection) — *the closing piece*. Gave her both the recovery and a felt sense of resilience to future infections that earlier rounds had not produced.
- **Sea swimming** when accessible (geographic constraint).
- **Switching off the news** (Radio 4 → Radio 3 / Petroc Trelawny). Removing the daily onslaught of bad news was directly therapeutic. Re-engage when stronger.

## What was load-bearing emotionally

- **Writing & sharing poetry on Instagram.** Started in lockdown; broke her own privacy preference. Vulnerability → connection → mutual support both ways. Found uplifting creative communities that re-framed her view of social media.
- **Friendship-as-season** ("Well-Dressed" poem) — different friends right for different phases of recovery; not loss but rotation.
- **Switching off the news** (above) — a self-permission act, not avoidance.

## What she'd tell past Anna

- You can recover. You can recover *more than once*.
- Don't try to take on the world while still ill — the energy isn't there. Wait until strong, then advocate.
- The lessons stay with you. Don't dwell, but don't pull the ladder up either — talk about having recovered.

## Recovery posture

She is now in the *upward spiral* phase — gym induction, swimming, building stamina. **Recovered, not cured** language: she expects life to keep teaching the same lessons; pacing-as-skill-for-life remains true.
