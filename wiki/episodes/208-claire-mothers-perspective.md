**Claire** — UK occupational therapist, mother. Her son developed paediatric ME/CFS at age 13 (October, post-viral, year 8). Now 23, fully recovered and working full-time + climbing in the evenings. Claire shares the parental view of an 8-year recovery arc.

## Acute and progressive phase

- October 2010s viral infection in year 8 (age 13)
- Steadily declined over months — went from half days at school to no school by spring
- Fatigue dominant; PEM; swollen toe (the visible symptom); poor temperature regulation; cervical lymph node tenderness; palpitations; dizziness
- Background: history of intense early childhood (sibling died of SMA before he was born; family stress; high-strung mother)

## Care pathway

- Excellent GP who took her lead and referred to Bristol/Bath ME/CFS clinic (Dr Esther Crawley) within 3 weeks
- She had heard that early specialist care raises recovery odds from ~6% to 75%+ in paediatric ME/CFS — gold-standard route
- Local follow-up via paediatric ME/CFS specialist who taught colour-coded activity-management diary
- Got him to ~40% baseline. Plateaued for 2 years.

## What helped — in stages

1. **Pacing / activity management with paediatric ME specialist** — 0–40% functional capacity over 18 months
2. **ANS Rewire** ([[ans-rewire]]) at age 16¾–17 — videos were geared to adults and demanding, but he did it. **40% → 80% in months**, plateau-breaking. Got him through A-levels (over 3 years) and 2 years of engineering at university
3. **Mickel therapy** ([[mickel-therapy]]) at age 21 — 3 sessions, 1/week. **Fully recovered**. Mickel therapy listens to the body's symptoms-as-signals about overstepped boundaries, anger, guilt, etc.
4. **Joy-led activity at home** — Rubik's cube, diabolo, juggling, kite-flying, RSPB volunteering, gentle weeding. Play state as nervous-system regulation, especially for a teenager who'd resist standard "do this breathing exercise" approach.

## What didn't help

- Homeopathy — first remedy fixed nausea/weight-loss, nothing else
- Perrin technique
- Various supplements (Teitelbaum-style)
- Diet experiments (gluten-free, carnivore)

## A mother's pivot moment

- Mid-illness, after Claire had been correcting him during a sink repair, his speech slurred and energy collapsed. She realised: 10 minutes of negativity → cascade. *His thoughts were driving the condition.* That changed everything.

## On boundaries Claire set

- "If you come back from university because of ME, you can't stay here — go live with your father" — said in love, because she couldn't keep helping while he refused recovery work. He came back ill anyway and *the same evening she was about to remind him* he said: "I think I'll use the year to recover."

## Notes for Eric

Important addition to the wiki: this is the wiki's **first Mickel therapy recovery case**, and a strong one. Mickel therapy is a small-footprint UK-origin modality where the therapist reads symptoms as signals about overstepped boundaries / suppressed emotion. Claire's son recovered fully in 3 sessions after 8 years of illness — n=1, but a sharp inflection. Worth keeping the page. The *paediatric-care-improves-prognosis* claim (6% → 75%+) is from Claire's reading rather than rigorous data, but the Bristol/Bath clinic pathway is real and worth flagging for any UK paediatric LC case. The "mother sets a hard boundary, son immediately self-organises" pattern is striking — separation-as-care.
