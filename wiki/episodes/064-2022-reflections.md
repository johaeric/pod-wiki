# #64 — 2022 Reflections (Jackie host-solo)

A reflective host-solo episode — Jackie Baxter looking back on her own ~33 months of LC and what she's learned in the previous six months. The most useful single document on this wiki for understanding **what Jackie herself credits** as primary in her recovery arc, since she is the host and her implicit framing is everywhere across the other 210 episodes.

## Jackie's self-declared top three

In her own words, asked the same end-of-interview question she asks her guests:

1. **Breathing** — fixing her breathing-pattern disorder first, then the broader power of breath. *"I've done lots of interviews with wonderful breathing people."* See [[breathwork]], [[buteyko-breathing]], [[diaphragmatic-breathing]], [[long-covid-breathing]].
2. **Yoga Nidra** and **deep rest**. *"I thought that reading a book was resting but it really isn't. I have a brain like a hamster wheel — it goes round and round and round and it never stops."* Hard at first; persisted. See [[yoga-nidra]].
3. **Cold water (especially in nature, with friends)**. Started end-March 2022. First "swim" was ~1 minute. By summer, up to 30 min in Loch Ness. See [[cold-water-exposure]], [[wild-swimming]].

Honourable fourth (her words): *"keep going — believe — don't give up. Listen to your body. Get well but do it at your own pace."*

## Concepts she names as load-bearing

- **Forgiveness** (from [[040-boon-lim-autonomic|Boon Lim, ep #40]]) — anger at government / schools / public health / partner / self was understandable but actively triggering. Trying to embody Boon Lim's "forgive" frame is helping. See [[gratitude-and-forgiveness]].
- **Belief in recovery** (from [[040-boon-lim-autonomic|Boon Lim]] and [[063-michael-bagnell|Bagnell #63]]) — *"believe that you can get better until proved otherwise."* If you stop believing, you stop trying.
- **Don't let what you cannot do prevent you from doing what you can** — Boon Lim, post-it-noted on her wall.
- **Don't let your history define who you are today** — Boon Lim, also post-it-noted.
- **Safety**. Nervous system stays in fight-or-flight when it senses danger; recovery requires re-regulation. *"How are we supposed to feel safe when we are surrounded by so much danger?"* — names the paradox without resolving it; SSP is one tool she's trying.
- **Improvement → mental crash**. *"There's something about improvement making you realise how much further you have to go and that can be hard."* — July 2022 crash after months of physical gains was devastating.

## Negative experiences explicitly logged

- **Food intolerance test** is logged as a cautionary case. Specifically: long restriction → reintroduction can cause sharper reaction than baseline. Eric-applicable lesson if he ever does an elimination diet.
- **Comparison with other recovery stories** — *"why are they recovering and I'm not?"* — named as natural but unhelpful.

## Eric-relevance

- Jackie's top 3 (breathing / yoga nidra / cold water) is the wiki's clearest single summary of "what the host credits." All three are zero-cost, zero-prescription, and Eric-applicable. He already has yoga nidra in rotation.
- The food-intolerance cautionary tale is worth Eric having on file.
- Her **symptom-escalation-plan** concept (a written list of regulation techniques deployed at the first symptom warning) is a practical structure Eric could replicate.
