# #023 — Sally Riggs — Psychologist & Long-Hauler

**Guest**: **Dr Sally Riggs** — psychologist (CBT-for-psychosis specialism), private practice in Manhattan, originally UK-trained. Caught covid March 2020 (acute infection 13 March, never tested positive — multiple negative tests across PCR/antibody). **Substantial recovery** by Feb 2022; working full-time, slow exercise return, recurring dips (a Christmas-2021 relapse). This is the **third recovery arc** on the wiki.

## Recovery trajectory & what helped

Worsened steadily through 2020 to a low point in early 2021 (bedbound-ish, couldn't shower or cook). Improvement began around March 2021; significant uplift by July 2021; substantial improvement from September 2021 onwards.

What she credits as turning points (her own framing — multi-component, not one silver bullet):

1. **Low-dose naltrexone (LDN)** — started ~March 2021 from a chronic-fatigue / infectious-disease specialist. EBV reactivation found in bloodwork; antiviral trial unclear if helpful, but **LDN was the beginning of feeling better**.
2. **Zero-sugar keto diet** (started July 2021) — "game-changer" for GI symptoms; got her back to her normal weight.
3. **Safe and Sound Protocol (SSP)** — Stephen Porges-derived listening intervention rooted in **polyvagal theory**. Discovered ~Sept 2021. Key marker of inflection in her arc.
4. **Cognitive framing** via polyvagal theory — understanding why she couldn't access "acceptance / gratitude / self-compassion" earlier (because nervous system stuck in fight-flight or below it in **immobilization**, not because of personal failure).

## What didn't help her

- **Low-histamine diet** — tried, did not help her. (Notable mismatch with ep #21 Moira's enthusiasm.)
- **Stasis breathing** (referred to by her first LC clinic) — tried twice, stopped both times. Doesn't blame the intervention, blames spoon-budget conflict (couldn't fit it alongside SSP, gut hypnosis, treadmill).

## The polyvagal frame she emphasises

Three states (Stephen Porges):
- **Rest & digest** — connection, gratitude, acceptance accessible here
- **Fight or flight** — sympathetic, panic, no acceptance accessible
- **Immobilization (dorsal vagal shutdown)** — heaviness, brain fog, can't think, no higher functions accessible. Most LC fatigue lives here.

> "If acceptance isn't working, it's not that something is wrong with you. It's that your nervous system can't access it from where you are. Get back up the ladder first." — Sally

Her warning: meditation / yoga / Vagus-nerve YouTube tricks **don't work for everyone**, especially those with trauma history, and pushing them when they don't work just throws you back into immobilization.

## Tools she names

- **Journal Speak** (Nicole Sachs, chronic-pain origin) — 20-minute timed dump-write
- **Observe and Describe** (DBT) — name body sensations and emotions; even peak emotions only last ~40 minutes; ride the wave
- **Sticky-note prompts**: "what does this feel like *now*?" — version of positive mindset that works in immobilization (vs the unhelpful generic "be positive")
- **Boundaries** — explicit framing as self-care: "I can't do last-minute. I plan my energy envelope."
