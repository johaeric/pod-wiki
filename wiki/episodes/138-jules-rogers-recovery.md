# Episode 138 — Jules Rogers — Long Covid Recovery

UK mother of two; previously a part-time emotionally-stressful job + family stressors at home → "perfect storm". Got covid at the very start of the first UK lockdown (March 2020) with atypical symptoms; LC fatigue hit ~2 weeks later and shaped the next ~3+ years.

## Backstory

- Had **chronic fatigue syndrome (post-glandular-fever) at age 18** — ~1 year ill, recovered without active intervention. The body recognised the pattern when LC fatigue hit in April 2020 ("oh, we know this").
- Recovery in 2020 was very different from her year-2000 recovery: no spontaneous resolution, much more active research and self-experimentation.

## What she tried

1. **Diet experiments** (~1 year):
   - Low-histamine diet — small benefit, not enough for the stress of restriction.
   - Green-smoothie / raw-foods phase — same.
   - **Elimination diet with a nutritionist** (May–Sept 2021) — felt the most well she had during this whole period. Significant.
2. **Pamela Rose 3-week fatigue-recovery course** ([[pamela-rose]]) — picked up useful tips, especially the "**it's just for now**" framing for accepting current limits while protecting recovery time. Not the final answer.
3. **Mind-Body therapy** (~8 sessions over 3 months) — *the inflection point*. Learning to associate symptoms + emotions with thought patterns; to take certain actions and change the symptom outcome; to regulate her own nervous-system state between sympathetic and parasympathetic. **80% of recovery** by her account.
4. **Studying as a life coach + Mind-Body practitioner** (training as the *last 20%*) — coaching herself through the curriculum was therapeutic in itself; gave her brain back ("I was worried I'd lost my brain forever"); restored confidence she'd lost during severe brain fog.
5. **Walking with mantras** — when ready to start walking, she had to actively over-ride the "be careful, you'll crash" neural pathway with new mantras (*"walking makes me strong, walking makes me healthy"*) on repeat. Required for her to break the conditioned-fear cycle.
6. **HRT (perimenopause)** — late addition, after she found research showing benefit in some perimenopausal LC patients. **Big shift** for the brain fog specifically. Hadn't planned ever to take HRT.

## Recovery character

- Not a single "yay-I'm-better" moment. Gradual realisation: doing things again without the negative aftermath, doing things again without the *fear* of negative aftermath.
- Recovery defined as **walking up a mountain without the fear-of-PEM internal monologue**, then experiencing slight tiredness afterward and recognising it as normal-fitness-fatigue rather than as PEM warning.

## What she'd tell past Jules

- You **can** get better. Hold the belief — it's load-bearing through the dark days.
- You'll learn things along the way that will create the life you actually want. Recovery is teaching, not just removing illness.
- Trust yourself. You'll know yourself better than anyone else by the end of this — listen to those messages from within.
- Be self-compassionate. The competitive-perfectionist tendency many fatigue-illness patients share ("I'm going to be the best at recovery") is *itself* the obstacle.
