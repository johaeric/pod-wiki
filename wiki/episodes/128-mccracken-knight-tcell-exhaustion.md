# Episode 128 — McCracken & Knight — T-Cell Exhaustion Research

**Nigel McCracken** — COO, Virax Biolabs (immune-profiling diagnostics). Background: 30 years drug development, oncology and infectious disease.
**Dr Sean Knight** — academic respiratory clinician, Lydia Becker Institute, Manchester, working with Prof Tracy Hussell. Set up the **CIRO study** (Manchester longitudinal Covid cohort) early in the pandemic.

**Backstory — CIRO study:** Recruited acute-Covid hospital patients and followed them through recovery. Some made full recoveries from severe acute infection; others with milder acute disease did not. CIRO data being published as the partnership with Virax begins.

**Core hypothesis: T-cell exhaustion is a central LC mechanism.**
- T-cells are the cytotoxic arm of the adaptive immune system. In oncology, drug targets like PD-1 (e.g. pembrolizumab) reverse T-cell exhaustion in tumours.
- Same receptors (PD-1 and others) up-regulate in chronic viral infection. If they don't down-regulate after virus clearance, T-cells are stuck "keeping virus at bay" rather than clearing it.
- Result: chronic low-grade inflammation, oxidative stress, knock-on mitochondrial dysfunction → fatigue, brain fog. Mirrors the ME/CFS pattern.
- Prior viral history (EBV, CMV, herpes) may prime someone for T-cell exhaustion. Covid was the tipping straw for some, not the sole cause.

**Why it might explain population vulnerability:**
- SARS-CoV-2 is novel → minimal residual immunity → unusually high inflammatory load (especially first waves, almost every hospital patient had x-ray changes).
- High oxidative stress from acute inflammation can rewire adaptive immunity.

**Two studies launching:**
1. *Cytokine signature work* — looking for a T-cell-derived signature (IL-2, TNF-α, IFN-γ etc.) specific to LC vs other post-viral states. Distinct from existing serum-cytokine ELISAs because Virax separates PBMCs and tracks cell-of-origin (CD4/CD8/B/dendritic).
2. *Longitudinal LC cohort, 2 years* — tracking immune-system change against symptom change. Will also examine **B-cell affinity maturation** — CIRO data suggests LC patients refine antibody specificity differently from those who recover.

**Diagnostic ambition:** epitope-specific peptide mix specific to SARS-CoV-2 to stimulate PBMCs and read out T-cell phenotype. Could be paired with similar mixes for Lyme, CMV, EBV — same T-cell-exhaustion endpoint, virus-specific stimulus. Goal: CE-mark / FDA approved IVD that GPs can act on (vs current self-report symptom questionnaires). Early diagnosis matters because adaptive immunity may "rewire" if the state persists.

**Treatment implications:** even pre-drug, an exhaustion-positive readout could justify antioxidant/anti-inflammatory interventions to reduce oxidative load and protect mitochondria. Direct PD-1 blockade is too aggressive for a non-cancer indication.

**Subset story matters:** they emphasise LC is probably several conditions sharing symptoms. A T-cell-exhausted subgroup may exist alongside non-immune subgroups (deconditioning, lockdown sequelae, etc.). Diagnostic should *identify* the subgroup, not relabel everyone.

> "Long Covid... we're trying to be in a situation that we can react to that data but also provide meaningful tests because there is no diagnosis for long Covid — it's self-diagnosis or a questionnaire."
> — Nigel McCracken, ep #128
