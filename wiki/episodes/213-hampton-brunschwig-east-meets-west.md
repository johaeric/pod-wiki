**Margaret Hampton** — Doctor of Chinese Medicine (master's + doctorate). **Pierre Brunschwig** — MD, integrative-medicine internist; runs **Helios Integrated Medical** in Boulder, Colorado (33 years). They've worked together for 12+ years and have developed a paired East-West LC treatment model. Each language fills the other's blind spots.

## The framework — subtyping the patient

- "What version of LC do you have, and *who* has it?" — the Chinese-medicine question is baked into the medicine itself
- Western tools subtype by markers (T-cell exhaustion, latent virus reactivation, persistent viral antigens, etc.)
- Chinese tools subtype by *constitution* — Shao Yin pattern (kidney + heart channels) is the most common LC pattern they see: deficient kidney qi (translates roughly as adrenal fatigue) + deficient heart energy (connection, receiving, love)

## Pierre's "five things that make everything better"

1. Restorative sleep
2. A diet that works for *you*
3. A good inner life (meaning, purpose)
4. A good outer life (social connection that serves you)
5. Exercise

LC damages all five. Recovery requires re-establishing each.

## The adrenaline ↔ oxytocin teeter-totter

- Adrenaline side = "I'm not safe, do something now" — million-year-old brainstem program
- Oxytocin side = "I'm safe, I'm receiving connection" — where the healing systems engage
- LC patients are stuck on the adrenaline side
- Acupuncture is potent at flipping it: patients walk out *needle-drunk* (oxytocin-saturated)

## On latent viruses

- T-cell exhaustion → loss of immune competence to suppress latent viruses (EBV, CMV, herpes-family)
- "Jailbreak" pattern: multiple latent viruses reactivate
- Treatment: tip the balance — antivirals to suppress while rebuilding immune competence
- Persistent SARS-CoV-2 antigen subtype is real and emerging — needs better treatment data

## Blood building (Chinese medicine angle)

- Spike protein damages blood; many LC patients (especially pre-menopausal women) start blood-deficient
- **Quality protein from animal sources** — strong recommendation; plant protein is ~25% the concentration. Hard line: vegan diets struggle to support recovery in their experience.
- **Cooked food** (50% less digestive energy than raw)
- Often need to repair digestion first: stomach acid production, *H. pylori* testing, prebiotics + LC-specific probiotics

## Practical tools

- **Cordyceps mushrooms** daily for >1-year LC sufferers
- Korean research on **electro-stimulation acupuncture** that releases stem cells — they're using this on LC patients now
- Build a *team* — at least one conventional physician + one practitioner who can pivot strategy when something isn't working

## On framing

- "Long COVID isn't broken-machine medicine. It's a traumatic event that calls for a different paradigm."
- Goal: restore the patient to feeling like themselves (subjective measure that patients can rate within seconds)

## Notes for Eric

The Hampton-Brunschwig pairing is one of the more rigorous integrative-LC clinical models on this wiki. Three takeaways: (1) **the team-and-pivot model** is exactly the right mental model for navigating LC — no single practitioner has all the tools; (2) **Shao Yin (heart + kidney) deficiency** as the dominant LC pattern in their practice is a useful Chinese-medicine framing parallel to Western "POTS + autonomic dysfunction + adrenal fatigue + emotional flatness" — same patient, two languages; (3) the **anti-vegan / high-protein** stance is sharp and should be weighted carefully — it's clinical experience from one practice, not RCT data, but it converges with what other practitioners (e.g. Galland #191) say about animal-source nutrient density. The persistent-antigen subtype call is consistent with Polybio-led research (cross-ref ercipedia). Cordyceps is a low-risk addition worth flagging.
