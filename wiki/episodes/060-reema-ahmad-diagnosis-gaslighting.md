# #60 — Reema Ahmad — Diagnosis, Gaslighting & Life with Long Covid

[[reema-ahmad]] is an Indian NLP-based life coach, relationship and trauma counsellor, speaker, and published author. Three COVID infections (March 2020, December 2020, January 2022 Omicron) + spinal surgery in March 2022. Severe LC since (still recovering at recording).

The episode is a **life-with-LC and healthcare-gaslighting story** rather than a treatment-focused one — but contains specific practical adjuncts.

## Key narrative beats

- Brother in the UK NHS recognised LC early on and advocated for her
- Family-wide LC pattern: brother, father, multiple friends — uncommon coverage in one family
- Took 2 years before diagnosis ("up down up down")
- Indian-cultural pressure to not "make a fuss" + women-coded "just get on with it" pattern delayed diagnosis
- After surgery → physio → severe POTS / dysautonomia revealed as full LC
- Diagnosis felt like validation but also like grief (looking at others not recovering)

## What helps her (#60)

| Lever | Notes |
|---|---|
| POTS / dysautonomia medications (~1 month to titrate) | Specific drugs not named |
| Gut supplements: prebiotics + probiotics + digestive enzymes | ~3 months to start helping |
| **Withdrawing to her "bat cave"** when symptoms escalate — sound + light + stimulation cut, no calls/texts | Critical sensory-sensitivity tool |
| **Ice packs on neck and face** for the heating-up/temperature-dysregulation episodes | Practical, concrete |
| **Breathwork** | Used for the regulation phase post-trigger |
| Crying when needed | "It makes me feel lighter, I'm able to think straight" — explicitly endorses the cry as a parasympathetic shift |
| **Pacing — week planned in advance every Saturday** | Work intensity dropped from 4–5 sessions/day → 1 session every other day |

## Themes the episode adds to the wiki

- **Comparison guilt**: feeling "I'm not as bad as my friends with severe LC, so my LC isn't valid." Reema flags it as a common LC self-gaslighting pattern. Add to [[self-compassion]].
- **Sensory hypersensitivity at events**: she did her book launch and overheated 5 minutes in. Photos show her with hands over ears. The visible-vs-invisible-illness divide.
- **Identity loss + spontaneity loss** — common across LC narratives, articulated cleanly here.
- **Friendship attrition** — letting go of friends who couldn't bring curiosity. The remaining few are the ones who do the work.
- **Cross-cultural framing**: LC patients in cultures with strong "don't call attention to yourself" norms face additional barriers.
- **Romantic relationships during chronic illness** — new relationship as encouragement, but constant "what have you got yourself into" anxiety.
- **Forgiveness**: her own LC made her able to forgive a friend who'd ghosted her with LC the year before — symptom severity finally translated.
