# #014 — Long Covid Creativity 2021

A **compilation** episode of poems, songs, art, and reflections from long-covid patients around the world. Not an interview — Jackie introduces each contributor and plays/reads their work. Strong thread: **making something beautiful out of something horrible** as a coping mechanism. No intervention outcomes to log; the value here is the patient testimony itself and the new people surfaced.

## Contributors and what they contribute

| Contributor | Location | Form | Notable |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bri** | Edinburgh, UK | Poem ("recovery is a long time coming") | LC since March 2020, off work >1 year. Member of Lesley McNiven's creative-writing group. |
| **John Kennedy** | UK | Rhyme | Promotes the **microclot hypothesis** explicitly: *"the cause of long covid is microclots."* LC since Dec 2020. |
| **Chiara Berardelli** | Glasgow, UK | Song ("Tyrannosaurus") | Already on [[003-chiara-berardelli]]. Song personifies her LC as "Tyrannosaurus" — a way to externalise the headaches. |
| **Callum Modry** | UK | Poem | Long, dense reflection on time-perception under chronic illness. "I am the professional time-waster." |
| **Ruby Engel** | USA, age 56 | Poem ("the ties that bind") + 12 Months of LC carol (sung by friend Shola) | LC since March 2020. **Heart failure** as part of her LC picture. 19 months in. Lists her symptoms by month — high fever, dyspnea, headaches, purple toes, brain fog, phantom smells, fatigue, achy muscles, twitches/tremors, palpitations, **alopecia**, insomnia. |
| **Emily** | Netherlands, 31 | Cartoon art (Instagram) | Hypersensitivity to stimuli. Uses cartooning to explain the invisible illness to friends/family. |
| **Shireen Smith** | UK | Poem ("Dear Me") | Featured in a long-covid documentary. Letter from past-self to present-self. |
| **Daphne Stockings** | NL | Poem ("I forgot") + blog | Survived three electromasses; now LC for 21 months. Brain fog dominant. Discovered LC was a real condition via a Polish woman who read her blog. Nickname from the **compression stockings** she wears for pain. |
| **Long Covid Choir** | International | "Silent Night" | Led by **Zoe Chalena**. Members from 6yo to elderly. Zoom-rehearsed, recordings stitched together. *Zoe's own episode #31 had no captions on YouTube — see `_meta/failures.json`. This is currently the only direct mention of her work in the wiki.* |

## Why this episode matters to the wiki

- Surfaces **9 new patient voices** — most do not get their own dedicated episodes. The episode page is their primary mention; track them lightly via `mentioned-in-creativity-episode`.
- John Kennedy's microclot rhyme is a **theme-marker** for the microclot hypothesis (Resia Pretorius et al., not yet on the podcast directly at this point) — worth flagging for future ingest correlation.
- Ruby Engel's **heart-failure-with-LC** presentation is unusual and worth tracking in case she returns.
- Long Covid Choir is a notable named program/community. Episode #31 (Zoe) wasn't transcribed — the choir gets first appearance here.
- **Daphne's "I forgot" poem** is one of the more concrete brain-fog expressions across the wiki — clinical descriptions struggle to convey what the poem does in 30 seconds.
