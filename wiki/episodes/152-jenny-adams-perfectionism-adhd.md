# #152 — Jenny Adams — Embracing Self-Compassion: Navigating ADHD, Perfectionism, and Chronic Illness

Return appearance after [[145-jenny-adams-recovery]]. Recorded **unedited as a deliberate perfectionism exercise**. Topic: ADHD, perfectionism, all-or-nothing thinking, and the pattern of pushing through that lands so many people in ME/CFS / LC.

## Why this episode matters

- **Anchor episode for the perfectionism theme on the wiki.** Pairs with #94 (Jackie's recovery story) and #142 (3-year reflections); first explicit guest-led deep-dive on perfectionism-as-illness-driver.
- **Anchor articulation of perfectionism as a trauma response** (Gabor Maté lineage). Adams: "if I do x, y, z perfectly, therefore I will be loved / accepted / safe." Surfaced via live compassionate-inquiry exercise on Jackie.
- **"Done is better than perfect"** as a working motto for recovery + post-recovery.
- **First explicit on-air ADHD discussion** on the wiki. Adams: in her client base "probably 80% are women with undiagnosed ADHD" presenting with chronic illness — masking + shame + RSD + cycle-blind productivity → burnout pipeline. Adams's own ADHD assessment was the week of recording.
- **First explicit articulation of the 90-second emotion-wave** (the wave of an emotion typically passes in ~90s if you let it move through; suppression compounds it).

## Key tools / framings introduced

- **Disregulation-first protocol for overwhelm**: notice you're in freeze (procrastinating, can't get up, can't start); orient (look around the room; gentle movement; deeper breath; laughter); shift into ventral / sympathetic-doing; *then* approach the to-do list. "It's not the things, it's the nervous system."
- **Cycle-aware living for menstruating people** (and following moon cycles otherwise): match tasks to phase — ovulation = social/network/podcast; bleed = hibernate/admin. Don't fight your phase.
- **"Rest is productive"** — the more you push without breaks, the worse work quality gets. Mantra: "the less I do, the more I make."
- **"I'll be happy when" syndrome** — the productivity treadmill where the goalposts move with each achieved task. The feeling we're chasing usually isn't behind the task.
- **Cold shower 30s daily** — Adams uses this as a "I can do hard things" anchor (NS exercise + agency-building, not punishment-fitness).
- **Internal Family Systems** (already on wiki via [[145-jenny-adams-recovery]]) — her primary post-recovery dysregulation tool.

## Live demonstration in episode

Jackie was the guinea pig for a brief **compassionate-inquiry exercise**:

1. Surfaces a triggering example (the prior unedited episode #145).
2. Two underlying beliefs identified: *I could do better, therefore I should* + *fear of the unknown of leaving it raw*.
3. Traces back to early teenage years; "if I did things perfectly, parents would be proud → loved / accepted." Acceptance-for-being-imperfect would have been more useful.
4. Body scan: squirmy stomach, gentle rocking, hunched shoulders. Welcomes the sensation rather than soothing/pushing-away. Within ~90s the sensation moves from "melon to baked bean" and posture opens.

Useful as a worked example for any wiki reader trying to operationalise "feel it through" instead of "push it down."
