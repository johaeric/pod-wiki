# #154 — Sean Moran's Healing Journey from Chronic Fatigue

Australian (Melbourne), diagnosed CFS April 2012 at age ~22, **recovered ~2022 after a ~10–12 year arc**. **18th full recovery on the wiki.** Now coaches others. The most detailed **mould + heavy-metals + skull-infections route to recovery** logged so far — distinct shape from any prior recovery story.

## The arc in one paragraph

Vulnerable to colds/flu through teens; overworked legal job; one severe acute illness late 2011 / early 2012, never recovered. CFS for ~5–6 years before clawing back to ~50% function on a kitchen-sink protocol he can't disentangle. Working remotely during covid lockdown 2020 — air-conditioning split system riddled with mould — relapsed completely. That collapse triggered the methodical-detective phase: building biologist, GP-driven testing, mercury and lead high on a microtoxin panel, **MARCoNS** sinus colonisation, dental infection. Slow Christopher-Shade-style detox + drainage-funnel rehab (~18–20 months) + 30-day MARCoNS protocol + dental extraction = exponential recovery. Now full-time, exercising, plays sport.

## What Sean explicitly names as load-bearing

1. **Mould remediation + recognition that the AC unit was a chronic-exposure source.** Building biologist + integrative GP made the call.
2. **Functional lab testing → drainage-funnel rehab → slow gentle heavy-metal detox** (Christopher Shade / Quicksilver Scientific lineage). DIY-ing this without a knowledgeable practitioner made him much sicker; that was the inflection point to engage one. Skin-route + bowel/kidney/liver/lymph drainage *before* mobilisation; binders later.
3. **MARCoNS clearance** (multiple antibiotic-resistant coagulase-negative staphylococci colonising his sinuses). Tried multiple things, none worked, until a 30-day protocol from a US practitioner-recovered-from-this-himself. **"My brain just started to switch on again for the first time in 9–10 years."**
4. **Dental extraction (infected tooth)**. Woke up the next morning with a step-change in physical + cognitive energy — wiki's clearest single-event signal for **dental cavitations / chronic oral infections** as a hidden energy drain.

## What Sean tried that he doesn't credit (the early kitchen-sink phase)

In the first ~5–6 years he threw money + savings at: **IV vitamin C, ozone therapy, acupuncture, cupping, EFT tapping, homeopathy, brain retraining, chi gong, restorative yoga, chiropractic, hypnotherapy, graded exercise therapy, CBT, bioresonance therapy, matrix reimprinting, glutathione injections.** Doesn't strongly credit any single one in retrospect — frames the early phase as the "scattergun" approach common to anyone newly ill, and explicitly cautions against it. *"Some of them maybe contributed a little, it's hard to gauge. But I was always so focused on getting back to normal that 50% wasn't satisfying — and so I couldn't tell what was actually working."*

## Sean's advice to past-self

- Don't try to expedite. The kitchen-sink approach burns time and money before the right diagnostic frame surfaces.
- Find one practitioner who looks at *you*, not the label.
- Get tests run for individual drivers (mould, heavy metals, infections, oral) rather than chasing modality after modality.
- "Don't look backwards, you're not going that way." (From his social media — he found the quote useful.)
