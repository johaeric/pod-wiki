# #163 — Carrie Bailey: Immune Boosting + Elimination Diets (pt 2)

Continuation of [[162-carrie-bailey-functional-nutrition-pt1]]. Two main topics: **supplement scaffold** and **expanded elimination diet**.

## Bailey's foundational supplement stack

Built up like a house, not "kill-the-virus" first:

- **B-vitamins (B1, B2, B5, B6, B9; methylated caveat)** — every cell needs them for detox.
- **B12 — most-deficient nutrient.** No toxic upper limit. Can need up to 5,000 μg/day (vs US RDA ~50 μg).
- **Magnesium glycinate** — broad enzymatic role, tolerable form.
- **Buffered vitamin C** (esterified or buffered, NOT ascorbic acid from GMO corn). Up to 4,000+ mg/day; LC symptom set overlaps with scurvy.
- **Zinc sulfate 7 mg twice daily** as preferred (sulfur compound aids detox + clears toxic copper). Picolinate (15-30 mg capsules) as backup.
- **Lysine 2,000 mg twice daily** — first amino acid depleted under viral load; missed by most practitioners.

## Antiviral / nervous-system herbs (alcohol-free tinctures only)

- **Lemon balm (Melissa)** — antiviral, calming.
- **Cat's claw** — antiviral, no Herxheimer reaction.
- **Hibiscus tea** — for adrenal fatigue (HR-up-late-night, salt cravings, reverse-energy pattern).
- **Licorice** — adrenal support.
- **Ashwagandha** — wired-but-tired, sleep, breakfast + dinner dosing.

For super-sensitive patients: literally start with one drop on skin → titrate by one drop daily. Reactivity ≠ harm; reactivity often = pathogen-die-off.

## Vitamin D scepticism

Bailey takes a **minority position**: pulls vitamin D out of routine recommendation because it's fat-soluble (her thesis: liver fat-stagnation already a problem). Snapshot blood-test markers don't tell you what cells are doing. If 1,000 IU helps you, fine — but "everyone's piling on D" while ignoring vitamin C, zinc, lysine. Try and notice symptoms vs Mega-dosing. (Logged as one minority stance on vitamin D; cf. [[hormonal-dysfunction]] / Khan / others on this podcast who recommend it actively.)

## Expanded elimination diet

Beyond gluten + dairy:
- **Eggs** (often appear on intolerance panels; vaccine-related speculation)
- **Soy, corn, vinegars** (vinegars "pickle the liver")
- **Citric acid** specifically (NOT citrus fruit) — flavor enhancer made from GMO corn grown with mould → triggers histamine + mould-sensitised people. Hides in canned tomatoes, condiments, supplements.
- **"Natural flavors"** in US labelling can contain MSG (excitotoxin to brain).

Key reframe: **avoiding by category is wrong** ("I'm on low-histamine so no citrus") — avoid the actual offender (citric acid) and keep the healing food (lemon).

## CBC-with-differential reading

For self-tracking immune state, Bailey's functional ranges (narrower than conventional cancer-screen ranges):
- **WBC**: 5–7 functional. >10 = active infection (need C/zinc/lysine). <5 = chronic suppression — needs *more* support.
- **Pattern in heavy viral load**: high neutrophils + low lymphocytes.
- Eosinophils/basophils elevated → adrenal/liver/parasite suspicion.

## Reintroduction philosophy

"Cheating" reframed as "going off plan." Even strict patients will go off plan; the data point is *did you crash as hard as you did before?* If your worst-case off-plan reaction is now milder, that's recovery progress regardless of strict diet adherence.

- **First wiki anchor for "build the immune-system foundation" supplement stack** — distinct from Teitelbaum's SHINE/HRG80/Recovery Factors approach. Bailey's stack is OTC and cheaper but wider (8+ daily items).
- **Anti-citric-acid finding** is wiki-new. Plausibly useful for the histamine-reactive cohort.

## Eric-relevant takeaways

- **Lysine 2 g twice daily** is a contained low-cost experiment if Eric ever wants to add to his stack — Bailey's claim is it's first-depleted under viral load.
- **B12 high-dose (~5,000 μg/day)** sublingual is a low-harm OTC trial worth considering.
- **Buffered vitamin C** specifically (not ascorbic acid) — Bailey's distinction is uncommon but cheap to honour in supplement choice.
- **Cat's claw + lemon balm** pair as a low-Herxheimer antiviral starter; Bailey emphasises alcohol-free tinctures (alcohol in tinctures hits liver + brain — the two LC-vulnerable organs).
- Bailey's diet stance overlaps Eric's existing antihistamine stack: **citric acid hidden in supplements + jarred tomatoes** is worth a label-read for any histamine-reactive day.
- *Caveat*: Bailey is N=1 practitioner, no podcast cohort outcome data yet. Logged as a `recommended` row on each named intervention.
