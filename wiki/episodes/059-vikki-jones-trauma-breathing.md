# #59 — Vikki Jones — Long Covid, Trauma & Breathing

[[vikki-jones]] is a US-based personal trainer, dietician, wellness coach, and former international athlete (modern pentathlon, triathlon). 27 months into LC at recording. **Co-founder of [[long-covid-breathing]] with [[jackie-baxter]]**. Trained as a breathwork instructor under **Patrick McKeown** (Oxygen Advantage / Buteyko Method). The episode is the **anchor practical-breathwork-protocol episode**.

## Trauma framing

> "Long covid is one of those things that happens to us without our consent. That is the definition of trauma."

The breath connects to trauma:
- Trauma drives sympathetic over-activation
- Breathwork is the fastest direct lever to switch the nervous system into rest-and-digest
- "The quickest way to change your biochemistry is to change your breath. Faster than thoughts, faster than food, faster than supplements, faster than medication."

## The Control Pause (CP) test

Sit calmly. Breathe in normally through nose, breathe out normally, then **pinch nose and time how long until first urge to breathe**. (Not maximum hold — first urge.) 

- **>40s**: healthy breather
- **20–40s**: borderline
- **<20s**: dysfunctional breather (eligible for breathwork program)
- LC patients often start at 3–8s

Half the CP determines the breath-hold length used in subsequent exercises.

## Adapted Buteyko/Oxygen-Advantage protocol for LC

### 1. Many Small Breath Holds (calming)

- Inhale + exhale normally through nose
- Pinch nose, hold for **half your CP** (e.g. 4s if CP=8s)
- Release, breathe normally through nose for 10–20s
- Repeat for 5 minutes
- Indicators of effect: more saliva in mouth, warmer hands → parasympathetic shift achieved
- Use during panic attacks, anxiety, PEM-onset

### 2. Cadence Breath (vagal tone)

- Inhale shorter than exhale
- Healthy: 4 in / 6 out
- LC: scale to capacity — 2/4, 3/5, even 1/3
- Don't pinch nose

### 3. Diaphragmatic Breathing (biomechanics)

- Hands on either side of rib cage
- Inhale → hands move out (ribs expand)
- Exhale → hands return
- Or: book on belly, push it up on inhale
- Targets the diaphragm muscle that LC patients have decoupled from in upper-chest panic-pattern breathing

### 4. Box Breathing (modified)

- Standard 4–4–4–4 unsuitable for LC (causes air hunger)
- Modified 1–1–1–1 → calming, achievable

### 5. Mouth Taping for Sleep

- Aim: prevent night-time mouth breathing (negates daytime nasal-breathing work)
- 3M tape across lips, NOT zipped shut — small piece as a reminder
- Start while watching TV; build up
- Side benefit: morning saliva (good dental); reduces snoring

## Core principle

**Nasal breathing 100% of the time** (except talking and eating). This is the platform under all other breathing exercises.

## Specific cautions

- Standard breathwork apps and protocols (e.g. classic Wim Hof, Buteyko intensive) can WORSEN LC patients — Vikki initially crashed trying these.
- Air hunger is scary and *not* therapeutic for LC patients. Back off if any exercise produces it.
- Pacing applies to breathwork — too many sessions, too long, will trigger PEM.
