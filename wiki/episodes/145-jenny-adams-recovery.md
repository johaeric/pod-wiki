# #145 — Jenny Adams: recovery from ME/CFS

**Recovery story #17 on the wiki** (full recovery from ME/CFS, ~6 years stable). Now 33; took 8 years from glandular fever at 15 to a CFS diagnosis. Trained in **compassionate inquiry** with Gabor Maté; works as chronic-illness coach (`@chronicillnesscoach` on Instagram); runs the membership programme [[heal-good]].

## Triggering arc

- **Age 15** — glandular fever; never fully recovered. Adult-life of "everyone says they're tired, I must just be an adult."
- **2015** — car accident; nervous system already on hyper-alert. The accident was the spoonful that broke the bucket. **Wheelchair-bound for 3–4 years; deconditioned.**
- **8 years to ME/CFS diagnosis** — repeatedly told "depression," tried antidepressants, missed [[adhd]] (now diagnosed; she sees the link clearly).
- **2017** — saw a private doctor (infectious-diseases interest) who took a 1-hour history, named the trauma-stack pattern (father died at 13, abusive relationships, glandular fever, work/landlord chaos). Started a multi-component recovery.
- **2018** — talk therapy + Pilates + vegan diet + safe movement + boundary work.
- **May 2018** — last time using mobility scooter (Beyoncé concert).
- **August 2019** — partying solo in Ibiza. Recovery year.
- **2024 (now)** — fully recovered ~5 years, working as a coach.

## What she identifies as load-bearing

1. **Stopped fighting her body, got curious about pain** — listened to the screaming knee instead of telling it to be quiet. The "tantruming toddler" framing: pain quietens when attended to.
2. **A 1-hour history-taking by a doctor who *listened*** — first time anyone connected the trauma stack to the illness.
3. **Pilates in a chronic-illness-friendly studio (Bristol)** — instructors said "if you just need to lie down for the hour, that's fine." Group of others with chronic illness. **Sense of belonging + safe movement** — first time movement wasn't push-through.
4. **Vegan diet (6 years stable)** — went from cake-factory dairy buyer (who'd been buying 300,000 L of dairy/year) to one-week vegan trial; relapsed dairy → 3 days nausea → kept vegan. "Like someone put my batteries back in." Inflammation quieted, then movement became possible.
5. **Talk therapy** — discovered the pattern of attracting controlling/abusive partners; "if I keep ending up here I'm the common denominator." Generic talk therapy, not somatic, but enough to be heard weekly.
6. **Boundary work** — getting out of codependent relationships, ditching unsupportive friends, naming her **luteal phase** as a real cycle pattern with permission to ask for what she needs.
7. **Internal Family Systems (Dick Schwartz, *No Bad Parts*)** — she met Schwartz a couple of months ago. Uses IFS to handle dysregulation: "what does this part need? Can the protectors move aside?" Drives off in a huff after argument → IFS exercise in the car → home, regulated, "when you said that, that really upset me."
8. **Compassionate Inquiry training (Gabor Maté)** — now working with PTSD / cPTSD clients.

## Distinctive framings

- **"My nervous system was wired for chaos and trauma — familiar = safe, even when familiar is sickness."** Why patients sometimes seek out chaos unconsciously.
- **The boiling frog** — slow stress accumulation goes unnoticed until you're cooked.
- **"Recovery isn't linear; the wins start to outnumber the bad days."**
- **PTSD/cPTSD reminder she gives to clients** — "you have been through some of the worst horrific things known to man and you survived. Whatever's going on now, you survived worse with no tools — you can survive this with the tools you have."
- **"The way you got here is not the way you'll get out."** Pre-illness life patterns are not the recovery template.
