# #58 — Fiona Jones — the LISTEN Trial

[[fiona-jones]] is Professor of Rehabilitation Research at St George's University of London, founder/CEO of **Bridges Self-Management** (social enterprise), and **co-Chief Investigator of the LISTEN trial** alongside Prof Monica Busse (Cardiff). NIHR-funded; started August 2021; results expected Autumn 2023.

## What LISTEN is

A **co-designed self-management support intervention** for long covid:

- **6 remote 1:1 sessions** with a trained "LISTEN practitioner" (physio, OT, nurse, assistant psychologist, psychologist)
- A **co-designed book** ("a community in your hand") — not an app, deliberately, because the patient codesign group rejected another app
- 8-hour training for practitioners, **co-delivered with people with LC**
- Patient-narrative-driven; not formulaic; explicitly resists "fix and sort" reflex
- Eight core principles of the intervention (not detailed in episode)

## Codesign process

- 30+ patients with LC + 10 clinicians (some of whom have LC)
- Codesign participants explicitly **excluded from the trial** (becomes therapeutic in itself; would confound)
- Patients shaped book content, framing, language; clinicians trained on it cover-to-cover

## Trial design

- **N=550 participants**
- RCT: LISTEN intervention vs usual care
- **No positive COVID test required** (unusual in LC research; explicitly accommodates the early-2020 cohort)
- ≥12 weeks symptoms
- Self-referral permitted
- 3-month outcome window
- Sites: all Wales (central practitioner pool); England — Wigan, Coventry, South Warwickshire, parts of London, Yorkshire, Essex; opening: Oxford, Sheffield
- Wales has central self-referral; England limited to trial-site catchments

## Theory of change

The trial is **not a drug trial**. It is testing whether a structured, narrative-driven, co-designed self-management support package, delivered remotely by trained practitioners, improves outcomes vs whatever the patient's local "usual care" looks like. The **mechanism is the relationship + the book + the practitioner-as-detective stance**, not a specific symptom-targeted treatment.
