# #176 — Carl Robot — When Your Body Becomes a Stranger

UK English teacher (Leeds), former Head of PE in Bristol — runner, cyclist, swimmer. Got COVID summer 2020 (mild — never tested). Long COVID symptoms emerged through autumn into 2021: breathlessness, weight gain, swollen abdomen, then a major A&E presentation (chest pain, breathing crisis) on the last day of school year 2021. ECG and lung function eventually normal; head teacher noticed POTS-pattern HR/BP swing on standing. Confirmed long-COVID dx via Leeds LC clinic in early 2023. Recovered to running marathons within ~12 months.

## Carl's stack — what he tried

- **Cold-water immersion** — saltwater seapool in Westwood Ho! (Devon) on family holiday, frost on ground, Feb 2023. Felt peace immediately. Continued as **daily cold showers** in Leeds when sea-pool wasn't possible (per Andrew Huberman + Søberg: end on cold).
- **Yoga nidra** — discovered via Huberman; afternoon sessions to combat crash fatigue.
- **Breathwork** — initially Wim-Hof-adjacent (he's hazy on which method now); later something gentler. *Note Jackie's caveat: she warns guests away from Wim Hof in active LC.*
- **Electrolytes** every morning — helped some.
- **A pile of supplements** — about 7. Stopped them all, no difference. Concluded they weren't doing anything for him.

## The thing that landed: SimPro probiotic clinical trial

Three-month placebo-controlled trial at **Sheffield Hallam University** (gut-bacteria probiotic SymProve). Cognitive baseline test put him in bottom 7% nationally (he fell asleep doing it). After three months, marked improvement: back on the bike, 3-stone weight gain reversed alongside fasting, dysautonomia lifting. Was on the active arm. After trial ended, he kept taking SymProve for a fourth month (their gift), then **switched to making homemade kefir** to maintain gut microbiome.

> "What got me back to baseline was the combination — but if I had to pick one thing, the probiotic trial was the inflection point." — Carl Robot, ep #176

## The marathon punctuation

By Jan 2024 he was running. Started a couch-to-marathon programme to mark "I'm not going back from here." Ran first marathon in ~6 months. Planned 45-mile **Green Man Challenge** around Bristol for May 2026.

Key reflection: graded exercise told to LC patients without their other recovery work doesn't work. Carl's own escalation worked because every other piece had been laid first; he listened to his body and would back off when needed.
