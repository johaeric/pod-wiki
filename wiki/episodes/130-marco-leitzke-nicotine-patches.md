# Episode 130 — Dr Marco Leitzke — Using Nicotine Patches for Long Covid

German ICU consultant (intensive care + narcotics) at Helios Klinikum Leipzig with a long-running research interest in **nicotinic acetylcholine receptors (nAChR)** in critical illness / sepsis. Came into LC research because acute-covid clinical pictures resembled sepsis and his nAChR work mapped onto a hypothesis for how SARS-CoV-2 disrupts host physiology.

## Hypothesis

- Spike protein has RNA-sequence similarity to **snake-venom toxins** (cobratoxin, bungarotoxin) — Jean-Pierre Changeux's group, Pasteur (Paris). Those toxins are potent because of their affinity for nAChR.
- By analogy, spike protein **binds and blocks nAChR** without producing chemical signals — silent occupation rather than active reaction.
- Every cell in the body has nAChR (~12,000 per cell × ~10 billion cells). If a fraction of receptors are blocked across many cell types, the autonomic and immune coordination of the body breaks down — the heterogeneous symptom profile of LC.
- **Nicotine has ~30× higher affinity** for nAChR than spike does. Transdermal nicotine should compete spike off the receptors over time.

## Key clinical claims

- **Smoking ≠ nicotine.** A cigarette delivers ~8,000 toxins; nicotine is just one of them. The reputation problem hides the substance.
- **Transdermal nicotine is non-addictive.** Slow venous (not pulmonary-arterial) rise, monoamine-oxidase clearance happens before dopamine spikes. UK NICE (2013) statement: 5-year transdermal nicotine without health risk.
- **Dose protocol Leitzke recommends:** start at the *lowest* commercially available patch (~7–7.5 mg/24h), then **further reduce by covering** (NOT cutting — the gel matrix gets destroyed). 1.75 or 3.5 mg/24h for 3–5 days, habituate, then escalate cautiously. Avoid going above 14 mg routinely; 21 mg "not advisable."
- **Long not high.** Time matters more than dose. Some users respond after weeks; some need months.
- **Expected initial worsening.** As nicotine displaces spike from receptors, freed viral particles trigger an infection-like flare for a few days. This is the immune system finally seeing the antigen. Don't stop in week 1.
- Mechanism explains "post-vaccination syndrome" symmetry: vaccine spike epitope can also occupy nAChR.

## Safety notes

- Nicotine is **vasodilatory** (raises intracellular NO in endothelium), not constrictive — opposite of the popular myth.
- Not a carcinogen first-rank, may have *co-carcinogenic* effects with existing carcinogens (unproven). For weeks-to-months use in chronic disabling illness, Leitzke argues the risk-benefit clearly favours trial.
- Approved adjunct for stable angina patients (cardiac safety profile is well-characterised).
- **Avoid combining with monoamine-oxidase inhibitors** ("quite a dangerous thing"). Otherwise existing meds can stay.
- Antibiotics not needed; Paxlovid not needed (and may *blunt* the immune system's ability to recognise freed virus).

## Status

- All evidence is **anecdotal** — tens of thousands of users worldwide via word-of-mouth, no published RCT yet. Leitzke and German collaborators (Berlin Charité, Berchtesgaden clinics) trying to launch one — barriers: medication-law (not device-law) regulatory hurdles + funding.
- He receives no money from patch manufacturers or pharma; explicitly states this on the episode.
- Wiki position: **hypothesis-of-record + accessible cheap candidate intervention** worth tracking. Logged as `recommended` from the clinician — no recovered-patient outcomes presented in this episode.
