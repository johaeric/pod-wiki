# #151 — Dr Lydia Knutson — Chiropractic Approach to Long Covid

US chiropractor in her 24th year of practice (Lydian Chiropractic, Cambridge Massachusetts), creator of the **Axial Stability Method (ASM)** — a low-force chiropractic protocol blending established low-force techniques with acupuncture-meridian / chakra Eastern Energy Medicine work. Adapted ASM into an LC protocol summer 2020. Has now treated **150+ LC and post-vaccine patients** with "meaningful improvement" common and "many dramatic recoveries." Two small completed studies; planning a controlled trial.

## Why this episode matters

- **First chiropractic-anchored LC protocol on the wiki.** Distinct from the Perrin Technique (osteopathic lymphatic drainage, [[160-dr-raymond-perrin-lymphatic-pt1]]) but adjacent — both target ANS rebalancing via low-force spinal work.
- **Hard outcome data from a small open-label series**: n=13 LC patients tracking 40 symptoms; 67 severe symptoms reported; **average 18 visits → 90% reduction in severe-symptom severity**; fatigue dropped from 80th-percentile US-population norm to **32nd percentile** (less tired than the average American at end of treatment).
- **Phase 1 (8 visits, parasympathetic / upper-cervical work) alone produced ~70% reduction in severe symptoms** — front-loaded results.
- Anchor articulation of the **"living systems return to blueprint when impediment is removed"** framing — different vocabulary from neuroplastic-symptoms model ([[146-dr-becca-kennedy]]) but operationally similar.

## Key claims and framings

- **Hierarchy of systems**: every body wants to restore order in the same sequence — parasympathetic first (vagus / upper cervical), sympathetic second (thoracic-spine concordances mapping organ systems), then biomechanical (old neuromuscular injuries) in phases 2–3.
- **Rain-barrel analogy** for predisposition: chronic stressors raise the water level invisibly; covid is "an 800-lb gorilla cannonballing into the rain barrel" — overflows at each person's individual weakest point. Hence the variability in LC presentation.
- **"You've seen one LC patient, you've seen one LC patient"** — every protocol must be individually tuned, but the systems-sequence is universal.
- **Holistic-medicine licence to experiment**: "the lower the possibility of harm, the more freedom there is to try things." Argues this is the gap mainstream research is missing — outcomes research on low-harm modalities.
- Three-hour intake; long-history-taking surfaces predisposing chronic illness (mono, lyme, fibromyalgia, vaccine reactions). "Most LC patients, when you dig deep enough, have some history of chronic illness."
- Acknowledges co-existence of brain-retraining / breathwork / cold-plunge work — frames ASM as **treatment** (you come in for sessions; not a daily practice).

## Notable cases mentioned

- **Lawyer (LC patient #1, late 2020)**: bedridden post-acute, crushing fatigue + brain fog, couldn't walk a flight of stairs. ~7–8 visits, then ran up two flights and reported "all better."
- **32-year-old man**: night sweats so severe he draped ice-water-soaked towels over himself for 4 hours nightly to sleep. Resolved in protocol.
- **Woman with movement disorder + visual processing deficit** (couldn't turn head left/right while walking, legs "felt like jelly"): danced in heels at her niece's wedding after phases 1–3.
- **New patient**: 55 doctors / 3 continents over 3 years post-2021 covid booster injury; movement disorder resembling Parkinson's. Started ASM the week of recording.
