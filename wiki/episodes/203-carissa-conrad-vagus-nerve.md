Carissa Conrad — Doctor of Physical Therapy and therapeutic-pain specialist; recovered from LC after May 2022 infection. Now coaches LC/CFS recovery internationally with **vagus-nerve stimulation** (VNS) as the primary tool. Sells kits via carissadpt.com.

## The vagus nerve — the headlines

- Latin *vagus* = "wandering"; longest nerve in the body, two of them
- Carries **75% of parasympathetic nervous system fibres**
- Modulates HR, BP, digestion, immune function, hormones, HPA axis, neuroinflammation
- Cut the vagus in animal models → inflammation cascade essentially stops

## Why VNS for LC

- LC patients are typically stuck in sympathetic overdrive (often pre-illness too)
- Vagal "tone" can be congenitally low + further blunted by childhood experiences
- Re-toning the vagus enables parasympathetic time → healing state

## Methods of VNS

- **Surgical implant** (FDA-approved for epilepsy, treatment-resistant depression, post-stroke rehab, rheumatoid arthritis) — not relevant here
- **Transcutaneous (tVNS)** — through skin
  - **Auricular branch** — in ear (most common)
  - **Cervical branch** — neck
  - **Ultrasound** — newer, penetrates deeper than electrical; available in her shop
- **Internal stimulation** — humming, breathing (5.5-second paced breathing has research support), cold exposure
- **TENS units** are the most cost-effective auricular tVNS option (~$38 in US)

## Carissa's recovery sequence

- Low-dose **LDN** + low-dose **SSRI** "got me off the couch"
- **VNS via TENS** "got me out of the house" — the inflection
- Took 8 months from couch to 5 hours/week of work

## Common mistakes with TENS for VNS

- Wrong parameters — out-of-box presets are *not* correct for VNS
- Going too fast (start small)
- Pushing too far when feeling better → crash
- Pre-set TENS units (with "back" / "shoulder" buttons) often can't be tuned to VNS parameters — buy a unit that lets you set mode/pulse-rate/pulse-width manually

## Practical kit notes

- Standard TruMedic 1077 ~$38 on Amazon (US)
- Ear clips needed; aloe-vera gel works as well as proprietary gel
- ~$20 more for a manually-adjustable unit is worth it over a pre-set one
