# #010 — Amanda Finley

**Guest**: [[amanda-finley]] — archaeologist (and former opera singer) in Kansas City, Missouri. Founder/admin of the **Covid-19 Long-Haulers Discussion Group** on Facebook (~14,000 members at recording). First-wave (6 March 2020). Reinfected October 2020; suspected Delta third wave 2021. Recording ≈late summer 2021.

## What this episode is

The **US-side parallel** to ep #9: same patient-movement story, but anchored in the Kansas City / Missouri / Federal-policy context. Brings the **socio-economic** picture sharply into focus — Amanda lost her home and is camping in the woods at time of recording.

## US-specific structural barriers (the new content)

- **No insurance** during the first 8 months of LC (uninsured first wave is a large cohort).
- **Federal post-covid clinics require a positive PCR** — locks out almost the entire first wave.
- **No paid sick leave (FMLA is unpaid)** + huge gig-economy workforce → many lose income, many become homeless.
- **Disability claims** typically rejected first try; 6–12 months even when eventually granted.
- **Texas (and similar states)**: adult males can only access Medicaid via prior disability approval — practical impossibility.
- **NIH $1.15B** announced for LC research; Amanda's worry: established institutions with grant-writing departments will absorb the funds for tangentially-related work that won't help patients.

## Documented advocacy and connections

- Featured in an **RT documentary** (Jan 2021, Alexei Bykov) — that broadcast triggered messages from Cuba, Peru, Venezuela, Colombia, North Africa, Liberia. They send groceries to a member in Liberia.
- Names [[patient-led-research-collaborative]] (Hannah Davis, Lisa McCorkell, Elisa Perego) as the group most worth funding directly.
- Names **Ziyad Al-Aly** (Washington University St Louis) as a researcher-to-watch — published a Nature paper estimating ~50% of covid+ go on to have ongoing symptoms.
- **Ed Yong** (Atlantic, Pulitzer-winning covid coverage) is name-checked for taking the socio-economic LC story seriously.

## Symptom + healthcare picture

- **Tachycardia** documented on 2-week Holter; cardiologist initially blamed anxiety (she pushed back hard).
- **Echocardiogram** showed valve regurgitation + leaflet inflammation — written off as "normal" until she demanded a meticulous-note-taking colleague-cardiologist code it properly. Pattern she's seeing in many group members.
- Lifelong sleep disorder pre-covid; her sleep-med doctor left practice; new doc told her "intractable, learn to live with not sleeping."
- Mid-pandemic O₂ sat dipping into 60s–80s while the public-health line was "go to ER if <95%" — she stayed home because there were no beds.

## Group operation

- **14,000+ members** at recording.
- One-third of them got sick in **March 2020** (first wave with no PCR) — still the sickest cohort in her group.
- Lost a 40-year-old member ("Jake") on 4 Sep 2021 — the first death in the group; no prior medical history.
- **Symptom list** kept open and accreting; chipped teeth, tinnitus, valve regurgitation all surfaced this way.

## Notable arguments

- **Listen to patient-led research first** before designing new grants.
- **Mask-wearing should be normalised culturally** as in East Asia post-SARS — wear a good-seal mask if you're symptomatic at all.
- **Vaccinated-people behaving as immortal** is the new transmission engine.
- **Reach out for help; people will help.** "Some people will be jerks; ignore them."
