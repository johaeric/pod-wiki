# #167 — Dr Jacob Teitelbaum: Reclaiming Health and Hope (Return)

Return episode (~2.5 years after [[055-jacob-teitelbaum-shine-protocol]]). Less time on the SHINE walkthrough; more on **specific symptom-targeted interventions** that have come into Teitelbaum's stack since 2022. Promotes new book *You Can Heal from Long Covid* and the more detailed *From Fatigued to Fantastic* (blue-cover).

## Updated mechanism story

- LC = "spinoff from the same lying fire" as ME/CFS / fibromyalgia / post-viral chronic pain.
- **Hypothalamic circuit-breaker** is still the central frame — virus hijacks mitochondrial energy machinery for replication; body shuts down energy production to starve virus; if viral fragments persist, the energy production doesn't switch back on. Hypothalamus controls sleep + hormones + autonomic function; tripping it cascades into POTS, fatigue, insomnia, MCAS, central sensitisation.
- TBI / concussion → **44% have overt hypothalamic-pituitary dysfunction** post-injury. Same circuit-breaker; different trigger (brain inflammation).

## New / refined interventions

### Pain (Teitelbaum splits LC pain into ~7 named categories)

- **Muscle pain** (the bulk of fibromyalgia / LC pain) — low-energy muscles stuck in shortened position; 100s-of-tender-knots hours-to-months pattern.
- **Brain pain** — chronic pain → brain amplification → makes any pain worse. **PEA (palmitoylethanolamide) 1,200–2,400 mg/day** is the standout new recommendation. Cites a recent study on disc/compression pain showing ~70% pain reduction at 3 months. *Use a high-absorption / micronised form* — non-micronised PEA is poorly absorbed.
- **Pair with luteolin 70 mg/day for 3 months** for smell + taste loss recovery.
- Curamin (curcumin-based) as starter for any LC pain.
- Migraine: **vitamin B2 riboflavin 400 mg/day for 6 weeks decreases migraines 67–70%**; plus magnesium; plus food sensitivities; plus iMatrix-family medications taken *before* migraine reaches eye/ear-tenderness threshold (after that the stomach is shut down and absorption fails).
- **Lyrica/gabapentin → over a dozen-step nerve-pain medication sequence** in his book chapter (CRPS/RSD email request also referenced).
- Mast-cell activation sensitivities — PEA refills the same exhausted "superhero molecule" hypothalamus uses for stress response.

### Energy

- **HRG80 red ginseng** — Teitelbaum's published study (188 people, half post-viral CFS/fibromyalgia): **60% improved**, average **61% energy increase, ~70% stamina increase** on chewable tablets. *Half a chewable = full effect of 2-4 capsules; cuts cost ~75%.* Available on iherb internationally.
- **Recovery Factors** — unique poly-peptide product; two studies showing dramatic post-viral CFS reversal + improved gamma globulin levels. UK/EU shipping via recoveryfactors.com (then the EU/UK button); not available US-direct (Teitelbaum wishes it were).
- **D-ribose 5 g 2-3x/day** — ATP precursor.
- **Magnesium glycinate** — broad enzymatic role.

### Sleep

- **EP120 sustained-release melatonin** (US brand specific).
- **Revitalizing Sleep Formula** (multi-herb).
- Diphenhydramine (Benadryl).
- Chamomile *flower buds*, not tea bags.

### Adrenal

- **Adrenoplex** (US/Canada) — for "hangry" pattern (irritable when hungry = adrenal fatigue).

### Brain-retraining

- **Gupta** (UK), **ANS Rewire** (Dan Neuffer, Australia), **DNRS** (Annie Hopper) — Teitelbaum recommends all three for the hypothalamic-circuit-breaker reset, particularly for patients who don't tolerate or can't afford pharmaceuticals.

## Cost / access notes

- **iherb.com** as the international/EU/UK supplement source — Teitelbaum mentions a European warehouse for local shipping.
- US-only: `endfatigue.com` for some products.
- *Free email service*: `fatiguedoc@gmail.com` will send free condition-specific information sheets (LC-organ-damage, shortness-of-breath, neuropathy chapter, CRPS/RSD chapter, etc.).

## Why this episode matters

- **Wiki-new anchor for PEA + luteolin** — for *both* smell-and-taste loss (1,200 mg PEA + 70 mg luteolin × 3 months) *and* brain pain / central sensitisation / mast-cell-sensitivities.
- **HRG80 red ginseng study results** are wiki-new (60% improved, ~61% energy increase). Promotes a much narrower N=188 evidence-base than SHINE itself — but this is the strongest single-supplement N on the wiki for CFS/fibromyalgia/post-viral.
- Reinforces Teitelbaum's position as the **"treat everything at once"** counterweight to [[041-joachim-gerlach-vedicinals-9]] / [[045-keith-littlewood-hormones-thyroid]] / [[046-gez-medinger-patient-advocate]] [[polypharmacy-warning]].
- The **pain taxonomy (muscle / brain / nerve / migraine / CRPS / etc.)** is wiki-new as an explicit framework. Distinct from the [[053-deepak-ravindran-pain]] nociplastic-only framing — Teitelbaum is more reductive (different pain-types respond to different interventions; not all chronic LC pain is nociplastic).
- **Vitamin B2 riboflavin 400 mg/day for migraines (67–70% frequency reduction)** is wiki-new and a contained low-cost trial for the LC-with-migraine subset.

## Eric-relevant takeaways

- **PEA (palmitoylethanolamide)** is the most concretely-actionable wiki-new item for Eric's stack: contained, cheap, low-harm, specific evidence base. Eric's pain load is light, but if any neuropathic / nerve-pain / mast-cell sensitivities emerge, this is the first item to try.
- **HRG80 red ginseng** at half a chewable daily is a contained low-cost trial. Energy + stamina were the primary outcomes; not POTS-specific. Worth knowing about; not load-bearing for Eric's autonomic-dominant phenotype.
- Teitelbaum's commercial-supplement engagement remains a confounder. The underlying SHINE + brain-retraining structure is more durable than any specific brand call-out.
- **B2 riboflavin 400 mg + magnesium for migraines** — useful note if Eric ever develops migraine on top of POTS.
- *Important caveat*: Teitelbaum's strict line that "the doctor who says you can't get better is right — find someone who can" is rhetorically bracing but should not be confused with peer-reviewed evidence; many of his protocols are off-label, his RCT (91% improved) is in fibromyalgia/CFS not LC, and is older.
