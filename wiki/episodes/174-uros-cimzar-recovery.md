# #174 — Uroš Čimžar — From Crisis to Rebirth

Slovenian entrepreneur / mentor (47). Got LC after second COVID infection (Dec 2023). Bedbound within weeks; ~300 steps/day. Crashed after gym, mobility class, walking with his son to a bookstore. Searched in Slovenian, found Jackie's Raelan Agle interview — "the first positive thing on this journey." Recovered to "back at the gym, full life" within ~10 months.

## What Uroš tried, and what landed

- **Yoga nidra** — first daily practice, "the calm moment in a day." Saw mental rest before physical recovery. Game-changer for someone who had never rested his mind in 15 years.
- **Long Covid Breathing course** (Jackie + Vicky) — six people, twice a week. Diaphragmatic breathing → first felt rest-and-digest at ~3 weeks (saliva, tummy rumble). "Surreal — should be stressed, was calm. First time in my life."
- **Becca Kennedy's class** — 8 modules covering nervous-system regulation. Movement was Module 7 (he was frustrated it was that late, now sees it was right).
- **Expressive writing + self-compassion** (Kristin Neff guided meditation) — broke through a 20-year backlog of suppressed emotions. Crying for the first time in decades. Stuff that "bothered him for 20 years just stopped" after 3 days.
- **Core belief work** — identified "only if you give everything are you a good boy." Symptoms returned at month 12 from falling back into duty mindset; gone in a week once he reframed activities away from obligation.
- **Intermittent fasting** — quit coffee cold turkey (no headache — body too depleted to even react).
- **Cold showers** — too soon, didn't help. Nervous system was still too dysregulated.

## Recovery framing

Uroš describes five phases:
1. Calming the nervous system
2. Removing stressors
3. Gradual exposure back to life
4. Leaving the "sick person" mindset behind
5. Integration of what was learned into normal life (where he sits now)

> "I don't want to be recovered in a sense that my life would be the same as before. I want to take everything I learned and have a better life." — Uroš Čimžar, ep #174
