# #025 — Scottish Opera's Breath Cycle for Long Covid

**Guests**: **David Douglas** (classical tenor, Scottish Opera education / chorus) and **Gareth Williams** (composer, Scottish Opera). Co-leads of the **Breath Cycle** singing-and-songwriting programme adapted for long covid.

Major content: creates new program page [[breath-cycle]].

## What is Breath Cycle

A 12-week online programme in two parallel strands, lunchtime sessions to suit LC energy budgets:

- **Wednesday singing sessions** (~1 hour) — three vocal coaches: David Douglas, **Daniela Hathway**, **Jessica Leary**. Posture → breathing exercises → fricative warm-ups → gentle vocal warm-up → introduce a song over 2 weeks → break-out groups (10–12 each) → final whole-group reflection / meditation.
- **Friday songwriting sessions** — led by writer **Martin O'Connor** with Gareth. Lyric/song generation from participant prompts; songs become repertoire for the singing strand.
- **Callum** (Scottish Opera) and **Lisa** (producer), under head of Outreach **Jane Davidson**, run the admin/check-in side.

## Lineage — and why this is novel

Breath Cycle was originally a 2013 pilot for **cystic fibrosis** patients — Scottish Opera + Gartnaval Hospital respiratory team — already online via Skype because of cross-infection risk. Mimicked airway-clearance physio with sung breath-out exercises.

**Reported pilot finding (cystic fibrosis cohort, n≈18, 12 weeks):** ~14 % increase in lung capacity. Small numbers; cited as design rationale, not LC-validated yet.

**Long covid version (now)** is "in pilot" — three 12-week cohorts running. **Not currently measuring lung function** for LC; tracking experience, well-being, community via questionnaires. Daniela Hathway has a COPD/lung-condition singing background.

## Patient design lessons

- Initial scheduling instinct (evening, like a community choir) was wrong — focus group of LC participants pushed back ("you think I have energy at 7:30 pm?"). Moved to lunchtime.
- Cameras can be off, mics muted; people participate from bed/sofa, in slippers, with snacks. Online removes both the energy cost of travel *and* the social pressure of being seen.
- Songs written by participants (one example: a song about being "too damn tired to do the dishes") become the singing-class repertoire — closing the loop.

## Who is it for

UK-wide via Zoom. Free, run by Scottish Opera. Recruitment open for Cohort 3 (post Easter 2022).
