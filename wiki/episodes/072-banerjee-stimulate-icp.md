# #72 — Prof Amitava Banerjee on STIMULATE-ICP

Cardiologist + UCL professor of clinical data science. Chief Investigator on **STIMULATE-ICP**, the largest UK long-covid platform trial.

## What STIMULATE-ICP is

> Symptoms, Trajectory, Inequalities and Management — Understanding Long COVID to Address and Transform Existing Integrated Care Pathways.

**Three arms.** Co-CI Melissa Heightman (UCLH; founded the first UK LC clinic April 2020).

### Arm 1 — symptoms & trajectory

- Detailed EHR data from UCLH LC clinic (running since April-May 2020)
- National EHR data via NHS England across 90+ LC clinics
- Six anchor sites for deep cohorts: **Hull, Leicester, Derby, Liverpool, Exeter, North Central London (UCL)**
- Patient + clinician interviews

### Arm 2 — the platform trial

Recruiting **4,500 people** (largest UK biobank for LC). At time of recording: ~400 enrolled; aim to complete recruitment early 2024. Three components:

| Component | Description |
|---|---|
| **Cover-scan** | Multi-organ MRI from Perspectum (originally a liver-imaging company); used 2020 cohort to show lasting organ impairment 1y post-acute (Royal Society of Medicine, in press) |
| **Drug platform** | Three arms: **colchicine** (anti-inflammatory), **rivaroxaban** (anticoagulant), **loratadine + famotidine** (H1+H2 antihistamines) |
| **Digital rehab** | "Living with Covid Recovery" app from Living With (UK industry partner) |

Primary outcome: **fatigue at 3 months on the Fatigue Assessment Scale**, plus broad physical/functional/psychological measures.

> "We haven't tested even one anticoagulant in a trial setting yet — that's why we can't run a triple-therapy trial. Twitter wants us to skip ahead, but the bleeding-risk regulatory bar is real."

### Arm 3 — inequalities + cross-condition comparison

- Inequalities led by Prof Nzar Akhalon (race/class gating of LC care)
- Cross-condition comparison led by Prof **Christina van der Feltz-Cornelius** (psychiatrist, York University) — comparing LC to cardiac, respiratory, mental-health and ME/CFS in healthcare utilisation. Goal: lessons for treating long-term conditions broadly.

## On PPI/E (patient and public involvement & engagement)

Banerjee describes a **personal epiphany during the pandemic** about needing patients leading rather than just included.

- 11 regular PPI panel members for STIMULATE-ICP, monthly meetings, active WhatsApp.
- All scientific outputs reviewed with PPI before submission.
- Banerjee himself a **trustee of Long Covid SOS** ("conflict of interest, in a good way").
- PPI panel co-leading an ebook on lived experience.
- Caveat: must be representative, not just the Twitter cohort. Hardest work is reaching unheard voices.

## Recruitment criteria

- Must be in one of the six study sites
- Newly referred to that LC clinic (no prior LC treatment) — so the trial sees an "untouched" population
- Drug-only sub-cohort recently added at struggling sites to accelerate

## Notable claims

> "More progress in long covid in 2.5 years than pretty much any other newly recognised disease."
> "We don't yet have the precision to say 'your variety of long covid is X and will respond to Y'. The clusters work isn't there yet."
> "Many of our research team and PPI panel are themselves affected by long covid — work has been delayed because people get sick."

## Eric-relevant signal

- **STIMULATE-ICP confirms Eric's whole-body antihistamine recommendation as a trialled hypothesis** (loratadine + famotidine). Saperstein #68 + Khan #37 + this trial = three converging signals.
- Cover-scan as a multi-organ biomarker — useful frame for "blood tests look normal but something is wrong" (#73 Li makes a similar point with vegf).
- Rivaroxaban (oral) as the trialled anticoagulant (vs Khan's triple therapy) — softer evidence path.
