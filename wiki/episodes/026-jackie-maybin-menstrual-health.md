# #026 — Dr Jackie Maybin — Long Covid & Women's Reproductive Health

**Guest**: **Dr Jacqueline Maybin** — Senior Clinical Research Fellow + Consultant Gynaecologist, MRC Centre for Reproductive Health, University of Edinburgh. Splits time clinic ↔ lab. Royal Society of Edinburgh-funded LC reproductive-health programme.

First **menstrual-health-and-LC** episode in the wiki. Creates [[ergo-menstrual-study]] trial page; updates [[reproductive-health]] theme.

## Three-strand research programme

International team — Edinburgh + Oxford + Bristol + Newson Health (menopause):

**1. Pandemic-and-menstruation survey (Oxford / Mellier — closed)** led by **Dr Alexandra Alvergne** (anthropologist). First analysis published: ~80 % of respondents had no menstrual change post-vaccination; ~20 % did (irregular bleeding, longer/heavier flow, missed periods, more frequent). **No long-term effect signal**; people on hormonal contraception had less disturbance. Follow-up analyses on COVID-itself and long-COVID-specifically queued.

**2. App-based daily-tracker study (UK-wide, open)** — Bristol epidemiologist **Dr Gemma Sharp** + Edinburgh team. Short daily questionnaire over 2–3 cycles tracking bleeding pattern + LC symptoms. Inclusion: regular cycles, with or without hormonal contraception. Targeting thousands. Patient-friendly design (Long Covid Support Scotland reviewed before submission).

**3. ERGO study (Edinburgh-only, open)** — n≈10–20, **tissue-and-blood study**. Three timepoints across the cycle (proliferative phase, secretory phase, period itself). Bloods + optional endometrial biopsy. Compared against pre-pandemic endometrial tissue bank. Recruiting at Royal Infirmary of Edinburgh / East Lothian Community Hospital. Looking at hormone receptors, immune cells, endometrial mechanisms.

## Inclusion-criteria caveat

Most studies require regular cycles — explicitly excludes many LC patients with irregular periods. Maybin acknowledges this is frustrating but necessary for cycle-phase analysis. Future studies will broaden.

## Why this matters

- Menstrual data is systematically under-collected; without data, no recognition, no funding.
- Patient/social-media pressure (vaccine-and-period reports) drove this research agenda.
- Long Covid Scotland support group reviewed PIS / consent forms — meaningful PPI.

## Quotes

> "If you don't have data, you don't know there's a problem and you can't do anything about it." — Maybin
