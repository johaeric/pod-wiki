Dr Leo Galland — internal medicine specialist, 40+ years working on complex chronic illness, post-infectious syndromes, post-Lyme, MCAS overlap. Author of *Power Healing* (1996). Has been in this field for decades; LC fits a long-standing pattern (Russian flu of 1890, 1918 sequelae, Royal Free disease/Icelandic disease/CFSME).

## The Web of Long COVID — 10 strands

Posted at drgalland.com. Two **central** strands:

1. **ACE2 damage** — entryway for the virus into cells. Restoring (not blocking) ACE2 is core. Key supports: **vitamin D, curcumin (turmeric), resveratrol**, fruits/veg.
2. **Mitochondrial damage** — direct viral attack on mitochondria *plus* impairment of mitophagy/biogenesis (the recycling). Compounds chronic energy failure.

Surrounding strands (selected):

- **Endothelial / microvascular damage + microclots** — capillary-level. Reduces O₂ and perfusion to working tissue.
- **Gut dysbiosis** — direct hit. Specifically: *Faecalibacterium prausnitzii* (a keystone species supporting bifidobacteria) is depleted. Restore via diet, fermented foods, or specific probiotics. **AB21** (Lactobacillus plantarum, Spanish-made, available via a London pharmacy) showed faster acute COVID recovery in a controlled trial.
- **Viral persistence** — open question whether replication-competent virus remains or just debris. Approach differs.
- **Immune dysregulation** — T/B cells, monocytes, macrophages
- **MCAS** — for the most-debilitated patients, mass cell activation is *the* gating issue. Can drive POTS in a subset (blocking MC reduces POTS symptoms).
- **POTS / autonomic dysfunction** — three subtypes; distinct from neurally-mediated hypotension (the form he saw with classic CFSME).
- **Joint hypermobility** — striking comorbidity with post-Lyme and post-COVID populations. Connective tissue + mass cells co-implicated.

## Theory of post-exertional malaise

PEM (which Galland wants to rename *post-exertional **agony*** because "malaise" understates it) =

> capillary damage → relative hypoxia/ischaemia in working tissue → mitochondria run their KB cycle *backwards* → further mito damage

Implication: generic "mitochondrial support" can be useless or harmful. Need protocol that:
1. Improves blood-vessel and microclot situation (oxygen delivery)
2. Prevents mitochondria from running the KB cycle backwards under stress
3. Adds — not generic — specific mito support, sometimes including **red light therapy at the right frequency** (wrong frequency makes things worse) or **hydrogen water**

## On supplements

- **CoQ10**: ubiquinone/ubiquinol ratio matters. Too much ubiquinol disrupts the electron transport chain. Don't blindly switch to "the better form."
- **NAD/NMN injections** (Hollywood trend): high doses up-regulate NAD breakdown — creates dependency. Some cell types can't even use external NAD. Precursors more useful — but they also don't always reach where you want.
- *None of these are universal answers.*

## On exercise

Two-edged sword. Exercise normally helps ACE2. In LC, "judicious" exercise has crashed many of his patients. Capillary-perfusion theory above explains why.
