# #159 — Jackie's 2024 Reflections — Journey to self-worth & inner peace

Brief end-of-year reflection (11 minutes). Triggered by a journal prompt at her **virtual art room** (a new format she's running in the LC creativity group): *"If you could tell yourself from one year ago something, what would it be?"*

## What's in the episode

- A single journal-prompt response, read out at the end. Topline message:
  - **Health is the most valuable thing you have, guard it as you reclaimed it.**
  - **You can't pour from an empty cup.**
  - **You are enough whether you do or don't.**
  - **Treasure yourself; respect yourself; you are worthy whatever you do or don't do.**
- Frame: continuing the recovery framing she set in #75 + #94 + #112 + #123 — recovery is a milestone, not the end of the journey; ongoing self-improvement is lifelong; falling-into-old-patterns risk is real.
- Plug for the new [[long-covid-creativity-group]] virtual art-room format (Zoom; bring your own thing; ~40-minute sit-and-create + optional share).

## Why this episode matters

- **Fourth annual host-solo reflection** (#64 2022 → #75 3-year → #112 2023 → #123 4-year → #159 2024). Pattern is now durable and worth noting in the [[overview]] — Jackie's anniversary cadence is the closest the wiki has to a longitudinal recovery-narrative case.
- **First explicit on-air mention of the virtual art-room format** for the LC creativity group.
- **Reaffirms perfectionism as recovery-blocker** — no new evidence-log rows but consolidates the theme: "perfectionism, planning every moment, is not only impractical, it takes the fun out of life." Pairs with #94 + #142 + #145.
