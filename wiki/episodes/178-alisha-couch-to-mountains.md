# #178 — Alisha — Rebuilding Safety: from Couch to Mountains

US mountain-living organic-farm worker (~10 years in). Got COVID in 2020, then again 2021. Couch-bound for 1.5 years through ~15 symptoms (extreme fatigue, anxiety, GI dysfunction — bloating/constipation/burning, dizziness, panic attacks, fear of sun/heat). Functional-medicine workup gave her diagnoses (Lyme, EBV, mold, heavy metals, parasites) and a **20-supplement detox** for a year — none helped meaningfully. Now a mind-body coach, farming part-time, hiking again. Recovered fully.

## The shift

Heard **Dan Buglio** on Raelan Agle's channel discuss someone with chronic fatigue who could do *X* but not *Y* with no rational pattern. Read Dr Sarno's *The Mind Body Prescription*. Noticed the same in herself: walking up the stairs would crash her; walking the same number of steps in the yard wouldn't. **The recovery turn was believing she wasn't broken.**

## Tools (consistency-first stack)

- **Somatic tracking** with Rebecca Tolin's daily meditation — the central practice. Calming voice "telling me I'm safe in this moment."
- **Self-Reiki** — became Reiki-certified during recovery. At peak, 2 hours/day. Engaged her mind in something other than her health (key — gave her joy and learning).
- **Calming meditations** — many of them, every day.
- **Reading widely** — story books, learning books, anything that engaged her mind elsewhere.
- **Smiling on purpose** — alone in a room; felt fake at first, but tilted nervous-system signal toward safety. Cites Dan Buglio's "24 ways to create safety" video.
- **Boundaries / saying no** — learning she's an empath. Setting hard limits became core to safety, both during and after.
- **Slow reintroduction** with joy framing — "first time walking into my garden in 2 years" treated as celebration, not failure marker.
- **One session with Rob Ensor** (TMS coach) cleared her last lingering "detox sensations" — burning after activity, GI issues. Went on her first date the next day; that's when she said she was healed.

## What Alisha tells coaching clients now

- Every safety moment counts. 10 minutes of feeling safe stacks.
- Every doctor / decision question: ask if it feels *loving and supportive*. The doctor whose detox didn't help her was loving and supportive — that mattered.
- Wherever you're at, you are completely lovable right now. Productivity ≠ worth.

> "Believing I wasn't broken — even just believing it for ten minutes — calmed my nervous system. All those moments build on each other." — Alisha, ep #178
