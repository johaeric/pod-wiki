# #41 — Joachim Gerlach — Vedicinals-9

Industry/founder interview: Joachim Gerlach is a retired German businessman (sold his company ~10 years before recording, moved to Spain) who became a long-time funder of organic-agriculture and ethnobotany research. When COVID-19 emerged in January 2020 he redirected his network of ethnobotanists and nutraceutical scientists to develop a multi-molecule formulation against SARS-CoV-2. The result is **Vedicinals-9** — a 9-molecule nutraceutical (the named molecules in this conversation include quercetin, EGCG, piperine, curcumin, and baicalein; four others are not named on-air).

## Selection rationale

The team started from a database of ~8,000 medicinally active molecules and ran a grid-match against ~150–200 dysregulated drug-target pathways in COVID-19 (viral replication via 3CL protease, ACE2 cleavage enzymes, hypersensitivity, mast-cell activation, inflammation, myocardial damage, neuronal damage, thrombosis, glutathione depletion). They contacted Harvard Medical contacts on Jan 26, 2020 to ask which parts of the SARS-CoV-2 genome were *unchanged* relative to SARS-1/MERS — the conserved 3CL protease was the early target. Funnel: 8,000 → ~20 → 9 final molecules. Followed by 500+ in-silico 3D simulations, cell-culture, animal trials (efficacy and toxicity), then a Phase 2 RCT in India (n=124, 62 treatment vs 62 control on the standard Indian early-treatment protocol of hydroxychloroquine + antibiotics + paracetamol + dexamethasone + supplemental O₂).

## Phase 2 trial results (acute COVID, India)

- Average daily PCR Ct value rose 1.76 cycles/day (faster viral clearance vs control).
- IL-6, TNF-α, and other inflammatory markers significantly reduced.
- Thrombotic markers reduced.
- ~95% of patients had abnormal lung x-ray on admission; 75% reduction in lung-x-ray abnormalities at day 12 — described by trial doctors as unusually fast.
- 45-day post-trial follow-up showed no long-term symptoms or imaging abnormalities reported in the treatment group.
- Toxicity testing classified Class 5 (LD50 ≥ 5,000 mg/kg).

The product is now sold as a "food for special medical purpose" in India and shipped to ~27 countries via online order.

## On nutraceuticals vs pharmaceuticals

Gerlach distinguishes dietary supplements / nutraceuticals / functional foods / phytopharmaceuticals / pharmaceuticals as a tier of regulatory class. His framing:

- Nutraceuticals are recognised by the body as familiar molecules; they tend to *accumulate at the site of need* (cites curcumin in arthritic joints — low serum levels but high tissue levels on biopsy).
- Same molecule can do opposite things depending on cell state: e.g. baicalein triggers apoptosis in irreparably damaged cells but supports repair in salvageable ones.
- Pharmaceuticals are "more like a tank" — targeted, blunt, no body-feedback loop on dosing.

> "It's always the choice of weaponry for the battle you want to go into." — Gerlach, ep #41

## Polypharmacy warning

A repeated theme of the conversation: long-haulers self-stacking many supplements is a genuine harm signal.

> "When I get the list from many long-haulers what they are all taking at the same time, I'm flabbergasted. Take much doesn't mean it will help much." — Gerlach, ep #41

He advises: be selective, target categories (anti-inflammatory / anti-allergic / anti-pathogenic), don't stack ten supplements that do the same job. Mixing supplements with pharmaceuticals can elevate or suppress metabolising enzymes (CYP450 implied) — risk of overdose-by-stack on the pharmaceutical. Also notes that "natural" does not mean "safe": even vitamin D3 / zinc without serum-level checks can harm; probiotics can trigger MCAS-style hypersensitivity.

## Long Covid Coalition

Gerlach is co-founder. Premise: ~150–300 dysregulated pathways need to be mapped systematically; each researcher / company / clinician can "tick the boxes" for which pathways their tool covers. Goal — dissolve the coalition once the matrix is filled. Third conference held by recording date. He explicitly invites competitor companies (apheresis, mechanical interventions, oxygen) into the coalition.
