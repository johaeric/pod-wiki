Annual end-of-year creativity episode. Members of the LC community share music, poetry, writing, and visual art that emerged from or carried them through illness.

## Contributors

- **Kirsten Melian** — guitar, vocals; recorded the song *I Don't Mind* (about boundaries and people-pleasing)
- **Fiona Lane** — read from her self-illustrated book *Like the Cat: Feline Wisdom for Healing and Transformation* (following her cat as a self-care experiment)
- **Matteo** — bedbound long-hauler since Nov 2020; runs the *COVID is Stupid* podcast (with two O's, not a U)
- **Ben Stanley** — Ireland; "rhythm and rhyme therapy"; read *The Rotisserie Chicken*, *She Dances by Starlight*, and *Relinquish*
- **Naomi Vandernoot** — leads the [[long-covid-kids-choir]]; played the choir's song *Roller Coaster* (composed in collaboration with Frankie Mand)
- **Paula** (Germany) — beadwork, jewellery, occupational-therapy framing
- **Stella Taylor** — read *Groundhog Day* (prose)
- **Jasmine** — Newsweek health reporter; read from her recovery memoir which won the *Letter of Views* unpublished-book prize Jan-Mar 2025
- **Gerbin G. van Dijk** — uses suno.com (AI music) to create comforting tracks; played a 3-minute AI-generated song *You Are Enough*

## Notes for Eric

A genre episode — no clinical content. Worth keeping the index entry concise. The notable theme is **AI as creative tool for severely-ill patients** (van Dijk's suno.com workflow) — relevant if Eric ever wants a low-energy creative outlet.
