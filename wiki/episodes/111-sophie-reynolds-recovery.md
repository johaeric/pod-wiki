# #111 — Sophie Reynolds: ME/CFS recovery (pre-Covid; **11th full recovery on the wiki, but pre-LC source**)

Pre-Covid ME/CFS recovery story. Sophie fell ill in **March 2018** as a UK prison officer doing a full-time Masters; recovered to teaching Pilates daily and running her own business. **The wiki's first dedicated pre-Covid ME/CFS recovery — useful as cross-condition signal, but Sophie was never a Covid long-hauler.**

## Pre-illness pattern (anchor — perfect storm)

- Prison-officer graduate scheme — high adrenaline, daily fear; "white macho men where physical strength signals mental strength" — culture against showing weakness
- Full-time Masters in criminology + psychology in parallel
- Recent breakup
- **Obsessed** with the gym, productivity, eating "healthily"; later diagnosed with an **eating disorder** (the obsessive over-exercise variety)
- Isolating from friends/family; "circle weighted in work + body image"

The wiki's recurring pre-illness pattern across recovered patients: chronic over-extension + cortisol/adrenaline running + suppressed emotion. Sophie articulates the eating-disorder dimension cleanly.

## Onset and dropping

- Mid-March 2018: started feeling flu-like; called sick to work (a big deal in the prison service — "take five days minimum or they'll think you're faking")
- Two weeks → a month → bedbound most of the day, "saving herself for an evening run"; ran to end of road, came back in floods of tears
- 4-5 months in: GP says **"this could last 6 months, a year, two years"** — "Waterworks just flooded"
- Started antidepressants — partial relief from anxiety + decision-paralysis; framed as "what I needed at the time"
- Forced back to work within 2 months on adjusted duty (mental-health unit, sit-and-observe role) — read prisoners' files, talked with prisoners; this incidentally gave her cognitive-distance from her own symptoms

## What helped — recovery stack

1. **[[optimal-health-clinic]]** — first proper framework. Anchored the **STOP technique** (hands on chest → deep breath → "stop" → notice the calm). Sophie's most-named single tool.
2. **Pilates** ([[pilates]]) — discovered via low-impact YouTube videos to "tick the exercise box." Became more than exercise; the **breath-with-movement** structure taught her to enter the parasympathetic state. Now a Pilates teacher; she credits this as the single biggest recovery factor.
3. **Yoga** ([[yoga]]) — initial entry-point but found it "harder to concentrate"; transitioned to Pilates as primary practice.
4. **Breathwork integrated with movement** — Pilates' lateral-rib breathing, exhale on exertion, mindful-breath structure.
5. **Understanding the nervous system** — the "lightbulb": *not 20 different things wrong, one thing not functioning*. Then she could target it.
6. **Self-compassion** development — letting symptoms be a signal not a failure.

## What didn't work

- **Cold showers / cold water** — drained her; "massive energy rush during, knackered after." Worth surfacing — Sophie is the wiki's first explicit *no_effect / harmed* signal for cold water. Possible interpretation: too early in recovery, no scaffolding (cf. [[107-raelan-agle-recovery-stories]] scaffolding-first principle).
