# #029 — Lorna Nicholson — POTS & Breathing

**Guest**: **Lorna Nicholson** — RN background (A&E, then ANP in primary care). **Co-founder of POTS UK (2010)** with Susan Whitfield; now in background information role. Pilates teacher; **Buteyko-trained** breathing instructor; trained with Brad Cliff for LC; **SIRPA practitioner** (Stress-Induced Recovery Practitioner — a mind-body framework). Multi-modality LC clinician. Recovery status not stated.

This is the wiki's most consolidated **POTS-and-breathing** episode and triples up SIRPA / polyvagal / autonomic framing. Major updates to [[pots]], [[breathwork]], [[mind-body]]; creates [[sirpa]] program, [[buteyko]] / [[pilates]] / [[cold-water-exposure]] interventions, [[breathing-pattern-disorder]] symptom, [[trauma-and-illness]] theme.

## POTS — what it is, how to measure

- **Postural Tachycardia Syndrome.** ≥30 bpm HR rise from supine to standing, sustained.
- Common companions: **low blood pressure**, **vasovagal syncope** episodes (BP drops, faints).
- Sits under the autonomic umbrella with [[mcas]] overlap (mast-cell activation common in POTS).
- Diagnosis: **tilt-table test** (gold standard; rules out other causes) **or** **at-home stand test** (lie 15 min, take supine BP/HR, stand up, log every 3/5/10 min). Bring the at-home record to specialist — captures fluctuating-day bias.

## Standard POTS management she names

- Beta-blockers / HR-lowering meds (cross-ref [[beta-blockers]], [[ivabradine]])
- Drugs to raise BP if needed
- **Compression stockings** (waist-high) — "doesn't really help most people I see; uncomfortable, hot, mostly ditched"
- Increased **fluids** + **salt** (table salt, generous)
- Whole-food diet, **low-carb** for many
- Activity — but **not cardio first**: Pilates-style work on muscle strength, joint range, balance/coordination, leaving cardio for later

## Her actual framing — nervous-system regulation

Same model she sees across POTS, LC, and ME: **dysregulated autonomic + immune + mitochondrial + hormonal systems**, all driven by sympathetic overdrive. **The cause sits upstream** of any one organ system.

Asks every LC client about pre-existing patterns:
- "Running on adrenaline for years"
- Personality types: perfectionist, over-responsible, helper, controller, anxious, worrier
- Childhood: critical parents, neglect, medical traumas → nervous-system shaped before symptoms appear
- Even **in-utero** maternal stress and **trans-generational** trauma she names as influences

> "Covid is the trigger. The pre-existing pattern is what makes it stick." — Lorna

## Breathing-pattern disorder

Cites a research finding that **POTS patients have a breathing-pattern disorder** as a core feature — symptom set near-identical to LC: dizziness, fast HR, air hunger, fatigue, chest discomfort, balance loss. Breathing as a continuum from optimal → hyperventilation; we all sit somewhere on it.

Habit + cause: even when the original trigger is resolved, the breathing habit persists. So she works **both axes**:
- Mechanics: **nose breathing**, slow belly breathing, calming the upper-chest accessory muscles
- Upstream: SIRPA approach to chronic sympathetic overdrive

Says high-counting Buteyko exercises (e.g. 7-in-5-out) are too hard for most LC patients to start with — start simpler.

> "Heart rate you can't consciously slow. Breathing you can. So you can influence the influencers." — Lorna

## Cold water / wild swimming

She's actively interested. Forces breath control + present-moment focus + nervous-system input. Anecdotal benefits in POTS, ME, depression. **Caveats**: don't start in winter; don't go alone; learn from a guide. **Safe entry point: cold face splash / cold shower.**

She invites listener case-reports.

- **At-home stand test** is a free, repeatable data point worth logging alongside Eric's existing HR data
- **Nose breathing during walking** — low cost, low risk, plausibly improves exercise tolerance
- **Cold face splash** (not full plunge) — same low-cost low-risk category
