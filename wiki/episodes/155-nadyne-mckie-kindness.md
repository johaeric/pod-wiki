# #155 — Nadyne McKie — Embracing Kindness on the Healing Journey

UK psychotherapist + yoga therapist + breathing coach, working in NHS acute psychiatric services + a private practice specialising in management of chronic illness (predominantly fatigue-based). Personal LC recovery (still residually managing it; identifies as "lifelong management"); her son also had post-COVID LC two years before the recording and is now well. Co-author with **Fiona** of a recently-launched book on convalescence-and-self-compassion in fatigue-based illness.

## Why this episode matters

- **Anchor convalescence-and-self-compassion practitioner episode.** Pairs with [[030-gavin-francis-recovery]] (GP/author who introduced "convalescence" to the wiki) but from the therapy / yoga-therapy side rather than the medical side.
- **Practitioner with personal + family LC experience** — adds to the small set of clinicians who've been on both sides ([[023-sally-riggs-psychologist-long-hauler]], [[078-lucy-gahan-psychology]], [[085-fiona-agombar-yoga-therapy]]).
- **First episode on the wiki to explicitly name screen-time-as-breathing-disruption** ("screen apnoea" — shallow breathing while scrolling) as a daily energy-drain LC patients underweight.
- **Reframe of exercise as "movement"** — a recurring wiki pattern, here from a yoga-therapist who herself was a "leave-shavasana" person before LC. Exercise often functions as **distraction from uncomfortable emotional content**; LC removes the distraction and forces the encounter.
- **Unworthiness-driving-doing pattern** named explicitly: "I felt I was failing as a human because I'm not doing the things I'm supposed to do as a human" — articulated jointly by Jackie and Nadyne; matches Sara Pover, Adams, etc.

## Key concepts

- **The art of convalescence**: rest as the actual treatment, not a regrettable side-effect of being too ill to do anything else. Cultivating rest as practice, not waiting-it-out.
- **Different categories of energy-stress**: physical, cognitive, emotional, *social* ("social battery") — many LC patients overweight physical stress and underweight cognitive + social load.
- **Screen apnoea**: scrolling phone / shorts / online work → shallow breath / breath-holding → cognitive fatigue compounded by hypocapnia. Practical lever Nadyne uses with clients (and kids — see [[156-nadyne-mckie-caregivers-children]]).
- **Yoga therapy as Invitational, not corrective**: "the right way is the way that is right for you." Slowness reveals body-signals usually masked by the activity.
- **Just for now** (her phrase) — recovery framing that makes acceptance + hope coexist; same operative move as [[109-julie-black-recovery]]'s "submit now ≠ accept forever."
- **Nervous-system-to-nervous-system transmission** between caregiver and unwell child / partner — the calmer the carer, the better the substrate for recovery.
