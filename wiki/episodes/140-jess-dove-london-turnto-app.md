# Episode 140 — Jess Dove London — Turn To, the daily-health-breakthrough app

Founder of [[turnto-app]] — a mobile app launched in late April 2024 for cerebal palsy + LC + ME/CFS communities, intended to surface daily breakthrough health information (research papers, trial findings, practitioner content, peer-shared experience) in a swipe-through TikTok-style 5-10 minute experience.

## Backstory

- Jess had **severe ME/CFS for 2 years in her 20s** — mostly bedbound, mostly couldn't use her eyes. Recovered ~12 years before recording.
- Has a son with rare cerebal palsy. At his 18-month appointment, the consultant told her *don't bother reading any research, nothing will help him*. She read everything anyway, found a proprioception suit overnight, tried it; he sat up perfectly within seconds and has continued to walk since. That experience drove the app idea.
- Industry background in social-impact work; founder before; took her family's house deposit to fund the startup instead of buying a house.

## What the app does

- **Daily 5-10-minute experience**, swipe-through full-screen.
- Aggregates research papers, trial findings, events, user-generated experience tips, practitioner Q&A across multiple conditions.
- Captioning + translations are coming; some accessibility friction at launch.
- AI-driven search ("crowd question" → six relevant resources) in development; not in launch build.
- Public website where users vote up/down what should be built next.
- Started with cerebal palsy (already thousands of users); LC + ME/CFS just launched 6 days before recording.

## Wiki position

- Logged as `mentioned_only` — Turnto is information infrastructure, not an LC intervention itself. But a tool worth tracking for Eric's information-firehose problem.
- The app's design philosophy aligns with the [[research-aid-networks]] / lay-summary / patient-research-bridge ethos that's now visible across the community.
- Jess's own story is a useful **early ME/CFS recovery in her 20s** counterpoint to the older-onset LC stories on the wiki. Recovery she didn't actively engineer ("rest because I couldn't do anything else") — pre-internet era recovery.
