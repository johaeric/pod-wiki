# #75 — Jackie's 3-Year Reflections

Solo episode marking three years since Jackie got covid. The wiki's clearest single statement of **how she narrates her own recovery arc.**

## The arc, year by year

### March 2020 — got sick

- Hill-walking in March 2020; struggling to walk, breathing wrong but unaware. Not yet recognising covid (atypical-symptoms; pre-test era).
- Paramedics confirm "almost certainly covid; rest and isolate; you'll be fine in a week" (she was 30, fit).
- **Pre-illness lifestyle in retrospect**: "stress on top of stress on top of stress — long commute, exam season, multiple jobs, yes-person culture. My nervous system was a tinder box."

### March 2021 — 1-year mark

- Worse mentally; still working from home; **convinced she was coping but wasn't**.
- "Push-through" was the only strategy she knew.
- YouTube one-year video: "I look like a corpse."
- Counsellor since described her stubbornness as both her best and worst quality.

### March 2022 — 2-year mark

- **Stopped working ~6 months earlier** ("not entirely my choice; turned out to be the best thing that could have happened").
- Started the podcast. Connected with people who gave her tools.
- Her toolkit by this point: **breathwork, yoga nidra, salt + hydration, less, deep rest, counselling**.
- "I don't like the word *acceptance* but I had at least accepted that pushing through wasn't working."

### March 2023 — 3-year mark (this episode)

- **Most symptoms largely gone.** Occasional chest pain still a worry.
- Able to push physically more; nervous system feels much more regulated.
- Removed a "big stressor" recently — felt the weight literally lifting (later episodes will reveal: separation from her partner — see #77 with Suzy Bolt).
- Then on the Monday before recording: "wiped out" by what she now believes was a stomach bug — first non-covid illness in 3 years.

## What she credits as her toolkit

| Intervention | Use |
|---|---|
| **Breathwork** | Helped breathing + heart palpitations |
| **Yoga Nidra** | Real deep rest |
| **Salt + hydration** | Standing dizziness |
| **Cold-water exposure** (Loch Ness) | Nervous-system regulation |
| **Counselling** | Mental health, processing trauma |
| **Reduced doing** | PEM management |

## Notable claims

> "When I come through this — and it's *when*, not if — I will not be the person I was before I got sick. I think that's a good thing."

> "Don't give up. Keep doing the things that help, try to avoid the things that don't, and don't beat yourself up when you inevitably slip up. We're all human."

> "Things are so much better than they were."

## Eric-relevant intake

- **Three converging credits across all of Jackie's appearances** to date: breathwork, yoga nidra, cold water. **Same triad as #64.** This is the strongest pattern in the wiki for "what worked for the host."
- **Stopping work** is now explicitly framed as the single inflection point in her arc. Worth weighing for anyone in burnout-overlap LC.
- Stomach-bug → "pushed less, recovered fast" demonstrates the toolkit's value for *any* viral/bacterial setback, not just LC crashes.
