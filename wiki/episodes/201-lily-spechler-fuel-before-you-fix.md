Lily Spechler — registered dietitian specialising in long COVID, herself a long-hauler with POTS. Returns after #140-era to talk about **weight changes in LC** — both the weight gain and weight loss directions — and why the conventional "just cut calories" advice can make LC worse.

## Two ends of the same metabolic dysfunction

- **Rapid weight loss** — body in hyper-metabolic state; more medically risky in the short term
- **Weight gain on low calories** — metabolism slowing to conserve fuel; very common; often seen on near-liquid diets
- Both are *byproducts* of the underlying dysfunction, not the root cause

## Why cutting calories backfires in LC

- Inflammation in any chronic condition (cancer, burns, COPD) requires *more* calories and protein, not fewer
- Cutting fuel further depletes immune/metabolic reserves → symptom worsening
- BMR + 10–15% (10–20% if severe) is roughly what LC patients need, not BMR

## Blood glucose dysregulation as an underrated driver

- LC patients often spike glucose *without eating* — e.g. on getting out of bed (autonomic stress response releases stored liver glucose)
- Erratic blood glucose — especially low blood glucose — may be **the biggest single driver of LC symptoms** (Lily's strongest claim of the year)
- CGMs are eye-opening: clients can see symptoms tracking glucose trends
- Practical: 10–15 g protein paired with ~20 g carb; tight ratio
- Combine with electrolytes + compression *before* getting upright to blunt the morning spike

## When weight loss makes sense in LC

- Medical reasons: estrogen-MCAS link (adipose tissue is estrogenic → drives histamine), hypermobility joint load, sleep apnea, raised CRP
- Mental health reasons are valid too — body autonomy
- *Stability first*: if booming and busting or in regular crashes, do not pursue weight loss. Stabilise first.

## Lily's weight-loss protocol (for LC)

1. Add calories first (BMR + 15%) until weight gain stalls for ~2 weeks
2. Then create *small* deficits from that buffer (target 1–2 lb/month, not week)
3. Keep histamine support throughout
4. Watch for flu-like detox symptoms as adipose-stored toxins release — pump brakes if symptomatic

## When weight gain is needed (severe loss)

- Same BMR + 15-20% target but the challenge is delivery
- High-calorie low-volume foods (fats: 9 cal/g vs 4 for carbs/protein)
- Olive-oil-rich foods, Himalayan butter tea (drinkables expand blood volume *and* deliver calories — useful in severe POTS)
- Benecalorie (tasteless food enhancer) as last-resort
- Cooked food, simple repetition; prioritise macros over micros

## Notes for Eric

The blood-glucose claim is the new high-signal piece. Worth considering a 2-week CGM trial during a stable period to see if Eric's symptoms track glucose dips — this is a low-cost, high-information experiment. The "BMR + 15%" framing is also worth holding next to current intake. The estrogen-MCAS-adipose loop adds nuance to MCAS interventions for any future weight discussions.
