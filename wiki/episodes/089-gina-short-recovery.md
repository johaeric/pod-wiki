# #89 — Gina Short: Recovery Story

US (Washington State) recovery story. **Onset July 2021** at her mother's 80th birthday outdoor party — half of 24 vaccinated guests caught covid; recovered acutely OK except for shortness of breath that lingered. By month 3-4: severe neuro-vegetative cascade — bedbound, internal tremors, HR 160 just putting shoes on, couldn't drive, naps multiple times per day, showering only every few days. **Declared recovery December 17, 2022.**

Sixth full recovery story logged on this wiki. Fits in the [[suzy-bolt-recovery]] / mind-body family alongside [[032-esther-recovery-story]] (TMS), [[020-amy-anker-positively-covid]] (NS-loop), [[056-johanna-rayl-recovery]] (ANS Rewire), with her own distinct flavour: **declaration as inflection point**.

## The recovery arc — what worked, in order

### 1. Suzy Bolt's free 10-min supine yoga (the first crack)

At her worst, internal tremors all over the body, bedbound. Couldn't tolerate a walk. Found Suzy Bolt's 10-minute gentle bedtime yoga video. Started doing it nightly, then multiple times a day.

The pivotal moment: Bolt said in the video *"this is temporary."* Gina realised she'd lost belief that recovery was even possible and felt the language re-open it.

### 2. Joining Bolt's Fern programme (12-person small-group cohort)

Built on the gentle yoga. Started learning Mind-Body framing, language re-framing, "playing again."

### 3. Stasis Breathing programme — alongside

Picked up [[stasis-breathing]] for the breath-pattern dysfunction (couldn't read aloud to her son). Reports being "stronger now than before".

### 4. Pacing + the realisation that she was pre-planning crashes

Around month 6 she noticed she was unconsciously pre-planning crashes — anticipating "if I do that event in three days I'll crash for two days after." Counter-intuitive learning: **she needed to *play more* and *task less* to recover** — exactly opposite to the pre-LC pattern.

### 5. Stripping outside inputs

- Took off the heart-rate-watching wearable.
- Stopped news.
- Pulled away from negative-skewed online communities.
- Curated her social circle down to the people who *understood and supported* her plus her LC community.

### 6. Reset of the inner landscape

- Joy strolls — drove to a trail head, walked 100 metres to a bench, sat, sometimes cried, drove home. Built up gradually from there.
- "Joy mix" playlist; danced a little while walking.
- Mom-as-mom again — chose to play board games / jump on trampoline / watch funny videos with her two boys instead of telling herself "it's too loud, I'm too tired."
- Mindfulness without trying — lay on grass, "feeling photos" instead of camera photos at viewpoints.

### 7. The Declaration (Dec 17, 2022)

After Suzy Bolt asked her in a recovery interview *"are you recovered?"* and Gina paused, she went home, wrote a journal entry stating *"I am recovered from long covid"*, signed and dated it, told family and friends.

> "It's amazing the difference Declaration made. Telling other people. Even my 8-year-old started cheering."

This is the wiki's first explicit articulation of **declaration-as-inflection** as a recovery technique. Pairs with #20 Amy Anker's neuroplasticity framing and #56 Johanna Rayl's ANS Rewire framing — different vocabularies for the same lever (training the brain to stop expecting illness).

## On her teenage son's role

She told her 16yo to give her a hug whenever he came home from school and to **say one sentence: "Mom you're getting better."** Every day. Eventually he started believing it before she did.

This is operationally useful: a designated witness who repeats a forward-looking sentence on schedule. Doesn't require anyone to know what they're doing.

## On her mother (parallel context)

Her mum had Parkinson's disease from age 30 — lived ill but giving / loving / inspirational. Gina says: *"You don't have to be well to love and to give."* Her mum passed away mid-recovery; instead of derailing recovery, the support and inner-strength frame she'd built around her mum's example *carried her through it.*

This is the recovery story's gentlest pushback against the "productivity = self-worth" trap that several other wiki guests name (Munslow #34, Towers #79, Gahan #78). Worth in chronic illness is a real thing.

## On her advice to early-onset patients

1. **Truly rest.** Not Netflix-rest, not phone-scroll rest. Unplug.
2. **Be knowledgeable but selective.** She names *Cured* and *Breaking Free* as books she found.
3. **Go play.** Dream big. Not counter-intuitive, just true.
4. **Find your people.** Community matters; choose it to skew forward-looking.

## Quotes

> "I planned my crashes — I'd see an event coming and pre-decide I'd be wrecked for two days. That's when I realised I was teaching my body to crash."

> "When I could see my future self — a *new* version of myself, not the old one — that's when real healing began."

> "You don't have to be well to love and to give." (Re her mum, Parkinson's from age 30.)

## Eric-relevant intake

- **Declaration as a technique** is genuinely new in this wiki — write it down, sign it, tell people, then let *their* belief stabilise yours when yours wobbles. Low-cost, low-risk; Eric could deploy it whenever the moment feels right.
- **Joy strolls** = micro-walks driven by destination *(this bench)* not distance — coherent with #79 Towers's 5-minute principle and #77 Bolt's 5% stretch. Three independent confirmations of the pattern.
- **The designated-witness sentence** ("Mom you're getting better") is portable to Eric's domestic context. Pair it with [[078-lucy-gahan-psychology]]'s Weingarten-witnessing framing — the practice has a name.
- **"You don't have to be well to give"** — explicit reframe against the productivity-as-worth tendency Eric has named in himself.
- The **Bolt → Fern programme → Stasis breathing → joy + community** stack maps cleanly onto interventions Eric already has access to and uses partially.
