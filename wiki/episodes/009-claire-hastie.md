# #009 — Claire Hastie (Long Covid Support founder)

**Guest**: [[claire-hastie]] — founder of [[long-covid-support]] (UK). First-wave (17 March 2020); single parent of three boys (15, 11+11), all of whom got covid. Wheelchair user >1 year. Recording ≈Sep 2021.

## What this episode is

The **origin story of the patient movement** and the term *"long covid"*. Group started 2 May 2020 after she saw a Guardian article showing she wasn't alone. Now (recording) **45,000 members across 100+ countries**.

## How "long covid" became the term

- Spring 2020: Claire and [[barbara-melville]] (ep #1) on late-night WhatsApps brainstorm a 3-word slogan → **"rehab, research, recognition"** (later adopted by the WHO for its three LC webinars).
- May 2020: Italian archaeologist **Elisa Perego** uses *long covid* on Twitter; Claire and Barbara recognise it instantly and rename the Facebook group accordingly.
- The term enters global use; later attempts to displace it ("post-acute covid syndrome", "post-covid-19 condition", etc.) keep failing — patient-named terms hold.

## The group and its operation

- Strict joining criteria: 4+ weeks of symptoms, must agree to rules, every post moderator-checked.
- Active social/well-being programming: long covid choir (with Zoe Challenor — see ep #31, untranscribed), chair yoga, opera breathing, weekly Zoom socials.
- International team of admins/moderators across time zones.

## Notable arguments

- **Patient-led research is dramatically ahead of academic LC research.** The [[patient-led-research-collaborative]] symptom paper remains unequaled.
- **Research misdirection**: too much funding still going to symptom-tracking apps and well-trodden questionnaires, run by groups with no patient input, asking the wrong questions.
- **Stress is one of the biggest drivers of physical LC symptoms** — being disbelieved is itself a symptom-amplifier.
- **Menstrual disruption** is huge in the group; she's been trying to find researchers willing to actually take samples / biopsies for 18+ months, with [[louise-newson]] interest but no funded study yet.
- **Mask-wearing is the cheap, simple, durable answer** — far-East cultural norm of masking when even mildly symptomatic should be a pandemic legacy.
- **Devolved nations don't have LC services** the way England does — England's services are imperfect but exist; Scotland, Wales, NI lag.

## Healthcare experience

- 5 visits to A&E in the first 3 months ("dread to think how many people died on the 'come back when your lips go blue' advice").
- Took herself each time because of bad ambulance experiences in her network.
- Eventually a brilliant GP — believed her, was honest about not knowing what to do, accepted patient-shared webinars and resources.
