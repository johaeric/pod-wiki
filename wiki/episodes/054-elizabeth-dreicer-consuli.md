# #54 — Elizabeth Dreicer — Consuli, Long Covid & Research

[[elizabeth-dreicer]] is the CEO of **Consuli**, a public-benefit company that connects patients to clinical trials and data-research opportunities. The episode is about **research infrastructure**, not about interventions. No clinical claims; the value is in the meta-pattern.

## Key facts

- **1–4% of people ever participate in clinical trials** — yet **83–84% say they would be willing** when asked.
- The bottleneck is that trials are recruited through doctors (the busiest people on Earth) and tend to flow through large academic teaching hospitals, leaving most patients invisible.
- Women, BIPOC, and Indigenous people are systematically under-represented — Consuli treats this as an equity-of-access problem.
- Consuli is a membership organization. Members opt-in, vetted offers come to them.
- Consuli vets every study (bioethicists, advisors).

## La Jolla Institute for Immunology — LC Neurological Study

Consuli is recruiting for a La Jolla Institute LC neurological symptoms study (San Diego, requires in-person blood draw):

- Recruiting LC patients with **lasting neurological symptoms (>3 months)** seen by a neurologist
- **Requires a positive COVID test** (excludes the early-2020 cohort, including Eric and Jackie)
- Aim: identify biomarkers / circumstance correlates for LC neurological subtype
- Bio-material (blood) collection; must be local-ish to San Diego County

## Wiki value

- **Logged primarily as a research-infrastructure data point.**
- The pattern that **early-pandemic patients without positive tests are systematically excluded from "first-wave" research** is itself a wiki-worthy structural issue — explains why early-onset patients (the longest-suffering, with most data to give) are systematically left out of biomarker studies.
- Reinforces the **research coordination gap** theme — there is no global rationaliser of which LC studies should be funded first.
