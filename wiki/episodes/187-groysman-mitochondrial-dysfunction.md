Anesthesiologist / pain doctor (US) — return guest. Author of the *Complete Long COVID Handbook* series. This episode is mechanism #2 of his six-mechanism model: **mitochondrial dysfunction**.

## His six mechanisms of LC

1. **Dysautonomia** (3 subtypes: fight-or-flight, POTS, vagus nerve dysfunction) — must be treated *first*, especially fight-or-flight
2. **Mitochondrial dysfunction** — this episode's focus
3. Gut dysbiosis / leaky gut
4. Endothelial dysfunction (microclots)
5. MCAS / histamine intolerance
6. Hormone imbalance (cortisol, sex hormones, thyroid)

> Long COVID is a multi-mechanism disease. You must treat all involved mechanisms — until then, no remission.

## Why mitochondria fail in LC

- SARS-CoV-2 hijacks mitochondria for replication (so do many viruses) **but uniquely also damages the *mitophagy/recycling* mechanism**. Spike protein is the chief culprit.
- You cannot make mitochondria from scratch — they have their own DNA and must be built from existing ones. Damaged-AND-can't-recycle is the catastrophic combination.
- Result: chronic fatigue, brain fog, **post-exertional malaise** as a low-mitochondrial-reserve crash.

## Mitochondrial bursting → inflammation

- Damaged mitochondria can burst, releasing mitochondrial DNA into the cell
- The **cGAS–STING** sensor mistakes mtDNA for viral DNA → inflammatory cell death → collateral damage to neighbouring cells

## Why some treatments backfire

- **Hyperbaric oxygen / ozone**: oxidative therapies. Already-stressed mitochondria + depleted antioxidant stores = additional damage rather than the hoped-for hormetic response. Pre/post-load with antioxidants if used.
- **Aggressive fasting / mitophagy boosters (rapamycin, prolonged IF)**: can over-induce mitophagy when you have nothing to recycle into → more cell death. Great for healthy anti-aging, dangerous in severe LC.
- **Crashing daily/weekly = active damage** — Groysman is firm: routine PEM crashes accumulate mitochondrial harm.

## His protocol shape

- **Step 1 (daily, prep)**: antioxidants + mitochondrial matrix support — gets the cells ready before any mitophagy push
- **Step 2 (~7 days/month)**: gentle mitophagy + biogenesis support
- Span: 3–12+ months. Slow and gentle wins.
- *Treat dysautonomia first* — body in survival mode won't allocate resources to mitochondrial repair.

## Key quote

> "Long COVID is a multi-mechanism disease. No one supplement is the magic pill. The point is combination of supplements in a specific pattern, a specific order, so you use their synergistic properties."
> — Dr Robert Groysman, ep #187
