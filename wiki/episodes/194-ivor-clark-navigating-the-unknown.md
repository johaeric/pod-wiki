Ivor Clark — Edinburgh, Scotland; former Great-Britain American-football player and health coach. Got COVID + UTI June 2023, antibiotics gave false sense of recovery, crashed when he overdid garden/office work. ~15-month illness, recovered late 2024.

## Acute phase

- Off work from September 2023 for 6 months
- Sleeping all day; couldn't tolerate music, alcohol (even half a glass), caffeine; major dysautonomia
- Best man at friend's wedding August 2023 — popping paracetamol, sat down as much as possible, couldn't dress himself

## Chest, Heart and Stroke Scotland LC advice line

- Cat (advice-line coach) was a major emotional lifeline — having an advocate who understood
- GP (initially clueless) eventually advocated and referred him

## What helped — in roughly the order he applied them

- **HBOT at Compass charity (Edinburgh)** — 28 sessions in ~3 months (initial 20 in 5 weeks, then 1–2/week for a month). Believes this was the **catalyst** that turned things around. (Profits from his book go to Compass.)
- **Pacing with heart-rate cap** at 55% of max HR (~95 bpm for him) using a Fitbit. Theory he held: crashes reactivate the virus, so don't crash. Pacing is "art, not science."
- **Breathwork**
- **Visible app** (later in illness — found earlier-version too cognitively demanding)
- **Non-sleep deep rest** + binaural beats, 20-min rest at lunch in his car when he returned to office work
- **Vagus-nerve work** + general nervous-system regulation
- **Peptides** — the inflection. **MOTS-c** (mitochondrial peptide, weekly injection) gave a clear step-up after ~4 weeks ("best I've felt in a year"). Then immune-support peptides for another 4–8 weeks.
- **Methylene blue, urolithin A, rhodiola rosea** — tried; unclear independent contribution
- **Cleaned diet**: cut alcohol, caffeine, overstimulating music

## Return to work

- 6 months of full-pay sick leave, then financial pressure forced return
- Manager extremely supportive; HR less so
- 1 day/week → 2 days → built up; finished recovery by mid-September 2024

## Notes for Eric

A clean **HBOT + peptides** recovery testimony. Two contributions worth holding: (1) the **Compass charity** in Edinburgh provides HBOT outside the NHS — relevant if Eric ever wants UK-side HBOT access; (2) **MOTS-c** was the clear inflection — adds a peptide-based recovery row that complements the Stewart-style peptide framework (#185), but in a private-individual rather than US-clinic setting. The "treat dysautonomia first, then layer mitochondrial work" sequencing is consistent with Groysman (#187). Ivor wrote a book about his journey; 50% of profits go to Compass.

## Key quote

> "If I could give myself one piece of advice — put the foundations in earlier. Pacing, breathwork, NSDR, vagus-nerve work. Save the supplement scattergun. And try peptides sooner."
> — Ivor Clark, ep #194
