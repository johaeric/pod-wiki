# LCP #214 — Lalita: Out of the Long Covid Maze

**Guest:** Lalita — neuroplasticity coach in Tasmania, Australia. Trained with **[[howard-schubiner]]** and his team (Schubiner's neuroplastic-symptoms / EAET methodology). Previously recovered from lupus, fibromyalgia, chronic fatigue, and chronic pain (~14 years well before LC) using the same nervous-system framework — this is her **second** rebuild on the same playbook.

**Recovery status:** Full recovery in **12–14 months max**. Tinnitus was the last symptom to go.

## Trigger and clinical picture

- **First COVID bout (pre-pandemic awareness)** — six weeks of dreadful illness after a big interstate conference. Left a residual: BP went from chronically low to high (started on lowest-dose BP medication), numb lips, fatigue, afternoon naps needed.
- **Second bout end of August 2024** — mild acute illness but symptom cascade after. ~30 different symptoms over the months that followed:
  - Tachycardia (early, drove first cardiology workup)
  - Vertigo / dizziness — couldn't lie flat, slept sitting up on 3-4 pillows
  - Lead-feeling in legs and arms; chest tightness
  - Three ER visits for chest pain — all cleared cardiac; told it was anxiety
  - Air hunger, claustrophobia (couldn't tolerate confined spaces / scans)
  - Brain fog, headaches, muscle aches, costochondritis-pattern chest tightness
  - Stabbing pains that migrated (right breast → spleen → ovary)
  - GI issues, irritable bladder, hormonal disruption
  - **POTS-pattern** — got formal **dysautonomia umbrella diagnosis**
  - Tinnitus (last to resolve)
  - Anemia / low iron (new for a lifelong vegetarian)
  - Reactivated **EBV, herpes** (picked up on bio-resonance scan; both dormant for ~30 years prior)

## The recovery framework

Lalita's central thesis: **autonomic nervous-system dysregulation is the upstream driver of LC** and she "knew exactly what to do" from her prior recovery. She did not chase the diagnostic rabbit hole. She had:

1. **100% belief in the framework** ("I had a long history of chronic illness before and the way that I recovered from that was using nervous system")
2. **Schubiner's "5 Fs" (now 7 Fs)** — Fear, Focus, Frustration, etc. as the danger-response inventory
3. **In-the-moment tools, applied relentlessly** (sometimes 30-40×/day)

### Primary tool: breathwork

> *"The thing that really really I think propelled my healing more than anything else was the breath work."*

- **4-in / 6-out** as the default pattern. Used dozens of times per day at the first hint of symptom (dizziness, air hunger, chest pain, panic).
- **"Sniff-sniff-poo"** (a grandchild's phrase for a quick double-inhale-long-exhale) for panic/anxiety surges.
- **Self-talk during the breath**: *"You're going to be okay. Everything's all right. This is just your nervous system. You're not in danger."* Combined breath + self-compassion is what landed.
- Three long slow breaths would resolve the symptom in real time — the **proof loop**: tool → relief → brain registers "she's safe."

### Stacked tools

- **[[eft-tapping]]** — combined with breathing and self-talk; three-way combo for high-symptom moments
- **[[somatic-tracking]]** — body-scan + notice + acknowledge emotion + self-compassion + breath
- **[[yoga-nidra]]** — every night; mid-day if a nap was needed
- **Schubiner's [[eaet]] framework** — used to dig deeper when patterns repeated. Identified a specific pattern: more panic on heart symptoms *when alone* → traced to her father dying of heart attack at 48, alone. After an EAET-format session with another practitioner, *"that never happened again."*
- **Trauma processing for repeating patterns** — the dig-deeper move when symptoms loop

### Supplements (naturopath-guided)

Done in parallel with the nervous-system work. Goal: support the body's healing state, not replace the central work.

- [[nattokinase]] (fibrinolytic — for microclot-adjacent concern)
- Dandelion tincture, stinging nettle tea (anti-inflammatory / liver / herbal)
- [[quercetin]] (mast-cell stabiliser)
- Black seed oil (immune)
- Multivitamins, D
- **[[antihistamines]]** — specifically credited with helping the tinnitus resolve
- "Saltwater therapy" (electrolytes)
- Prebiotics + probiotics (gut)
- [[curcumin]]
- Iron tablets (when anemia found via bio-resonance)

### Hands-on / diagnostic adjuncts

- **[[bowen-therapy]]** — for aches and pains; used previously, brought back in this rebuild
- **[[bio-resonance-therapy]]** — diagnostic frequency-based scan (popular in Germany/Poland); picked up reactivated EBV + herpes + iron deficiency. **Caveat — bio-resonance is not mainstream medicine; mechanism is contested. Lalita uses it as one input.**
- **[[cranial-sacral-therapy]]** — for nervous-system support

### Lifestyle

- **Low-carb, paleo-pattern diet**, small meals (gut support)
- Good sleep, good hydration
- Yoga nidra nightly
- Quit chasing — *"I didn't go down the rabbit hole like I see so many people doing"*

### The mindset move that mattered

- **Decided up-front**: "I will be done in 12 months max." Lalita acknowledges this might have been a placebo / expectancy effect — she's fine with that. Coupled with a no-pressure framing: if she'd been past 12 months she'd have reassured herself ("look at all the symptoms that have gone, only a couple left") rather than turning the deadline into a stick.
- **Self-compassion as the daily protocol**, not as a vague aspiration. Imagine talking to a child or a best friend; build the muscle.
- **"Trust yourself"** — the most consolidated reframe in the episode. Strangers' recovery stories are data; your own knowing about your body is the substrate.

## Recovery arc

- Second bout: late August 2024
- POTS symptoms among the **first to resolve** (which she found striking — "previously had POTS for years")
- GI, sleep, brain fog resolved progressively over months
- **Tinnitus the last**, helped substantially by the antihistamines
- Full recovery declared at **12–14 months** (so spring–autumn 2025)

## Coaching anecdote — N=1

Lalita's coaching practice: a woman in her mid-20s, multiple ER visits, all told it was anxiety, couldn't drive or work. Lalita's approach: identify 3-4 main pre-LC stressors/traumas + pair with breathing and small NS exercises. **Next session a week later** — patient was driving herself + part-time back at work. Full recovery in ~1 month.

> *"It can be different for everybody. Different for everybody."*

This N=1 is a striking second data point inside her own episode — but obviously not replicated elsewhere and not externally verified.

## Notable framing

- **Belief × consistency** is the engine. The work *itself* (breath, tapping, self-talk) isn't novel; it's the **30-40×/day relentlessness** and the absolute conviction in the framework that distinguish the recovered cohort from the trying-cohort.
- **"Reconnecting to yourself"** — Lalita and Jackie agree the journey is partly an unwinding of *living for other people's expectations*. The wiki has heard this same arc from many recovered guests ([[077-jackies-thoughts-2023]], [[100-michelle-irving-emotional-empowerment]], [[178-alisha]]).
