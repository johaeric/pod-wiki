# #162 — Carrie Bailey: Functional Nutrition (pt 1)

US functional nutritionist (Institute for Integrative Nutrition health-coaching origin → functional medicine → functional-nutrition training). Niche-shifted to LC after a YouTube video on LC went viral. Two-part episode; this is the framing + digestion piece. Pt 2 (#163) covers supplements + elimination diets.

## Bailey's working model

**Root cause of all chronic illness = pathogens + toxins.**

- *Pathogens*: viruses (esp. EBV, shingles), unwanted bacteria (H. pylori, E. coli), microbes, fungi, mould, parasites/worms (one client passed nematodes after starting celery juice).
- *Toxins*: heavy metals (Mercury, lead, aluminum since the Industrial Revolution), pesticides, plastics. Toxins **feed the pathogens**.
- Triggering events (covid, vaccine, divorce, marriage, car accident) activate latent pathogens — *"like waking a hibernating bear"*. Once awake, pathogens eat, replicate, excrete neurotoxins → mental health symptoms / brain fog / inflammation / vagus-nerve inflammation.

Mental health (anxiety, depression) framed as **rooted in physical health** — pathogen waste + heavy metals interfere with neurotransmitter precursors (tryptophan → serotonin shunted down inflammation pathway).

## Digestion mechanics

- Most LC patients have **insufficient stomach acid** (should be "battery acid, not vinegar"). Reflux/GERD is often *low* not high acid.
- Stomach acid → triggers bile from liver → triggers pancreatic enzymes. Vagal damage from virus → reduced peristalsis → SIBO / bacterial overgrowth.
- Bailey's contrarian framings: **Liver is over-burdened with fat** (US protein-pushing → too much fat in diet → blood thickens → liver can't filter → nutrient malabsorption). Fruit + vegetables anti-viral, anti-bacterial; stripping them out (low-FODMAP, carnivore) starves the body of healing compounds.
- **Food combining**: don't combine fat with sugar/starch (cookie = flour + sugar + butter + eggs = insulin resistance not from sugar but from sugar+fat combo). Fruit best on empty stomach in morning before fat is in the system.

## Diet stance

- Crowding-out > taking-out. Get fruits + veg in first; bad foods naturally decrease.
- **Carnivore / strict elimination = short-term tool, not long-term plan.** Bailey sees clients arrive on 3-food carnivore diets, increasingly reactive — interpretable as starvation of healing compounds + reactivity to pathogens being killed (a banana as antimicrobial → "skunk-like" toxin release on death = patient feels worse and blames the banana).
- Low-FODMAP misapplied long-term as "I have a banana problem" when the issue is dosage / timing not the food.

## Why this episode matters

- **First wiki anchor for the functional-nutrition / pathogen-and-toxin frame.** Bailey sits in the same lineage as Lorrie Rivers (#70 — hidden parasites/dysbiosis) and overlap with Carrie Vitt / Anthony William (Medical Medium) world but more clinically grounded. Names EBV, shingles, H. pylori as common LC drivers.
- **Liver-and-fat thesis is contrarian.** Most LC dietary content emphasises protein-up; Bailey says protein-up = fat-up = liver-stagnant. Logged as a minority position; not endorsed.
- **Food-combining framing** is wiki-new. Pairs with [[low-fodmap-diet]] critique.
- **Banana-as-medicine / dose-dependent reactions** reframes "food intolerance" as "killing pathogens too fast." Useful counterfoil for patients who have eliminated long lists of foods.

## Eric-relevant takeaways

- Bailey's framework is **not** in evidence parity with Eric's current stack — but the *food-combining* idea (fruit on empty stomach in morning; don't pair fat with sugar) is a low-cost contained experiment.
- Her stance on long elimination diets (carnivore, low-FODMAP) being short-term is broadly aligned with the wiki's existing pattern (cf. Saperstein #68 cautions, Sampson #99 microbiome work).
- Heavy-metal / toxin frame overlaps with [[154-sean-moran-recovery]] but Bailey extends it to *all* chronic illness — wider claim.
