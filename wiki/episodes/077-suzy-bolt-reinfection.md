# #77 — Suzy Bolt on Fears of Reinfection

Suzy returns (after #7). Her third covid infection had just resolved at recording — second and third were "much milder" than 2020. The episode is the wiki's first dedicated treatment of **reinfection-fear as itself a clinical issue** rather than a clinical risk.

## Bolt's empirical view from her community

> "For 99% of people I've had contact with, current variants are much gentler. Bodies recognise it and get through it much quicker. Liberating once people see this — they can come out of isolation."

Calls out a specific bias: long-covid sufferers tend to **read horror stories of reinfection** rather than the (more common) milder reinfection stories. The community has both — but the negative bias is louder.

## The mantra reframe — "immune system as superpower"

Bolt's central technique:

1. **Identify your current belief about your immune system.** Often: "rubbish / always getting sick / can't cope."
2. **Notice that belief is a primer to the brain → immune system command chain.** A pre-loaded "I won't cope" is a self-fulfilling instruction.
3. **Replace with a deliberately useful belief**: "my immune system is my superpower."
4. **Embody it**: hand on heart while saying it; make it a daily mantra.
5. **Build evidence**: track real wins (didn't catch X from kids, dealt with Y faster than expected).

> "I started saying 'my immune system is my superpower' and I would laugh because it wasn't really. Then it slowly became the truth. Since 2020 I've only got sick with covid — not the strep, the colds, anything else my kids brought home."

This is **not** a "positive thinking cures things" claim. It's a **brain → immune-system → body coupling** argument: the same priming that the polyvagal/mind-body literature uses elsewhere in the wiki.

## "5% stretch" — the gradual exposure principle

For a partner returning to socialising / pubs / offices while the LC patient stays home, this creates relationship strain. Bolt's frame:

- The fear is not curing itself by isolation — it's **the loudest signal driving the nervous system**.
- Sitting at home in fear creates the "perfect storm" for the next exposure.
- **Do not stretch by 100%**. Stretch by 5% — go to one part of one event, then leave.
- "Just begin to build up confidence that it's possible. Otherwise you're both signing up to a relationship that's dysfunctional and that will cause tension."

## The Boon Lim quote (#40, anchored here too)

> "Don't let what you cannot do prevent you from doing what you can."

Bolt invokes this twice in this episode as Eric's Post-it-note quote (Eric mentions to her that Boon said it to him; this episode confirms it back).

## Bolt's third reinfection — narrative contrast

- **2020 (first)**: most unhappy in years, stressful relationship, multiple unresolved life-stressors. **Severe long covid for 18+ months.**
- **2023 (third)**: the major life-stressor (separation from husband) had been resolved gracefully. She felt "ecstatically happy" in bed during the infection. Septic-tonsillitis-level acute illness; cleared in 2-3 weeks. Back to HIIT class on the day of recording.

Her conclusion: **the same virus + a different nervous-system substrate = a different illness**. Aligns with #76 Groysman ("compensation, not cure"), #66 Stiles, #57 Gupta, #40 Lim — converging on autonomic substrate as a risk modifier.

## The friend with lupus

A friend with lupus + degenerative lung disease was told to never leave the house at the start of the pandemic. Her psychotherapist's framing: locking down forever → states of fear/depression → autoimmune activation → worse outcomes. She **chose to live**. Got covid twice; bad cough, recovered. Bolt's takeaway: even the highest-risk individuals face a tradeoff between *biological exposure risk* and *psychological/autoimmune exposure risk*.

## Eric-relevant intake

- **Most relevant single episode in the wiki for Eric's reinfection-fear.** Worth re-listening to.
- Confirms: fear-of-reinfection is itself a pattern that may keep autonomic dysregulation locked in. The work is on the fear, not (only) on avoiding the virus.
- **Combine with [[073-william-li]]'s metformin-prevention insight**: if reinfection happens, there is now a real prevention option to ask for. Reduces the catastrophic frame.
- 5% stretch is a directly applicable technique.
- Bolt emphasises: get **proper professional support** for the fear-work — not "just decide to stop being afraid."

## Notable claims

> "It's not the virus that creates the response in the body — the brain creates the response to the virus. That's why some people die and some don't notice it."

> "Every reinfection of mine has been milder than the last. I have changed my brain priming around this."

> "Engage in joy and gratitude. That creates a super-state of immune health that fear cannot."
