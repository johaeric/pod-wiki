# Episode 127 — Rachael's Long Covid Recovery Story

Rachael is an ex-competitive-tennis player, corporate professional, personal trainer, married, with a toddler born during her illness. Caught Covid July 2021 while wife was 7 weeks pregnant; pushed through into a boom-bust pattern; reinfected later that year and became bedbound/housebound. Daughter born February 2022 with Rachael still severely ill.

**Two phases of illness:**
1. *Boom-bust* — six months of dragging through workdays, weekends spent recovering. Crashed badly in office at 5 months in; sent home for rest of year.
2. *Bedbound* after reinfection. Encountered a respected ME specialist who told her LC was ME and non-recoverable — sent her into spiral including suicidal ideation 2 months before her daughter's birth.

**What turned it around (sequence matters):**
- *Stopped working.* Sat down with her wife, looked at finances, agreed worst-case was acceptable. First and biggest catalyst — gave her capacity to actually try things.
- *Suzy Bolt's program* — first felt seen in community + gentle movement.
- *Curable app* (~$50/yr) — couldn't afford or commit to face-to-face Gupta. Liked the bite-size neuroscience modules. Heard recovery story of a 14-year ME patient → formed her own hypothesis: she had become *conditioned* to need bed. Decided not to lie down no matter how rubbish she felt; 2–3 weeks later she didn't routinely need to. That self-experiment gave her enough evidence to commit and ~12 more months of practice produced gradual non-linear improvement.
- *Move from London flat to countryside* — body audibly "exhaled."
- *Cold water exposure.* Tried at 6 months in (worst phase) — awful, made fatigue worse. Tried again ~1 year later with body more resilient — game-changing.

**Recovery markers:** No single line in the sand. Glastonbury 2023 on a friend's shoulders during *Rocket Man* — first time she ever told herself she was proud. By spring 2024, residual post-bug-from-toddler dampening, otherwise fully engaged in life.

**Now runs Rebound Athletic** — online fitness coaching with a 12-week reconditioning program designed for de-trained / post-LC bodies. Coaching includes fear-of-exercise work. ~50% LC clients.

**Advice to past self:**
1. Stop working immediately.
2. Drop ego about identity and timelines.
3. Try not to respond with so much anxiety — her wife being pregnant amplified the anxiety loop.
4. *Movement with joy* — swings with her daughter, dancing to drum-and-bass with toddler on her back. Same activity reframed (different physical or emotional context) doesn't trigger the same fear association.

> "If you can recover this much, then full recovery has to be possible."
> — Rachael, ep #127
