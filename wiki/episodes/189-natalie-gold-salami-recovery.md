Natalie Gold — CFO of an Italian company, lives on a small farm in California with 16-year history of **psoriatic arthritis** managed well before LC. Got COVID July 2021, mild acute illness, but never recovered. Severe POTS-presentation, orthostatic intolerance — could only be upright 5 minutes, then 90 minutes to recover.

## Acute phase

- Unresolved trauma background: severe **wildfires** had hit her community years before
- Within 6 months: brain fog, insomnia, sound sensitivities, isolated palpitations without mental panic
- Continued working from bed for 2 years (CFO of a small company) — couldn't acquire new knowledge but could leverage old patterns
- Lowest point ~15% function. Repetitive intrusive thoughts (the famous "you should buy salami" loop)

## What helped

- **Pacing** at much smaller increments than she initially thought
- **B vitamins** — modest boost
- **Low-dose aspirin** (her own decision, not prescribed) — clinical hunch on microclot/burning-lung sensation. Boost may be placebo, but *she reinvested it into recovery*.
- **Discovered recovery stories**: heard Joanna Rayl's recovery story on this podcast (#173-ish era) and identified strongly. Took 6 weeks to commit to **ANS Rewire** (Dan Neuffer).
- ANS Rewire — slow-burn recovery over 2 years. She specifically liked: small daily videos, education-first framing, root-cause focus on autonomic/limbic dysregulation, and Dan's encouragement to *adapt rather than follow rigidly* ("don't do the Dan Neuffer program — do the Natalie Gold program")
- **Recognised stuck personality patterns** — controller, perfectionist, helper, worrier, achiever. Created characters and talked to them (e.g. "the rebel returns library books late")
- **Meditation + body-sensation tracking** — noticed where in body wounded-child reactions lived (e.g. "the tree is dying and it's my fault" → little-girl stomach feeling). Trauma work without big-T trauma — "small-t messages" from a good childhood still shape the nervous system.
- **Creativity / Artist's Way** — let perfectionism off
- **Goal: write a book that's adequate, not the best book ever** — perfectionist reframe

## Final marker of recovery

Returning to **Lindy Hop swing dancing** late nights, sometimes without earplugs. Friends say she dances better than before. Psoriatic arthritis, chronic migraine, chronic back/neck pain — **all resolved or reduced to whispers** alongside LC recovery.

## Key quote

> "I was so unaware of how unwell I was. When I left my job I said I was at 65%. A few months later I realised I'd been at 30%."
> — Natalie Gold, ep #189
