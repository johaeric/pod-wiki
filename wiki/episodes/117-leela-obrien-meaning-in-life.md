# #117 — Leela O'Brien: navigating LC + finding meaning in life

US-based aerospace engineer (PhD; ex-NASA contractor), rock climber, ice climber, runner, skier — became ill with Covid; long-hauler; **partial recovery once, then re-flared**, currently still managing motor-function symptoms. **Not a full-recovery story. Logged for the philosophical / acceptance-and-hope framing.**

## Pre-illness pattern

- 36 years old at recording; aerospace engineer; PhD; ex-NASA contractor on a space mission
- Rock climber, ice climber, runner, skier
- "Type-A perfectionist, very driven, high stress"
- **Burnout pre-Covid:** within a year of finishing PhD she hit a focus wall — couldn't focus, calling her mum crying, no break taken
- Self-aware in retrospect: "Covid was the straw that broke the camel's back"

## LC profile

- Cognitive function affected → couldn't do her job well
- Severe motor dysfunction at peak — couldn't walk, could hardly move, hands and feet
- 2-3 months at the worst when she "could only sit on the couch and drink hot chocolate"
- Initial recovery substantial — she got back to climbing/running at strength
- Then re-flared (her ego getting back in driver's seat = "life served me a slice of humble pie")

## What helped

1. **Meditation** — became a substitute for outdoor activities when bedbound; got her into the same flow-state / right-brain mode she got from climbing/running
2. **Walking outside** — eventually; even just shuffling outside and looking at the sky
3. **Acceptance + hope balance** — Leela's load-bearing recovery framework (see below)
4. **Reframing identity away from accomplishments** — therapy + reflection; "I'm so much more than what I achieve"

## Anchor: hot chocolate as the can-do reframe

Leela articulates this most explicitly:

> "There was a 2-3 month period where I could hardly walk and all I could do was sit on the couch and drink hot chocolate. And I was like — actually, this is nice. Let me find the blessings, let me find the good. It feels a lot better than feeling miserable all the time and playing victim."

Same wiki principle as Jackie + Sarah Reynolds + Charlotte Hammond etc — *the can rather than the can't* — but Leela's specific framing (what's literally on the table right now, as small as a hot drink) is the most concrete handle the wiki has logged.

## Anchor: the acceptance ↔ hope balance

> "For the hope piece — we don't know anything for certain. However I have no reason to believe I'm not going to heal. I might have all the data against me, but why should I give in to that? It limits the possibility. Mind and body together can make it happen. But I shouldn't put pressure on myself to heal — that pressure is unhealthy and it makes you more sick."

Leela names this as the wiki's first articulation of **belief-without-pressure**. Pairs with [[107-raelan-agle-recovery-stories]] hope-as-input — but Leela adds: *believing in healing AND removing the pressure to heal both at once.*

## Anchor: ego vs passion fuel

The flare-and-relapse pattern Leela traces:

- Pre-Covid: started fueled by passion + creativity → drifted into ego (need accomplishment for self-worth)
- LC dropped her into deep introspection — she dropped the ego
- Initial recovery → ego came back ("look at me defying long covid") → re-flare
- Now she names this as the flare's mechanism

The wiki's strongest formulation of "*what fuels you matters more than what you do*." Cf. [[094-jackies-recovery-story]] perfectionism-as-recovery-blocker.

## Anchor: Stephen Hawking framing

> "I look at people who can't move at all and I see joy in their eyes. They've found a way. I always think back to Stephen Hawking — what he made of his ALS. He went deep within his mind and found his meaning. We're all capable of that."

Generalises: **physical capacity sets the *frame* but not the *quality* of life inside it.** The wiki's mood-vs-physical-recovery decoupling — [[100-michelle-irving-emotional-empowerment]] articulates this most explicitly via the Emotional Empowerment Map; Leela articulates it as a single-image anchor.

## Eric-relevance

- **Hot-chocolate anchor** is portable and operational — when Eric is in a low-symptom-tolerance day, the question becomes "what's the hot-chocolate equivalent right now."
- **Belief-without-pressure** is the cleanest articulation of the recovery mindset Eric will need post-recovery: *believing this can heal AND not pushing for it to heal* simultaneously.
- **Ego-fuel as relapse mechanism** is a useful lens — Eric is already aware of his perfectionism (CLAUDE.md context); Leela's flare pattern is a concrete warning: *initial-recovery + ego-return → re-flare.*
- **Outdoor-connection as recovery substrate** continues to accumulate evidence (Jackie + Leela + Sarah Reynolds for cold-water-no, Sarah-Lotus-Path for walking) — the wiki should hold "find an outdoor practice scaled to your current envelope" as a recurring recovery feature.
