# #114 — Sarah (Lotus Path Yogi): full LC recovery via Myhill protocol + Raelan Agle's gradual movement + microbiome rehab (**11th full recovery on the wiki**)

Sheffield-based yoga teacher and host of the **Long Covid Hope podcast**. Caught Covid first wave March 2020 with a baby on maternity leave; full LC for ~2 years; **declared recovery ~spring 2022**. Wiki's 11th full recovery.

## Pre-illness context

- Mum to a baby (was on maternity leave; UK 1-year maternity)
- Yoga teacher, very active outdoors (Peak District)
- Worked also in anatomy publishing
- Full active life pre-Covid

## Acute illness

- Sick Friday 13 March 2020
- Lower respiratory hit "like a ton of bricks"; chest squeezing pain; turning blue; SpO2 88
- Hospital A&E — pneumonia + ground-glass opacities on X-ray; **never tested positive for Covid** (early; tests not given to under-35s)
- Sent home with broad-spectrum antibiotics; ~3 weeks of "back to normal" before LC
- Then: hiked 10 miles in 25°C heat with baby in carrier — major chest pain on return = **start of LC**

## LC profile

- Chronic fatigue → bedbound, crawling to toilet
- POTS (diagnosed via tilt-table Sept 2020 — among the earliest LC POTS dx in Sheffield); beta-blockers helped
- Inflammation in lungs
- Boom-bust on cortisol/adrenaline running mom-duties till 2-3pm then total crash
- Worsening peripheral neuropathy (numb foot tip; tingling) after Covid vaccinations
- Gluten + (lesser) dairy intolerance developed post-Covid

## Recovery stack — what worked

1. **[[body-politic]] Slack** (early) — validation; learned about POTS, did self stand test, asked for tilt; **knowledge as power**
2. **Beta blockers** ([[beta-blockers]]) post-tilt-test (Sept 2020) — first win; could sit/stand more
3. **Pacing + activity tracking** via NHS chronic fatigue clinic — useful diagnostic; clinic was still doing GET so she discharged herself before that part
4. **Yoga nidra + meditations + breath-work practice 10-12× per day** — "enforced rest"; Sarah credits this as **the single biggest factor** for nervous-system + pacing stability. Multiple short sessions throughout the day, not one long one.
5. **Dr Sarah Myhill protocol** ([[018-dr-sarah-myhill]] indirectly — see #118): paleo ketogenic diet (9 months); **B12 injections** (for neuropathy + fatigue); **D-ribose powder** ([[ribose]]) — Sarah felt the D-ribose effect within 1-2 weeks: "my body felt like it really needed it"; mitochondrial supplements
6. **Gluten-free diet** — Sarah's interpretation: the keto helped mostly because it removed gluten (intolerance she developed post-Covid)
7. **[[107-raelan-agle-recovery-stories|Raelan Agle's YouTube]]** — listened (couldn't watch screen) to many recovery stories; got hope + the **scaffolding-first graded resistance protocol** (1 minute body-weight resistance per day, increase weekly if no PEM)
8. **Fermented foods** ([[probiotics]]) — Raelan's prebiotics + fermented foods recommendation; Sarah went all-in (whole bottle of kombucha day 1 — Herx-reaction caveat). Pre this she'd been on capsule probiotics for a year with no obvious effect. Sarah believes the **gut microbiome was a missing piece**.
9. **Gradual movement re-introduction** — Raelan's protocol; built up to 15 min resistance + walks 1 → 3 → 5 → 7 miles with no PEM = **she knew she'd recovered**
10. **Daily yoga (1 hour) — but the right kind** — supportive, seated/lying, no inversions, no fast vinyasa; Sarah explicitly calls out yoga's own ableism and the wrong-style-yoga problem for LC

## Anchor: enforced rest is not lying-down-on-the-phone

> "Lying down and scrolling on your phone — that's not rest either. That's exerting." — Sarah

The principle: **completely calm the nervous system, multiple times a day, structured, with breath/meditation guidance.** Sarah's mechanism interpretation: the body needs leftover energy in the envelope to heal.

## Anchor: scaffolding-first graded resistance (cf. Raelan #107)

Sarah's articulation of Raelan's protocol:

- 1 minute body-weight resistance/day (e.g., 4 squats, or a yoga pose held for 60s)
- Hold for a week; if no PEM, increase to 90s
- Iterative; listen to body; **never prescriptive on a fixed daily increase**
- Built up to ~15 min/day over months
- The key is **avoid PEM at all costs** while slowly extending the envelope

This is what GET was *supposed* to be but never operationally was — patient-driven, PEM-stopping, scaffolding (sleep / stress / gut / NS) in place first.

## Recovery declaration

- Spring 2022: 5+ months without PEM; doing 5-7 mile walks; "very nervous" about declaring; eventually posted in Body Politic's "recovered" channel
- ~2 years from initial Covid
- Now: runs **Lotus Path Yoga** classes (Mon Sheffield, Thu online, Fri guesting for Yoga for ME charity); 4 on-demand courses; **400-500 students taught**; *zero patients reported PEM after one of her sessions*; hosts the **Long Covid Hope podcast**

- **B12 injections** — the wiki has implicit support from the Myhill protocol (#118); Sarah's neuropathy reversal is a concrete data point. Worth flagging that vaccine-onset neuropathy is in the mechanism story.
- **The gut microbiome diversification piece** lines up exactly with [[099-viola-sampson-microbiome]]; Sarah is the patient-recovery side of that mechanism.
- **Yoga done right matters more than yoga in general** — Sarah is the wiki's clearest articulation that "I tried yoga and crashed" usually means "I tried wrong yoga." Pairs with [[085-fiona-agombar-yoga-therapy]].
- **Scaffolding-first graded resistance** — Sarah is the wiki's first detailed *recovery-template* of Raelan's framework with specific numbers Eric could try.
- **Enforced rest 10-12×/day** — most concrete operational version of "rest as treatment" yet seen on the wiki. The number is specific and reproducible.
- For Eric's own POTS profile: Sarah's beta-blocker → activity titration → recovery is a clean POTS-route success template. (Eric is on ivabradine instead of beta-blockers; mechanism similar enough for principle to apply.)
