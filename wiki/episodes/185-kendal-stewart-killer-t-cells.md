Texas physician — ENT-trained surgeon (skull-base) turned **functional/integrative doctor** running genetics labs ("neutrigenomics"). Hosts the *Coffee with Dr Stewart* podcast.

## Core mental model

Long COVID is a **syndrome state**, not a disease — six mechanism strands intersecting. Stewart's framework prioritises **inflammation control** before any other layer.

### Microglial activation
- Brain's resident immune cells. Once turned on, they release glutamate (stimulant) → racing brain → can't rest → can't repair.
- Three off-switch systems for inflammation: JAK-STAT, TNF, toll-like receptors. Stewart genetic-tests which off-switch is broken in each patient.
- Particularly relevant: ~76% of Northern European Anglo descent have aggressive on-switches; ~60% have at least one broken off-switch.

### Four levers to turn microglia off
1. **Steroids** — vitamin D, cortisol, testosterone, progesterone (good for JAK-STAT)
2. **Endorphin/opiate receptor** — **LDN at 3mg** (vs the 100mg for addiction); preserves endorphin tone while turning down immune over-activation
3. **CB2 receptor** (cannabinoids)
4. **Thymic receptor → peptides** — see below

### Thymic peptides (his signature intervention)
- **Thymosin alpha-1** and thymosin beta-4 — secreted by thymus to recover T-cells. Bioidentical peptides.
- Used in his clinic to recover exhausted CD4/CD8/CD56 cells after they've been overwhelmed by chronic spike-protein presence.
- Story: many peptides came from leaked Russian sports-doping research (2018 whistleblower in Australia).

### Quieting the racing brain
- **Amantadine** (1972 Parkinson's drug from star anise) — blocks glutamate / NMDA receptor; "lets the brain take a break" so it can recover even after the immune trigger is removed.

### Other points
- **IL-5 mutation** found in ~95%+ of his Long COVID patients — also handles herpes-family viruses (EBV, CMV, VZV), explaining secondary reactivations.
- **D-chiro-inositol (vitamin B8 form)** — supports insulin-mediated glucose delivery to mitochondria.
- **Glutathione/methylation** — detoxification arm.
- Old plant-derived medicines: **ivermectin, hydroxychloroquine** — uses both off-label.
- **Plasmapheresis** mentioned: even if it removes spike, brain still needs to be calmed for cognitive recovery.

## Key quote

> "Even if you cleared the immune challenge, if the brain's in this racing state and I can't slow it down, good luck getting rid of the cognitive problems. The causation may be gone but the brain still has to heal."
> — Dr Kendal Stewart, ep #185

## Notes for Eric

Layer this against ercipedia: many of these claims (LDN dose rationale, microglial activation, IL-5/herpes link) are clinical-experience claims, not RCT-grounded. The amantadine angle is novel on this podcast. The thymic-peptide framing is less well evidenced than it sounds — peptide therapy in the US is still largely outside FDA-approved use. Use this episode for *generating hypotheses to test with your clinicians*, not as a treatment menu.
