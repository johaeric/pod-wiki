# #53 — Dr Deepak Ravindran — Long Covid & Pain

[[deepak-ravindran]] is a consultant in pain medicine, lead for the **Berkshire Long Covid Integrated Service** (NHS, England — one of ~90 LC clinics; 1,800+ patients seen at recording), and author of a Vermillion-published book on pain. The episode is the **anchor pain episode** for the wiki: Ravindran walks through the mechanism, classification, and treatment strategy for LC pain.

## Headline framing

Pain in LC is **mostly nociplastic** (central sensitization), not nociceptive (tissue damage) or neuropathic (nerve damage). That changes everything about what should be tried.

## Pain prevalence in LC

- In the Berkshire clinic, **>70% of patients score ≥6/10** on aches and pains in muscles/joints in the **Yorkshire Rehab Screen** (a validated LC questionnaire developed in Leeds).
- Pain is therefore as common as fatigue and breathlessness in LC — but **systematically under-reported** because most LC clinics elsewhere don't use a pain-specific questionnaire. *If you don't measure it, you don't see it.*

## The three types of pain (and why this matters)

| Type | Mechanism | Responds to |
|---|---|---|
| **Nociceptive** | Tissue injury/inflammation; chemicals released, transmitted via nerves | NSAIDs, paracetamol, tramadol, opioids — short term. Steroids/injections for inflammation. |
| **Neuropathic** | Nerve damage (stroke, diabetes, chemo, sciatica) | Gabapentin, amitriptyline, duloxetine |
| **Nociplastic** | Central sensitization — nervous + immune system stay sensitized; no structural damage; no chemical inflammation visible on labs | **Drugs and injections rarely effective.** Calm immune + nervous system: sleep, nutrition, mind-body, gentle movement |

The category **nociplastic pain** was only formalized by IASP in 2017–18. It is the same biological pattern long understood in fibromyalgia, ME/CFS, chronic migraine, IBS, and some chronic low back pain. **LC fits this pattern.**

## Why scans come back clean

> "More than 90% of my patients are not coming up with anything sinister or anything structurally damaging on scans... To me that means more than 90% are having a reversible potentially reversible immune and nervous system hypersensitivity." — Ravindran

There is no correlation between MRI/X-ray findings and pain intensity. Asymptomatic adults routinely have ≥40–60% rates of disc bulges, cartilage tears, and arthritic changes on imaging. **Listen to the patient, not the scan.**

## What works for nociplastic LC pain

Drugs are largely a side dish. The main course is **calming the immune + autonomic nervous system**:

- **Sleep strategies** (foundational)
- **Nutrition** — whole foods, minimally processed, anti-inflammatory
- **Mind–body**: yoga, tai chi, Pilates, mindfulness, **yoga nidra**, relaxation techniques
- **Gentle physical activity** (carefully paced)
- **TENS machines**
- **Cold water** (with PEM caution)
- **Hydrotherapy / gravity-unaided water therapy**
- **CBD/cannabis** — clinical interest but NHS-unavailable; some patients self-report benefit
- **Pre-existing arthritis flares** may genuinely respond to drug therapy — distinguish via history

## Berkshire clinic structure

- ~1,800 patients seen in 18 months
- Secondary-care assessment + diagnostic + investigation arm
- Community rehab arm
- Pediatric: small spoke; Hub is Oxford
- Secured **£50,000 from NHS Charities** for adjunctive interventions (hydrotherapy, machine-assisted gentle movement) — pragmatic acknowledgment that orthodox NHS care doesn't cover what nociplastic-pain patients actually need.
