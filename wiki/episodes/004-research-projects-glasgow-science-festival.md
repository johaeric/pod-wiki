# #004 — Research Projects & the Glasgow Science Festival

**Guests**: [[jane-ormerod]] (Long Covid Scotland research subgroup co-chair, retired NHS) and [[chris-white]] (mental health background, research subgroup co-chair). Both still ill, both using prior professional skills to bridge patients and academic researchers.

A meta-episode about **how patient-and-public-involvement (PPI) shapes long-covid research in Scotland** — not about any specific treatment.

## Studies / trials named

- [[loco-rise]] — Eddie Duncan; rehabilitation services for LC across four Scottish health boards. Jane on management group. (Own ep #22.)
- [[redirect]] — remote intervention for weight management to benefit LC. Just launching; 2-year study. (Own ep #16.)
- A **pacing study** using an app linked to a Fitbit + HR feedback (name not given — researcher messages back when activity/HR cross thresholds). Jane involved.
- A **reproductive health in LC** study just being shaped.
- [[zoe-covid-study]] — app-based prevalence/symptom-tracking; anyone can join, including never-tested first-wavers.

## Notable arguments

- **PPI changes research, not just decorates it**: critique of language, questionnaire design, app usability, cohort framing. "Are these even the questions I'd be asking?"
- **Online has democratised PPI**: Jane (north of Scotland) couldn't participate in person at Glasgow / Edinburgh / UWS — virtual format overcomes more barriers than it creates, even with the digital-divide caveat.
- **Symptom relief is the right near-term target** while cures remain distant.
- **Cross-condition learning matters**: the [[redirect]] approach borrows from T2D weight-management protocols; Chris reversed his own T2D this way before getting LC.
- **Carer voices belong in research too** — partners observe symptoms patients minimise.

## Recruitment caveats

- Participation must accommodate PEM ("crash and burn"); pacing the patient-research workforce mirrors the science.
- Younger / paediatric LC needs different cohorts (Long Covid Kids).
