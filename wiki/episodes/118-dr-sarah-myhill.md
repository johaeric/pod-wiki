# #118 — Dr Sarah Myhill: paleo-keto + mitochondrial + upper-fermenting-gut framework for ME/CFS + LC

UK GP since 1981. Author of multiple books on CFS / ME / mitochondrial dysfunction. Operates outside mainstream NHS medicine; has been adjacent to / in dispute with UK regulators historically. **The wiki should treat her framework as heterodox-clinician evidence — strong internal coherence, weak external validation, but a frequently-cited reference point in the recovery community (especially for [[114-sarah-lotus-path-yogi-recovery|Sarah / Lotus Path Yogi]]'s recovery).**

## Anchor framework: car analogy for energy delivery

Myhill's road map for ME / CFS / LC, in order:

1. **Right fuel in the tank** — paleo ketogenic diet (+ time-restricted eating); fix upper fermenting gut
2. **Oxygen** — slow breathing (Buteyko), prevent CO2 washout (Bohr effect)
3. **Mitochondrial engine** — supplements + remove toxins
4. **Thyroid accelerator pedal** — empirical thyroid replacement when blood tests normal but symptoms scream
5. **Adrenal gearbox** — adrenal support
6. **Inflammation control** — addressing chronic infections (LDN, methylene blue, DMSO, photodynamic therapy)

## Anchor: paleo ketogenic diet (PKD) is non-negotiable starting point

- Cut: gluten grains, dairy, yeast, sugar, processed oils
- Keep: meat, fat (lard, coconut oil, palm oil, cocoa butter, fish oils), eggs, vegetables, nuts, seeds, pulses
- 30 different foods per week for microbiome diversity
- Time-restricted eating (6-hour window)
- Verify ketosis with **continuous glucose monitor** (~£60 for 2 weeks of data; e.g. Abbott Freestyle Libre) + **Acetrack ketone breath meter**

Mechanism claim: ketones are anti-inflammatory and the preferred mitochondrial fuel; sugars/carbs are markedly pro-inflammatory.

## Anchor: upper fermenting gut (UFG) as cause of MCAS / "histamine intolerance"

Myhill's strongly-held position:

> "MCAS — too much histamine — that's not the cause, that's the messenger. It comes from eating sugars and carbohydrates that are getting fermented, or unfriendly microbes in the upper gut."

Treatment for UFG:

- **Vitamin C 5 g/day** in 2 doses (away from iodine, which neutralises it) — Myhill argues the average mammal makes ~15-20g/day of vitamin C; humans are deficient because we can't synthesise it
- **Lugol's iodine 3 drops at night** — broad-spectrum antimicrobial
- Time-restricted eating
- Eat to feed the *lower* fermenting gut (microbiome) but starve the *upper* fermenting gut (UFG)

The wiki holds this **adjacent to but stronger-claim than [[099-viola-sampson-microbiome]]** — Sampson is more cautious about broad-spectrum antimicrobials; Myhill positions vitamin C + iodine as essentially universal first-line.

## Anchor: mitochondrial supplement stack (only after UFG fixed)

Myhill warns: don't supplement mitochondrial cofactors before fixing UFG, because the bacteria in the gut also love these and you're feeding them.

Once UFG fixed:

- Magnesium 300 mg
- Vitamin D 10,000 IU (with magnesium for absorption)
- CoQ10 100 mg
- Niacinamide (B3) 1500 mg
- Acetyl-L-carnitine 1 g (especially for vegetarians; from meat normally)
- D-ribose 5 g as rescue dose
- Thiamine (B1) for some

This is essentially the [[shine-protocol]] / Teitelbaum stack ([[055-jacob-teitelbaum-shine-protocol]]) but with the **UFG-first sequencing constraint**.

## Anchor: detox protocols

For toxic-load reduction:

- **Epsom salt baths** — 0.5 kg per bath; ~£35 for 20kg delivered; pulls out pesticides + VOCs into surface fat layer to wash off; also magnesium + sulfate
- For heavy metals: minerals, glutathione, diatomaceous earth, chelation

## Anchor: thyroid + adrenal underdiagnosis

Myhill's position (consistent with [[045-keith-littlewood-hormones-thyroid]]):

- TSH-only blood tests miss many clinical hypothyroid cases
- Clinical signs (slow pulse, low core temp, fluid retention, puffy face, carpal tunnel / sciatica from tissue swelling, owl chronotype, voice changes) often more reliable than TSH
- DIY thyroid replacement via thyroid glandulars per her book "*The Underactive Thyroid*"

Wiki should flag this as **heterodox** — DIY thyroid replacement carries real risks; the wiki logs the position, doesn't endorse self-medication.

## Anchor: chronic-infection antimicrobial trio

Myhill's current "Groundhog Chronic" infection protocol:

- **[[methylene-blue]]** — broad-spectrum antimicrobial + mitochondrial ETC support; serious SSRI/MAOI interaction
- **DMSO** (dimethyl sulfoxide)
- **Photodynamic therapy** (light-activated)

Mechanism claim: combined broad-spectrum effect on viral persistence, fungal infections, biofilm-protected bacteria — without requiring expensive lab tests for specific pathogens.

The wiki holds this as **controversial / under-evidenced**; logged for completeness but not endorsed for self-administration.

## "Groundhog regimes" framework

- **Groundhog Basic** — what everyone should do all the time (PKD + vitamin C + iodine + supplements + Epsom baths)
- **Groundhog Acute** — for acute infections (high-dose vitamin C, iodine, fasting)
- **Groundhog Chronic** — for chronic disease (Basic + Acute + methylene blue + DMSO + PDT)

## Eric-relevance

- The framework as a whole is **heterodox**; Eric should treat it as a single coherent clinician's lens, not as evidence-based mainstream medicine. Cross-check against ercipedia.
- **Specific takeaways with stronger evidence:**
  - **D-ribose 5g** — felt within 1-2 weeks per [[114-sarah-lotus-path-yogi-recovery]]; cheap; low-risk trial
  - **Vitamin C 5g/day** — well-evidenced safe upper bound; bowel-tolerance dose-finding
  - **Continuous glucose monitor** — actually useful diagnostic regardless of which dietary framework Eric chooses; ~2-week trial cheap
  - **Epsom salt baths** — almost-no-risk; magnesium + sulfate + relaxation; cheap
  - **Buteyko slow breathing** — Eric already does breathwork; principle (don't blow off CO2) is the same
- **Specific takeaways to be cautious about:**
  - **Lugol's iodine** — interacts with thyroid medication; Eric not currently on thyroid meds but worth flagging
  - **Methylene blue** — SSRI/MAOI serious-interaction risk; Eric not on these but should know
  - **DIY thyroid replacement** — wiki does *not* recommend
  - **DMSO + photodynamic therapy** — wiki does *not* endorse self-administration
- The **upper-fermenting-gut → MCAS framing** is interesting and deserves cross-reference against [[099-viola-sampson-microbiome]] (more cautious) and [[038-dr-tina-peers-mcas]] (mast-cell-first framing). Three different models of "what causes MCAS in LC patients."
