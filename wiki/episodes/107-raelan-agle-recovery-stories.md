# #107 — Raelan Agle: ME/CFS recovery + the YouTube recovery-story platform

Canadian recovered ME/CFS patient (10 years ill, ~4 years recovered at recording). Author of *Finding Freedom* (her recovery book) and host of the **Raelan Agle YouTube channel** — the largest and most-cited single archive of ME/CFS and LC recovery stories. **9th full recovery on the wiki**, but more importantly the **single best meta-source** for recovery patterns across hundreds of stories.

## Background

- Mother had CFS from when Raelan was 5; lifelong; mother died of it.
- Raelan got "really bad flu" within a year of mother's death; never recovered. Had been over-exercising, high-stress, high-anxiety pre-trigger.
- Severe (housebound) for first ~2 years; then mild but symptomatic for ~8 more years.
- **Eight years lived in Southeast Asia** during illness arc; recovered there.

## Recovery — two phases

**Phase 1 (~first 2 years, partial improvement):** scattergun approach — ~**100 pills/day** of supplements from an integrative doctor; antivirals; heavy antibiotics for suspected chronic Lyme; HRT; sauna; dry skin brushing; all-organic diet; meditation; mould testing of home. Estimates "less than 50%" improvement; couldn't isolate which interventions did what.

**Phase 2 (~final 2 years, full recovery):** stopped seeing doctors; built her own program from what had shown promise:
- **Plant-based diet** (at the time — now no longer plant-based; logged separately)
- **Gut health work** — increased plant foods, **intermittent fasting**, **juice fasting**, **fermented foods** (probiotics)
- **Sleep at all costs** — full priority; whatever she had to do.
- **Sunlight + outdoors daily**
- **Therapy + journaling + meditation** for stress
- **New healthy relationship** (now-husband moved in) — articulated retrospectively as a major (under-credited) factor; loneliness + stress dropped
- **Graded exposure to movement** starting at **1 minute/day** of gentle wall push-ups or squats in her living room; +1 min/week. Within a year: full-hour gym workouts at full intensity, **no PEM**.

## Critical operational insight: scaffolding makes graded movement work

Raelan tried movement many times in the previous years and crashed every time. The difference in the recovery phase: **she had everything else dialled in first** (sleep, stress, gut, environment). Without that, the same exercise dose crashed her.

**This is a re-frame the wiki should preserve.** "Graded exercise therapy doesn't work" (the ME/CFS community's hard-won lesson) is about *exercise prescribed without the scaffolding*. With the scaffolding, gentle gradual re-exposure does work — Raelan articulates it as re-exposure therapy showing her nervous system that movement was safe, not as fitness training.

## What she found doing 100+ recovery interviews

Three universal themes:

1. **Nervous-system / fear-response work** — every recovered person addresses it, often without naming it that way. "Quit a stressful job", "left a stressful relationship", "moved house" all do this work by another name.
2. **Brain-training / neural-pathway work** — most recovered people do *some* version of this, named or unnamed. Includes graded re-exposure to triggers (food, exercise, environments).
3. **Physical-mechanism work** — *some, not all*. Mould detox, heavy metals, gut, diet. **Many recovered people address only #1 and #2 and report all their tests came back normal.**

> "The body was fine. The tests came back normal because the body *was* normal. It was the brain and nervous system that had become hypersensitised — not built for our modern crazy world." — Raelan Agle, ep #107, paraphrased

This is **directly compatible with — and complementary to** the polyvagal frame ([[sally-riggs]] #101) and the dysautonomia frame ([[dan-neuffer]] #102). It's also **somewhat in tension with** the viral-persistence frame ([[amy-proal]] #103) and the mould-mechanism frame ([[taylor-krick]] #106), which would say tests come back normal because the right tests aren't being run. The wiki holds both.

## Outliers worth surfacing

- **Cambó frog poisoning** (Thai jungle Shaman ritual) — one woman recovered after 15+ years using rounds of skin-applied ranid frog secretion. Logged as the wiki's most exotic recovery vector.
- **Carnivore diet** — at least one recovery; histamine-allergy presentation
- **Plant-based diet** — Raelan's own original recovery diet; she has since moved off it without re-symptoms
- **The squirrels in her backyard** — one woman gave significant credit to feeding squirrels daily as part of her nervous-system / joy-restoration work. The "small, seemingly insignificant" interventions (pet, garden, daily ritual) are surprisingly common.

## Anchor "advice to patients early in their journey"

Raelan's articulation, paraphrased and condensed:

- It is going to be hellish. Loneliness, gaslighting, and shocking lack of answers are part of it.
- Conventional medicine often won't help — not because doctors are bad, but because **they don't have the information either**.
- Each thing that *doesn't* work for you is data, not failure.
- **Don't run the recovery as a boot camp** — high-achievers turn the recovery itself into a stressful project, defeating the work.
- **Look behind you, not just at the peak.** Mountain-climbing metaphor: pause and notice progress already made, or the climb feels endless.
- **Trust your gut** about what resonates. Different stories will call different patients — bookmark the ones that pick you up on bad days.
- **Never give up.** The way you don't give up is by holding hope. The way you hold hope is by keeping recovery stories in your input.

## Anchor reflection on identity post-recovery

Raelan articulates the same observation as Jackie #94, Charlotte #98, Michelle Irving #100: **the recovered person is not the pre-illness person**. New confidence, new priorities, new bandwidth for adventure, more cuts-through-BS in conversations. She has lived in five countries since recovery. Says she would not, given a magic wand, take the 10 years back — *not* because illness was a gift, but because she would not be the person she now is without them. **Compatible with [[100-michelle-irving-emotional-empowerment]]'s vertical/horizontal life model.**

## Eric-relevance

- **Most-cited recovery-story archive in the LC/ME/CFS world.** Eric should know the channel exists as a primary source for recovery patterns.
- **The scaffolding-first principle** for movement is the operational takeaway most directly portable to Eric's situation. He has POTS + PEM; "exercise reintroduction" without scaffolding (sleep, stress, gut, NS-state, drug stack stable) will crash him; with scaffolding it can succeed at very small doses.
- **Three-themes meta-pattern** is the wiki's most evidence-based prediction of what Eric's recovery stack will look like — heavy on #1 and #2, *possibly* #3 if there's a mould or persistence component.
- **Hope as a recovery input.** Eric's already past the worst of his illness arc, but for any future relapse the principle holds: stay-in-the-input-stream of recovery stories, not just symptom forums.
