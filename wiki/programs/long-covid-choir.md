# Long Covid Choir

Online weekly choir established April 2021 by [[zoe-chaler|Zoe Chaler]] and [[claire-hastie|Claire Hastie]] in response to having Long Covid themselves. Currently led by **Merrill Vaughan**. Meets weekly on Zoom; members can sing on mute (low-confidence-vocal-friendly). Sessions include gentle breathing exercises, mindfulness, and chat. International reach: UK, US, Germany, China.

## What's notable

- **Singing-on-mute** is a deliberate accessibility design — lowers the participation bar for self-conscious singers.
- The breathing/phonation work overlaps mechanistically with [[063-michael-bagnell|Bagnell #63]]'s vagal-stim-via-singing recommendation — wiki cross-link logged.
- Strong **community** function — friendships have formed across the international group.
