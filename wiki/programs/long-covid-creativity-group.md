# Long Covid + ME/CFS Healing Through Creativity (Facebook group)

**Jackie Baxter's online creativity community** for LC + ME/CFS patients. Anchored by the annual creativity-compilation episodes (#14 / #65 / #113 / #158). Has expanded over time to include events and shared sessions. Released a new format in late 2024: the **virtual art room** — Zoom-format ~40-minute sit-and-create sessions with optional sharing afterwards (named in [[159-jackies-2024-reflections]]).

## What it offers

- **Facebook group** for LC + ME patients with a creative practice (or wanting to start one).
- **Annual creativity compilation episodes** on the podcast — contributors share their work + practice.
- **Virtual art room** (started 2024) — Zoom sessions; bring your own thing to work on; optional share at end.
- Cross-references to [[restart-creative]] (Jess Taylor's UK CIC) and [[long-covid-choir]] (Marielle Vaughan's choir).

## Why it matters on the wiki

- **The wiki's most durable host-led community programme.** 4 annual compilation episodes + ongoing group activity.
- **Anchor for the [[creativity]] theme.**
- **Virtual art room** is a wiki-new format (2024) — useful pacing-friendly access point for housebound + bedridden patients to participate in shared creative practice.
