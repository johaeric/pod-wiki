# Heal Good

Monthly membership programme run by [[jenny-adams]] (recovered ME/CFS coach, trained in compassionate inquiry with Gabor Maté). Opens shortly after #145 aired in September 2024.

## Components

- **Somatic practices** (Mind-Body connection)
- Slow, gentle, **chronic-illness-friendly movement**
- **Meditation** library
- **Mindset** work
- **Nervous-system toolkit**
- **Community** (cohort-based support)
- Affordable price point ("recovery costs money — I built this in response to that").

Adams also offers 1:1 coaching for deeper work (focused on burnout, ADHD-burnout, ME/CFS recovery).

## Wiki position

Logged as `mentioned_only` for now — Adams's own programme, just launched. Watch for participant outcomes in subsequent episodes / recovery interviews. The model is in the same family as [[suzy-bolt-recovery]] (community + somatic + chronic-illness-friendly) but with stronger trauma/coaching emphasis from Adams's compassionate-inquiry training.
