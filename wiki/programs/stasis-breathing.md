# Stasis breathing program

Free **breathwork program** developed by Josh Duntz in collaboration with **Mt Sinai's Centre for Post-Covid Care** (so the project page reports). Specific protocols designed for LC, pitched at autonomic regulation (rest-repair mode). Free at the point of use.
