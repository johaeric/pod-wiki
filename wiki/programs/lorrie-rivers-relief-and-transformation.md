# Lorrie Rivers — Relief and Transformation Course

A multi-month online course run by [[lorrie-rivers|Lorrie Rivers]], a recovered ME/CFS coach, built around her **hidden-infection / dysbiosis theory** of all chronic illness. Treats Long Covid, ME/CFS, fibromyalgia, MCAS, and others as variants of the same upstream gut-and-systemic dysbiosis driven by parasites, candida, and other "bad microbes."

## Phases (per #70)

1. **Pre-treatment** (~2–3 months): strict low-carb / sometimes carnivore eating plan; open detox pathways; learn binders and herxheimer monitoring. Some students "start passing worms" at this stage from the eating plan alone.
2. **Treatment** (~6–12 months): drug-based anti-parasitic and anti-fungal protocol; individualised by recovery-phase and clinician availability.
3. **Heavy metals + lingering infections** (later phase): chelation; lyme treatment if needed.
4. Continued **graduate program** for ongoing support after the initial 3-month course.

## Notable claims

- Recovery (not just management) achievable in ~12 months for clients who complete the protocol.
- Clients with 30+ years severe ME/CFS recovering. (Self-report; no peer-reviewed publication.)
- Recovery phases 1–5 (1: bedbound; 5: fully recovered) used as a self-rating scale.
