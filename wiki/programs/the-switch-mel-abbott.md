# The Switch (Mel Abbott)

A 4-day online brain-retraining program created by **Mel Abbott**, an Australian who had 11 years of severe CFS before recovering and developing the program. Reports thousands of patient recoveries (her own claim). Same family as [[primal-trust]] / [[ans-rewire]] / [[i-can-thrive]] / [[gupta-program]] / [[dynamic-neural-retraining-system]].

Format per Linley's account: 4 consecutive days, ~7-hour sessions (12pm–7pm). Education-first; calming-down techniques + cognitive interruption + "I'm safe" self-talk during scary activities.
