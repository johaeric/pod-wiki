# Hugo Health

US digital-data-agency platform founded by [[harlan-krumholz]] (Yale). Mission: **give patients agency over their own healthcare data**.

Mechanism:
- Patients link their accounts (medical records, wearables, pharmacies, insurance).
- Data flows to participant's cloud-based account, refreshed.
- Permission to share data into a study is **granted explicitly + revokable**.
- Long-term vision: data assets that grow over time, usable for research and care — but always patient-controlled.

## Use in LC research

[[097-harlan-krumholz-listen-paxlovid]] uses Hugo Health as the data-agency layer for both the [[yale-listen-study]] and [[paxlovid-lc-trial]]. Participants:
- Permission their EHR + wearable data to flow to the study.
- Can revoke at any time.
- Get returned-results — the study commits to share findings back.

This is the wiki's first explicit articulation of **digital-data-agency-as-research-infrastructure**. Pairs with [[054-elizabeth-dreicer-consuli]]'s research-equity framing, with [[086-harry-leeming-visible]]'s digital-biomarkers framing, and with [[095-laina-gauthier-mamaril-zombies]]'s cripistemology / format-as-political-act framing.

## Caveats

- US-only at present.
- Currently embedded in Yale-led LC research; broader adoption pending.
- Long-term sustainability of the data-agency model depends on regulatory + market evolution beyond the founder's control.
