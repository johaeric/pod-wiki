Mickel Therapy is a UK-origin mind-body modality developed by **David Mickel** while helping his partner recover. The framework reads physical symptoms as the body's way of signalling that emotional boundaries are being overstepped — anger, guilt, etc. — rather than as direct manifestations of disease. The newer **Mind Body Reconnect** program by Claire Caldwell extends and adds to it.

## How it works (as described in #208)

- Wake up symptomatic *before* doing anything → body protesting against repeating yesterday's pattern (boredom, frustration with sameness)
- Wake up fine *and develop symptoms during the day* → something in the present environment or interaction is the trigger (overstepped boundary, suppressed anger, guilt)
- Therapy work is to identify these triggers and respond differently

## Notes on rigor

- Not RCT-evidenced; works on a clinical-experience and patient-testimony basis
- Less mainstream than CBT or somatic therapy; n of practitioners is small
