Late-stage recovery program from **Suzy Bolt** focused on supporting the move out of illness and into wellness — specifically the fear-of-reinfection block. Jackie Baxter took the program herself near the end of her own recovery and now teaches some of the breath sessions inside it.

Distinct from a generic brain-retraining program — it targets the specific psychological/nervous-system blocks that show up at the 80–95% recovery point.
