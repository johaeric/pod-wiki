CFS Health is an online recovery program for ME/CFS and long COVID, founded by Toby Morrison (Australia). Multi-coach model — different specialists handle movement, mindset, pacing, lifestyle. Includes video resources, Facebook community, and group coaching calls.

## Distinguishing features

- All coaches have themselves recovered from ME/CFS
- Specialised coaches (e.g. dedicated movement coach) rather than one-coach-fits-all
- Substantial free YouTube content as on-ramp before paid membership
- Monthly subscription model — financial accessibility is a real barrier

## What it teaches

- Pacing and activity management as primary skill
- Nervous system regulation
- Mindset work without slipping into "think yourself well" cliché
- Building skill at finding baseline, progressing in small increments
