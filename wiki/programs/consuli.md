# Consuli

US-based public-benefit company. Membership organization that connects individuals (members, opt-in) to clinical trials and data research. Acts as the patient's agent, not as an extractive aggregator. Vets every study via bioethicists + advisors. Currently North America-only; planning international expansion.

Operates a "marketplace" model where studies offer terms (cash, donation-to-charity, or volunteer participation) to members.

## Strategic relevance

- Hosts a monthly "patients meet researchers" forum.
- Has submitted equity-in-trial-participation policy submissions to the FDA.
- Currently recruiting for the La Jolla Institute for Immunology LC neurological study (#54).
