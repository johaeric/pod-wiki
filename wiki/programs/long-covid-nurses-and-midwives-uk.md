# Long Covid Nurses & Midwives UK (LCNMUK)

Advocacy and peer-support group for registered nurses, midwives, and nursing associates across the four UK nations who have long covid. Launched **28 January 2022** by Dr [[alison-twycross]]. Members predominantly caught COVID March/April 2020.

This is not a recovery program — it is an **advocacy / peer-support / occupational-rights** organisation. Filed under `/programs/` because it functions as a named multi-component community offering with its own infrastructure (website, Twitter, Instagram, Facebook, two WhatsApp support groups, friends-of-LCNMUK supporter network).

## Activities (year 1)

- Lobbied NHS England to remove a statement permitting dismissal of staff with > 12 months LC sick leave (succeeded mid-Feb 2022).
- Contributed to the U-turn on NHS Wales half-pay-after-1-year policy (1 April 2022 reprieve until end of June).
- Wrote same-day briefing for UK Parliament debate on COVID-19 & employment.
- Published a survey via *Evidence-Based Nursing* on dismissals, half-pay, no-pay outcomes for NHS England nurses with LC.
- Catalysed the Royal College of Nursing's consolidated LC member resource hub.
- Met with Chief Nursing Officers of England, Wales, and Scotland.
- Members presented to nhsx, NHS Improvement, Queen's Nursing Institute, ward-team-leader conferences, Welsh Senedd cross-party group, Scottish government cross-party group.
- Working with University Hospitals of Morecambe Bay's national long-covid network for nurses.
- Cross-collaboration with [[long-covid-physio]] (placeholder), Long Covid SOS (placeholder), Long Covid Support Employment Group, Long Covid Support.

## Stated limits

LCNMUK does **not** offer individual support — capacity-constrained because all members are themselves ill. Focus is on national-level lobbying and best-practice sharing. The website is the resource hub.
