# Long Covid Breathing

Six-week, twelve-session online course of breathwork tailored for long covid, **co-founded by [[jackie-baxter]] and [[vicky-jones]]**. Includes group community / cohort element. Advertised as a sponsor segment in early episodes (`lcbreathing.com`).

This program is *adjacent to the podcast* (the host runs it). When evaluating outcomes from guests, treat host promotion separately from third-party reports.
