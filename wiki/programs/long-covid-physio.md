# Long Covid Physio

International, patient-led association of physiotherapists (and allied health professionals) living with Long Covid, plus their allies. Founded **November 2020** on Twitter as a peer-support group for physios who realised they were getting worse using the very rehabilitation tools they normally prescribe. Founding chair: [[darren-brown]].

This is not a recovery program — it is a **peer support + advocacy + education** organisation in the LC research-and-policy space. Filed under `/programs/` because of its multi-component community offering.

## What it does

- **Peer support** in members-only safe spaces for physios / allied health professionals with LC, internationally.
- **Education** — website resource library; the **World Physiotherapy Briefing Paper** (June 2021, "Safe Rehabilitation Approaches for People Living with Long Covid", translated into 11 languages, the bedrock of rehab guidelines globally); the physio.cam animated video series (8 parts planned, 4 released by mid-2022, 144k+ views).
- **Policy** — engagement with NHS England, NHS Improvement, World Physiotherapy, partner LC orgs.
- **Events** — the **Long Covid Physio International Forum** (Sept 2022) — 80 speakers, 17 countries, disability-inclusive design, scholarship-funded for PWLC.

## Position on safe rehab (the core deliverable)

The Brown / LC-Physio position is that **rehabilitation in LC must be stability-first**, not progression-first. This has direct policy implications and is in deliberate opposition to the GET / progressive-overload tradition that hurt the ME/CFS community for decades.

> "Just because you can doesn't mean you should." — Brown, ep #47

Pacing IS rehabilitation. Education IS rehabilitation. Heart-rate monitoring IS rehabilitation.
