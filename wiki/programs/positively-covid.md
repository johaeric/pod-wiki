# Positively Covid

Free web resource founded by [[amy-anker]] after her ~14-month full LC recovery. Hosts:
- **Meditations** Amy wrote during her own illness ("born of necessity")
- **Recovery stories** from other LC patients (her own and submitted)
- **Daily-routine framing** that drove her recovery: positive mindset · real rest · nourish the body · move (walking)

## Why she built it

She could not find recovery stories during her own illness. Recovered patients leave LC support groups and move on with their lives, leaving the visible LC discourse dominated by the still-suffering — which she identifies as triggering and de-hopefying for those still inside it. She vowed not to leave, and built Positively Covid as a counter-current.

> "We needed positivity. We needed hope. We needed encouragement. Because if you know it's possible — it will happen. If you only hear *this is so terrible and just going to get worse* — that's what's going to happen." — Amy, ep #20

## Treat-as-caveat

This is **founder's-own-recovery**: the recovery story is logged as `key_to_recovery` because Amy is herself fully recovered and credits this routine. But it shares the structural caveat with [[suzy-bolt-recovery]] — the program is the founder's own recipe, the strongest patient is the founder.
