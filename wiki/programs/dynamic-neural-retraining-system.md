# Dynamic Neural Retraining System (DNRS)

Annie Hopper's brain-retraining program. Same conceptual family as [[ans-rewire]] and [[gupta-program]]. Originally developed for multiple chemical sensitivity (MCS) and electromagnetic hypersensitivity, expanded to CFS, fibromyalgia, MCAS, and LC.

Format: ~6-month commitment, video-based course + structured daily practices (limbic-system retraining; visualisation; structured neural pathway interruption).
