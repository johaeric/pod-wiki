# Research-Aid Networks (RAN)

A non-profit organisation working as a **bridge between scientific researchers and community advocacy groups**. Mission scope is broader than LC (humanitarian and pandemic preparedness too) but LC has been a major focus since 2020.

- President: [[jeremy-rossman]] (UK virologist).
- Director of Development: [[vicky-van-der-togt]] (Netherlands; LC-patient advocate).

The model: support local advocacy groups through information sharing, lay-summary translation, training in research literacy, and emotional/organisational support for advocates themselves. RAN is *not* the originating voice — local groups are. RAN reduces duplicated work across countries.

Held weekly meetings during the worst pandemic phase to disseminate research into community languages; relaunched in 2024.

## Wiki position

Logged as `mentioned_only` — RAN is not an LC intervention or treatment. It's a **research-coordination infrastructure** worth knowing about for two reasons:

1. They co-authored the 2024 [[acid-base-disregulation]] hypothesis paper that may surface in subsequent LC research.
2. They are a model for how LC research-community bridging can work at international scale.
