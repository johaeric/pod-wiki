# Bateman Horne Center

US clinical and research centre (Salt Lake City, UT) specialising in ME/CFS, fibromyalgia, and post-acute infection syndromes including LC. Founded by Dr Lucinda Bateman; produces clinician education and patient-facing protocols widely used in the ME/CFS care community.

## Why this is on the wiki

- **Crash care plan template** — Bateman Horne's filled-in protocol document is the most widely-recommended structure for what to have prepared *before* a crash hits: warning signs, intervention list, contact info, basic medical info for advocates. Davenport (#141) cites it as the canonical document to use.
- Crash-care-plan ingredients align with [[post-exertional-malaise]] best practice: lying supine, lower-extremity + abdominal compression, hydration + salt + electrolytes, designated medications, pre-listed warning signs.

## Wiki position

Logged as `recommended` (clinician reference). The crash care plan is the deliverable Eric should download for his own kit, given the Bateman Horne Center's depth of ME/CFS clinical experience.
