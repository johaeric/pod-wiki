# Bridges Self-Management

UK social enterprise founded by [[fiona-jones]]. Provides co-designed self-management programs and clinician training across multiple long-term conditions: stroke, brain injury, major trauma, and (now) **long covid via the LISTEN trial**.

## Approach

- **Patient-narrative-driven** — the patient's story is the start point, not a structured pathway
- **1:1 support** rather than group education
- **Trained practitioner network** (cross-disciplinary: physio, OT, nursing, psychology)
- **Codesign with people living with the condition** — not tokenistic involvement
- "Detective" stance — the practitioner helps the patient piece together what works for them, rather than prescribing a generic protocol

## In LC

The [[listen-trial]] tests an LC-adapted Bridges intervention: 6 remote sessions + co-designed book. Practitioners trained 8 hours including patient co-trainers.
