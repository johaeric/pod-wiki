# Lightning Process

A **three-day brain-retraining training course** developed by Phil Parker. Combines elements of NLP, osteopathy, life coaching, and a self-management protocol patients are taught to use whenever they notice early signs of crash/symptom escalation. Highly controversial — celebrated by some recovered patients, criticised by others as gaslighting in workshop form.

> Cross-ref: see `ercipedia/wiki/interventions/lightning-process.md` for the trial-evidence picture (the SMILE trial in paediatric ME/CFS, methodological critiques).

## Notable claims (Lightning Process side)

- Beliefs and habitual thought patterns shape autonomic state and therefore symptoms.
- Patients can recognise pre-crash thought patterns ("being on the edge of the pit") and intervene.
- "You have enough energy; the leaking bucket is fear-driven fight-flight."

## Common outcomes (per podcast)

- **Best results when patient is already on an upward trajectory** and "psychologically ready".
- Strong reactions in both directions reported in the wider community; null outcomes are likely under-represented because non-responders disengage.
