# Restart Creative

UK Community Interest Company (CIC) founded by **Jess Taylor** (qualified art teacher + special-needs tutor + professional artist; LC since Dec 2021). Delivers **online low-demand art workshops + journaling sessions for people with long covid** and other post-COVID health issues.

- Founded after Jess realised she couldn't run in-person classes — both because she was too ill to deliver them, and because LC patients couldn't reasonably commute and spend their energy on travel
- Receives grants (third bout of funding by recording)
- Pilot programme delivered (small group in Leeds + online)
- Builds a sketchbook over the course; teaches new art skills as additions to participant lives

## Why it matters on the wiki

- The wiki's **first dedicated online LC-specific art programme with funded delivery**.
- Filling a specific gap: many in-person community-arts options are inaccessible to LC patients; this one was **designed by an LC patient for LC patients.**
