# TMS / Mind-Body Syndrome

A lineage rather than a single product. Roots in **John Sarno**'s *Tension Myositis Syndrome* (1980s–1990s) — chronic pain framed as the brain's strategy to distract from repressed emotion. Continued by **Howard Schubiner** (*Unlearn Your Pain*) and **Nicole Sachs** (*The Cure for Chronic Pain*; "journal speak" technique).

The unifying claim:
> Once structural damage is ruled out, chronic symptoms in a sensitised nervous system can be perpetuated by **fear of the symptoms themselves** + repressed emotion. Letting both surface — being seen and felt — releases the pattern.

Crucially **gated by structural rule-out**: the framework requires a clinician to confirm there's no tissue / organ damage first. It is *not* "your symptoms are imaginary."

## How practitioners differ

| Source | Method | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| Sarno | book + clinic | older, blunt, knowledge-as-cure |
| Schubiner | online programme + book *Unlearn Your Pain* | medical, structured, neuroplasticity vocabulary |
| Sachs | podcast + courses + journal-speak technique | accessible, daily-practice, emotion-first |

## Compatible adjuncts patients combine with this

- **Self-compassion** practices (Kristin Neff) — Esther flags this as essential because TMS-prone people are often high self-critics.
- **Slow re-introduction of activity** — small acts the brain can recategorise as safe.
- **Community / peer recovery stories** — pattern recognition.

## How this differs from other recovery routes in the wiki

| Route | Episode | Mechanism story |
|---|---|---|
| Lightning Process | #8 (Whitfield) | NS pattern interruption (Phil Parker training) |
| Neuroplasticity self-built | #20 (Anker) | NS-loop interruption (Founder's eclectic) |
| Multi-component pharma + diet + SSP | #23 (Riggs) | LDN + keto + Safe and Sound Protocol |
| **TMS / Mind-Body Syndrome** | **#32 (Esther)** | **Repressed-emotion release + fear-of-symptoms recognition** |
| Coaching / mental-skill rebuild | #34 (Munslow) | Inner-critic recognition + acceptance |

These are linguistically different, mechanistically convergent on **NS-regulation + fear-loop interruption + agency restoration**. None is a silver bullet; all require active practice over weeks.
