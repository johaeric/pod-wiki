# Integrative Breathing Therapy (IBT)

A structured, three-axis breathing-therapy framework developed by [[rosalba-courtney]]. Distinguishes itself from technique-first approaches (e.g. straight Buteyko, straight cadence-breathing) by treating breathing as **multi-dimensional**:

1. **Biomechanics** — pattern, rate, accessory-muscle dominance.
2. **Biochemistry** — CO₂ / pH / oxygen handling.
3. **Psycho-physiological** — perception of breathing, hyper/hypo-vigilance.

Each axis is assessed and addressed independently with structured measurement (capnometry, Nijmegen, SEBQ, Dyspnea Index) at intake and re-assessment.

The model emphasises that **the technique is not the treatment** — the assessment → personalised protocol → reassessment cycle is.

## LC adaptations (Hadas Golan's clinic)

In LC, IBT is run as a 6-week group class (1 hr × 6 sessions, Zoom-delivered) + individual measurement sessions, with this order:

1. Nasal function (covers all three axes by default).
2. Mind-body / posture / breath awareness.
3. Gentle diaphragmatic breathing.
4. Address hyperinflation / hyperventilation.
5. Cadence breathing (slow paced; 4-in / 6-out style, built up to gradually).
6. Speech & physical-effort pacing.

Many patients repeat the 6 weeks before moving to advanced material. Some stay in ongoing care.

Hadas's clinical position: in LC, **reduced-volume Buteyko-style work is too aggressive** at the start — hypocapnia in LC is often driven by under-production of CO₂ (mitochondrial), not over-breathing, so cadence-first works better.
