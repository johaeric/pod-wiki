Software-based cognitive-training program built on brain plasticity research. Created by **Posit Science** (CEO Henry Mahncke; PhD UCSF). Available in 14 languages at brainhq.com — free trial exercise daily; subscription for full library.

## Mechanism

Trains underlying cognitive processing speed and accuracy ("bottom-up" approach), reasoning that better signal quality improves higher-order memory, attention, and decision-making. Validated initially in healthy aging populations (large trials with Mayo Clinic, USC).

## Use in Long COVID brain fog

The "double decision" exercise was the cognitive-training arm of the **CI Cognitive Therapy** trial led by Gitendra Uswatte at UAB — alongside in-clinic everyday-task training and a transfer package. Initial small RCT (n=7+7) showed 80% return-to-work in the CI Cognitive Therapy arm vs 0% in usual care. Confirmatory study underway.
