# Stress for Health

Med-tech startup founded ~2022 by [[peter-deen]] (ex-Radboud water-homeostasis biologist, age 62 at founding). Two product targets, both in development:

1. **Diagnosis** — blood-test panel to be sent to a clinical lab; intended to give GPs a fast structural way to identify ME/CFS / LC.
2. **Continuous biosensor** — glucose-CGM-style adhesive wearable tracking an inflammatory marker that *predicts an upcoming PEM crash*. Specific marker under IP.

Status at recording: pre-launch website; seeking investors; long regulatory horizon (medical-devices law); years from market.

## Why it's tracked here

The PEM-prediction wearable target is the kind of pacing-tool the wiki has long flagged as missing. Existing tools (HR pacing, BOLT score, HRV biofeedback) all require *interpretation*; a continuous predictive biosensor would give Eric and other patients real-time lights-out signals before a crash lands.

Logged as `mentioned_only` — no patient outcomes yet; product not on market.
