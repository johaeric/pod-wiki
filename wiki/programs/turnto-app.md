# Turnto

Mobile app from [[jess-dove-london]] for daily health-breakthrough information consumption. Swipe-through TikTok-style full-screen 5-10-minute daily experience aggregating:

- New research papers
- Trial findings
- User-generated experience tips
- Practitioner AMAs / Q&As
- Events

## Conditions covered (April 2024)

- Cerebal palsy (already thousands of users; original launch).
- Long covid + ME/CFS (launched April 2024, days before this recording).

Treated together as one community — explicit decision based on user feedback about cross-content following — but with personalisation knobs that can split them per-user.

## Wiki position

Logged as `mentioned_only` — not an LC intervention; it's information infrastructure. Worth tracking as one possible solution to the *information-overload* problem chronic-illness patients face. Sits alongside [[research-aid-networks]] / [[long-covid-handbook]] (Medinger #46) / health-rising / patient-led-research-collaborative as a curation-and-distribution layer.

Build philosophy: lay summaries, captioning + translations as fast as the team can build them, public roadmap-voting, AI-driven search in development.

## Eric-relevance

Probably *not* the right tool for Eric — he reads research papers directly and is past the firehose-overload phase. More relevant for the people Eric knows who are earlier in their LC journey and need a guided 5-min daily window into the field.
