# Optimal Health Clinic

Alex Howard's UK-based clinic for ME/CFS, fibromyalgia, and post-viral fatigue. Frames LC under the **maladaptive stress response** model — autonomic nervous system stuck in fight-flight. Offers paid programs and a free 3-hour video series widely circulated in LC recovery communities.
