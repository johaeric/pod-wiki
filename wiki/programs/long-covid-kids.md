# Long Covid Kids

UK-headquartered international charity advocating for children and young people with Long Covid. CEO: [[sammy-mcfarland|Sammy McFarland]]. Provides peer support, advocacy at government level, awareness campaigns, and runs the [[long-covid-kids-choir|Long Covid Kids Choir]] (with Merrill Vaughan, ep #65).

## Notable activities

- Letter delivery to 10 Downing Street (mentioned ep #61 in [[colin-pidgeon|Colin Pidgeon]]'s account) — coincided with the day all UK Covid protections + testing ended.
- "I am still here" art exhibition (December 2022) — curated by [[rosie-pidgeon|Rosie Pidgeon]], aged 17, with submissions from young LC artists internationally.
- Long Covid Kids Choir (Nov 2022 onward).
