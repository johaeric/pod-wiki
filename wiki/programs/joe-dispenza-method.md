# Joe Dispenza Method

A structured mindset / meditation / neuroplasticity-via-belief-change program built around chiropractor and lecturer **Joe Dispenza**. Materials include the books *Breaking the Habit of Being Yourself*, *You Are the Placebo*, and *Becoming Supernatural*; paid online workshops and live retreats; a heavy daily meditation practice. Adjacent to but distinct from the other named brain-retraining programs in this wiki ([[gupta-program]], [[dynamic-neural-retraining-system]], [[ans-rewire]], [[primal-trust]]) — Dispenza's framing leans more on consciousness/neuroplasticity language than on autonomic-nervous-system retraining specifically.

> Caveat: Dispenza's published material includes claims (e.g., "stem-cell activation through meditation") that are well outside mainstream evidence and that this corpus does not endorse. The interest here is solely **whether long-haulers credit it as a recovery factor**, not whether the underlying biological claims are correct.
