# ENO Breathe

The English National Opera's online LC breath / singing programme. Group sessions led by professional voice coaches, focused on breath retraining via singing exercises rather than clinical respiratory rehab. Has published peer-reviewed outcome evidence showing benefit in their cohort.

Sits alongside Scottish Opera's [[breath-cycle]] (#25) as the cultural-institution-led LC breathing program family.
