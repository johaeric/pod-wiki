# Breath Cycle

A 12-week, twice-weekly online singing-and-songwriting programme for people with long covid, run by **Scottish Opera** as part of their education / outreach work. Originally a 2013 pilot for cystic fibrosis patients (Scottish Opera + Gartnaval Hospital respiratory team); reinstantiated in September 2021 for a long-covid cohort.

Free to participants. UK-wide via Zoom.

## Structure

- **Wednesday — singing session (~1 hr).** Three vocal coaches (David Douglas, Daniela Hathway, Jessica Leary). Posture → fricative warmups → vocal warmup → song-of-the-week (introduced over 2 weeks) → small-group break-outs → reflection / meditation.
- **Friday — songwriting session.** Writer Martin O'Connor + composer Gareth Williams. Lyric / melody generation from participant prompts. Songs become singing-class repertoire — feedback loop between strands.
- **Lunchtime sessions** (not evening) — to suit LC energy budgets.

Admin / check-in support: Callum (Scottish Opera) + producer Lisa, under head of education Jane Davidson.

## Lineage / what we know works

The 2013 cystic-fibrosis pilot (n≈18, 12 weeks) reported **~14 % lung-capacity uplift**. Mechanism: mimicking airway-clearance physio with sung breath-out exercises. **Currently in pilot for LC** — three 12-week cohorts running. Lung function not currently measured for LC; tracking experience, well-being, and community.

## Reported benefits (from #25)

- Sense of community — the *between-the-songs* chat between participants generating peer advice
- Singing as a daily practice + ritual, not as performance training
- Songwriting as a cathartic axis — songs about LC reality (e.g. "too damn tired to do the dishes")
- Camera-off / mic-mute friendliness — participants attend from bed/sofa, in slippers
