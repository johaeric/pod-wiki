# UK CV Family

UK-based support group for people who have experienced adverse reactions to covid vaccines. Provides peer support, advocacy, and (per #84) symptom-pattern surveys to capture the breadth of reported sequelae.

This is the wiki's first dedicated entry on a vaccine-injured cohort organisation, distinct from general LC support groups.

## What it does (per #84)

- Peer support — explicitly framed by Pover as the single most important thing to do early in the journey.
- Symptom-pattern survey across categories (neurological, skin/hair, vision/eye, ENT, cardiovascular, GI, sensory, muscular, hormonal/gynaecological, immune, nervous-system, bladder, energy, blood/circulation, breathing, sleep, psychological).
- Public advocacy — Pover gives speeches; gets community feedback that informs trial protocols she undertakes herself.
