# ATNS / symptomatic.me

**Association for the Treatment of Neuroplastic Symptoms** — a non-profit professional association founded by Dr David Clarke (US, see #179). Public-facing site **symptomatic.me** carries:

- A **12-item self-assessment questionnaire** for whether your symptoms are likely neuroplastic.
- A 7-minute documentary edited by a recovered film-director patient (interviews 110 researchers / clinicians / patients).
- Educational content for patients and clinicians.

## Framework

ATNS treats brain-to-body / neuroplastic symptoms as a real, common, treatable diagnosis driven by:

1. **Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACEs)** — broadly defined: anything that happened to you as a child that, if it happened to a child you love, would make you sad or angry — or that made you think less of yourself.
2. **Stressful personality traits** stemming from those ACEs (perfectionism, low self-esteem, putting others first).
3. **Triggers** — current people, situations, events that re-activate the original adversity.
4. **Unrecognised emotions** — typically anger at someone the patient also loves.

## Treatment levers

- Imagine your own child going through what you went through — the magnitude exercise.
- Reframe self as heroic, not weak (Olympic-weightlifter analogy).
- Letters to people who hurt you — written, not mailed.
- Set firm boundaries with current triggers.
- Restore time for joy / play with no purpose.
