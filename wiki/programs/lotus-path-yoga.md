# Lotus Path Yoga

Yoga programme run by **[[sarah-lotus-path-yogi|Sarah / Lotus Path Yogi]]**, designed specifically for **POTS / LC / ME/CFS** patients. Style: seated or supine; no inversions; no fast vinyasa; long meditation + yoga-nidra blocks; breath-with-movement.

- **Mondays**: in-person Sheffield (UK)
- **Thursdays**: online live class
- **Fridays**: guest teaching for the **Yoga for ME** charity
- **4 on-demand courses** also available
- **400-500 students** taught at recording (#114)
- **Zero patients reported PEM after one of her sessions**
