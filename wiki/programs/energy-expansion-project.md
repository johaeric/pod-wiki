# Energy Expansion Project

Group program co-launched by [[lily-spechler]] (RD) and Dr Meg Anderson (PT). Combines:
- Self-paced **autonomic-retraining modules** (modified Levine/CHOP protocol with stability-before-movement framing)
- Self-paced **nutrition modules** (matching metabolic needs for the disease state, blood-sugar stability, fibre, mass-cell-aware design)
- Weekly group calls with both practitioners
- Patient platform for between-call questions

Stance: pacing must not become inactivity once stable. Deconditioning is itself a risk; gradual movement is essential when introduced at the right time.
