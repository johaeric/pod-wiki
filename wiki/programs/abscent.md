# AbScent

UK charity (registered May 2019) supporting people with smell loss and parosmia. Founded by [[chrissi-kelly]] in 2015 as a Facebook group; charity status May 2019. Membership grew from ~1,500 (Feb 2020) to ~85,000 (mid-2022) in line with the pandemic.

Provides peer support, smell-training instructional resources, the **Sniff** app, an embedded olfactory questionnaire, and a covid-kids subgroup. Trustee panel includes rhinologist [[simon-gane]].

## Five pillars of support (Kelly's framework)

1. Peer support
2. Clinical support
3. Food support
4. Practical advice (smell training, smell-lock management)
5. Mental health support

## Outputs

- Website with smell-training instructions
- YouTube channel — Kelly + smell-training collaborators
- Sniff app
- Olfactory-impact questionnaire (research-validated)
- Facebook groups — main + covid-specific + covid-kids
