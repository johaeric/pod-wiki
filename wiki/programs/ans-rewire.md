# ANS Rewire

Online program developed by [[dan-neuffer]], an Australian recovered-CFS patient. Frames CFS / fibromyalgia / LC as an autonomic nervous system stuck in chronic dysregulation, and treats it with **autonomic / nervous system retraining** — guided online, ~2 months. In the same family as [[dynamic-neural-retraining-system]] (Annie Hopper) and the broader [[neuroplasticity]] / brain-retraining movement.

## Mechanism story

- Post-infectious illness leaves the ANS stuck in sympathetic-dominant / vagal-dysregulated patterns
- Chronic dysregulation drives the multi-system symptom load
- Targeted neuroplasticity practices retrain the ANS back to a regulated baseline
- Specifically frames as **upstream of most LC symptoms** — once the ANS recovers, downstream symptoms resolve

## Why this keeps appearing

Multiple guests recommend it explicitly as an autonomic-circuit-breaker reset (alongside or instead of pharmaceutical pillars):

- [[gez-medinger]] (#46) names it as part of his recommended stack
- [[jacob-teitelbaum]] (#55) recommends it as the autonomic-rewire layer to put on top of biochemistry — "people get well and they get on with their life"
- [[dan-neuffer]] (#102, founder, recovered ME/CFS) explains the model in his own voice — multilateral approach, somato-neurological framing, individualised application

## The multilateral / "somato-neurological" framing (per #102)

Dan's own framing (anchor): the program isn't pure brain-retraining. It explicitly **rejects the brain-training-purist contraindication-of-physical-treatment stance**. Instead it teaches patients to combine:

- **Upstream dysautonomia work** (the brain-training core)
- **Targeted treatment of secondary biological dysfunctions** (medical, naturopathic, dietary — done *in context* of the retraining, not as the load-bearing recovery piece)
- **Sensory / somatic re-training** — Dan claims to have coined "somato-neurological" for the body→nervous-system→body retraining loop; distinguishes from pure mind→body retraining

Implication: ANS Rewire integrates with — not replaces — pharmacological pillars (e.g. mestinon, ivabradine, antihistamines for an MCAS-overlap presentation). This is the wiki's clearest articulation of why the brain-training / biomedical "war" is a false dichotomy.

## Diagnostic tell-tale (per #102)

Dan flags **multi-sensory triggering** (sounds, smells, light, cold, hot, even barometric pressure) as a key signal that a patient's illness is in the dysautonomia-rooted post-viral cluster vs something else. A heuristic, not a clinical test.

## Engagement gate

Dan **discourages buying the program** until the patient has watched the four free intro lessons. If the dysautonomia frame doesn't resonate, the program won't work — engagement requires belief in the model. Some patients return six months later when ready. **Worth surfacing**: this is structurally honest, and contrasts with brain-training programs that hard-sell.

## Caveats

- Not yet RCT-tested specifically
- Brand-retraining programs vary widely in quality and price
- Patient testimonials skew positive (survivor selection bias)
