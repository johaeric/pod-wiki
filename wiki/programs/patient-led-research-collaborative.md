# Patient-Led Research Collaborative (PLRC)

A research organisation **founded by long-haulers**, originally inside the [[body-politic]] Slack — in a channel called "Data Nerds." Ran the **first major LC symptom survey** in May 2020, characterising what is now widely called Long Covid. Now an independent, funded entity working with academic institutions, the WHO, the CDC, the White House, and many national health bodies.

Founders / lead figures: [[gina-asaff|Gina Assaf]], [[hannah-davis|Hannah Davis]], [[lisa-mccorkel|Lisa McCorkell]], [[athena-akrami|Athena Akrami]], [[fiona-lowenstein|Fiona Lowenstein]], and others (see PLRC website for full list).

## What they do

- LC symptom surveys (multiple waves)
- Research priority-setting from the patient perspective
- Patient-research education (how to read papers, how to navigate the medical literature)
- Advocacy at policy level (CDC, WHO, White House, etc.)
- The wiki considers PLRC the **single most influential patient-led research organisation in LC**.
