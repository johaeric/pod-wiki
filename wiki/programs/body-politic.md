# Body Politic

The **first major global Long Covid support group**. Originally a queer-feminist wellness collective founded in NYC ~2018 by [[fiona-lowenstein|Fiona Lowenstein]]; pivoted in March 2020 into a LC support group when [[fiona-lowenstein|Fiona]]'s NYT op-ed went viral and 2,000 people signed up overnight via the Google form linked from the article.

The group migrated within days from Instagram chat → WhatsApp → **Slack**, where it has lived ever since. Slack's channel-by-topic structure and security made it possible to scale to (eventually) 40,000+ cumulative signups; ~14,000+ active users at recording.

## Structure

- Channels by **body system**, **demographic group** (BIPOC, LGBTQ+, parents, healthcare workers), and **geographic region**.
- Volunteer moderators; no institutional funding ever received (per #69).
- Onboarding guide with explicit norms (no unsolicited medical advice; no diet-culture / fat-phobic posts; assume nothing about visible health).

## What it incubated

- **[[patient-led-research-collaborative|Patient-Led Research Collaborative (PLRC)]]** — formed in a "Data Nerds" channel; ran the first survey of LC symptoms in May 2020. Now an independent, funded organisation.
- **[[long-covid-sos-uk|Long Covid SOS]] (UK)** — formed by UK members who recognised each other regionally and split off into their own group.
