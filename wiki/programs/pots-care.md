# POTS Care

Texas-based clinic founded by **Dr Diana Driscoll** (recovered POTS / dysautonomia patient, optometrist, see #173). Treats POTS / dysautonomia / postviral inflammatory illness using Driscoll's commonalities-first algorithm.

## Working model (Driscoll Theory)

1. **High intracranial pressure** secondary to inflammation — frequently missed on MRI.
2. **Parasympathetic / acetylcholine deficiency** — receptors work; production / availability is the bottleneck. Treated with [[parasym-plus]].
3. **Oxidation from chronic inflammation** — vascular leakage, BBB compromise, anxiety / OCD-flavour hyperarousal. Treated with [[nac-max]].
4. **Search for additional drivers** — depending on the patient: viral persistence, MCAS, autoimmunity, etc.

The clinic also runs **Genetic Disease Investigators**, a separate research entity Driscoll founded to study genetic propensities for inflammatory POTS / postviral disease — so recovered patients can stay well after future viral triggers.

## Community channel

Driscoll runs **POTS Rebels** on Patreon (weekly meetings) — a way to push out information about the model, the genetics, and treatment strategies more broadly than the clinic alone can reach.
