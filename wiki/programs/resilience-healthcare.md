# Resilience Healthcare

Clinical practice founded by [[becca-kennedy]] (former Kaiser Permanente LC clinic lead), built around the **neuroplastic-symptoms / Howard Schubiner model** for chronic pain, fatigue, and other "medically unexplained" symptoms.

## What's offered

- Rule-out medical evaluation as the first step — "this is the doctor's job."
- Education in the neuroplastic-symptoms framework: body not damaged; nervous system hypervigilant; signals real but reversible.
- Tools layered as patient is ready:
  - **Stress-management basics** — meditation, breathwork, gentle exercise.
  - **Expressive writing** (Pennebaker-style focused emotional disclosure).
  - **EAET (Emotional Awareness and Expression Therapy)** — Schubiner + Lumley.
  - **Trauma-context mapping** — connecting current dysregulation to lifetime trauma context.
