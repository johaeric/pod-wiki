# Rebound Athletic

Online fitness company founded by [[rachael-rebound-athletic]] after her own LC recovery. Group-coaching program built around a 12-week reconditioning protocol designed specifically for de-trained / post-LC bodies.

Designed to avoid the two failure modes Rachael saw in the LC community:
- *Treating pacing as permanent inactivity* — risks deconditioning.
- *Returning to pre-LC training intensity* — risks PEM relapse and injury.

Coaching includes work on **fear of exercise**, redefining athletic identity, and managing residual PEM-adjacent triggers.

~50% of clients are LC patients.
