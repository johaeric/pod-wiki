# Hibernation Sessions

An **online concert series for housebound and chronically-isolated people**. Founded by **Olga**, a recovering LC musician + music teacher, in autumn 2024. Zoom-format concerts of musician friends, free-or-pay-what-you-can. Audience grew beyond the LC community to include older / isolated / housebound / age-restricted listeners.

## What it is

- **Zoom concerts**, scheduled in advance.
- **Free entry** (donations accepted but no obligation).
- **International musician roster** — first season featured a Cuban violinist, an Irish folk trio, and an English folk singer.
- **Season 2 launching early 2025** at the time of recording.

## Why it matters on the wiki

- **First wiki-anchored online concert series specifically for the homebound LC + ME + chronic-illness community.** Pairs with [[long-covid-choir]] (singing-side) but on the listening / concert side.
- **Low-energy participation** — listening to music is often within capacity even for severe-cohort patients.
- **Founder's framing**: organising concerts when she can't yet play "makes me feel like myself again." Identity-restoration via productive non-physical work — same operative move as the wiki's other founders-in-LC ([[harry-leeming]]'s Visible, [[fiona-lowenstein]]'s Body Politic).
