# Long Covid Kids Choir

Online weekly choir for children and young people with Long Covid — collaboration between **Merrill Vaughan** and the [[long-covid-kids|Long Covid Kids]] charity (CEO Sammy McFarland). Started **6 November 2022** with a six-week trial. Sessions: gentle breathing, tongue twisters, warm-ups, songs; ukulele; African drums; guest cat performances.
