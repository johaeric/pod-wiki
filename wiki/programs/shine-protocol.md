# SHINE Protocol

A multi-pillar treatment framework developed by [[jacob-teitelbaum]] for chronic fatigue syndrome and fibromyalgia, **explicitly applied to LC**. Acronym:

- **S** — **Sleep**: melatonin SR, herbal stack (valerian, lemon balm, 5-HTP), low-dose multi-agent pharmaceuticals (trazodone, cyclobenzaprine, gabapentin, low-dose olanzapine), Z-drugs, Epsom-salt bath, target 7–8 hr.
- **H** — **Hormones + Hypotension**: thyroid trial regardless of TSH; adrenal support (licorice, vitamin C+B5, low-dose hydrocortisone); bio-identical sex hormones if cyclic worsening; OI: salt + compression + midodrine + fludrocortisone + DDAVP. Beta-blockers cautious in CFS.
- **I** — **Infections**: candida (fluconazole 6 weeks), EBV reactivation (famciclovir + Celebrex 6 months), famotidine 40mg BID for immune support, *fluvoxamine* for residual COVID. Avoid PPIs long-term.
- **N** — **Nutrition**: salt up, sugar down, protein in frequent small meals, water up, multivitamin (B + Mg), **ribose 5g 2–3×/day**, CoQ10, acetyl-L-carnitine, curcumin, glutathione, boswellia.
- **E** — **Exercise**: walking only at submaximal capacity; cut 20% if PEM; very gradual increases. NO graded exercise / NO push-through.

## Adjuncts

- **LDN 3–4.5mg** (universally recommended for CFS/LC patients not on narcotics)
- **Low-dose Abilify 0.25mg** for severe near-bedbound (Stanford research)
- **ANS Rewire** (Dan Neuffer) or **DNRS** for autonomic-circuit-breaker reset

## Evidence

- Teitelbaum's **fibromyalgia/CFS RCT**: 91% improved, 90% average QoL increase. (Not LC-specific; not independently replicated for LC.)
- Mechanism story: **hypothalamic dysfunction** is the central circuit-breaker; SHINE resets it.

## Caveats

- Teitelbaum has commercial relationships with several supplements he names (Recovery Factors, HRG80 red ginseng, Smart Energy System).
- Several pharmaceutical recommendations (famciclovir+Celebrex, fluvoxamine, low-dose Abilify) are off-label and contested.
- The "treat everything at once" approach is at the opposite pole from the [[polypharmacy-warning]] critiques in #41/#45/#46.
