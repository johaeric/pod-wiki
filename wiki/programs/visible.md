# Visible Health

Wearables-driven measurement-and-management app for long covid, ME/CFS, and adjacent fluctuating chronic illnesses. Founded by [[harry-leeming]] (#86) after his own LC onset in September 2020.

## What it offers

### Free app
- Morning HR + HRV reading via smartphone camera (60s, finger on lens).
- Symptom + exertion (physical/cognitive/emotional) + menstrual + sleep tracking.
- Daily stability score against personal 3-month baseline.
- Trends tab for pattern-spotting (e.g. cognitive-exertion ↔ symptoms; cycle ↔ symptoms).

### Visible Plus (paid; beta as of recording)
- Continuous HR + HRV via Polar wearable (deliberately screenless to avoid hypervigilance).
- Real-time heart-rate-limit alerts.
- More accurate digital-biomarker capture (e.g. all-day orthostatic HR-jump).

## Distinctive design choices

- **No screen on the wearable** — explicit choice against hypervigilance entrenchment (cf. [[082-jay-wiles-hrv]]'s "data is motivation only" framing).
- **Tracking deliberately limited** to morning + evening + episodic — they push back against feature requests for hour-by-hour tracking because actionability and patient energy budget both argue against it.
- **Asynchronous community feedback** loop with development team; bi-weekly engineering blog posts.
- **Half the team has LC or ME** themselves; fully remote, asynchronous-by-default.

## Research-data sharing

- Opt-in (default off), per-study consent flow.
- First study in ethics: **Imperial College London (Dr Viktor Mehta)** — menstrual cycle ↔ LC symptom severity.
- "One of the largest data sets on LC and ME" — 25k users, 20M rows at recording, growing via word of mouth.
