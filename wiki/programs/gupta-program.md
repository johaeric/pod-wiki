# Gupta Program

Online neuroplastic-retraining programme founded by **Ashok Gupta**. Targets ME/CFS, fibromyalgia, MCAS, anxiety, and now LC. Curriculum is video-based, multi-week, with daily practice protocols that combine cognitive retraining + mindfulness + somatic exercises.

Cited as a representative of the broader brain-retraining family (alongside [[dnrs]], [[ans-rewire]], [[curable]]). Not yet appearing on the wiki with a `key_to_recovery` row from a recovered LC patient — when it does, the count moves.
