# Mount Sinai Long Covid Clinic (NYC)

US LC clinic that treats Joshua Roman (ep #126) and many other NYC-area LC patients. Multidisciplinary; well-known on this podcast for the mouth-breathing / upper-chest dysfunctional-breathing pattern Wibbs Coulson cited (ep #17) drawn from their patient observations.
