# Baseline Formula (Rachael / Rebound Athletic)

A codified three-component framework for **safely reintroducing strength work after LC / ME-CFS** without triggering PEM. Designed by [[rachael-rebound-athletic]] over her own 12-week reconditioning protocol; turned into a teachable framework for [[rebound-athletic]] members in 2024-2025.

The Baseline Formula sits in the **"movement, not exercise"** lineage that runs through [[047-darren-brown-long-covid-physio]], [[051-darren-brown-disability]], [[141-todd-davenport-pem]], [[155-nadyne-mckie-kindness]], and [[170-amy-mooney-flipping-the-iceberg]]. It is the **operationalised counterpart** to the philosophy.

## Mindset prerequisite

**Consistency over progression.** Counter-intuitive for ex-athletes / driven personalities. On good days, do *the same* not *more*. Consistency *is* progression — at the pace the body can actually absorb without crashing. Without this shift the rest fails.

## Three components

### 1. Movement modality — [[isolation-exercises]] / muscle activation

- Single-muscle-group movements (clams, glute bridges, scapular squeezes, hip-flexor activation) — *not* compound movements (squats, lunges, presses).
- Lowest energy demand → most efficient route to repeatable strength work given mitochondrial dysfunction.
- Often horizontal — POTS-friendly.
- Slow + controlled + mindful → supports Mind-Body reconnection.

### 2. Dosage / volume

- **Handbrake question**: *Could I repeat this daily without symptoms worsening?* (You wouldn't actually do daily — rest matters — but the answer being yes is the test.)
- **Do less than you think you should / can / want to.**
- **Effort gauge: should always feel *easy*.** Sweating / huffing / straining = too much. As capacity rebuilds, the volume that feels easy increases — that's the progress signal.

### 3. Predictable progressions

- **Time**: stay at a baseline for as long as it takes to *build confidence in tolerance* — could be 2 weeks, 4 weeks, 6 weeks. **Loose definition deliberate.** No predetermined weekly progression schedule (key contrast with classic Progressive Overload + with graded exercise therapy).
- **Micro-progressions**: tiny incremental changes — small volume bump, one exercise added — and re-ask the handbrake question.
- **Flexibility / permission to scale back**: not "going backwards." Recovery isn't linear; movement isn't linear. Some weeks = mobility + stretches only.
