Online brain-retraining program founded by Dr Cathleen King (referenced in ep #188 via Raelan Agle's interview with her). Focuses on nervous-system regulation and somatic re-patterning.

Jamie (#186) used it in the early-mid recovery phase, doing the lessons from bed (program is meant to be done standing). She used the light-visualisation exercise while gently expanding her walking radius, reframing symptoms with self-talk: "this is just a hypersensitive nervous system."
