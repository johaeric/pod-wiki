# I Can Thrive (Jason McAuliffe brain-retraining program)

A brain-retraining program by **Jason McAuliffe** called **"I Can Thrive,"** featured prominently on Raelan Agle's channel. Identity disambiguated by RA #448 (Katrine, Belgium) who names both Jason and the program directly. Multiple Raelan guests credit it as the load-bearing program for their recovery (`key_to_recovery` 3× now). Operates as a paid subscription program with online courses + individual coaching sessions.

Methodology described by guests:
- Visualisations 10–20× per day (relaxed/healthy self imagining)
- Identity / self-confidence training (transferable beyond illness — climbing, presentations, life goals)
- "Self-fulfilling expectations" framing
- Pattern identification + replacement of "thought patterns that aren't conducive to wellbeing"
- Tailored / individualised work via 1:1 coaching

Adjacent to but distinct from [[gupta-program]] / [[dynamic-neural-retraining-system]] / [[ans-rewire]] / [[primal-trust]].

## Mechanism (as guests describe it)

- Visualisation work: imagine a relaxed, healthy version of yourself doing the activities you can no longer do — repeated 10–20× per day
- Identity / self-confidence training: not just for illness but for life goals (climbing problems, sports performance, presentations)
- "Self-fulfilling expectations" framing: build the belief, the body responds
- One-on-one program with regular check-ins; paid subscription model
