Thrive90 is a recovery program co-founded by **Katie Brennan** (LC patient → recovered) and **Andrea Heinberg** (German Olympic biathlon competitor and personal trainer). Built on cognitive-motor coordination drills layered into low-load fitness and nervous-system practices. Aim: brain-body recoupling for LC, ME/CFS, and aging-related cognitive decline (the "thrive at 90" goal).

## Distinguishing features

- Bridges sports-performance and chronic-illness recovery — "elite athletes use these tools too"
- Drills are tiny, low-energy, can be done at home (ankle tilts, eye movements, joint mobility with cognitive overlay)
- Self-assessments before/after each drill let the user *measure* their own response
- Aimed at women 90% (Katie's framing: longer-term life-quality)

## Theory

- Cognitive-motor coordination drills create new brain-body connections that may have been damaged by virus or compensated for by deconditioning
- Exercises are short enough to *not* trigger PEM but stimulate the nervous system to recalibrate
- Pacing-as-signal: symptoms are guides, not enemies
