# Dysautonomia International

US-based **501(c)(3) non-profit** focused on dysautonomia (POTS, OI, OH, autonomic neuropathy, etc.) — co-founded ~2012 by [[lauren-stiles|Lauren Stiles]] and a group of like-minded patients, parents, and doctors. Now the largest dysautonomia advocacy and research-funding organisation in the world.

URLs: `dysautonomiainternational.org` and `cps.org` (both reach the same site). For Long Covid specifically, they also run `longidresearchfund.org`.

## What they do

- **Patient support** — support groups in all 50 US states + 60 other countries; patient-curated lists of dysautonomia-savvy doctors (cardiologists, gynaecologists, dentists, etc.) per region.
- **Clinician education** — training programs to expand the dysautonomia-savvy clinician pool.
- **Research funding** — including **Long Covid research** since the pandemic:
  - **Long Covid POTS IVIG immunotherapy clinical trial** (announced #66)
  - [[low-dose-naltrexone|LDN]] research
  - **ECP** (enhanced external counterpulsation — leg compression therapy)
  - **Auto-antibody / immune-marker discovery** in LC
- **Research collaborations** — e.g. with Stanford on the survey finding that **67% of LC patients have moderate-to-severe dysautonomia**.
