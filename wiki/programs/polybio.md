# PolyBio Research Foundation + Long Covid Research Consortium

Non-profit research organisation founded ~2018 (pre-COVID) by [[amy-proal]] and collaborators, originally to fund and coordinate research into **infection-associated chronic illness** (ME/CFS, post-Ebola, post-Lyme). When COVID hit, it pivoted to also coordinate the **Long Covid Research Consortium (LCRC)** — a multi-institution collaborative network specifically studying SARS-CoV-2 viral persistence and downstream mechanisms.

This is included on the wiki as a **program/organisation** because it is the central node of the viral-persistence research effort the podcast keeps pointing at.

## Member institutions (per ep #103)

- Harvard Medical School (incl. Mike Van Elzakker, mitochondrial-hijacking paper with Proal)
- Stanford
- Karolinska Institute (Sweden)
- UCSF (Henrich + Peluso group — PET-imaging T-cell activation in LC)
- University of Pennsylvania (tissue-model antiviral screening)
- Mt Sinai (David Putrino — clinical-translation collaboration)
- Johns Hopkins (Diane Griffin — measles RNA-virus persistence)
- *(additional sites — see polybio.org for the up-to-date list)*

## What the Consortium does (per ep #103)

- **Coordinates collaborative projects** across multiple sites that share data, methodology, and findings.
- **Funds projects via private support** — not NIH-dependent. Calls publicly for NIH to follow.
- **Tissue biopsy studies**: gut (colonoscopy), lung (bronchoscopy), lymph node — looking for SARS-CoV-2 reservoirs in tissue rather than blood.
- **PET-imaging studies**: radiolabeled antibody T-cell activation maps; spike-protein imaging in development; method portable to other pathogens (EBV, Lyme spirochete) once SARS-CoV-2 work is complete.
- **Clinical-trial design**: planning Truvada trial at Mt Sinai for EBV-component cases; advising other antiviral RCTs; advocating combination-therapy designs.
- **Review papers**: multi-author Consortium reviews summarising the case for viral persistence (referenced in #103, likely *Nature Reviews Microbiology* Proal et al. 2023).

## Why it matters on the wiki

- **The viral-persistence research effort the podcast keeps pointing at has a name and a structure** — PolyBio + LCRC. The wiki should treat this as the canonical reference point for that mechanism research, not individual papers in isolation.
- **Privately funded** — patients can donate directly. PolyBio is one of the few research bodies where individual donations meaningfully accelerate work.
- **Open-model clinic at Mt Sinai (with Putrino)** — the explicit goal is to make the clinical-translation model replicable so other groups can copy it.
