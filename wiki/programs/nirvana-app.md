Nirvana is a neuroplastic-recovery app co-founded and led by **Nora Rodden** (Harvard MBA, recovered from chronic back pain, GI symptoms, and severe insomnia). The app productises the mind-body / TMS-style methodology that helped her — drawing on the [Psychophysiologic Symptom Relief Therapy (PSRT) clinical trial](../trials/psrt-back-pain-trial.md) protocol.

## What it offers

- Education on pain neuroscience and the mind-body framework
- Somatic tracking, breath work, expressive writing prompts
- Symptom tracking — but designed *not* to encourage daily symptom-watching, since attention to symptoms can amplify them
- Aimed at making neuroplastic recovery accessible without needing a coach or in-person trial

## Theory

- Many chronic symptoms (pain, GI, insomnia) are nociplastic / neuroplastic in origin — the brain's threat system stays "on" after the original cause has resolved
- Recovery requires retraining the brain that the symptom signals are no longer threats
- Outcome independence ("stop caring about whether you sleep") is a core technique
