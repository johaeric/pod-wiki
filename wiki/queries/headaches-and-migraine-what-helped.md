# Severe headaches / migraine — what helped (and what didn't)

For a friend with the same profile as Eric (POTS, PEM, brain fog) **plus** severe headaches/migraine — the kind that drove her to ER for morphine.

Based on podcast testimony — this is anecdotal evidence, not clinical proof. Cross-check against `ercipedia/` for peer-reviewed evidence before acting.

## The framing first (matters)

Most LC headache is **nociplastic** — central nervous-system sensitisation, not tissue damage. This is [[deepak-ravindran]]'s framework (LCP #53):

> "More than 90% of my patients are not coming up with anything sinister or anything structurally damaging on scans — that means their pain is reversible nervous-system hypersensitivity." — Ravindran, LCP #53

The clinical correlate of this — repeatedly seen in the corpus — is **new-onset migraine** or **dramatic worsening of pre-existing migraine** after COVID, in patients whose imaging and bloodwork are clean. Same mechanism family as fibromyalgia, ME/CFS, IBS.

**This matters because**: the therapeutic lever for nociplastic pain is calming the nervous + immune systems, not analgesia at the symptom level. That's why standard migraine tablets often fail in LC patients, and why nervous-system work shows up in the recovery stories.

**BUT** — ER-level severity requiring morphine warrants a proper workup *first*. From the corpus:
- Rule out **medication side effects** (Groysman, LCP #92 — many drugs cause headaches; check current stack)
- Rule out **structural / thyroid** issues — Karen Wright's migraine + tinnitus turned out to be partly driven by a thyroid nodule that needed surgery (LCP #182 / RA #961). Worth a workup.
- The patient story from Devon Carter (RA #984) is a warning the other way: three rounds of antibiotics for presumed sinus infection while the real issue was LC.

## What helped — by category

### Specific drug / supplement protocols (clinician-recommended)

| Intervention | Source | What was said |
|---|---|---|
| **Vitamin B2 (riboflavin) 400 mg/day × 6 weeks** | [[jacob-teitelbaum]] LCP #167 | "In repeated studies decreases migraine frequency by 67 to 70%." Cheap, OTC, well-evidenced outside LC; he uses it routinely. |
| **Magnesium** | Teitelbaum LCP #167 | Adjunct to B2; also reduces migraine. |
| **Triptans (imitrex-type) — taken *early*** | Teitelbaum LCP #167 | Critical pacing detail: take them *before* pain wraps around the eyes/ears. Once you're at that level, the stomach has shut off and oral meds won't absorb. A shot of strong coffee with the triptan can sometimes re-open absorption. |
| **H1 + H2 antihistamines (e.g. loratadine + famotidine, cetirizine + famotidine)** | [[tina-peers]] LCP #38, [[david-saperstein]] LCP #68 | If there's any MCAS pattern — flushing, food/scent reactivity, hives, BP swings — headache is one of the classic MCAS targets. Saperstein routinely uses **higher-than-OTC dose, twice daily** under supervision. See [[antihistamines]] for full protocol. |
| **Gabapentin / amitriptyline / duloxetine** | Ravindran LCP #53 | Only if there's a clear *neuropathic* component (a discrete nerve injury). Largely ineffective for nociplastic pattern — which is most LC headache. |

### Nervous-system / mind-body (where the *recoveries* concentrate)

This is the strongest signal in the corpus by far. Patients who fully recovered from LC frequently resolved chronic migraine **alongside** the LC, through brain-retraining and somatic work — not through migraine-specific treatment.

- **[[natalie-gold]] (LCP #189)** — recovered through ANS Rewire. Resolved 16 years of psoriatic arthritis *and chronic migraine* alongside the LC. Frames migraine as part of the same nervous-system dysregulation pattern. Closest profile match to Eric in the entire podcast.
- **[[rebecca-tolin]] (LCP #166)** — recovered from ME/CFS, *migraines*, insomnia, interstitial cystitis. Now teaches the approach. "There are some conditions created by the brain and then they manifest in the body."
- **[[esther-recovery]] (LCP #32)** — extraordinary single anecdote: after learning to notice the emotion underneath a symptom, a headache she'd had for months would "be just gone in five minutes" once she identified what her brain was protecting her from. Worth listening to specifically for the migraine angle.
- **Karen Wright (LCP #182 / RA #961)** — migraine + tinnitus persisted through her brain-retraining recovery and then resolved after thyroid surgery + a posture of surrender. Honest reminder that not every migraine is purely nervous-system.

### Diet / trigger management

| Intervention | Source | What was said |
|---|---|---|
| **Anti-inflammatory whole-foods diet** | Ravindran LCP #53, Myhill LCP #118 | Foundation, not magic. |
| **Low-histamine diet** | Peers LCP #38 | Pair with antihistamines if MCAS pattern present. |
| **Aggressive trigger removal** | Miguel Bautista RA #623 (recovered) | In the acute phase his nervous system was so reactive that "even a small piece of chocolate would give me a migraine." Reduced to salads, rice, bone broth, chicken, yams until things settled. He frames this as nervous-system overload, not food allergy. |
| **Homeopathy (constitutional)** | [[roddy-schrock]] LCP #210 | One patient case: "extraordinary migraine headaches" majorly decreased in frequency and intensity after constitutional homeopathic treatment. N=1, low evidence grade, but logged. |

## What clearly did NOT help (multiple stories)

- **Standard migraine tablets / triptans when the headache wasn't actually migraine** — Chiara (LCP #3, herself a former GP) was misdiagnosed as "menopausal migraine," tried the standard meds, found they did nothing because the underlying mechanism was post-viral, not migrainous.
- **Ibuprofen / OTC painkillers** — Rosie (LCP #61) had constant migraine for >1 year. Nothing OTC made any noticeable difference; she stopped mentioning it because "nothing you can do makes any difference."
- **Repeated rounds of antibiotics for presumed sinus infection** — Devon Carter (RA #984) — three escalating rounds before LC was identified.
- **Pushing through ("if you've got a headache or period pain, you keep going")** — Karen Wright (RA #961) explicitly names this as the pattern that kept her stuck for years before recovery.

## Bottom-line read for Eric's friend

The single highest-value combination if she has no contraindications, in roughly the order I'd think about it:

1. **Workup first** — given ER-level severity, rule out structural / thyroid / drug-side-effect causes before treating empirically. Imaging if not done.
2. **B2 400 mg + magnesium daily** — cheapest, best-evidenced supplement combination in the corpus for migraine frequency reduction. ~6 weeks for effect.
3. **If any MCAS-pattern features** (food/scent reactivity, flushing, BP swings, hives, GI symptoms) — H1 + H2 antihistamine trial via the [[antihistamines]] protocol; can be done OTC for the first step.
4. **A neurology referral for non-invasive vagus stimulation (gammaCore)** if the migraine pattern persists — best device-level evidence in the corpus.
5. **Nervous-system work in parallel** — this is where the *recoveries* are, even though it's not where the *acute relief* is. Natalie Gold (LCP #189) is the closest analogue: ANS Rewire as the path that resolved both LC and chronic migraine. Brain-retraining programs that explicitly include migraine in their scope: DNRS, ANS Rewire, Gupta, Curable (the last is most pain-focused).
6. **Trigger management** — anti-inflammatory diet baseline, plus low-histamine if MCAS pattern.

Read this as: anecdote + clinical opinion across 17 episodes. Strongest **patient-credited-recovery** signal is on the nervous-system side. Strongest **specific-symptom-frequency-reduction** signal is on the B2/magnesium side. Neither is a replacement for proper workup of severe headache.
